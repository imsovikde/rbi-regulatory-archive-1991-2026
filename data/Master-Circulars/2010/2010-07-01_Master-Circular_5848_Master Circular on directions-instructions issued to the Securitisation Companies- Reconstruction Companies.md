## securitisation or asset reconstruction

## (3)Commencement of business by Securitisation Companies/Reconstruction Companies

The Bank had issued the guidelines vide Notification No.DNBS.6/CGM (PK)- 2006 dated October 19, 2006 that the Securitisation Company or Reconstruction Company should commence business within six months from the date of grant of Certificate of Registration. The Bank may on application made by SC/RC grant extension of time beyond six months but in no case such extension of time shall extend 12 months from the date of grant of Certificate of Registration.
