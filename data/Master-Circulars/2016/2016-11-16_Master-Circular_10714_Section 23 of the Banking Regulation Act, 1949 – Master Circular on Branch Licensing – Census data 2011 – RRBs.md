## Section 23 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 - Master Circular on Branch

## Licensing-Census data 2011

Please refer to our Master circular on Branch Licensing <u>DBR.CO.RRB.BL.BC.No.17/31.01.002/2015-16 dated July 1, 2015</u>. As the Census data for 2011 are available in public domain, Regional Rural Banks are advised to follow Census 2011 for all purposes of categorisations. The details of Tier-wise Population Groups, List of Underbanked Districts in Underbanked States and List of Underbanked Districts in other States based on Census 2011 are enclosed at Annex 1, 2 and 3 respectively.
