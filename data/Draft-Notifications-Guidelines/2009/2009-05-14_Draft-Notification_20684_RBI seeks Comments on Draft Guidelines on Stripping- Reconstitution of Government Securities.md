## May 14, 2009

## RBI seeks Comments on Draft Guidelines on Stripping/

## Reconstitution of Government Securities

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website "<u>Draft Guidelines</u> <u>on Stripping/Reconstitution of Government Securities</u>" for comments/views**.** Comments/views on the draft guidelines may be sent by post to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai 400001 or can be <u>emailed</u> on or before May 29, 2009.

The Reserve Bank of India will finalise and issue the guidelines after examining the views/feedback received.
