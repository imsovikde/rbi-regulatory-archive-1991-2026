## September 17, 2009

## RBI seeks Comments on Draft Guidelines on Repo in Corporate Debt Securities

The Reserve Bank of India has placed on its website today "<u>Draft Guidelines</u> <u>on Repo in Corporate Debt Securities</u>" for comments/views, which may be sent by post to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai 400001 or may be sent by <u>email</u> on or before October 05, 2009.

The Reserve Bank of India will finalise and issue the guidelines after examining the views/ feedback received.
