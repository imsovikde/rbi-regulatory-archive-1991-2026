## RBI announces Draft Framework on introduction of Tri-Party Repo

The Reserve Bank of India, today, released the <u>draft framework</u> on the introduction of Tri-Party Repo. Tri-party repo will enable market participants to use underlying collateral more efficiently and facilitate development of the term repo market in India. Draft directions allow introduction of tri-party repo on both Government securities and corporate bonds.

Comments on the draft framework are invited from market participants by May 5, 2017. Comments may be <u>emailed</u> or sent by post to the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Financial Markets Regulation Department, Central Office, Main Building, Mumbai – 400001.
