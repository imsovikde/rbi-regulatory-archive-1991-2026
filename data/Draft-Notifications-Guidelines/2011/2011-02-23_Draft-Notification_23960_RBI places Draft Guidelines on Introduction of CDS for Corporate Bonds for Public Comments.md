## February 23, 2011

## RBI places Draft Guidelines on Introduction of CDS for Corporate Bonds for Public Comments

The Reserve Bank of India has, today, placed on its website, ‘<u>draft guidelines</u> on introduction of credit default swaps (CDS) for corporate bonds’ for public comments.

The <u>draft report</u> of the Internal Working Group on introduction of CDS for corporate bonds was placed on the Reserve Bank’s website on August 4, 2010 for public comments. The <u>report as finalised</u> after taking into account public comments is also placed on the Reserve Bank’s website. Draft guidelines on introduction of single name CDS on corporate bonds have been prepared in the light of suggestions and feedback received.

Comments on the draft guidelines may be forwarded, latest by March 8, 2011, to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve rd Bank of India, Central Office Building, 23 Floor, S.B.S. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400001 or <u>emailed</u>.
