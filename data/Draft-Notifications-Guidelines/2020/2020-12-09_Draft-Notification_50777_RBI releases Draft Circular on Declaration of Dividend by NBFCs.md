## December 09, 2020

## RBI releases Draft Circular on Declaration of Dividend by NBFCs

In pursuance of the announcements made in the <u>Statement on Developmental</u> <u>and Regulatory Policies dated December 04, 2020</u>, the Reserve Bank of India has released today the <u>Draft Circular on Declaration of Dividend by NBFCs</u>. Comments on the Draft Circular are invited from NBFCs, industry participants and other interested parties by December 24, 2020.

Feedback on the Draft Circular may be forwarded to:

The Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Department of Regulation (NBFCs), 2 nd Floor, Main Office Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort, Mumbai – 400001

*or*

by <u>email</u> with the subject line “Feedback on Draft circular on declaration of dividend by NBFCs.”
