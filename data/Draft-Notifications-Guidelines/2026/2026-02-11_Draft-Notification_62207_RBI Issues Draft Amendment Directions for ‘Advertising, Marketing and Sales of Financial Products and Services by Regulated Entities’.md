## February 11, 2026

**RBI Issues Draft Amendment Directions for** ‘**Advertising, Marketing and Sales** **of Financial Products and Services by Regulated Entities’**

Currently, instructions on customer appropriateness and suitability and other related matters in the context of insurance agency business have been issued to Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding Regional Rural Banks) and Housing Finance Companies. Upon a review, it has been decided to issue comprehensive instructions on advertising, marketing and sales of financial products and services (including third-party products and services) to all banks and NBFCs, which shall include various aspects related thereto, such as activities of Direct Sales Agents (DSAs) / Direct Marketing Agents (DMAs), dark patterns, prevention of mis-selling, etc.

**2.** Accordingly, in pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on</u> <u>Developmental and Regulatory Policies dated February 6, 2026</u>, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has today issued the following draft Amendment Directions for public comments, which propose to amend existing Directions issued by the Department of Regulation, RBI.
(i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Responsible Business Conduct)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks -Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (iii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks-Responsible Business Conduct)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (iv) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks-Responsible Business Conduct)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
(v) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks -Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (vi) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks-Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (vii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Rural Co-operative Banks -Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (viii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (All India Financial Institutions-Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (ix) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies -Responsible</u> <u>Business Conduct) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
