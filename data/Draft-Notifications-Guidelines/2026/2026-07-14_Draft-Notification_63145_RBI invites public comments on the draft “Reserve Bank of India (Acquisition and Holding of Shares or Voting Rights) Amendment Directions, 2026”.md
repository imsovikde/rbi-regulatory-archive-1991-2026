## July 14, 2026

## RBI invites public comments on the draft “Reserve Bank of India (Acquisition

## and Holding of Shares or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026”

Based on a review undertaken in light of representations received from Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and in order to simplify approval process for subsequent acquisitions of major shareholding in a banking company by mutual funds, insurance companies and pension funds, the Reserve Bank of India has released today the following draft Amendment Directions:

(i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Acquisition and Holding of Shares</u> <u>or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>. (ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Acquisition and Holding of</u> <u>Shares or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>. (iii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks – Acquisition and Holding of Shares or</u> <u>Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>. (iv) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks – Acquisition and Holding of Shares</u> <u>or Voting Rights) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>.
2. The comments / feedback on the said draft Amendment Directions may be submitted by the regulated entities and members of public / other stakeholders on or before **August 4**,**2026,** through the following channels:
(i) the ‘<u>Connect 2 Regulate</u>’ Section available on the Reserve Bank’s website by following the corresponding hyperlink provided against each document in the page where they are hosted; or (ii) by <u>email</u> with the subject line ‘Feedback on (full name of the draft Amendment Directions (including the type of Regulated Entity))’.
