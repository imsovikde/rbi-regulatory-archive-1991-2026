## August 6, 2026

## RBI invites comments on the draft “Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking

## Financial Companies – Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026”

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>draft “Reserve Bank of India (Non-</u> <u>Banking Financial Companies – Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026”</u>. Comments/feedback on the draft Amendment Directions are invited from regulated entities and other interested stakeholders by August 28, 2026.

Feedback on the draft Amendment Directions may be submitted through ‘Connect <u>2 Regulate</u>’ or by <u>email</u> with subject line “Feedback on draft Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Credit Facilities) Amendment Directions, 2026”.
