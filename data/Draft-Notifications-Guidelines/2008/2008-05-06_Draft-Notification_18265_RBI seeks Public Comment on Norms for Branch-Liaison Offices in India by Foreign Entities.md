RBl seeks Public Comment on Norms for

Branch/Liaison Offices in India by Foreign Entities

The Reserve Bank of India has proposed to delegate certain powers to

authorised dealers regarding extension of validity period of liaison offices of

foreign entities and closure of their branch/liaison offices in India. It has proposed

necessary time to authorised dealers to assimilate the revised dispensation.

Further, under the present provisions of Foreign Exchange Management

Act, a person resident outside India requires prior approval of the Reserve Bank

of India for establishing branch/liaison offices in India. The applications are
