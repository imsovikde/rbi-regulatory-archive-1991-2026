## October 26, 2023

## RBI invites comments on draft Master Direction on Managing Risks and Code of

## Conduct in Outsourcing of Financial Services

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website the <u>draft Master Direction</u> <u>on Managing Risks and Code of Conduct in Outsourcing of Financial Services</u>. Comments / Feedback, if any, may be sent by <u>email</u> with the subject line “Comments on draft Master Direction on Managing Risks and Code of Conduct in Outsourcing of Financial Services”, by **November 28, 2023**.

2. The draft Directions have been prepared by incorporating, updating, and where required, harmonizing the extant directions/guidelines/instructions to enable REs to have all current instructions on outsourcing of financial services at one place for reference.
3. It was announced in the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies</u> issued as part of the <u>Monetary Policy Statement dated August 05, 2022</u>, that the Reserve Bank of India will issue a draft Master Direction on Managing Risks and Code of Conduct in Outsourcing of Financial Services.
