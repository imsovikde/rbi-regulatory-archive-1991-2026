## April 21, 2010

## RBI issues Draft Guidelines regarding proposed

## Regulatory Framework for Core Investment Companies (CICs)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) today posted on its website the proposed Guidelines for regulatory framework for Core Investment Companies (CICs). The Reserve Bank evolved the <u>draft guidelines</u> as it had been receiving requests from companies and further in view of the systemic implications of access to public funds by CICs.

To adopt a consultative approach, the guidelines have been placed on the Reserve Bank's website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>) for study by a wider audience. Comments/suggestions may please be sent by <u>email</u> at the earliest but within 15 days of hosting of the guidelines, for consideration of the Reserve Bank.
