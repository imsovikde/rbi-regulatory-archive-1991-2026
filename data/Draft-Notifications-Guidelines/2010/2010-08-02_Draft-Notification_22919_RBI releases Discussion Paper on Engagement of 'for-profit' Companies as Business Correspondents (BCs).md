## August 2, 2010

## RBI releases Discussion Paper on Engagement of 'for-profit' Companies as Business Correspondents (BCs)

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website, a <u>Discussion Paper</u> on "Engagement of 'for profit' Companies as Business Correspondents (BCs)" to seek the views / comments of banks, non-banking financial institutions, ‘for-profit’ companies, regulators, academicians, NGOs and the public at large.

Comments/feedback on the Discussion Paper may please be sent by **August 20, 2010** to the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Department th of Banking Operations and Development, Central Office, 5 floor, World Trade Centre-1, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai-400005 or <u>emailed</u>.

## Alpana Killawala
