## April 26, 2024

**RBI invites comments on the Draft Circular on “Digital Lending – Transparency in Aggregation of Loan Products from Multiple Lenders”**

In pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on Developmental and</u> <u>Regulatory Policies dated December 08, 2023</u> regarding issuance of a regulatory framework for aggregation of loan products by lending service providers (LSPs), the Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website the <u>Draft Circular on ‘Digital</u> <u>Lending – Transparency in Aggregation of Loan Products from Multiple Lenders’</u>.

Comments/ feedback, if any, may be sent by <u>e-mail</u> with the subject line “Comments on Draft Circular on Digital Lending – Transparency in Aggregation of Loan Products from Multiple Lenders”, by May 31, 2024.
