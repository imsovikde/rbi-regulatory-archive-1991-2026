## October 24, 2025

## RBI issues Draft Directions pursuant to Policy Announcement

In pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on Developmental and</u> <u>Regulatory Policies dated October 01, 2025</u>, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has today issued the following draft Directions for public comments:

## 1. Draft Reserve Bank of India (Capital Market Exposure) Directions, 2025

i) <u>Draft Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Capital Market Exposure)</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u> ii) <u>Draft Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks-Capital Market Exposure)</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u>
## 2. <u>Draft ‘Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale</u>

## <u>Based Regulation) Amendment Directions, 2025’</u>

The comments on the draft guidelines are invited from public/stakeholders by November 21, 2025. The comments/ feedback may be submitted through the link under the ‘<u>Connect2Regulate</u>’ Section available on the Reserve Bank’s website. Comments may alternatively be forwarded to The Chief General Manager, Credit Risk Group, Department of Regulation, Central Office Reserve Bank of India, 12th/ 13th Floor Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort Mumbai – 400 001 or by <u>email</u>.
