## August 06, 2025

## RBI invites comments on the draft circular on ‘Reserve Bank of India

## (Settlement of Claims in respect of Deceased Customers of Banks) Directions,

## 2025’

In pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on Developmental and</u> <u>Regulatory Policies dated August 06, 2025</u> regarding the review of extant regulatory guidelines on <u>settlement of claims in respect of deceased depositors</u>, Reserve Bank has released today the <u>draft circular</u> in this regard.

Comments/ feedback by the stakeholders and members of public on the draft circular may be submitted through the respective link under the ‘<u>Connect 2 Regulate</u>’ Section available on the Reserve Bank’s website or alternatively through <u>e-mail</u> by **August 27, 2025**. Final circular shall be issued after considering the stakeholder/ public comments.
