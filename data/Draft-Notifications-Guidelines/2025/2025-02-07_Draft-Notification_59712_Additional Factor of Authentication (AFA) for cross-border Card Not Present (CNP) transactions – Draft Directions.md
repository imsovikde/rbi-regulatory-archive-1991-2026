## February 7, 2025

## Additional Factor of Authentication (AFA) for cross-border Card Not Present

## (CNP) transactions – Draft Directions

As announced in <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies dated</u> <u>February 07, 2025</u>, Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website draft directions on <u>Additional Factor of Authentication (AFA) for cross-border Card Not Present (CNP)</u> <u>transactions</u> for public comments.

The draft directions require a card issuer to validate AFA for non-recurring cross- border CNP transaction, whenever a request for AFA is raised by the overseas merchant or the overseas acquirer.

Comments / feedback on the draft directions may be sent by <u>email</u> or by post to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department of Payment and Settlement Systems, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 14th Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai-400001, on or before March 10, 2025.
