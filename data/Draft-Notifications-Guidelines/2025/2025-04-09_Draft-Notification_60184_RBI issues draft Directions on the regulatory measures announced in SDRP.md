## April 09, 2025

## RBI issues draft Directions on the regulatory measures announced in SDRP

In pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on Developmental and</u> <u>Regulatory Policies today</u>, the Reserve Bank has released the draft Directions on the following subjects:

i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Securitisation of Stressed Assets) Directions, 2025</u> ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Co-Lending Arrangements) Directions, 2025</u> iii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Lending Against Gold Collateral) Directions, 2025</u> iv) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Fund Based Credit Facilities) Directions, 2025</u> The comments on the draft Directions are invited from public/stakeholders till May
12, 2025. Comments/feedback may be submitted through the respective links under the *‘*<u>Connect 2 Regulate</u>*’* Section available on the RBI’s website or may alternatively be forwarded to:

The Chief General Manager Credit Risk Group Department of Regulation, Central Office Reserve Bank of India, 12/13th Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort Mumbai – 400 001 Or by <u>email</u>
