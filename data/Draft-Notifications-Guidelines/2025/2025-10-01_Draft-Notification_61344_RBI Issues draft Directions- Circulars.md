## October 01, 2025

## RBI Issues draft Directions/ Circulars

In pursuance of the announcement made in the <u>Statement on Developmental</u> <u>and Regulatory Policies dated October 1, 2025</u>, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has today issued the following draft Directions/ Circulars for public comments:

1. Reserve Bank of India (Transaction Accounts) Directions, 2025
(i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u> (ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u> (iii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u> (iv) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u>
(v) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u> (vi) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Rural Co-operative Banks – Transaction Accounts</u>) <u>Directions, 2025</u> (vii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks – Transaction Accounts)</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u>
2. <u>Guidelines on Enhancing Credit Supply for Large Borrowers through Market</u> <u>Mechanism (Repeal Circular), 2025</u>
3. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account) Directions</u>, <u>2025</u> The comments on the draft Directions/ circulars are invited from the banks, market participants, and other interested parties till <u>October 24, 2025</u>. The comments/ feedback may be submitted through the link under the ‘<u>Connect 2</u> <u>Regulate</u>’ Section available on the Reserve Bank’s website. Comments may alternatively be forwarded to *The Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department* *of Regulation, Central Office Reserve Bank of India, 12*
*th* */ 13th Floor Shahid* *Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort Mumbai – 400 001* or by <u>email</u>.
