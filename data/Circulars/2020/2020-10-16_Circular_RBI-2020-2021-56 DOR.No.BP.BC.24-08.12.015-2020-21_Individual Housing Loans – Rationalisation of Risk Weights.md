## Individual Housing Loans – Rationalisation of Risk Weights

Please refer to paragraph 6 of the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory</u> <u>Policies dated October 9, 2020</u> on rationalization of risk weights on Individual Housing Loans.

2. |---|---|
|≤ 80|35|
