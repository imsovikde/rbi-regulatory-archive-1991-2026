# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2019-2020/183 FMOD.MAOG.No.139/01.18.001/2019-20 March 27, 2020

All Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) participants Madam/Sir, **Marginal Standing Facility** As announced in the <u>Seventh Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2019-20</u> today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the policy Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 75 basis points from 5.15 per cent to **4.40 per** **cent** with immediate effect.

2. Consequently, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate stands adjusted from 5.40 per cent to **4.65 per cent** with immediate effect.
Yours sincerely

## (Radha Shyam Ratho)

Chief General Manager
