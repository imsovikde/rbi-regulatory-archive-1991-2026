# Change in Bank Rate

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

भारतीय रज़वर् बक

## _____________ RESERVE BANK OF INDIA______________

**www.rbi.org.in** RBI/2019-20/240 DOR.No.Ret.BC.74/12.01.001/2019-20 May 22, 2020

### All Scheduled and Non Scheduled Banks

Dear Sir / Madam <u>Change in Bank Rate</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.Ret.BC.53/12.01.001/2019-20 dated March 27</u>, <u>2020</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As announced in the <u>Monetary Policy Statement 2020-21 dated May 22, 2020</u>, the Bank Rate is revised downwards by 40 basis points from 4.65 per cent to 4.25 per cent with immediate effect.
3. All penal interest rates on shortfall in reserve requirements, which are specifically linked to the Bank Rate, also stand revised as indicated in the <u>Annex</u>**.**
# Withdrawn
