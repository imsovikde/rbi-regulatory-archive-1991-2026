To All Regulated Entities of the Bank

Madam/ Dear Sir,

## Inclusion of Goods and Service Tax Network (GSTN) as a Financial Information

## Provider under Account Aggregator Framework

Please refer to the <u>Master Direction – Non-Banking Financial Company - Account</u> <u>Aggregator (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2016 dated September 02, 2016</u>.

2. With a view to facilitate cash flow-based lending to MSMEs, it has been decided to include Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN) as a Financial Information Provider (FIP) under the Account Aggregator (AA) framework. Department of Revenue shall be the regulator of GSTN for this specific purpose and Goods and Services Tax (GST) Returns, viz. Form GSTR-1 and Form GSTR-3B, shall be the Financial Information.
# Withdrawn

3. Accordingly, the select instructions contained in the Master Direction referred to above have been amended, as detailed in the <u>Annex</u>. Yours faithfully, (J.P. Sharma) Chief General Manager
