## All Eligible Market Participants

Madam / Sir,

## Rupee Interest Rate Derivatives (Reserve Bank) Directions-Review

Please refer to Paragraph 5 of the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory</u> <u>Policies</u>, issued as a part of the <u>sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2021-</u> <u>22 dated February 10, 2022</u> regarding permitting banks in India to deal in foreign currency settled Overnight Indexed Swaps (OIS). Attention is also invited to the <u>Rupee</u> <u>Interest Rate Derivatives (Reserve Bank) Directions, 2019 dated June 26, 2019</u> (hereinafter, Directions).

2. Banks in India having Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Cat-I) license under FEMA, 1999, shall be eligible to offer Foreign Currency Settled OIS (FCS-OIS) based on the Overnight Mumbai Interbank Outright Rate (MIBOR) benchmark published by

Banks can undertake these transactions through their branches in India, through their International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) Banking Units (IBUs) or through their foreign branches (in case of foreign banks operating in India, through any branch of the parent bank). Banks may undertake FCS- OIS transactions beyond onshore market hours.
