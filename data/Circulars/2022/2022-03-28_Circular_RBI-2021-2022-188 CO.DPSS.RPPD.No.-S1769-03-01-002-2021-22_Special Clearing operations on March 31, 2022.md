## <u>Special Clearing operations on March 31, 2022</u>

A reference is invited to the circular issued by Department of Government and Bank Accounts (DGBA) vide <u>CO.DGBA.GBD.No.S1595/42-01-029/2021-2022 dated March 24, 2022</u> addressed to all the agency banks on Annual Closing of Government Accounts –Transactions of Central / State Governments – Special Measures for the Current Financial Year (2021-22).
