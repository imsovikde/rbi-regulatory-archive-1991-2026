## Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Financial Statements:

## Presentation and Disclosures) – Amendment Directions, 2026

Please refer to <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Financial Statements:</u> <u>Presentation and Disclosures) Directions, 2025</u> (hereinafter referred to as ‘the Directions’).

2. On a review, consequent to the issuance of the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small</u> <u>Finance Banks – Credit Risk Management) – Amendment Directions, 2026</u>, and in exercise of the powers conferred by the sections 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 and all other provisions / laws enabling the Reserve Bank of India (hereinafter called the Reserve Bank) in this regard, the Reserve Bank being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby issues the Amendment Directions hereinafter specified.
3. The Amendment Directions modifies the Directions as under: 3(1) In Chapter-III ‘Disclosure in Financial Statements – Notes to Accounts’, a new sub-paragraph (ix) shall be inserted under sub-paragraph 10(5) on ‘Exposures’, as given below: 10(5)(ix) **Exposures to Related Parties** Details of exposures to related parties as defined in <u>Reserve Bank of India</u> <u>(Small Finance Banks – Credit Risk Management) Directions, 2025</u> shall be disclosed as per the following table:
