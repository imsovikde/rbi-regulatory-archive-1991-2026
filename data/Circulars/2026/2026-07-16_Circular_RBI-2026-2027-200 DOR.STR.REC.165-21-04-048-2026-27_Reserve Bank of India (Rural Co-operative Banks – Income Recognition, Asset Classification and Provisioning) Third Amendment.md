(i) The following shall be inserted in Chapter V – Income Recognition:
## “A1. Income Recognition in case of acquisition of Specified Non-Financial

## Assets (SNFA)

Any income received from an SNFA shall be recognised in the income* *statement as ‘non-interest / other income’, in the financial year in which it is* *realised.
