## Data quality in Electronic Accounting system in Central Excise and Service Tax (EASIEST)

Please refer to our circular RBI/2007-08/225 DGBA.GAD.No.H-7633 /41.07.006/ 2007-08 dated January 15, 2008 on the above subject. Point 4 of para 2 thereof deals with situations where the assessee does not have a valid assessee code. In this connection, we advise that the Central Excise and Customs has created a new category of registrants (Non-Assessees) in Central Excise and Service Tax. The registration numbers/codes of these persons will be generated by the CBEC system and NSDL will be transmitting the same to the Link Cells of the Banks participating in EASIEST.
