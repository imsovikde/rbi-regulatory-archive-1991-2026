# Alteration in the name of Bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –" Industrial Development Bank of India Limited to IDBI Bank Limited"

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2008-09/159

DBOD.No.Ret.BC.40/12.01.001/2008-09

September8,2008

Bhadra 17, 1930 (S)

AllScheduledCommercialBanks

Dear Sir,

Alteration in the name of Bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,

1934 -" Industrial Development Bank of India Limited to IDBl Bank Limited".

We advise that the name of "lndustrial Development Bank of India Limited" has been changed to

"IDBl Bank Limited"in the Second Schedule to theReserve Bank of IndiaAct,1934 with effect

from May 7，2008 by notification DBOD.BP.BC.No.21.01.002/2007-08 dated May 16,2008,

published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill-Section 4) dated June 14, 2008.

Yours faithfully,

(A. K. Singh)
