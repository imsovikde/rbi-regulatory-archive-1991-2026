Dear Sir,

## <u>Interest Rate on FCNR(B) Deposits</u>

Please refer to our circular RPCD.CO.RF.BC.No.50/07.38.01/2008-09 dated October 15, 2008 on Interest Rate on FCNR(B) Deposits. In view of prevailing market conditions, it has been decided that until further notice and with effect from close of business in India as on November 15, 2008, the interest rates on FCNR(B) Deposits will be as under:

In respect of FCNR(B) deposits of all maturities contracted effective from the close of business in India as on November 15, 2008, interest shall be paid within the ceiling rate of LIBOR / SWAP rates plus 100 basis points for the respective currency / corresponding maturities (as against LIBOR / SWAP rates plus 25 basis points effective from close of business on October 15, 2008). On floating rate deposits, interest shall be paid within

## RPCD.CO.RF.BC. Dir.No.65/07.38.01/2008-09

## November 17, 2008

## <u>Foreign Currency (Non-Resident) Accounts (Banks) Scheme</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 35 A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As Applicable to Co-operative Societies), the Reserve Bank of India, being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby directs the following:

## <u>Interest rates applicable to deposits accepted under Foreign Currency Non-Resident Accounts (Banks) Scheme</u>

In respect of FCNR(B) deposits of all maturities contracted effective from the close of business in India as on November 15, 2008, interest shall be paid within the ceiling rate of LIBOR / SWAP rates plus 100 basis points for the respective currency / corresponding maturities (as against LIBOR / SWAP rates plus 25 basis points effective from close of business on October 15, 2008). On floating rate deposits, interest shall be paid within the ceiling of SWAP rates for the respective currency / maturity plus 100 basis points. For floating rate deposits, the interest reset period shall be six months.

# Withdrawn
