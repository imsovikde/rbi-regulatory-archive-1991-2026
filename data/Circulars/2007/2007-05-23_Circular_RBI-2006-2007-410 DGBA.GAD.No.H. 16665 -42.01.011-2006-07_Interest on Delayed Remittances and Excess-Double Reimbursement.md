# Interest on Delayed Remittances and Excess/Double Reimbursement

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2006-2007/410

DGBA.GAD.No.H. 16665 /42.01.011/2006-07 May 23, 2007

The Chairman/Managing Director State Bank of India & its Associates All Nationalised Banks Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd. IDBI Ltd/HDFC Bank Ltd. ICICI Bank Ltd. /UTI Bank Ltd.

## Dear Sir

## Maintenance of Government Accounts -Interest on Delayed Remittances and Excess/Double Reimbursement

Please refer to our Circular No.RBI/2006/169 (DGBA.GAD.No.H.7377/42.01.011/2006-07) dated

November 7, 2006 on the above subject.

We advise that the rate of interest on delayed remittances and double/excess reimbursement would

remain unchanged at 8% (i.e. Bank Rate - 6% + 2%) till further instructions.

Yours faithfully

# Withdrawn

(A.S. Kulkarni) Deputy General Manager
