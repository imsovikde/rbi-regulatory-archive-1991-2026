# Loans and advances to directors, relatives and firms/ concerns in which they are interested-UCBs

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2006-2007/285

UBD.PCB.Cir.No. 32/13.05.000/06-07 March 12, 2007

The Chief Executive Officers of All Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks

Dear Sir / Madam,

## Loans and advances to directors, relatives and firms/ concerns in which they are interested-UCBs

Please refer to the instructions on the captioned subject contained in our circular UBD.PCB.Cir.50/13.05.00/2002-03 dated April 29, 2003 and the subsequent relaxations advised vide circular no. UBD.PCB.Cir.No.14 /13.05.000/05-06 dated October 6, 2005.

2. As a further measure of relaxation, it has been decided to permit directors of UCBs and their relatives to avail loans against Fixed Deposits and Life Insurance Policies standing in their own name.
# Withdrawn Yours faithfully,

(Sathyan David) General Manager
