# NOTIFICATION on CRR

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RPCD.CO.RRB.No.7523/03.05.28(B)/2006-07 February 14, 2007

## <u>NOTIFICATION</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred under sub-section (1) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and in supersession of its notification RPCD.RF.No.5541/07.02.01/2006- 2007 dated December 11, 2006, the Reserve Bank of India hereby specifies that the average Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) required to be maintained by Regional Rural Banks shall, from effective dates mentioned below, be as indicated thereagainst:

Effective date CRR on net demand (i.e., the fortnight beginning from) and time liabilities (per cent) February 17, 2007 5.75 <u>March 3, 2007 6.00</u>

## (V.S.Das)

## Executive Director
