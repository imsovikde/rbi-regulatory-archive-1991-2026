# PMRY - Cut-Off date for disbursement for cases relating to milch animals extended

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2007-2008/150 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 26 /09.04.01/ 2007 –2008

## October 01, 2007

The Chairman / Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

**PMRY-Cut-Off date for completion of disbursement for cases** <u>relating to milch animals under the Programme Year 2006 -2007</u>

Please refer to our circular RBI / 2006-07/ 316 / RPCD. PLNFS .BC .No. 68 / 09.04.01/ 2006 – 2007 dated April 10, 2007. In partial modification of the circular, the Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises, Government of India has since decided to extend the cut-off date for completion of disbursements of those cases of purchase of milch animals under PMRY for the Programme Year 2006-2007 from **30.06.2007** to **30.11.2007**.

2. You may issue necessary instructions to your Regional / Controlling Offices/ Branches in this regard.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(R.Sebastian) General Manager
