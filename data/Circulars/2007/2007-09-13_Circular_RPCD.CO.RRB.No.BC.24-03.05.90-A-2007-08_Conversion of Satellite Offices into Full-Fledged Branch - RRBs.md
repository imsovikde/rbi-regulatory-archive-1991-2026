# Conversion of Satellite Offices into Full-Fledged Branch - RRBs

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2007-2008/136 RPCD.CO. RRB. No. BC.24 /03.05.90-A /2007-08 September 13, 2007

## All Regional Rural Banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Conversion of Satellite Offices into full-fledged branch-RRBs</u>

Please refer to paragraph 4 of our circular RPCD.CO.RRB.No BL.BC.09 /03.05.90-A/2007-08 dated July 2, 2007 permitting RRBs to convert their existing loss making branches into satellite offices. With a view to providing better customer service in rural areas, it has been decided to allow RRBs to convert their satellite offices into full-fledged branches after obtaining concurrence from the Empowered Committee on RRBs. RRBs should also obtain necessary licence from the concerned Regional Office of RBI (RPCD).

Yours faithfully,

# Withdrawn
