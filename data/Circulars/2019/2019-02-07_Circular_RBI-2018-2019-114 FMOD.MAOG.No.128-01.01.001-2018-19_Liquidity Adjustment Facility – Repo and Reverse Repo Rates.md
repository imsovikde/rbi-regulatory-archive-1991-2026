# Liquidity Adjustment Facility – Repo and Reverse Repo Rates

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2018-2019/114 FMOD.MAOG.No.128/01.01.001/2018-19 February 7, 2019

## All Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) participants

Madam/Sir,

## <u>Liquidity Adjustment Facility – Repo and Reverse Repo Rates</u>

As announced in the <u>Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19</u>, today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the policy Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 6.5 per cent to **6.25** **per cent** with immediate effect**.**

2. Consequently, the Reverse Repo rate under the LAF stands adjusted to **6.0 per cent** with immediate effect.
3. All other terms and conditions of the extant LAF Scheme will remain unchanged.
