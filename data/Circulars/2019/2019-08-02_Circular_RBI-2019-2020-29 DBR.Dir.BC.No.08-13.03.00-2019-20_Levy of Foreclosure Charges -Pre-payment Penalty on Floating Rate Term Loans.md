# Levy of Foreclosure Charges /Pre-payment Penalty on Floating Rate Term Loans

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2019-20/29 DBR.Dir.BC.No.08/13.03.00/2019-20 August 02, 2019

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs) All Small Finance Banks All Local Area Banks

## Dear Sir / Madam

## <u>Levy of Foreclosure Charges /Pre-payment Penalty on Floating Rate Term Loans</u>

Please refer to our <u>circulars DBOD.No.Dir.BC.107/13.03.00/2011-12 dated June 5,</u> 2012 and <u>DBOD.Dir.BC.No.110/13.03.00/2013-14 dated May 7, 2014</u>, in terms of which banks are not permitted to charge foreclosure charges / pre-payment penalties on home loans / all floating rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers.

2. In this connection, it is clarified that banks shall not charge foreclosure charges/ pre- payment penalties on any floating rate term loan sanctioned, for purposes other than business, to individual borrowers with or without co-obligant(s). Yours faithfully, (Dr. S. K. Kar) Chief General Manager
