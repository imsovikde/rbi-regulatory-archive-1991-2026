# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2019-2020/76 FMOD.MAOG.No.137/01.18.001/2019-20 October 4, 2019

## All Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) participants

Madam/Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u>

As announced in the <u>Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2019-20</u>, today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the policy Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 5.40 per cent to **5.15** **per cent** with immediate effect.

2. Consequently, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate stands adjusted to **5.40 per** **cent** with immediate effect.
3. All other terms and conditions of the extant MSF scheme will remain unchanged.
