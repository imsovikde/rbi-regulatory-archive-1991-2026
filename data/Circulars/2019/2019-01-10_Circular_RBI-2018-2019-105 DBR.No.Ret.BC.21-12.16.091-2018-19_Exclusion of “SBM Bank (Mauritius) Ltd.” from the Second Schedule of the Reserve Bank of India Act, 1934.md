# Exclusion of “SBM Bank (Mauritius) Ltd.” from the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2018-19/105 DBR.No.Ret.BC.21/12.16.091/2018-19 January 10, 2019

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

<u>Exclusion of “SBM Bank (Mauritius) Ltd.” from the Second Schedule of the</u> <u>Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “SBM Bank (Mauritius) Ltd.” has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.4611/23.13.043/2018-19 dated December 03, 2018 and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated December 29, 2018 – January 04, 2019.
