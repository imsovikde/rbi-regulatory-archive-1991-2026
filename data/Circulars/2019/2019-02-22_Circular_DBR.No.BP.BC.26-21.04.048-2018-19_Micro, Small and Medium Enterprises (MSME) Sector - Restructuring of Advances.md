# Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Sector - Restructuring of Advances

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2018-19/127 DBR.No.BP.BC.26/21.04.048/2018-19 February 22, 2019

All banks and NBFCs regulated by the Reserve Bank of India

Dear Sir / Madam,

## <u>Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) sector-Restructuring of Advances</u>

Please refer to <u>circular DBR.No.BP.BC.18/21.04.048/2018-19 dated January 1, 2019</u> on the above subject. One of the conditions for restructuring of existing loan of MSMEs without a downgrade in the asset classification is as under: “the borrowing entity is GST-registered on the date of implementation of the restructuring. However, this condition will not apply to MSMEs that are exempt from GST-registration” (c.f. para 1 (iii) of the above referred circular).

2. It is clarified that the eligibility for restructuring without GST-registration, as per the

circular under reference, should be determined on the basis of exemption limit obtaining as on the date of the aforesaid circular, i.e., January 1, 2019.
