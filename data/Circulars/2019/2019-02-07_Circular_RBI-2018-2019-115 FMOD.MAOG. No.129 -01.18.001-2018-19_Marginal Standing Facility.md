# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2018-2019/115 FMOD.MAOG. No.129 /01.18.001/2018-19 February 7, 2019

## All Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) participants

Madam/Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u>

As announced in the <u>Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19</u>, today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the policy Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 6.50 per cent to **6.25** **per cent** with immediate effect.

2. Consequently, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate stands adjusted to **6.50 per** **cent** with immediate effect.
3. All other terms and conditions of the extant MSF scheme will remain unchanged.
