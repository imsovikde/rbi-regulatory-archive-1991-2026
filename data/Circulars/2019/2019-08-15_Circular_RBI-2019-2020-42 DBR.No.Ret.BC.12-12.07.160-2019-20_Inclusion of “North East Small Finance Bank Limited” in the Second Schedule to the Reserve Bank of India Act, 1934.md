## Dear Sir

## <u>Inclusion of “North East Small Finance Bank Limited” in the Second</u>

## <u>Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the **“North East Small Finance Bank Limited”** has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.NBD.No.31/16.02.010/2019-20 dated July 1, 2019, and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated August 10-August 16, 2019.
