# Withdrawal of exemptions granted to Housing Finance Institutions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2019-20/98

**DOR NBFC (PD) CC.No.105/03.10.136/2019-20** November 11, 2019

To

## All Housing Finance Companies

Madam/Sir,

## Withdrawal of exemptions granted to Housing Finance Institutions

Please refer to Para 1 of our <u>Master Direction – Exemptions from the provisions of RBI</u> <u>Act, 1934</u>.

2. Housing Finance Institutions as defined under Clause (d) of Section 2 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987 are currently exempt from the provisions of Chapter IIIB of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. On a review, it has been decided to withdraw these exemptions and make the provisions of Chapter IIIB except Section 45-IA of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, applicable to them.
# Withdrawn

3. <u>Master Direction – Exemptions from the provisions of RBI Act, 1934</u> has been updated accordingly.
4. Necessary notification withdrawing the exemption under Section 45 NC of the RBI Act, 1934 shall be issued separately. Yours faithfully, (Manoranjan Mishra) Chief General Manager
