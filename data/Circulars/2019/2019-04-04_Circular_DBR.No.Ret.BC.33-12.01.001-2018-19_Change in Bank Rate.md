# Change in Bank Rate

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2018-19/162 DBR.No.Ret.BC.33/12.01.001/2018-19 April 04, 2019

The Chairperson / CEOs of all Scheduled and Non Scheduled Banks

Dear Sir / Madam, <u>Change in Bank Rate</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.Ret.BC.23/12.01.001/2018-19 dated February</u> <u>07, 2019</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As announced in the <u>First Bi-Monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2019-20 of</u> <u>April 04, 2019</u>, the Bank Rate is revised downwards by 25 basis points from 6.50 per cent to 6.25 per cent with immediate effect.
3. All penal interest rates on shortfall in reserve requirements, which are specifically linked to the Bank Rate, also stand revised as indicated in the <u>Annex</u>**.**
## Yours faithfullyWithdrawn

(Dr.S.K.Kar) Chief General Manager **Encl. : As above**
