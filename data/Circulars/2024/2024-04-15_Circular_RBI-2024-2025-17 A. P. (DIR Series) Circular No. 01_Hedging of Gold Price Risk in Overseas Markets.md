## Hedging of Gold Price Risk in Overseas Markets

Please refer to Paragraph 2 of the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory</u> <u>Policies</u> announced as a part of the <u>Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2023-</u> <u>24 dated February 08, 2024</u>, regarding hedging of price risk of gold in overseas markets. Attention is also invited to the <u>Master Direction – Foreign Exchange</u> <u>Management (Hedging of Commodity Price Risk and Freight Risk in Overseas</u> <u>Markets) Directions, 2022</u>.

2. P. (DIR Series)</u> <u>Circular No. 19 dated December 12, 2022</u>.
