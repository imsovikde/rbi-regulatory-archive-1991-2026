## Formation of new districts in the State of Arunachal Pradesh -

## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Arunachal Pradesh vide Gazette Notification dated March 3,

2014 had notified the creation of four new districts in the State of Arunachal Pradesh.

It has been decided to assign the lead bank responsibility of the new districts to State

Bank of India as detailed below:-
