# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2017-2018/31 FMOD.MAOG. No. 122/01.18.001/2017-18

## August 2, 2017

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Madam / Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u>

As announced in the <u>Third Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18</u>, today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 6.25 per cent to **6.0 per cent** with immediate effect.

Consequent to the change in the Repo rate, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate stands adjusted to **6.25 per cent** with immediate effect.

All other terms and conditions of the extant MSF scheme will remain unchanged.
