## Revision in Bank Rate

Please refer to our <u>circular UBD.BPD.(PCB).Cir. No. 33 /16.11.00/2013-14 dated</u> <u>October 29, 2013</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As announced in the <u>Third Quarter Review of Monetary Policy Statement for</u> <u>2013-14 dated January 28, 2014</u>, the Bank Rate stands adjusted by 25 basis points from 8.75 per cent to 9.0 per cent with effect from January 28, 2014.
3. All penal interest rates on shortfall in reserve requirements, which are specifically linked to the Bank Rate, also stand revised as indicated in the Annex. The interest rate on refinance for SSI under Section 17(2)(bb) read with Section
