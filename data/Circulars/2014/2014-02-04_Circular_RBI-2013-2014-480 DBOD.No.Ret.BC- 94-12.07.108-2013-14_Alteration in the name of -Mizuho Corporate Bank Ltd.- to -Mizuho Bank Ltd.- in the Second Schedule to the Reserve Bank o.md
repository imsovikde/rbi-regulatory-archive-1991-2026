Alteration in the name of "Mizuho Corporate Bank Ltd."

to "Mizuho Bank Ltd."in the Second Schedule to

the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

"Mizuho Bank Ltd." in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,

1934 vide NotificationDBOD.lBD.No.12056/23.13.078/2013-14 datedDecember

27, 2013, published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill-Section 4) dated January 25,

2014.
