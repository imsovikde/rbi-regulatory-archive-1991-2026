**Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms /Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards /** **Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) /Obligation of banks under Prevention of** **Money Laundering Act (PMLA), 2002 Obligation of banks-Amendment to Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules 2013**

The Government of India had notified the "Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Amendment Rules, 2013" (Rules) and published the same in the extraordinary official gazette vide G.S.R. No.576 (E) dated August 27, 2013. In order to align the existing instructions contained in our Master Circular UBD.BPD. (PCB).MC.No.16/12.05.001/2014- <u>15 dated July 1, 2014</u> on Know Your Customer (KYC) norms / Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards / Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) / Obligation of banks under PMLA, 2002 with the amendment to PML Rules, it has been decided to notify the corresponding changes on account of the amendments.

2. The important changes are mentioned in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. A <u>Copy of the Notification</u> is attached for detailed reference.
4. Banks may revise their KYC policy in the light of the above instructions and ensure strict adherence to the same.
## Yours faithfully

(Scenta Joy) General Manager
