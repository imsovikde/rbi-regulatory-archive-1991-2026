**A .P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 145** June 18, 2014 To All Category-I Authorised Dealer Banks

Madam / Sir, **Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets Reporting by Indian Companies – Revised format**

Attention of the Authorised Dealer Category – I banks is invited to <u>A.P. (DIR Series)</u> <u>Circular No.133 dated June 20, 2012</u> which stipulated that all Indian companies which have received FDI and/or made FDI abroad in the previous year(s) including the current year, should file the annual return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) in the soft form to the Reserve Bank by July 15 every year.

2. In order to collect information on Indian companies’ Outward Foreign Affiliated Trade Statistics (FATS) as per the multi-agency global ‘Manual on Statistics of International Trade in Services’, the <u>FLA return</u> has been modified marginally and is made available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> à Forms category à FEMA Forms) along with the related <u>FAQs</u> (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> → FAQs category → Foreign

No. 400(E) dated June 12, 2014.
