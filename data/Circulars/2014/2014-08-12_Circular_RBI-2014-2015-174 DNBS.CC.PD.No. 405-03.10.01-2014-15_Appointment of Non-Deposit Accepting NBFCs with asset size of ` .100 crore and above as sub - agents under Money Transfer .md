Dear Sirs,

Appointment of Non-Deposit Accepting NBFCs with asset size of .1o0 crore and above

as sub- agents under Money Transfer Service Schemes (MTsS)

In order to broaden the network of sub-agents under the Money Transfer Service Schemes

(MTSS), it has been decided to permit Non-Deposit Accepting NBFCs with asset size of .100

crore and above to act as sub-agents under MTSS subject to the following conditions:

a)  There is no co-mingling of the Indian agent's funds with that of the NBFC's funds.

b)  The Indian agent should maintain with a designated bank, a security deposit in favour of

the NBFC sub-agent. The amount of the security deposit to be maintained may be

mutually decided between the Agent and the sub-agent. It should be ensured that the
