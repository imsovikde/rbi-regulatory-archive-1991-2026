All Domestic Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

## Dear Sir/Madam

## Branch Authorisation Policy-Information to be submitted by

## <u>Banks along with Annual Branch Expansion Plan (ABEP)</u>

Please refer to our <u>circulars DBOD.No.BAPD.BC.54/22.01.001/2013-14 dated</u> <u>September 19, 2013</u>, and <u>DBOD.No.BAPD.BC.60/22.01.001/2013-14 dated October</u> <u>21, 2013</u> regarding relaxations in Branch Authorisation Policy. ## Yours faithfully, Withdrawn
