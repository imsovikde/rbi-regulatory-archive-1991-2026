## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) -Standards

Please refer to our letter <u>DBOD. AML.No.1930 /14.01.036/2009-10 dated August 2,</u> <u>2010</u> on risks arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of Iran, Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK), and Sao Tome and Principe**.**

2. Financial Action Task Force (FATF) has issued a further Statement on October 22, 2010 on the subject (<u>copy enclosed</u>). It may be observed that the statement divides the strategic AML/CFT deficient jurisdictions into two groups as under:
# Withdrawn

a) Jurisdictions subject to FATF call on its members and other jurisdictions to apply countermeasures to protect the international financial system from the ongoing and substantial money laundering and terrorist financing (ML/FT) risks emanating from the jurisdiction : Iran
b) Jurisdictions with strategic AML/CFT deficiencies that have not committed to an action plan developed with the FATF to address key deficiencies as of October
2010. The FATF calls on its members to consider the risks arising from the deficiencies associated with each jurisdiction: Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).
