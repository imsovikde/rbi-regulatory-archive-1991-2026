Dear Sir

Scheme of 1% interest subvention on housing loans upto Rs. 10 lakh - Guidelines

Please refer to our circulars RPCD.SME & NFS.BC.No.16/06.11.01/2010-11 dated August 9.

2010 and RPCD.SME & NFS.BC.No.52/06.11.01/2010-11 dated February 8, 2011 on the

captioned subject.

2. In para 43 of the Union Budget Speech of 2011-12, it has been proposed to liberalise the

existing scheme of interest subvention of 1 per cent on housing loans by extending it to

housing loan upto Rs.15 lakh where the cost of the house does not exceed Rs.25 lakh from

the present limit of Rs.10 lakh and Rs.20 lakh respectively.

3. You are therefore advised to issue necessary instructions to your Controlling Offices and

Branch Offices to ensure that these guidelines are implemented immediately. The other terms

and conditions of the housing subvention scheme remain unchanged.

4.Please acknowledge the receipt.
