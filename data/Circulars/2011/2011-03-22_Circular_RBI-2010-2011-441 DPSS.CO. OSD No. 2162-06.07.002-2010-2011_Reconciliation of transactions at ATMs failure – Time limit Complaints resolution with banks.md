www.rbi.org.in

RBI/2010-11/441 DPSS.CO. OSD No.**2162**/06.07.002/2010-2011 March 22, 2011

To,

### All authorized ATM Network Operators

Dear Sir,

### Reconciliation of transactions at ATMs failure – Time limit

### <u>Complaints resolution with banks</u>

All authorized ATM Networks provider/operator are advised to submit the quarterly data pertaining to resolution of complaints between ATM network operator and banks in the following <u>excel format</u>. The quarterly data should reach us within a month on completion of2023 each quarter through <u>email</u>. It may also be ensured that the format and timelimit prescribed25, is strictly adhered to.
