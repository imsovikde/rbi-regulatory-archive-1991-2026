Dear Sir,

## Investment in SLR Securities by Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

Please refer to our <u>circular RPCD.RRB.BC.No.68/03.05.34/2008-09 dated April 9, 2010</u> on the captioned subject.

2. On a review of the issue it has been decided that the exemption granted to RRBs up to financial year 2009-10 from ‘mark to market’ norms in respect of their investment in SLR securities be extended by for three years i.e. for the financial years 2010-11, 2011- 12 and 2012-13. Accordingly, RRBs will have the freedom to classify their entire
# Withdrawn

investment portfolio of SLR securities under ‘Held to Maturity’ for the financial years 2010-11, 2011-12 and 2012-13 with valuation on book value basis and amortization of premium, if any, over the remaining life of securities.

3. Please acknowledge receipt to the respective Regional Office.
## Yours faithfully

(C.D.Srinivasan) Chief General Manager
