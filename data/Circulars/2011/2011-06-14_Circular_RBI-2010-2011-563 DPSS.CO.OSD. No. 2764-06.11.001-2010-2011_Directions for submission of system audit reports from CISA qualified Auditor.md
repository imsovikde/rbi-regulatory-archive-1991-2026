# Directions for submission of system audit reports from CISA qualified Auditor

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2010-11/563 DPSS.CO.OSD. No. 2764 /06.11.001/ 2010-2011 June 14, 2011

To

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Directions for submission of system audit reports from CISA qualified Auditor</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular Ref No. DPSS.1444/ 06.11.001/ 2010-2011 dated</u> <u>December 27, 2010</u> on the captioned subject.

It is clarified that the contents of the above circular are applicable to only those entities which operate a payment system under the Payment and Settlement

# Repealed

Systems Act, 2007. Accordingly, the requirement of system audits is not applicable where a bank/entity is a participant in various payment systems such as RTGS, NEFT, CFMS, ECS, NECS, Card payment systems (Visa, MasterCard, etc.), ATM networks (illustratively like NFS, BANCS) etc.

## Please acknowledge receipt.

## Yours faithfully

G. Srinivas (General Manager)
