6. All NEFT enabled bank branches in India would participate in this cross border remittance initiative. The Nepal remittances would be processed in different NEFT batches in NCC, Mumbai and would be channellised to Nepal using the SBI payment gateway specially created for Nepal remittances. Accounting and format conversion would take place in the SBI payment gateway.
7. Remittances will be distributed to the beneficiaries in Nepal through the branches of Nepal State Bank Ltd. and their approved Agents.
8. Since the target group is poor workers from Nepal, a concessional charge structure has been devised. The structure is as follows:
a) For remittances from a bank account in India to a bank account with any branch of Nepal State Bank Ltd., there shall be no charge.
b) For cash remittances/disbursements up to INR 5,000.00, there shall be a flat charge of INR 50.00.
c) For cash remittances/disbursements from INR 5,001.00 to INR. 50,000.00, there shall be a flat charge of INR 75.00.
