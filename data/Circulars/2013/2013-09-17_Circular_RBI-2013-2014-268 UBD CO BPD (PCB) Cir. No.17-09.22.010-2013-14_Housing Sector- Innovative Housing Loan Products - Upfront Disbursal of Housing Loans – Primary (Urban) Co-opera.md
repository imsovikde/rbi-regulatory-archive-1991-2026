3. In view of the higher risks associated with such lump-sum disbursal of sanctioned housing loans and customer suitability issues, UCBs are advised that disbursal of housing loans sanctioned to individuals should be closely linked to the stages of construction of the housing project / houses and upfront disbursal should not be made in cases of incomplete / under-construction / green field housing projects.
4. It is emphasized that UCBs while introducing any kind of product should take into account the customer suitability and appropriateness issues and also ensure that borrowers / customers are made fully aware of the risks and liabilities under such products.
