## Unsecured Exposure Norms for UCBs

Please refer to our circular No. <u>UBD.BPD.(PCB) Cir No.21/13.05.000/2010-11 dated</u> <u>November 15, 2010</u> on ‘Maximum Limit on Unsecured Loans and Advances’ prescribing that the total unsecured loans and advances granted by a UCB to its members should not exceed 10 per cent of its total assets.
