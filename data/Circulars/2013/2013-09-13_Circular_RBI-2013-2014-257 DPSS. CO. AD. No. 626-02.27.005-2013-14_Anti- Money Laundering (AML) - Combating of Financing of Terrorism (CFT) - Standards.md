भारतीय ररज़र्व बैंक RESERVE BANK OF INDIA <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

RBI/2013-14/257

DPSS. CO. AD. No. 626/02.27.005/2013-14 September 13, 2013

Chief Executive Officers of All the Payment System Operators Authorised under the PSS Act, 2007

## Dear Sir

## Anti- Money Laundering (AML) / Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) -Standards

Please refer to our <u>circular DPSS.CO.AD.No.2051 /02.27.005/2012-13 dated May 8, 2013</u> on risk arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of certain jurisdictions.

2. Financial Action Task Force (FATF) has updated its Statement on the subject and document ‘Improving Global AML/CFT Compliance: On-Going Process’ on June 21, 2013 (<u>copy</u> <u>enclosed</u>). The statement / document can be accessed from the following URLs also: <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/key/public-statement-june-2013.html](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/key/public-statement-june-2013.html)</u> and <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-cooperativejurisdictions/documents/compliance-](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-cooperativejurisdictions/documents/compliance-)</u> <u>june-2013.html</u>
3. All the Payment System Operators authorised under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 are accordingly advised to consider the information contained in the enclosed Statement. This, however, does not preclude Payment System Operators from legitimate trade and business transactions with these countries and jurisdictions.Withdrawn w.e.f November 16, 2021
4. Nodal Officer/Principal Officer may please acknowledge receipt of this circular.
## Yours faithfully

(K C Anand) General Manager
