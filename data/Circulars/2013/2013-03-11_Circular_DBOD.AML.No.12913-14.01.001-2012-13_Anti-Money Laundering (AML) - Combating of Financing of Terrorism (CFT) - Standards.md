## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) -Standards

Please refer to our letter <u>DBOD. AML.No.8422/14.01.001/ 2012-13 dated December</u> 12, 2012 on risks arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of certain jurisdictions.

2. Financial Action Task Force (FATF) has updated its Statement on the subject and document ‘Improving Global AML/CFT Compliance: On-Going Process’ on February 22, 2013 (<u>copy enclosed</u>). The statement / document can be accessed from the following URLs also: <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/documents/documents/fatfpublicstatement22february2013.html](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/documents/documents/fatfpublicstatement22february2013.html)</u> and <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-)</u> <u>cooperativejurisdictions/documents/improvingglobalamlcftcomplianceon-goingprocess-</u> <u>22february2013.html</u>
# Withdrawn
