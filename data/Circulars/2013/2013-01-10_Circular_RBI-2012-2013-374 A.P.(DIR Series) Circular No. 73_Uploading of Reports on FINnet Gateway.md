Madam/ Dear Sir,

## Uploading of Reports on FINnet Gateway

Please refer to our <u>A.P.(DIR Series) Circular No. 43 dated October 12, 2012</u>, advising all Authorised Persons, who are Indian Agents under Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS) to initiate submission of reports on the FINnet Gateway in 'Test Mode' from August 31, 2012.

2. FIU-IND have now advised that the 'go-live' date is October 20, 2012 and that Authorised Persons, who are Indian agents under MTSS may discontinue submission of reports in CD format after October 20, 2012, using only FINnet gateway for uploading of reports in the new XML reporting format.
