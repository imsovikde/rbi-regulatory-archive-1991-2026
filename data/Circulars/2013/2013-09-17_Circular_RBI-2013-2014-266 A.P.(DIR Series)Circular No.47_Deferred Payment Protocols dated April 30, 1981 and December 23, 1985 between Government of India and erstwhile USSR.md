# Deferred Payment Protocols dated April 30, 1981 and December 23, 1985 between Government of India and erstwhile USSR

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## Foreign Exchange Department

## Central Office

## <u>Mumbai - 400 001</u>

## RBI/2013-14/266 A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 47 September 17, 2013

To

All Category-I Authorised Dealer Banks

Madam / Sir,

**Deferred Payment Protocols dated April 30, 1981 and December 23, 1985 between Government of India and erstwhile USSR**

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to

<u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No.33 dated September 04, 2013</u>, wherein the Rupee value of the Special Currency Basket was indicated as Rs.86.857663 effective from August 23, 2013.
2. AD Category-I banks are advised that a further revision has taken place on August 28, 2013 and accordingly, the Rupee value of the Special Currency Basket has been fixed at Rs.92.985396 with effect from September 02, 2013.
# Withdrawn

3. AD Category-I banks may bring the contents of this circular to the notice of their constituents concerned.
4. The Directions contained in this circular have been issued under sections 10(4) and 11(1) of the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 (42 of
1999) and are without prejudice to permissions / approvals, if any, required under any other law.
Yours faithfully,

## (C.D.Srinivasan) Chief General Manager
