## D. <u>Fees for additional certifications</u>

It has been decided that an additional remuneration @ 12% of the basic audit fees shall be payable for the following certifications/validations required to be made in terms of various circulars/guidelines issued by RBI and <u>any other</u> <u>certification/validation included from time to time as per RBI requirements</u>.

i) Verification of SLR requirements under Section 24 of BR Act, 1949 on 12 odd dates in different months in a year, not being Fridays. ii) A certificate to the effect that the bank has been following RBI guidelines regarding (a) asset classification, (b) income recognition
(c) provisioning, and also to the effect that the bank has followed RBI guidelines in regard to the investment transactions/treasury operations.
iii) A certificate in respect of reconciliation of bank’s investments (on own account as also under PMS). iv) A certificate for compliance in key areas by the banks.

v) A certificate in respect of custody of unused BR forms.

As regards fee for additional certificates / attestations prescribed by SEBI and other regulators, the banks may decide in consultation with the Audit Committee of the Board/ Board.

## F. <u>Fees for auditing of consolidated financial statements</u>

For this purpose banks may pay a maximum of Rs.20,625/- only per subsidiary / associate whose accounts are to be consolidated in the balance- sheet of a bank. The banks have freedom to offer lesser fee if the subsidiary / associate concerned is not active or is dormant.

## G. <u>Fee for quarterly / half yearly limited review</u>

The fee for carrying out quarterly / half yearly limited review to be paid to statutory central auditors may continue to be 20% of the basic audit fee. It is further clarified that revised basic audit fee payable from 2012-13 will be applicable for computing the fee for limited review from the quarters ending June 30, 2013 onwards and <u>not for the review carried out during the quarters</u> <u>ended June 30 / September 30 / December 31, 2012</u>.
