# Financing of Infrastructure - Definition of 'Infrastructure Lending'

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2013-14/172 DNBS.PD.CC.No. 354/03.10.001/2013-14 August 02, 2013

## All NBFCs

Dear Sir / Madam,

## <u>Financing of Infrastructure-Definition of 'Infrastructure Lending'</u>

Government of India, vide its Gazette Notifications dated April 5, 2013 and May 9, 2013, has updated the Harmonised Master List of Infrastructure sub-sectors and added the following new sub-sectors to the Notification dated March 27, 2012:

i. Capital Dredging, under the sub-sector ‘Ports’. ii. Slurry Pipelines iii. Telecommunication & Telecom Services
2. Accordingly, the extant definition of infrastructure loan given in the NBFC Prudential Norms Directions, 2007 stands amended with immediate effect. The revised definition of 'infrastructure loan' is given in the Annex to this circular. Also please find enclosed the amending notification of date for meticulous compliance. Yours faithfully, (C. R. Samyuktha) Chief General Manager

# <u>Annex</u>

# <u>List of sub-sectors for ‘Infrastructure Lending’</u>

"Infrastructure loan" means a credit facility extended by NBFCs to a borrower for exposure in the following infrastructure sub-sectors: **Sr.** **Category Infrastructure sub-sectors** <u>No.</u>

1. Transport i Roads and bridges
1 ii Ports iii Inland Waterways iv Airport v Railway Track, tunnels, viaducts, bridges² vi Urban Public Transport (except rolling stock in case of urban road transport)

2. Energy i Electricity Generation
ii Electricity Transmission iii Electricity Distribution iv Oil pipelines v Oil / Gas / Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) storage facility³ vi Gas pipelines⁴

3. Water & Sanitation i Solid Waste Management
ii Water supply pipelines iii Water treatment plants iv Sewage collection, treatment and disposal system v Irrigation (dams, channels, embankments etc) vi Storm Water Drainage System
