Dear Sir,

Amendments to Banking Regulation Act 1949 -- Banking Laws

(Amendment) Act 2012 - Applicability to private sector banks

As you are aware, the Government of India, Ministry of Finance, notified the

Banking Law Amendments Act, 2012, vide Gazette Notification dated January

17, 2013, (published in Part Il- Section 3--sub-section (ii) of the Gazette of India

No.177 dated January 18, 2013), making certain amendments to the Banking

Regulation Act, 1949.

2. In the context of the above, we advise

the amendments are binding on

banks  notwithstanding  any

the

contrary contained in the

clause

Memorandum of Association

1 Articles of Association(AoA)of the

banks. Banks are therefore,

to make necessary amendments in the MoA

Vithdraw

and AoA, under advice to this Department.
