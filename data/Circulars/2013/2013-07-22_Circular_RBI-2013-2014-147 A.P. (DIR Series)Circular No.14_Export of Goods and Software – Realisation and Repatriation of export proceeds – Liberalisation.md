## Export of Goods and Software – Realisation and <u>Repatriation of export proceeds – Liberalisation</u>

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to

<u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 52 dated November 20, 2012</u> extending the enhanced period for realization and repatriation to India, of the amount representing the full value of goods or software exported, from six months to twelve months from the date of export up to March 31, 2013. Further, in terms of <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 105 dated May 20, 2013</u> it was decided, in consultation with the Government of India to bring down the above stated realization period from twelve months to nine months from the date of export valid till September 30, 2013.
