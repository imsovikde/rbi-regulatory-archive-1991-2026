## RBI / 2012-13/497

## DPSS. CO. AD. No. 2051/02.27.005/2012-13 May 8, 2013

Chief Executive Officers of All the Payment System Operators Authorised under the PSS Act, 2007

## Dear Sir

## Anti- Money Laundering (AML) / Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) -Standards

Please refer to our <u>circular DPSS.CO.AD.No.1120 /02.27.005/2012-13 dated January 8,</u> <u>2013</u> on risk arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of certain jurisdictions.

2. Financial Action Task Force (FATF) has updated its Statement on the subject and document ‘Improving Global AML/CFT Compliance: on-going processes’ on February 22, 2013 (<u>copy enclosed</u>). The statement / document can be accessed from the following URL also: <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/documents/documents/fatfpublicstatement22february2013.html](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/documents/documents/fatfpublicstatement22february2013.html)</u> and <u>[http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-](http://www.fatf-gafi.org/topics/high-riskandnon-)</u> <u>cooperativejurisdictions/documents/improvingglobalamlcftcomplianceon-goingprocess-</u> <u>22february2013.html</u>
3. All the Payment System Operators authorised under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 are accordingly advised to consider the information contained in the enclosed Statement.
# Withdrawn w.e.f November 16, 2021

4. This, however, does not preclude Payment System Operators from legitimate trade and business transactions with these countries and jurisdictions.
5. Nodal Officer/Principal Officer may please acknowledge receipt of this circular.
## Yours faithfully

**(**K C Anand) Deputy General Manager
