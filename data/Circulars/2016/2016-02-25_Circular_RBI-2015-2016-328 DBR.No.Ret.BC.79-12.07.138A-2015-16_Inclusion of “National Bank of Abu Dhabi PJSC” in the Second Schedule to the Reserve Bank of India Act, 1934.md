## Inclusion of “National Bank of Abu Dhabi PJSC” in the Second

## <u>Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the **“National Bank of Abu Dhabi PJSC”** has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD. No.5196/23.03.030/2015-16 dated October 26, 2015, and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated January 30 – February 05, 2016.
