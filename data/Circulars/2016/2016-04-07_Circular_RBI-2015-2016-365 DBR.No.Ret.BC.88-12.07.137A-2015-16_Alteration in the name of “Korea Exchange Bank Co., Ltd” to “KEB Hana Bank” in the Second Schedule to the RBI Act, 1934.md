# Alteration in the name of “Korea Exchange Bank Co., Ltd” to “KEB Hana Bank” in the Second Schedule to the RBI Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/365 DBR.No.Ret.BC.88/12.07.137A/2015-16 April 7, 2016

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

**Alteration in the name of “Korea Exchange Bank Co., Ltd” to “KEB Hana Bank” in the** **Second Schedule to the RBI Act, 1934**

We advise that the name of **“Korea Exchange Bank Co., Ltd”** has been changed to **“KEB** **Hana Bank”** in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.4793/23.13.065/2015-16 dated October 06, 2015 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III Section 4) dated December 19, 2015.

## Yours faithfully

(M.K.Samantaray) General Manager
