# Withdrawal of Legal Tender Character of existing ₹ 500/- and ₹ 1000/- Bank Notes –Limit for Withdrawal of Cash

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-17/124 DCM (Plg) No.1256/10.27.00/2016-17 November 11, 2016

The Chairman / Managing Director/ Chief Executive Officer, Public Sector Banks/ Private Sector Banks / Foreign Banks/ Regional Rural Banks / Urban Cooperative Banks/ State Cooperative Banks

## Dear Sir

## Withdrawal of Legal Tender Character of existing ₹ 500/- and ₹ 1000/- Bank

## Notes –Limit for Withdrawal of Cash

Please refer to our <u>circular DCM (Plg) No.1251/10.27.00/2016-17 November 10,</u> <u>2016</u> on the captioned subject and see the following additional instructions in this regard:

2. Governments Departments may be allowed to draw cash beyond the stipulated limit of ` 10,000/-, in exceptional cases, only on production of evidence justifying their cash requirements in writing, at the discretion of an officer in the rank of General Manager and above.
# Withdrawn

## Yours faithfully

(P Vijaya Kumar) Chief General Manager
