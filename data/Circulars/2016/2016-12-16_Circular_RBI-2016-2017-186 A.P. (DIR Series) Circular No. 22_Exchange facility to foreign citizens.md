## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA <u>Mumbai - 400 001</u>

## RBI/2016-17/186 A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 22 December 16, 2016

To All Authorised Persons

Madam / Sir, <u>Exchange facility to foreign citizens</u> Attention of Authorized Persons is invited to the <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No.</u> <u>20 dated November 25, 2016</u> permitting foreign citizens to exchange foreign exchange for Indian currency notes up to a limit of ` 5000/- per week till December 15, 2016.

2. On a review it has been decided that the instructions contained in the <u>A.P.</u> <u>(DIR Series) Circular No. 20 dated November 25, 2016</u> shall continue to be in force till December 31, 2016.
3. Authorised Persons may follow the above instructions and bring the
