# Incentive for improving service to non-chest branches under Linkage Scheme

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/293 DCM (NPD) No. 2564/09.40.02 / 2015-16 January 21, 2016

The Chairman & Managing Director Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer All Banks having currency chests

Dear Sir / Madam, <u>Incentive for improving service to non-chest branches under Linkage</u> <u>Scheme</u> Please refer to our <u>circular DCM (NPD) No. 6316 / 09.40.02 / 2006-07 dated</u> <u>March 9, 2007</u> on the captioned subject. It has been decided to allow the currency chest holding banks to enhance the service charges to be levied on cash deposited by non-chest bank branches from the existing rate of ` 2/- per packet of 100 pieces to ` 5/- per packet. These instructions come into effect from February 1, 2016. The non-chest bank branches linked with currency
