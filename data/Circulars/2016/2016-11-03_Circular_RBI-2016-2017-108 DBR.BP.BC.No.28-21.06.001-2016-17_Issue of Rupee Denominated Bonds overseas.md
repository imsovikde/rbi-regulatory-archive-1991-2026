# Issue of Rupee Denominated Bonds overseas

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-17/108 DBR.BP.BC.No.28 /21.06.001/2016-17 November 03, 2016

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding Regional Rural Banks)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Issue of Rupee Denominated Bonds overseas</u>

Please refer to <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No.14 dated November 03, 2016</u> on issue of rupee denominated bonds overseas by Indian banks.

2. We advise that banks are permitted to raise funds through issuance of rupee denominated bonds overseas for the following purposes:
- Perpetual Debt Instruments (PDI) qualifying for inclusion as Additional Tier 1 capital under the extant Basel III Capital Regulations prescribed vide <u>circular Ref. DBR.No.BP.BC.1/21.06.201/2015-16 dated July 1, 2015</u>, as amended from time to time;
- Debt capital instruments qualifying for inclusion as Tier 2 capital under theWithdrawn extant Basel III Capital Regulations prescribed vide <u>circular Ref.</u> <u>DBR.No.BP.BC.1/21.06.201/2015-16 dated July 1, 2015</u> as amended from time to time;
- Financing of infrastructure and affordable housing in terms of instructions contained in <u>circular Ref. DBOD.BP.BC.No.25/08.12.014/2014-15 dated</u> <u>July 15, 2014</u> on Issue of Long Term Bonds by Banks - Financing of Infrastructure and Affordable Housing, as amended from time to time.
