# Financial Literacy Centres - Revised reporting format

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-17/40 FIDD.FLC.BC.No.12/12.01.018/2016-17 August 25, 2016

To Chairman/MD & CEO Scheduled Commercial Banks (Including RRBs)

Dear Sir/Madam,

## Financial Literacy Centres-Revised reporting format

Please refer to our <u>circular FIDD.FLC.BC.No.18/12.01.018/2015-16 dated January 14,</u> <u>2016</u> on Financial Literacy Centres (FLCs)-Revised guidelines enclosing therewith the reporting Mechanism (Annex III- Part A, B, and C) for FLCs and rural branches of the banks. In this connection it has been decided to modify the reporting format as per <u>Annex</u> (Part A, Part B, Part C and Part D). Sample entries have been filled in the format for guidance. SLBC/UTLBCs will submit the enclosed excel sheet on a quarterly basis to the respective Regional offices of RBI within 20 days from the end of the quarter, beginning quarter ended September 2016.

Yours faithfully,

## Sd/-Withdrawn w.e.f May 13, 2022

(A Udgata) Principal Chief General Manager
