## Export of Goods and Services – Project Exports

Attention of Authorised Dealers is invited to Regulation 18 of <u>Notification No.</u> <u>FEMA 23/2000-RB dated 3rd May 2000</u> viz. Foreign Exchange Management (Export of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2000 in terms of which export of goods or services on deferred payment terms or in execution of a turnkey project or a civil construction contract requires prior approval of the approving authority, which shall consider the proposal in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank from time to time. Further, attention of Authorized Dealers (AD) is also invited to <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No.11 dated July 22,</u>
