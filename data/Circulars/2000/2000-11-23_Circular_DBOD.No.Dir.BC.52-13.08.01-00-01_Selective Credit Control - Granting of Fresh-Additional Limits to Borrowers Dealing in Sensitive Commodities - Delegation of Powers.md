13.A.III.11 – Levying of penal rates of interest Penal rate represents additional interest charged over and above the normal interest rates. Since the banks’ Boards have been empowered to decide the Prime Lending Rates (PLR) as also the spread over PLR, the Board may now take decision on penal interest that should be levied for reasons such as default in repayment of loans, irregularities in cash credit accounts, non-submission of financial statements, etc. However, banks should formulate transparent policy for charging penal interest rates and implement it with discrimination and selectivity in order to avoid indiscriminate levy of penal interest. The policy should be governed by well accepted principles of transparency, fairness, incentive to service the debt and due regard to genuine difficulties of customers.
