## Special Financial Package for large value exports-Rupee Credit Interest Rates

DS.SUCB.Cir.15/13.04.00/2001-2002 May 2002 Jyestha 1924(s) The Chairman/Chief Executive Officers of all scheduled urban co-operative banks

## Dear Sir

## <u>Special Financial Package for large value exports-Rupee Credit Interest Rates</u>

Please refer to our circular UBD.No.DS.SUCB.Cir.5/13.04.00/2001-2002 dated 24 September 2001. It has been decided in consultation with Government of India to include products of aluminium, petroleum products, sugar and foodgrains in the products eligible for special financial package for large value exports subject to the same terms and conditions stipulated in our circular referred to above.

Yours faithfully,

## (Sudarshan Sen) General Manager
