# Investments in Equities/Bonds Issued by All-India Financial Institutions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

Ref. DBOD No. Dir.BC. 6/13.07.05 /2002-03 July 22, 2002

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs and LABs)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Investments in Equities/Bonds issued by All-India Financial Institutions</u>

Please refer to paragraph 5.1.3 of our Master Circular DBOD No. Dir. 12/13.03.00/2001- 02 dated August 13, 2001 in terms of which banks’ investments in equity/bonds issued by the All-India Financial Institutions enumerated in Annexure 3 of the above circular are exempted from the 5 per cent ceiling prescribed in terms of paragraph 5 of our Circular DBOD No. BP. BC. 119/21.04.137/2000-2001 dated May 11, 2001. It has been decided that investments by banks in equities/bonds issued by Infrastructure Development Finance Company Ltd. (IDFC) should also be exempted from the abovesaid 5 per cent ceiling. Accordingly, a revised list of All-India Financial Institutions is enclosed for your information.

## Please acknowledge receipt.

Yours faithfully,

# Withdrawn

(M. R. Srinivasan) Chief General Manager
