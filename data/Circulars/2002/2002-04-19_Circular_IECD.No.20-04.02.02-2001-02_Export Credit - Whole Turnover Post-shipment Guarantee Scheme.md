## April 19, 2002

The Chairmen/Chief Executives of all Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Export Credit – Whole Turnover Post-shipment Guarantee Scheme

Please refer to Circular IECD No.EFD 15/04.02.02/96-97 dated November 19, 1996 wherein it was advised that the cost of premium in respect of the Whole Turnover Post- shipment Guarantees taken by banks may be absorbed by themselves and not passed on to the exporters.

# Withdrawn

2. It has come to our notice that some banks have been recovering the premium in respect of Whole Turn Over Post-Shipment Guarantee from the exporters. We, therefore, reiterate that the cost of premium in respect of the Whole Turnover Post-shipment Guarantees taken by banks may be absorbed by the banks themselves and not passed on to the exporters.
3. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, (S.D.Sapkal) Deputy General Manager
