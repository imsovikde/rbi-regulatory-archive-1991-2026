## Reliefs/concessions for exporters affected by cyclone in Orissa

As you are aware, the cyclone in Orissa during October 1999 has affected the export consignments from the region. It has now been decided to extend the reliefs/concessions enumerated in our circular IECD.No.8/04.02.01/98-99 dated August 20, 1998 issued in connection with the cyclonic storm in Gujarat during June 1998, to the exporters affected by the cyclone in Orissa.
