All the banks authorised to import Gold / Silver / Platinum Dear Sir,

st In terms of our <u>circular No.DBOD.IBS.1519/23.67.001/98-99 dated 31 December 1998</u>, authorized banks have been permitted to grant Gold (metal) loan as per Export Import Policy 1997-2002 and the Hand Book of Procedures of the Exim Policy subject to certain conditions mentioned in the circular.

2. In this connection we advise that the authorised banks may extend gold (metal) loans also to the jewellery exporters who are the customers of other non-authorized banks by accepting the
