## Dear Sir

## Implementation of Section 51-A of UAPA, 1967 - Updates of the UNSCR 1988

## (2011) Sanctions List

Please refer to our <u>circular DPSS. CO. AD. No 1328 /02.27.005/2011-12 dated January</u> <u>24, 2012</u>. We have since received from Government of India, Ministry of External Affairs, UNP Division copy of note dated February 14, 2012 forwarded by the Chairman of UN Security Council's 1998 Committee (<u>copy enclosed</u>) regarding changes made in the “1988 Sanctions List”, i.e. list of Individuals and entities linked to Taliban.

2. All the Payment System Operators (PSOs) are required to update the list of individuals/entities as circul
