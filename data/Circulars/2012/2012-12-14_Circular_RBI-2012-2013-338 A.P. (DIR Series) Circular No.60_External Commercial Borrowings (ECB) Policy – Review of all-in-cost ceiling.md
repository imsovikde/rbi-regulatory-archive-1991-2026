## A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 60 December 14, 2012

To, All Category-I Authorised Dealer Banks Madam / Sir,

## External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) Policy – Review of all-in-cost ceiling

Attention of Authorized Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to <u>A.P. (DIR</u> <u>Series) Circular No. 99 dated March 30, 2012</u> and <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 40 dated</u> <u>October 09, 2012</u> relating to ECB.

2. It has been decided that the all-in-cost ceiling as specified in A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 99 dated March 30, 2012 will continue to be applicable till March 31, 2013 and subject to review thereafter. All other aspects of ECB policy remain unchanged.
3. The amended ECB policy will come into force with immediate effect and is subject to
# Withdrawn

review based on the experience gained in this regard.

4. AD Category-I banks may bring the contents of this circular to the notice of their constituents and customers concerned.
5. The directions contained in this circular have been issued under sections 10(4) and 11(1) of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) and are without prejudice to permissions / approvals, if any, required under any other law.
Yours faithfully,

## (Rashmi Fauzdar) Chief General Manager
