## Anti‐Money Laundering (AML) / Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) ‐ Standards

Please refer to our <u>circular RPCD.CO.RRB.AML.No.1203 /03.05.28 (A)/2011‐12</u> <u>dated July 29, 2011</u> on risks arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of certain jurisdictions.

2. Financial Action Task Force (FATF) has updated its Statement on October 28, 2011 on the subject (<u>copy enclosed</u>).
3. All RRBs are accordingly advised to consider the information contained in the enclosed statement.
# Withdrawn

4. This, however, does not preclude Indian banks or financial institutions from legitimate trade and business transactions with these countries and jurisdictions.
