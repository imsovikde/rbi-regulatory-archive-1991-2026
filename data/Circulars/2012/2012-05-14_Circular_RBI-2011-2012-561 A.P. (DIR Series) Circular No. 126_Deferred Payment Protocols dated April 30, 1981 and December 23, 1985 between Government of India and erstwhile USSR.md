## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## Foreign Exchange Department

## Central Office

## <u>Mumbai - 400 001</u>

## RBI/2011-12 /561 A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 126 May 14, 2012

To

All Category-I Authorised Dealer Banks

Madam / Sir,

**Deferred Payment Protocols dated April 30, 1981 and December 23, 1985 between Government of India and erstwhile USSR**

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to <u>A.P.</u> <u>(DIR Series) Circular No. 105 dated April 10, 2012</u>, wherein the Rupee value of the Special Currency Basket was indicated as Rs. 70.965327 effective from March 13, 2012.

2. AD Category-I banks are advised that a further revision has taken place on April 23, 2012 and accordingly, the Rupee value of the Special Currency Basket
# Withdrawn

has been fixed at Rs.73.305676 with effect from April 26, 2012.

3. AD Category-I banks may bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of their constituents concerned.
4. The directions contained in this circular have been issued under sections 10(4) and 11(1) of the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 (42 of
1999) and are without prejudice to permissions / approvals, if any, required under any other law.
Yours faithfully,

## (Rashmi Fauzdar) Chief General Manager
