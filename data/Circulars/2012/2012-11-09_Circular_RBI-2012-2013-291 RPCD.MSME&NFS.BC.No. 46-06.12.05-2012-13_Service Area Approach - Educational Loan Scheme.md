Service Area Approach -Educational LoanScheme

We have been receiving a number of complaints where students have been refused

educational loan as the residence of the borrower does not fall under the bank's

service area. In this connection, we advise that Service area norms are to be

followed only in the case of Government sponsored schemes as advised in our

circular RPCD.LBS (SAA).BC.No.62/08.01.00/2004-05 dated December 8. 2004 and

are not applicable to sanction of educational loans.
