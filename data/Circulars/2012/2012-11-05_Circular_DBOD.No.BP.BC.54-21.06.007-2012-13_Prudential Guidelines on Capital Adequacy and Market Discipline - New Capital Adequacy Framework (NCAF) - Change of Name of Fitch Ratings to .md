2. In terms of para 6 of the circular, Fitch India has been accredited for the purpose of

risk weighting the banks' claims for capital adequacy purposes alongwith other credit

Ratings into domestic and international operations, the name of the wholly owned

subsidiary to be responsible for assigning domestic ratings has been changed to 'India

Ratings and Research Private Limited (lndia Ratings)'. The Securities and Exchange

Board of India (SEBl) has issued the necessary Certificate of Registration on May 20,

2012.

Department of Banking Operations & Development, Central Office, 12th Floor, Central Office Building,

Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai - 400 001 E-mail : cgmicdbodco@rbi.org.in

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

3. In view of above, it has been decided to revise para 6 of the circular under reference to include the new name of the CRA viz. 'India Ratings and Research Private Limited (India Ratings) in place of the earlier name of the CRA viz. 'Fitch India'. The revised rating symbols of the CRA after change in name are enclosed in the Annex.
4. All other provisions regarding external credit ratings of the master circular remain unchanged. Yours faithfully,
## (Deepak Singhal)

Chief General Manager-in-charge
