## Foreign investment in Single – Brand Retail Trading

## Amendment to the Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) Scheme

Attention of Authorised Dealers Category – I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to the Foreign Exchange Management (Transfer or Issue of Security by a Person Resident Outside India) Regulations, 2000, notified vide <u>Notification No. FEMA</u> <u>20/2000-RB dated May 3, 2000</u>, as amended from time to time. In terms of Schedule 1 of the Notification, FDI up to 51 per cent is permitted in Single Brand product trading under Government route of FDI Scheme.

2. The extant FDI policy has since been reviewed and it has now been decided that FDI up to 100 per cent would be permitted in Single Brand product
