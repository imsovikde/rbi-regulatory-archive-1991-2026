## Introduction

**2.12** A valid authorisation from the Reserve Bank of India is required for opening a new place of business including extension counters, off-site ATMs or changing the location of any existing place of business (except to the extent permitted vide paragraphs 5.1 to 5.5) under Section 23 of the BR Act, 1949 (AACS).
