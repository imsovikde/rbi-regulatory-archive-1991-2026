Section 42(1) of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – <u>Maintenance of Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR)</u>

Please refer to our <u>Circular UBD (PCB).No.3/12.03.000/2009-10 dated April 21, 2010</u> on the captioned subject.

2. The Reserve Bank in its <u>Third Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 issued on</u> <u>January 24, 2012</u>, decided to reduce the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) of Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks by 50 basis points from 6.00 per cent to 5.50 per cent of their net demand and time liabilities (NDTL), with effect from the fortnight beginning January 28, 2012.

## <u>Notification</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred under the sub-section (1) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and in partial modification of the earlier <u>notification</u> <u>UBD.(PCB).No./2/12.03.000/2009-10 dated April 21, 2010</u>, the Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies that the average Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) required to be maintained by every Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Bank shall be 5.50 per cent of its net demand and time liabilities, from the fortnight beginning January 28, 2012.
