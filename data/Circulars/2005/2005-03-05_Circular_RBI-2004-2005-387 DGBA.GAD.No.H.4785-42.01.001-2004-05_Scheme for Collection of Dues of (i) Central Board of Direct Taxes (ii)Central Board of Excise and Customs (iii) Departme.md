## March 5, 2005

The Chief Executive Officers All Agency Banks (As per list enclosed)

## Dear Sir

<u>Scheme for Collection of Dues of ( i ) Central Board of Direct Taxes ( ii)Central Board</u> <u>of Excise and Customs ( iii) Departmentalised Ministries Account -Reporting and</u> <u>Accounting of March Transactions -Special Arrangements-Financial Year 2004-2005</u>

Please refer to Circular DGBA.GAD.No.H-760/42.01.001/2003-04 dated February 13, 2004 advising the procedure to be followed for reporting and accounting of collection of Direct Taxes (CBDT ) and Indirect Taxes (CBEC ) and transactions of Departmentalised Ministries at the Receiving /Nodal / Focal Point branches of your bank.

2. It has been decided in consultation with Government of India that the date of closure of Residual Transactions for the month of March 2005 be fixed as April 16, 2005 (Saturday).
3. In view of the ensuing closing of Government Accounts for the financial year 2004-05, you may please reiterate the instructions regarding introduction of **s**<u>pecial messenger</u> <u>arrangements</u> at your receiving branches (situated locally) from the second fortnight of March 2005. Receiving branches not situated locally should also adopt <u>special</u> <u>arrangements</u> such as **courier service** etc. from the second fortnight of March 2005 for passing of challans/scrolls etc. to the Nodal/Focal Point

## <u>ANNEXURE</u>

<u>Reporting of March Transactions</u> Beginning from 1st April 2005, the Nodal/Focal Point branches will segregate on a daily basis all scrolls/challans pertaining to March 2005 received from the receiving branches concerned and prepare separate main scrolls for :

(a) scrolls for transactions of March 2005 or earlier period (i.e. effected during the previous financial year 2004-05 and
(b) scrolls pertaining to current transactions (i.e. those effected from 1st April 2005 onwards ).
2. The main scrolls for March 2005 transactions prepared from April 1, to April 16, 2005 are to be <u>distinctly marked as March Residual - 1, March Residual - 2</u> and so on upto March Residual - 16th April 2005. In other words, serial number should be allotted in consecutive order for each <u>main</u> scroll of March 2005 transactions sent from 1st April to 16th April 2005. These scrolls alongwith the copies of daily summary of Receipts and Payments prepared separately for March 2005 transactions will be forwarded to the Departmental Officials concerned ( i.e. Zonal Accounts Officers/Pay and Accounts Officers and Designated Officers) in the usual way. <u>The Nodal/Focal Point branches will also be required to report the above</u> <u>transactions to the Link Cell through separate Daily Memos.</u> These advices must be sent by telegrams/FAX to enable the Link Cell of each bank at Nagpur, to make daily settlement with Reserve Bank of India, Central Accounts Section (CAS) Nagpur. On receipt of advices (Memo/Telegrams/FAX) from the Nodal/Focal Point branches, the Link Cell should <u>segregate</u> <u>the advices for the March Residual transactions</u> and forward them <u>separately</u> to Reserve Bank of India, CAS, Nagpur for being processed on the computer. <u>This procedure should</u> <u>continue upto and inclusive of the April 16, 2005 only.</u> All transactions reported thereafter by
