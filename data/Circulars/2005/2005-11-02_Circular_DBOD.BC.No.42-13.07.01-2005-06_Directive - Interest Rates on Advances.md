DIRECTIVE DBOD.BC.No. 42/13.07.01/ 2005-06 November 2, 2005

## <u>Interest Rates on Advances</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred by Sections 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, the Reserve Bank of India, being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby directs that, with effect from November 1, 2005, the interest rates on rupee export credit would be as indicated in the Annex enclosed to this Directive.

(Usha Thorat) Executive Director

# Withdrawn
