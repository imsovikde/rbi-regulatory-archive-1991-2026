# Changes in the list of reserved items for exclusive manufacture in the Small Scale Sector

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2004-05/**346** RPCD/PLNFS/BC. No. **73** /06.02.31/2004-05 January 15, 2005 The Chairman/Managing Director All Scheduled Commercial Banks (including RRBs ) Dear Sir, <u>Notification No.S.O.1169 (E) dated 20.10.2004 regarding-changes in the list of reserved items for exclusive manufacture in the Small Scale</u>

## <u>Sector.</u>

Please refer to our circular RPCD/PLNFS/BC.No.23/06.02.31/2003-04 dated 15 September 2003 on the captioned subject.

2.We advise that Government of India, Ministry of Commerce & Industry (Deptt. of industrial Policy & Promotion) have since issued a Gazette Notification No.S.O 1169 (E) dated 20 October 2004 along with names of the 85 de-reserved items (which include 15 sub-items) from the list of items reserved for exclusive manufacture in the small scale sector. With the issue of aforesaid notification, the number of items reserved for exclusive manufacture in the small-scale sector as on 20 October 2004 stands at 605.
3. A copy of letter No.21(1)/2004-SSI Bd.& Pol. Dated 5 November 2004 received from the Government of India, Ministry of SSI, together with a copy of the Gazette Notification dated 20 October 2004 referred to above is enclosed for your information.
4. Further we also forward herewith for your information a copy of letter
