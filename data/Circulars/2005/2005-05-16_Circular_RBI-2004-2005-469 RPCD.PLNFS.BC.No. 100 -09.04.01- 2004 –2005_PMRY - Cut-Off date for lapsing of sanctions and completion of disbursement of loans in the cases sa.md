# PMRY - Cut-Off date for lapsing of sanctions and completion of disbursement of loans in the cases sa

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2004-05/469 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 100 /09.04.01/ 2004 –2005 May 16, 2005

The Chairman / Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir, <u>PMRY-Cut-Off date for lapsing of sanctions and completion of disbursement of loans in the cases sanctioned during 2004-05</u>

Please refer to para 1(iii) of our circular RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No.97/09.04.01/2004 – 2005 dated May 04, 2005.

2. The Ministry of Agro and Rural Industries, Government of India has since decided to extend the cut-off date for lapsing of sanction and completion of disbursement for the cases sanctioned under the Programme Year 2004-05 by a further period of **one month, i.e., from** **30 June 2005 to 31 July 2005.**
3. You may issue necessary instructions to your Regional /Controlling Offices/ Branches in this regard.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(G.P.Borah) Deputy General Manager
