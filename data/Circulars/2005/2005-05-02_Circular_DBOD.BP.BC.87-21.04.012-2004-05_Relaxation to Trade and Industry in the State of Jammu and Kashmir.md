# Relaxation to Trade and Industry in the State of Jammu and Kashmir

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2004-05/450 DBOD.BP.BC. 87 /21.04.012/ 2004-05 2 May 2005

The Chairmen of all Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Relaxation to trade and industry in the State of Jammu and Kashmir</u>

Please refer to our circular DBOD.No.BP.BC.77/21.04.012/2003-2004 dated 21st April 2004 extending the period of concessions/credit relaxations to borrowers/customers in Jammu and Kashmir up to 31 March 2005. It has been decided that the concessions/credit relaxations to borrowers/customers in the state of Jammu & Kashmir as laid down in our Circular No. DBOD.No.BP.BC.77/21.04.012/2003-2004 dated 21st April 2004 will continue to be operative for a further period of one year i.e. upto 31 March 2006.

2. Suitable instructions may please be issued to your controlling / branch offices in this regard.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.Withdrawn
Yours faithfully,

Sd/- (Anand Sinha) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
