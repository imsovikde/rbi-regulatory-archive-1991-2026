# Target under PMRY for the year 2005-2006 – Revision there of

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2005-06/58 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 18 /09.04.01/ 2005 –2006

## July 14, 2005

The Chairman / Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

|Please refer to our circular 2004 –2005 dated April 20, 2005|Target under PMRY for the year 2005-2006 – Revision there of RBI/2004-05/ 427 / RPCD.PLNFS. BC .No. 95 /09.04.01 / on the captioned subject. The Ministry of agro & rural Industries has since decided to enhance / reduce target allocated to some states/UTs for the year 2005-06 under PMRY as per the details given below:||
|---|---|---|
|State / Union Territory|Previously communicated Target (No.)|Revised Target (No.)|
|Pondicherry|900|650|
|Chandigarh|350|100|
|Andaman & Nicobar|400|200|
|Uttaranchal|7000|8000|
|Nagaland|1200|2800|

All other terms and conditions will remain the same as advised earlier.

2. You may issue necessary instructions to your Regional /Controlling Offices/ Branches for active participation and achievement of allocated targets by 31.03.2006.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(G.P.Borah) Deputy General Manager
