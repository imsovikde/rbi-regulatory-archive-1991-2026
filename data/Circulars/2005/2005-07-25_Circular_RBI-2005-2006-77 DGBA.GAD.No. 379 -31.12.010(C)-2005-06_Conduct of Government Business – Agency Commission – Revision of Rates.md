Dear Sir,

<u>Conduct of Government Business – Agency Commission – Revision of Rates</u>

As you are aware, the agency banks are at present being paid a commission of 11.80 paise per Rs.100/- of Government transactions (receipts and payments). In this connection, we refer to our letter DO.DGBA.GAD.No. 3/31.12.010(C)/2005-06 dated July 8, 2005, proposing modifications in the method of calculation as well as the rates of agency commission and seeking banks' comments in this regard. After deliberations, it has now been decided to prescribe the rates of agency commission payable to banks
