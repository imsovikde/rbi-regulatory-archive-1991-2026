## All Local Area Banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Submission of periodical returns and statements</u>

Please refer to our letter DBOD.No.PSBS. 889 /16.13.204/2003-04 dated February 25, 2004 on the captioned subject.

2. It is advised that the Local Area Banks may, henceforth, submit returns and statements to the departments/offices of RBI as indicated in the ‘Annexure – A’ and ‘Annexure B’. It is also advised that Rural Planning and Credit Department of RBI will be issuing separate instructions

Yours faithfully,

(K. N. Rajan) Deputy General Manager
