RBI/2005-06/186 UBD(PCB)/BPD/Cir.15 /09.72.000 /2005-06 October 20, 2005 The Chief Executive Officers of All Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks Dear Sir/Madam, Donations/Contributions for public/charitable purposes out of profits of UCBs Please refer to our circular UBD(PCB). / BPD/Cir. 43 /09.72.00 /2004-05 dated April 11, 2005 on the captioned subject.

3. All other terms and conditions mentioned in the circular under reference will continue to apply.
4. Please acknowledge receipt to the concerned Regional Office of the Reserve Bank of India.
