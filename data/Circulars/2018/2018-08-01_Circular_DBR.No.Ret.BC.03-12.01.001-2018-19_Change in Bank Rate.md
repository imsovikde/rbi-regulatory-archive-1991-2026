## _________________ RESERVE BANK OF INDIA________________

**www.rbi.org.in** **RBI/2018-19/32** **DBR.No.Ret.BC.03/12.01.001/2018-19 August 1, 2018**

The Chairperson / CEOs of all Scheduled and Non Scheduled Banks

Dear Sir / Madam, <u>Change in Bank Rate</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.Ret.BC.107/12.01.001/2017-18 dated June 6</u>, <u>2018</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As announced in the <u>Third Bi-Monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2018-19</u> <u>dated August 1, 2018</u>, the Bank Rate stands adjusted by 25 basis points from 6.50 per cent to 6.75 per cent with immediate effect.
3. All penal interest rates on shortfall in reserve requirements, which are specifically linked to the Bank Rate, also stand revised as indicated in the <u>Annex</u>**.**
