Investment in the units of an infrastructure investment trust (InvlT)

by sponsor CIC-NDSI

In order to enable Systemically Important Core Investment Companies (CIC-NDSl) to

act as a sponsor of InvlTs, it has been decided to permit CIC-NDSls to hold InvlT

units only as a sponsor. Exposure of such CiCs towards InvlTs shall be limited to

their holdings as sponsors and shall not, at any point in time, exceed the minimum

holding of units and tenor prescribed in this regard by SEBl (lnfrastructure

Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014.

reckoned as investments in equity

shares in group companies, for the purp

of compliance with the norms prescribed

at paragraphs 2(1) (i) & (i) of the

ster Direction-CoreInvestment Companies

(Reserve Bank) Directions,2016

updated from time to time.

3. The aforesaid Master Direction has been updated accordingly.
