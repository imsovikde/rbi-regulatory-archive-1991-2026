# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2017-2018/184 FMOD.MAOG.No.124/01.18.001/2017-18 June 6, 2018

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Madam/Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u>

As announced in the <u>Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19</u>, today, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to increase the policy Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 6.0 per cent to

**6.25 per cent** with immediate effect.
2. Consequent to the change in the Repo rate, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate stands adjusted to **6.50 per cent** with immediate effect.
3. All other terms and conditions of the extant MSF scheme will remain unchanged.
