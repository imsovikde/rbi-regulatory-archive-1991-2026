# Change in Bank Rate

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

भारतीय रज़वर् बक

## _____________RESERVE BANK OF INDIA________________

**www.rbi.org.in** **RBI/2017-18/185** **DBR.No.Ret.BC.107/12.01.001/2017-18 June 06, 2018**

The Chairperson / CEOs of all Scheduled and Non Scheduled Banks

Dear Sir / Madam, <u>Change in Bank Rate</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.Ret.BC.82/12.01.001/2017-18 dated August</u> <u>02, 2017</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As announced in the <u>Second Bi-Monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2018-19</u> <u>dated June 06, 2018</u>, the Bank Rate stands adjusted by 25 basis points from 6.25 per cent to 6.50 per cent with effect from June 06, 2018.
3. All penal interest rates on shortfall in reserve requirements, which are specifically linked to the Bank Rate, also stand revised as indicated in the **Annex.** Yours faithfully
# Withdrawn
