Madam/Dear Sir,

## Section 23 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Branch Authorisation Policy –

## Left Wing Extremism affected districts – Revised List

Please refer to our circular on ‘<u>Rationalisation of Branch Authorisation Policy-Revision of</u> <u>Guidelines’ No.DBR.BAPD.BC.69/22.01.001/2016-17 dated May 18, 2017</u>. As per the contents of paragraph No.4.2 (c) of the circular, a list of 106 Left Wing Extremism (LWE) affected districts in the country was furnished under Annex III of the circular, which was based on Government of India notification dated February 24, 2016.

2. As the Government of India has revised the list to 90 LWE affected districts, banks are advised to follow the revised list (effective from the date of this circular) as <u>annexed</u> herewith*.*
# Withdrawn
