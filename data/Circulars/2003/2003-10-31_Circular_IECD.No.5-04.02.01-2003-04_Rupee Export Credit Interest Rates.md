## Rupee Export Credit Interest Rates

IECD No.5 /04.02.01/2003-04 October 31, 2003

## The Chairmen/Chief Executives of all commercial banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Rupee Export Credit Interest Rates</u>

Please refer to our circular IECD.No.18/04.02.01/2002-03 dated April 30, 2003 on the captioned subject.

2. The validity of the interest rates on rupee export credit indicated in the above circular would now remain in force upto April 30, 2004. In this connection, you may please refer to circular No.MPD 240/O7.01.279/2003-04 dated October 31, 2003. The rates of interest applicable have been incorporated in the Annexure to the Directive DBOD No.BC. 41/13.07.01/2003-04 dated October 31, 2003 enclosed to this circular.
3. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully,
## (Smt.R.K.Makhija) Chief General Manager

## <u>Interest Rates on Advances</u>
