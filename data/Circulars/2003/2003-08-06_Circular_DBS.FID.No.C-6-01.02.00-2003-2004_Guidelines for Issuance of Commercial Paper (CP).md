**The CEOs of all India Term Lending & Refinancing Institutions**.

Dear Sir,

## <u>Guidelines for issuance of Commercial Paper (CP)</u>

As you are aware, the detailed guidelines for issuance of Commercial Paper (CP) by, *inter alia,* the banks and all-India financial institutions have been issued vide RBI Circular No. IECD.2/08.15.01/2001-2002 dated July 23, 2001. In terms of para 18 of Annexure II to the circular, credit enhancement for CP in the form of unconditional and irrevocable guarantee to be given by a non-bank entity, such as a corporate was not envisaged.

**2**. In order to provide flexibility to both the issue and investor in the CP market, it has been decided that the non- bank entities, including corporates, may provide unconditional and irrevocable guarantee for credit enhancement for CP issue subject to the following conditions (as already conveyed to the banks vide RBI circular IECD.No. 19/08.15.01 /2002-2003 dated April 30, 2003):
a) The issuer fulfills the eligibility criteria for issuance of CP;
b) The guarantor has a credit rating, atleast one notch higher than the issuer by approved credit rating agency;

## <u>Guidelines For Issue of Commercial Paper (CP)</u>

Please refer to paragraph No. 99 of the Monetary and Credit Policy Statement for the year 2003-04 dated 29th April 2003, ( copy of the paragraph enclosed), indicating certain modifications to our earlier guidelines relating to provision of credit enhancement for CP issues. The relevant guidelines are modified as under:

2. In terms of para 18 of Annexure II to our circular No.IECD.2/08.15.01/2001-02 dated 23rd July 2001, banks and all-India financial institutions (FIs) have the flexibility to provide, for a CP issue, credit enhancement by way of stand-by assistance/credit, back-stop facility etc. based on their commercial judgement, subject to prudential norms as applicable and with specific approval of their Boards. The present guidelines do not provide for credit enhancement for a CP issue in the form of an unconditional and irrevocable guarantee to be given by non-bank entities such as a corporate.
3. In order to provide further flexibility to both issuers and investors in the CP market, it has been decided that non-bank entities including corporates may provide unconditional and irrevocable guarantee for credit enhancement for CP issue provided :

4. It is further advised that banks are allowed to invest in CPs guaranteed by non-bank entities provided their exposure remains within the regulatory ceiling as prescribed by RBI for unsecured exposures.

## " Stand-by facility

In view of CP being a 'stand alone' product, it would not be obligatory in any manner on the part of the banks and FIs to provide stand-by facility to the issuers of CP. Banks and FIs have, however, the flexibility to provide for a CP issue, credit enhancement by way of stand-by assistance/credit, back-stop facility etc. based on their commercial judgement, subject to prudential norms as applicable and with specific approval of their Boards. Non-bank entities including corporates may also provide unconditional and irrevocable guarantee for credit enhancement for CP issue provided:
