# Interest Rates on Deposits - Capital Gains Accounts Scheme, 1988

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Interest Rates on Deposits – Capital Gains Account Scheme – 1988

March 19, 2003 Ref No. DBOD. Dir. BC. 82/13. 03.00 /2002-03

## All Public Sector Banks

Dear Sirs,

## Interest Rates on Deposits – Capital Gains Account Scheme – 1988

Please refer to our Notification DBOD No. Dir.BC.108/13.03.00/2000-01 dated April 19,2001 in terms of which the banks were advised of the rates of interest on deposits made under Capital Gains Accounts Scheme, 1988, introduced by Government of India. We have reviewed the rates and our Notification DBOD No. Dir.BC. 81/13.03.00/2002-03 dated 19 March 2003 advising the revised rates of interest, is enclosed. The revised rates will come into effect from March 01,

2003. Yours faithfully, **(R.C. Agrawal)** **General Manager** Encl: As above
