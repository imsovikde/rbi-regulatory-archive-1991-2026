# Investment in Corporate Debt Securities by Persons Resident Outside India through Special Rupee Vostro account

# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

RBI/2025-26/90

A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 13 October 03, 2025 To, All Authorised Dealer Category-I banks Madam / Sir **Investment in Corporate Debt Securities by Persons Resident Outside India** **through Special Rupee Vostro account** Attention of Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to Schedule 1 to the Foreign Exchange Management (Debt Instruments) Regulations, 2019 notified, *vide* <u>Notification No. FEMA. 396/2019-RB dated October 17, 2019</u>, and the Foreign Exchange Management (Deposit) Regulations, 2016 notified, *vide* <u>Notification</u> <u>No. FEMA. 5(R)/2016-RB dated April 01, 2016</u> amended from time to time and the as relevant Directions issued thereunder. A reference is also invited to the <u>Master</u> <u>Direction - Reserve Bank of India (Non-resident Investment in Debt Instruments)</u> <u>Directions, 2025 dated January 07, 2025</u>hereinafter “Master Direction”). (
**2.** Persons resident outside India that maintain a Special Rupee Vostro Account (SRVA) for international trade settlement in Indian Rupees in terms of <u>A.P. (DIR</u> <u>Series) Circular No. 10 dated July 11, 2022</u>ere permitted to invest their rupee surplus w balance in the aforesaid account in Central Government Securities (including Treasury Bills), *vide* <u>A.P.(DIR Series) Circular no. 9 dated August 12, 2025</u>. It has now been decided to also permit investment of these balances in non-convertible debentures/bonds and commercial papers issued by an Indian company.
**3.** The Master Direction has been updated as under:
(a) **In paragraph 3 (i) (e) of Part-1,** the words “<u>Government Securities</u>*”* shall be replaced by the words, namely: - “<u>eligible instruments</u>”

(b) **In paragraph 4.2 of Part-2,** under the section ‘Note’, after the existing paragraph (c), the following paragraph, shall be inserted, namely: - <u>“(d) Investments of rupee surplus balances in Special Rupee Vostro</u> <u>Account in non-convertible debentures/bonds and commercial papers</u> <u>issued by an Indian company shall be reckoned under the investment limit</u> <u>for corporate debt securities under the General Route</u>*.*
(c) **In paragraph 7A.2 of Part-5A,** after the words *“*<u>(including Treasury Bills</u>*)”,* the following words shall be inserted, namely: - <u>“on and n-convertible debentures/bonds and commercial papers issued by</u> <u>an Indian company</u>*”*
(d) **In Part-5A,** after the existing paragraph 7A.2, the following paragraph shall be inserted, namely: - <u>“Explanation: “non-convertible debentures/bonds issued by an Indian</u> <u>company” and “commercial papers issued by an Indian company” shall</u> <u>mean instruments as specified respectively at paragraph 1A(b) and</u> <u>paragraph 1A(c) of Schedule 1 to Foreign Exchange Management (Debt</u> <u>Instruments) Regulations, 2019 notified, vide, Notification No. FEMA.</u> <u>396/2019-RB dated October 17, 2019.”</u>
(e) **In Part-5A,** after the existing paragraph 7A.4, the following paragraph shall be inserted, namely: - <u>ì7A.4.1 Investments in non-convertible debentures/bonds and commercial</u> <u>papers issued by an Indian company shall be in terms of the following:</u>
*(i)* <u>The investments shall be subject to the investment limit and</u> <u>stipulations specified for FPI investments under the General Route</u> <u>as set out in paragraphs 4.2 and 4.4 of these Directions respectively.</u> <u>Provided that the minimum residual maturity requirement as set out</u> <u>in paragraph 4.4 (i) and the issue-wise limit as set out in paragraph</u>
<u>4.4 (iv) of these Directions shall not apply to investments made under</u> <u>the SRVA route.</u>

*(ii)* <u>The primary responsibility of complying with all applicable limits for</u> <u>such investments shall lie with the SRVA holders and the AD</u> <u>Category – I banks where these accounts are maintained.</u>*”*

(f) **In paragraph 7A.5 of Part-5A,** the words *“*<u>Central Government securities</u> <u>(including Treasury Bills)</u>*”* shall be replaced by the words, namely: - “<u>eligible</u> <u>instruments</u>”
(g) **In Part-5A,** after the existing paragraph 7A.6.(i), the following paragraph shall be inserted, namely: - <u>“(i-a) facilitate opening of separate demat accounts for SRVA holders for</u> <u>holding all their investments in non-convertible debentures/bonds and</u> <u>commercial papers issued by an Indian company</u>”
(h) **In Part-5A,** after the existing paragraph 7A.6.(iii), the following paragraph shall be inserted, namely: - <u>“(iii-a) report the transactions by SRVA holders in non-convertible</u> <u>debentures/bonds and commercial papers issued by an Indian company to</u> <u>depository(ies) registered with SEBI, for reckoning them under the</u> <u>investment limits for corporate debt securities under the General Route</u>*”*
(i) **In paragraph 7A.6 (v) of Part-5A,** the words *“*<u>Central Government</u> <u>securities (including Treasury Bills)</u>*”* shall be replaced by the words, namely:
-*“*<u>eligible instruments</u>*”*
**4.** These instructions shall be applicable with immediate effect.
**5.** AD Category – I banks may bring the contents of this circular to the notice of their constituents and customers concerned.
**6.** The Directions contained in this circular have been issued under sections 10(4) and 11(1) of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) and are without prejudice to permissions/approval, if any, required under any other law.
Yours faithfully,

(Dimple Bhandia) Chief General Manager
