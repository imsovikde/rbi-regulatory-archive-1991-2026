**Non-achievement of PSL targets – Prudential treatment of contribution towards eligible funds with NABARD, NHB, SIDBI and MUDRA Ltd.**

### A. Exemption from exposure norms

In terms of para 2.1 of <u>circular DOR (PCB).BPD.Cir No.10/13.05.000/2019-20 dated</u> <u>March 13, 2020</u> on ‘*Limits on exposure to single and group borrowers/parties and large* *exposures and Revision in the target for priority sector lending – UCBs’*, the prudential exposure limits for UCBs for a single borrower/party and a group of connected borrowers/parties are specified as 15 per cent and 25 per cent, respectively, of their tier-I capital.

2. On a review, it has been decided that the contribution¹ by UCBs towards eligible funds with NABARD, NHB, SIDBI, MUDRA Ltd., or any other entity specified by RBI,
