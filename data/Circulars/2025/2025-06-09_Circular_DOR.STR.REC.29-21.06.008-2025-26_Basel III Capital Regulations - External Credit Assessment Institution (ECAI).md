# Basel III Capital Regulations - External Credit Assessment Institution (ECAI)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## भारतीय रज़वर् बैंक RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

RBI/2025-26/50 DOR.STR.REC.29/21.06.008/2025-26 June 09, 2025

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (including Small Finance Banks) (excluding Local Area Banks, Payments Banks and Regional Rural Banks)

Dear Sir/ Madam,

### Basel III Capital Regulations-External Credit Assessment Institution (ECAI)

Please refer to paragraph 6.1.2 of the <u>Master Circular</u> <u>No. DOR.CAP.REC.2/21.06.201/2025-26 dated April 1, 2025</u> on **Basel III Capital** **Regulations**, wherein the list of domestic credit rating agencies accredited for the purpose of risk weighting banks' claims for capital adequacy purposes has been prescribed.

2. A reference is also invited to the <u>circular DOR.STR.REC.26/21.06.008/2024-25</u>
# Withdrawn

<u>dated July 10, 2024</u> in terms of which, banks were permitted to use the ratings of Brickwork Ratings India Private Limited (BRIPL) for risk weighting their claims for capital adequacy purposes subject to the restrictions/limits specified therein.

3. On a review, it has been decided to remove the restrictions/ limits placed on the use of ratings of BRIPL by the banks.
4. All other provisions regarding external credit ratings stipulated in the Master Circular ibid remain unchanged. Yours faithfully, (Vaibhav Chaturvedi) Chief General Manager
