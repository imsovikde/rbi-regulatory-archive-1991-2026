### Penal Interest on shortfall in CRR and SLR requirements-Change in Bank Rate

Please refer to Chapter VIII of <u>Master Direction-Reserve Bank of India [Cash Reserve Ratio</u> <u>(CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)] Directions – 2021</u> as well as our <u>circular</u> <u>DoR.RET.REC.57/12.01.001/2024-25 dated February 07, 2025</u> on the captioned subject.
