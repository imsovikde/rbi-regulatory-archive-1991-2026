### Processing and Monitoring System (IDPMS) – reconciliation of export /import entries – Review of Guidelines

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category – I banks (AD banks) is invited to the instructions in the <u>Master Direction – Export of Goods & Services</u> and <u>Master Direction</u> <u>– Import of Goods & Services</u>, related to processing of bills in EDPMS and IDPMS respectively.

2. To facilitate timely closure of entries in EDPMS & IDPMS, and to reduce compliance burden on small exporters and importers, the following directions are being issued.
3. Notwithstanding anything contained in the aforesaid master directions, AD banks shall adopt the following procedure while closing entries (including outstanding entries)
