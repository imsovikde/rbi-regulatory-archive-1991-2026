## Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Credit Information Reporting)

## Amendment Directions, 2025

The Reserve Bank had issued <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Credit</u> <u>Information Reporting) Directions, 2025</u>or efficient functioning of credit f information reporting system in the country. There is a need to further amend the same based on a review of the extant instructions.

2. Accordingly, in exercise of the powers conferred under Section 11 of the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005, the Reserve Bank being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest to do so, hereby issues the Amendment Directions hereinafter specified.
3. (i) These directions shall be called the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Credit Information Reporting) Amendment Directions, 2025. (ii) These directions shall come into force from July 01, 2026.
4. These directions modify the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks –</u> <u>Credit Information Reporting) Directions, 2025</u>, hereinafter referred to as the said Directions, as under:
