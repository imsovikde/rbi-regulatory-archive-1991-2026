## Compliance with Know Your Customer (KYC) norms

Attention of Authorised Persons is invited to instructions contained in Section I and Section VI of ‘<u>Master Direction – Money Changing Activities</u>’; Para 27(4) of ‘<u>Master</u> <u>Direction – Overseas Investment</u>’; Para 6.5 of ‘<u>Master Direction – Other Remittance</u> <u>Facilities</u>’; and Para 3.2.b and 8(i) of ‘<u>Master Direction – Money Transfer Service Scheme</u> <u>(MTSS)</u>’ on compliance with KYC norms.

2. ‘<u>Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016</u>’ has since been substituted with regulatory instructions applicable separately to each type of entity regulated by the Department of Regulation, Reserve Bank of India. Therefore, it is directed as under:
(a) Authorised Persons, which are regulated by the Department of Regulation, Reserve Bank of India, shall be governed by the respective ‘Know Your Customer’ directions as applicable to them.
(b) Authorised Persons, which are not regulated by the Department of Regulation, Reserve Bank of India, shall be governed by ‘<u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking</u> <u>Financial Companies – Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025</u>’.
(c) Authorised Persons shall ensure compliance of directions, as applicable to them, by their agents/ sub-agents/franchisees.
3. The directions contained in this circular have been issued under sections 10(4) and 11(1) of the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 (42 of 1999) and are without prejudice to permissions / approvals, if any, required under any other law.
