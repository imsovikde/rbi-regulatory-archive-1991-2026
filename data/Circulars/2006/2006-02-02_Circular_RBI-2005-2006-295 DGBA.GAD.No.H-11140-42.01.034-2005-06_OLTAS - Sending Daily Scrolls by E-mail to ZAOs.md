# OLTAS - Sending Daily Scrolls by E-mail to ZAOs

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005-2006/295 DGBA.GAD.No.H-11140/42.01.034/2005-06 February 2, 2006

The Chairman and Managing Director All Agency Banks

Dear Sir,

<u>Online Tax Accounting System (OLTAS)- Sending daily scrolls by e-mail to</u> <u>ZAOs</u>

Please refer to our circular DGBA. GAD. No. H-6586/42.01.034/2005-06 dated November 28, 2005 on the captioned subject. We now enclose herewith the revised format of Electronic Nodal Branch Daily Main Scroll for Direct Tax Collection by agency banks. You may use the revised format to send the Daily Main Scroll electronically to all ZAOs pending receipt of physical challans/scrolls from all receiving branches. Yours faithfully, (Sd/-) (M.T.Varghese)

# Withdrawn General Manager
