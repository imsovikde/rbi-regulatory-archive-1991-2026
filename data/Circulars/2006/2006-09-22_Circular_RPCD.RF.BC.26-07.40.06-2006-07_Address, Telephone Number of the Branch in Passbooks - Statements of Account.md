# Address, Telephone Number of the Branch in Passbooks / Statements of Account

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2006-07/134 RPCD.RF.BC. 26/07.40.06/2006-07 September 22, 2006 All State and District Central Co-operative Banks Dear Sir, <u>Address, telephone number of the Branch</u> <u>in passbooks/statements of account</u> In order to improve the quality of service available to customers in branches, it would be useful if the address and telephone number of the branch are mentioned on the passbooks/statements of account.

2. Banks are, therefore, advised to ensure that full address and telephone number of the branch are invariably mentioned in the passbooks/ statements of account issued to accountholders.
3. Please acknowledge receipt to our Regional Office concerned.
## Yours faithfully

(C.S.Murthy) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
