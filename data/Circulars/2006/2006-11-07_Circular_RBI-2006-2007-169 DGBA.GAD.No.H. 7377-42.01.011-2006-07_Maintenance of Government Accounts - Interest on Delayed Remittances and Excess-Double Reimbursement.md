November 7, 2006 Kartika 16, 1928 (Saka)

The Chairman/Managing Director State Bank of India & its Associates All Nationalised Banks Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd. IDBI Ltd/HDFC Bank Ltd. ICICI Bank Ltd./UTI Bank Ltd.

## Dear Sir

Maintenance of Government Accounts-Interest on Delayed Remittances and Excess/Double Reimbursement

Please refer to our Circular No.RBI/2006/387(DGBA.GAD.No.H.17981/42.01.011 /2005-06) dated May 17, 2006 on the above subject.

2. We advise that the rate of interest on delayed remittances and double/excess reimbursement would remain unchanged at 8% (i.e. Bank Rate - 6% + 2%) till further instructions.
# Withdrawn

Yours faithfully

(A.S. Kulkarni ) Deputy General Manager
