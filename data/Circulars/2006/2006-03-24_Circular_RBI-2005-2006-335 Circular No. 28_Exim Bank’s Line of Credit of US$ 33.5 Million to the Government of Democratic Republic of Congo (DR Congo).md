# Exim Bank’s Line of Credit of US$ 33.5 Million to the Government of Democratic Republic of Congo (DR Congo)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005-06/335

A.P.(DIR Series) Circular No. 28
March 24, 2006

To,

All Banks Authorised to Deal in Foreign Exchange

Madam / Sir,

<u>Exim Bank’s Line of Credit of US$ 33.5 Million</u> <u>to the Government of Democratic Republic of Congo (DR Congo)</u>

The Export-Import Bank of India (Exim Bank) has concluded an agreement with the Government of Democratic Republic of Congo (DR Congo) making available to the latter a Line of Credit (LOC) upto an aggregate sum of USD 33.5 million (US Dollar Thirty three million five hundred thousand only). The credit agreement has become effective on February 20, 2006. The credit is available for financing export from India, of 228 Buses (US$ 12.5 mn), equipments for Miniere de Bakwanga (MIBA) (US$ 2 mn) and other equipment, goods and services including consultancy services for setting up a cement factory (US$13 mn) and rehabilitation of mine in Idsenge Manganse (US$ 6 mn), in Congo, which are eligible for export under the Foreign Trade Policy of the Government of India.
