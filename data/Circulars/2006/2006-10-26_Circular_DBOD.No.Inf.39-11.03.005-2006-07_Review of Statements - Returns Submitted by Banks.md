# Review of Statements / Returns Submitted by Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2006-07/161 DBOD No. Inf.39/ 11.03.005/2006-07 October 26, 2006

The Chairman/ Managing Director/ Chief Executives of all Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

Review of statements/returns submitted by banks As you are aware all scheduled commercial banks are required to forward to us on a periodical basis, statements/returns in terms of various circulars issued by us from time to time. However, in the light of changes that have taken place in the industry, some of the statements/returns being submitted by banks have lost their relevance. On a review it has therefore been decided to discontinue forthwith submission of the following statements/returns.
