# Assignment Lead Bank responsibility for new district in Punjab

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005-06/402 RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No.89/02.08.01/2005-06 The Chairman All Lead Banks June 07, 2006 Dear Sir, <u>Formation of new district in Punjab – Assignment of Lead Bank responsibility</u> The Department of Revenue and Rehabilitation, Government of Punjab vide their Gazette Notification No. 2/7/98-RE-II(I)3336 dated April 5, 2006 have advised about the formation of new district viz. Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar covering three tehsils, viz Kharar Tehsil (200 villages), Mohali Tehsil (82 villages) both carved out of existing Ropar district and Dera Bassi Tehsil (154 villages) carved out of existing Patiala district, with effect from April 14,

2006.
2. It has been decided to assign the Lead Bank responsibility of the new district to Punjab National Bank.
3. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of other districts in the State.
Yours faithfully,

(A.K.Bhandari) Deputy General Manager
