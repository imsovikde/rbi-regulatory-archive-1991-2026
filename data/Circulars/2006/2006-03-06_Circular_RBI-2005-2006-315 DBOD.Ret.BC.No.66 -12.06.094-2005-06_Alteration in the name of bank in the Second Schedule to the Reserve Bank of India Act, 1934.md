# Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005-06/315 DBOD.Ret.BC.No.66 /12.06.094/2005-06 March 06, 2006

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

## Dear Sir

<u>Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve</u> <u>Bank of India Act, 1934.</u>

We advise that the name of " Centurion Bank Limited " has been changed to " Centurion Bank of Punjab Limited " in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with effect from October 17, 2005, in terms of Notification DBOD.No.PSBD.390/16.01.130/2005-06 dated October 21, 2005, which was published in the Gazette of India ( Part III-Section 4 ) on December 10, 2005

## Yours faithfully

(S.Oram) Deputy General Manager
