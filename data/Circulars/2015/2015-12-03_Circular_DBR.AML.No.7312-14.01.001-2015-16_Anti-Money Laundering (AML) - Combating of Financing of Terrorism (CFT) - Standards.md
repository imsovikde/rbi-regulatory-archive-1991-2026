## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/ Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT) -Standards

Please refer to our letter <u>DBR. AML. No. 1637/14.01.001/2015-16 dated July 30, 2015</u> on risks arising from the deficiencies in AML/CFT regime of certain jurisdictions.
