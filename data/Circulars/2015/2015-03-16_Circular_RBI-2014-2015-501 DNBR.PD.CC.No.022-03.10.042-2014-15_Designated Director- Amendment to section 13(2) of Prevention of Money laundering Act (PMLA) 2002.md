Designated Director- Amendment to section 13(2) of Prevention of Money

laundering Act (PMLA) 2002

Please refer to our Circular DNBS (PD).CC.No.378/03.10.42/2013-14 dated May 29.

2014 wherein NBFCs were advised to nominate a Director on their Boards as

"Designated Director” to ensure compliance with the obligations under the

Prevention of Money Laundering (Amendment) Act, 2012.

2. In this connection, it is clarified that NBF

can also designate a person who

equivalent as a “Designated Director".

However, in no case, the Principal

should be nominated as the “Designated

Director".
