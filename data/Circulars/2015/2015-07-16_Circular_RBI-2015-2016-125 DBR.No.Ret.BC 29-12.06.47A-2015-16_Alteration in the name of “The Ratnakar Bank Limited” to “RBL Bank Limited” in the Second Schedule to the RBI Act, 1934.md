## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir, **Alteration in the name of “The Ratnakar Bank Limited” to “RBL Bank Limited”** **in the Second Schedule to the RBI Act, 1934**

We advise that the name of **“The Ratnakar Bank Limited”** has been changed to **“RBL Bank Limited”** in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 *vide* Notification DBR.PSBD.No.8931/16.01.072/2014-15 dated December 15, 2014 and published in the Gazette of India, Issue No.3 (Part III Section 4) dated January 17- 23 January, 2015 read with the corrigendum published in The Gazette of India, Issue No. 20 (Part III Section 4) dated May16 - May 22, 2015.
