# Calendar of reviews

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2014-15/614 DBR.No.BC.95 /29.67.001/2014-15 May 28, 2015 The Managing Director & Chief Executive Officers All Private Sector Banks Dear Sir/Madam, <u>Calendar of reviews</u> Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.BC.93/29.67.001/2014-15 dated May 14, 2015</u> (copy enclosed), addressed to all the CMDs / MD & CEOs of all public sector banks on the captioned matter.

2. We advise that the private sector banks may also adhere to the instructions contained in the above mentioned circular, to the extent applicable.
3. This supersedes our <u>circular DBOD.No.PSBD.BC.93/16.13.100/2007-08 June 9,</u> <u>2008</u> on the subject. Yours faithfully,
# Withdrawn

Sd/ (Sudha Damodar) Chief General Manager Encl. As above
