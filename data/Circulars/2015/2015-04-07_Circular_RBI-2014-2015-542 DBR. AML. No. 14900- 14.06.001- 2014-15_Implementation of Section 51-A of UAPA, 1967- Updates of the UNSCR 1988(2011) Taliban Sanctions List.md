## Implementation of Section 51-A of UAPA, 1967-Updates of the UNSCR 1988(2011) Taliban Sanctions List

Please refer to our <u>circulars DBR. AML. No. 9476/14.06.001/2014-15 dated</u> th rd <u>December 26, 2014</u> on the captioned subject, releasing 9 update of 2013 and 3 update of 2014 regarding UNSCR “1988 Sanctions List”, i.e. list of Individuals and entities linked to Taliban.

Links to the press releases is given below: <u>[http://www.un.org/press/en/2015/sc11843.doc.htm](http://www.un.org/press/en/2015/sc11843.doc.htm)</u> Press releases in which the relevant changes to the list are announced are posted on the Committee’s website at the following URL: <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pressreleases.shtml](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pressreleases.shtml)</u> A link to updated list of individuals and entities linked to Taliban incorporating all the above updates is available at <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988.pdf](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988.pdf)</u>.
3. Banks/ All India Financial Institutions are required to update the list of individuals/ entities as circulated by Reserve Bank and before opening any new account, it should be ensured that the name/s of the proposed customer does not appear in the list. Further, banks should scan all existing accounts to ensure that no account is held by or linked to any of the entities or individuals included in the list.
