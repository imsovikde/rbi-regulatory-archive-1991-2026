RBI/2014-15/375 DGBA.GAD.No.H - 2887/42.01.035/2014 -15 January 1, 2015

The Chairman/Chief Executive Officer All agency banks

## Dear Sir

**Implementation of Cheque Truncation System-Dispensation of the requirement of forwarding of government cheques in physical form to**

**government**

Please refer to our <u>circular DGBA.GAD.No.H - 1463/42.01.035/2014-15 dated</u> <u>September 30, 2014</u> wherein it was stated that the discontinuation of P2F for government cheques was to be implemented with effect from January 1, 2015.

2. However, it has now been decided to postpone the implementation of discontinuation of P2F. The revised date would be advised shortly.
# Withdrawn
