# Payment of agency commission – Certification of claims by external auditors

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/233 DGBA.GAD.No. 1636/31.12.010/2015-16 November 10, 2015

The Chairman / Chief Executive Officer All agency banks

## Dear Sir / Madam

## Payment of agency commission – Certification of claims by external auditors

Please refer to our circulars DGBA.GAD.No.2528/31.12.010(C)/2012-13 and <u>DGBA.GAD.No.H-2995/31.12.010/2014-15</u> dated October 31, 2012 and January 7, 2015 respectively prescribing the format for certification of agency commission claims by external auditors (Chartered Accountants). The certificate to be furnished by the auditors has since been slightly amended. The agency commission claims submitted to our Regional Offices and Central Accounts Section, Nagpur may henceforth be accompanied by a certificate as per the revised format enclosed.Withdrawn

## Yours faithfully

(Anita Kumari) Assistant General Manager
