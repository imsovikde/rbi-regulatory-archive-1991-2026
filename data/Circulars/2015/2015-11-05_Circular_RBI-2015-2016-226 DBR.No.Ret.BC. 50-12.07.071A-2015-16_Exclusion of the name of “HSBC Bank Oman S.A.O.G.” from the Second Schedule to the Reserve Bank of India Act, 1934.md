## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Exclusion of the name of “HSBC Bank Oman S.A.O.G.” from

## the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

We advise that the name of **“HSBC Bank Oman S.A.O.G.”** has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.14566/23.03.029/2014-15 dated April 01, 2015, published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated May 16–May 22, 2015.

## Yours faithfully

(M.K.Samantaray) General Manager
