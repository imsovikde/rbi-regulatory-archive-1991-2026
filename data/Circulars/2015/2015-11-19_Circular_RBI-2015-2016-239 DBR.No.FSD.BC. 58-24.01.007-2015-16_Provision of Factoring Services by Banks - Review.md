# Provision of Factoring Services by Banks - Review

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2015-16/239 DBR.No.FSD.BC. 58/24.01.007/2015-16 November 19, 2015

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

**(excluding RRBs)** Dear Sir/Madam **Provision of Factoring Services by Banks-Review** Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.No.FSD.BC.32/24.01.007/2015-16 dated July 30,</u> <u>2015</u> on *Provision of Factoring Services by banks- Review.*

2. In paragraph 4(a), it has been mentioned that *in order to ensure that the bank* *offering factoring services has enough margin to cover any deficiencies in the* *payment of the related invoice, it should be ensured that the pre-payment amount* *offered by banks for the receivables acquired under factoring should not exceed 80%* *of the invoice value*.
3. On a review, it is advised that banks offering factoring services may decide percentage of the invoice to be paid upfront based on their own assessment of the credit worthiness of the assignor/ buyer, due diligence carried out by them and other
