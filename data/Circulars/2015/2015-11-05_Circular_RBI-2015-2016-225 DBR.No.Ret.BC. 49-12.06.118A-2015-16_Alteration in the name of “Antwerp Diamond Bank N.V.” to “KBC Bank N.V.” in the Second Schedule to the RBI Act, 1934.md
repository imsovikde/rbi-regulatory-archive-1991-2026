## RBI/2015-16/225

DBR.No.Ret.BC. 49/12.06.118A/2015-16 November 5, 2015

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Alteration in the name of “Antwerp Diamond Bank N.V.” to

## “KBC Bank N.V.” in the Second Schedule to the RBI Act, 1934

We advise that the name of **“Antwerp Diamond Bank N.V.”** has been changed to **“KBC Bank N.V.”** in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD. No.451/23.13.077/2015-16 dated July 08, 2015 published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated July 27, 2015.

## Yours faithfully

(M.K.Samantaray) General Manager
