## <u>SHG-Bank Linkage Programme – Revision of progress reports</u>

In terms of our <u>Circular RPCD.CO.MFFI.BC.No.103/12.01.01/2006-07 dated June 20,</u> <u>2007</u> on Microfinance – Submission of progress report and <u>Master Circular</u> <u>RPCD.FID.BC.No.06/12.01.033/2014-15 dated July 01, 2014</u> on SHG Bank Linkage Programme, banks have been submitting progress report in the format specified to NABARD on a half yearly basis as on 30 September and 31 March every year.

2. Keeping in view the recent developments, it has been decided to revise the reporting format with a view to capture data pertaining to financing of SHGs under ‘National Rural Livelihood Mission’ (NRLM) and ‘National Urban Livelihoods Mission’ (NULM) separately. Accordingly, the progress under SHG Bank Linkage may be reported state- wise in the enclosed revised format directly to the Micro Credit Innovations Department of NABARD, on half yearly basis. The first such progress report as on 31 March 2015 in the revised format may be sent to NABARD not later than June 30, 2015 and subsequent half year return within 30 days from the due date.
3. In addition to hard copy, a soft copy of the progress report in excel format may be sent by <u>email</u> to NABARD.

## <u>Notes for filling up Part B</u>

एमएफओ/एमएफआईसे िरप्रसारसे अ ता तयहदऋर्णस्वसससजववतररतासएाएिैंतजलािा‘स्वससम लल’ से अ ता तदिा याजाए।यहदएमएफओ/एमएफआईद्वारा प्रत्यक्षूपपमेंअला-अलासदस्योंसजऋर्णववतररतासएाएिैंतजउसेाैर-स्वससम ललसे अतातसूयचतासयाजाए।

Under each type of MFO/MFI, if the loans are disbursed to SHGs, then data may be shown under ‘SHG model’. If the loans are disbursed by MFO/MFI directly to individual members, then it may be reported under ‘Non-SHG model’.
