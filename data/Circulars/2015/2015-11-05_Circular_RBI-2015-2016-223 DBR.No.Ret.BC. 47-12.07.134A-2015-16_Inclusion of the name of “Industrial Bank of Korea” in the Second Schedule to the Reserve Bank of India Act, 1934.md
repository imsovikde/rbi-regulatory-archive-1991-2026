## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Inclusion of the name of “Industrial Bank of Korea” in the

## Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

We advise that the name of **“Industrial Bank of Korea”** has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD. No.472/23.13.0158/2015-16 dated July 08, 2015, published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated July 27, 2015.

## Yours faithfully

(M.K.Samantaray) General Manager
