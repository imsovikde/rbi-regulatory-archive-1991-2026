# Conduct of Government Business by Agency Banks – Payment of Agency Commission

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/153 DGBA.GAD.No. 617/31.12.010(C)/2015-16 August 13, 2015

The Chairman/Chief Executive Officer All Agency Banks

Dear Sir / Madam, **Conduct of Government Business by Agency Banks – Payment of Agency** **Commission**

Please refer to paragraphs 2 to 7 of our <u>Master Circular No RBI /2015-16/81 dated July</u> <u>01, 2015</u> on the captioned subject, wherein government transactions eligible for and not eligible for agency commission are discussed.

2. In this connection, it is clarified that the following activities do not come under the purview of agency bank business and are therefore not eligible for payment of agency commission.
(a) Furnishing of bank guarantees/security deposits, etc through private sector banks by government contractors/suppliers, which constitute banking transactionsWithdrawn undertaken by banks for their customers.
(b) The banking business of autonomous/statutory bodies.
(c) ayments of a capital nature such as capital contributions/subsidies/grants m P ade by governments to cover losses incurred by autonomous/statutory bodies.
(d) Prefunded schemes which may be implemented by a Central Government Ministry/Department (in consultation with CGA) and a State Government Department through any bank without reference to RBI.
