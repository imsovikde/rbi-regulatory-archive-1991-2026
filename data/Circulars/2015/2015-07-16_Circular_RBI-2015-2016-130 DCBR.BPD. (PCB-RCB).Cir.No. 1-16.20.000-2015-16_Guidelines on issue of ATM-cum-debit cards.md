## Guidelines on issue of ATM-cum-debit cards

Please refer to our <u>circular UBD(PCB) Cir No.6/09.18.300/2007-08 dated July 13,</u> <u>2007</u> issued to UCBs on the captioned subject and <u>circular DCBR.CO</u>. <u>RCB.No.BC.29/19.51.008/2014-15 dated April 16, 2015</u> issued to StCBs on installation of offsite/ mobile ATMs.

2. We have been receiving representations from banks not having their own ATMs seeking permission to issue ATM-cum-debit cards to their customers in tie-up with banks having their own ATM network. The matter has been examined keeping in
