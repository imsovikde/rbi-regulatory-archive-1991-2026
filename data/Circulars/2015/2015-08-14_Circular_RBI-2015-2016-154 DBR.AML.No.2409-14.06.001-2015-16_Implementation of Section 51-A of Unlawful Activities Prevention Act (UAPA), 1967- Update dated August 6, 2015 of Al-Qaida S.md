# Implementation of Section 51-A of Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA), 1967- Update dated August 6, 2015 of Al-Qaida Sanctions List

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RBI/2015-16/154** **DBR.AML.No.2409/14.06.001/2015-16 August 14, 2015** **The Chairpersons/ CEOs of all Scheduled Commercial Banks/ Regional Rural** **Banks/ Local Area Banks/ All India Financial Institutions/ all NBFCs/ All** **Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks /State and Central Co-operative Banks** **(StCBs / CCBs) /All Payment System Providers/ System Participants and** **Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuers/ All authorised persons including those who are agents of Money Transfer Service Scheme**

Dear Sir/ Madam,

## Implementation of Section 51-A of Unlawful Activities Prevention Act (UAPA),

## 1967- Update dated August 6, 2015 of Al-Qaida Sanctions List

Please refer to our <u>circular DBR.AML.No.1223/14.06.001/2015-16 dated July 22</u>, th th <u>2015</u> on the captioned subject releasing the 17 and 18 update dated July 20, 2015 regarding UNSCR 1267(1999)/ 1989(2011) Committee's Al Qaida Sanctions List.

2. Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), UNP Division has forwarded update dated August 6, 2015 regarding addition of one entry and amendment to one entry of the sanction list (<u>copy enclosed</u>). Press release pertaining to the update dated August 6, 2015 is available at: <u>[http://www.un.org/press/en/2015/sc11998.doc.htm](http://www.un.org/press/en/2015/sc11998.doc.htm)</u>. A link to updated list of individuals and entities linked to Al Qaida is available at: <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1267/1267.pdf](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1267/1267.pdf)</u>
3. Regulated Entities (REs) are required to update the list of individuals/entities as circulated by Reserve Bank and before opening any new account, it should be ensured that the name/s of the proposed customer does not appear in the list. Further, banks should scan all existing accounts to ensure that no account is held by or linked to any of the entities or individuals included in the list.
