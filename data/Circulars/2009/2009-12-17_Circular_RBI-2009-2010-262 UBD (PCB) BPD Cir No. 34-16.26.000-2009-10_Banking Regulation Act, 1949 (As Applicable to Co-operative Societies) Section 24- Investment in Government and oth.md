**Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As Applicable to Co-operative Societies)** **Section 24- Investment in Government and other approved securities by** **Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)**

Please refer to our circulars UBD.BR.Cir.19/16.26.00/2001-02 dated October 22, 2001 and <u>UBD(PCB) CO BPD Cir No:28/16.26.00/2008-09</u> dated November 26, 2008 on the captioned subject.

2. With a view to disseminating information on the SLR status of a Government security, it has been decided that:
(i) the SLR status of securities issued by the Government of India and the State Governments will be indicated in the Press Release issued by the

Reserve Bank of India at the time of issuance of the securities; and

(ii) an updated and current list of the SLR securities will be posted on the Reserve Bank's website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>) under the link " Database on Indian Economy".
