# Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – First Rand Bank Ltd

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RESERVE BANK OFINDIA

www.rbi.org.in

RBI/2009-10/111

Ref:DBOD.No.Ret.BC.27/12.06.125/2009-10

August 3, 2009

All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - First Rand

Bank Ltd

We advise that the name of "First Rand Bank Ltd" has been included in the Second

Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification DBOD. IBD. No. 20525

/23.13.145/2008-09 dated June 12, 2009, published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill - Section

4) dated July 11, 2009.
