#### <u>Rupee Export Credit Interest Rates</u>

Please refer to our circular <u>DBOD.Dir.(Exp).BC.No.101/04.02.01/2008-09</u> dated December 16, 2008 wherein interest subvention of 2% was extended w.e.f.

01.12.2008 to 31.03.2009 on pre and post shipment rupee export credit for certain employment orientated export sectors. The scheme was, thereafter, extended for a further period upto 31.03.2010 vide our circular <u>DBOD.Dir.(Exp).BC.No.26</u> <u>/04.02.001/2009-10</u> dated July 31, 2009.
