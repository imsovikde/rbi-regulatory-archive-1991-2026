### Financial restructuring of Urban Cooperative Banks

Please refer to our circular <u>UBD.PCB.Cir.No. 39 /09.16.900/2008-09</u> dated January 23, 2009 on the captioned subject. It was prescribed vide Para 3 (vii) therein that post-restructuring, the management of the bank should be in the hands of a Board of Administrators consisting of representatives of individual depositors as well as professional bankers to ensure proper implementation of the reconstruction scheme including recovery of NPAs.
