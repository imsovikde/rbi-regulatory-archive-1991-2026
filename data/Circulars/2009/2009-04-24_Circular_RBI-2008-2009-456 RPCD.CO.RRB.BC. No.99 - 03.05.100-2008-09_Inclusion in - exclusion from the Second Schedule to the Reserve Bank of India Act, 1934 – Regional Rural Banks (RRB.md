## All Regional Rural Banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Inclusion in / exclusion from the Second Schedule to the</u>

## <u>Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)</u>

We advise that the names of 8 amalgamated Regional Rural Banks have been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification RPCD.CO.RRB.No.7430/03.05.100 /2008-09 dated January 27, 2009 published in the Extraordinary Gazette of India (Part III- Sec.4) dated March 18,

2009.
2. Simultaneously, the names of 29 erstwhile Regional Rural Banks have been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification RPCD.CO.RRB.No.7431/03.05.100/2008-09 dated January 27, 2009 published in the above Extraordinary Gazette of India.
3. Copies of the above two notifications RPCD.CO.RRB. No.7430 /03.05.100/ 2008-09 and RPCD.CO.RRB.No.7431 /03.05.100 /2008-09 dated March 18, 2009 are enclosed.
4. Please acknowledge receipt to our Regional Office concerned.
## Yours faithfully

(B.P. Vijayendra) Chief General Manager

## <u>NOTIFICATION</u>

In pursuance of clause (a) of sub section (6) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Reserve Bank of India hereby directs the inclusion in the Second Schedule to the said Act of the following Regional Rural Banks, namely:

1. Marwar Ganganagar Bikaner Gramin Bank, Pali, Rajasthan
2. Assam Gramin Vikash Bank, Guwahati, Assam
3. Sarva Uttar Pradesh Gramin Bank, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh
4. Baroda Uttar Pradesh Gramin Bank, Raebareli, Uttar Pradesh
5. Purvanchal Gramin Bank, Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh
6. Maharashtra Godavari Gramin Bank, Aurangabad, Maharashtra
7. Vidharbha Kshetriya Gramin Bank, Akola, Maharashtra
8. Chhattisgarh Gramin Bank, Raipur, Chhattisgarh
