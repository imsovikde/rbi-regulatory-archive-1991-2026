## Relaxation to trade and industry in the State of Jammu & Kashmir

## BP.BC. 118 /21.04.012/ 2000- 2001

## May 10, 2001

All Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Relaxation to trade and industry in the State of Jammu & Kashmir</u>

Please refer to circular letter DBOD No.BP.BC.17/ 21.04.012/ 99 dated August 10, 2000 extending the period of concessions/credit relaxations to the borrowers/customers in the State of Jammu & Kashmir upto 31 March 2001. It has been decided that the existing concessions/ credit relaxations to borrowers/customers in the State of Jammu & Kashmir will continue to be operative for a further period of st one year i.e. upto 31 March 2002.

2. Suitable instructions may please be issued to your controlling/branch offices in this regard.
3. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, sd/-
# Withdrawn

## (B. Mahapatra )

General Manager
