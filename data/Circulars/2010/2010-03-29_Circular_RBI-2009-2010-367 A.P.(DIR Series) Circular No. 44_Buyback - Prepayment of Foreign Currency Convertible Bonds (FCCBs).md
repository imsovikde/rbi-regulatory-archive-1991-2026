To All Category-I Authorised Dealer Banks

Madam / Sir,

## Buyback / Prepayment of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs)

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category – I (AD Category – I) banks is invited to the <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 39</u> dated December 08, 2008 and <u>A.P.</u> <u>(DIR Series) Circular No. 65</u> dated April 28, 2009 on the captioned subject. In terms of <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 58</u> dated March 13, 2009, Indian companies were allowed to buyback their Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs) both under the automatic route and approval route until December 31, 2009. The Scheme was discontinued with effect from January 1, 2010.

(DIR Series) Circular No.39 dated December 08, 2008 and A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No.65 dated April 28, 2009.
