## <u>Electronic Funds Transfer Infrastructure in India – Usage of RTGS and NEFT</u>

Please refer to our circular <u>DPSS (CO) RTGS No. 1008 / 04.04.002 / 2010-2011 dated</u> <u>November 3, 2010</u> on the captioned subject.

2. Based on requests received from the participant member banks, it is clarified that the revised customer charges for the transactions routed through RTGS are applicable for amounts starting from ` 2 lakhs and above.
