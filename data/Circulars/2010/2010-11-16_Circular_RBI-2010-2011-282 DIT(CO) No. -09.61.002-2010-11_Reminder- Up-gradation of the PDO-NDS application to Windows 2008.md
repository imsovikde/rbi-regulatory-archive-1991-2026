# Reminder: Up-gradation of the PDO-NDS application to Windows 2008

# भारतीय िरज़वर् बैंक RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

www.rbi.org.in

RBI/2010-11/282 DIT(CO) No. /09.61.002/2010-11 November 16, 2010

## To All NDS Members

Dear Sir, <u>Reminder: Up-gradation of the PDO-NDS application to Windows 2008</u> Kindly refer to our circular DIT(CO) Circular NO. /09.61.002/2009-10 dated November 30, 2009 on up-gradation of PDO-NDS System to Windows 2008 environment wherein the specification for hardware and software required for the up-gradation was given and members were advised to up-grade the system.

2. The new setup and the installation document are uploaded on the RBI Secured Website. Members are requested to complete the up-gradation process by December 31, 2010.
3. You are requested to give us the status report for up-gradation at the earliest by <u>email</u>.
4. Please acknowledge receipt of this circular by return e-mail. Yours faithfully, (K Nikhila) Deputy General Manager
