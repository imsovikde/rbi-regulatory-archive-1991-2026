# Participation in Currency Options

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2010-11/205

## DNBS (PD) CC No. 199 / 03.10.001/ 2010-11 September 16, 2010

To, All non-deposit taking NBFCs with asset size of Rs. 100 crore and above (NBFCs-ND- SI) Dear Sirs, <u>Participation in Currency Options</u> Reserve Bank had issued guidelines to banks on trading in currency options in <u>recognised stock/new exchanges on July 30, 2010.</u>

2. Accordingly, it has been decided that NBFCs may participate in the designated currency options exchanges recognized by SEBI **as clients,** subject to RBI (Foreign Exchange Department) guidelines in the matter, **only for the purpose of hedging their** **underlying forex exposures.** Appropriate disclosures may be made regarding transactions undertaken in the Balance sheet.
Yours faithfully,

(Uma Subramaniam) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
