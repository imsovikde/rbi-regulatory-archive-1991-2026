## <u>ANNEXURE</u>

(Enclosure to DGBA Circular No.RBI/2010/ dated March 4, 2010 <u>Reporting of March Transactions</u>

Beginning from April 1, 2010, the Nodal/Focal Point branches will segregate on a daily basis all scrolls/challans pertaining to March 2010 received from the receiving branches concerned and prepare separate main scrolls for:

(a) scrolls for transactions of March 2010 or earlier period (i.e. effected during the previous financial year 2009-10) and
(b) scrolls pertaining to current transactions (i.e. those effected from April 1, 2010 onwards ).
2. The main scrolls for March 2010 transactions prepared from April 1 to April 15, 2010 are to be <u>distinctly marked as March Residual - 1, March Residual - 2</u> and so on upto April 15, 2010. In other words, serial number should be allotted in consecutive order for each <u>main</u> scroll of March 2010 transactions sent from April 1 to April 15, 2010. These scrolls alongwith the copies of daily summary of Receipts and Payments prepared separately for March 2010 transactions will be forwarded to the Departmental Officials concerned (i.e. Zonal Accounts Officers/Pay and Accounts Officers and Designated Officers) in the usual way. <u>The Nodal/Focal Point</u> <u>branches will also be required to reportWithdrawnthe above transactions to the Link Cell</u> <u>through separate Daily Memos.</u> These advices must be sent by telegrams/FAX to enable the Link Cell of each bank at Nagpur, to make daily settlement with Reserve Bank of India, Central Accounts Section (CAS) Nagpur. On receipt of advices (Memo/Telegrams/FAX) from the Nodal/Focal Point branches, the Link Cell should <u>segregate the advices for the March Residual transactions</u> and forward them <u>separately</u> to Reserve Bank of India, CAS, Nagpur for being processed on the computer. <u>This procedure should continue upto and inclusive of April 15, 2010 only.</u> All transactions reported thereafter by the receiving branches will be reported and accounted for in the usual manner in the accounts of the month of report irrespective of the date of transaction.
