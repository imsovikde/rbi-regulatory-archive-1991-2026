## <u>NOTIFICATION</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred under the sub-section (1) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and in partial modification of the earlier notification <u>DBOD.No.Ret.BC.70/12.01.001/2009-10</u> dated January 29, 2010, the Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies that the average Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) required to be maintained by every Scheduled Commercial Bank shall be 6.00 per cent of its net demand and time liabilities from the fortnight beginning April 24, 2010.
