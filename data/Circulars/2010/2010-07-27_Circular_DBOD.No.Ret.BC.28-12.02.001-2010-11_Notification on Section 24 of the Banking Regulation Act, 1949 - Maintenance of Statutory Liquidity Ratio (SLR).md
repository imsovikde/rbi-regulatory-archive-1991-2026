## NOTIFICATION

In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2A) of Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (10 of 1949) and in partial modification of the Notification DBOD.No.Ret. BC. 40/12.02.001/2009-10 dated September 08, 2009, the Reserve Bank hereby specifies that the following proviso shall be inserted after clause (c) of the said Notification, namely, –

“Provided that the securities (including margin) referred to above, if acquired under the Reserve Bank– Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF), shall not be treated as an eligible asset for this purpose.”

## (Anand Sinha) Executive Director
