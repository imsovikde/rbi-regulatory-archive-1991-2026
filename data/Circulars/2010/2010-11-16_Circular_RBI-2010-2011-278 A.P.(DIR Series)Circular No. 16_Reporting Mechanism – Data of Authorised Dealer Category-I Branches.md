## Category-I banks (Directory of the Scheduled Commercial Banks)

i) The website address is <u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in/](http://dbie.rbi.org.in/)</u> ii) After logging on the website, either select the tab ‘Data Series’ or click on the link “To view Data Series – click here”. iii) In order to select the following options, Double click on the same. Select the option 'Corporate Categories' Æ Reports ÆBy subject Æ Financial sector Æ Money and Banking Æ Banking. Double click on the report titled **“Directory of AD Category Branches".** Select the required Bank name from the list and click on the **‘>’** box. The name of the bank should appear on the right hand panel. Now, click on the button “Run Query”. The list of the AD Category branches pertaining to that particular bank will get generated. Use the navigation keys to go to the next page. In case the file is to beWithdrawn saved, then click on the tab “Document” Æ Save report to my computer. The report can be saved either in PDF or in Excel format. iv) The report can also be seen by selecting the tab ‘Data Series’ then the tab ‘Corporate Categories'ÆBy Frequency Æ Daily ÆFinancial sector Æ Banking. Click on the report titled as **“Directory of AD Category Branches".** Then follow the procedure mentioned in para iii above.
