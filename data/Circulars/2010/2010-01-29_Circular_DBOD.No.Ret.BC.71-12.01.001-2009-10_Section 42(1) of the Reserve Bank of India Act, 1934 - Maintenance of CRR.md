## RBI/2009-10/296

Ref: DBOD.No.Ret.BC. 71/12.01.001/2009-10 January 29, 2010 All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding Regional Rural Banks)

Dear Sir,

## Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Maintenance of CRR

Please refer to our Circular <u>DBOD. No. Ret. BC.103/12.01.001/ 2008-09</u> dated January 02, 2009 on the captioned subject.

2. On the basis of the current macroeconomic assessment, as set out in the <u>Third</u> <u>Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2009-10</u> issued on January 29, 2010, it has been decided to increase the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) for Scheduled Commercial Banks by 75 basis points from 5.00 per cent to 5.75 per cent of their net demand and time liabilities (NDTL) in two stages, effective the fortnights as indicated below:
## Effective date CRR on net demand and time liabilities

## (i.e., the fortnight beginning from) (per cent)

February 13, 2010 5.50 February 27, 2010 5.75

# Withdrawn

3. A copy of the relative notification DBOD. No. Ret. BC.70/12.01.001/2009-10 dated January 29, 2010 is enclosed.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours faithfully

## (P. K. Mahapatra) General Manager
