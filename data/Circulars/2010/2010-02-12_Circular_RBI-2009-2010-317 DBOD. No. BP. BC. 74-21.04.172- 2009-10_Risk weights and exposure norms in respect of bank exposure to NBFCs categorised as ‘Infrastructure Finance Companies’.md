3. A reference is also invited to paragraph 8 of the Master Circular on Bank Finance to Non Banking Financial Companies (<u>DBOD. BP.BC. No. 5/21.04.172/2009-10 dated July</u> <u>1, 2009</u>) in terms of which prudential ceilings for exposure of banks to NBFCs have been prescribed. In this regard, it is advised that the exposure of a bank to the IFCs should not exceed 15 per cent of its capital funds as per its last audited balance sheet, with a provision to increase it to 20 per cent if the same is on account of funds on-lent by the IFCs to the infrastructure sector.
