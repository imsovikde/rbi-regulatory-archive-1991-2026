# Investment in SLR Securities by Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

भारतीय िरज़वर् बैंक **___________________________RESERVE BANK OF INDIA________________________** <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

RBI/2009-10/391 RPCD.RRB.BC.No.68 /03.05.34/2009-10 April 9, 2010

## The Chairman All Regional Rural Banks/Sponsor Banks

Dear Sir,

## <u>Investment in SLR Securities by Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)</u>

Please refer to our <u>circular RPCD.RRB.BC.No.94/03.05.34/2008-09</u> dated March 26, 2009 on the above subject.

2. On a review of the issue it has been decided that the exemption granted to RRBs up to the financial year 2008-09 from 'mark to market' norms in respect of their investments in SLR securities be extended by one more year i.e. for the financial year 2009-10. Accordingly, RRBs will have the freedom to classify their entire investment portfolio of SLR securities under 'Held to Maturity' for the financial year 2009-10 with valuation on book value basis and amortisation of premium, if any, over the remaining life of securities.
# Withdrawn
