# Revised Subsidiary General Ledger (SGL) Account Guidelines and Constituents' Subsidiary General Ledger (CSGL) Account Guidelines

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2021-22/107 IDMD.CDD.No.S931/11.22.001/2021-2022 October 5, 2021

## All SGL/CSGL Holders

Madam/Sir, **Revised Subsidiary General Ledger (SGL) Account Guidelines and** **Constituents' Subsidiary General Ledger (CSGL) Account Guidelines** In exercise of powers conferred by Section 4 of Government Securities Act 2006, Reserve Bank of India has issued and gazetted revised <u>Eligibility Criteria and</u> <u>Operational Guidelines for opening and maintaining of Subsidiary General Ledger</u> <u>(SGL) Accounts and Constituents’ Subsidiary General Ledger (CSGL) Accounts dated</u> <u>September 22, 2021</u>. These Guidelines have been issued in supersession to earlier <u>Guidelines dated October 29, 2018</u>.

Yours faithfully,

(Rajendra Kumar) Chief General Manager
