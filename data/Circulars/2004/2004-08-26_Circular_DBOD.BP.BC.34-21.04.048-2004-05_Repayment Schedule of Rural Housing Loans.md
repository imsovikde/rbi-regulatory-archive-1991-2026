Dear Sir,

Repayment schedule of rural housing loans

As announced by the Hon'ble Finance Minister in the Union Budget 2004-05, it has been

decided to revise the norms for repayment for rural housing loans extended by banks under

Indira Awas Yojana and Golden Jubilee Rura

tousing Finance Scheme by linking

repayment to the crop cycles. Accordingly

advise that while fixing the repayment

schedule in case of rural housing adva

granted to agriculturists under Indira Awas

Yojana and Golden Jubilee Rural

Finance Scheme, banks may ensure that the

interest/ instalment payable on such advances are linked to crop cycles.
