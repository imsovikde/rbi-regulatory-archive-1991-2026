# Gold Card Scheme for Exporters

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI / 2004-2005/ 56 DBOD.IECS.No. 13 /04.02.02 (Gold Card)/2004-05 July 20, 2004 The Chairman / Chief Executives of all Commercial Banks Dear Sir <u>Gold Card Scheme for Exporters</u> Please refer to our letter IECD.No.12/04.02.02/Gold Card/2003-04 dated May 18, 2004 on the captioned subject, enclosing a copy of the Gold Card Scheme. It has been reported that some banks have fixed certain minimum annual turnover for issuance of Gold Card, as an eligibility criterion. As the objective is to extend this Scheme to all credit worthy exporters, including those in SME segment, to simplify credit access, banks are advised not to prescribe such a criterion for eligibility for the Gold Card Scheme.

# Withdrawn

Yours faithfully

(A. Sreekumaran) General Manager
