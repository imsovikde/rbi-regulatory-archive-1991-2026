# Concurrent Audit - Serious irregularities

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI / 2004 / 111

UBD. No. BSD. IP. 39 /12.05.01/ 2003-2004 March 20, 2004 The Chief Executive Officers of All Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks Dear Sir, <u>Concurrent Audit – Serious irregularities</u> Please refer to our circular UBD No. BPD.CIR. 37 /09.06.00 / 2002-03 dated March 6, 2003 advising all Urban Co-operative Banks to introduce concurrent audit.

2. We advise that if any serious irregularities are brought out in the Concurrent Audit Report by the Concurrent Auditors, the bank should immediately report the matter to the respective Regional Office of Reserve Bank of India together with the details of action taken to rectify the same.
3. Please acknowledge receipt of this circular to our Regional Office. Yours faithfully, (A.V.Sabhapathy) Deputy General Manager
