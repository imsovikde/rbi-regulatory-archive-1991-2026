## Dear Sir

## <u>Sale of Investments held under Held to Maturity (HTM) category</u>

In terms of our <u>Master Circular No. DBOD.BP.BC.18/21.04.141/2010-11 dated July</u> <u>1, 2010</u> on ‘Prudential Norms for Classification, Valuation and Operation of Investment Portfolio by Banks’, securities acquired by banks with the intention to hold them up to maturity may be classified Withdrawnunder Held to Maturity (HTM) category. Banks are, however, allowed to shift investments to/from HTM with the approval of the Board of Directors once a year. Such shifting is normally allowed at the beginning of the accounting year and no further shifting to/from HTM is allowed during the remaining part of that accounting year.
