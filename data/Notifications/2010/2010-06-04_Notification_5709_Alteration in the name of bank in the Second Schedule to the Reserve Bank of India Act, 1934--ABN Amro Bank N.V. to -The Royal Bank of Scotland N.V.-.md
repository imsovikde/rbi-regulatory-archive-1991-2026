# Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934-"ABN Amro Bank N.V. to "The Royal Bank of Scotland N.V."

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

www.rbi.org.in RBI/2009-10/484

## Ref.DBOD.No.Ret.BC/ 106/12.007.054/2009-10 June 04, 2010

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

**Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank** **of India Act, 1934-"ABN Amro Bank N.V. to "The Royal Bank of Scotland**

**N.V."** We advise that the name of "ABN Amro Bank N.V." has been changed to "The Royal Bank Of Scotland N.V." in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification DBOD.IBD.No./16083/23.13.020/2009-10 dated March 19, 2010, published in the Gazette of India (Part III- Section 4) dated April 10, 2010. Yours faithfully,
## (P. K. Mahapatra)
