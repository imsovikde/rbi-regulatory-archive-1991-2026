2. RRBs are required to update the consolidated list of individuals/entities as

circulated by Reserve Bank and before opening any new account, it should be

ensured that the name/s of the proposed customer does not appear in the list.

or linked to any of the entities or individuals included in the list.

to the Order issued by the Government.
