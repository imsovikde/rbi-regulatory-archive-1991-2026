# PMRY - Manipur -Cut-off Date for Disbursement of Loan Extended

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005-06/144 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 37/09.04.01/2005 –2006 August 26, 2005

The Chairman / Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir, PMRY – Extension of Cut-Off date for completion of disbursement of loans for the <u>programme year 2004-05 - for the state of Manipur</u>

Please refer our circular RBI/2005-06/88/RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No.25 /09.04.01/2005–2006 dated August 01, 2005.

2. Ministry of Agro & Rural Industries, Government of India has since decided to extend the cut-off date for lapsing of sanction and completion of disbursement for the cases sanctioned under the Programme Year 2004-05 for the state of Manipur from August 31, 2005 to December 31, 2005.
3. You may issue necessary instructions to your Regional /Controlling Offices/ Branches in this regard.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(G.P.Borah) Deputy General Manager
