Undertaking

1. On your acceptance of my/our bid,
i) I/we agree/undertake to immediately collect the letter of acceptance from your office and to deposit the requisite amount at Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai on the day/time as indicated therein. ii) I/We authorise you to debit my/our Current account maintained with Reserve Bank of India with the requisite amount.
2. I/We have read the terms and conditions for non-competitive bidding contained in the ‘Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities’ for the auction to be held on the captioned date and undertake to abide by them.
