The Chairmen & Managing Directors All Lead Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

## Formation of seven new districts in the State of Manipur -

## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Manipur vide Gazette Notification dated December 8, 2016 had

notified the creation of seven new districts in the State of Manipur. It has been

decided to assign the lead bank responsibility of the seven new districts as detailed

below:-
