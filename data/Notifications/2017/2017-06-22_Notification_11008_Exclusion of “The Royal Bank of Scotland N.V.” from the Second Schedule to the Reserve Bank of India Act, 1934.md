<u>Exclusion of “The Royal Bank of Scotland N.V.” from the Second</u> <u>Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “**The Royal Bank of Scotland N.V.**” has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.9999/23.13.020/2016-17 dated February 28, 2017, and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated May 06 – May 12, 2017.
