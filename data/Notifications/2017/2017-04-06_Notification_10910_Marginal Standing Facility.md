# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-2017/269 FMOD.MAOG. No.119/01.18.001/2016-17 April 6, 2017

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Madam / Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u> As announced in the <u>First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18 Resolution of the</u> <u>Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)</u>, consequent upon the narrowing of the LAF corridor, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate now stands adjusted at **6.50 per cent**. All other terms and conditions of the current MSF scheme will remain unchanged.

## Yours sincerely

(Radha Shyam Ratho) Chief General Manager
