## Trade Repository for OTC Foreign Exchange and Interest Rate Derivatives

Attention of Authorised Dealer Category – I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to <u>circular no.FMD.MSRG.No.75/02.05.002/2012-13 dated March 13, 2013</u> on the captioned subject wherein a threshold of USD 1 million, and equivalent thereof in other currencies, was stipulated for reporting FCY-INR and FCY-FCY forward and options trades between AD Category-I banks and their clients to the Trade Repository (TR). Subsequently, Clearing Corporation of India Limited (CCIL), in consultation with Reserve Bank on June 02, 2016, had informed its members the removal of this threshold limit for reporting FCY-INR and FCY-FCY option trades
