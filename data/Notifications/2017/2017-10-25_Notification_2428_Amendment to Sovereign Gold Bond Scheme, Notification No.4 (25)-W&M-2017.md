# Amendment to Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme, Notification No.4 (25)-W&M/2017

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government of India Ministry of Finance Department of Economic Affairs

New Delhi, dated the October 25, 2017

## NOTIFICATION

## <u>Amendment to Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme, Notification No.4 (25)-W&M/2017</u>

**1. GSR —** In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (iii) of section 3 of the Government Securities Act, 2006 (38 of 2006), the Central Government hereby amends the conditions specified in clause 13 of the <u>Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme</u> notified vide <u>Notification No. F.</u>
th <u>4 (25)-W&M/2017 dated 06 October 2017</u> [Notification No. GSR 1225(E)]

2. In place of clause 13 of the original notification the following shall be substituted: **“13. Eligibility for Statutory Liquidity Ratio –** Bonds acquired by the banks through the process of invoking lien/hypothecation/pledge alone, shall be counted towards Statutory Liquidity Ratio.”
