# Inclusion of “Au Small Finance Bank Limited” in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2017-18/86 DBR.No.Ret.BC.93/12.07.150/2017-18 November 09, 2017

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

<u>Inclusion of “Au Small Finance Bank Limited” in the Second Schedule of</u> <u>the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “Au Small Finance Bank Limited” has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.NBD.(SFB-AFL).No.2689/16.13.216/2017-18 dated September 18, 2017 and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated November 01,

2017.
## Yours faithfully

(M.G.Suprabhat) Deputy General Manager
