# Exclusion of “Commonwealth Bank of Australia” from the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2017-18/85 DBR.No.Ret.BC.95/12.07.150/2017-18 November 09, 2017

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

<u>Exclusion of “Commonwealth Bank of Australia” from the Second Schedule of</u> <u>the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “**Commonwealth Bank of Australia**” has been excluded from the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.2223/23.13.127/ 2017-18 dated September 05, 2017, and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated October 28- November 03, 2017.

## Yours faithfully

(M.G.Suprabhat) Deputy General Manager
