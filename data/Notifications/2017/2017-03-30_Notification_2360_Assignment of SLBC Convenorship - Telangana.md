# Assignment of SLBC Convenorship - Telangana

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-17/261 FIDD.CO.LBS.BC. No. 25/02.01.001/2016-17 March 30, 2017

The Chairmen and Managing Directors SLBC Convenor Banks

Dear Sir,

## Assignment of SLBC Convenorship-Telangana

As per the Gazette of India Notification G.S.R. 157 (E) dated February 22, 2017, the merger of State Bank of Hyderabad with State Bank of India has been notified. The Order called the ‘Acquisition of State Bank of Hyderabad Order, 2017’ comes into effect on April 1, 2017.

2. Therefore, it has been decided to assign the SLBC convenorship of the State of **Telangana to State Bank of India**.
3. There is no change in the SLBC convenorship of the other States.
## Yours faithfully

(Ajay Kumar Misra) Chief General Manager
