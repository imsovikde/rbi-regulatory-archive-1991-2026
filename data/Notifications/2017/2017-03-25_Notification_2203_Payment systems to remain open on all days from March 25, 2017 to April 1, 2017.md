# Payment systems to remain open on all days from March 25, 2017 to April 1, 2017

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-17/257 DPSS.CO.CHD.No./2695/03.01.03/2016-17

March 25, 2017

The Chairman and Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer All Scheduled Commercial Banks including Regional Rural Banks/ Urban Co-operative Banks / State Co-operative Banks / District Central Co-operative Banks/Local Area Banks

**Payment systems to remain open on all days from March 25, 2017 to April 1, 2017**

A reference is invited to the <u>circulars DPSS.CO.CHD.No./2656/03.01.03/2016-17 dated</u> <u>March 23, 2017</u> on “Special Clearing operations on March 30 and 31, 2017” and <u>DBR.No.Leg.BC.55/09.07.005/2016-17 dated March 24, 2017</u> on “All Agency Banks to remain open for public on all days from March 25, 2017 to April 1, 2017”.

2. With a view to facilitate accounting of all the Government transactions for the current financial year (2016-17) by March 31, 2017, it has been decided that all payment systems, including RTGS and NEFT would operate, as on a normal working day, during the period March 25 to April 1, 2017 (including Saturday, Sunday and all holidays). Besides, the extended timings on March 30 & 31, 2017 for the centralised payment systems viz., RTGS & NEFT, have already been a advised to the member banks via a broadcast message.
## Yours faithfully

(Nanda S. Dave) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
