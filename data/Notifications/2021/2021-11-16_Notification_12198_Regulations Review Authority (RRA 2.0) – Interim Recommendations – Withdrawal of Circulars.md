# Regulations Review Authority (RRA 2.0) – Interim Recommendations – Withdrawal of Circulars

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

RBI/2021-22/129 November 16, 2021 FMRD.DIRD.09/14.03.059/2021-22

To,

## All Eligible Market Participants

Madam/Sir,

## Regulations Review Authority (RRA 2.0) – Interim Recommendations –

## Withdrawal of Circulars

Reference is invited to the announcement on setting up of a new Regulations Review Authority (RRA 2.0), vide <u>press release dated April 15, 2021</u> and the publication of the interim recommendations of the RRA 2.0, vide <u>press release dated November 16</u>,

<u>2021</u>.
2. As part of the implementation of the interim recommendations of the RRA 2.0, it has been decided to withdraw the following circulars with immediate effect.
a) Introduction of Credit Default Swaps for Corporate Bonds: Date for Operationalisation Changed <u>(IDMD.PCD. No.12/14.03.04/2011-12) dated October</u> <u>20, 2011</u>.
b) Guidelines on Credit Default Swaps (CDS) for Corporate Bonds- Permitting All India Financial Institutions <u>(IDMD.PCD.4085/ 14.03.04/2011-12) dated April 23,</u>
<u>2012</u>.
Yours faithfully,

(Dimple Bhandia) Chief General Manager
