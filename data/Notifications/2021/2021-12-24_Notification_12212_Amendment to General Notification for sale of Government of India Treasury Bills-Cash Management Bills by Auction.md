RBI/2021-22/143 CO.IDMD.GBDP.No.S1565/08-01-001/2021-2022 December 24, 2021

All participants in the Government Securities market. Madam/Sir,

## Amendment to General Notification for sale of Government of India Treasury

## Bills/Cash Management Bills by Auction

The terms and conditions for Sale of Government of India Treasury Bills/Cash Management Bills by Auction were notified by the Government of India vide <u>General Notification No.</u>

<u>F.No.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated March 27, 2018</u> along with the <u>Amendment Notification</u> <u>No.F.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated April 05, 2018</u>.
2. Government of India have now issued the <u>Amendment Notification No.F.4(2)-</u> <u>W&M/2018 dated December 23, 2021</u> notifying the amendment to the above mentioned General Notification for sale of Government of India Treasury Bills/Cash Management Bills by Auction dated March 27, 2018, a copy of which is enclosed. Yours faithfully, (Dr. Sudarsana Sahoo) General Manager
