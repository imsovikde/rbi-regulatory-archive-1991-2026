## 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018 - Amendments

Please refer to our <u>Circular RBI/2017-18/114 IDMD.CDD.No.1671/13.01.299/2017-18</u> <u>January 03, 2018</u>, informing you about the issuance of 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018 with effect from January 10, 2018 in terms of <u>GoI notification F.No.4(28) - W&M/2017</u> <u>dated January 03, 2018</u>.

2. The GoI, has now issued a revised Notification F.No.4(28) - W&M/2017 dated January 08, 2018 (<u>copy attached</u>), notifying amendments to their original <u>Notification dated January</u> <u>03, 2018</u>, the contents of which are self-explanatory. Receiving Offices may take note of these amendments while accepting subscriptions under the Scheme.
