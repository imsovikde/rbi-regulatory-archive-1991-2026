# Inclusion of “SBM Bank (India) Limited” in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2018-19/91 DBR.No.Ret.BC.14/12.16.006/2018-19 December 06, 2018

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

<u>Inclusion of “SBM Bank (India) Limited” in the Second Schedule of the</u> <u>Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “SBM Bank (India) Limited” has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.3645/23.13.043/2018-19 dated October 30, 2018 and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated December 01 – December 07,

2018.
## Yours faithfully

(M.G.Suprabhat) Deputy General Manager
