All Scheduled Commercial Banks Dear Sir/Madam **Alteration in the name of "Qatar National Bank SAQ" to** "**Qatar National Bank** **(Q.P.S.C.)" in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934** We advise that the name of "Qatar National Bank SAQ" has been changed to "Qatar National Bank (Q.P.S.C.)" in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification DBR.IBD.No.191/23.03.032/2018-19 dated July 06, 2018 published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated August 18 – August 24, 2018.

## Yours faithfully

(M.G. Suprabhat) Deputy General Manager
