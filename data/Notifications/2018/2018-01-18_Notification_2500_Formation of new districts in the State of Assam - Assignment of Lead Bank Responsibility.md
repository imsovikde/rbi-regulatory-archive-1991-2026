## Formation of new districts in the State of Assam -<u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Assam vide Gazette Notifications dated January 25, 2016,

February 26, 2016 and August 5, 2016 had notified the creation of eight new districts

in the State of Assam. It has been decided to assign the lead bank responsibility of

the new districts as detailed below:-
