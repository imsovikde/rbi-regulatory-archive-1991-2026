# Change in name of “The Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” to “GS Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2017-18/196 DCBR.RAD.(PCB/RCB) Cir. No.08/07.12.001/2017-18 June 14, 2018

All Commercial Banks, Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs) State Co-operative Banks (StCBs) and District Central Co-operative Banks (DCCBs)

## Dear Sir/ Madam

**Change in name of “The Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” to “GS** **Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” in the Second Schedule to the** **Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934**

We advise that the name of “The Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” has

been substituted with “GS Mahanagar Co-operative Bank Limited, Mumbai” in the

Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification DCBR.RAD.

(PCB). Not. No.07/08.27.008/2017-18 dated March 25, 2018 published in the Gazette of

India (Part III-Section 4) dated May 19, 2018.

Yours faithfully,

(Neeraj Nigam) Chief General Manager
