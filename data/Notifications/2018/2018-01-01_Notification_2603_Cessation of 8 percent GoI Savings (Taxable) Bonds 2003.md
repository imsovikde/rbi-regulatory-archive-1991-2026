# Cessation of 8 percent GoI Savings (Taxable) Bonds 2003

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government of India

## Ministry of Finance

## Department of Economic Affairs

## Budget Division, (W&M Section)

## New Delhi, January 01, 2018

## <u>Notification</u>

## <u>Cessation of 8 percent GoI Savings (Taxable) Bonds 2003</u>

No.F.4(10)-W&M/2003 : The Government of India, hereby notifies that the 8 percent GoI Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2003 as per <u>Notification F.4(10)-W&M/2003, dated March 21, 2003</u> shall cease for subscription with effect from the close of business on Tuesday, the 2 nd January, 2018.

## By Order of the President of India

## (Prashant Goyal) Joint Secretary (Budget)
