# GOI Notification - 7.95% - 2032

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government of India

## Ministry of Finance

## Department of Economic Affairs

## Budget Division

New Delhi, dated August 1, 2008 **NOTIFICATION** <u>Auction for Sale (Re-issue ) of ‘7.95 per cent Government Stock, 2032</u>

F. No.4 (2 )-W&M/2008 (i) : Government of India hereby notifies sale (reissue) of ‘7.95 per cent Government Stock, 2032’(hereinafter called 'the Stock') for an aggregate amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal). The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in this notification (called ‘Specific Notification’) as also
th the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification F. No.4 (9)–W&M/2000, dated 6 May 2002 issued by Government of India as amended from time to time.

## <u>Method of Issue</u>

2. The Stock will be sold through Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 in the
th manner as prescribed in paragraph 5.1 of the General Notification F. No.4 (9)-W&M/2000, dated 6 May <u>2002 by a price based auction using uniform price auction method.</u>

## <u>Allotment to Non-competitive Bidders</u>

3. The Government Stock up to 5 % of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the enclosed Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities (**Annexure**).
## <u>Place and date of auction</u>

4. The auction will be conducted by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 on August 8, 2008. The application form duly filled in with the bids should be submitted to the aforesaid office on August 8, 2008, by 12.30 P.M.
## <u>When Issued Trading</u>

5. The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India.
## <u>Tenure</u>

6. The Stock will be of thirty-year tenure commencing from August 28, 2002. The Stock will be repaid at par on August 28, 2032.
## <u>Date of issue and payment for the stock</u>

7. The result of the auction shall be displayed by the Reserve Bank of India at its Fort, Mumbai office on August 8, 2008. The payment by successful bidders will be on August 11, 2008, i.e., the date of re-issue. The <u>payment for the Stock will include accrued interest on the nominal value of the Stock allotted in the auction</u> <u>from the date of last coupon payment, i.e., February 28, 2008 to August 10, 2008.</u>
## <u>Interest</u>

8. Interest at the rate of 7.95 per cent per annum will accrue on the nominal value of the Stock from the date of last coupon payment and will be paid half yearly on August 28 and February 28
By Order of the President of India

(L.M. Vas) Additional Secretary to the Government of India
