## <u>Swarnjayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana- Amendments in the Guidelines</u>

Please refer to our Circular RPCD.SP.BC.03/09.01.01/2002-03 dated August 13, 2002 containing amendments in the guidelines on Swarnjayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana (SGSY) scheme. Government of India, Ministry of Rural Development, on the basis of the recommendation made by the Central Level Coordination Committee (CLCC) during its th meeting held on 24 December 2002 at New Delhi, have further amended the guidelines on SGSY which are enclosed. The salient features of the amendments are as under:

## <u>Para 3.17 Revolving Fund</u>

SHGs that are in existence for about six months and have demonstrated the potential of a viable group enters the third stage, wherein it receives the Revolving Fund from DRDA and banks as a cash credit facility. The DRDAs may release subsidy, which is equal to the group corpus with a minimum of Rs. 5000/- and a maximum of Rs. 10000/- linked with bank credit. The banks would sanction credit which would be in multiples of the group corpus and could go up to four times of the group corpus as cash credit facility based on the absorption capacity and credit worthiness of the group.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

Subsequently, if it is found that the group has not been able to reach the micro enterprise stage and requires further financial support to continue in the micro finance stage for some more time, performance of such groups may be got evaluated. In the evaluation if it is observed that the group has been successfully utilising the revolving fund, they could be considered for sanction of further doses of subsidy fund up to a maximum of Rs. 20000/- inclusive of previous doses linked with bank credit.

The group corpus would be defined as the total amount available with the group inclusive of cash with the group, amount in Savings Bank account of the group, loans outstanding against members of the group and interest earned on the loans as well as deposits.

The subsidy of Rs. 20000/- released by DRDA will be adjusted against the loan at the end of the cash credit period on the request of the group.

## <u>Para 3.21 Payment to facilitators/Self Help Promoting Institutions (SHPIs)</u>

Where banks are involved as facilitators/ SHPIs, the amount towards the cost of social mobilisation, training and capacity building of groups may be decided in the State Level2022 SGSY Committee keeping in view of the local need and requirements. Further, payment of the cost of social mobilisation should be based on the stage of development of the group. 13,
