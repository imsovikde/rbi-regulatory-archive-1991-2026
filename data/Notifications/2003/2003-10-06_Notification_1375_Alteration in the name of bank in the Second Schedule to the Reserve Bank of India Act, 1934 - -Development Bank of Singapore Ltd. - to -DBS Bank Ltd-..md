# Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - "Development Bank of Singapore Ltd. " to "DBS Bank Ltd".

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India** **Act, 1934 - "Development Bank of Singapore Ltd. " to "DBS Bank Ltd".**

DBOD.No.BC29/12.06.096/2003-04 October 6, 2003 All Scheduled Commercial Banks Dear Sirs,

## Alteration in the name of bank in the

## Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - "Development

## <u>Bank of Singapore Ltd. " to "DBS Bank Ltd".</u>

We advise that the name of "Development Bank of Singapore Ltd." has been changed to "DBS Bank Ltd." in the second schedule to Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with effect from 9th August 2003, i.e. the date on which the relative notification No. DBOD. IBS. 74/23.13.046/2003-04 dated 21st July 2003 was published in the Gazette of India ( Part III-Section 4). Yours faithfully, **(S. Oram)** **Deputy General Manger**
