## Introduction of New Software for Form A Returns-Revised version

DBOD.CO.Ret.BC. 57/12.05.005/2002-03 January 11, 2003

## To All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding Regional Rural Banks and Local Area Banks)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Introduction of New Software for Form A Returns-Revised version</u>

As you are aware, a new Software has been developed for submission of Form A returns to RBI. CDs of the revised version have already been made available to all scheduled commercial banks through our Regional Offices.

2. It has been decided to implement the package with immediate effect. Accordingly, all scheduled commercial banks may submit a soft copy of Form A returns (provisional and final) along with an authenticated hard copy in the new software format to the Regional Offices as hitherto and an additional set of all the above returns should also be furnished to the Central Office, Returns Section of this Department at the abovementioned address (E-mail address: <u>returnsco@rbi.org.in</u>).
3. This procedure may be implemented with effect from the fortnight ending 10th January
2003.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours faithfully

## (R.C.Mittal) General Manager

# Withdrawn with effect from February 18, 2022
