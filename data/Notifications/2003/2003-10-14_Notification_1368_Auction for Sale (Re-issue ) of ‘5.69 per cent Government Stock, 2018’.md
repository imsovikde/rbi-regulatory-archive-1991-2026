## Auction for Sale (Re-issue ) of ‘5.69 per cent Government Stock, 2018’

## Government of india

## Ministry of finance

## (Department of Economic Affairs)

## (Budget Division)

New Delhi, the October 14, 2003

## NOTIFICATION

## <u>Auction for Sale (Re-issue ) of ‘5.69 per cent Government Stock, 2018’</u>

No.4(8)-W&M/2003 : Government of India hereby notifies sale (reissue) of ‘5.69 per cent Government Stock, 2018’ for an aggregate amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal). The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in this notification (called ‘Specific Notification’) as also the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification F No.4 (9)– th W&M/2000,dated 6 May 2002 issued by Government of India as amended by Notification th No.4 (9)–W&M/2000 dated 20 September 2002.

## <u>Method of Issue</u>

2 The Government Stock will be sold through Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, and Mumbai- 400 001 in the manner as prescribed in paragraph 5.1 of the General Notification F th No.4 (9)–W&M/2000,dated 6 May 2002 by a <u>price based auction</u> using <u>multiple price auction</u> <u>method</u>.

## <u>Allotment to Non-competitive Bidders</u>

3. Government Stock up to 5 % of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the enclosed Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities (Annex).
## <u>Place and date of auction</u>

4. The auction will be conducted by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 on October 21, 2003. The application form duly filled in with the bids should be submitted to the aforesaid office on October 21, 2003, by 12.30 pm.
## <u>Tenure</u>

5. The Stock will be of fifteen-year tenure commencing from September 25, 2003. The stock will be repaid at par on September 25, 2018.
## <u>Date of issue and payment for the stock</u>

6. The result of the auction shall be displayed by the Reserve Bank of India at its Fort, Mumbai office on October 21, 2003. The payment by successful bidders will be on October 22, 2003, i.e., the date of issue. <u>The payment for the Stock will include accrued interest on the nominal value of</u> <u>the Stock allotted in the auction from the commencement of the tenure of the stock, i.e.,</u> <u>September 25, 2003 to October 21, 2003.</u>

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

## <u>Interest</u>

7. Interest at the rate of 5.69 per cent per annum will accrue on the nominal value of the Stock from the date of commencement of the tenure of the stock and will be paid half yearly on March 25 and September 25.
By Order of the President of India

## (D.Swarup)

## Additional Secretary (Budget)
