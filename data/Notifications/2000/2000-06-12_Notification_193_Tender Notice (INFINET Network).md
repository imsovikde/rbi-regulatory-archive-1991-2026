## Tender Notice (INFINET Network)

"Tenders are invited from various hardware, software, networking, system integration vendors for setting up of the Leased Line Network of the Indian Financial Network. The detailed tender can be collected by hand, on payment of Rs.1,000/- in cash or Demand Draft drawn in favour of IDRBT payable at Hyderabad, from the Office of The Director, IDRBT, Castle Hills, Road No.1, Masab Tank, Hyderabad - 500 057, from 09/06/2000 onwards.

Completed tenders, in all respects, must reach the Office of The Director, IDRBT, Castle Hills, Road No.1, Masab Tank, Hyderabad - 500 057, on or before 1400 hours, on 03/07/2000."
