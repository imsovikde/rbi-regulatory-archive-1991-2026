Sealed QUOTATIONS are invited for Supply, Installation, Operationalisation of Computer Systems and implementation of Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone, Firewall/NAT/Proxy server, Domain Controller, Domain Name Server, Certification Server, Internet Information Server (IIS), etc in all the Regional Offices/locations of Reserve Bank of India.

2. <u>Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone for Reserve Bank of India</u>.
2.1 Reserve Bank of India plans to implement Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone in all its Regional Offices/locations, connected through a wide area backbone network called Indian Financial Network (INFINET) based on VSATs, leased lines, ISDN, etc in a phased manner. In the first phase of implementation, setting up of Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone at Reserve Bank of India’s Central Office Building, Mumbai is already at the advanced stage of completion. In the second phase of implementation, about 22 Regional Offices/locations of Reserve Bank of India in the country connected to the VSAT hub site at the Institute for Development and Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT), Hyderabad through TDM/TDMA and Hybrid VSATs of upto 64 Kbps bandwidth will be covered. Reserve Bank of India is also having X.25 Network – BANKNET, based on the Leased Lines and Department of Non-Banking Supervision, Reserve Bank of India, World Trade Centre, Mumbai has implemented WAN using ISDN lines. All the 22 Offices of Reserve Bank of India are also being connected using leased lines. IP is the protocol used on this network. Reserve Bank of India is in the process of setting up of Local Area Networks (LANs) in all its Regional Offices/locations. Sixteen centres of Reserve Bank of India to be covered presently with the implementation of the e-mail and Workflow Backbone are given in Annexure I. Six other locations specified in Annexure I where Lotus Notes based Workflow is implemented/being implemented will be connected to the proposed Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone using necessary connectors.
2.2 Each of the sixteen locations of Reserve Bank of India (Annexure II and III) will have an individual e-mail server, Firewall/NAT/Proxy Server, Domain Controller server, Internet Information Server, etc. Central server (e-mail) setup at IDRBT, Hyderabad forming hub and spokes architecture will act as a Gateway for routing e-mail. Internet e-mail will also be routed through this

3. <u>Scope of Work</u>
2.1 The Local Area Network (LAN) at all the Regional Offices/locations of the Bank specified in the Annexure I is already in place or being setup and TDM/TDMA based VSATs have already been installed. A private Class B IP address (172.16.x.x) with Fixed Length Subnet Mask is used on the LAN with private Class A IP address (10.x.x.x) with Variable Length Subnet Mask (VLSM) is used on the VSAT. The Institute for Development and Research in Banking Technology (IDRBT), Hyderabad has already installed necessary hardware and software at VSAT hub site, Hyderabad for routing Intra-Bank, Inter-bank and Internet e-mail. The Corporate e-mail and Workflow Backbone for Reserve Bank of India will be implemented on INFINET and Leased lines. Reserve Bank of India is also in the process of acquiring Routers for routing WAN traffic on VSATs as well as leased lines and ISDN lines. The scope of work is to supply, install and operationalise the Computer Systems, systems software, Microsoft server products, etc., by the vendor for implementation of e-mail and Workflow Backbone, Firewall/NAT/Proxy Server, Domain Controller, Domain Name Server, Certification Server, Key Management Server, Systems Management Server, SQL Server, Site Server, SNA Server and Internet Information Server, at the sixteen Regional Offices/locations specified in Annexure II and III and Firewall/NAT/Proxy Server at the six offices/locations of the Bank specified in Annexure IV.
2.2 The e-mail and Workflow Backbone setup at each Regional Office/location as the case may be, will be as under:
