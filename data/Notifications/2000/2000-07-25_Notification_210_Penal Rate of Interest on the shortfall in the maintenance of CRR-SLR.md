# Penal Rate of Interest on the shortfall in the maintenance of CRR/SLR

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Penal Rate of Interest on the shortfall in the maintenance of CRR/SLR

DBOD.No.BC.9/12.02.001/2000-2001 July 21, 2000 Aashadha 30, 1922(Saka)

## <u>All Scheduled Commercial Banks</u>

Dear Sir,

## Penal Rate of Interest on the <u>shortfall in the maintenance of CRR/SLR</u>

We invite your attention to circular No.MPD.198/07.01.279/2000-2001 dated July 21, 2000 issued by our Monetary Policy Department, in terms of which the Bank Rate has been increased by one percentage point from `7.0 per cent per annum' to `8.0 per cent per annum' st with effect from the close of business on 21 July 2000. Consequently, the penal rate of interest charged on the amount of shortfalls in the maintenance of Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) which is linked to Bank Rate stands revised from the st close of business on 21 July 2000.

Yours faithfully,

( R.C.Mittal ) General Manager
