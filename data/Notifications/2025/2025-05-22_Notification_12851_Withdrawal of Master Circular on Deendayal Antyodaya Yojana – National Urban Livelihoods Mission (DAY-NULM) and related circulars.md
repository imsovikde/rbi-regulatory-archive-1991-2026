## Withdrawal of Master Circular on Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Urban

## Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM) and related circulars

officially concluded on September 30, 2024. Consequently, the <u>Master Circular dated April</u>

<u>5, 2021</u> on DAY-NULM and other related circulars listed in the <u>Annex</u>, stand withdrawn

## with effect from October 1, 2024.
