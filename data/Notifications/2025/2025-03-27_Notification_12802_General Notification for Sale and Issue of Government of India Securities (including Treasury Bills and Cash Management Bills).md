# General Notification for Sale and Issue of Government of India Securities (including Treasury Bills and Cash Management Bills)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2024-25/133 Ref.No.IDMD.2320/08.01.01/2024-25 March 27, 2025

All participants in the Government Securities market.

Madam/Sir,

## General Notification for Sale and Issue of Government of India Securities (including

## Treasury Bills and Cash Management Bills)

Please refer to our <u>circulars IDMD.2592/08.01.01/2017-18 dated April 09, 2018</u>, forwarding therewith a copy of the <u>General Notification F.No.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated March 27, 2018</u> issued by the Government of India for sale of Government of India Securities, and <u>IDMD.2593/08.01.01/2017-18 dated April 09, 2018</u>, forwarding therewith a copy of the <u>General Notification F.No.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated March 27, 2018</u> issued by the Government of India for sale of Government of India Treasury Bills/Cash Management Bills by auction.

3. Government of India have now issued the <u>General Notification F.No.4(2)-B(W&M)/2018</u> <u>dated March 26, 2025</u>, for sale and issue of Government of India Securities (including Treasury Bills and Cash Management Bills) in supersession of the above-mentioned General Notifications, a copy of which is enclosed. Yours faithfully, (Dr. Sudarsana Sahoo) General Manager
