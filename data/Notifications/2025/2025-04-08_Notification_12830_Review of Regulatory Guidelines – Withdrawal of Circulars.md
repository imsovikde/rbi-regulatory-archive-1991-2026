# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> RBI/2025-26/21 DOR.CO.SOG(Leg) No.15/09.08.024/2025-26 April 8, 2025 All Scheduled Commercial Banks All Co-operative Banks Madam / Dear Sir <u>Review of Regulatory Guidelines – Withdrawal of Circulars</u> As a sequel to the recommendations of Regulation Review Authority (RRA) 2.0, a further review has been undertaken on the existing regulatory guidelines on cheques. Accordingly, the circulars listed in ‘<u>Annex</u>’ are withdrawn with immediate effect. Yours faithfully (Sunil T S Nair) Chief General Manager
