# Investment in Government Securities by Persons Resident Outside India through Special Rupee Vostro account

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

RBI/2025-26/72

A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 09 August 12, 2025 To, All Authorised Dealer Category-I banks Madam / Sir **Investment in Government Securities by Persons Resident Outside India** **through Special Rupee Vostro account** Attention of Authorised Dealer Category-I (AD Category-I) banks is invited to Schedule 1 to the Foreign Exchange Management (Debt Instruments) Regulations, 2019 notified, *vide* <u>Notification No. FEMA. 396/2019-RB dated October 17, 2019</u>, and the Foreign Exchange Management (Deposit) Regulations, 2016 notified, *vide* <u>Notification</u> <u>No. FEMA. 5(R)/2016-RB dated April 01, 2016</u> as amended from time to time and the relevant Directions issued thereunder. A reference is also invited to the <u>Master</u> <u>Direction - Reserve Bank of India (Non-resident Investment in Debt Instruments)</u> <u>Directions, 2025 dated January 07, 2025</u> (hereinafter “Master Direction”).
**2.** Persons resident outside India that maintain a Special Rupee Vostro Account (SRVA) for international trade settlement in Indian Rupees in terms of <u>A.P. (DIR</u> <u>Series) Circular No. 10 dated July 11, 2022</u> may invest their rupee surplus balance in the aforesaid account in Central Government Securities (including Treasury Bills).
**3.** Necessary operational instructions in this regard have been incorporated in the Master Direction; and the <u>updated Master Direction</u> is issued herewith.
**4.** These directions shall come into immediate effect.
**5.** AD Category – I banks may bring the contents of this circular to the notice of their constituents and customers concerned.
