Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Credit

Suisse A.G

We advise that the name of “Credit Suisse A.G" has been included in the Second

Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,1934 by notification DBOD IBD. No. 13983 /

23.03.025/2010-11 dated March 08, 2011, published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill -

Section 4) dated April 02, 2011.

Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Reserve Bank of India hereby directs the inclusion in the

Second Schedule to the said Act of the following bank namely:
