## <u>NOTIFICATION</u>

In pursuance of clause (a) of sub section (6) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Reserve Bank of India hereby directs the inclusion in the Second Schedule to the said Act of the following Regional Rural Banks, namely:

1. Utkal Gramya Bank, Orissa.
2. Ballia- Etawah Gramin Bank, Uttar Pradesh.
3. Allahabad UP Gramin Bank, Uttar Pradesh.
4. Maharashtra Gramin Bank, Maharashtra.
5. J & K Grameen Bank, Jammu & Kashmir.
Sd/- (V.K.Sharma) Executive Director
