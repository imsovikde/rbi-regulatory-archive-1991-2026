Know Your Customer Norms - Letter issued by Unique Identification Authority of India

officially valid document under Rule 2(1) (d) of the PML Rules, 2005.

2. In this connection, attention of UCBs is invited to paragraph 2.4 (d) of the Master Circular on

Know Your Customer (KYC) norms / Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards /Combating of

Financing of Terrorism (CFT) / Obligation of Banks under Prevention of Money Laundering

(PMLA), 2002 dated July 01, 2011, dealing with customer identification and it is advised that

while opening accounts based on Aadhaar also,banks must satisfy themselves about the

current address of the customer by obtaining required proof of the same as per extant

instructions.
