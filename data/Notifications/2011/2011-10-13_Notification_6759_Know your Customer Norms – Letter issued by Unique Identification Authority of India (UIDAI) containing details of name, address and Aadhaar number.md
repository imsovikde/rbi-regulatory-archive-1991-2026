as an officially valid document as contained in Rule 2(1)(d) of the PML Rules, 2005.

2.ln this regard， a reference is invited to paragraph 5 of our

circular

conditions and limitations applicable to ‘Small accounts as detailed in the Govt

notification referred to above.After further consultations with Government,ithas

nowbeendecided to accept theletterissuedbytheUiDAlas described above as an

officially valid document for opening bank accounts without the limitations

reference.
