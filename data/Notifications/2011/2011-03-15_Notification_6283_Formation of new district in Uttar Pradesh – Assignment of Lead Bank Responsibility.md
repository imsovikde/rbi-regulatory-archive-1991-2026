Formation of new district in Uttar Pradesh -

Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility

The Revenue Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh vide their Gazette Notification

No. 1858/1-5-2010-181-2002T.C.-Rev.-5 Lucknow dated July 01, 2010 has advised

about the constitution of a new district viz. Chhatrapati Shahuji Maharaj Nagar with

effect from July 01, 2010. The new district, with its headquarter at Gauriganj, has been

carved out from the existing Raebareli & Sultanpur districts and comprises Tiloi, Salon,

Amethi, Gauriganj and Jagdishpur talukas.
