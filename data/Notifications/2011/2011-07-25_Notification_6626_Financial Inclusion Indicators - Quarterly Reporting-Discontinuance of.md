# RBI/2011-12/122 RPCD. FID. BC.No.13 /12.01.011/2011-12 July 22, 2011

The Chairman/Managing Director/ Chief Executive Officer All Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

## Dear Sir/ Madam

## Financial Inclusion Indicators: Quarterly Reporting-Discontinuance of

Please refer to our Circular RPCD. MFFI. No 11324/12.01.011/2007-08 dated April 24, 2008 in terms of which you were advised to submit quarterly statements pertaining to certain financial inclusion indicators.

As the progress of Financial Inclusion Plan of banks is now being monitored through detailed monthly reports consisting of various Quantitative and Qualitative parameters, the above Quarterly Report of Financial Inclusion Indicators stands discontinued.

## Yours faithfully

(A.K.Misra) General Manager
