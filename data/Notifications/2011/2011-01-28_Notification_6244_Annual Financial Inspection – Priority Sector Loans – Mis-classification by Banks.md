## <u>Annual Financial Inspection – Priority Sector Loans – Mis-classification by Banks</u>

The Annual Financial Inspection conducted by the Department of Banking Supervision, Reserve Bank of India, inter-alia, reports cases of misclassification of loans under priority sector and/or its sub-sectors.

**2.** It has been decided that henceforth the amount of loans wrongly classified under priority sector identified and reported by Principal Inspecting Officers (PIOs) during Annual Financial Inspection of banks will be taken into account for arriving at the shortfall under priority sector lending targets.
**3.** Accordingly, to begin with, such misclassifications reported during the current year will be added to the shortfall reported by banks as on the last reporting Friday of following year, for allocation to various funds.
