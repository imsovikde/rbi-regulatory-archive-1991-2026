Formation of nine new Districts in the State of Chhattisgarh -

Allotmentof Lead Responsibility

The Revenue Department, Government of Chhattisgarh vide their Notification No. F-

6-292/Seven-3/2011 dated December 27, 2011 (Serial Number 332) had notified the

formation of nine new districts in Chhattisgarh State viz. Sukma, Kondagaon,

Balodabazar, Gariyaband, Bemetara, Balod, Mungeli, Surajpur and Balrampur with

effect from January 01, 2012. It has been decided to assign the lead bank

responsibilities of the new districts as detailed below:-
