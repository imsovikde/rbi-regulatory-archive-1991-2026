# Reporting of OTC Call/Notice/Term Money transactions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2012-13/221 FMD.MSRG. No.71/02.02.001/2012-13 September 25, 2012

The Chairmen/Chief Executives of All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs) / Co-operative Banks / Primary Dealers

## Dear Sir/Madam

## Reporting of OTC Call/Notice/Term Money transactions

The Reserve Bank of India is in the process of implementing a core banking solution. With the implementation of the core banking solution, the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) would not be available for reporting of OTC Call/Notice/Term Money transactions.

2. In this context, it may be mentioned that all the OTC Call/Notice/Term money deals, which are presently being reported over NDS, will be reported over the reporting platform of NDS-Call by the parties who are having NDS-Call membership from November 1, 2012. Parties who are not having membership of NDS-Call are advised to report the deals to FMD either through <u>e-mail</u> or through fax (022-22630981) in the Reporting format given in Annex II of Call/Notice Money Master Circular dated July 2, 2012.
3. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours sincerely, (G. Mahalingam) Chief General Manager
