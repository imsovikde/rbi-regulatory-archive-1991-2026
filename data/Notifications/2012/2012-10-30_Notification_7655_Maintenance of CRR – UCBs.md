## Section 42(1) of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –

## <u>Maintenance of Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR)</u>

Please refer to our <u>Circular UBD (SCB).Cir.No.1/12.03.000/2012-13 dated September</u> <u>17, 2012</u> on the captioned subject.

2. As set out in the Reserve Bank's <u>Press Release 2012-2013/ 713 dated October 30,</u> <u>2012</u>, it has been decided to reduce the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) of Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks by 25 basis points from 4.50 per cent to 4.25 per cent of their Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) with effect from the fortnight beginning November 03, 2012.
3. A copy of the relative notification UBD.BPD.(SCB).No. 3 /12.03.000/2012-13 dated October 30, 2012 is enclosed.
4. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, (A.Udgata) Chief General Manager-in-Charge Encl: 1

# भारतीय रज़व बक

## <u>Notification</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred under the sub-section (1) of Section 42 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and in partial modification of the earlier <u>notification</u> <u>UBD.BPD(SCB).DIR.No. 2 /12.03.000/2012-13 dated September 17, 2012</u> the Reserve Bank of India hereby notifies that the average Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) required to be maintained by every Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Bank shall be 4.25 per cent of its net demand and time liabilities, from the fortnight beginning November 3,

2012. (S. Karuppasamy) Executive Director
