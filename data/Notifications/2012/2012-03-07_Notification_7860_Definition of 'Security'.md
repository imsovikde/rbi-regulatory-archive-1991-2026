# Definition of 'Security'

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## (Foreign Exchange Department)

## (CENTRAL OFFICE)

## Mumbai

## Notification No.FEMA.223/2012-RB March 7, 2012

## Definition of 'Security'

In pursuance of clause (za) of Section 2 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) (FEMA), the Reserve Bank notifies Indian Depository Receipts (IDRs) as defined under clause © of sub-rule (i) of Rule 3 of Companies (Issue of Indian Depository Receipts) Rules, 2004, as "security" for the purposes of FEMA.

## ( Meena Hemchandra)

## Chief General Manager-in-Charge
