Assignmentof LeadBankresponsibility-

Newly formed district of Bhim Nagar in the State of Uttar Pradesh

The Government of Uttar Pradesh vide their Gazette Notification No. 2876/1-5-2011-

154/2011-R-5 dated September 28, 2011 has advised about the constitution of a new

district viz. Bhim Nagar with effect from September 28, 2011. The new district has been

carved out from the existing Muradabad and Budaun districts and comprises three

Tehsils viz. Sambhal, Chandausi & Gunnaur.

2. It has been decided to assign the lead bank responsibility of the new district Bhim

Nagar to Syndicate Bank. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of other

districts in the State.

3. The newly formed district Bhim Nagar has been allotted District working code No. 317

for the purpose of reporting.
