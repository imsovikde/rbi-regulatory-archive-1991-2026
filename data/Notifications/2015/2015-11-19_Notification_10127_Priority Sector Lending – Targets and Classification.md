# Priority Sector Lending – Targets and Classification

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/241 FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.13/ 04.09.01/2015-16 November 18, 2015

The Chairman/ Managing Director Chief Executive Officer [All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding Regional Rural Banks)]

Dear Sir/ Madam,

## <u>Priority Sector Lending – Targets and Classification</u>

It was communicated vide our <u>Circular No. FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.08/04.09.01/2015-16</u> <u>dated July 16, 2015</u> on the captioned subject, that the system-wide average of the last three years achievement with regard to overall direct lending to non-corporate farmers will be notified shortly, and henceforth at the beginning of each year.

2. It is hereby brought to the notice of all concerned that the applicable system wide average figures for computing achievements under priority sector lending for the FY 2015-16 is 11.57 percent.
3. The foreign banks with more than 20 branches will continue to be guided by their action plans as submitted by them and approved by RBI. Yours faithfully, (T V Rao) General Manager
