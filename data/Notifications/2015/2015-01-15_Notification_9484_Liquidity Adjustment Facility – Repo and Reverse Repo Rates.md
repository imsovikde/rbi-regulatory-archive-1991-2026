# Liquidity Adjustment Facility – Repo and Reverse Repo Rates

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2014-2015/403 FMOD.MAOG. No. 104 /01.01.001/2014-15 January 15, 2015

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs), Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks and Standalone Primary Dealers

Dear Sir, <u>Liquidity Adjustment Facility – Repo and Reverse Repo Rates</u> <u>As announced by the Governor today</u>, it has been decided to reduce the Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 8.00 per cent to **7.75 per cent** with immediate effect.

2. Consequent to the change in the Repo rate, the Reverse Repo rate under the LAF will stand adjusted to **6.75 per cent** with immediate effect.
3. All other terms and conditions of the current LAF Scheme will remain unchanged.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours sincerely

(M. Rajeshwar Rao) Chief General Manager
