## Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –

## Apna Sahakari Bank Ltd, Mumbai

We advise that the name of “Apna Sahakari Bank Ltd., Mumbai" has been included

in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification

DCBR.CO.BPD.01/16.05.000/2015-16 dated July 28, 2015 published in the

Gazette of India vide (Weekly No. 36 - Part III-Section 4) dated September 5,

2015. (Scenta Joy) General Manager
