# Auction for Sale (Re-issue) of 7.88 per cent Government Stock 2030

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government of India

## Ministry of Finance

## Department of Economic Affairs

## Budget Division

New Delhi, dated July 20, 2015 NOTIFICATION

## <u>Auction for Sale (Re-issue) of 7.88 per cent Government Stock 2030</u>

F. No.4 (2) W&M/2015(i): Government of India hereby notifies sale (reissue) of “7.88 per cent Government Stock 2030’ (hereinafter called 'the Stock') for an aggregate amount of ` 6,000 crore (nominal). The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in this notification (called ‘Specific Notification’) as also the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification F. No. 4 (13)–W&M/2008, dated October 8, 2008 issued by Government of India.
## Method of Issue

2. The Stock will be sold through Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 in the manner as prescribed in paragraph 5.1 of the General Notification F. No. 4 (13)– W&M/2008, dated October 8, 2008 by a <u>price based auction using Multiple price auction</u> <u>method</u>**.**
## Allotment to Non-competitive Bidders

3. The Government Stock up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the enclosed Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities (**Annex**).
## Place and date of auction

4. The auction will be conducted by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 on **July 24, 2015**. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **July 24, 2015**. The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 11.30 a.m. and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon.
## When Issued Trading

5. The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India.
## Tenure

6. The Government Stock will be of **’14 years 10 months and 8 days’** tenure commencing from **May 11, 2015**. The Stock will be repaid at par on **March 19, 2030**.
## Date of issue and payment for the stock

7. The result of the auction shall be displayed by the Reserve Bank of India at its Fort, Mumbai Office on **July 24, 2015**. The payment by successful bidders will be on **July 27,** **2015** i.e. the date of re-issue. <u>The payment for the Stock will include accrued interest on</u> <u>the nominal value of the Stock allotted in the auction from the date of original issue i.e.,</u> <u>May 11, 2015 to July 26, 2015.</u>
## Interest

8. Interest at the rate of **7.88** per cent per annum will accrue on the nominal value of the Stock from date of original issue i.e. May 11, 2015 to September 18, 2015 which will be paid on September 19, 2015 and the interest thereafter will be payable half-yearly on **March 19** and **September 19.**
By Order of the President of India

(Dr. Rajat Bhargava) **Joint Secretary to the Government of India**
