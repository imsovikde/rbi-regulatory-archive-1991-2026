Committee's Al Qaida Sanctions List and UNsCR 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List-

Modification in the Listing Format

A reference is invited to our circulars issued from time to time advising about amendments

/additions/ deletions to UNSCR 1267(1999) /1989 (2011) Al Qaida Sanction list and UNSCR

1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List.

2.Government of India, Ministry of External Affairs has now advised that the United Nations

Sanction Committee has adopted a new listing format which is designed for all Sanctions

Committees. The key modification in the new list is a newer permanent referencing system

that isharmonized with all other United NationsSanctionLists.
