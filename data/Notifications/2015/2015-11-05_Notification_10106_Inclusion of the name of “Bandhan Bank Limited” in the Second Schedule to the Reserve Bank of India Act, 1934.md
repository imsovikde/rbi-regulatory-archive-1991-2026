## Inclusion of the name of “Bandhan Bank Limited” in the

## Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

We advise that the name of **“Bandhan Bank Limited”** has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.PSBD. No.3427/16.01.0145/2015-16 dated September 03, 2015 published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated September 24, 2015.
