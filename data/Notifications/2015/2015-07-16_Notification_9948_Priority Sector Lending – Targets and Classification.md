## <u>Priority Sector Lending – Targets and Classification</u>

Please refer to our <u>Master Circular No. FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.04/04.09.01/2015-16</u> <u>dated July 1, 2015</u> on the above subject.

2. The target for direct lending by banks to agriculture under Priority Sector Norms has aimed to increase the flow of credit directly to farmers. Direct lending to the most disadvantaged farmers, the small and marginal farmers, has been around 6 percent of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (or Credit Equivalent Amount of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure, whichever is higher). In an effort to increase direct lending to agriculture, the target for direct lending to small and marginal farmers under the recently revised Priority Sector Norms has been increased to 7 percent for 2015-16 and to 8 percent for 2016-17. Furthermore, a variety of corporate loans have been precluded from getting direct lending status. This should ensure that overall direct lending to agriculture, including medium and large farmers, will increase.
