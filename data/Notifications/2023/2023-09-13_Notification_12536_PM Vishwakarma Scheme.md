## PM Vishwakarma Scheme

Government of India (GoI) has introduced the ‘PM Vishwakarma Scheme’ which aims to provide

support to artisans and craftspeople to enable them to move up the value chain in their respective

trades. The Scheme envisages, among other measures, credit support to the beneficiaries at

concessional interest rate, with interest subvention support by GoI.

2**.** In this regard, eligible lending institutions may refer to the Scheme <u>guidelines</u> issued by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, for appropriate action. Yours faithfully, (Nisha Nambiar) Chief General Manager
