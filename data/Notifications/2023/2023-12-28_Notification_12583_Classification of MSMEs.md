## Classification of MSMEs

Please refer to para 2.4 to 2.7 of the <u>Master Direction-Lending to Micro, Small & Medium</u> <u>Enterprises (MSME) Sector dated July 24, 2017</u> (as updated on July 29, 2022), inserted in terms of the <u>circular FIDD.MSME & NFS.BC.No.3/06.02.31/2020-21 dated July 2, 2020</u> on the new definition of MSME. The revised criteria for classification of enterprises as Micro, Small and Medium enterprises were notified by the Ministry of MSME, GoI vide <u>Gazette</u> <u>Notification S.O. 2119 (E) dated June 26, 2020</u>.
