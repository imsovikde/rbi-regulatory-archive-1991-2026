|(i)|Whether tax will be invest after 1.4.2007?|TDS on 8% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2003 deducted on existing bond is effective from 1.6.2007. Any interest holders or only those who credited or paid on 8% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2003 on or after 1.6.2007 would attract TDS if the amount of interest exceeds Rs. 10,000 for the financial year. Therefore, date of investment is not a relevant factor. TDS would, thus, apply to existing bond holders also.|
