# Formation of new districts in Jammu & Kashmir State – Assignment of Lead Bank responsibility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2007-08/160 RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No. 31/02.08.01/2007-08 October 15, 2007

The Chairman All Lead Banks (As per list)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Formation of new districts in Jammu & Kashmir State – Assignment of Lead Bank responsibility</u>

Please refer to our Circular No. RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No. 23/02.08.01/2007-08 dated September 12, 2007, about the assignment of Lead Bank responsibility on regular basis in eight newly created districts viz. Ganderbal, Bandipur, Shopian, Kulgam, Samba, Reasi, Ramban and Kishtwar in J & K State.

In partial modification therein, it is advised that Kulgam district has been carved out of Anantnag district and not Pulwana district as mentioned earlier.

2. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(A.K.Bhandari) Deputy General Manager
