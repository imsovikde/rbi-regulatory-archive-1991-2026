# Lead Bank Scheme Allocation of Lead Bank responsibility in respect of Anantnag, Budgam, Pulwana and Srinagar districts in J & K State

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2006-2007/295 RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No. 59 /02.08.01/2006-07 March 29, 2007

The Chairman All Lead Banks (As per mailing list)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Lead Bank Scheme Allocation of Lead Bank responsibility in respect</u>

## <u>of Anantnag, Budgam, Pulwana and Srinagar districts in J & K State</u>

Please refer to our Circular RPCD.No.LBS.BC. 87/02.01.01/2004-05 dated March 07, 2005 extending the period of transfer of lead responsibility in respect of Anantnag, Budgam, Pulwana and Srinagar districts to the Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd. upto March 31, 2007.

On a review, it has been decided to extend this period of temporary transfer of lead bank responsibility to Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd. for a further period of one year ending 31 March, 2008.

3. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of other districts in the State of Jammu and Kashmir.
Yours faithfully,

(R. Sebastian) General Manager
