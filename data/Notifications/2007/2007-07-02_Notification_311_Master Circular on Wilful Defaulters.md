# Master Circular on Wilful Defaulters

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2007-2008/59 DBOD No.DL.BC.12928/20.16.003/2007-08 July 2, 2007

The Chief Executives of

i) All scheduled commercial banks (excluding RRBs and LABs) and ii) All India Notified Financial Institutions Dear Sirs,
## <u>Master Circular on Wilful Defaulters</u>

As you are aware, the Reserve Bank of India has, from time to time, issued a number of circulars to banks and financial institutions (FIs) containing instructions on matters relating to wilful defaulters. In order to enable the banks/FIs to have all the existing instructions on the subject at one place, this Master Circular has been prepared. The Master Circular incorporates all the instructions/ guidelines issued on cases of wilful default, which are operational as on date. The Master Circular has also been placed on the RBI web-site ([http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)).

Yours faithfully,

(Vinay Baijal) Chief General Manager
