# Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Lord Krishna Bank Ltd

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2007-2008/164

Ref: DBOD.No.Ret.BC.36/12.06.043 /2007-08 October 23, 2007 All Scheduled Commercial Banks Dear Sir, **Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - Lord Krishna** **Bank Ltd** We advise that the name of "Lord Krishna Bank Ltd" has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification DBOD. No. PSBD. 2536/16.01.130/2007-08 dated September 6, 2007, published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated September 19, 2007.

Yours faithfully,

(B.K.Biswas) Deputy General Manager
