# GOI Notification - New Security 2017

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

## MINISTRY OF FINANCE

## (Department of Economic Affairs)

## (Budget Division)

New Delhi, dated June 29, 2007 **NOTIFICATION**

## <u>Auction for Sale of Government Stock of 10 Years</u>

F. No.4 (5)-W&M/2007): Government of India hereby notifies sale of Government Stock (securities) of 10- year tenure for an aggregate amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal). The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in this notification (called ‘Specific Notification’) as also the terms and conditions
th specified in the General Notification F. No.4 (9)–W&M/2000,dated 6 May 2002 issued by Government of India as amended from time to time.

## <u>Method of Issue</u>

2 The Stock will be sold through Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 in the th manner as prescribed in paragraph 5.1 of the General Notification F. No.4 (9)-W&M/2000,dated 6 May <u>2002 by a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.</u>

## <u>Allotment to Non-competitive Bidders</u>

3. The Government Stock up to 5 % of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the enclosed Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities (**Annexure**).
## <u>Place and date of auction</u>

4. The auction will be conducted by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 on July 6, 2007. The application form duly filled in with the bids should be submitted to the aforesaid office on July 6, 2007, by 12.30 P.M.
## <u>When Issued Trading</u>

5. The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading in accordance with the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India in its circular No. RBI /2006-07/178 dated November 16, 2006.
## <u>Tenure</u>

6. The Government Stock will be of ten years tenure. The tenure of the Stock will commence from July 9, 2007. The Stock will be repaid at par on July 9, 2017
## <u>Date of issue and payment for the stock</u>

7. The result of the auction shall be displayed by the Reserve Bank of India at its Fort, Mumbai Office on July 6, 2007. The payment by successful bidders will be on July 9, 2007, i.e., the date of issue.
## <u>Interest</u>

8 The coupon rate for the securities will be set at the cut-off yield to maturity rate decided in the auction. The interest will be payable half-yearly on January 9 and July 9.

By Order of the President of India

(L.M. Vas) Joint Secretary to the Government of India
