# Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - The Sangli Bank Ltd

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2007-2008/132

Ref: DBOD. No. Ret.BC.31 /12.06.048 /2007-08 September 7, 2007

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - The

## Sangli Bank Ltd

We advise that the name of "The Sangli Bank Ltd" has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification DBOD. No. PSBD. 11188/16.01.073/2006-07 dated May 14, 2007, published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated June 2, 2007.

Yours faithfully,

(Malvika. Sinha) General Manager
