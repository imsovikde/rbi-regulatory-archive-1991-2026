## Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026

Please refer to <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Securitisation</u> <u>Transactions) Directions, 2025</u>.

2. On a review, and in exercise of the powers conferred by the section 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, the Reserve Bank being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby issues the Amendment Directions hereinafter specified.
3. The Amendment Directions shall come into effect from April 01, 2027.
4. The Amendment Directions modify the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks</u>
<u>- Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025</u> as under:
(1) Paragraph 27 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “27. The issuance, holding and subsequent transfer of securitisation notes under these Directions shall only be in a dematerialised form. The minimum ticket size shall be ₹1 crore at the time of issuance as well as at any subsequent transfers. The originator shall ensure that the agreement with the SPE requires the latter to put in place necessary arrangements with entities such as the registrar, depository, stock exchange etc., as may be applicable, to ensure compliance with the minimum ticket- size requirement. Provided that securitisation notes with amortisation structures shall be permitted to be traded at amortised value, if the ticket size falls below ₹1 crore.
