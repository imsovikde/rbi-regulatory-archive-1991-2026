## Formation of new districts in the Union Territory of Ladakh –

## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Administration of Union Territory (UT) of Ladakh has notified formation of five new districts,

viz., Sham, Nubra, Changthang, Zanskar and Drass in the UT of Ladakh vide Gazette Notification

SG-LD-E-27042026-1712 dated April 27, 2026. Accordingly, it has been decided to designate the

Lead Bank of the new districts as below:
