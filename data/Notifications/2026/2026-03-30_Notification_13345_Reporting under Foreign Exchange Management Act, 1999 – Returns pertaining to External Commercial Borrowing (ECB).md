(i) Form ECB 1 and Revised Form ECB 1 (known as Form ECB and Revised Form ECB respectively prior to notification of <u>Foreign Exchange Management</u> <u>(Borrowing and Lending) (First Amendment) Regulations, 2026</u>) shall be treated as returns which do not capture flows. Late Submission Fee for delayed submission of these forms shall be computed accordingly; (ii) LSF amount is per return.
