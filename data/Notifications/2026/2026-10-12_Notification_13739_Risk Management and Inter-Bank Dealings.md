### Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings

Attention of Authorised Dealers is invited to <u>Master Direction-Risk Management and</u> <u>Inter-Bank Dealings dated July 05, 2016</u>, as amended from time to time.

2. In terms of Para 2.3 (iii) of Section I of Part A of the Master Direction *ibid*, Authorised Dealer Category-I banks with an operating IFSC Banking Unit and Standalone Primary Dealers authorised as Authorised Dealer Category-III may offer non deliverable derivative contracts (NDDCs) involving INR to non-resident users without any restriction in terms of purpose. On a review, it has been decided that henceforth, Authorised Dealers shall offer NDDCs involving INR to a non-resident user only for the purpose of hedging.
3. Further, it has been decided that:
i. An Authorised Dealer shall be required to maintain a Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) for foreign exchange derivative contracts involving INR undertaken by it for hedging the currency risk of its own assets and liabilities; ii. The FERR shall be applicable to all foreign exchange derivative contracts involving INR where the Authorised Dealer is purchasing foreign currency against INR;
iii.
