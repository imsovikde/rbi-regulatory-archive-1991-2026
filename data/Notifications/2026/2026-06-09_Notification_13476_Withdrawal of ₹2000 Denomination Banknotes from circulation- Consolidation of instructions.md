# Withdrawal of ₹2000 Denomination Banknotes from circulation: Consolidation of instructions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2026-27/107 DCM(Plg) No.S736/10.02.060/2026-27 June 09, 2026

The Chairman / Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer All Banks

Madam / Dear Sir,

## Withdrawal of ₹2000 Denomination Banknotes from circulation: Consolidation of instructions

The ₹2000 denomination banknote was introduced in <u>November 2016</u> under Section 24(1) of the RBI Act, 1934. Thereafter, in pursuance of the “Clean Note Policy” of the Reserve Bank of India, on <u>May 19, 2023</u>, it was decided to withdraw ₹2000 denomination banknotes from circulation.

2. Facility for exchange/ deposit of the ₹2000 denomination banknotes was extended to members of the public through bank branches across the country till October 07, 2023. Since October 09, 2023, the facility for exchange/ deposit of the ₹2000 banknotes is available for individuals / entities at the 19 RBI Issue Offices¹, and the same shall be available till further advice. ₹2000 denomination banknotes continue to be legal tender.
3. This consolidates the instructions issued earlier on the subject, as detailed hereunder².
## Yours faithfully

(Sanjeev Prakash) Chief General Manager-In-Charge
