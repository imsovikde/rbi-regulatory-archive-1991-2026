## “D1. Income Recognition in case of acquisition of Specified Non-Financial

## Assets (SNFA)

*40C. Any accrued but unrealised interest and / or charges from the extinguished* *exposure pertaining to periods prior to acquisition of an SNFA, shall not be* *recognised as income upon acquisition of the SNFA. Where such income has* *been recognised in respect of any SNFA outstanding in the books of a NBFC* *as on September 30, 2026, it shall be reversed through Profit and Loss account,* *latest by September 30, 2027, to the extent remaining unrealised as on that* *date.*
