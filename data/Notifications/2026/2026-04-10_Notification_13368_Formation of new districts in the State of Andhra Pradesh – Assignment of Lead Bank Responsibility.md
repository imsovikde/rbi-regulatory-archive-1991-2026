## <u>Formation of new districts in the State of Andhra Pradesh –</u>

## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Andhra Pradesh has notified formation of two new districts, viz., Polavaram,

and Markapuram in the state of Andhra Pradesh vide Gazette Notification No. G.O. MS. No. 517

Revenue (Lands-IV) Department and G.O. MS. No. 524 Revenue (Lands-IV) Department,

respectively, both dated December 30, 2025. Accordingly, it has been decided to designate the

Lead Bank of the new districts as below:

## Sr Newly Created Lead Bank Responsibility District Working Code

## No District assigned to allotted to new district

02X 1 Polavaram Union Bank of India (to be read as ‘Numeral Zero, <u>Numeral Two and Alphabet X’)</u> 02Y 2 Markapuram Union Bank of India (to be read as ‘Numeral Zero, <u>Numeral Two and Alphabet Y’)</u>

2. There is no change in the Lead Banks of the other districts in the state of Andhra Pradesh. Yours faithfully, (Nisha Nambiar) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
