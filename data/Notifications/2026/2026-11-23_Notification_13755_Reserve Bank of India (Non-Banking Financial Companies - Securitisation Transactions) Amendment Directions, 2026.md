## Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies-Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026

Please refer to <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies-</u> <u>Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025</u>.

2. On a review, and in exercise of the powers conferred by the Sections 45JA, 45K, 45L, 45M and 45MA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (Act 2 of 1934), Sections 30A, 32 and 33 of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987, Sections 3, 31A and 6 of the Factoring Regulation Act, 2011 (Act 12 of 2012), the Reserve Bank being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby issues the Amendment Directions hereinafter specified.
3. The Amendment Directions shall come into effect from April 01, 2027.
4. The Amendment Directions modify the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking</u> <u>Financial Companies-Securitisation Transactions) Directions, 2025</u> as under:
(1) Para 26 shall be substituted by the following, namely: “26. The issuance, holding and subsequent transfer of securitisation notes under these Directions shall only be in a dematerialised form. The minimum ticket size shall be ₹1 Crore at the time of issuance as well as at any subsequent transfers. The originator shall ensure that the agreement with the SPE requires the latter to put in place necessary arrangements with entities such as the registrar, depository, stock exchange etc., as may be applicable, to ensure compliance with the minimum ticket- size requirement.
