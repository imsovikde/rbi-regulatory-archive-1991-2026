Inter-Governmental Agreement (IGA) with United States of America (US) under

Foreign Accounts Tax Compliance Act (FATCA)- Registration

Government of India (Gol), has advised that India and US have reached an

agreement in substance on the terms of an Inter-Governmental Agreement (IGA) to

implement FATCA and India is now treated as having an IGA in effect from April 11,

2014. However, IGA would be signed only after the approval of Cabinet.
