# Levy of foreclosure charges/pre-payment penalty on Floating Rate Term Loans

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**भारतीय ररज़र्व बकैं** **-----------------RESERVE BANK OF INDIA------------------** **www.rbi.org.in** RBI/2013-14/612 RPCD.CO.RCBD.RRB.BC.No.102/07.51.013/2013-14 May 27, 2014

The Chairman All State and Central Co-operative Banks (StCBs and CCBs) All Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)

## Dear Sir/Madam

## <u>Levy of foreclosure charges/pre-payment penalty on Floating Rate Term Loans</u>

Please refer to our <u>circulars RPCD.CO.RCBD.BC.No.84/03.03.01/2011-12 dated</u> <u>June 15, 2012</u> addressed to StCBs and CCBs and <u>RPCD.CO.RRB.BC.No.85/</u>

<u>03.05.033/2011-12 dated June 18, 2012</u> addressed to RRBs on ‘Home Loans-Levy of Fore-closure Charges/ Pre-payment Penalty’.
2. A reference is invited to <u>Part B of the First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement</u> <u>2014-15 announced on April 1, 2014</u> proposing certain measures for consumer protection. It was indicated that in the interest of their consumers, banks should consider allowing their borrowers the possibility of prepaying floating rate term loans without any penalty. Accordingly, it is advised that StCBs, CCBs and RRBs will not be permitted to charge foreclosure charges/ pre-payment penalties on all floating rate term loans sanctioned to *individual borrowers*, with immediate effect. Yours faithfully, (A. Udgata) Principal Chief General Manager
