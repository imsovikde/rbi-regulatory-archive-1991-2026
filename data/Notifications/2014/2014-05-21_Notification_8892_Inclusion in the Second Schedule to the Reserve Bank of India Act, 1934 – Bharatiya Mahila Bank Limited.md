# Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – Bharatiya Mahila Bank Limited

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2013-14/599 Ref: DBOD. No.Ret. BC.112/12.07.138/2013-14 May 21, 2014

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

## Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –

## Bharatiya Mahila Bank Limited

We advise that the name of **“**Bharatiya Mahila Bank Limited**"** has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification DBOD.BP. No.15936/21.07.002/2013-14 dated March 25, 2014, published in the Gazette of India (Part III – Section 4) dated April 12, 2014.

Yours faithfully,
