# Liquidity Adjustment Facility- Reverse Repo

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2013-14/451 FMD.MOAG. No.95/01.01.001/2013-14 January 17, 2014 All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs) and Standalone Primary Dealers Madam / Sir, <u>Liquidity Adjustment Facility- Reverse Repo</u> Based on the feedback received from the market participants, it has been decided to revise the timings of <u>Reverse Repo window under Liquidity Adjustment Facility</u> <u>(LAF)</u>. The Reverse Repo window will now be available between 7.00 pm and 7.30 pm instead of the existing timings of 4.45 pm to 5.15 pm. The change in timings will <u>take effect from January 20, 2014 (Monday)</u>.

2. All other terms and conditions of the current Reverse Repo scheme under LAF will remain unchanged.
Yours sincerely (G. Mahalingam) Principal Chief General Manager
