# RRBs - Levy of penal charges on non-maintenance of minimum balances in inoperative accounts

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2013-14/611 RPCD.RRB.BC.No.105/03.05.33/2013-14 May 27, 2014

The Chairmen All Regional Rural Banks

Dear Madam/Sir,

## Levy of penal charges on non-maintenance of minimum balances in inoperative accounts

Please refer to <u>Part B of the First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2014-15 announced on</u> <u>April 1, 2014</u>, proposing certain measures towards consumer protection such as non-levy of penal charges for non-maintenance of minimum balance in any inoperative accounts.

2. In this connection, a reference is invited to para 4.4 of our <u>circular</u> <u>RPCD.CO.RRB.BC.No.100/03.05.33/2013-14 dated May 12, 2014</u> on Customer Service in Regional Rural Banks wherein RRBs were advised to inform customers regarding the requirement of minimum balance in savings bank account and levy of charges for non- maintenance of the same at the time of opening the account in a transparent manner.
3. In terms of para 3 of our <u>circular RPCD.CO.RRB.RCB.BC.No.24/07.38.01/2012-13 dated</u> <u>August 22, 2012</u> on ‘Financial Inclusion- Access to Banking Services-Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts’ RRBs were advised that no charge should be levied for non- operation/activation of Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDAs).
4. It is advised that, henceforth RRBs are not permitted to levy penal charges for non- maintenance of minimum balances in any inoperative account. Yours faithfully, (A.Udgata) Principal Chief General Manager
