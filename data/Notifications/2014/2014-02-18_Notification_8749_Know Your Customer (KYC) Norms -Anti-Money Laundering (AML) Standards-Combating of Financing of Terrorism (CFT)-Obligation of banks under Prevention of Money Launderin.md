## Section 13(2) – Primary (Urban) Cooperative Banks (UCBs)

Please refer to our <u>Master Circular UBD.BPD. (PCB).MC.No.16/12.05.001/2013-14</u> <u>dated July 1, 2013</u> on Know Your Customer (KYC) Norms / Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards/Combating of Financing of Terrorism (CFT)/Obligation of banks under PMLA, 2002.

2. *(a) issue a warning in writing; or*
*(b) direct such reporting entity or its designated director on the Board or any* *of its employees, to comply with specific instructions; or*
