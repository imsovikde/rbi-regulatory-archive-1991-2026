# Auction for Sale (Re-issue) of 1.44% Inflation Indexed Government Stock 2023

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government of India

## Ministry of Finance

## Department of Economic Affairs

## Budget Division

New Delhi, dated January 22, 2014 NOTIFICATION

## <u>Auction for Sale (Re-issue) of 1.44% Inflation Indexed Government Stock 2023</u>

F. No.4 (16) W&M/2013: Government of India hereby notifies sale (re-issue) of **1.44%** <u>Inflation Indexed Government Stock-2023</u> (securities) for an aggregate amount of `500 crore (nominal). The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions spelt out in this notification (called ‘Specific Notification’) as also the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification F. No. 4 (13)–W&M/2008, dated October 8, 2008 issued by Government of India.
## Method of Issue

2. The Stock will be sold through Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 in the manner as prescribed in paragraph 5.1 of the General Notification F. No. 4 (13)– W&M/2008, dated October 8, 2008 by a price based auction using uniform price auction <u>method</u>.
## Allotment to Non-competitive Bidders

3. The Government Stock up to 20% of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the enclosed Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility in the Auctions of Government Securities (**Annex**).
## Place and date of auction

4. The auction will be conducted by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 on **January 29, 2014**. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **January 29,**
**2014**. The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 11.30 a.m. and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon.
## Tenure

5. The Stock will be of ten-year tenure commencing from June 5, 2013. The Stock will be repaid at par on June 5, 2023.
## Date of issue and payment for the stock

6. The result of the auction shall be displayed by the Reserve Bank of India at its Fort, Mumbai Office on **January 29, 2014**. The payment by successful bidders will be on **January** **30, 2014** i.e., the date of re-issue. <u>The payment for the Stock will include accrued interest</u> <u>on the Indexed value of the Stock allotted in the auction from the date last coupon</u> <u>payment i.e. December 5, 2013 to January 29, 2014.</u>
## Interest

7. Interest at the rate of 1.44 per cent per annum will accrue on the indexed principal value of the Stock from the date of last coupon payment and will be paid half yearly on June 5 & December 5.The indexed principal will be calculated by multiplying the original principal value i.e., Rs 100 with the index ratio (IR).
## Price Index

8. Final Wholesale Price Index (WPI) will be used for calculating index ratio and thereby
st providing inflation protection. The monthly final WPI will be used as reference WPI for 1 day st of a calendar month and the reference WPI for the days between 1 day of two contiguous calendar months will be computed through linear interpolation. Further, monthly final WPI will

be used with a lag of four months, viz. final WPI for August 2013 and September 2013 will st st be used as reference WPI for 1 January 2014 and 1 February 2014, respectively. The **th** reference WPI for <u>29 December 2014 will be 180.59032</u> (through linear interpolation of st st reference WPI for 1 January 2014 and 1 February 2014 at 179.0 and 180.7 respectively). In case of revision in the base year for WPI series after issuance of <u>Inflation Indexed</u> <u>Government Stock</u>, new WPI index will be used for indexation purposes and the past values of new WPI index would be computed through splicing the base. The monthly data on final WPI published by the Office of the Economic Adviser to the Government of India, Ministry of Commerce and Industry will be used.

## Index ratio

9. Index ratio is computed by dividing reference WPI of the settlement/coupon date with the reference WPI of the issue date (Base index value). Index ratio for a specific date is truncated to six decimal places and rounded off to five decimal places. Accordingly, the index ratio applicable for this auction is **1.05993 (180.59032 / 170.38).**
