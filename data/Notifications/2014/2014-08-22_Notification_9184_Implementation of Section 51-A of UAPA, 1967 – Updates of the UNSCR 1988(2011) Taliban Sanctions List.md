## RBI/ 2014-15/191 DBOD.AML.No.2868/14.06.001/2014-15 August 22, 2014

## The Chairpersons / CEOs of all Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding

## RRBs)/ Local Area Banks / All India Financial Institutions

Madam/Dear Sir,

## Implementation of Section 51-A of UAPA, 1967 –

## Updates of the UNSCR 1988(2011) Taliban Sanctions List

Please refer to our <u>circular DBOD.AML.No.2472/14.06.001/2014-15 dated August</u> <u>14, 2014</u> on the captioned subject. We have since received from Government of th India, Ministry of External Affairs, UNP Division, copy of press release regarding 5 update, dated August 21, 2014 (<u>copy enclosed</u>) regarding changes made in the “1988 Sanctions List”, i.e. list of Individuals and entities linked to Taliban.

2. The press releases concerning amendments to the list are available at <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pressreleases.shtml](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pressreleases.shtml)</u> and press release
th pertaining to 5 update is available at <u>[http://www.un.org/News/Press/docs//2014/sc11530.doc.htm](http://www.un.org/News/Press/docs//2014/sc11530.doc.htm)</u>

The updated Taliban Sanctions list is also available:

a. in PDF format at <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pdf/1988List.pdf](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/pdf/1988List.pdf)</u>
b. in XML format at <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988List.xml](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988List.xml)</u>
c. in HTML format at <u>[http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988List.htm](http://www.un.org/sc/committees/1988/1988List.htm)</u>
3. Banks/All India Financial Institutions are required to update the list of individuals/entities as circulated by Reserve Bank and before opening any new account, it should be ensured that the name/s of the proposed customer does not appear in the list. Further, banks should scan all existing accounts to ensure that no account is held by or linked to any of the entities or individuals included in the list.
