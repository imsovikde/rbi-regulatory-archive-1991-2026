Dear Sirs,

Data Format for Furnishing of Credit Information to Credit Information Companies

(ClCs) and other Regulatory Measures

A committee to Recommend Data Format for Furnishing of Credit Information to Credit

Information Companies (Chairman: Shri Aditya Puri) was constituted by the Reserve

Bank of India (RBl). The Report of the Committee was placed on RBl's website on

March 22, 2014 inviting comments on the recommendations of the Committee.

2. Subsequently, the Bank issued the Circular DBOD.No.CID.BC.127/20.16.056/2013-

14 dated June 27, 2014 laying down instructions regarding the following:

i)

Creating Awareness about Credit Information Report (CiR);

ii)

Usage of ClR in all Lending Decisions and Account Opening;

ii)

Populating Commercial Data Records in Databases of all ClCs;

iv)

Standardisation of Data Format;

v)

Constitution of a Technical Working Group;

vi)

Process of Rectification of Rejected Data;

vii)

Determining Data Quality Index,

vii)

Calibration of Credit Score and Standardising Format of CIR.

ix)

Best practices for Banks/Fls.
