# Inclusion of “UBS AG” in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

# भारतीय रज़वर् बैंक RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

RBI/2024-25/65 DoR.RET.REC.36/12.07.160/2024-25 August 22, 2024

## All Commercial and Co-operative Banks

Madam / Dear Sir,

**Inclusion of “UBS AG” in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934** It is advised that “UBS AG” has been included in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DoR.LIC.No.S1371/23.03.025/2024-25 dated June 4, 2024 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated August 17 - August 23, 2024.

Yours faithfully,

(Latha Vishwanath) Chief General Manager
