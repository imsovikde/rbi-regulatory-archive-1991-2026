The Chairman/ Managing Director All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

## <u>Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana (DAY)-National Urban Livelihoods Mission</u>

Please refer to <u>Master circular FIDD.GSSD.CO.BC.No.10/09.16.03/2015-16 dated</u> <u>July 30, 2015</u> on National Urban Livelihoods Mission (NULM) Scheme.

2. With a view to improving the livelihood opportunities for the poor in urban areas, Ministry of Housing and Urban Poverty Alleviation (UPA Division), Government of India vide their <u>Office Memorandum No.K-14011/2/2012-UPA/FTS-5196 dated</u> <u>February 19, 2016</u> has decided to enhance the scope of National Urban Livelihoods Mission. The Mission with enhanced scope will be renamed as **“Deendayal** **Antyodaya Yojana (DAY)-National Urban Livelihoods Mission”.** Accordingly the amendments in the NULM Mission documents are annexed for information of the banks.
## Yours faithfully

(Uma Shankar) Chief General Manager
