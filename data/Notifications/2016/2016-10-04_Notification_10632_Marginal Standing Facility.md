# Marginal Standing Facility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2016-2017/77 FMOD.MAOG. No.115/01.18.001/2016-17 October 4, 2016

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs)

Madam / Sir, <u>Marginal Standing Facility</u> <u>As announced in the Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement today</u>, it has been decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) to reduce the Repo rate under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by 25 basis points from 6.50 per cent to **6.25 per** **cent** with immediate effect. Consequent to the change in the Repo rate, the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate will stand adjusted to **6.75 per cent** with immediate effect. All other terms and conditions of the current MSF scheme will remain unchanged.

## Yours sincerely

(M. Rajeshwar Rao) Chief General Manager
