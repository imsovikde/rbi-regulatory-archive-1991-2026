# Exclusion of “UBS AG” – from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2015-16/405 DBR.No.Ret.BC.99/12.07.124A/2015-16

## May 19, 2016

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir, **Exclusion of “UBS AG” – from the Second Schedule to the** **Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934**

We advise that the **“UBS AG”** has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.7718/

23.13.062/2015-16 dated January 12, 2016 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III Section 4) dated April 30, 2016 – May 6, 2016.
## Yours faithfully

(M.G. Suprabhat) Deputy General Manager
