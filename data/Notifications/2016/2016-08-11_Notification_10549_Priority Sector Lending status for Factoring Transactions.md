## <u>Priority Sector Lending status for Factoring Transactions</u>

Please refer to <u>Master Direction FIDD.CO.Plan.1/04.09.01/2016-17 dated July 7,</u> <u>2016</u> on Priority Sector Lending (PSL) and Guidelines dated December 3, 2014 for setting up of and operating the Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) issued by Reserve Bank of India.

2. To increase liquidity support for the MSME sector, it has been decided that factoring transactions on ‘with recourse’ basis shall be eligible for priority sector classification by banks, which are carrying out the business of factoring departmentally. The factoring transactions taking place through TReDS shall also be eligible for classification under priority sector upon operationalization of the platform.
3.
