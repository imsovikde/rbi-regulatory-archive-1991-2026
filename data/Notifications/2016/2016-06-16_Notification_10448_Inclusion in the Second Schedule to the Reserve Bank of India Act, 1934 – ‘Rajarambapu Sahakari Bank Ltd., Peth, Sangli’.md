## Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –

## ‘Rajarambapu Sahakari Bank Ltd., Peth, Sangli’

has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,

1934 by Notification DCBR.CO.BPD.05/16.05.000/2015-16 dated May 6, 2016
