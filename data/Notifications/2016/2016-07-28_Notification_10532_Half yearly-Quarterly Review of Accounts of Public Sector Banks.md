## RBI/2016-17/29

**Ref.DBS.ARS.No.BC.2/08.91.001/2016-17** July 28, 2016

## All Public Sector Banks

Dear Sir/Madam,

## Half yearly/Quarterly Review of Accounts of Public Sector Banks

Please refer to our circular DBS.ARS.No.BC.17/08.91.001/2002-03 dated June 5, 2003 on conduct of Half yearly/Quarterly Review of Accounts of Public Sector Banks.

2. The reporting format has been revised as per the <u>annex</u>. You may, accordingly, arrange to prepare the review in the revised format as enclosed, effective from the next review date.
3. The other instructions, as amended from time to time, shall remain unchanged.
## Yours faithfully

Sd/-

(Indrani Banerjee)

# Withdrawn w.e.f. March 01, 2024
