# Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2005 - 06/ 326 DBOD.Ret.BC.No. 70 /12.06.062/2005-06 March 17, 2006

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

## Dear Sir

<u>Alteration in the name of bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve</u> <u>Bank of India Act, 1934.</u>

We advise that the name of "The Bank of Tokyo-Mitsubishi, Ltd" has been changed to "The Bank of Tokyo-Mitsubishi UFJ, Ltd" in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with effect from January 01, 2006, in terms of Notification DBOD. No.IBD.937/23.03.007/2005-06 dated December 26, 2005, which was published in the Gazette of India ( Part III-Section 4) on January 21, 2006.

## Yours faithfully

Sd/- (S.Oram) Deputy General Manager
