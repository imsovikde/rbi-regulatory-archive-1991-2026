# PMRY– Cut-Off date for lapsing of sanction and completion of disbursement of loans for the programme year 2005-06 – Further extension regarding

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI / 2005-06/415 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 91/ 09.04.01/ 2005 –2006 June 15, 2006

The Chairman / Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir, PMRY– Cut-Off date for lapsing of sanction and completion of disburse- <u>ment of loans for the programme year 2005-06 – Further extension regarding</u>

Please refer our circular RBI / 2005-06 / 336/ RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No.70/ 09.04.01 /2005 –2006 dated March 24, 2006. Considering the request of certain States for further extension of the cut off date for the programme year 2005-06 under PMRY, the Ministry of Agro & Rural Industries, Government of India has since decided to extend the cut-off date for completion of disbursement of loans against the cases sanctioned during the Programme Year 2005-06 upto June 30, 2006.

2. All implementing banks of PMRY are requested to strictly adhere to this deadline and hence complete the disbursement of all loans against the cases sanctioned during 2005-06 by June 30, 2006.
3. You may issue necessary instructions to your Regional /Controlling Offices/ Branches in this regard.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

(G.P. Borah) Deputy General Manager
