# Alteration in the name of the bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - " Chohung Bank to Shinhan Bank"

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2006-07/127 DBOD.CO.Ret.BC.No.29/12.06.105/2006-07 September 18, 2006 Bhadra 27, 1928

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

## Dear Sir

<u>Alteration in the name of the bank in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of</u> <u>India Act, 1934 - " Chohung Bank to Shinhan Bank"</u> We advise that the name of " Chohung Bank " has been changed to " Shinhan Bank " in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with effect from 12th August 2006,

i.e. the date on which the relative notification DBOD.No.IBD. 114/23.13.072/2006-07 dated July 25th 2006 was published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4).
## Yours faithfully

(S.Oram) Deputy General Manager
