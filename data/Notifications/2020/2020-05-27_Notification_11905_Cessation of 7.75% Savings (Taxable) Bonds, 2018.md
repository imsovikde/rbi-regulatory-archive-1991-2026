## Cessation of 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018

Government of India has vide <u>Notification F.No.4(28)-(W&M)/2017 dated May 27,</u> <u>2020</u> announced that the 7.75 percent Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018 shall cease **th** for subscription with effect from the close of business on Thursday, the **28 of May,**

**2020.**
2. All the Receiving Offices are advised to take note and communicate the same to their branches/dealing officers.
