**hours**

Attention of Authorised Dealers (ADs) is invited to the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Exchange Derivative Contracts) Regulations, 2000 notified vide <u>Notification No.</u> <u>FEMA.25/RB-2000 dated May 3, 2000</u>) issued under clause (h) of sub-section (2) of Section 47 of FEMA, 1999 (Act 42 of 1999), as amended from time to time and the <u>Master</u> <u>Direction- Risk Management and Inter-bank Dealing dated July 05, 2016</u>, as amended from time to time.

2. As announced in the <u>Statement of Developmental and Regulatory Policies dated</u> <u>October 04, 2019</u> it has been decided to accept the recommendation of the Task Force on Offshore Rupee Market to permit AD Cat-I banks to offer foreign exchange prices to users at all times, out of their Indian books, either by a domestic sales team or through their overseas branches.
3. Accordingly, the following section is being added in Part C (Inter-Bank Foreign Exchange Dealings) of the <u>Master Direction- Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings</u>: *“**6.
