## Basel III Framework on Liquidity Standards – Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

Please refer to our <u>circular DOR.BP.BC.No.46/21.04.098/2019-20 dated March 27,</u> <u>2020</u> on Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) guidelines.
