## Annexure I

Illustrations showing adherence by PDs to Commitments on aggregative bidding in Central Government Securities' issues and to required amount of successful bids [Ref. paragraphs 4(i) and 4(ii) of the Guidelines]

The success ratio to be maintained by the PD is 40 per cent each in respect of Dated Govt. Securities and Treasury Bills.
