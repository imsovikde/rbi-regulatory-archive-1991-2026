Dear sirs,

Minimum Net Owned Fund (NOF) for commencement <u>of business of Non-Banking Financial Institution (NBFI)</u>

With a view to imparting greater financial soundness and achieving the economies of scale in terms of efficiency of operations and higher managerial skills, the requirement of minimum NOF of Rs.25 lakhs has been raised to Rs.200 lakh for the NBFC which commences business of a non-banking financial institution on or after April 21, 1999. This stipulation will not, however, be applicable to NBFCs which are already registered with RBI or to such companies whose applications for a certificate of registration are submitted on or before April 20, 1999.

2. In sum, henceforth, any NBFC making an application to Reserve Bank of India for a certificate of registration should have NOF of Rs.200 lakhs.
3. A copy of the notification issued under the powers derived from the provisions of clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 45-IA of the RBI Act is enclosed. Yours faithfully,

## NOTIFICATION No. DNBS. 132/CGM (VSNM) - 99 dated APRIL 19, 1999.

In exercise of the powers conferred under clause (b) of the sub-section (1) of section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, (2 of 1934) Reserve Bank of India specifies that on and from April 20, 1999, no non-banking financial company shall commence the business of a non-banking financial institution without having the net owned fund of rupees two hundred lakhs.

(V. S. N. Murty) Chief General Manager
