## Collateral free loans- Small Scale Industries.

RPCD/PLNFS/ No.BC. 58 / 06.02.80/ 2001-02 23 January 2002 All Scheduled Commercial banks (Including RRBs)

Dear Sir,

## <u>Collateral free loans- Small Scale Industries.</u>

Please refer to our circular RPCD / PLNFS/ No.BC.65/ 06.02.31/99-2000 dated 3 March 2000, in terms which the exemption limit of borrowal accounts for obtention of collateral securities was raised from Rs.1 lakh to Rs.5 lakh for the <u>tiny sector.</u>

2. It has now been decided to extend the benefit under the increased limit of Rs.5 lakh <u>to</u> <u>all SSI units</u> so as to ensure flow of credit to this sector.
3. We shall be glad if you will please implement the above decision and issue necessary instructions to your controlling offices/ branches in the matter for appropriate action.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours faithfully,

**Sd-** **(Varughese John)** **General Manager**
