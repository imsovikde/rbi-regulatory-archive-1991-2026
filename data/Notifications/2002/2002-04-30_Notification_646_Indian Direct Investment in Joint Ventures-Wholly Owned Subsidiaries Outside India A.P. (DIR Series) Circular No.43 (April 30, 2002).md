## <u>Indian Direct Investment in Joint Ventures/ Wholly Owned Subsidiaries Outside India</u>

Attention of authorised dealers is invited to Foreign Exchange Management (Transfer or Issue of any Foreign Security) Regulations, 2000 notified by the Reserve rd Bank vide Notification No. FEMA.19/RB-2000 dated 3 May 2000 as amended from time to time.

2. With a view to streamlining the monitoring mechanism for overseas direct investment by Indian parties, it has been decided that authorised dealers may designate
