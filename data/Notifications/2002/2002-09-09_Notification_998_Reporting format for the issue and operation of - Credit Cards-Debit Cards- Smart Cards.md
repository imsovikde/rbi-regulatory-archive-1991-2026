## Reporting format for the issue and operation of-Credit Cards/Debit Cards/ Smart Cards

DIT. No. 440 /03.23.01/2002-2003 September 9, 2002

Dear Sir,

## <u>Reporting format for the issue and operation of-Credit Cards/Debit Cards/ Smart Cards</u>

It has been decided to collect information on the operations of Credit / Debit / Smart Cards issued by banks. Accordingly, you are advised to furnish the required monthly report in the enclosed format by e-mail to the following address: cgmincditco@rbi.org.in. The first report should cover the operations in the month of September 2002 and may be submitted in a manner, th so as to reach our office latest by 10 October 2002. Thereafter, it may be ensured that the th required monthly report is received by our office latest by 10 of the succeeding month to which it relates. If no data is to be reported even then a ‘nil’ report may be sent. Kindly acknowledge receipt.

Yours faithfully,

## (A.R. Nawab) Deputy General Manager
