## Investment portfolio of banks <u>transactions in securities</u>

Plan.PCB.CIR. 46 /09.29.00/2001-02. June 6, 2002 The Chief Executive Officers of all Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks Dear Sir/Madam, **Investment portfolio of banks** <u>transactions in securities</u>

Please refer to Para 2(ii) of our circular UBD.No.Plan.PCB.CIR.41/09.29.00/ 2001- 02 dated April 20, 2002 on the captioned subject. We clarify that, in addition to the entities mentioned in the said para, primary (urban) co-operative banks may also seek insurance companies, mutual funds and provident funds as counter parties.

Yours faithfully,

(Sudarshan Sen) General Manager
