## GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS:

1) The figures to be given should correspond to the fiscal year, viz., from April 1 to March 31.
2) Where accurate figures cannot be furnished for certain items, reasonable estimates may be provided.
3) Please use separate sheet if additional space is required for furnishing information on any item(s).
4) Please write N.A. against items, which are not applicable.
5) Please write D.N.A. against items where details are not available.
6) Item codes and country codes are for office use only.
7) In case the company has more than one office in India its registered office should send a consolidated return.

<!-- PAGE 3 -->

8) The completed Return may please be forwarded to the **Director, Division of International Trade, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy, Reserve Bank of India,** **Central Office Building (Seventh Floor), Post Box No. 1036, Mumbai** – 400 001, as early as possible but not later than July 15, 2002.
## SPECIFIC INSTRUCTIONS:

Note 1: Please indicate holdings by foreign companies only. Note 2: The information should relate to foreign currency loans directly obtained from private/official agencies abroad which involves repayment in foreign currency. All foreign currency loans obtained from Indian financial institutions should be excluded. Note 3: Please indicate the payments made for imports during the year. Note 4: Please indicate the export receipts realised during the year. Note 5: Value of total production represents sales (net of excise duty, rebate and discount) plus change in value of stock of finished goods and work-in-progress. Note 6: Gross profits are profits after depreciation but before providing for tax and interest. Note 7: Net profits = Gross profits – (Tax + Interest) Note 8: Total capital employed = Total assets – Accumulated losses Note 9: Net worth = Paid up capital + Free reserves & surplus Note 10: Examples of export restrictive clauses:

(i) Total ban on exports. (ii) Prohibition of exports to collaborator’s country. (iii) Prohibition of exports to countries in which the collaborator operates through branches/subsidiaries or is having similar collaboration agreement.
(iv) Permission of collaborator for exports.

(v) Exports only through collaborator/his agents/distributors. (vi) Prohibition on the use of trademarks for exports. (vii) Restrictions on export prices.
