## Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Account <u>by a person Resident in India) (Amendment) Regulations, 2002</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of Section 9 and clause (e) of sub-section

(2) of Section 47 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) and in partial
rd modification of its Notification No.FEMA 10/2000-RB dated 3 May, 2000, Reserve Bank of India makes the following amendments to Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a person Resident in India) Regulations, 2000, namely;

|a person Resident in India) Regulations, 2000, namely;|||
|---|---|---|
|1.|Short title and commencement (i)|These Regulations may be called the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a Person Resident in India) (Sixth Amendment) Regulations,|
||(ii)|2002. They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the official Gazette.|
|2.|Amendment to the Regulations||

In the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a Person Resident in India) Regulations, 2000 in the Schedule, in paragraph 1, in sub-paragraph (1A), in clause (ii), after the words “to a unit in Domestic Tariff Area”, the following words shall be added, namely,

“and also payments received in foreign exchange by a unit in Domestic Tariff Area for supply of goods to a unit in Special Economic Zone (SEZ).”
