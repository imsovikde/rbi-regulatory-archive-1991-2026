## Reg: Priority Sector Advances -Credit Deployment to SSI Sector -<u>Revised List of SSSBEs</u>

UBD.POT.Cir.PCB.No. 50 /09.09.01/2001-02 June 20, 2002 Jyestha 30, 1924(S) To **All Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks**

Dear Sir/Madam,

## Reg: Priority Sector Advances -Credit Deployment to SSI Sector -<u>Revised List of SSSBEs</u>

Please refer to our Circular UBD.Plan.PCB.23/09.09.01/2000-01 dated 1 January 2001 forwarding an illustrative list of activities qualifying as SSSBEs, for information.

2. The list of activities eligible for registration as Small Scale Service Business (Industry Related) Enterprises (SSSBEs) has since been revised by Government of India, Ministry of SSI. We forward herewith a revised list of activities which qualify for registration as SSSBEs, as also, list of those which do not qualify for the registration, for your information. Yours faithfully, **(R. Derhgawen)** **Dy.General Manager**
