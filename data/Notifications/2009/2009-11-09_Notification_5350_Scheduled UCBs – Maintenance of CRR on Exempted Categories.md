Please refer to our circular UBD (PCB) Cir. No. 6/12.03.000/2006-07 dated April 25,, 2007 and the Master Circular <u>UBD.CO.BSD.NSB 1/Ret/MC.No.15/12.03.000/2009-10</u> dated July 1, 2009 on the captioned subject..

2. As announced in the Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2009-10 released on October 27, 2009, it has been decided that liabilities of scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks arising out of transactions in Collaterised Borrowing and Lending Obligations (CBLO) with Clearing Corporation of India Ltd. (CCIL) shall be subject to maintenance of CRR with effect from the fortnight beginning November 21, 2009. Accordingly, net demand and time liabilities (NDTL) reckoned for CRR shall include liabilities arising out of CBLO transactions for the reporting Friday of November 6, 2009 which forms the base for computation of CRR for the fortnight beginning November 21,
