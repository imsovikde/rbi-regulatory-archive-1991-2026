# Formation of new district in Tamil Nadu –  Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2008-09/480 RPCD. CO. LBS.BC. No. 107/02.08.01/2008-09 May 22, 2009

The Chairman All Lead Banks

Dear Sir,

Formation of new district in Tamil Nadu – <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Revenue Department, Government of Tamil Nadu vide their Gazette Notification No.G.O(Ms)No.618 dated 24th October 2008 have advised about the formation of new district viz. Tiruppur carved out of the existing Coimbatore and Erode districts covering Tiruppur, Avanashi, Palladam, Dharapuram, Kangeyam and Udumalaipettai talukas, with effect from the date of Notification i.e. 24th October 2008.

2. It has been decided to assign the Lead Bank responsibility of the new Tiruppur district to Canara Bank. The Lead Bank responsibility of existing Coimbatore and Erode districts will, however, continue to be with Canara Bank.
3. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of other districts in the State.
4. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, Sd/- (Molina Chowdhury) Deputy General Manager
