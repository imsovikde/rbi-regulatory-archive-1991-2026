### Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934-American

### Express Banking Corp.

We advise that the name of “American Express Banking Corp.” has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,1934 by notification DBOD IBD.No.8739/23.03.018/2008-09 dated December 04, 2008 published in the Gazette of India (part III – section 4)dated December 27, 2008.
