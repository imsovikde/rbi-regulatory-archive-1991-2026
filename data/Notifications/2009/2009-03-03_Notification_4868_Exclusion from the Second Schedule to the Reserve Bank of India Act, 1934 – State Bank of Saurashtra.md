# Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – State Bank of Saurashtra

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RESERVE BANK OFINDIA

www.rbi.org.in

RBI/2008-09/398

Ref:DBOD.No.Ret.BC.111/12.06.007/2008-09

March 03,2009

All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir,

Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 - State

Bank of Saurashtra

We advise that the name of "State Bank of Saurashtra" has been excluded from the Second

Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by notification DBOD. No. Ret. BC.

49/12.06.007/2008-09 dated October 16, 2008, published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill -

Section4) datedNovember22,2008.

Yours faithfully,

(A. K. Singh)

Deputy General Manager
