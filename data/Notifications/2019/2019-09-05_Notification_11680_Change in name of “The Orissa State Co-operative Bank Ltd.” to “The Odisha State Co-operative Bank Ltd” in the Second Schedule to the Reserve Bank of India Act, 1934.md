# Change in name of “The Orissa State Co-operative Bank Ltd.” to “The Odisha State Co-operative Bank Ltd” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## भारतीय रज़व बक

# __________________RESERVE BANK OF INDIA____________________ <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>

**RBI/2019-20/56** Bhadrapada 1, 1941 **DCBR.RCB.No.03/19.51.025/2019-20** August 23, 2019

## All State Co-operative Banks/ Central Co-operative Banks

## Madam / Dear Sir

**Change in name of “The Orissa State Co-operative Bank Ltd.” to “The Odisha State** **Co-operative Bank Ltd” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act,** **1934**

We advise that the name of “The Orissa State Co-operative Bank Ltd.” has been substituted

with “The Odisha State Co-operative Bank Ltd.” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve

Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification DCBR.CO.RCBD.No.01/19.51.025/2016-17 dated

December 09, 2016 published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated August 10 –

## August 16, 2019.

## Yours faithfully

(Neeraj Nigam) Chief General Manager
