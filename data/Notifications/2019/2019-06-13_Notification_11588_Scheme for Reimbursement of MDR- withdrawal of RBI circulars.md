# Scheme for Reimbursement of MDR- withdrawal of RBI circulars

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

# भारतीय रज़वर् बक ________________RESERVE BANK OF INDIA __________________

**www.rbi.org.in** RBI/2018-19/210 DGBA.GBD.No.3089/43.33.001/2018-19 June 13, 2019 To All Acquiring Banks of Debit Card/ BHIM UPI/ BHIM Aadhaar Pay transactions

Dear Sir **Scheme for Reimbursement of MDR- withdrawal of RBI circulars** Please refer to our following instructions on the MDR reimbursement schemes:

|(i)|Circular no. RBI/2016-17/228|
|---|---|
||DGBA.GAD.No.2128/44.02.001/2016-17 dated February 16, 2017|
|(ii)|Circular no. RBI/2017-18/55 DGBA.GBD.No.505/31.02.007/2017-18 dated September 7, 2017|
|(iii)|Letter no. DGBA.GBD.No.2773/43.33.001/2017-18 dated May 4, 2018|
|(iv)|Letter no. DGBA.GBD.No.470/43.33.001/2018-19 dated August 23, 2018|

2. It has been decided that reimbursement of MDR claims will be handled directly by Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) w.e.f. January 01, 2019. In view of this, the above mentioned circulars/letters issued by Reserve Bank of India stand withdrawn from the date of this circular.
## Yours faithfully

(Charulatha S Kar) Chief General Manager
