## Priority Sector Targets-Lending to Non-Corporate Farmers – FY 2019-20

Please refer to our <u>Circular No. FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.08/04.09.01/2015-16 dated July</u> <u>16, 2015</u> advising, inter alia, that the system-wide average of the last three years achievement with regard to overall direct lending to non-corporate farmers will be notified every year.
