# Exclusion of “National Australia Bank” from the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2019-20/25

**DBR.No.Ret.BC.04/12.07.134/2019-20** August 01, 2019

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

## Dear Sir/Madam

<u>Exclusion of “National Australia Bank” from the Second Schedule of the</u> <u>Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934</u>

We advise that the “National Australia Bank” has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.10667/23.13.085/2018-19 dated June 24, 2019 and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated July 27, 2019 – Aug 02, 2019.

## Yours faithfully

(S M Parida) General Manager
