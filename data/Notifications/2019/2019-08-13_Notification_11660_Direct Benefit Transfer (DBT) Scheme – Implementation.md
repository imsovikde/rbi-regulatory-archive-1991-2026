# Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Scheme – Implementation

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2019-20/40 FIDD.CO.LBS.BC.No.09/02.01.001/2019-20 August 13, 2019

The Chairmen / Managing Directors & CEOs Scheduled Commercial Banks (including Regional Rural Banks), Small Finance Banks and Payments Banks

Madam / Dear Sir,

## Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Scheme – Implementation

Please refer to <u>Circular RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No.75/02.01.001/2012-13 dated May 10, 2013</u> and <u>RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No.11/02.01.001/2013-14 dated July 9, 2013</u> regarding the use of Aadhaar to facilitate delivery of social welfare benefits by direct credit to the bank accounts of beneficiaries.

2. In this connection, banks are advised to ensure that opening of bank accounts and seeding of Aadhaar numbers with existing or new accounts of eligible beneficiaries opened for the purpose of Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) under social welfare schemes, is in conformity with the provisions listed under Section 16 of the <u>Master Direction-Know Your</u> <u>Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (updated as on May 29, 2019)</u> and extant provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering (PML) Rules.
3. The above guidelines will be in supersession of <u>Circular</u> <u>FIDD.CO.LBS.BC.No.17/02.01.001/2015-16 dated January 14, 2016</u> on “Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Scheme – Seeding of Aadhaar in Bank Accounts – Clarification”. Yours faithfully, (Gautam Prasad Borah) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
