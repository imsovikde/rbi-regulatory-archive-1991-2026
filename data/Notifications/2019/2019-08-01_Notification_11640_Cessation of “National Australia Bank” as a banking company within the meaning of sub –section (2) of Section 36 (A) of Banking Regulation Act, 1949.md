# Cessation of “National Australia Bank” as a banking company within the meaning of sub –section (2) of Section 36 (A) of Banking Regulation Act, 1949

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI/2019-20/24

**DBR.No.Ret.BC.03/12.07.134/2019-20** August 01, 2019

## All Scheduled Commercial Banks

## Dear Sir/Madam

<u>Cessation of “National Australia Bank” as a banking company within the</u> <u>meaning of sub –section (2) of Section 36 (A) of Banking Regulation Act, 1949</u>

We advise that the “**National Australia Bank**” has ceased to be a banking company within the meaning of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 vide Notification DBR.IBD.No.10668/23.13.085/2018-19 dated June 24, 2019, and published in the Gazette of India (**Part III-Section 4**) dated July 27, 2019 – Aug 02, 2019.

## Yours faithfully

(S M Parida) General Manager
