## <u>Formation of new districts in the State of Andhra Pradesh –</u>

## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Andhra Pradesh has notified formation of 13 new districts in the State of

Andhra Pradesh vide Gazette Notifications No.472-497 dated April 03, 2022. Accordingly, it

has been decided to designate Lead Banks of the new districts as below:
