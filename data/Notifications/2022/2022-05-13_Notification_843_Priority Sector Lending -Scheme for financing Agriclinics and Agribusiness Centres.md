## Priority Sector Lending -Scheme for financing Agriclinics and Agribusiness Centres

## Reserve Bank of India

Rural Planning & Credit Department Central Office Central Office Building, 13th floor, Fort, Mumbai - 400 001

RPCD.PLFS.BC.NO./12/ 05.02.02 /2002-2003 31 August 2002

The Chairman/ Chairman & Managing Director Chief Executive Officer All Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs)

Dear Sir,

Priority Sector Lending -Scheme for financing Agriclinics and Agribusiness Centres -------------2022

Please refer to our circular RPCD.PLFS.BC.NO./ 18 / 05.02.02 /2001-2002 dated 31 August 13, 2001 advising banks that advances granted under the Scheme for Financing Agriclinics and Agribusiness Centres will qualify for inclusion under 'Indirect Finance to Agriculture under Priority Sector Lending'.May

2. It has since been decided to classify advances for financing Agriclinics and Agribusiness Centres as 'Direct Finance under Agriculture'. Accordingly, the finance grantedw.e.f under the above Scheme will qualify for inclusion under 'Direct Finance to Farmers for Agricultural Purposes under Priority Sector Lending'.
3. The revised classification may be taken into account while reporting the advances in the half-yearly returns on Priority Sector Lending.
# Withdrawn

4. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, Sd/- (Smt. Vani J. Sharma) Chief General Manager
