# Formation of new district in the State of Meghalaya – Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility

RBI/2021-22/184 FIDD.CO.LBS.BC.No.18/02.08.001/2021-22 March 23, 2022

The Chairman / Managing Director & Chief Executive Officer All Lead Banks

Madam/ Dear Sir,

## <u>Formation of new district in the State of Meghalaya – Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility</u>

The Government of Meghalaya vide Gazette Notification No.HPL.84/2021/253 dated

November 09, 2021 had notified formation of a new district in the State of Meghalaya. It has

been decided to assign the lead bank responsibility of the new district as under:

Sub-Division/ Tehsil **Lead Bank District Working** **Sr Newly Carved** Erstwhile under newly created **Responsibility Code allotted to** **No District** District District **assigned to new district** West Mairang Civil Sub-**00U (to be read as** **Eastern West Punjab** 1 Khasi **Khasi Hills** Division **National Bank "zero zero U")** Hills

2. Further, the District Working Code of the new district has also been allotted for the purpose
## of BSR reporting by the banks.

3. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of the erstwhile district and of other
## districts in the State of Meghalaya.

Yours faithfully,

(Sonali Sen Gupta) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
