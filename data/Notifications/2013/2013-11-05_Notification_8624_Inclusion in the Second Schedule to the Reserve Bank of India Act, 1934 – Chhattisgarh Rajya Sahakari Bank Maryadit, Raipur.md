# Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 – Chhattisgarh Rajya Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Raipur

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2013-14/357(A) RPCD.RCB.BC.No.52 /07.04.007/2013-14 November 05, 2013

**To**

## All State Cooperative Banks/District Central Cooperative Banks

Dear Sir,

## Inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 –

## Chhattisgarh Rajya Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Raipur

We advise that the name of “Chhattisgarh Rajya Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Raipur " has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification RPCD.RCB.BC.No.34/07.04.007 /2013-14 dated September 16, 2013, published in the Gazette of India vide serial no.280 (Extraordinary-Part III-Section 4) dated October 25, 2013.

Yours faithfully,

Sd/- (A. G. Ray) General Manager
