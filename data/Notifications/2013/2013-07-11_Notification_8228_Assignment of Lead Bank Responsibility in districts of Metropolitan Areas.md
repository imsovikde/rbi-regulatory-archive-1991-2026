## <u>Assignment of Lead Bank Responsibility in districts of Metropolitan Areas</u>

Please refer to <u>paragraph 70</u> of Monetary Policy Statement 2013-14, in terms of which it was decided to bring all districts in metropolitan areas under the fold of the lead bank scheme.
