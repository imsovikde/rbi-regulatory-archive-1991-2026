## <u>Priority Sector Lending-Revised Reporting System</u>

Please refer to the revised priority sector guidelines issued vide <u>circular RPCD.CO. Plan.</u> <u>BC.13/04.09.01/2012-13 dated July 20, 2012</u> wherein it was advised that separate guidelines relating to priority sector data reporting system will be issued in due course. Accordingly, based on the discussions with stakeholders, it has been decided to rationalize the monthly, quarterly and yearly statements on priority sector in supersession of our <u>circulars RPCD.No.Plan.BC. 21/04.09.01/2007-08 dated September 3, 2007</u> and <u>RPCD.CO.Plan. BC.11/ 04.09.01/2011-12 dated July 13, 2011</u>.

2. It has also been decided to discontinue the following statements with effect from April 01, 2013:

|Name of the return/statement|Description|Periodicity|
|---|---|---|
|Special Return I|Disbursal and outstanding of direct agricultural advances|Yearly. Last reporting Friday of June|
|Special Return III|Disbursal of priority sector advances|Yearly. Last reporting Friday of June|
|Outstanding credit to Medium Enterprises|Outstanding credit to Medium Enterprises|Quarterly|
|Education & Housing Loan|Outstanding amount & number of accounts to Education & Housing Loan|Quarterly|
