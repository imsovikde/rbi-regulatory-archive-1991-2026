(**Extract of Monetary Policy Satement-2013-14 announced on May 03, 2013**)

Distribution of Banknotes and Coins – Alternative Avenues

110. With a view to effectively meeting the growing demand for banknotes and coins in the country, there is a need for identification of alternative avenues for their distribution by banks. For this purpose, banks may explore the possibility of offering these services through Business Correspondents (BC) and consider engaging the services of Cash in Transit (CIT) entities for the purpose of distribution of banknotes and coins, thereby addressing the last mile connectivity issues. Detailed guidelines will be issued by end-June 2013.
