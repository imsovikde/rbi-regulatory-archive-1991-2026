## Annex

Documents to be submitted to Reserve Bank of India while applying for inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

(1) Copy of resolution passed by the Annual General Body / Board of Directors to make an application to RBI for inclusion in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 and containing the name(s) of one / two officials authorized to correspond with RBI in this regard;
(2) Major financial details of the bank together with copies of the published balance sheet for the last three years and
(3) Fortnightly balance of Demand and Time Liabilities as on each reporting Friday for the last financial year, duly certified by a Chartered Accountant.
