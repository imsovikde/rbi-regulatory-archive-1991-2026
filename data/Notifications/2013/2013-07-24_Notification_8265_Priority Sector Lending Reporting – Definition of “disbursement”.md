## <u>Priority Sector Lending Reporting – Definition of “disbursement”</u>

Please refer to the <u>circular RPCD.CO.Plan. BC 56/04.09.01/2012-13 dated January 7, 2013</u> on Priority Sector Lending –Revised Reporting System.

2. Disbursement to be reported in the monthly and yearly reporting formats is defined as under:
(i) **Cash credit/over draft account and running accounts of similar nature**: Debit summation minus interest and other charges or sanctioned limit, whichever is lower for the particular period under consideration (monthly/quarterly/half yearly/yearly). (ii) **Term Loans**: Debit summation minus interest and other charges for the particular period under consideration (monthly/quarterly/half yearly/yearly).
