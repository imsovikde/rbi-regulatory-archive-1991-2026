# Rationalisation of Returns

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2012-2013/556 June 27, 2013 MPD.PMD.BC. 365 /07.01.279/2012-13 Ashadha 6, 1935 (S) To, All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sir / Madam, <u>Rationalisation of Returns</u> Please refer to our <u>circular MPD.BC.218/07.01.279/2002-03 dated July 24, 2002</u> on the captioned subject.

2.

|At present, banks are submitting following SFR Returns/Statements.||
|---|---|
|i.|SFR II|
|ii.|SFR III|
|iii.|SFR VIII|
|iv.|Statement on daily maintenance of SLR|

3. On further rationalisation, it has been decided to discontinue submission of SFR VIII Return with effect from the fortnight beginning July 12, 2013.
4. However, bank, would continue to submit other SFR returns/statement viz. SFR II, SFR III and “statement on daily maintenance of SLR”
5. Kindly acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully, **(Michael Debabrata Patra)** **Pr. Adviser**
