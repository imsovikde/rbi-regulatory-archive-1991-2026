# PMRY – Cut off date for Completion of disbursement for the programme year 2003 –2004 – extended to 15.11.2004.

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI /2004-05/ 190 RPCD.PLNFS.BC. No. 35 / 09.04.01 /2004-2005 September 24, 2004 The Chairman/ Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs) Dear Sir, <u>PMRY – Cut off date for Completion of disbursement for the programme year</u> <u>2003 –2004 – extended to 15.11.2004.</u>

In view of the large number of cases remaining undisbursed by banks, it has been decided by the Govt. of India to extend the cut off date for completion of disbursement for the cases sanctioned for the programme year 2003 –2004 to <u>15.11.2004</u> from the present cut off date of <u>30.09.2004.</u>

2. Please issue suitable instructions to all your branches/controlling offices in this regard.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours faithfully

(G.P.Borah)
