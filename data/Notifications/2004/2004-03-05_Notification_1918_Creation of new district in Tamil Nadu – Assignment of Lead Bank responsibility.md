# Creation of new district in Tamil Nadu – Assignment of Lead Bank responsibility

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI / 2004 / 87 RPCD.CO.LBS.BC.No. 68 /02.01.01/2003-04 March 5, 2004 Phalguna 15, Saka 1925 The Chairmen All Lead Banks Dear Sir, <u>Creation of new district in Tamil Nadu – Assignment of Lead Bank responsibility</u> The Government of Tamil Nadu have, vide their notification dated November 11, 2003, created a new district viz. Krishnagiri by re-organising the existing district of Dharmapuri, with immediate effect.

2. It has been decided to assign the Lead Bank responsibility of the new district to Indian Bank.
3. There is no change in the lead bank responsibilities of other districts in the State.
## Yours faithfully

(R.S.Jain) Deputy General Manager
