# Banks advised to furnish Data on Agricultural Lending on Monthly Basis

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## <u>RBI/2004-05/298</u>

RPCD. Plan. BC. 63 /04.09.01/2004-05 December 13, 2004

## The Chairman / Managing Director

*(Private Sector Banks)*

Dear Sir/ Madam,

## <u>Data on Priority Sector Advances – Agricultural Lending</u>

As you are aware, the scheduled commercial banks are required to achieve a target of 18 per cent of net bank credit (NBC) towards lending to agriculture. The Government of India has decided to monitor/review the progress made in this regard on a monthly basis.

2. Accordingly, in order to enable us to provide the progress reports to Government of India, you are advised to furnish data on lending to agriculture <u>on a monthly basis</u> **(as** **on the last reporting Friday of each month)** to us, <u>commencing from November</u> <u>2004</u>, in the enclosed format **on or before the seventh day of the following month to** **which the data pertains.**
3. You are also advised to take appropriate steps to increase the flow of credit to agriculture sector so that the stipulated target is achieved by the end of the year 2004-
05.
4. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully,

## (N. K. Bhatia)

## Deputy General Manager

Encl: As above.
