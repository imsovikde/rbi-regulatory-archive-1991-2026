# Annual Policy Statement for the year 2004-05 : Guidelines on infrastructure financing

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI No. 2004/ 207 DBOD. BP.BC. 92 / 21.04.048 / 2003- 2004 June 16, 2004

All Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs & LABs) & All India Financial Institutions

Dear Sir,

## Annual Policy Statement for the year 2004-05 :

## Guidelines on infrastructure financing

Please refer to paragraph 1 of the Annex to Circular No. DBOD. BP. 67/ 21.04.048/ 2002-03 dated February 4, 2003 defining the scope of 'infrastructure lending'.

2. In this connection, a reference is invited to paragraph 90 of the annual policy Statement for the year 2004-2005 enclosed to the Governor’s letter No. MPD. BC. 249/ 07.01.279/ 2003-2004 dated May 18, 2004 (copy of the paragraph enclosed). On a review, it has been decided to expand the scope of definition of 'infrastructure lending' to include the following projects/sectors :
(i) construction relating to projects involving agro-processing and supply of inputs to agriculture; (ii) construction for preservation and storage of processed agro-products, perishable goods such as fruits, vegetables and flowers including testing facilities for quality; and (iii) construction of educational institutions and hospitals.
# Withdrawn

3. The revised definition of 'infrastructure lending' is furnished in Annex.
Yours faithfully,

(C. R. Muralidharan) Chief General Manager-in-Charge
