3. With a view to enabling banks to have a smooth transition to the revised norms, it has been decided that the revised prudential norms in respect of State Government guaranteed exposures may be implemented in a phased manner as under:
(a) For the year ending March 31, 2005, State Government guaranteed advance and investment in State Government guaranteed securities would attract asset classification and provisioning norms, if interest and/or principal or any other amount due to the bank remains overdue for more than 180 days.
(b) With effect from the year ending March 31, 2006, State Government guaranteed advance and investment in State Government guaranteed securities would attract asset classification and provisioning norms, if interest and/or instalment of principal or any other amount due to the bank remains overdue for more than 90 days.
