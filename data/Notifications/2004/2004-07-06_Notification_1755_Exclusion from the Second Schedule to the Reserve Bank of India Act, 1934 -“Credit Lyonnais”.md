# Exclusion from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934  -“Credit Lyonnais”

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

V+1.}3 !n-32

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

www.rbi.org.in

RBI/2004/19

6th July, 2004

DBOD.Ret.BCNo.1/12.06.115/2004-05

All Scheduled Commercial Banks

Dear Sirs,

Exclusion from theSecond Schedule to the

Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 -

"Credit Lyonnais"

We advise that the name of “Credit Lyonnais"_has been excluded from the Second

Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with effect from 22nd May 2004, i.e. the

April 2004 was published in the Gazette of India (Part Ill - Section 4).

Yours faithfully,

Sd/-

(S.Oram)

Deputy General Manager
