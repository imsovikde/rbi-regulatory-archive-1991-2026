# Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a person Resident in India) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## FOREIGN EXCHANGE DEPARTMENT

## CENTRAL OFFICE

**MUMBAI 400 001**.

## Notification No.FEMA.113/ 2004-RB

## Dated 6 March 2004

## Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts <u>by a person Resident in India) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004</u>

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of Section 9 and clause (e) of sub-section

(2) of Section 47 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) and in partial modification of Notification No.FEMA 10/2000-RB dated May 3, 2000 as amended from time to time, Reserve Bank of India makes the following Regulations to amend the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a Person Resident in India) (Amendment) Regulations, 2004 namely:-
## 1. Short title and commencement :-

(i) These Regulations may be called the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a Person Resident in India) ( Amendment) Regulations,2004. (ii) They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
## 2. Amendment to the Regulations :-

In the Foreign Exchange Management (Foreign Currency Accounts by a Person Resident in India) Regulations, 2000 in sub-para (iv) of para 3 of the Schedule, the words “not exceeding US $ 3 million” shall be deleted.

## ( Shyamala Gopinath ) Executive Director
