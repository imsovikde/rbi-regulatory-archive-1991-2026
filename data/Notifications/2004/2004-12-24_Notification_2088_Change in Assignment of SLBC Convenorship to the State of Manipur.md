# Change in Assignment of SLBC Convenorship to the State of Manipur

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2004-05/ 321 No.RPCD.LBS.BC.65 /02.01.01/2004-05 December 24, 2004

The Chairman & Managing Director All Public Sector Banks

Dear Sir, **Change in Assignment of SLBC Convenorship to the State of Manipur**

Please refer to our circular RPCD.LBS.BC.71/02.01.01/2001-02 dated March 27, 2001 advising you the change made in the assignment of SLBC convenorship to the States of Madhya Pradesh and Bihar respectively. An updated list of convenors of State Level Bankers Committees (SLBCs)/Union Territory Level Bankers Committees (ULTBCs) incorporating the changes was also enclosed with our circular No.RPCD.LBS.BC.50 /02.01.01/2000-01 dated February 2, 2001 for your information.

In this connection, we have since been informed by the Government of India vide copy of their letter dated November 25, 2004 the following change in the SLBC convenorship in respect of the state of Manipur which may please be noted and incorporated in the list referred to above.

## <u>Name of the State Change in the convenorship for SLBC</u>

1. Manipur “State Bank of India” in place of “United
Bank of India”.

Please acknowledge receipt to the concerned Regional Office of RBI.

Yours faithfully,

Sd/-

(A.K. Bhandari) Deputy General Manager
