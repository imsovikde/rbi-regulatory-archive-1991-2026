RBI/2004-2005/139

DGBA.GAD.No.H-95/45.02.001/2004-05 August 24, 2004 The Chief Executive Officer Agency Banks (as per list)

Dear Sir, <u>Updation of List of Link Branches of Agency Banks- Submission of Statement on quarterly basis-Defence</u> <u>Pension</u> Please refer to the letter No.AT/ORs/Tech/070/XV dated May 21, 2004 addressed to us and copy endorsed to you by the Office of the Principal Controller of Defence Accounts (PCDA), Allahabad on the captioned subject.

2. In this connection, we draw your attention to our D.O letter DGBA.GAD. NO.736/45.02.001 /2002-03 dated October 26, 2002 wherein it was advised to submit the updated list of Link Branches pertaining to Defence Pension directly to PCDA(P), Allahabad on quarterly basis. However, we have been advised by the PCDA(P), Allahabad that the desired information has not been forwarded by the banks concerned even after the lapse of more than two years. You are, therefore, once again requested to furnish the requisite information directly to PCDA(P), Allahabad, immediately.
3. Please report compliance urgently.
Yours faithfully, Sd/- (M.T.Varghese) General Manager
