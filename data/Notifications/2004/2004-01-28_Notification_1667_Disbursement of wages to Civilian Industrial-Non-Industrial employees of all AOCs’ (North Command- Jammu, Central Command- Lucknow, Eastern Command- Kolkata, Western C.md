## Disbursement of wages to Civilian Industrial/Non-Industrial employees of all AOCs’ (North

## Command- Jammu, Central Command-Lucknow, Eastern Command-Kolkata, Western st

**Command- Chandigarh and Southern Command- Pune) on 31 January 2004 instead of Ist week of February 2004**

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We have been advised by the Controller General of Accounts, Government of India that in view of nd the ensuing "Id-Ul-Zuha" festival on 2 February 2004, Government have decided that wages of

civilian Industrial/Non-Industrial employees of all AOCs’ North Command-Jammu, Central

Command-Lucknow, Eastern Command-Kolkata, Western Command-Chandigarh and Southern st Command-Pune for the month of January 2004 be paid in advance on 31 January 2004 instead

## of first week of February 2004.

The wages so disbursed in advance are to be treated as advance payments and will be subject to

adjustment after the full month's wages of each employee are determined. The adjustment, if any,

will be made without exception from the wages for the month of February, 2004.

## Please arrange accordingly.

Yours sincerely, Sd/- (Girish Kallianpur) Deputy General Manager
