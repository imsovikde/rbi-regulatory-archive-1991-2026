## <u>Detection of Forged Notes -Issue of Receipt to Tenderers</u>

# Withdrawn

It has been decided that, as and when a currency note tendered at the counter of a bank branch is found to be forged, and the same is impounded by branding with a stamp “ FORGED NOTE”, an acknowledgement receipt in the following format should be issued to such tenderers. The receipt book should be printed in duplicate with running serial numbers and each receipt should be authenticated by the cashier at the counter as well as by the tenderer. (contd. on page 2 )
