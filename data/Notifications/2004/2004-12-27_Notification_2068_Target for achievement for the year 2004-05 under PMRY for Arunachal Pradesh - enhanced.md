RBI /2004-05/ **316** RPCD.PLNFS.BC. No**. 67** / 09.04.01 /2004-2005 December 27, 2004

The Chairman/ Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Excluding RRBs) Dear Sir, **Target for achievement for the year 2004-05 under PMRY-** <u>Enhancement in respect of Arunachal Pradesh-regarding</u>

Please refer to our letter RBI /2004-05/ 216- RPCD.PLNFS.BC. No. 39/09.04.01 /2004-2005 dated October 12, 2004 regarding the revised target for achievement under PMRY for the year 2004-05. On the request of Govt. of Arunachal Pradesh, the Ministry has decided to allocate an additional target of 450 to Arunachal Pradesh, over and above the existing target of 400 presently. Hence, the revised target in respect of Arunachal Pradesh for the year 2004-05 will be 850.

2. Please issue suitable instructions to all your branches/controlling offices in this regard.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.
Yours faithfully Sd/-
