# NOTIFICATION No.FEMA.123/2004-RB

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## (FOREIGN EXCHANGE DEPARTMENT)

## CENTRAL OFFICE

## MUMBAI 400 001

Notification No.FEMA.123/2004-RB dated September 9, 2004

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (h) of sub-section (2) of Section 47 of the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 (42 of 1999) and pursuant to change of nomenclature of Exchange Control Department of Reserve Bank of India from "Exchange Control Department" to "Foreign Exchange Department", the Reserve Bank hereby notifies that with effect from January 31, 2004, the words "Exchange Control Department" and "ECD" appearing in the Regulations notified from time to time by Reserve Bank of India, shall be read as "Foreign Exchange Department" and "FED", respectively.

## Shyamala Gopinath Executive Director

## Published in the Official Gazette of Government of India-Extraordinary-Part-II, Section 3,
