## <u>CO R R I G E N D U M</u>

In the Notification No.FEMA.82/2003-RB dated January 10, 2003 of Reserve Bank of India, Exchange Control Department, Central Office published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-section (I) vide G.S.R.533(E) dated July 9, 2003, the following shall be added at the end.

## Foot-note :

The Principal Regulations were published in the Official Gazette vide G.S.R.No.386(E) dated May 3, 2000 in part II, Section 3, Sub-Section (i) and subsequently amended vide G.S.R.No.674(E) dated August 14, 2000, G.S.R.No.476(E) dated April 29, 2002 and G.S.R.No.854(E) dated November 1, 2002 and G.S.R.No.531(E) dated January 8, 2003.

That as rectified and modified as aforesaid, the Principal Regulations shall remain in full force and effect.
