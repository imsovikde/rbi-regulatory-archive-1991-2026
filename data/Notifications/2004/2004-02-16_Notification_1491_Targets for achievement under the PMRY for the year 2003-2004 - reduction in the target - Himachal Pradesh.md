# Targets for achievement under the PMRY for the year 2003-2004 - reduction in the target - Himachal Pradesh

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2004/60 RPCD.PLNFS.BC.No. 64 /09.04.01/2003-2004 February 16, 2004 The Chairman/Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial banks (Except RRBs) Dear Sir, Targets for achievement under the PMRY for the year 2003-2004 - <u>reduction in the target-Himachal Pradesh</u> Please refer to our Circular RPCD.No.BC.3/09.04.01/2003-2004 dated 7th July 2003 on the captioned subject.

2. We advise that Government of India has decided to reduce the targets for the State of Himachal Pradesh for the programme year 2003-2004, from 4000 to the basic target of 3200.
3. All other terms and conditions will remain the same as advised earlier.
4. You may please issue necessary instructions to your Regional/ Controlling Offices/Branches in the State of Himachal Pradesh and achievement of allocated targets.
5. Please acknowledge receipt. Yours faithfully, ( G.P. Borah ) Deputy General Manager
