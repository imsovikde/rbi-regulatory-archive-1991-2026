# G.S.R. No.121(E)/17.02.2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

## EXCHANGE CONTROL DEPARTMENT

## CENTRAL OFFICE

## MUMBAI

## <u>C O R R I G E N D U M</u>

In the Notification No.FEMA.79/2003-RB dated 15th January 2003 of the Reserve Bank of India, Exchange Control Department, Central Office, published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (i) vide G.S.R.34(E) dated 15th January 2003, the following shall be added at the end :

"**Foot-note** : The Principal Regulations were published in the official gazette vide G.S.R.No.456(E) dated

08.05.2000 in Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (I) and subsequently last amended vide G.S.R. nO.475(E) dated 24th April 2002. That as rectified and modified as aforesaid, the Principal Regulations shall remain in full force and effect.
## (Shyamala Gopinath) Executive Director 06.02.2004
