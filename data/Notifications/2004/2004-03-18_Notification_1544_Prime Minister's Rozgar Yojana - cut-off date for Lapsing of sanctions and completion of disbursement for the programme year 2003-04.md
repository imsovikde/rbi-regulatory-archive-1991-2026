# Prime Minister's Rozgar  Yojana - cut-off date for Lapsing of sanctions and completion of disbursement for the programme year 2003-04

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RBI/2004/103 RPCD.No.PLNFS.BC. 71 /09.04.01/2003-04 March 18, 2004

The Chairman/Managing Director All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks (Except RRBs)

Dear Sir, **Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana-cut-off date for** **Lapsing of sanctions and completion of** <u>disbursement for the programme year 2003-04</u>

It has been decided to extend the cut off date for lapsing of sanctions and completion of disbursement for the programme year 2003-2004 under the PMRY to 30.09.2004.

2. Please issue necessary instructions to all your branches/controlling offices in this regard and to ensure achievement of target for the programme year 2003-2004 under the PMRY.
3. Please acknowledge receipt.
## Yours faithfully

(G. P. Borah) Deputy General Manager
