## Reporting system on progress under SJSRY

RPCD. SP. BC. 27 /09.16.01/2001-02 September 21, 2001 The Chairman, All Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks, (Excluding RRBs) Dear Sir, <u>Reporting system on progress under SJSRY</u>

Please refer to our circular letter no. RPCD.SP. 54/09.16.01/ 2000-2001 dated February 12, 2001, wherein we had advised that the copies of the quarterly report from quarter ended March 2001 may be forwarded to the Government of India, Ministry of Urban Development and Poverty Alleviation (MUDPA). As advised by the (MUDPA), you may now stop endorsing the copies of quarterly report to them. Yours faithfully, (Deepali Pant Joshi) General Manager
