# Banks allowed to offer FCNRA deposits only of three year maturity

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***March 1994* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Banks allowed to off er FCNRA deposits only of three year maturity** **(February 14, 1994)** **The Reserve Bank of India today announced withdrawal, effective February 15, 1994 of the** **maturity 'two years and above but less than three years' under the Foreign Currency** **(Non-Resident) Accounts (FCNRA) Scheme.** **Accordingly, authorised dealers will now be allowed to mobilise FCNRA deposits for a single** **maturity of three years only. The existing** **FCNRA deposit under the maturit!' slab of two years and above but less than three years will,** **however, be allowed to continue till maturity.** **FCNRA scheme carries exchange guarantee to banks.**

**There will be no change in the maturities available to non-resident depositors as authorised** **dealers can continue to accept deposits under the** **FCNRA (Banks) Scheme for maturities ranging from six months and over up to three years.**
