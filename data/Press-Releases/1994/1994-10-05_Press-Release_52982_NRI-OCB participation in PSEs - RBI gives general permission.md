# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**NRVOCB participation in PSEs : RBI gives general permission** **(October S, 1994)** **The Government of India has decided to disinvest a portion of the shares held by it in** **certain Public Sector Enterprises (PSEs) by inviting sealed tenders keeping in tune with the** **liberalised foreign investment policy of the** **Government of hKlia. It has also been decided to permit NRis/OCBs to participate in these bids** **with full repatriation benefits. NRls/OCBs wilJ be permitted to hold one per cent of the equity** **of these PSEs. In order to enable NRis/OCBs to acquire the shares of the publicsector enterprises** **on their bids being accepted by the government, the Reserve Bank of India has issued a** **notification No. FERA. 159/94-RB dated** **October 5, 1994 granting general permission** **under Section** 29(1) **and Section 19(4) of FERA.** **1973 permitting NRls/OCBs to purchase the shares on repatriation basis and the PSEs to** **register in their books the overseas addresses of these NRls/OCBs.**
