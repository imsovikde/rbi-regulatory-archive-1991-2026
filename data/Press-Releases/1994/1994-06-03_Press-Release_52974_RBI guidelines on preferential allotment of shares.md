# RBI guidelines on preferential allotment of shares

***}UM 1994*RESERVE BANlC OF INDIA BULLETIN** **RBI guidelines on preferential allotment of shares** **(June 3, 1994)** **Under the government's liberalised foreign investment policy, the Reserve Bank has been** **granting permission to Indian companies for raising foreign equity from any level upto 51 per** **cent of the capital under the automatic route. The pennission is subject to compliance of prescribed** **norms.** **Apropos the abolition of the office of the** **Controller of Capital Issues (CCI) and issu of guidelines by the Securities and Exchange Board** **of India, the applicant company was allowed to fix the price. As such. permission granted for** **issue of such shares to foreign collaborator was, *inter* aUa. subject to the condition that the price** **for the issue of shares should be freely determined by the shareholders in a special** **resolution under Section 81 ( 1 A) of the** **Companies Act.**

**Of late, the proposals received from the existing companies for enhancement of foreign** **uity had been showing a tendency for the issues to be significantly underpriced in relation** **to the market price. While as per the Reserve** **Bank policy for disinvestment, shares etc. were to be sold at market related price, the companies** **p oposed to issue foreign equity at a large di count to the market price. This, among other** **things, caused distortions in inflow/outflow of foreign exchange.**

**To bring the pricing policy under the market oriented approach and with a view to preventing** **a few shareholders from getting substantial and undue enrichment and unearned gains as also,** **undue reductions, in foreign equity inflow and making both, investment and disinvestment** **market-related, the Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India** **(Ministry of Finance), has decided that preferential allotment of shares by companies** **shall be at market-related price.** **According to the guidelines issued, every preferential allotment of shares by companies** **(other than allotment on rights basis) shall be at market value of the shares to be determined on** **the basis of their average price during the immediate preceding six months at the main** **listing centre calculated on the monthly average of high and low rates quoted for the shares at** **such centres.** **However, in the absence of a market price (as in the case of unlisted companies, listed** **companies where shares are not regularly traded, etc.), the Reserve Bank will be guided by the net** **assets value and earnings per share as notified by the erstwhile Controller of Capital Issues in** **December 1990.** **The guidelines for pricing in connection with preferential allotment to non-residents will apply** **to all foreign investment approvals to be issued by the Reserve Bank as well as the government** **(Secretariat of Industrial Approvals).**
