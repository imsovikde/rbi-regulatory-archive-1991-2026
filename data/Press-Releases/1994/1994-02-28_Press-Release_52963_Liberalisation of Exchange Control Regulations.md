# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Liberalisation of Exchange Control Regula tions** **(February 28, 1994)** **Since July 1991, there has been a progressive liberalisation of the exchange control regulations.** **As part of the move to current account convertibility, it has been decided to further** **liberalise the exchange control regulations as detailed below:**

***Exchange Earners Foreign Currency Accounts*** **Presently, resident beneficiaries of inward remittances and exporters of goods and services** **are permitted to retain upto 15 per cent of foreign exchange receipts in an account** **designated in foreign currency in their hooks in** **India. To further reduce the exchange risk and conversion cost to exporters it has been decided** **to increase the percentage of retention under the** **Exchange Earners Foreign Currency Accounts** **(EEFCA) from 15 per cent to 25 per cent.** **In the case of 100 per cent export oriented units (EOUs) and units located in Export** **Processing Zones (EPZs), software technology parks and electronic hardware technology parks** **(EHTP) this retention is being raised to 50 per cent.**

## Basic Travel Quota

**Basic travel quota of US $ 2,000 per person once a year for private visits abroad would be** **made available by authorised dealers as against** **once in three years of US $** *500* **under the** **Foreign Travel Scheme and US$ 250 under the** **Neighbourhood Travel Scheme.**
