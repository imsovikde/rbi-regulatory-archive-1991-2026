## Banks' Lendings to Securities Trading Cor poratioa of India Ltd. (STCI) - Netting Con

## cept for Reserve Requirements

**(December** 30, **1994)** **The Securities Trading Corporation of India** **(STCI) was not considered as a part of the banking system in terms of the relative** **provisions of the sections of the Reserve Bank** **of India Act,** 1934· **and the Banking Regulation** **Act,** 1949-**for the maintenance of reserve** **requirements by banks. Hence, while banks' borrowings from STCI were treated as** **'Ii abilities' and were subject to reserve requirements, their lendings to STCI were not** **treated as'assets' and as such the benefit of netting of assets with banking system against** **liabilities to the banking system was not available to banks. As result, on reporting** **Fridays, banks preferred not to lend to the STCI.** **On the recommendation of the Reserve Bank of India, Government of India have notified the** **financial institution, Securities Trading** **Corporation of India Ltd., for the purposes of the relative provisions of the sections of the Reserve**

**Bank of India Act,** 1934 **and the** **Banking Regulation Act,** 1949. **Thus, banks** **are now placed in a position to get the benefit of netting concept and they are enabled to deal** **with STCI on all days including reporting** **Fridays. The above measure would be beneficial both to banks and STCI and would** **also facilitate smoother operations in the money market.**
