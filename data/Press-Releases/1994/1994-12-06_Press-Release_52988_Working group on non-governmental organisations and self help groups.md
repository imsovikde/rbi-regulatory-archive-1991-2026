## The tenns of reference of the working group will be as follows:

**(i) To review the progress and perfonnance of self help groups (SHGs) as conduits of the**
## rural credit delivery system and assess the role

of **non-governmental organisations (NGOs) in** **promoting such groups.** **(ii) To examine the suitabmty** of **SHGs as** **purveyors of credit to the weaker sections and their comparative advan tage over other** **constituents of the credit delivery system.** **(iii) To identify the factors hindering the progress of SHGs and suggest measures to** **overcome them; and** **(iv) To examine the linkages which can be forged between SHGs/NGOs and the banking**

**system to the mutual advantage** of **both and also** **suggest a suitable model for· SHGs and NGOs in the Indian context on an enduring basis. .,.**

**It may be recalled that at the RBI-NABARD consultations on rural credit which were held on** **September** *1* **and** 2, 1994, **it was felt that** **non-governmental organisations and self help groups had a vital role to play and that their** **working should be studied for expanding their aclivilies and deepening their role in the ruraJ** **sector.** **The working group will submit its repon by the end of May 1995.**
