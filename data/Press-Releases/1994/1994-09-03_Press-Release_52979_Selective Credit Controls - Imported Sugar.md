# Selective Credit Controls - Imported Sugar

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***September 1994* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Selective Credit Controls-Imported Sugar** **(September 3, 1_994)** **The Reserve Bank of India has advised all commercial banks (including regional rural**

**banks) that with effect from September** 3, 1994, **the minimum margin on bank advances against stocks of sugar imponed on a cash basis (sight** **letter of credit) should be 25 per cent for a period** **not exceeding six weeks after the arrival** of**sugar** **in India as against eight weeks hitherto.** **In the case of imports of sugar made on usance letters of credit (LIC), viz, documents** **against acceptance** *(DI***A) basis, banks have been** **advised that they should, as far as possible, not** **establish letters of credit for more than** 30 **days** **on** *DIA* **basis for import of sugar, effective** **September** 3, 1994, **as against** 45 **days hitherto.** **In case, however, banks establishletters of credit** **for more than** 30 **days on** *DI***A basis, they should** **stipulate that, after 30 days from the date of anival of the commodity in the port till the date** **of retirement of the import bills, the importer should deposit with the banks an amount** **equivalent to the margin amount as applicable to bank advances against sugar produced in the** **countty.** **The other stipulations applicable on bank advances against imported sugar remain** **unchanged.**
