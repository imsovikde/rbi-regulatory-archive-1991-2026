# RBI sets up committee to study payment and settlement systems

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BUU.ETIN *June 1994***

**RBI sets up committee to study payment and settlement systems** **(June 3, 1994)** **The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has set up a committee to study all aspects of technology** **upgradation relating to transfer of funds, payment system and settlement procedures,** **cheque clearing and the related information flow.** **It was necessary to undertake a critical review of the ex.istitl procedures and practices in the wake** **of rapidly growing technology needs of the banking industry.**

**Set up under the chairmanship of Shri W.S.** **Saraf, Executive Director, RBI, the committee will recommend a suitable plan of action,** **step-by-step solutions on the different items keeping the long term perspective of widening** **the use of modem technology in the banking industry. Other members *of* the committee are:** **S/Shri R. Narasimham, State Bank of India,** **Dinesh Nayak, Indian Bank, Biswajil** **Chowdhury, UCO Bank, A.K. Bakhsby, Indian** **Banks' Association. S.M.
