# Reintroduction of Selective Credit Controls on Cotton and Kapas

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***March 1994* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

## Reintroduction of Selective Credit Controls on would now be brought back into force.

## Cotton and Kapas Mills/processing units are exempted from the

**(February 18. 1994) provisions of selective credit controls.** **On a review of recent price-output developments, bank advances against cotton and** **kapas which were exempt from the provisions of selective credit controls are being brought back** **under the controls with effect from February 19,**

**1994. The minimum margins on advances against stocks of cotton and kapas will be 45 per** **cent for borrowers other than mills/processing units and 30 per cent against warehouse receipts.** **The level of credit ceiling will be I 00 per cent** **of the peak level of credit maintained by a party in any of the three years ended 1991-92** **(November-October). All other stipulations/ exemptions which were in force prior to the** **removal of selective credit controls on advances against cotton and kapas on October 12, 1993**
