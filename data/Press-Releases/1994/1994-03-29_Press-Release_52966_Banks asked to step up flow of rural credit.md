# RBI PRESS RELEASES

## Banks asked to step up flow of rural credit (March 29, 1994)

A meeting of Chairmen of some public sector banks was taken by Dr. C. Rangarajan, Governor today to discuss the efforts required to be made by banks for stepping up the flow of rural credit, particularly production and investment credit for agriculture. Banks were advised to formulate special plans for achieving a marked improve ment in their agricultural !endings in the next financial year (1994-95). The meeting also dis cussed other issues related to rural credit. such as, measures required to be taken for improving the recovery performance under agricultural lending and poverty alleviation programmes, modifications, if needed, in the Service Area Ap proach to mral lending with a view to making it a more effective tool for ensuring smooth and enhanced flow of credit to the rural sector, mea8ures to be taken by banks for adequately meeting credit requirement of high-tech innova tive projects, such as acquaculture, floriculturc, tissue culture, etc., the support required to be provided by sponsor **banks** for revamping of Regional **Rural Banks (RRBs)** selected for the purpose, proper manning of rural branches, rout ing of credit through co-operative societies on selective basis, greater involvement of Non Governmental Organisations (NGOs) and Self help groups in the banks' rural financing, etc. In view of the importance of agriculture in the na tional economy, the need for stepping up of private investment in agriculture with the aid of hank finance was emphasized.
