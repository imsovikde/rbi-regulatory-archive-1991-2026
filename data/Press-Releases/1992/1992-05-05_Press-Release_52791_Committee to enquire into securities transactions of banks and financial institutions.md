## RESERVE HANK OF INDIA BULLETINJune /992

# Committee to enquire into securities transactions of banks and financial

# institutions (May 5, 1992)

Certain enquiries had been made by the Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Operations and Development into transactions of the State Bank of India in, Government Securities.- With a -view to investigating the irregularities that came to light, under the instructions of the Governor who was abroad, the following Committee was set up on 30 April 1992 to look into. the funds management by commercial banks and financial institutions, in particular in relation to dealings in Government Securities, public sector bonds and money market instruments.

The Committee will investigate various aspects of the transactions of the State Bank of India and other commercial banl<s as well as financial institutions in this regard. This Committee will be headed by Shri R. Janakiraman, Deputy Govrnor and will consist of Shri **Y. H.** Malegam, Chartered Accountant, Shri V. G. 'Hegde, Principal Legal Adviser and Kum. V. Visvanathan, Executive Director as Member-Secretary. The Committee will co-op t experts from other required disciplines as necessary to assist it in its deliberations.

Specifically, the Committee will enquire into the extent of non-compliance by banks and financial institutions with the guidelines of the Reserve Bank of India regarding securities transactions including transactions in PSU bonds, units, etc., the inadequacies in systems and procedures in force in these institutions generally, and the extent of use of' Bank Receipts which have been in vogue in regard to transactions in Government Securities and other instruments. The Committee will suggest such corrective steps as may be necessary to have a more efficient and accountable system for the future. The Committee will further examine and determine the extent of malpractices, if any, indulged in by officials of banks and financial }l institutions, w ether their funds have been allowed to be used for speculative transactions by brokers and other intermediaries and undue benefits have been thereby derived by brokers and others through unauthorised access to borrowed funds, and fix responsibility therefor and recommend the action to be taken.

It will also scrutinise the procedure adopted by the Public Debt Offices of the Re erve Bank of India in regard to mamtenance of SG L Accounts and other related matters and suggest remedial measures to tone up the responsiveness of the system.

The Committee will submit its report expeditiously, not later than 30 June 1992.
