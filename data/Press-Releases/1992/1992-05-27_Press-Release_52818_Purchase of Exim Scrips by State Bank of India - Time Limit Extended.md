# Purchase of Exim Scrips by State Bank of India - Time Limit Extended

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***July 1992*** **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Purchase of Exim Scrips by State Bank of** **India - Time Limit Extended** **(May 27, 1992)** **Reserve Bank has made arrangements with designated branches of State Bank of India** **(SBI) to purchase Exim Scrips, post paid REP licences etc. against payment of premium of** **20% of the unutilised portion of the value of such Exim Scrips/ licences. Designated** **branches of SBI will accept up to 31st July 1992 such Exim Scrips/ REP etc. licences covered by** **the Scheme, issued up to 30th June 1992, for payment of premium. REP licences issued by** **JCCIE's offices after 30th June J 992 will, however, continue to be accepted by designated** **branches of SBI for payment of premium even beyond 31st July 1992, provided they are** **tendered within one month from the date of issue.** **Reserve Bank has agreed to provide exchange at official rat for imports against** **Exim Scrips. Holders of Exim Scrips have also been given the option of surrendering such** **Exim Scrips to SBI for payment of premium.** **As payments for imports covered by Exim** **Scrips valid for imports from Rupee Payment** **Area (RP A) are to be made in non-convertible rupees, the question of** **providing foreign exchange at official rate does not arise and, therefore, such Exim** **Scrips/REP licences are not covered by the scheme ofpurchaseof Exim Scrips.**
