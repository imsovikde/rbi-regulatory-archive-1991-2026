# Interest rate for export finance for storing and sale through warehouses abroad

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLE'llN February 1993

**Interest rate for export finance for storing and sale through warehouses abroad** **(December 18, 1992)** **In view of the longer period of realisation pennitted in the case of exports of goods through** **warehouses abroad, the Reserve Bank of India has**

**decided to rationalise the interest rates on** *post* *shipment credit against exports through approved* *warehouses.* **Accordingty, in respect of post** **shipment credit extended to exports through overseas warehouses the interest rate applicable** **will be:**

|Period of Post|• Rate of Interest|
|---|---|
|shipment credit||
|(a) Upto 90 days|The rate applicable for usance bills for period upto 90 days (al present 14% p. a.)|
|(b) Beyond 90 days and|The rate applicable for usance|
|upto 15 months from|bills beyond 90 days and upto|
|the date of shipment|six months from the date of shipment (at present 18% p.a.)|

**Earlier, these rates were being fixed by** **Reserve Bank of India from time to time. In case the sale proceeds of goods consigned to such** **warehouses cannot be realised within the pennitted period of fifteen months to liquidate the** **post-shipmem credit, higher rate of interest as applicable for bills realised beyond 6 months from** **the date of shipment will apply for the entire period beyond 6 months. The rate currently is** **23%.** **The instructions will come into force with immediate effect. There is no change in the rates** **of interest on pre-shipment credit.**
