# The Metropolitan Co-operative Bank Ltd., Bombay

# ( May 28, 1992)

**Simultaneously with the supersession of the existing Board of Directors of the** **Metropolitan Co-operative Bank Ltd.,** **Bombay and appointment of an administrator by the Registrar of Co-operative Societies,** **Maharashtra State,·under Section l lOA (iii) of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies .Act** **1960, which empowers the Reserve Bank of** **India to issue, among others, directions to the** **Regi strar for such supersession of the Boards of** **Management, the Reserve Bank has issued directions to the said bank under sub-section (i)** **of Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act,** **1949 read with Section** *56* **of the said Act',** **effective from the close of business on 27th**

**May 1992. Section 35A empowers the Reserve** **Bank to issue directions to any cooperative bank where it is satisfied that these are** **necessary in the publicinterest and_ to prevent the affairs of the bank being conducted in a** **manner · detrimental to the interests of depositors. The directions which have been** **issued, prohibit the bank from granting or renewing of loans·and advances and prevent** **disproportionate payment to big depositors to the detriment of small depositors as also** **preferential payments to depositors and other creditors except to the extent and in the** **manner provided and after obtaining the approval of RBI, in specified cases. The** **directions soissued require the bank to pay to depositors a sum not exceeding 10 per cent of** **the total balances in the savings bank account or current account or any other deposit** **account provided that the sum total of the amount paid in respect of all the accounts** **standing in the name of any one person does not exceed Rs. 500. ·** **The position of the said bank is being kept under continuing review.**
