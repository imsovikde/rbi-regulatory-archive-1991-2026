# Automatic Approval of Foreign Investment & Technology Transfer

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***October 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Automatic Approval of Foreign** **Investment & Technology Transfer** **(September 30, 1992)** **Proposals by Reserve Bank of India** **It has been a year since the RBI started granting approvals _to prosposals for direct** **foreign investment and transfer of technology in terms of the liberalised foreign investment** **policy announced by the Government in July,**

**1991.** **The. Foreign Investment & Technology** **Transfer (FITT) Section which was set up in the Central Office of the Exchange Control** **Department at Bombay on 16th September,** **1991 had, till 26th September, 1992, received** **996 applications of which 733 were approved and 221 returned unapproved since they did** **not satisfy the conditions stipulated for automatic approval. All proposals were** **disposed of within a fortnight.** **Out of the approvals granted, 221 were for equity investment amounting to US$ 241 749** **million.
