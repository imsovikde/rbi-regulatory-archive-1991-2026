# Opening of branch offices in India by foreign companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*October 1992* RESERVE RANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

## Opening of branch offices in India by foreign companies

## (September 9, 1992)

The Reserve Bank of India will hereafter consider applications for opening of branches in India of fqreign companies engaged in manufacturing and trading activities for carrying out following activities in India-

(i)To represent the parent company/other
foreign companies in various matters in India,

e.g .• acting as buying/ selling agents in India, etc. (ii) To conduct research work in which the parent company is engaged provided the results of the research -work are made - available to the Indian companies; (iii) To undertake export and import trading activities; (iv) To promote the possible technical and financial collaborations between the Indian c<;>mpanies and overseas companies.
. 2. Applications for this purpose may be
submitted in Form FNC-12 to the undernoted address:- The Controler, Exchange Control Department, Reserve Bank of India, Foreign Investment Division, Central Office, Central Office Building, 11th floor, **Bombay-40O 023.**
