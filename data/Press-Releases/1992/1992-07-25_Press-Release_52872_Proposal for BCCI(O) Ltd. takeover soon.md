# Proposal for BCCI(O) Ltd. takeover soon

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *September 1992*

**Proposal for BCCI(O) Ltd. takeover soon** **(July 25, 1992)** **The process of indentifying suitable buyers for the overseas branch of the Bombay branch** **of the Bank of Credit and Commerce** **International (Overseas) Ltd., and taking over its entire liabilities, including the claims ofthe** **depositors in full is nearing completion. It is expected that the proposal will be shortly** **placed before the High Court at Bombay for necessary orders.** **As stated in the RBI's earlier press releases, the depositors are reassured that the** **arrangements which will be put in position will safeguard the interestsof the depositors in** **full.**
