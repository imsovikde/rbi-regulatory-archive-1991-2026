# RBI PRESS RELEASES

## Liquidation of Metropolitan Co-operative Bank Ltd., Bombay (June 19, 1992)

· In view of serious irregularities observed in the working of the Metropolitan Co-operative Bank Ltd., Bombay, and the issue of Bank Receipts (BRs) in total disproportion to its as sets, the RBI had directed the Registrar of Co operative Societies, Maharashtra, to supersede the Board of Directors of the Bank on 26th May 1992, prohibiting it from granting or renewing loans and advances and payments to deposil'Ors of an amount exceeding Rs.500 or 10 per cent of the total balance in their deposit accounts whichever is lower.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN· *August 1992***

**A further investigation of the affairs of the bank has revealed that it does not satisfy the** **minimum capital requirements of uneroded paid-up share capital and reserves of Rs.1 lakh** **and that the erosion in its assets will not only** **wipe out** *its* **entire owned funds but will affect** **deposits to a substantial extent. Furthermore, the bank has issued a number of BRs and the** **value of such BRs outstanding invoves securities exceeding Rs.1300 crores in value as** **against the total working capital of less 11:tan** **Rs.8 crores. In the circumstances, the con tinuance of the bank constitutes a danger to the** **depositors and would be prejudicial to the public interest.**

**The Reserve Bank has also been approached by the Divisional Joint Registrar, Co-operative** **Societies, Bombay, informing that the special audit of the bank for the year ended 30th June** **1991 had brougt out serious irregularities and deficiencies in its working and the bank had** **been classified as 'D' class and requesting the** **Reserve Bank to accord sanction to the liquida tion of the bank.**

**After due consideration and consultation with the concerned authorities, the RBI has accorded** **its sanction to the windiag up of the** **Metropolitan Co-operative Bank.** **The Deposit insurance & Credit Guarantee** **Corporation stands ready to pay to the depositors of the bank the amounts outstanding** **to their credit upto a maximumof Rs.30,000 per depositor.**

**The Liquidator of the bank is being advised to expeditiously file a claim with the Corpora** **tion for such payment.** */*
