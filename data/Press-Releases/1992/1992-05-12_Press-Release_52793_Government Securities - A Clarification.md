# Government Securities : A Clarification

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***July 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Government Securities :** **A Clarification** **(May 12, 1992)** **A report has appeared in a Bombay newspaper to the effect that the Reserve Bank** **of India had called for a statement of** **Government Securities holdings from all banks andon scrutiny, a gapofover Rs. 5,000** **crores in government security holdings reported by various banks and the balance** **held to the credit of banks in the SGL accounts maintained in the Public Debt Office of the** **Reserve Bank of India has been discovered.** **Reserve Bank emphatically denies this news item. The Inspecting Officers of RBI are** **currently scrutinising the investment tran sactionsof banks and as a part of this exercise**

**they** will **also look into thereconciliation made** **by banks themselves of the balances ot government securities shownin the banks• own** **books and those appearing to the credit of the bank concerned in its SG L account maintained** **with the RBI. The. Inspecting Officers will ensure that the accounts have been properly** **reconciled and the difference, if any, is accounted for. Any discrepancy will be** **brought out in the report and will be considered by the Committee appointed by the** **RBI to lookinto the investment transactions of the banks. The Bank has not called for a** **statement of government securities holdings from all banks as reported and the question of** **discovering a gap, whether of Rs. 5,000 crores or any other figure does not, therefore, arise.**
