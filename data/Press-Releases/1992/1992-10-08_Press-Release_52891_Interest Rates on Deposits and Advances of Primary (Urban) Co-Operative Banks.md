# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Interest Rates on Deposits and Advances of** **Primary (Urban) Co- Operative Banks** **(October 8, 1992)** **Consequent on the announcement of the** **Credit Policy for scheduled commercial banks for the 2nd half of 1992-93 by the Governor, certain** **changes have been made in the lending and deposit rates of the Primary (Urban) Co-operative** **Banks effective October 9, 1992 as under:**

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

***November 1992*** **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

## (a) Lending Rates

**(i) It has been decided** to **reduce the lending**
**rate on credit limits of over Rs.2 lakhs in respect of general rate (Category A) by one percentage**

**point from** 19.0 **per cent (minimum) to** 18.0 **per** **cent (minimum) for Primary (Urban) Co-opera tive Banks.**

## The lending rate for commodities coming within the purview of selective credit control is

**also reduced from** 19.0 **per cent (minimum) to**

18.0 **per cent (minimum).**
## (ii)Interest Rates on Export Credit

## It has been decided to reduce effective from

**October** 9, 1992 **the interest rates on export credit** **(rupee) provided by the Primary (Urban) Co operative Banks by one percentage point across**

**the- board.**

## All other lending rates for Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks remain unchanged.

## (b) Deposit Rates

## It has been decided to reduce, effective from

**October** 9, 1992, **the deposit rates for maturity of** **46 days to 3 years and above by one percentage** **point to "not exceeding** 12.0 **per cent per annum".** **The revised structure of deposit rates will apply only to fresh deposits and on renewal of maturing**

**deposits.**
