# RBI's fiat on account payee cheques

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*October 1992* RESERVE RANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

## RBl's fiat on account payee cheques (September 9,1992)

In a circular issued to the Chairmen and Chief Executives of scheduled commercial banks. the Reserve Bank today emphasised •.hat:

- Banks should, as per existing instructions,
utilise the accounts maintained with RBI only for the purpose of fulfilling their statutory obligations, settlement of transactions with **RBI/** Government, settlement of inter bank ***transactions or adverse clearing balances.*** These accounts should not be used for facilitating credit to accounts of third. parties.

*If the account payee cheque is not drawn in the name of constituent nor does it contain any direction to pay the proceeds of the cheque to the constituent, or any other independent direction to that effect, the amount cannot be paid to the constituent.

*Crediting of proceeds of account payee cheques to parties other than that clearly delineated in the instructions of the issuer of the cheques is unauthorised and should not be done under any circumstances.

*Banks cannot and should not involve market practice in justification of doing that which is not supported by law or recognised banking practice.

The circular emphasised that if any bank credited the account of a constituent who was noi the payee named in the cheque without, proper mandate of the drawer, it would do so at its own risk and would be responsible for the unauthorised payment. Banks which indulge in any deviation **from** these instructions would invite severe penal action.
