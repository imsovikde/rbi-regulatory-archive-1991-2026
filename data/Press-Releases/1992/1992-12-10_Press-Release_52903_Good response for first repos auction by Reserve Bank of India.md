# Good response for first repos auction by Reserve Bank of India

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***January 1993* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLE11N**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Good response for first repos auction by** **Reserve Bank of India** **(December 10, 1992)** **· Thirteen bids for an aggregate sum of** **Rs. 2120 crore were recieved at the first repos auction in Government of India dated securities** **held on December 10, 1992. All the bids quoted** **at a rate of 6.00 per cent and below for a total amount of Rs.2120 crore were accepted.** **For its first one day repos auction the Reserve** **Bank offered two Central Government securities, viz., 11.50% 2006 and 11.50% 2009, at prices** **Rs. 91.15% and Rs. 89.90% respectively.** **The Scheme of repos auction has been introduced to improve shon-term management of** **liquidity and to even out interest rates in the call/ notice money market within the make up** **fonnight.**
