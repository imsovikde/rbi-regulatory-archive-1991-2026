**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN***July 1992*

# The Metropolitan Co-opertive Bank Ltd., Bombay

# (May 27, 1992)

**On the basis of investigations conducted by the Reserve Bank of India in connection with** **the issue of Bank Receipts representing sale proceeds of securities, bonds, units, it was** **observed that the Metropolitan Co-operative.** **Bank Ltd., 67 Hanuman Building, Mumbadevi** **Road, Tamba Kanta, Bombay-3 had issued** **Bank Receipts to· some banks for amounts highly disproportionate to the total assets of** **the said bank and without taking adequate safeguards. Considering the seriousness of the** **irregularities and with a view to securing the interests of the depositors, in terms of Section** **1 IO A (iii) of the Maharashtra Co-operative** **Societies Act, 1960, the Reserve Bank of India has· required the Registrar of Co-operative** **Societies, Maharashtra toimmediately replace the Board of Directors by an Administrator** **who will take charge and run the affairs of the** **Bank. The Reserve Bank has also advised the** **Registrar of Co-operative Societies to hold an enquiry into the working and financial** **conditions of the bank under Section 83 of the** **Act *ibid.***
