# Fairgrowth Financial Services Ltd. Prohibited from Transacting Fresh Business

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***August /992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

## Fairgrowth Financial Services Ltd.

## Prohibited· from Transacting Fresh Business

(July 1, 1992)

The Reserve Bank has prohibitcd.Fairgrowth Financial Services Ltd. and three other com panies in the group from transacting any fresh business with immediate effect. This decision has been taken in publicinterest and in view of certain serious irregularities observed in the operations of Fairgrowth Financial Services

Ltd. The matter is being referred to investigat-. ing agencies.

The companies are, however, allowed to dis charge all their existing lawful obligations and also to exercise all their rights regarding recovery of dues.
