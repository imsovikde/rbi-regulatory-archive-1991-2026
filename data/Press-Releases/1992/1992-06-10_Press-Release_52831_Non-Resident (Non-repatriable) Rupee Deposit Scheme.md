# Non-Resident (Non-repatriable) Rupee Deposit Scheme

# (June 10, 1992)

With a view to providing further Incentives and giving wider options to persons of Indian nationality/ origin residing abroad (NRls) and overseas corporate bodies predominantly owned by NRis (OCBs) as well as giving opportunities to persons of nc;>n-lndian origin (except Pakistani and Bangladeshi nationals) and overseas corporate bodies owned by them for making investments in India, Reserve Bank has formulated a new Deposit Scheme. viz., Non-resident (Non-repatriable) Rupee Deposit Scheme. The Scheme will come into force with effect from 15th June 1992. Under the Scheme, accounts in Indian ruppees can be opened with Authorised Dealers by remittance of funds in freely convertible foreign exchange from abroad or by transfer of funds from the existing Non-resident (External)/ FCNR Accounts. No penalty will be levied in case of premature withdrawal of ex1stmg Non-resident (External)/ FCNR deposits for the purpose of making investment in the proposed Scheme.

Authorised dealers are free to fix the maturity period of such deposits between six months and three years. They are also free to fix the rate. of interest payable on such deposits. The maturity proceeds of the deposit including interest accrued thereon. will not qualify for repatriation outside India at any time. Authorised dealers may renew the•

deposits as also allow shifts from one bank to another. However, if the amount of the deposits ·is withdrawn or invested in other Schemes. it will not qualify for renewal under the Scheme. The deposits under the Scheme would not be considered as part of net demand and time liabilities for the purpose of CRR and SLR and as such these deposits would be free from any reserve requirements. Lending against these deposits would not be considered as part of net bank credit for the purpose of determining priority sector lending. These special benefits will be available so long as the funds remain in these deposits. The amount of the deposit can be gifted by individual account holders to a non resident or to the account holder's close relative in India or to any Charitable Trust in India recognised under the Indian Income•tax Act, 1961 without payment of any gift tax for one ti me gifting in the case of N RI s only. Authorised dealers may allow premature withdrawals of the deposits and levy penalty for premature withdrawal as per their discretion.

The income from the deposits under the Scheme will be free from Indian Income Tax. The deposits will also be exempt from Gift Tax for one time gifting in the case of N Ris only. Exemption from Income, Tax will, however, not be available to resident donees and those residents being joint hol4ers who become owners of the deposits as survivors of the non-resident depositor.

Authorised dealers may grant rupee loans/overdrafts in India without any limit to the deposit holder against the securi_ty of the said. deposit for purposes other than investment. The margin and rate of interest for loans may be decided by the lending bank. Repayment of loan may, however, be allowed to be made by debit to NRE/ FCNR/NRO Account of the. deposit holder or against adjustment· of maturity proceeds of the deposit or by way of remittance of freely convertible foreign exchange from abroad.
