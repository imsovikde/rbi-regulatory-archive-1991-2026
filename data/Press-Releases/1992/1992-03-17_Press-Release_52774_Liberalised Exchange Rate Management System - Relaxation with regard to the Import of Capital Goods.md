**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *April 1992***

# Liberalised Exchange Rate Management System - Relaxation with regard to the

# Import of Capital Goods (March 17, 1992)

**Authorised Dealers are at present permitted to provide foreign exchange fo** **· import of capital goods up to the value of** **Rs. 50 lakhs for each transaction. With a view** **·-. to facilitating expeditious release of foreign exchange for import of capital goods,** **Authorised Dealers are now permitted to release foreign exchange without any ceiling.** **At present financial institutions· are allowd to extend credit facilities only up to** **US $ 5 million in each case out of the foreign currency loan and credit lines available with** **them for the purpose of financing of import of capital goods. On a review, it hasbeen decided** **to remove all Reserve Bank's restrictions en the utilisation of foreign currency loans and** **credit lines with financial institutions for the purpose of financing import of capital goods.**

## At present, a number of licences both for import .of raw materials and capital goods

**contain specific financing pattern. In view** of **the Liberalised Exchange Rate Management** **System, the pattern of financing indicated in such licences is no longer obligatory.** **Accordingly, it has been decided to provide access to foreign exchange at market rate in** **all such cases irrespective of the financing pattern indicated in the licences.** **Authorised. Dealers and Financial Institu tions may keep · these. instructions in view** **while dealing with applications relating to, import of capital goods.**
