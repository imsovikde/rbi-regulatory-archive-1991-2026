## Exports to Entwhil

**!** **USSR** **(April 1, 1992)** **Under the present arrangements between the Government of India and the Reserve** **Bank of India, claims from banks handling export transactions to erstwhile USSR have** **been reimbursed provided the relative letters of credit were established by banks in the** **erstwhile USSR and advised on or. before** **December 31 1991. Similar reimbursements are also being made in respect of advance**
