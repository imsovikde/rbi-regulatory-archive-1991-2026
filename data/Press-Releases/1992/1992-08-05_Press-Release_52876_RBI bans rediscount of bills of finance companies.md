# RBI bans rediscount of bills of finance companies

# (August 5, 1992)

The Reserve Bank of India has prohibited banks from rediscounting bills earlier discounted by non-bank final}cial companies. According to a circular issued recently by RBI to all scheduled commercial banks, bills rediscounts should be restricted to usance bills held by other banks.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *Septemher 199]***

**In its recent circular, the RBI has also cautioned banks that if they obtain any excess** **finance against ineligible usance bills, it will be treated as borrowing and banks will have to** **maintain additional SLR/CRR on such additional liabilities for the concerned periods.**

**RBI has advised .the banks that - (i) they should not grant fund-based (including bills** **finance) or non-fund based facilities like opening letters of credit, providing guarantees** **and acceptance facilities to any borrower outside the consortium arrangements in case of** **large borrowers; (ii) bills financing should be provided only as part of the working capital** **credit provided to the borrower after proper appraisal of the working capital needs based on** **well established credit norms; (iii) only bills covering purchase of raw material/inventory** **for production purpose and sale of goods should be discounted by them; (iv) bills** **covering payments of electricity charges, custom duty, hire-purchase/ lease/ rental** **instalments, sale of securities and other types of financial accommodation should not be** **discounted; and**

**(v) accommodation bills should not be discounted under any circumstances. Banks**
**have also been advised not to deploy funds accepted by them for portfolio management** **from their constituents for discounting bills.**

**Banks' Jending to finance companies including bills discounting should be part of** **the overall working capital credit limit sanctioned to such companies after proper** **appraisal of their genuine working capital needs. In any case the overall limit should not** **exceed three times the net worth of the company.**

## The Reserve Bank has cautioned the banks that violation of above instructions will invite

**penal action.**
