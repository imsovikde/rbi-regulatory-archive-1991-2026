# Trade between India and Russian Federation under the Trade Protocol for 1992

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***July 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Trade between India and Russian** **Federation under the Trade Protocol for 1992** **(June 4, 1992)** **In pursuance of the agreement signed between the Government of India and the** **Government of Russian Federation on 22nd** **February 1992, it has been decided that besides the trade which can be carried out** **under the clearing mechanism of the Trade** **Protocol, import and export of goods and services may. take place· in any freely** **convertible currency in accordance with the** **Trade Regulations and E;cchange Control** **Regulations of the two countries.** **Additionally, natural and juridical persons of both the countries shall be free to import and** **export goods and services from each other on the basis of counter trade, commodity** **exchange, barter, buy-back operations or any internationally recognised form of business** **co-operation. For this purpose, Reserve Bank will allow opening of Special Escrow** **Accounts in U.S. dollar in the names of** **Russian exporters with the commercial banks in India, wherever warranted, upon** **presentation of certificates issued by the** **· Central Bank of Russia authorising them to open bank accounts in India. The transactions** **in· such accounts will be on a balancing basis and these accounts will not be funded by any** **transfer of funds to any other account without the prior permission of the · Reserve Bank.** **Russian exporters may be permitted subject to availability of balance in the account to utilise** **up to 20 per cent of the amount accruing in this account to meet the trade related rupee** **expenses in India in accordance with the laws and regulations of India. Necessary** **instructions have been issued by the Reserve** **Bank of India to the Authorised Dealers.**
