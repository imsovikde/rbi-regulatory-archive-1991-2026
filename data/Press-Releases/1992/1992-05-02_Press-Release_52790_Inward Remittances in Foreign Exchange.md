In order to avoid such delays, banks** **have been given the discretion to provide immediate payments against such inward**

**remittance instruments up to** US$ 1000 **or its** **equivalent.** **Under the Foreign Inward Remittance** **Payments System** (Fl **RPS) quick** **transmission of funds is provided to the beneficiaries in India against inward** **remittances received from abroad through banking channels. This system is used by the** **banks for arranging payment of personal remittances received from abroad in favour of** **resident Indians as well' as for credit to** **Ordinary Non-resident Account and Non resident External Account. Under this system** **Authorised Dealers must, without delay, convert into Indian Rupees foreign currency** **remittances received in the form of** **TTs/MTs/Pay orders and arrange payment of proceeds thereof by FIRPS instruments** **which are payable at par at all branches in** **India of Authorised Dealers as also other scheduled commercial banks which are not** **authorised. to deal in foreign exchange but which agree to abide by,the regulations issued** **by FED Al governing issue and encashrnent of** **FIRPS instruments..
