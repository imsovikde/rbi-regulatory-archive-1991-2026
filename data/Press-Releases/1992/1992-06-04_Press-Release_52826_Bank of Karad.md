# Bank of Karad (June 4, 1992)

**Due to large scale irregularities in securities transactions in Bank of Karad Ltd.,** **the High Court of Bombay on the application of Reserve Bank of lnqia passed an ad interim** **order on 27 May 1992 under Section 38 of the** **Banking Regulation Act 1949, taking the bank into liquidation and appointed a** **provisional liquidator to take charge of all assets and properties of the bank.**

**As the outsid-e liabilities of the bank far exceed the available assets, the liabilities to** **the depositors cannot be. discharged in the normal course. Even repayment to · the** **depositors as per the provisions of the Deposit** **Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation** **Act, 1961 would require due completion of formalities which would be time-consuming.** **However, with a view to mitigating the hardships caused to the depositors,** **particularly the small depositors. the Reserve** **Bank of India, as a special case, has made arrangements with the DICGC for payment of** **deposits immediately up to the maximum of** **Rs. 30,000 per depositor. The High Court of** **Bombay has passed necessary orders in this regard on 4 June 1992. Arrangements have** **also been made to enble locker holders to operate their lockers.**

## The provisional liquidator is accordingly making arrangements to make payment as per the Court order.
