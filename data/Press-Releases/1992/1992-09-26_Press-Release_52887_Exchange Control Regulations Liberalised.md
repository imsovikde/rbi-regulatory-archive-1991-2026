# Exchange Control Regulations Liberalised

# (September 26, 1992)

**In an attempt to further liberalise exchange control regulations the Reserve** **Bank recently allowed authorised dealers in foreign exchange to release foreign exchange** **without RBl's approval for certain purposes.** **The limits of allowable exchange wtre also enhanced in a few cases.**

*Now,* **foreign exchange will be allowed at** *the* **rate** *of not* **exceeding US$ 300 per day for**

Chief** **Executives of export houses/ trading houses/ star trading houses will now be** **allowed entertainment expenses up to USS** **5,000 per trip.
