#### Rise in flow of credit to export sector (December 16, 1992)

##### Outstanding export finance as on 27th

**November 1992 was** Rs. **13,009 crore which was** **an increase of more than** Rs. **2,300 crore over the** **outstanding of March 1992. The share of export credit in total net bank credit also increased from**

**8.6 per cent to 9.2 per cent during the same period.** **This was evident from the data collected by the** **RBI for monitoring flow of bank credit to export sector.** **Toe export credit refinance limits sanctioned to banks by RBI have also risen sharply from Rs.** **5594 crore to** Rs. **8064 crore between March 1992** **and 27th November 1992. In terms of percentage, export credit refinance limits have increased from**
##### 52.3 per cem in March 1992 to 62.0 per cent as on 27th November 1992.

##### In fact, bank credit to export sector has increased considerably over the past three years

##### as can be seen from the following figures:

**It may be recalled that the RBI has, in the recent past, taken several measures to increase the** **flow of credit to export sector considering its importance. Briefly, the measures include:**

- **Availability of pre-shipment credit at a rate of interest of 14 per cent upto 180 days and post** **shipment credit in rupees at the same rate in the initial stage, which is far lower than the** **commercial lending rate.**
- **Availability of post-shipment foreign currency denominated credit at a rate of interest of**
**6.5 per cent.**
- **Availability of RBI's export credit refinance window through which liberal refinance is** **available to banks despite the present tight monetary and credit policy.**
- **Close monitoring of the flow of credit to the export sector by RBI, so that no worthwhile** **export order is frustrated due to Jack of bank finance.**
