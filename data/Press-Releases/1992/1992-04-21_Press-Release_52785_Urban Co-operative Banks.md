Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks are advised to use utmost discretion in. offering deposits rates on various maturities within the cap. The deposit rates offered for .various maturities need to be based on the cost of funds, return on funds and the inter-bank borrowing/lending status of individual banks. At any point of time, individual banks-have to adopt uniform rates at all their branches and for all customers. While Primary (Urban) Co- operative Banks are free to determine the maturities and deposit rates, they would be required to prescribe a minimum of three maturities of their choice and the rates for each maturity should have an interest rate differential between any two successive maturity slabs of at least 0.25 percentage point.

On the lines of changes in the interest rate on deposits structure of the scheduled commercial banks, it has. also been decided that with effect from April 22, 1992, the deposit rates for maturity of 46 days to 3 years and above in the case of Primary (Urban) Co- operative Banks also would be a single prescription of'not exceeding 13% per annum'. The Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks (both scheduled and non-scheduled) will, however, have the discretion to pay an additional interest· not exceeding 0.5% per annum. **(c) Restriction on Credit to Certain** **Sectors** It has been decided to withdraw, with effect from April 22, 1992, the restriction on Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks' credit to the following sectors, viz., (i) Loans for purchases of consumer durables and (ii) Other non-priority sector personal loans. Banks have been advised to continue to ensure that there is no increase in credit outstanding to individuals against shares and debentures/ bonds. Furthermore, effective April 22, 1992, loans to individuals against shares and debentures/ bonds would be subject to a minimum margin of 75 per cent.
# ( d) Selective Credit Control

The changes in the selective credit controls announced in the case of scheduled commercial banks with effect from April 22, 1992, in respect of reference period, level of credit ceilings for paddy /rice and wheat and minimum margins for cotton and kapas will *ipso facto* be applicable in the case of Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks also.
