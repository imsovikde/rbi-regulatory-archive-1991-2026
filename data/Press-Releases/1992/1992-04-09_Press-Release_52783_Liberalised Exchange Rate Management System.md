# Liberalised Exchange Rate Management System

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *May 1992***

**Liberalised Exchange Rate Management** **System** **(April 9, 1992)** **It has been reported in a local English daily that announcement of an indicative free**

market rate by Foreign Exchange Dealers' Association of India does not really serve any purpose. It has also been stated that dealers are not aware of any FEDAI indicative free market rate. It is clarified that the fact that FEDAI would be announcing indicative free market exchange rate on every business day was made known to the authorised dealers in the Reserve Bank's A.O. Circular No. 4 dated 29th February 1992. The indicative free market exchange rate is flashed on the Reuter Monitor Screen by FEDAI after 12.00 noon every day on page FXIR. Similar rates for 3 other currencies viz.,Pound sterling, Deutsche mark and Japanese yen are also announced by FEDAI. The rates remain on the Screen throughout the day and upto 12.00 noon next day when they are changed.
