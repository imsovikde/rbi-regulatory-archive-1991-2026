# Change in the accounting year of the Co-operative Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *April /992***

# · Change in the accounting year of the Co-operative Banks

# (March 5, 1992)

**In exercise of the powers conferred under** **Sub-section (I) of Section 29 read with Clause**

**(s) of Section** *56* **of the Banking Regulation** **Act, 1949 ( JO of 1949) as amended recently, the Central Government has specified in the** **official gazette extra-ordinary·, the 31st day of** **March in each year as the date for the purpose of closing annual accounts by co-operative** **banks. Further, to facilitate transition from one period of accounting to another (i.e. from** **30 June to 31 March each year), the Central** **Government has issued an order in the above gazette stating that it shall be lawful for every** **co-operative bank to prepare a balance sheet and profit and loss account as on 31 March** **1992 with respect to the period comencing from the first day of July 1991 to the last day** **of March 1992. Accordingly, co-operative banks have been advised to prepare their** **annual accounts as on 31 March each year and as on 31 March 1992 for the period 1 July** **1991 to end-March 1992.** **The respective State Governments have also been requested to issue appropriate**
**notification under the State Co-operative** **Societies Acts to conform.. to the provisions of** **Banking Regulation Act *ibid.***
