# Proposal to set up National Rural Bank of India (NRBI)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***October 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

***Proposal to set up National* Rural Bank of India (NRBI)** **(August 28, 1992)** **A meeting of representatives of Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Agriculture and** **· Ministry of Rural Development, chairmen of a few selected banks and RBI officials was** **' taken by Governor today to discuss the** **feasibility of setting up** a **National Rural Bank** **of India to take care of Regional Rural Banks.** - **It was agreed that if an NRBI was set up all the 196 Regional Rural Banks should be** **merged to form a National Rural Bank which should have a federal structure. To consider** **the detailed modalities of the proposed bank, it was decided to set up two small working** **groups. The first group consisting of representatives of banks,the Indian Banks'** **Association and NABARD would examine operational issues while another group** **consisting of officials from RBI and** **N ABARD would examine matters relating to financing of the bank. Both the groups will** **work under a Steering Group to be coordinated by the NABARD and will submit** **their reports by the middle of September 1992 to the Reserve Bank. Based on these reports,** **the Reserve Bank will work out a proposal for the consideration of the Government, which** **will take a final decision in the matter.**
