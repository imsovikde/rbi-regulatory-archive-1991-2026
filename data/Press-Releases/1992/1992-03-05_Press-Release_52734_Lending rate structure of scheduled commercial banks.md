# Lending rate structure of scheduled commercial banks

# (March 5, 1992)

**The Reserve Bank of India has decided to reduce, effective March 2, 1992, the lending rate** **on credit limits of over Rs. 2 lakhs by one percentage point, from 20.0 per cent** **(minimum) to 19.0 per cent (minimum). All the scheduled commercial banks have been** **advised to reduce the rates for all borrowers ·** **. under this category by a minimum of one percentage point over the rates which were** **being charged to such borrowers prior to** **March 2, 1992. The details of the present.** **lending rate structure of all the scheduled commercial banks are given below :**
