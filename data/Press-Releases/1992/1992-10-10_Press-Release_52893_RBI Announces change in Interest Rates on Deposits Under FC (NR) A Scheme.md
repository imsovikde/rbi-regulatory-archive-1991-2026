# RBI Announces change in Interest Rates on Deposits Under FC (NR) A Scheme

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*November 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

RBI Announces change In Interest Rates on Deposits Under FC (NR) A Scheme {October 10, 19!>2)

Keeping in view the present level of interest rates on Pound Sterling, U.S. dollar, Deutsche mark and Japanese yen deposits in the overseas markets, the Reserve Bank of India announced changes in interest rates structure for deposits in these currencies under the Foreign Currency (Non-Resident) Accounts Scheme. While the in terest rates for Pound sterling deposits have been reduced by 1.25 per cent for 6 months, 1 per cent each for 1 year and 2 years and 0. 75 per cent for 3 years, the interest rates on U.S. dollar and Deutsche mark deposits have been reduced by

0.75 per cent for all the maturities. The interest rates on Japanese yen deposits have been reduced by 1 per cent each for 6 months and 1 year and
0.75 per cent each for 2 year and 3 year maturities. Tlie new schedule of interest rates for various currencies for transactions entered into with authorised dealers effective October 12, 1992 will be as under:-
