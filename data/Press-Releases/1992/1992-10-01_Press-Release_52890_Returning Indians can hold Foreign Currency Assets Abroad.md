***November 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Returning Indians can hold Foreign** **Currency Assets Abroad** **(October 1, 1992)** **Indians who return to India after a minimum continuous stay abroad of one year can now** **utilise balances in their bank accounts abroad.** **They can also utilise other foreign currency assets like shares, securities, immovable properties, etc.,** **earned or acquired by them lawfully (i.e. other wise than in contravention of FERA, 1973) while** **they were residents outside India freely. General exemptions have been granted by the central** **government and the Reserve Bank of India to this effect.**

**_ Indians who had returned to India on or after 18th April 1992 can maintain bank accounts** **abroad and/or hold other foreign currency assets, abroad without surrender/declaration to the** **Reserve Bank of India. They will also be free to utilise/dispose of these balances/assets without** **any permission from the Reserve Bank. They can also acquire new assets abroad out of the bank** **balances/income earned on/sale proceeds of as sets already held abroad.**
