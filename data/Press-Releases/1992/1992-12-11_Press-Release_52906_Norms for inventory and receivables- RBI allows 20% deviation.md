# Norms for inventory and receivables: RBI allows 20% deviation

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***January 1993* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLE11N**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Norms for inventory and receivables: RBI allows 20% deviation** **(December 11, 1992)** **With a view to mitigating the hardship of trade and industry, the RBI has advised banks that** **if warranted, they could permit upto 20 per cent deviation from the norms/past trend for the** **industry for holding of inventory and receivables.**

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *January 1993***

**The deviations permitted from norms should, however, be for a temporary period and banks** **should ensure that the levels of holding of inventory/receivables (current assets) are brought** **down to normal levels as soon as the situation permits.** **The RBI has in its circular issued to banks on December 10, 1992 also advised banks to** **permit this deviation in cases where the problems in· adherence to norms are industry-specific and** **need a review requesting banks to refer such cases to the RBI with their views for further** **examination.** **As per the RBI circular while permitting such deviations, banks should ensure that the additional** **credit made available is need-based and essential for meeting the borrowers' current level of** **production and is not utilised for speculative build-up of current assets under any** **circumstances.**
