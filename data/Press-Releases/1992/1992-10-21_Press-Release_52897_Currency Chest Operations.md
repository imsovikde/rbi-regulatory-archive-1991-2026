**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN** *November /992*

## Currency Chest Operations (October 21, 1992)

**Reports have appeared in some sections of the Press that monies held by the currency chests** **on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India are not subjected to any physical verification and that** **there is every likelihood of a gap between amounts shown in the registers of currency notes** **and actual cash balances.** **The Reserve Bank has a system of conduct ing inspection of over 4,300 currency chests** **spread all over the country through its teams of** **Officers. During such inspections, the main em phasis is to ensure the accuracy of the physical** **balances of cash held by the currency chests.** **Other aspects such as methods followed for hold ing chest balances in joint custody, security and** **storage of treasure etc., arrangements obtaining at the currency chests are also scrutinised and** **deficiencies observed brought to the notice of the banks concerned for corrective action.**

**The current instructions of the Reserve** **Bank governing the operation of currency chests require that the balance in every currency chest** **should be got physically verified every month by an Officer other than the Officer holding charge**
