# RBI caution to banks on PMS (August 5, 1992)

The Reserve Bank of India has cautioned banks that violtion of its guidelines on portfolio management scheme **(PMS)** will be viewed seriously and will invite deterrent action. In a circular issued recently to chairmen & chief executives of all commercial banks. the RBI has said that if banks are found violating RBI guidelines. it will not only prohibit them from undertaking the PMS activity but also take other deterrent action such as raising of reserve requirements, withdrawal of refinance from RBI and denial of access to money markets.

The RBI had specified the guidelines for PMS in January last year. It had, however, noticed that some banks were still considering **PMS** proposals with guaranteed returns. It had also observed that to avoid compliance with RBI's PMS guidelines, banks were accepting funds from clients for management under different schemes.

In view of this. the RBI has now made it clear that apart from deposit taking, banks should accept funds from their clients· for management only under terms and conditions stipulated for PMS.

The guidelines on **PMS,** as reiterated by RBI in the recent circular are:

(i) PMS should be entirely at customer's
risk, -without guaranteeing, either directly or indirectly, a pre-determined return.

(iv) Banks should maintain client-wise account/ record of funds accepted for management and investments and portfolio clients should be entitled to get a statement of account.

(v) Bank's own investments and invest

(vi) There should be a clear, functional separation of trading and back office functions relating to banks' own investment account and PMS clients' accounts.

The RBI had subsequently also made it mandatory to subject PMS clients' accounts to a separate audit by external auditors.

Besides, while prescribing detailed guide lines to banks regarding transactions in securities, the RBI had stipulated that no ready-forward and double ready-forwarddeals should be put through by banks inanysecurity including government securities, on behalf of PMS clients• accounts or on behalf of other constituents, including brokers.
