# Bank finance for infrastructure projects

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***January 1993* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLE11N**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Bank finance for infrastructure projects** **(December 11, 1992)** **With the changes in the overall policy pennitting private entrepreneurs to take up** **projects involving creation of infrastructural facilities, the RBI has lifted its ban on providing** **banlc finance for projects creating infrastructure but not financed through budgetary resources of** **the government.** **According to the RBI circular issued to banlcs, banlcs can now provide term loans for** **creation of infrastructural facilities to entrepreneurs/private sector undertakings, who** **undertake such projects at the instance or with the consent of Government of India/State** **Governments, without the support of budgetary allocations. In such cases, however, banlcs should** **ensure that the projects are technically feasible, financially viable and bankable. The rates of** **interest for such loans will have to be in conformity with the interest rate directives issued** **by the RBI from time to lime.** **The RBI circular also said that banks may provide term loan assistance preferably in** **panicipation with the State-level financial institutions for projects costing up to Rs.3 crore;** **whereas in case of projects costing more than** **Rs.3 crore bank finance should be in participation with the all-India financial institutions and to the** **extent of 25 per cent of the term loan component. Aggregate tenn finance from the** **banking system should not exceed Rs. 75 crore.**
