# Sale of goods/services against International Credit Cards

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*October 1992* RESERVE RANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

Sale of goods/ **services** against International **Credit Cards** **(September 9, 1992)** For sale of goods or services against International Credit Cards in lieu of receipt of foreign exchange the companies, firms concerned need a .licence under Section 7 of Foreign· Exchange Regulation Act. 1973. Application for obtaining the licence may **be** **n1ade** to the office **of** the Exchange Control Department of Reserve Bank of India under whose jurisdiction the applicant is resident.
