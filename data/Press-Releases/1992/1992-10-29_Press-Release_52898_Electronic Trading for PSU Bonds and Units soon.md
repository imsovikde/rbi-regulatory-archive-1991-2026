# Electronic Trading for PSU Bonds and Units soon

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***November 1992*** **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN** **Electronic Trading for PSU Bonds and** **Units soon (October 29, 1992)** **Announcing the acceptance of the Nadkami** **Committee's recommendation of introducing electronic clearing and depository system, the** **RBI, Governor, Shri S. Venkitaramanan said that** **UTI would work out such a system for trading in its own units whereas the Stock Holding** **Corporation of India Limited (SHCIL) would do it for PSU bonds. The Governor was** **speaking at the inaugural session of the international Conference on Securities Clearing** **and Settlement in India held here today.** **Nadkarni Committee set up to evolve an improved procedure for conducting transactions** **in PSU bonds and units of mutual funds had recommended an electronic clearing** **setOement and depository for such transactions.** **The Governor, howev er, ruled out reintroduction of ready forward transactions in** **the transitory phase as recommended by the** **Panel. He said that banks would have liquidity in** **Government securities through the refinance facility and the repo auction facility to be** **introduced by te RBI as announced recently in the busy season credit policy for 1992-93.** **The UTI and SHCIL, on the *other* hand, have been re-**

**quested to work out the electronic system for units and PSU bonds, respectively, in the next** **two-three months. To ensure alignment of deal rates with market rates, the Governor mentioned** **that a small group consisting of a representative each from RBI, SEBI and SHCIL would be set** **up.** **Ref erring to other reforms in the financial sector, the Governor dwelt on capital adequacy of** **banks at length. He reiterated that there was no point in declaring profits based on concealment** **of the true state of the health of the banks. There was no question of diluting the norms specified** **for provisioning for bad debts and income recog nition. "It is a strong and considered decision of** **the RBI and we will help banks in resolving their difficulties in its implementation by helping them** **increase their profits," he said. A small group under Deputy Governor, Shri S.S. Tarapore was** **set up to sort out banks' difficulties in this regard, he added.**

**In this context, he also ref erred to the capital adequacy norms that the banks would have to** **meet. This he said could be done by allowing them to approach the capital markets. Cross-hold** **ings of equity between PSUs and public sector banks and offering the equity to bank employees** **were other alternatives available to banks for rais ing their capital. He, however, ruled out** **denationalisation of banks and said that banks would be allowed to raise only additional capital** **through such methods for tier II as recommended** **· by the Basie Committee.** **Referring to the proposed asset reconstruc tion fund for cleaning up the banks' balance sheet** **of bad debts, the Governor said that this was a** **"moral hazard" as there was no guarantee that once cleaned up, banks would not be saddled** **with this problem again. Productivity of employees and quality of loan portfolio were the** **most important assets of banks, he said. Talking about the need to improve productivity, the** **Governor said that banks should hasten the com puterisation process. There was no reason why** **computerisation should be discussed at the time of wage negotiations only, he said.**
