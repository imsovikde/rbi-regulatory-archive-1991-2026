### Deposit Rates:

* With effect from October 9, 1992,
interest rates on fresh deposits and on renewal on maturity of deposits for maturity of 46 days to 3 years and above to be reduced by one percentage point-from 'not exceeding 13 per cent per annum' to 'not exceeding 12 per cent per annum.

* Interest rate oli savings accounts under
NRE scheme increased by one percentage point from 5 per cent to 6 per cent with effect from October 9, 1992. Interest rates on term deposits under this category also rationalised with a single prescription of 'not exceeding 13 per cent' for maturity of 46 days to three years and over.

Foreign Currency (Ordinary) Non- Repatriable Deposits (FCON):

* Banks to be exempt from maintaining
cash reserve ratio and statutory liquidity ratio on foreign currency (ordinary) non- repatriable deposits. While banks to be free to determine interest rates on such deposits, they will have to bear the exchange rate guarantee.

### Certificates of Deposit:
