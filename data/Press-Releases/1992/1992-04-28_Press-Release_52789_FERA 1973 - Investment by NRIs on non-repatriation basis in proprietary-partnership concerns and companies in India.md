# FERA 1973 - Investment by NRIs on non-repatriation basis in proprietary/partnership concerns and companies in India

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*June /992* RESERVE BANK OF **INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**FERA 1973 - lnvestmel\t by NRis on non repatriation basis in proprietary/** **partnership concerns and companies in** **India** **(April 28, 1992)** **With a view to simplifying the procedures relating to NRI investment, the regulations** **governing direct investment by NRis on non repatriation basis in proprietary/ partnership** **concerns and shares/ debentures of Indian companies have been liberalised further with** **immediate effect. Reserve Bank has issued two Notifications bearing Nos. FE RA 113** **and 114/92 dated 27th April 1992. The former notifies grant of general permission under** **Section 9 read with Section 29 of the FERA** **1973 to proprietary/ partnership concerns in** **India engaged in any industrial, commercial or trading activity for accepting investment by** **way of capital contribution from NRis and also permitting NRis to invest in such** **concerns. The Notification No. FERA 114/92 dated 27th April 1992 permits Indian** **companies to issue shares/ convertible debentures to NRis /OCBs on non** **repatriation basis under Section 19 read with** **Section 29 of FERA 1973.** **The investment is subject to the condition that the capital invested and income accruing** **thereon will not be allowed to be repatriated outside India at any time in future.** **The investee concern/ company has to file a declaration in the prescribed fom within 90** **days from the date of accepting investment.** **As a result of the above Notifications proprietary/ partnership firms and Indian** **companies will not be required to approach** **Reserv Bank of India for permission for accepting capital contribution from NRls or** **for issue of shares/ debentures to NRls/ OCBs on non-repatriation basis.**
