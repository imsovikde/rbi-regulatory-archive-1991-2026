The detailed guidelines in this regard are indicated below:

## (I) Shifting of bank branches from one

centre to another in rural and semi-urban areas

While banks cannot close down branches in rural areas, in order to enable 'them to rationalise their branch network in rural/semi- urban areas, it has now been decided that such shifting may be effected by banks themselves without obtaining the prior approval of the Reserve Bank subject to their complying with the following conditions:

(i) Both the existing and proposed centres
are within the same block and service area of the branch.

## (ii) It should be ensured that the relocated

branch would be able to cateradequatelyto the banking needs of the villages allocated under Service Area Approach.

(II) Shifting of bank branches in urban, metropolitan and port town centres

Banks may now take a decision in regard to shifting of their branches in urban/ metro- politan/ port town centres, within the same locality/ municipal ward, without the prior. approval of the Reserve Bank.

(III) Splitting or pal'! shifting ofbranches within the same centres

Banks can now take a decision for splitting or part shifting of branches within the same centre without obtaining the priorapproval of Reserve Bank subject to the conditions that identical business is not conducted from both premises.

## (IV) Spinning-off of business

It has now been decided *that* banks may resort to spin-off of the business at their

discretion without obtaining the priorapproval of the Reserve Bank.

(V) Opening ofregional/administrative/ zonal/controlling offices of banks The question of opening or closing
regional/administrative/ zonal/ controlling offices is now entirely left to the discretion of the banks.

## (VI) Opening of service branches

Banks are at present allowed to open service branches/ regional collection centres for facilitating clearing and allied work at bigger centres as per the report ofthe IBA Sub- Committee on standardisation of procedures pertaining to collection, business. It has now been decided that banks may open suchservice .branches without the prior approval of Reserve Bank of India.

## (VII) Opening of specialised branches

# It has now been decided to liberalise the

procedure relating to opening of specialised branches and banks may hereafter take a decision without the prior approval of Reserve Bank of India.

In view of the liberalisations proposed in the branch licensing policy, banks are advised to designate one of their specific branches in each district for the purpose. of housing finance, in addition to their normal banking functions.

As specialised branches are expected to take over the business from other local branches, they should normally be opened at centres where the bank has already 10 or-more, branches and/ or there is sufficient potential for adequate business without adversely affecting viability of other branches.
