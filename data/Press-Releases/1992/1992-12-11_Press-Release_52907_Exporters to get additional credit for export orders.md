# Exporters to get additional credit for export orders

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *January 1993***

**Exporters to get additional credit for export orders** **(December 11, 1992)** **In an attempt to meet the exporters' credit demands, the RBI advised the chief executives of** **all commercial banks to ensure that the credit requirements of the export sector are promptly**

**met. The** RBI **circular issued on December** I 0, **1992 also emphasised that banks should meet in full the additional credit needs of exporters for** **implementing export orders even if sanction of such additional credit exceeded maximum** **permissible bank finance limits.** **It may be recalled that RBI had in November advised banks to ensure timely flow of need based** **credit to their exporter-clients. The RBI Governor had also advised bank chairmen, to reach the** **target of 10 per cent of each bank's credit in export credit. It may be mentioned here that to** **monitor the flow of export credit on a quarterly basis, the RBI has simplified its format seeking** **data on export credit. Banks are required to send this data within specified time period.**
