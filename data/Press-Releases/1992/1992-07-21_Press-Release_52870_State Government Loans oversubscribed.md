# State Government Loans oversubscribed (July 21, 1992)

**The total amount ofsubscriptions received** **Jor the 15-year State Government loans was** **Rs. 1,450 crore, as against the notified amount of Rs. 1,200 crore. The loans carrying a**

## coupon rate of 13 per cent per annum were

**opened on** 20 **July and closed on the same** **day. While all the 24 States have received the notified amounts, six States viz., Gujarat,**

## Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar

## Pradesh and West Bengal have received additional subscription. The concerned States

## have issued separate notifications raising the original notified amounts.
