# Auction of Government of India Dated Securities

# (May 28, 1992)

**The Government of India has announced the sale of dated securities on an auction basis.** **This is _a departure from the practice followed hitherto of floatation of dated securifies at par** **with fixed coupon rates.**

**The auction** will **be conducted by the** **Reserve Bank of India. The salient f eatures of the scheme are :**

**(i) Sale of 5-year Government of India securities by auction for an aggregate amount**
**of Rs. 1,000 crore.** **(ii) Stock will be issued for a minimum amout of Rs. l0,000 (face value) and in** **multiples of Rs. 10,000 thereafter.** **(iii) Bids should be submitted on June 3,** *1992***-**the date **of** auction-between **11 A.M.,**

**and 3 P.M. by interested persons in a prescribed form which can be obtained from** **Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Fort,** **Bombay.** **· (iv) The results of the auction will be displayed by Reserve Bank of India, Bombay** **on June 4,1992.**

**(v) Successful bidders will be required to deposit with Reserve Bank of India, Bombay**
**the price amount of the stock covered by their bids, during banking hours on June 5, 1992 in ·** **cash or by a banker's pay order or cheque on their account with the RBI, Bombay.** **(vi) The yield per cent per annum expected by the bidder should be expressed up to two** **decimal points in multiples of five. On the basis of bids received, the RBI will determine** **the maximum rate of yield at which offers will be accepted. The cut off yield will be the** **coupon rate on the stock sold at the auction.** **(vii) Successful bids at the cut off rate determined by RBI will be accepted at par.** **Other successful bids tendered at lower rates will be accepted at prices above par according** **to the yield expected by the bidder. Other bids at rates higher than the cut off yield will be** **. rejected.** **(viii)Government stock will be issued to parties by credit to their Subsidiary General** **Ledger account maintained with RBI and in the form of stock certificates to others.** **The issue of a 5-year dated security by the** **Government of India on an auction basis is in tune with the objective of developing the** **government securities market. The measure also aims at developing dated securities as a** **monetary instrument with flexible yields to suit investor expectations and meeting·** **government credit needs directly -from the market.**
