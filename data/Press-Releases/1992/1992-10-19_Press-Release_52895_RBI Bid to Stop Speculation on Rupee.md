# RBI Bid to Stop Speculation on Rupee

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***November 1992*** **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN 188S**

**RBI Bid to Stop Speculation on Rupee** **(October 19, 1992)** **In terms of the extant instructions,** **Authorised Dealers are expected to maintain at the close of business each day square or near** **square position in each foreign currency. In order to prevent speculative positions being taken** **against the rupee, the RBI today instructed the** **Authorised Dealers not to have oversold position in the rupee against-foreign currencies** **in excess of US$ one million or its equivalent at the close of business on each day with** **immediate effect.**
