# Foreign Exchange Conservation (Travel) Tax

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***July 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Foreign Exchange Conservation** **(Travel) Tax** **(June 1, 1992)** **By virtue of the provisions in Chapter V of the Finance Act, 1987, travellers drawing** **exchange for travel abroad, other than the categories specifically exempted, are required** **to pay the Foreign Exchange Conservation** **(Travel) Tax at prescribed rates. Government of India have since abolished the levy of** **foreign travel tax with effect from 1.6.1992.** **Suitable instructions have been issued by RBI to authorised dealers/ money changers in this** **regard.**
