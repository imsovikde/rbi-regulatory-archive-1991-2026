# Interest Rates on Savings Bank Deposits of State and Central Co-operative Banks and Regional Rural Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK• OF INDIA BULLETIN May /992

# Interest Rates on Savings Bank Deposits of State and Central Co-operative Banks

# and Regional Rural Banks (April 24, 1992)

**It** has been decided to raise the interest rate on savings deposits of State and Central Co-operative Banks and Regional Rural Banks by one percentage point from 5% per annum to 6% per annum, effective 24 April

1992. Further, in respect of savings deposits the discretion available to offer- additional .interest of ¼ % per annum by State Cooperative Banks and ½% per annum **by** Central Co-operative Banks and Regional Rural Bank will continue.
