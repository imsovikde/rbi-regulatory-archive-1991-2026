# Good response to Government of India Securities - 2002 auction

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN *September 1992*

## Good response to Government of India Securities -2002 auction (August 4, 1992)

## The Reserve Bank of India received bids for an aggregate amount of Rs. 1.390.50 crore

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

**RF.SER VE BANK OF INDI..\ Bll 1.1.FTIN**

against the notified amount of Rs. 1.000 crore (nominal) at the auction held on August 3, 1992 for sale of JO-year Government of India securities, 2002. Of the 80 bids received, the RBI accepted 56 bids for Rs. 646.50 crore and subscribed to the balance amount of Rs. 353.50 crore. The accepted bids were quoted at the · cut off yield of 12.75 per cent per annum and below. The coupon rate on the security has been fixed at 12.75 per cent per annum.
