# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Violation of lendi_ng discipline not acceptable : RBI Governor** **(November 4, 1992)** **At a meeting held here today of major bank s, representafryes of All-lndia** **Chambers/ Confederations/ Associations as also officials from the Government of India,** **the Governor categorically stated that violation of lending disciplines would not be** **accepted. Consequent to the issue of the RBI cir cular dated Ju ly 27, 1992 on** **discounting/ re-discounting of bills as also on lending under consortium arrangement, a** **number of representations had been Il!ade by the Chambers/Confederations/ Associations** **of Trade and Industry voicing• their concern on the hardship faced by the industry on** **account of lack of adequate credit for financing their operations.** **To find a solution to the immediate problem of the industry, the meeting also** **decided that the units facing difficulties should approach their regular banks within** **the next 10-15 days to sort out the problems.** **The Go"'.ernor desired that banks, which had extended both fund and non-fund based** **credit to customers who are not their regular clients,**

**should submit such details to the leader of the consortium/regular banker _and the RBI. A** **meeting may also be held by the Indian Banks'** **Association with the rep·resentatives of the trade and industry within the next fortnight to** **discuss the long term issues such as changes in the policy, system, etc.**

**The representatives of industry expressed their views that the banks should be more** **flexible, and responsive to their needs, particularly, in the changing scenario after** **liberalisation of the economy. They also averred that quite often the lending discipline** **norms, particularly, computation of the** **Maximum Permissible Bank Finance and restrictions imposed on banks regarding** **lending under· consortium arrangement, had come in the way of the industry getting** **adequate bank finance. It may be added here that the credit norms and the consortium** **arrangements are being reviewed presently by the IBA. The industry also mentioned that** **there was a delay in sanctioning credit limits by the banks:Pursuant to RBI's instructions of** **. not granting non-fund based limits outside consortium, a large number of industries had** **been unable to raise resources by discounting genuine trade bills.**

**Representatives of the banks on the other hand, argued that they were aware of the** **liquidity crunch being faced by the industry and had sanctioned a number of ad-hoc credit** **proposals enhancing the existing credit limits.** **They opined that borrowers should be open** **·and prompt in submitting iQformation to them. They assured that all genuine credit** **needs could be met by banks within the financial discipline and there was no need to** **have recourse to either non-banking financial institutions or other banks (which are not** **members of the consortia or regular bankers) for additional credit needs.**
