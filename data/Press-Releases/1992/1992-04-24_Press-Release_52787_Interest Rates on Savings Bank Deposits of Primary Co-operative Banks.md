## RESERVE BANK• OF INDIA BULLETIN May /992

# Interest Rates on Savings Bank Deposits of Primary Co-operative Banks

# (April 24, 1992)

Consequent on the announcement **by** the Governor of Reserve Bank of India to raise the rates on savings deposits of scheduled commercial banks, it has been decided to raise the interest rates on savings deposits of Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks by I percentage point from 5% per annum to 6% per annum, effective April 24, 1992. Further, the discretion given t.o these banks (scheduled as also others) to allow additional interest at a rate not exceeding *1%* per annum on savings deposits is continued, as hitherto.
