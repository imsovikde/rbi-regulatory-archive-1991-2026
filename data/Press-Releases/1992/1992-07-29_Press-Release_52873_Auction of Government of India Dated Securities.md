# Auction of Government of India Dated Securities

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*September 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

**Auction of Government of India Dated** **Securities (July 29, 1992)** **The Government of India has announced the sale of Rs. 1,000 crore dated securities of** **ten year maturity on an auction basis. This is the second auction of dated securities during** **the current financial year. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India at** **Bombay. The first auction of dated government securities which were of 5-year** **maturity was held on June 3, 1992.** **The issue of dated securities on an auction basis is a major step in the direction of** **developing the government securities market.** **The measure also aims at developing dated securities as a monetary instrument with** **flexible yields to suit investor expectations and meeting government credit needs direct** **from the market.** **The salient features of the present auction are:**

**(i) Ten year Government of India securities will be sold by auction for an aggregate** **amount of Rs. I ,000 crore.** **(ii) The -stock will be issued for a minimum amount of Rs. 10,000 (face value) and in** **multiples of Rs. 10,000 thereafter.**
**(iii) Bids should be submitted on** **August 3, 1992 between 11 a.m. and** **3p.m. by interested persons in a prescribed form which can be obtained** **from Manager, Reserve Bank of India,** **Public Debt Office, Fort, Bombay.** **(iv) The yield per cent per annum expected by the bidder should be expressed upto** **two· decimal points in multiples of five.** **On the basis of bids received, the Reserve** **,Bank of India will determine the maximum rate of yield at which offers** **will be accepted. The cut off yield will be the coupon rate on the stock sold at the** **auction.**

**(v) Successful bids at the cut off rate determined by Reserve Bank of India** **will be accepted at par. Other successful bid tendered at lower rates will be** **accepted at prices above par according to the yield expected by the bidder. Other** **bids at rates higher than the cut off yield will be rejected.**
**(vi) The results of the_ auction will be displayed by Reserve Bank of India,** **Bombay on August 4, 1992.** **(vii) Successful bidders will be required to deposit with Reserve Bank of India,** **Bombay the price amount of the stock covered by their bids, during banking**

**hours on August** *5,* **1992 in cash or by a** **banker's pay order or cheque on their account with the Reserve Bank of India,** **Bombay.** **(viii) Government stock will be issued by credit to Subsidiary General Ledger** **account of parties maintaining such accounts with Reserve Bank of India and** **in the form of stock certificates to others.**
