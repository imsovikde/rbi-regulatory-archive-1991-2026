# Trade between India and Russian Federation under the Trade Protocol for 1992

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***July 1992* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Trade between India and Russian** **Federation under the Trade Protocol for 1992** **(June 2, 1992)** **In pursuance of a Memorandum of** **Understanding signed between the Govern ment of India and Government of Russian** **Federation on 5th May 1992, the former has agreed to the advance utilisation of technical** **credit to the extent of US $ 85 million for export of specified goods from India viz., tea,** **coffee, tobacco, spices including black pepper, · cigarettes, medicines and fruit pulp con** **centrates with a view to initiating trade under the Indo-Russian Trade Protocol for 1992. It** **has been decided that initially export of tobacco and cigarettes to the extent of US $ 25** **million and US $ 4 million respectively will be allowed to be made from India to Russia,** **pending finalisation of modalities for carrying out trade in respect of other items under** **Trade Protocol. All export transactions shall**

Necessary instructions have been issued by Reserve Bank of India to** **the Authorised Dealers.**
