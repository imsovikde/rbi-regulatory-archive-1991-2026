# Banks authorised to grant pre-shipment credit (or longer periods

# (March 12, 1992)

**With a view to supporting export efforts and giving operational flexibility to banks, the** **Reerve Bank of India have authorised banks to extend pre-shipment ·credit for periods** **b_eyond 180 days but up to 270 days in cases whee exporters have-not been able to ship the** **oods within the specified period of 180 days** **. for reasons beyond their control.- Banks have also been authorised to extend pre-shipment**

Hitherto, banks were required to obtain prior approval of the** **Reserve Bank of India for extending pre shi'pment credit for periods beyond 180 days.** **Banks would charge interest on pre shipment credit (i) for the period up to 180** **days and (ii) for the period beyond 180 days but up to 270 days, at the rates stipulated by·** **Reserve Bank of India from time to time.** **Presently, the rates of interest are 15 per cent and 17 per cent, respectively.** **Detailed guidelines have been issued to banks by Reserve Bank of India iri this regard.**
