## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹33,000 crore on November 27, 2026

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auction to be held on **November 27, 2026 (Friday)**.

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **November 27, 2026 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Reserve Bank of India’s Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
