April 29, 2026 **Relief Measures in areas affected by Calamities** The Reserve Bank of India had, on <u>January 27, 2026</u>, issued the Draft Directions on Relief Measures in areas affected by Natural Calamities for stakeholder feedback.

2. Feedback received on the draft Directions has been examined and consequent modifications, as deemed appropriate, have been suitably incorporated in the final Directions. A statement on the feedback received on the draft Directions is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the final Directions and two repeal Directions as given below:
