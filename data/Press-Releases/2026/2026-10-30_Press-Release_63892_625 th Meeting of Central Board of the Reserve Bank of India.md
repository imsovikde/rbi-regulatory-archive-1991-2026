## October 30, 2026

**th** **625 Meeting of Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India**

th The 625 meeting of the Central Board of Directors of Reserve Bank of India was held today in Mumbai under the Chairmanship of Shri Sanjay Malhotra, Governor. The Central Board took the Integrity Pledge as part of the observance of Vigilance Awareness Week 2026.

The Board in its meeting deliberated on the prevailing economic conditions and associated challenges and evaluated the potential risks to the economic outlook. The Board reviewed the activities of Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation and activities of select Central Office Departments of the Bank.

Deputy Governors, Shri Swaminathan J., Dr. Poonam Gupta, Shri Shirish Chandra Murmu, Shri Rohit Jain, and other Directors of the Central Board, viz., Ms. Anuradha Thakur, Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs; Shri Anand Gopal Mahindra, Shri Janmejaya Kumar Sinha, Shri Syed Akbaruddin and Ms. Annie George Mathew attended the meeting.
