## September 04, 2026

## Inclusion of “Coastal Local Area Bank Limited” in the Second Schedule to the

## Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

Coastal Local Area Bank Limited has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DoR.LIC.No.S3539/22-03- 070/2026-27 dated July 28, 2026 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated August 11, 2026.
