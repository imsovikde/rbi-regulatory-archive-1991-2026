## August 20, 2026

**RBI to conduct Second Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction under LAF on August 20, 2026**

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to conduct a Second Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction on Thursday, August 20, 2026, as under: **Notified Amount Tenor** **Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(₹ crore) (day)** 1 50,000 1 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM August 21, 2026 (Friday)
