4. The Reserve Bank reserves the right to:
- decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities.
- accept offers for less than the aggregate amount.
- purchase marginally higher/lower than the aggregate amount due to rounding- off.
- accept or reject any or all the offers either wholly or partially without assigning any reasons.
5. Eligible participants should submit their offers in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system **between 9:30 am and 10:30** **am on January 29, 2026**. Only in the event of system failure, physical offers would be accepted. Such physical offers should be submitted to Financial Markets Operations Department (<u>email</u>, Phone no: 022-22630982) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) **before 10:30 am**.
6. The result of the auction will be announced on the same day and successful participants should ensure availability of securities in their SGL account by **12 noon** **on January 30, 2026**.
