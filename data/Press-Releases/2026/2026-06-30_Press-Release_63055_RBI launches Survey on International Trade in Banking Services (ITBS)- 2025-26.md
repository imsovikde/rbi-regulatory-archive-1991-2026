## June 30, 2026

## RBI launches Survey on International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS):

## 2025-26

The Reserve Bank of India has launched the 2025-26 round of its <u>Survey on</u> <u>International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS)</u>.

The survey, conducted annually since 2006-07, provides information on financial services rendered by branches/subsidiaries/joint ventures of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India, based on explicit / implicit fees / commission charged to customers.

The survey schedule for the 2025-26 round is required to be filled in by the banks for branches/subsidiaries/joint venture of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India. The soft form of this survey schedule (both in <u>Hindi</u> and <u>English</u> – one of which can be used) is available on the RBI website under the head ‘Regulatory Reporting’-**→** ‘<u>List of Returns</u>’-**→** ‘ITBS – Survey Schedule’ [or under the head ‘Forms’ (available under the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’]. Duly filled-in schedule can be <u>emailed</u> by July 31, 2026.
