The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **April 02, 2026 (Thursday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
