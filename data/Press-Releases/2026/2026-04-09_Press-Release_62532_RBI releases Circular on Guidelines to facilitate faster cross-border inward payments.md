## April 09, 2026

## RBI releases Circular on Guidelines to facilitate faster cross-border inward payments

The Reserve Bank of India today issued the circular on <u>Guidelines to facilitate</u> <u>faster cross-border inward payments</u>.

The RBI had issued a <u>draft circular</u> on the subject on October 29, 2025, seeking feedback from stakeholders. Feedback received on the draft circular has been examined and consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the <u>final</u> circular. Statement on the major feedback received on the draft circular is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.

This circular is intended to address certain frictions identified in inward cross- border payments to facilitate the timely intimation of payment information and crediting of funds to the beneficiary’s account.
