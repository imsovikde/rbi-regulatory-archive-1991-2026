## June 05, 2026

## Recognition of Self-Regulatory Organisation for the Account Aggregator

## Ecosystem

The Reserve Bank had issued the press release on <u>'Invitation of applications for</u> <u>recognition of Self-Regulatory Organisation(s) for the Account Aggregator Ecosystem</u> <u>(SRO-AA)' dated March 12, 2025</u> inviting applications for recognising an SRO-AA.

2. An application seeking recognition as an SRO-AA by the Reserve Bank was received from Sahamati Foundation. The application was examined against the relevant requirements as prescribed under the <u>‘Framework for recognising Self-</u> <u>Regulatory Organisations (SROs) for the Account Aggregator Ecosystem’ dated</u> <u>March 12, 2025</u>.
3. Based on the examination, it has been decided to recognise Sahamati Foundation as an SRO for the Account Aggregator Ecosystem.
