## August 24, 2026

## Appointment of Shri Janmejaya Kumar Sinha, Shri Syed Akbaruddin and Ms.

**Annie George Mathew as part-time, Non-official Directors on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India**

The Central Government has appointed Shri Janmejaya Kumar Sinha, Shri Syed Akbaruddin and Ms. Annie George Mathew as part-time, Non-official Directors on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from August 24, 2026 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
