## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

||The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following||
|---|---|---|
|Surveys:|i) Urban Consumer Confidence Survey (UCCS) – March 2026 ii) Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – March 2026 iii) Rural Consumer Confidence Survey (RCCS) – March 2026 iv) OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q3: 2025-26 v) Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q4: 2025-26 vi) Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – Round|1|
||th 99 vii) Bank Lending Survey for Q4: 2025-26 viii) Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q4: 2025-26 The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.||

## Press Release: 2026-2027/46
