## August 04, 2026

## Scheme for writing books originally in Hindi on Economics/Banking/Financial subjects- Year 2025-26

With a view to encourage original writings and research in Banking Hindi, Reserve Bank of India has launched a ‘**Scheme for writing books originally in Hindi on** **Economics/Banking/Financial subjects**’. Under this scheme, working/retired Professors (Including Assistant and Associate Professor etc.) of Indian Universities (UGC recognized) are awarded three prizes of ₹1,25,000.00 (One lakh twenty-five thousand) each for writing books originally in Hindi on subjects related to Economics/Banking/Finance. Details of the scheme are given in the <u>annex</u>. All concerned willing to participate in the scheme are requested to submit their nominations along with copies of books in the prescribed proforma to **Chief General** **Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Rajbhasha Department, Central Office, C-9,** **8th Floor, Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai - 400 051** by **August 31, 2026,** before

05.00 PM.
