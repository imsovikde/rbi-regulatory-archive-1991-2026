## August 18, 2026

**RBI to conduct Second Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction under LAF on August 18, 2026**

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to conduct a Second Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction on Tuesday, August 18, 2026, as under: **Notified Amount Tenor** **Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(₹ crore) (day)** 1 75,000 1 12:00 Noon to 12:30 PM August 19, 2026 (Wednesday)

2. The operational guidelines for the auction as given in the Reserve Bank’s <u>Press</u> <u>Release 2019-2020/1947 dated February 13, 2020</u> will remain the same.
