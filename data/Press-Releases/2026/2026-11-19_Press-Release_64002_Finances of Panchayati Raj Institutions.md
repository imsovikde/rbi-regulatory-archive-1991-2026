## November 19, 2026

## Finances of Panchayati Raj Institutions

Today, the Reserve Bank released the second edition of its biennial Report titled “<u>Finances of Panchayati Raj Institutions</u>”. The theme of this edition is ‘Local Finances and Capacity Building in Rural India’. Drawing upon a balanced panel of 2.25 lakh *Gram* *Panchayats* for the period 2022-23 to 2024-25, the Report presents an assessment of their finances and their role in achieving sustainable and inclusive rural socio-economic development. **Highlights:**
