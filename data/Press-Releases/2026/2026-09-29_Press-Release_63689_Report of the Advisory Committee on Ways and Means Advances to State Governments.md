## September 29, 2026

## Report of the Advisory Committee on Ways and Means Advances to State

## Governments

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website the <u>Report of the</u> <u>Advisory Committee on Ways and Means Advances (WMA) to State Governments</u>.

The Reserve Bank had constituted an Advisory Committee, under the Chairmanship of Shri I S N Prasad, former Additional Chief Secretary, Government of Karnataka. The terms of reference of the Advisory Committee included, *inter alia*, review of State-wise WMA limits and other financial accommodation facilities available to the States.

The Committee has made various recommendations which shall be examined by the Reserve Bank.
