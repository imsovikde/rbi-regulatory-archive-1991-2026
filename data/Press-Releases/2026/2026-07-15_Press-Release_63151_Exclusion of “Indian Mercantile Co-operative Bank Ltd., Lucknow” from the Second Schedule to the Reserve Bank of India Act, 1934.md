## July 15, 2026

## Exclusion of “Indian Mercantile Co-operative Bank Ltd., Lucknow” from the

## Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

Indian Mercantile Co-operative Bank Ltd., Lucknow has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification CO.DOR.RAUG.No.S1887/08.08.007/2026-27 dated June 04, 2026 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated June 22, 2026.
