## November 25, 2026

**47** **th** **Half-Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves:** **April – September 2026**

It may be recalled that in <u>February 2004</u>, the Reserve Bank initiated the practice of compiling half-yearly reports on the management of foreign exchange reserves and making them public to promote greater transparency and enhance disclosure in the management of the country’s foreign exchange reserves.

In this connection, Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>47th Half-Yearly</u> <u>Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves</u> with reference to end- September 2026.

During April-September 2026, India’s foreign exchange reserves increased from US$ 691.11 billion to US$ 737.78 billion. The latest position of India’s foreign exchange reserves is available at the following link:

<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28849](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28849)</u>
