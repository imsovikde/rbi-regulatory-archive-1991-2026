## October 09, 2026

## RBI announces OMO Sale of Government of India Securities

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank has decided to conduct an OMO sale auction of Government of India securities for an aggregate amount of ₹25,000 crore on October 13, 2026, through multi-security auction using the multiple price method.

2. The securities being offered for sale in the auction are as below:

₹25,000 crore (There is no security- wise notified amount)

3. The Reserve Bank reserves the right to:
- decide on the quantum of sale of individual securities.
- accept bids for less than the aggregate amount.
- sell marginally higher/lower than the aggregate amount due to rounding-off.
- accept or reject any or all the bids either wholly or partially without assigning any reason.
4. Eligible participants should submit their bids in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system **between 9:30 am and 10:30** **am on October 13, 2026**. Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to Financial Markets Operations Department (<u>email</u>, Phone no: 022-22630982) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) **before 10:30 am**.
5. The result of the auction will be announced on the same day and successful participants should ensure availability of funds in their current account by **12 noon on** **October 14, 2026**.
