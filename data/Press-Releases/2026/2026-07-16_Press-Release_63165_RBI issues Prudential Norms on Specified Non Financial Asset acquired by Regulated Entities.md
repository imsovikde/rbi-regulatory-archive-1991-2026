## July 16, 2026

## RBI issues Prudential Norms on Specified Non Financial Asset acquired by

## Regulated Entities

The Reserve Bank of India had, on <u>May 05, 2026</u>, issued the Draft Directions on Prudential Norms on Specified Non-financial Assets (SNFA) Directions for stakeholder feedback.

2. Feedback received on the draft Directions has been examined and consequent modifications, as deemed appropriate, have been suitably incorporated in the final Directions. A statement on the feedback received on the draft Directions is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the following amendment Directions
i. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Third Amendment Directions, 2026</u> ii. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
iii. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Resolution of</u> <u>Stressed Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u> iv. <u>Reserve Bank of India (All India Financial Institutions – Resolution of</u> <u>Stressed Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u>

v. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Third Amendment Directions, 2026</u> vi. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Rural Co-operative Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
vii. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u> viii. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks – Resolution of Stressed</u> <u>Assets) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u> ix. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Income Recognition, Asset</u> <u>Classification and Provisioning) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u>

x. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Income Recognition, Asset</u> <u>Classification and Provisioning) Second Amendment Directions, 2026</u> xi. <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Income</u> <u>Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Third Amendment</u> <u>Directions, 2026</u>
