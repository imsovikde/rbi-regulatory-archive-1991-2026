## Result of Underwriting Auction conducted on June 19, 2026

In the underwriting auction conducted on June 19, 2026, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount (₹ crore)|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount (₹ crore)|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted (₹ crore)|Total Amount Commission underwritten (₹ crore)|ACU Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|6.03% GS 2029|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.50|
|6.68% GS 2033|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.38|
|7.24% GS 2055|5,000|2,520|2,480|5,000|0.59|
|7.50% GOI SGrB 2056|5,000|2,520|2,480|5,000|0.99|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on June 19, 2026.
