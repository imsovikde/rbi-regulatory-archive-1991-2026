## October 07, 2026

## Exclusion of “Paytm Payments Bank Limited” from the Second Schedule to the

## Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

Paytm Payments Bank Limited has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DoR.LIC.No.S3674/16.13.215/2026-27 dated July 31, 2026 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated September 07, 2026.
