## June 05, 2026

## Shri Swaminathan Janakiraman re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor

The Central Government has re-appointed Shri Swaminathan Janakiraman as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a period of two years with effect from June 26, 2026, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
