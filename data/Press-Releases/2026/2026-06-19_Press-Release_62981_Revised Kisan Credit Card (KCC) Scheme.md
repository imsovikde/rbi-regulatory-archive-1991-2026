## June 19, 2026

## Revised Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

The Reserve Bank of India had, on <u>February 12, 2026</u>, issued the Draft Directions on Revised Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme for public / stakeholder feedback.

2. Feedback received on the draft Directions has been examined and consequent modifications, as deemed appropriate, have been suitably incorporated in the final Directions. A statement on the feedback received on the draft Directions is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the final Directions as given below:
