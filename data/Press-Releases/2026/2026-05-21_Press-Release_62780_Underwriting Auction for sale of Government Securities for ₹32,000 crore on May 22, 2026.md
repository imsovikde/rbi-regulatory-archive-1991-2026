## May 21, 2026

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹32,000 crore on May 22, 2026

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auction to be held on **May 22, 2026 (Friday)**.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting commitment notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore)

|Security|Notified Amount|MUC amount per PD|Minimum bidding commitment per PD under ACU auction|
|---|---|---|---|
|6.03% GS 2029|11,000|262|262|
|6.68% GS 2033|11,000|262|262|
|7.24% GS 2055|10,000|239|239|

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **May 22, 2026 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
