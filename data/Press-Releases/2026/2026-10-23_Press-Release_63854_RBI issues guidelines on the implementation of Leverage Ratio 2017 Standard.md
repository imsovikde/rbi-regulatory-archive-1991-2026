## October 23, 2026

## RBI issues guidelines on the implementation of Leverage Ratio 2017 Standard

The Reserve Bank of India had issued the <u>draft Reserve Bank of India (Commercial</u> <u>Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Eleventh Amendment Directions, 2026 on</u> <u>August 7, 2026</u>, inviting feedback from stakeholders until August 28, 2026. The draft Amendment Directions proposed to implement the latest leverage ratio framework (‘Leverage Ratio 2017 Standard’) issued by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision.

2. Feedback received on the draft Directions has been examined and consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Amendment Directions. The statement on the feedback received on the draft Directions is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today released the <u>Reserve Bank of India</u> <u>(Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Eleventh Amendment</u> <u>Directions, 2026</u>.
4. These instructions will come into effect from April 1, 2027.
