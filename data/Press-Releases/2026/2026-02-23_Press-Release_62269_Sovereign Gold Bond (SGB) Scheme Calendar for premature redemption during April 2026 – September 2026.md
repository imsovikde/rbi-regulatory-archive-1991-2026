## Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme Calendar for premature redemption during

## April 2026 – September 2026

In terms of Para 13 of the Consolidated Procedural Guidelines on the Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme issued by the Reserve Bank of India vide <u>circular</u> <u>IDMD.CDD.1100/14.04.050/2021-22 dated October 22, 2021</u>, premature redemption of the gold bonds is permitted after five years from the date of issue of such bonds.
