## April 30, 2026

**th** **46 Half-Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves: October** **2025 - March 2026**

It may be recalled that in <u>February 2004</u>, the Reserve Bank initiated the practice of compiling half-yearly reports on the management of foreign exchange reserves and making them public to promote greater transparency and enhance disclosure in the management of the country’s foreign exchange reserves.

In this connection, Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>46th Half-Yearly</u> <u>Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves</u> with reference to end-March

2026. During October 2025-March 2026, India’s foreign exchange reserves decreased from USD 700.09 billion to USD 691.11 billion. The latest position of India’s foreign exchange reserves is available at the following link: <u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28399](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28399)</u>
