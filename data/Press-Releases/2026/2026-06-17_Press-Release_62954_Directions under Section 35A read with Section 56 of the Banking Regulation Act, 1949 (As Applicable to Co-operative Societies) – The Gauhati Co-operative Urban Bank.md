## June 17, 2026

## Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation

## Act, 1949 (As Applicable to Co-operative Societies) – The Gauhati Co-operative

## Urban Bank Ltd., Guwahati – Extension of period

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to The Gauhati Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd., Guwahati vide Directive No.GWH.DOS.ADM.No.S233/01-10-101/2025-2026 dated December 17, 2025 for a period of six months up to close of business on June 17,

2026. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on June 17, 2026.
2. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directions for a further period of three months from the close of business on June 17, 2026 to the close of business on September 17, 2026, subject to review.
3. The aforesaid extension and /or modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per-se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.
4. All other terms and conditions of the Directive under reference shall remain unchanged.
