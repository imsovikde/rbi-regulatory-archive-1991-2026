## April 28, 2026

**Inclusion of “Mizoram Co-operative Apex Bank Ltd.” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934**

Mizoram Co-operative Apex Bank Ltd. has been included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification CO.DOR.RAUG.No.S9213/07-12-000/2025-2026 dated March 12, 2026 and published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated April 10, 2026.
