## Result of Underwriting Auction conducted on August 14, 2026

In the underwriting auction conducted on August 14, 2026, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount (₹ crore)|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount (₹ crore)|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted (₹ crore)|Total Amount underwritten (₹ crore)|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|New GS 2029|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.23|
|New GS 2033|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.32|
|7.24% GS 2055|5,000|2,520|2,480|5,000|0.44|
|7.50% GOI SGrB 2056|5,000|2,520|2,480|5,000|0.58|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 14, 2026.
