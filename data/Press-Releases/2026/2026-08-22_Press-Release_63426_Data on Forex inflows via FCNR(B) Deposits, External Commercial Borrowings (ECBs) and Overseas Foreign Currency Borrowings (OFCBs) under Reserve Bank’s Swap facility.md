## Bank’s Swap facility

RBI had introduced a special USD-INR Forex Swap facility covering FCNR(B) deposits, ECB and OFCB inflows on June 08, 2026. As already announced vide <u>Press</u> <u>Release dated August 14, 2026</u>, the Scheme is open till August 31, 2026 for FCNR(B) deposits, and upto December 31, 2026 for ECBs and OFCBs.

The position of forex inflows as reported by Authorised Dealer Banks till August 21, 2026 under the above facility is given below.

|Type|Forex inflow (Amount in USD million)|
|---|---|
|FCNR(B) Deposits|65,397|
|OFCBs|4,860|
|ECBs|2,591|
|Total|72,848|
