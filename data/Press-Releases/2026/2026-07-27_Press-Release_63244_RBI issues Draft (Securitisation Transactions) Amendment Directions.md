## July 27, 2026

## RBI issues Draft (Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions

In line with the objective to improve efficiency, liquidity and transparency in issuance and subsequent transfer of Securitisation Notes (SNs), RBI has today issued following draft Amendment Directions for public comments:

a) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Securitisation Transactions)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
b) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks-Securitisation Transactions)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
c) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies-Securitisation</u> <u>Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
d) <u>Reserve Bank of India (All India Financial Institutions-</u>Securitisation Transactions) Amendment Directions, 2026
