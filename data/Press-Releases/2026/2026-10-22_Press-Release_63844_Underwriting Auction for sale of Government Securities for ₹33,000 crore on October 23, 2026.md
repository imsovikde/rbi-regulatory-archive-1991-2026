## October 22, 2026

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹33,000 crore on October 23, 2026

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auction to be held on **October 23, 2026 (Friday)**.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting commitment notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore) **Minimum bidding** **Notified MUC amount per** **Security commitment per PD** **Amount PD under ACU auction**

6.53% GS 2031 19,000 453 453
7.71% GS 2066 14,000 334 334
The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **October 23, 2026 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Reserve Bank of India’s Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
