## November 06, 2026

## Sectoral Deployment of Credit by NBFC – September 2026

Data on sectoral deployment of credit by non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) for the month of September 2026, collected from major NBFCs and housing finance companies (HFCs), are set out in the <u>Statement¹</u>.

On a year-on-year (y-o-y) basis, NBFCs’ credit registered a growth of 15.4 per cent in September 2026 as compared to 13.3 per cent a year ago.

## Highlights of the sectoral deployment of credit by NBFCs for the month ended

## September 30, 2026, are given below:

|•|Credit to agriculture and allied activities recorded a robust growth of 17.8 per cent|
|---|---|
||(y-o-y) in September 2026 as against 5.2 per cent a year ago.|
|•|Credit growth in industry sector declined to 7.4 per cent (y-o-y) in September 2026 as compared with 10.2 per cent in September 2025. Within industry, credit growth in ‘infrastructure’ remained subdued.|
|•|Credit growth in services sector moderated to 17.3 per cent (y-o-y) in September 2026 from 23.0 per cent a year ago. While credit to ‘commercial real estate’ marked buoyant expansion, ‘trade’ and ‘transport operators’ segments also witnessed higher credit growth.|
|•|Retail loans growth accelerated to 21.9 per cent (y-o-y) in September 2026 compared to 16.8 per cent a year ago. Within retail sector, ‘housing loans’, ‘loans against gold jewellery’ and ‘vehicle loans’ witnessed accelerated credit growth.|
