|---|---|---|
||Investment Position for Key highlights of India’s IIP in end-June 2026:|Today, the Reserve Bank released data relating to India’s International [1] end-June 2026.|
|•|foreign-owned assets (|Net claims of non-residents on India increased by US$ 16.5 billion during Q1:2026-27 to US$ 220.3 billion as at end-June 2026, mainly due to increase in external liabilities by US$ 11.6 billion and a decline of US$ 4.9 billion in the Table 1).|

- Accordingly, the ratio of India’s international assets to international liabilities moderated to 84.6 per cent in June 2026 from 85.7 per cent a quarter ago (<u>Chart 1</u> & <u>Table 1</u>).
- Rise in foreign liabilities of Indian residents was mainly on account of increased direct investment (US$ 15.7 billion) and other investment (US$ 4.2 billion) compensating for the decline in portfolio equity investments (US$ 14 billion).
- Reserve assets accounted for 55.1 per cent of India’s international financial assets while overseas direct investment accounted for over one fourth share (<u>Table 2</u>).
- The share of debt liabilities in total external liabilities increased gradually during the recent quarters and stood at 56.9 per cent as on end-June 2026 (<u>Table 3</u>).
