# June 03, 2026

# Clarification on gold holdings

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has come across reports in certain sections of the media about RBI’s sale of gold. The RBI emphasizes that these reports are not correct.

In this context, it is clarified that the physical stock of gold is disclosed by RBI in its Monthly Bulletin. The latest edition is available on the RBI website at: <u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=24213](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=24213)</u>, and the physical stock of gold remains unchanged at 880.52 tonnes as on date.

Members of public are, therefore, advised to rely on official information published by RBI from time to time in such matters.
