## September 01, 2026

## RBI appoints Shri Suman Ray as new Executive Director

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has appointed Shri Suman Ray as Executive Director (ED) with effect from September 01, 2026.

Prior to being promoted as ED, Shri Suman Ray was serving as Regional Director for Maharashtra.

As a career central banker, Shri Ray has experience of over three decades in the Reserve Bank, having worked in several areas including Currency Management, Financial Inclusion, Payment and Settlement System, Consumer Education and Protection, and Human Resources. Shri Ray has also served as Secretary to the Western Area Local Board.

As Executive Director, Shri Suman Ray will look after Deposit Insurance & Credit Guarantee Corporation and Premises Department.
