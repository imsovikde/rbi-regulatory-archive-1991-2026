## Supervisory Data Quality Index for Scheduled Commercial Banks

## (December 2025)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released <u>Supervisory Data Quality Index</u> <u>(sDQI) for SCBs for December 2025</u>. It measures data quality in terms of the Accuracy, Timeliness, Completeness and Consistency in the submission of returns. The objective of sDQI is to assess the adherence to the principles enunciated in the <u>Master</u> <u>Direction on Filing of Supervisory Returns 2024</u>.
