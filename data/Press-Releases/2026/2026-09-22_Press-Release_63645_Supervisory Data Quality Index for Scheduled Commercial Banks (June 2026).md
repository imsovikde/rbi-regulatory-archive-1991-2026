## September 22, 2026

## Supervisory Data Quality Index for Scheduled Commercial Banks

## (June 2026)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released the <u>Supervisory Data Quality Index</u> <u>(sDQI) for SCBs for June 2026</u>. It measures data quality in terms of the Accuracy, Timeliness, Completeness and Consistency in the submission of returns and microdata for supervisory assessments. The objective of sDQI is to assess the adherence to the principles enunciated in the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial</u> <u>Banks – Supervisory Returns) Directions, 2026</u> and <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small</u> <u>Finance Banks-Supervisory Returns) Directions, 2026</u>.
