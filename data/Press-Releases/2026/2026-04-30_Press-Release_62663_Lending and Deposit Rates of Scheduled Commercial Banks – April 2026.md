April 30, 2026 **Lending and Deposit Rates of Scheduled Commercial Banks – April 2026** Data on <u>lending and deposit rates</u> of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs) (excluding regional rural banks and small finance banks) received during the month of April 2026 are set out in Tables 1 to 9. **Highlights:** **Lending Rates:**

- The weighted average lending rate (WALR) on fresh rupee loans of SCBs stood at 8.40 per cent in March 2026 (8.44 per cent in February 2026).
- The WALR on outstanding rupee loans of SCBs marginally declined to 8.99 per cent in March 2026 from 9.00 per cent in February 2026.
- 1-Year median Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate (MCLR) of SCBs increased to 8.55 per cent in April 2026 from 8.40 per cent in March 2026.
- The WALR on fresh as well as outstanding rupee loans exhibited a mixed movement across sectors in March 2026.
## Deposit Rates:

- The weighted average domestic term deposit rate (WADTDR) on fresh rupee term deposits of SCBs stood at 6.07 per cent in March 2026 (5.65 per cent in February 2026).
- The WADTDR on outstanding rupee term deposits of SCBs remained unchanged at 6.62 per cent in March 2026.
