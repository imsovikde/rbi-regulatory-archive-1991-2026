## Bank’s Swap facility

RBI had introduced a special USD-INR Forex Swap facility covering FCNR(B) deposits, ECB and OFCB inflows on June 08, 2026. As announced vide <u>Press Release</u> <u>dated August 14, 2026</u>, the Scheme was open till  August 31, 2026 for FCNR(B) deposits, and will be open up to December 31, 2026 for ECBs and OFCBs.

The position of forex inflows reported by Authorised Dealer Banks till September 18, 2026, under the above facility is given below.

|Type|Forex inflow (Amount in USD million)|
|---|---|
|FCNR(B) Deposits|1,32,980*|
|OFCBs|5,320|
|ECBs|5,296|
|Total|1,43,596|

* FCNR (B) deposits mobilised till August 31, 2026.
