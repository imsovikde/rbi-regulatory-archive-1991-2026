## July 16, 2026

## Financial Inclusion Index for March 2026

The Reserve Bank of India had constructed a composite <u>Financial Inclusion</u> <u>Index</u> (FI-Index) in consultation with the concerned stakeholders including the Government, to capture the extent of financial inclusion across the country, which was first published in August 2021 for the FY ending March 2021.

Index for the year ending March 2026 has since been compiled. The value of FI- Index for March 2026 stands at **70.0** vis-à-vis **67.0** in March 2025, with growth witnessed across all sub-indices. Improvement in FI-Index, this year, is mainly on account of uptick in Usage, thereby reflecting deepening of financial inclusion.
