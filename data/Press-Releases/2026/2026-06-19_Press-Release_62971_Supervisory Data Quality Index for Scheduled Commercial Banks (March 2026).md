## June 19, 2026

## Supervisory Data Quality Index for Scheduled Commercial Banks

## (March 2026)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has released the <u>Supervisory Data Quality Index</u> <u>(sDQI) for SCBs for March 2026</u>. It measures data quality in terms of the Accuracy, Timeliness, Completeness and Consistency in the submission of returns and microdata for supervisory assessments. The objective of sDQI is to assess the adherence to the principles enunciated in the <u>Master Direction on Filing of</u> <u>Supervisory Returns 2024</u>.
