## August 06, 2026

## RBI Issues Amendment Directions on ‘Conduct of Regulated Entities in

## Recovery of Loans and Engagement of Recovery Agents’

The Reserve Bank had issued the revised draft Amendment Directions on 'Conduct of Regulated Entities in Recovery of Loans and Engagement of Recovery Agents' on <u>May 20, 2026</u> for seeking feedback from stakeholders. Feedback received on the above revised draft Directions has been examined and the consequent modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Amendment Directions. A statement on the feedback received is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.

2. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has today issued the following Amendment Directions, which shall come into effect from **January 1, 2027**.
(i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks -Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks-Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (iii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks -Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (iv)<u>Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks-Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u>
(v) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks -Responsible</u> <u>Business Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (vi) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Rural Co-operative Banks-Responsible Business</u> <u>Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (vii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (All India Financial Institutions -Responsible</u> <u>Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (viii)<u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies-Responsible</u> <u>Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026</u> (ix)<u>Reserve Bank of India (Housing Finance Companies) Third Amendment</u> <u>Directions, 2026</u>
3. The Amendment Directions include comprehensive instructions to all regulated entities (REs) on conduct related matters in recovery of loans and engagement of recovery agents, which *inter alia* cover aspects such as fair treatment to borrowers during recovery process, conduct of lender’s employees and recovery agents, and due diligence, training, code of conduct for recovery agents, etc. and also conduct of REs while deploying a technology-based mechanism for recovery of loan dues in financed mobile devices of a borrower.
