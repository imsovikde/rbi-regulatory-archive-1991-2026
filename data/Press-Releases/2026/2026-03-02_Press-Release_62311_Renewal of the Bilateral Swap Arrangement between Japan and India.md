## March 02, 2026

## Renewal of the Bilateral Swap Arrangement between Japan and India

Japan and India renewed the Bilateral Swap Arrangement (BSA) effective from February 28, 2026.

The Bank of Japan, acting as the agent for the Minister of Finance, Japan, and the Reserve Bank of India signed the third Amendment and Restatement Agreement of the BSA. The BSA is a two-way arrangement where both authorities can swap their local currencies in exchange for the US Dollar. The size of the BSA remains unchanged, that is, up to 75 billion US Dollars.

Japan and India believe that the BSA, which aims to strengthen and complement other financial safety nets, will further deepen financial cooperation between the two countries and contribute to regional and global financial stability.
