## April 01, 2026

## Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation

## Act, 1949 – Innovative Co-operative Urban Bank Limited, Delhi – Extension of

## Period

The Reserve Bank of India issued Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to Innovative Co-operative Urban Bank Limited, Delhi vide Directive No. DEL.DOS.EXG_SSM.No.S143/12-10-005/2025- 2026 dated July 03, 2025 for a period of six months up to close of business on January 04, 2026, which was extended up to close of business on April 04, 2026 vide Directive No. DOR.MON.D-27/12.28.123/2025-26 dated December 26, 2025. The Reserve Bank of India is satisfied that in the public interest, it is necessary to further extend the period of operation of the Directive beyond close of business on April 04, 2026.

2. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India, in the exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, hereby extends the Directive for a further period of three months from close of business on April 04, 2026 to close of business on July 04, 2026, subject to review.
3. The aforesaid extension by the Reserve Bank of India should not per-se be construed to imply that the Reserve Bank of India is satisfied with the financial position of the bank.
4. Other terms and conditions of the Directive under reference, shall remain unchanged.
## (Brij Raj)
