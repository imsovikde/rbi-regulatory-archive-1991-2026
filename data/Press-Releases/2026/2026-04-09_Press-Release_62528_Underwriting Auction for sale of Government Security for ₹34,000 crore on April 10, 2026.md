## April 09, 2026

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Security for ₹34,000 crore on April 10, 2026

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Security, as detailed below, through auction to be held on **April 10, 2026 (Friday)**.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting commitment notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore) **Minimum bidding** **Notified MUC amount** **Security commitment per PD** **Amount per PD** <u>under ACU auction</u>

6.48% GS 2035 34,000 810 810
The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **April 10, 2026 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
