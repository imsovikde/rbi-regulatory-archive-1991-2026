## June 8, 2026

# Sources of Variation in India’s Foreign Exchange Reserves during April-March 2025-26

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released the <u>balance of payments (BoP) data for</u> <u>the fourth quarter (Q4), i.e., January-March of 2025-26</u>, and for April-March 2025-26 on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during 2025-26 are detailed below in Table 1.

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), foreign exchange reserves decreased by US$ 23.6 billion during 2025-26 as compared to a depletion of US$ 5.0 billion during 2024-25. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (*i.e.*, including valuation effects) increased by US$ 22.8 billion during 2025-26 as compared to an accretion of US$ 21.9 billion in 2024-25 (<u>Table 2</u>).

The valuation gain, primarily reflecting higher price of gold and depreciation of US dollar against major currencies, increased to US$ 46.4 billion during 2025-26 from US$ 26.9 billion during 2024-25.
