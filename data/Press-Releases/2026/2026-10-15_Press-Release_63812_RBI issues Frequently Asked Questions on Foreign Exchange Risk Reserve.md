## RBI issues Frequently Asked Questions on Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve

The Reserve Bank of India has today issued a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) to provide clarifications on the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) requirements introduced *vide* <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 26 dated October 10, 2026</u> and <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 27 dated October 12, 2026</u>.

2. The <u>FAQs</u> are available on the Reserve Bank of India website
