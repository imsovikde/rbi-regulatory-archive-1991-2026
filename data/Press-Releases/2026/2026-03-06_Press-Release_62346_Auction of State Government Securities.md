The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **March 10, 2026 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions, subject to a maximum limit of one per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the ‘<u>Scheme</u> <u>for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>’. ndividual investors can I also place bids as per the non- competitive scheme through the Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on <u>March 10, 2026</u> <u>(Tuesday). The competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 A.M. and 11:30 A.M.</u> <u>and non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 A.M. and 11:00 A.M.</u>

In case of technical difficulties, Core Banking Operations Team may be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-69870466, 022-69870415).

For other auction related difficulties, IDMD Auction Team can be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22702431, 022-22705125).

Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to the Public Debt Office (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22603456, 022-22603457, 022- 22603190) i n the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) before the auction timing ends.
