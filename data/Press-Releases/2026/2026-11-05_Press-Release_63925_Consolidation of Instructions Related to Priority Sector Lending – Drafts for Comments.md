## November 05, 2026

## Consolidation of Instructions Related to Priority Sector Lending – Drafts for

## Comments

As part of the ongoing effort towards consolidation of various instructions issued from time to time, the Reserve Bank has undertaken a review of the extant guidelines relating to Priority Sector Lending (PSL) and proposes to issue Directions on the subject RE-wise.

2. Accordingly, the following draft Directions are being placed on the website for public comments:
a) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Priority Sector Lending – Targets</u> <u>and Classification) Directions, 2026</u>
b) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Priority Sector Lending –</u> <u>Targets and Classification) Directions, 2026</u>
c) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Local Area Banks – Priority Sector Lending – Targets</u> <u>and Classification) Directions, 2026</u>
d) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks – Priority Sector Lending –</u> <u>Targets and Classification) Directions, 2026</u>
e) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Urban Co-operative Banks – Priority Sector Lending –</u> <u>Targets and Classification) Directions, 2026</u>
3. Further, instructions which have not been included in the consolidated draft Directions would be repealed consequent to the issuance of the Directions. The same will be notified separately.
4. The comments / feedback may be submitted on or before **November 26, 2026**, through the following channels:
i. The ‘<u>Connect 2 Regulate</u>’ section on the website by following the corresponding hyperlink provided against each document in the page where they are hosted.
or

ii. by <u>e-mail</u> with the subject line ‘Feedback on [full name of the draft Direction / (including the type of Regulated Entity)]’.
