## January 14, 2026

## RBI issues Reserve Bank of India (Internal Ombudsman) Directions, 2026

The Reserve Bank of India had on October 07, 2025, issued the <u>draft Master</u> <u>Direction -Reserve Bank of India (Internal Ombudsman for Regulated Entities)</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u>, seeking feedback from the stakeholders and the public.

2. Feedback received on the draft Master Direction has been examined and necessary modifications have been suitably incorporated in the final Master Direction. A statement on the feedback received for the draft Master Direction and action taken is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India has today issued the following Master Directions, specific to each category of regulated entity:
a) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks-Internal Ombudsman) Directions</u>, <u>2026</u>
b) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks-Internal Ombudsman)</u> <u>Directions, 2026</u>
c) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks-Internal Ombudsman) Directions,</u> <u>2026</u>
d) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies-Internal</u> <u>Ombudsman) Directions, 2026</u>
e) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Bank Prepaid Payment Instruments Issuers-</u> <u>Internal Ombudsman) Directions, 2026</u>
f) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Credit Information Companies-Internal Ombudsman)</u> <u>Directions, 2026</u> The aforesaid directions are expected to strengthen the internal mechanism for
resolution of customer grievances within the regulated entities.
