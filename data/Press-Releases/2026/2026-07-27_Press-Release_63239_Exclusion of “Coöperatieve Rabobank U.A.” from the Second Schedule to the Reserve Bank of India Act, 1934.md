## July 27, 2026

## Exclusion of “Coöperatieve Rabobank U.A.” from the Second Schedule to the

## Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

Coöperatieve Rabobank U.A. has been excluded from the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 vide Notification DOR.LIC.No.S2621/23.03.027/2026-27 dated June 24, 2026, published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated July 02, 2026.
