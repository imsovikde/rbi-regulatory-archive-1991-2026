## April 16, 2026

## Launching of 114

**th** **Round of Quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) –** **Q1:2026-27**

The Reserve Bank has launched the <u>114</u> <u>th</u> <u>round</u> of the quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) of the Indian manufacturing sector for the reference period April-June 2026. The survey assesses business sentiments for the current quarter (Q1:2026-27) and expectations for the ensuing quarter (Q2:2026-27), based on qualitative responses on a set of indicators pertaining to demand conditions, financial conditions, employment conditions and the price situation. The outlook on key parameters for the two subsequent quarters (Q3:2026-27 and Q4:2026-27) are also covered.

2. <u>The survey findings are released in summary form on the website of the Bank</u> <u>regularly, where identity of the respondents is not revealed</u>.
3. M/s Genesis Management & Market Research Pvt. Ltd. has been authorised to conduct the survey for April-June 2026 on behalf of the Reserve Bank. While the agency will approach selected companies, other manufacturing companies are also encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> from the Bank’s <u>website</u>, under the sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed as per contact details given therein.
