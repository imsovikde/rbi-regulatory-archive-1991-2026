91-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.500 crore under regular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day

will be conducted on March 26, 2008 using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The

allocation to the non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the

discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified

in the General Notification No. F.2 (12)-W & M/97 dated 31st March 1998 issued by

Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, March

by successful bidders will be on Friday, March 28, 2008.
