SBI Rights Issue Gol Special Bonds,2024

The Government of India has announced the issue of '8.35 per cent SBl

(nominal). The Special Bonds are being issued at par to State Bank of India on

March 27, 2008 (Thursday) as subscription towards Rights lssue of equity

shares of State Bank of India.

The investment in the Special Bonds by the banks and insurance

companies will not be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government

securities for their statutory requirements. Such investment by insurance

companies will, however, be eligible to be reckoned as investment under “other

by the provident funds, gratuity funds, superannuation funds, etc., in the Special

Bonds will be treated as an eligible investment under the administrative order of

the Ministry of Finance.

SpecialBondswillbe transferable and eligiblefor marketreadyforward

transactions (Repo). The bonds, however, will not be an eligible underlying

security for ready forward transactions (Repo/Reverse Repo) with the Reserve

Bank of India.
