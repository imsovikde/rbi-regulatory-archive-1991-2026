NewFerriticStainlessSteel(FSS) Coin ofRs.5-

"Jagat Guru Sree Narayana Gurudev"

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation new Ferritic Stainless Steel

coins of Rs.5 issued by Government of India to commemorate "Jagat Guru Sree Narayana

Gurudev".
