## February 15, 2008

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS :

## Canara Bank

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase equity shares of Canara Bank under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), through secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that Canara Bank has gone below the trigger limit of 20 per cent of its paid up capital.
