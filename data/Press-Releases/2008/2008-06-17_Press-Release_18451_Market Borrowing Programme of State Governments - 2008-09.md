Market Borrowing Programme of State Governments - 2008-09

The estimate of gross normal market borrowings of State Governments for the

financial year 2008-09is placed at Rs.59,000crore takinginto account the

repayments of Rs.14,371 crore. The market borrowings of the State Governments

will be raised in a phased manner depending upon their requirements. So far (up to

June 17, 2008), six State Governments have raised Rs.5,912 crore through the sale

of their State Development Loans (SDLs) through auction method.

The State Governments/Reserve Bank of India will have the flexibility to

review the borrowing requirements and bring about modifications in the borrowings

keeping in view the emerging requirements of the State Governments, market

conditionsandotherrelevantfactors.

proposed, inter alia, that States, at their discretion and initiative, would be
