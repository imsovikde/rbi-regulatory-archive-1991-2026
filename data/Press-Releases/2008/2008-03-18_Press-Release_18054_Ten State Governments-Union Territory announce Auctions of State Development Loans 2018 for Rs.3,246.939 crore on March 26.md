Ten State Governments/Union Territory announce Auctions of

State Development Loans 2018 for Rs.3,246.939 crore on March 26

The following State Governments/ Union Territory have announced the sale of

10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs. 3,246.939 crore

through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

001 should be submitted to that Office on Wednesday, March 26, 2008. The

competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format

using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by

maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on March 26, 2008 and payment by

successful bidders will be made during banking hours on March 27, 2008 at Mumbai

and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India.

The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment

in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)

under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the

ready forward facility.
