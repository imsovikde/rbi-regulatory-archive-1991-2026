# Investment by FIIs and NRIs/PIOs : M/s Networth Stock Broking Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has notified today that M/s Networth Stock Broking Limited has passed resolution at its Board of Directors level and Extra-Ordinary General Meeting that equity shares or each series of convertible debentures under Portfolio Investment Scheme can be purchased through primary markets or stock exchanges in India upto the limits specified against each category as mentioned below :

1. Investment by FIIs upto : 49 per cent
2. Investment by NRIs/PIOs upto : 24 per cent
