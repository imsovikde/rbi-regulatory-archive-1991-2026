Investment by Flls/NRls/PIOs under PIS

TheReserve Bank of Indiahas today notified that ForeignInstitutional

Investors (Flls) / Non Resident Indians (NRls) / Persons of Indian Origin (PlOs) can

now purchase equity shares of the following banks under Portfolio Investment

Scheme (PlS), through secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India have

stated that the holdings of Flls in the following banks have gone below the trigger

limit shown against their paid up capital.
