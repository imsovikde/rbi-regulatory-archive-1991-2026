RBl's Special Fixed Rate TermRepoAuction for Rs. 54,965 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 6.5 per

cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs. 54,965 crore on December 26, 2008,

due for reversal on January 09, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity

outstanding amount under thisfacility as onDecember 24, 2008 stood at Rs.5,035 crore.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it

would conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till

June 30, 2009 up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,000 crore on outstanding basis and

that additional liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be

availed by banks exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs

and HFCs.
