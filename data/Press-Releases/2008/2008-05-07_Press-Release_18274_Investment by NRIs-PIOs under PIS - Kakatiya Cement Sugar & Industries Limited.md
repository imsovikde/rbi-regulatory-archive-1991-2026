Investment by NRls/PIOs under PIS :

Kakatiya Cement Sugar & Industries Limited

purchases of equity shares of Kakatiya Cement Sugar & Industries Limited, should

be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Non-Resident Indians

(NRls)/Persons of Indian Origin (PlOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS).
