February 25, 2008 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.40.05 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.59.39 on February 25, 2008. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 22, 2008) were Rs.39.98 and Rs.59.20 respectively.
