The Government of Kerala and the Reserve Bank of India have, on August 21,

2008 signed a Memorandum of Understanding with regard to Urban Co-operative

Banks in the State of Kerala. Consequent upon this, a State level Task Force for

Urban Co-operativeBanks (TAFCUB)has been constituted for Kerala.Shri

S.Ramaswamy, Regional Director for Kerala and Lakshadweep, Reserve Bank of

India, is the Chairman of the TAFCUB and Smt Rani George, IAS, Registrar of

Cooperative Societies, Government of Kerala is its Co-Chairman. The other members

of the TAFCUB include a representative each from State and National Federation of

Urban Cooperative Banks, a nominee of State Government of Kerala and a

Mumbai. The first meeting of the TAFCUB will be held shortly.
