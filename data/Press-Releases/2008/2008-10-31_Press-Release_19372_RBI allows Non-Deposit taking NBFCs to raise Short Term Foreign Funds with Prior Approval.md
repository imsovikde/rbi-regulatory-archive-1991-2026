RBI allows Non-Deposit taking NBFCs to raise

ShortTermForeignFundswithPriorApproval

The Reserve Bank recently allowed Systemically Important Non-Deposit taking Non-Banking

Financial Companies (NBFCs-ND-Sl) to raise funds by issuing Perpetual Debt Instruments that can

be included in their Tier 1 capital. It has now been decided as a temporary measure, to permit

NBFCs-ND-SI to raise short- term foreign currency borrowings, under the approval route, subject to

the following conditions.

i)

Eligibility: NBFCs-NDSl, complying with the prudential norms on capital adequacy and

exposurenorms.
