January 29, 2008 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.39.40 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.58.23 on January 29, 2008. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 28, 2008) were Rs.39.47 and Rs.57.92 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
