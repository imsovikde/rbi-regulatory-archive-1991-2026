Certificate of Registration of

M/s. Black Partridge Holdings Private Limited Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on September 17, 2008 cancelled the

certificate of registration granted to M/s. Black Partridge Holdings Private Limited

having its registered office at 1602, Ambadeep, 14, K.G. Marg, New Delhi-110001

for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution as the company

has voluntarily exited from the business of a non-banking financial institution and

has been wound up. Following cancellation of the registration certificate the

company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.
