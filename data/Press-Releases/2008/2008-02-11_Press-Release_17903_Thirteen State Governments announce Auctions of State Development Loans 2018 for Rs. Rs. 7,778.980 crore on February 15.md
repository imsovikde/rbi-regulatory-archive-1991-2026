# Thirteen State Governments announce Auctions of State Development Loans 2018 for Rs. Rs. 7,778.980 crore on February 15

## February 11, 2008

## Thirteen State Governments announce Auctions of State Development

## Loans 2018 for Rs. Rs. 7,778.980 crore on February 15

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs. 7,778.980 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

|---|---|

|10 Rajasthan|422.628|
|11 Sikkim|137.800|
|12 Tamil Nadu|800.000|
|13 Uttar Pradesh|1,500.000|

## Total 7,778.980

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional**

**subscription upto a maximum of Rs.200.00 crore over and above the notified amount.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on February 15, 2008 (Friday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Friday, February 15, 2008. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. on February 15, 2008. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on February 15, 2008 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on February 18, 2008 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
