Reserve Bank Cancels theLicence of Shri Siddhi Venkatesh Sahakari Bank Ltd.,

Jalgaon, Maharashtra

In view of the fact that Shri Siddhi Venkatesh Sahakari Bank Ltd.，Jalgaon,

Maharashtra, had ceased to be solvent, all efforts to revive it in close consultation with the

GovernmentofMaharashtrahadfailedandthedepositorswerebeinginconveniencedby

bankafterthecloseofbusinessonDecember06,2008.TheRegistrarofCo-operative

Societies, Maharashtra has also been requested to issue an order for winding up the bank and

appoint a liquidator for the bank. It may be highlighted that on liquidation, every depositor is

usualtermsandconditions.

The bank was granted a licence by Reserve Bank on February 16,1998 to commence

conducted withreference toits financialpositionas onMarch31，2007revealed further

deterioration inbank's financialposition.The ReserveBank of India issued directions under

Section 35 A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies) on

May 12, 2008 restricting its operations, including placing a ceiling on withdrawal of deposits at

Rs.1000/-.

did nothaveanyviableplanof actionforitsrevival.

In the absence of any viable proposal for turning around and achieving the required

regulatory prescriptions, the possibility of revival of the bank was remote. Therefore, the

Reserve Bank of India took the extreme measure of cancelling licence of the bank in the

interest of the bank's depositors. With the cancellation of its licence and commencement of

liquidation proceedings, the process of paying the depositors of Shri Siddhi Ventatesh Sahakari

Bank Ltd., Jalgaon, Maharashtra will be set in motion subject to the terms and conditions of the

DepositInsuranceScheme.

Consequent to the cancellation of its licence, Shri Siddhi Ventatesh Sahakari BankLtd.,

Jalgaon, Maharashtra is prohibited from carrying on “banking business' as defined in Section

deposits.
