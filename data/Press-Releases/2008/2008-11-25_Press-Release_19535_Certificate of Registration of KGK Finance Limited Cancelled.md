Certificate of Registration of KGK Finance Limited Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 29, 2008 cancelled the certificate

of registration issued to KGK Finance Limited having its registered office at ^Sri

Rangapadmam', No.2, Hasthigiri Street, West Mambalam, Chennai-600033, for

carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation

of the registration certificate the company cannot transact the business of a non-

banking financial institution.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-lA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India

Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-banking

clause (a) of Section 45-l of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
