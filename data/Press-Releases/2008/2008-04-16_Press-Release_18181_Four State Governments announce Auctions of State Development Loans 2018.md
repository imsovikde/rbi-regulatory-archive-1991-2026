April 16, 2008 **Four State Governments announce Auctions of State Development Loans 2018**

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of **Rs. 2,648.000 crore** through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State

|Sr. No.|Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|
||(Rs. crore)||
|1 Kerala||385.000|
|2 Nagaland||160.000|
|3 Uttarakhand||250.000|
|4 West Bengal||1,853.000|

## Total 2,648.000

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on April 22, 2008 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, April 22, 2008. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by

12.30 P.M. on April 22, 2008. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on April 22, 2008 and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on April 23, 2008 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility. Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock under the auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on April 21, 2008 (Monday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
