the Banking Ombudsman Scheme for the period 2007-08.

According to the Report, during the year 2007-08, the Banking Ombudsmen received

47,887 complaints, up from 38,638 complaints received in 2006-07.

The Reserve Bank of India introduced the BankingOmbudsman Scheme in June

1995 to provide low cost and effective forum for redressal of customer grievances against

banks. The Scheme was first revised in 2002 and again in 2006 to cover a wider range of

complaints concerning deficiency in banking services.In May 2oo7，the Scheme was

amended to allow for appeals from complainants and banks on the decisions of the Banking

Ombudsmen. The Scheme operates within the guidelines framed by the Reserve Bank,

whichalsoadministerstheScheme.
