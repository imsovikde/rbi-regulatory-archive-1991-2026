## January 21, 2008

## Twelve State Governments/Union Territory announce

## Auctions of State Development Loans 2018 for Rs.7,628.431 crore on January 24

The following State Governments / Union Territory have announced the sale of 10-Year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs. 7,628.431 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## Sr. No. State/ Union Territory Notified Amount

## (Rs. crore)

|---|---|---|
|1|Andhra Pradesh|500.000|
|2|Assam|246.000|

|4|Jammu & Kashmir|100.000|
|5|Jharkhand|1,000.000|
|6|Maharashtra|1,350.000|

|8|Punjab|456.101|
|9|Rajasthan|549.330|
|10|Tamil Nadu|600.000|
|11|Uttarakhand|250.000|
|12|West Bengal Total|1,400.000 7,628.431|

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional subscription**

**upto a maximum of Rs.150.00 crore over and above the notified amount.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on January 24, 2008 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, January 24, 2008. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. on January 24,

2008. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on January 24, 2008 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on January 25, 2008 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility. Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock and Puducherry Government Stock under the auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30
P.M. on January 23, 2008 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
