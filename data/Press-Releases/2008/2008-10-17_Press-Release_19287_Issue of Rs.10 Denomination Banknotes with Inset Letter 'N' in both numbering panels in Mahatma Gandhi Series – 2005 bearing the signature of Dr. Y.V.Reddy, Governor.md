Issue of Rs.10 Denomination Banknotes with Inset Letter 'N'

in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series - 2005

bearing the signature of Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue Rs.10 denomination banknotes

with inset letter 'N' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series - 2005

letter, the design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respects to the

banknotes in Mahatma Gandhi Series - 2o05, with additional / new security features

issued on April 27, 2006. All banknotes in the denomination of Rs.10 issued by the

Reserve Bank in the past will continue to belegal tender.
