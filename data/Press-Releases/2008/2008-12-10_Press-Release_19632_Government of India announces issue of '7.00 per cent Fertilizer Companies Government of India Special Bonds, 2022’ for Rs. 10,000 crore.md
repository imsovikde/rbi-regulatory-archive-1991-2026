Government of India announces issue of '7.o0 per cent Fertilizer Companies

Government of IndiaSpecial Bonds,2022' for Rs.10,000 crore

TheGovernmentofIndiahaveannouncedtheissueof'7.oopercentFertilizer

Companies Government of India Special Bonds, 2022' for Rs.10,000 crore (nominal). The

Special Bonds are being issued at par to 23 Fertilizer Companies (list enclosed)as

(Wednesday).

The investment in the Special Bonds by the banks and Insurance Companies will not be

reckoned as an eligible investment in Government securities for their statutory requirements.

However, such investment by the insurance companies will be eligible to be reckoned as

investment under“other Approved Securities" category as defined under Insurance Regulatory

and Development Authority (Investment) Regulations, 2ooo. Further, the investment by the

Provident Funds, Gratuity Funds, Superannuation Funds, etc. in the Special Bonds will be

treatedasaneligibleinvestmentundertheadministrativeorderof theMinistryofFinance.

The Special Bonds will be transferable and eligible for market ready forward transactions

(Repo).
