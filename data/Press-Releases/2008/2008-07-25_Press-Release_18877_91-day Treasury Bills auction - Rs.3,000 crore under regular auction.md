The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government

conducted on July 30, 2008 using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation to

the non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the

Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General

Notification No. F.2 (12)-W & M/97 dated 31st March 1998 issued by Government of

India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, July 30,

2008 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by
