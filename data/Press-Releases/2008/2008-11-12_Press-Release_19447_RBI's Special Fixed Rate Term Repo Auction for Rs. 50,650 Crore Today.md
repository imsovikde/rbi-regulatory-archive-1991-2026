RBl's Special Fixed Rate Term Repo Auction for Rs. 50,650 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 7.5 per

cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs.50,650 crore on November 12, 2008,

due for reversal on November 26, 2008, with a view to enabling banks to meet the

liquidity requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs) and Non-Banking Financial Companies

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by
