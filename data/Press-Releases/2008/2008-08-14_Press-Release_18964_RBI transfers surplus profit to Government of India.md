August 14,2008

RBl transfers surplus profit to Government of India

The Reserve Bank of India's Central Board, in its meeting held today,

approved the transfer of surplus profit to the Government of India amounting to

Rs. 15,011 crore for the year ended June 30, 2008. Last year, the Reserve Bank

had transferred Rs. 45,719 crore as surplus to the Government of India which

included Rs. 34,308.60 crore on account of profit on sale of SBl shares held by

the Reserve Bank. Excluding this amount, the transfer of surplus for the year

ended June 30, 2007 was Rs. 11,411.00 crore.
