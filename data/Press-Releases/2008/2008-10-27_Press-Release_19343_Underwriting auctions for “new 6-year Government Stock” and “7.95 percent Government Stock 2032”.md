Underwriting auctions for "new 6-year Government Stock"

and "7.95 percent Government Stock 2032"

The Government of India have announced the sale of a "new 6-year Government

Stock" for a notified amount of Rs. 6,0o0 crore (nominal) and “7.95 per cent

Government Stock 2032" for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through

auctions, to be held on October 31, 2008 (Friday). The underwriting auctions for both

the securities will be conducted on October 29, 2008 (October 30, 2008 being a

holiday).

As per the scheme of underwriting dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of

Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment

under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:
