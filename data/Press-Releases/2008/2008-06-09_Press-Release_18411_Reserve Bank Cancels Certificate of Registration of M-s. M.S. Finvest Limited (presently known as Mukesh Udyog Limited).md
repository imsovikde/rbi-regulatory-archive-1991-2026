Road, Miller Ganj, Ludhiana-141003 (Punjab) for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial

institution.Following cancellation of the registration certificate, M/s. M.S.Finvest Limited (presently known

as Mukesh Udyog Limited), cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

By the powers conferred under Section 45-lA(6)of the Reserve Bank of India Act,1934,the

ReserveBankcancancel theregistration certificate of a non-bankingfinancial company.Thebusinessof a

non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a)of Section 45-I of theReserveBank of India Act,

1934.
