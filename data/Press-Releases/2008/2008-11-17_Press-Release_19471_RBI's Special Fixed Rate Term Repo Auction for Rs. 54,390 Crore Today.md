RBl's Special Fixed RateTermRepoAuction for Rs.54,390CroreToday

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 7.5 per cent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs.54,390 crore on November 17, 2008, due for

reversal on December 01，2008, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity

requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs) and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) either on

incremental or on rollover basis. Of the outstanding amount of Rs. 6,920 crore as on November

14, 2008, Rs.1,310 crore is due for redemption today.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility every day until

further notice up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,0o0 crore and that additional liquidity support

to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks exclusively for the

purpose of meeting requirements of MFs and NBFCs.
