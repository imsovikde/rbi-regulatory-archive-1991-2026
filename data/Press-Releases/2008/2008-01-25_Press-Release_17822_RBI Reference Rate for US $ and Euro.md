January 25, 2008 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.39.40 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.58.14 on January 25, 2008. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 24, 2008) were Rs.39.43 and Rs.57.65 respectively.
