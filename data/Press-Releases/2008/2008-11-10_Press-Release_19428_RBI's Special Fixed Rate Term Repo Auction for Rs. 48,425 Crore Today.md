RBl'sSpecial FixedRateTermRepoAuctionforRs.48,425CroreToday

TheReserveBank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 7.5 percent per annum

2008,with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs) and Non-

Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. Of the outstanding

amount of Rs.11,725 crore as on Nov 07, 2008, Rs.150 crore is due for redemption today.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would conduct the

special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility every day until further notice up to a

cumulative amount of Rs60,0o0crore and that additional liquiditysupport tothe extent of up to1.5per

cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs

and NBFCs.
