91-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.3,0o0 crore under regular auction, Rs.2,0o0 crore under MsS

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government of

India Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.5,oo0 crore (Rs.3,0o0 crore under the

regular auction calendar and Rs.2,oo0 crore under the Market Stabilisation Scheme

(MSS)). The auction will be conducted on September 2, 2008 using "Multiple Price

notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and

1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

2008 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by

successful bidders will be on Friday, September 5, 2008.
