The Reserve Bank of India, today placed on its website for public comments,

draft format for reporting foreign direct investments (FDls). In the revised form the

class of investors has been broadened to include several new entities viz., foreign

capital investors (FVCls) registered with SEBl, foreign trusts, private equity and

other funds, pension/ provident funds, partnership / proprietorship firms, financial

institutions, NRls / PlOs. Secondly, the details of investment received in units of

venture capital funds from FVCls are proposed to be separately captured. Thirdly,

Part B of the form has been modified so as to capture breakup of the details of

foreign investor class.The date of filing of Part B of the form has been extended

from June 30 of every year to July 31.

The Reserve Bank has invited feedback from all interested stakeholders on
