RBl's Special Fixed Rate Term RepoAuction for Rs.57,850 CroreToday

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 7.5 per

centper annum againsteligible securities for Rs.57,850crore onDecember 03,2008,due

for reversal on December 17,2008,with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity

requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and

Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. Of the

outstanding amountof Rs.4,100crore as onDecember02,2008,Rs.1,950crore is due for
