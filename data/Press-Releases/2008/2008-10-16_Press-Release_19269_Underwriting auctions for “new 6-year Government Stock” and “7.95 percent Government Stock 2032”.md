Underwriting auctions for "new 6-year Government Stock"

and "7.95 percent Government Stock 2032"

The Government of India have vide announced the sale of a “new 6-year

Government Stock" for a notified amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal) and "7.95 per

cent Government Stock 2032" for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal)

through auctions, to be held on October 20, 2008 (Monday). The underwriting auctions

for both the securities will be conducted on October 17, 2008 (Friday).

As per the scheme of underwriting dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of

Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment

under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:
