91-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.3,ooocroreunderregular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government

of India Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.3,ooo crore. The auction will be

conducted on August 13, 2008 using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation

to the non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of

the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General

Notification No. F.2 (12)-W & M/97 dated 31st March 1998 issued by Government of

India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted  in the prescribed form on Wednesday,

August 13, 2008 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening.
