Certificate of Registration of Mukut Finlease Ltd. Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on June 13, 2008 cancelled the certificate

of registration granted to Mukut Finlease Limited, having its registered office at Mukut

Complex, Rekabganj, Faizabad (U.P.) for carrying on the business of a non-banking

financial institution. Following cancellation of the registration certificate, Mukut

India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-

defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
