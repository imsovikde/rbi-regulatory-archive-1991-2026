Certificate of Registration of M/s Royal Pacific Finance Pvt. Ltd. Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on June 20, 2008 cancelled the certificate

of registration granted to M/s Royal Pacific Finance Private Limited, having its

registered office at 8 Usha Chamber, 1st floor, 37-38 Central Market, Ashok Vihar,

Delhi-110052 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution.

Following cancellation of the registration certificate, M/s Royal Pacific Finance Private

Limited, cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.
