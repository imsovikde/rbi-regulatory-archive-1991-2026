RBl's Special Fixed Rate Term Repo Auction for Rs. 48,275 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 7.5

per cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs.48,275 crore on November 7,

the liquidity requirements of Mutual Funds(MFs) and Non-Banking Financial

amount under this facility as on November 6, 2008 stood at Rs.11,725 crore.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it

every day until further notice up to a cumulative amount of Rs 60,oo0 crore and that

additional liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be

availed by banks exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs and

NBFCs.
