October27,2008

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 8 per

cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs.11,200 crore on October 27, 2008, due

for reversal on November 10, 2008, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity

requirements of mutual funds.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it

would conduct the special fixed rate term repo under liquidity adjustment facility every

purpose. Banks have utilised Rs.8800 crore of this facility till October 24, 2008. In

addition, in terms of further measures for improving domestic and foreign currency

exclusively for the purpose of meeting the liquidity requirements of mutual funds to the

extent of up to 0.5 per cent of their NDTL.
