# Investment by FIIs : M/s. Parekh Aluminex Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has notified today that M/s Parekh Aluminex Limited has passed resolutions at its Board of Directors level and Extra-Ordinary General Meeting that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can purchase equity shares and convertible debentures through primary market or stock exchanges in India upto 74 per cent of its paid up capital.
