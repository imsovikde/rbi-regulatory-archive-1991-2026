## <u>Investment in Credit Information Companies</u>

In exercise of powers conferred by subsection (1) of Section 11 of the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005, the Reserve Bank of India, being satisfied that it is necessary and expedient in the public interest so to do, hereby directs that Investment directly or indirectly by any person, whether resident or otherwise, in any Credit Information Company shall not exceed ten per cent of the equity capital of the investee company.

(Anand Sinha) Executive Director
