## Underwriting auctions for “7.38 per cent Government Stock 2015” and “7.95 percent Government Stock 2032”

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.38 per cent Government Stock 2015**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal) and “7.95 per cent Government Stock 2032**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on April 11, 2008. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on April 10, 2008.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
