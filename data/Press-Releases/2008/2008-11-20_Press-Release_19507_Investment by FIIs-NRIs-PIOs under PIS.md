Investment byFlls/NRls/PiOs underPIS

TheReserveBank of India has today notified that the aggregate share

holding in Andhra Bank by Foreign Institutional Investors (Flls)/ Non Resident

Indians (NRls) / Persons of Indian Origin (PlOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme

(PIS) have gone below the trigger limit of 18 per cent of their paid up capital. It will

therefore be in order to purchase equity shares of the Andhra Bank on behalf of Flls/

NRls/PlOs who have obtained permission to invest under the Portfolio Investment

Scheme.
