364-dayTreasuryBills auction:

Rs.1,ooocroreunderregular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364-day Government of India Treasury

Bills for notified amount of Rs.1,000 crore. The auction will be conducted on June 18, 2008 using

"MultiplePrice Auction"method.The allocation to the non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified

General NotificationNo.F.2(12)-W&M/97dated 31March1998issued by Government of India and as

amendedfrom time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, June 18, 2008 by 12.30 P.M.

Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by successful bidders will be on Friday, June

20,2008.
