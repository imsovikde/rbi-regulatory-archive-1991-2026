April 2, 2008 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.39.98 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.62.25 on April 2, 2008. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 31, 2008) were Rs.39.97 and Rs.63.09 respectively.
