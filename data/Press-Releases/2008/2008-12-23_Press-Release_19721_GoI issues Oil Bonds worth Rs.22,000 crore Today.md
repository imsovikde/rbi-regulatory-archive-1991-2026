Gol issues Oil Bonds worth Rs.22,0o0 crore Today

The Government of India have announced the issue of 6.35 per cent Oil

Marketing Companies Government of India Special Bonds, 2024'for Rs.22,000 crore

(nominal). The Special Bonds are being issued to three Oil Marketing Companies as

compensation towards estimated under-recoveries on account of sale of sensitive

petroleum products during the current financial year.The Special Bonds are being

issued at par to the following Oil Marketing Companies on December 23, 2008

(Tuesday).

(1) Indian Oil Corporation Ltd.(IOCL)

for Rs. 11,975.51 crore

(2) Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (HPCL)

for Rs.4,693.73 crore

(3) Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL)

for Rs. 5,330.76 crore

The investment in the Special Bonds by the banks and Insurance Companies

will not be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government securities for their

statutory requirements. However, such investment by the insurance companies will

Superannuation Funds,etc.in the Special Bonds will be treated as an eligible

investment under the administrative order of the Ministry of Finance.

The Special Bonds will be transferable and eligible for market ready forward

transactions (Repo).
