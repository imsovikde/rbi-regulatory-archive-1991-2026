ReserveBankCancelsCertificateofRegistrationof

TheReserveBankof IndiahasonApril28,2008cancelled thecertificateofregistrationgranted to

M/s. Laxman Printers Private Limited, having its registered office at BA-126, Janakpuri, New Delhi-110058

for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution.Following cancellation of theregistration

certificate,M/s.Laxman Printers Private Limited,cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial

institution.

By thepowers conferred under Section 45-lA(6)of the Reserve Bank of India Act,1934,the

non-bankingfinancial institutionisdefinedinclause(a)ofSection45-IoftheReserveBankofIndiaAct,

1934.
