October 31, 2008

Certificate of Registration of

M/s. Varun Capital Services Private Limited Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 01, 2008 cancelled the certificate

of registration granted to M/s. Varun Capital Services Private Limited having its

registered office at 'Harikrupa' Santaram Road, Nadiad-387001 for carrying on the

business of a non-banking financial institution as the company has ceased to be a

non-banking financial institution and is not traceable. Following cancellation of the

financial institution. The company has not surrendered its original Certificate of

Registration. Members of public are hereby cautioned against misuse of the said

Certificate of Registration.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India

financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in

clause (a) of Section 45-l of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

G. Raghuraj
