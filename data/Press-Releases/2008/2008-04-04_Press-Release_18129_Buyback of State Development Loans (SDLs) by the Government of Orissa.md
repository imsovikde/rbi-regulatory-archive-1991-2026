## April 4, 2008

## Buyback of State Development Loans (SDLs) by the Government of Orissa

The Reserve Bank of India made secondary market purchases of State Development Loans (SDLs) as part of the buyback operations by the State Government of Orissa. As notified by the Government of Orissa on February 22, 2008, the securities stand prematurely redeemed to the extent of the amounts bought back. The details of security wise amounts bought back and the current outstanding stock (after premature redemption of stock bought back) in these securities is given below.
