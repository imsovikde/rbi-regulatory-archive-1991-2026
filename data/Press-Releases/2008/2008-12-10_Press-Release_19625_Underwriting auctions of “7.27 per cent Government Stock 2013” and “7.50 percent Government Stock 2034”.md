Underwriting auctions of "7.27 per cent Government Stock 2013"

and"7.50percentGovernmentStock2034"

The Government of India have announced the sale(reissue)of securities“7.27 per cent

Government Stock 2034" for a notified amount of Rs.4,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to

be held on December 12, 2008 (Friday). The underwriting auctions for both the securities will

be conducted on December 11, 2008.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum

Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional

Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:

Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PDO

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective

PDs at the RBl, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
