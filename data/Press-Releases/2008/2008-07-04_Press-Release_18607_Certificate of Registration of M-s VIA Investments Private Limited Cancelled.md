Certificate of Registration of M/sVlA InvestmentsPrivate Limited Cancelled

registration granted to M/s VlA Investments Private Limited having its registered office

at L-2, Eden Hall, Dr. Annie Besant Road, Worli, Mumbai-40o018 for carrying on the

business of a non-banking financial institution as the company has opted to exit from

the business of a non-banking financial institution.Following cancellation of the

business of a non-banking financial institution.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-lA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act,

1934,the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-banking financial

company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of

Section 45-I of theReserveBank of India Act, 1934.
