## February 19, 2008

## Seven State Governments announce Auctions of

## State Development Loans 2018 Rs. 5,814.175 crore on February 22

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs. 5,814.175 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State

|Sr. No.|Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|
||(Rs. crore)||
|1 Bihar||1196.000|
|2 Gujarat||1000.000|
|3 Jammu & Kashmir||976.000|
|4 Mizoram||42.175|
|5 Tamil Nadu||650.000|
|6 Uttar Pradesh||1,700.000|
|7 Uttarakhand||250.000|

## Total 5,814.175

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional subscription upto a**

## maximum of Rs.142.438 crore over and above the notified amount.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on February 22, 2008 (Friday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Friday, February 22, 2008. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30

P.M. on February 22, 2008. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on February 22, 2008 and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on February 25, 2008 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
