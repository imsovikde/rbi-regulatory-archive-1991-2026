April 9, 2008 **Data on ECB/FCCB for February 2008** The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs), both under Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the month of February 2008.
