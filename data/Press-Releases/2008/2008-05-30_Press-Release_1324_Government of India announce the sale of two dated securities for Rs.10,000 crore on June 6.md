Government of India announce the sale of two dated securities

for Rs.10,000 crore on June 6

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “8.24 per cent

Government Stock 2018" for a notified amount of Rs. 6,0o0 crore (nominal). The

Government of India have also announced the sale (re-issue) of "7.95 per cent

Government Stocks will be sold through price based auctions using multiple price

method. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai

Office, Fort, Mumbai on June 6, 2008 (Friday).

Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of both the stocks will be allotted

to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding

Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve

Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 40oo01 and RBl

website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on June 6, 2008. The NDS

members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in electronic

format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be

submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auctions will be announced on June 6, 2008 and payment by

successful bidders will be during banking hours on June 9, 2008 (Monday).

The Stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

The underwriting of the Government Securities under auctions by the “Primary

Dealers’ will be as per the “Revised Scheme of Underwriting Commitment and

dated November 14,2007.Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive

Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by *Primary Dealers' up to 12.30 P.M.

on June 5, 2008 (Thursday) using PMO module of NDS.

The Stocks will be eligible for "When Issued" trading for a period commencing

from June 2-6, 2008 in accordance with the guidelines on ‘When lssued

transactions in Central Government Securities' issued by the Reserve Bank of India

vide circular No. RBl /2006-07/178 dated November 16, 2006 as amended from time

to time.
