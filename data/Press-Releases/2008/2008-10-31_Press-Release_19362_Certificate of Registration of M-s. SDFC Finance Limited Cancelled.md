Certificate of Registration of M/s. SDFC Finance Limited Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 01, 2008 cancelled the certificate

of registration granted to M/s. SDFC Finance Limited having its registered office at

Vadodara-390o05 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution

as the company has ceased to be a non-banking financial institution. Following

Certificate of Registration and has reported that the certificate has been lost/

Certificate of Registration.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India

Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-banking

clause (a) of Section 45-l of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
