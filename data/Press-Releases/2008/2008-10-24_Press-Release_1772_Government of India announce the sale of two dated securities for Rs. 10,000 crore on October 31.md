Government of India announce the sale of

two dated securities for Rs.10,000 crore on October 31

The Government of India have announced the sale of a new 6 year

based auction using multiple price method. The Government of India have also

announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.95 percent Government Stock 2032" for a notified

amount of Rs. 4,ooo crore (nominal) through a price based auction using multiple

price method. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai

Office, Fort, Mumbai on October 31, 2008 (Friday).

Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of both the stocks will be allotted

to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding

Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve

Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 40oo01 and RBl

websitewww.rbi.org.inshouldbesubmittedtothatOfficeonOctober31,2008.The

NDS members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in

electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids

should be submitted by 12.30P.M.

The result of the auctions will be announced on October 31, 2008 and payment

by successful bidders will be during banking hours on November 3, 2008 (Monday).

The Stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

The underwriting of the Government Securities under auctions by the “Primary

Dealers' will be as per the “Revised Scheme of Underwriting Commitment and

dated November 14, 2007. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive

Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by *Primary Dealers' up to 12.30 P.M.

on October 29, 2008 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS.

The Stocks will be eligible for "When Issued" trading for a period commencing

from October 27-31, 2008 in accordance with the guidelines on “When lssued

transactionsinCentralGovernmentSecurities'issuedbytheReserveBankofIndia

vide circular No. RBl /2006-07/178 dated November 16, 2006 as amended from time

to time.
