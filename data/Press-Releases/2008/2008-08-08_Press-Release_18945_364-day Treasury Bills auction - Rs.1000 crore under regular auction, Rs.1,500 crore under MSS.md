364-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.1000 crore under regular auction, Rs.1,500 crore under MsS

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364-day Government

of India Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.2,500 crore (Rs.1oo0 crore under the

regular auction calendar and Rs.1,500 crore under the Market Stabilisation Scheme

(MSS)). The auction will be conducted on August 13, 2008 using "Multiple Price

notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and

1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, August 13,

2008 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by

successful bidders will be on Thursday, August 14, 2008.
