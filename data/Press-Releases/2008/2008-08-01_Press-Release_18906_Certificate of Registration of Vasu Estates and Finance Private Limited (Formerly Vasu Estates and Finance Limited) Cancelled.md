Certificate of Registration of Vasu Estates and Finance Private Limited (Formerly

VasuEstatesandFinanceLimited)Cancelled

The ReserveBank of India hasonJuly 18,2008cancelledthecertificate of

registration granted toVasu Estates and FinancePrivate Limited(FormerlyVasu Estates

andFinanceLimited)havingitsregisteredofficeat2/1,Enikepadu,Vijayawada-521108for

carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution as the company has opted to

registration certificate, Vasu Estates and Finance Private Limited (Formerly Vasu Estates

and Finance Limited), cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of

Section45-I of theReserveBankofIndiaAct,1934.
