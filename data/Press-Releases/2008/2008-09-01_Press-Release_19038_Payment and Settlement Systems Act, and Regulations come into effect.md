September 1,2008

The Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (51 of 2007) and the Payment

and Settlement Systems Regulations, 2008 have been notified and have come into

effect from August 12, 2008. The Payment and Settlement Systems Act stipulates that

no person other than the Reserve Bank, shall commence or operate a payment system

under the provisions of the Act.

payment system, as defined in Section 2(1)(i) of the Act should apply for authorisation

to the Reserve Bank, unless specifically exempted in terms of the Act. Existing payment

systems will cease to have the right to carry on their operations, unless they obtain an

authorisation within six months from the commencement of the Act (i.e., 12th August

2008).
