CertificateofRegistrationofCEEBEEInvestmentsPrivateLimitedCancelled

TheReserveBankof IndiahasonJune05,2008cancelled thecertificateofregistrationgranted

to CEE BEE Investments Private Limited having its registered office at No.227，1st Main Road,

companyhas opted to exit from thebusiness of a non-banking financial institution.Following cancellation

of the registration certificate,CEE BEE Investments Private Limited,cannot transact the business of a

non-banking financial institution.

Underpowers conferred bySection 45-IA(6)of theReserveBank of IndiaAct,1934,theReserve

banking financial institutionis defined in clause (a)of Section45-I of theReserveBank of IndiaAct,1934.
