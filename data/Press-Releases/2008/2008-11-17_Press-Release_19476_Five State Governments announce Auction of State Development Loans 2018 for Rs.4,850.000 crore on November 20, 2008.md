The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBl) at Mumbai on

November 20, 2008 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional

Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai- 400 001

by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronicformat usingPrimary Market

Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. on November

20, 2008. On the basis of bids received, RBl will determine the maximum yield at which

offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on November 20, 2008 and payment by

successful bidders will be made during banking hours on November 21, 2008 (Friday) at

Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India.

The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in

Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under

facility.
