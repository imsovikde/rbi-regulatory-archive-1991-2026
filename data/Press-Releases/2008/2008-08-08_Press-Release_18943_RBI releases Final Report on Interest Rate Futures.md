August 8, 2008

RBl releases Final Report on Interest Rate Futures

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website (www.rbi.org.in)

the Report of the RBl's Technical Advisory Committee on Money, Foreign Exchange

and Government Securities Markets on Interest Rate Futures.Following an

announcement in the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2007-08, the Technical

Advisory Committee set up a Working Group on Interest Rate Futures with Shri V.K.

Sharma, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India, as Chairman, to recommend

measures to facilitate development of a robust Interest Rate Futures market for

Futures was placed on RBl's website on March 3, 20o8 for public feedback and

taking into account the feedback and comments received from the public, experts,

banks, market participants and the Government of India.
