Welcoming the Finance Minister, the Governor stated that the Budget was

broadly consistent with theoverall objectivesof achievinggrowthalongwithprice

stability. The Finance Minister explained the philosophy of the Budget. He said

measures to stimulate private consumption through appropriate excise and

income tax reduction and through enhanced public expenditure, particularly in

rural areas, and in education and health. He also mentioned that the investment

incentivising investments, would help moderate the downside risks that could arise due to the anticipated slowdown in global growth. He explained the rationale of the agricultural loan waiver scheme, which was essential for the welfare of small and marginal farmers. He said that the banks would be appropriately compensated. He emphasised that it was important to accelerate the extension of credit to farmers.

The members of the Reserve Bank Board unanimously congratulated the Finance Minister on the Budget and commented on a range of issues. The Directors lauded the Budget for its unprecedented emphasis on skill development and its attention to research and development, which not only addressed current constraints but would also enhance Indian competitiveness in the future. This would help growth in manufacturing and employment. At the same time they emphasised the need to make a quantum jump in agricultural growth keeping in view the need for food security apart from enhancing rural employment.

The Governor said that the Reserve Bank would work closely with the Government and the banks to ensure that the loan waiver scheme was implemented within the announced time frame, so as to serve the interest of the farmers and improve credit flow while subserving the interest of the banking system. **Alpana Killawala** Chief General Manager
