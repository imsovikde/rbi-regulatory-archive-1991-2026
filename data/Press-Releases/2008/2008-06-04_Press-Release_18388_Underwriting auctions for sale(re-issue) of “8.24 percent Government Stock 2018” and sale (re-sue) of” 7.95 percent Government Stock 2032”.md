of"7.95percent GovernmentStock2032"

2018 for a notified amount of Rs.6,000 crore (nominal) and sale (re-issue) of 7.95per cent Government

Stock 2032 for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on June 6,

2008.TheunderwritingauctionsforboththesecuritieswillbeconductedonJune5,2008.
