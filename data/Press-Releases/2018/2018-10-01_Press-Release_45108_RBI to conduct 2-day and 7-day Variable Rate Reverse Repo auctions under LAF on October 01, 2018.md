## October 01, 2018

**RBI to conduct 2-day and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on October 01, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on October 01, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|500|7|12.30 pm to 1.00 pm|October 8, 2018 (Monday)|
|2|800|2|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|October 3, 2018 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
