# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8737 on March 16, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 15, 2018) was

64.9366. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 15, 2018 March 16, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.3201 79.9114 **1 GBP** 90.7229 90.4858 **100 YEN** 61.27 61.27 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
