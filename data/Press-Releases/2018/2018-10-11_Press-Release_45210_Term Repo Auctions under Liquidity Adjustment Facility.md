## October 11, 2018

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (in ` bn)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|October 12, 2018 (Friday)|235|14|October 26, 2018 (Friday)|
|2|October 16, 2018 (Tuesday)|235|14|October 30, 2018 (Tuesday)|
|3|October 19, 2018 (Friday)|235|14|November 2, 2018 (Friday)|
|4|October 23, 2018 (Tuesday)|235|14|November 6, 2018 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08,</u> <u>2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
