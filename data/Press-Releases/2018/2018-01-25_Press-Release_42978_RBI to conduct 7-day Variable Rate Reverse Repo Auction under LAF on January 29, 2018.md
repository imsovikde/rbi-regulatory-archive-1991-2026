January 25, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on January 29, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on January 29, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
