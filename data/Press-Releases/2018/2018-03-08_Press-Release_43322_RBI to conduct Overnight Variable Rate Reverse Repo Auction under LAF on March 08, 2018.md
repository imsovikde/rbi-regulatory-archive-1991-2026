## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auction under LAF on March 08, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on March 08, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1.|150|1|1.00 pm to 1.30 pm|March 09, 2018 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
