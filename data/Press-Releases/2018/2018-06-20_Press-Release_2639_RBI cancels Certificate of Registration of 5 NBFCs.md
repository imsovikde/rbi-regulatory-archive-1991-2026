## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of 5 NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has cancelled the certificate of registration of the following five Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act,

1934.
