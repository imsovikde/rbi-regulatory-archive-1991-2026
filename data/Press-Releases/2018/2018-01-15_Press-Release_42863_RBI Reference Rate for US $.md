# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4125 on January 15, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 12, 2018) was

63.5263. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 12, 2018 January 15, 2018** **1 EUR** 76.5301 77.4457 **1 GBP** 86.0527 87.1795 **100 YEN** 57.09 57.23 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
