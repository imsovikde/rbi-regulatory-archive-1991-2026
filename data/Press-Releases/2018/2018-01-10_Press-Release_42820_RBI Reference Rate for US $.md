# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.8264 on January 10, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 09, 2018) was

63.4690. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 09, 2018 January 10, 2018** **1 EUR** 75.9153 76.1385 **1 GBP** 86.1528 86.2805 **100 YEN** 56.33 56.81 **Note :** The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.
