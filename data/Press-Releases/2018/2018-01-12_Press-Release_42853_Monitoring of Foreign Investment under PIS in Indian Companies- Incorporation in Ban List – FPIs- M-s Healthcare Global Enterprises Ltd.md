## January 12, 2018

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List –

## FPIs: M/s Healthcare Global Enterprises Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) in <u>M/s Healthcare Global Enterprises Ltd.</u> has crossed the limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchase of shares of this company would be allowed through Stock Exchanges in India on behalf of FPIs.
