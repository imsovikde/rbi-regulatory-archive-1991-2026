## August 02, 2018

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|August 03, 2018 (Friday)|225|13|August 16, 2018 (Thursday)|
|2|August 07, 2018 (Tuesday)|225|14|August 21, 2018 (Tuesday)|
|3|August 10, 2018 (Friday)|225|14|August 24, 2018 (Friday)|
|4|August 14, 2018 (Tuesday)|225|14|August 28, 2018 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
