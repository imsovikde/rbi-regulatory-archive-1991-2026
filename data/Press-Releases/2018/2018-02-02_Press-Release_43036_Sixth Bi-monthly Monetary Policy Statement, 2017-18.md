## February 02, 2018

## Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on February 6 and 7, 2018 for the Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2017-18. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on February 7, 2018.
