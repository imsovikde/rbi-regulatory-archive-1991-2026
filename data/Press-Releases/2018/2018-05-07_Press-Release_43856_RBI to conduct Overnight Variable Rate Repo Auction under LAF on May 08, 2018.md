## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF on May 08, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on May 08, 2018, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
