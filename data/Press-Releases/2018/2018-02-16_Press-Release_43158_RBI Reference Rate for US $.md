# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.9097 on February 16, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 15, 2018) was

63.9222. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 15, 2018 February 16, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.6790 80.1683 **1 GBP** 89.5486 90.3108 **100 YEN** 60.05 60.46 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
