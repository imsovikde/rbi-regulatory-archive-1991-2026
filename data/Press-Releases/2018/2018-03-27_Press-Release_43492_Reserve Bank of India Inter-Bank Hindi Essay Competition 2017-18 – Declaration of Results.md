## Reserve Bank of India Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition 2017-18 –

## Declaration of Results

With a view to encourage original writing in Hindi on banking subjects, Reserve Bank of India conducted an Inter-Bank Hindi Essay competition for the year 2017-18 in which staff members (Except Rajbhasha Officers and Translators) of RBI, PSBs and FIs have participated.
