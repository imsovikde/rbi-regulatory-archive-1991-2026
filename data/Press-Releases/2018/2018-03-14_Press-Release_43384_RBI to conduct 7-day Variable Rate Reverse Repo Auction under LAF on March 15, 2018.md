March 14, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on March 15, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on March 15, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1.|500|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|March 22, 2018 (Thursday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
