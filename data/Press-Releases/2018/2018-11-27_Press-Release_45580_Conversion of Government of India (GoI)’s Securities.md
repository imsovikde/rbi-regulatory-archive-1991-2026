## Conversion of Government of India (GoI)’s Securities

The Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has converted a security maturing in 2018-19, having a total face value of ₹2,779.986 crore, to a longer tenor security maturing in 2023-24.

The transaction has been conducted on November 26, 2018 at the Financial Benchmarks India Private Limited (FBIL) prices with a scheduled commercial bank.
