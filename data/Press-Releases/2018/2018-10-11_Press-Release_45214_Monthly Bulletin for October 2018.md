October 11, 2018 **Monthly Bulletin for October 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>October 2018</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for the Year</u> <u>2018-19</u>, <u>Monetary Policy Report-October 2018</u>, a speech by the top management, two articles, press release of a Working Paper and Occasional Papers and Current Statistics.
