January 02, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on January 03, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on January 03, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Amount Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day/s)|||
|1|200|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|January 17, 2018 (Wednesday) January 10, 2018|

2 300 7 2.30 pm to 3.00 pm (Wednesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
