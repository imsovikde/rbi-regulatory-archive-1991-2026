## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on February 08, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on February 08, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
