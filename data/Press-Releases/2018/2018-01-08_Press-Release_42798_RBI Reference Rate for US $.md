# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.3482 on January 08, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 05, 2018) was

63.3823. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 05, 2018 January 08, 2018** **1 EUR** 76.5024 76.1509 **1 GBP** 86.0225 85.8875 **100 YEN** 56.06 55.95 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
