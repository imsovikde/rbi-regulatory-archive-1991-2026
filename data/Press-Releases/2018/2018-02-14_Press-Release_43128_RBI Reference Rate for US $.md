# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1259 on February 14, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 12, 2018) was

64.2838. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 12, 2018 February 14, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.9341 79.3879 **1 GBP** 89.0202 89.1735 **100 YEN** 59.14 59.84 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
