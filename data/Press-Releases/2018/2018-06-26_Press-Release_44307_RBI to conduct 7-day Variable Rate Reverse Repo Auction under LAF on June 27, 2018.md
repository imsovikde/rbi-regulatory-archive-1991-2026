June 26, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under** **LAF on June 27, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on June 27, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|

|1.|250|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|

## Date of Reversal

July 04, 2018 (Wednesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
