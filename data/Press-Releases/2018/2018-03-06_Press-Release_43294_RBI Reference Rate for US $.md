# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9941 on March 06, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 05, 2018) was

65.0530. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 05, 2018 March 06, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.0347 80.2092 **1 GBP** 89.6951 89.9063 **100 YEN** 61.64 61.20 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
