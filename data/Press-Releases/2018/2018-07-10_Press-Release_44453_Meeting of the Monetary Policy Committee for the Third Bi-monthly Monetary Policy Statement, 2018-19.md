## Meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee for the Third Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19

The Third Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2018-19 will be issued on August 1, 2018. The Monetary Policy Committee has decided to continue the practice of a three-day meeting format commencing on July 30, 2018 as was adopted in the meeting for the Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement of June 6, 2018.
