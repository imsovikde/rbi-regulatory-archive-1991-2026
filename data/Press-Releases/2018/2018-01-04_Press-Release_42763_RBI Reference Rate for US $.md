# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.3914 on January 04, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 03, 2018) was

63.4833. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
