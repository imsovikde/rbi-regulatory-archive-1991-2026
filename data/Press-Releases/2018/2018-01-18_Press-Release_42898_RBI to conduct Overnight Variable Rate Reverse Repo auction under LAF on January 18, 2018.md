January 18, 2018 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on January 18, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on January 18, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1|150|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|January 19, 2018 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
