August 23, 2018 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on August 23, 2018** In the underwriting auctions conducted on August 23, 2018 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

|7.37% GS 2023|2000|1008|992|2000||0.46|
|7.17% GS 2028|5000|2520|2480|5000||0.41|
|7.40% GS 2035|2000|1008|992|2000||1.98|
|7.06% GS 2046|3000|1512|1488|3000||1.93|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 24, 2018.
