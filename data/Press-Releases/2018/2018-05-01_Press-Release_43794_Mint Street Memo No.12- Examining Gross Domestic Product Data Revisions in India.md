## May 01, 2018

## Mint Street Memo No.12:

## Examining Gross Domestic Product Data Revisions in India

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the twelfth release under the series ‘<u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u>’ titled “<u>Examining Gross Domestic Product</u> <u>Data Revisions in India</u>”

The paper authored by Anupam Prakash, Avdhesh Kumar Shukla, Anand Prakash Ekka and Kunal Priyadarshi from the Department of Economic and Policy Research analyses the data revisions in Gross Domestic Product (GDP) estimates.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
