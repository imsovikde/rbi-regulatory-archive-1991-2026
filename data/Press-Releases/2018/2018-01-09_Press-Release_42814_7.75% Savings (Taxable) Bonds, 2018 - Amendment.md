## 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018 - Amendment

The Government of India has issued a <u>Notification F.No.4(28)-W&M/2017</u> <u>dated January 08, 2018</u> amending the original <u>Notification F.No.4(28)-W&M/2017</u> <u>dated January 03, 2018</u>. Receiving Offices are advised to take note of these amendments while accepting the subscriptions under the 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018. Instructions in this regard are being issued vide our <u>circular</u> <u>IDMD.CDD.No.1728/13.01.299/2017-18 dated January 08, 2018</u>.
