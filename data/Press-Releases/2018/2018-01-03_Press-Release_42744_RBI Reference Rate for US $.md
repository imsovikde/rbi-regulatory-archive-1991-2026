# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4833 on January 03, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 02, 2018) was

63.6728. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 02, 2018 January 03, 2018** **1 EUR** 76.5856 76.5418 **1 GBP** 86.1111 86.4071 **100 YEN** 56.52 56.55 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
