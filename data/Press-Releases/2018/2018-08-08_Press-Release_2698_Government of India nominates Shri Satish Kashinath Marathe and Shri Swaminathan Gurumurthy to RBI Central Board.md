## August 08, 2018

## Government of India nominates Shri Satish Kashinath Marathe and

## Shri Swaminathan Gurumurthy to RBI Central Board

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of the sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Central Government has nominated Shri Satish Kashinath Marathe and Shri Swaminathan Gurumurthy as directors of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from August 7, 2018 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
