## RBI to conduct 7-day and 11-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on February 05, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on February 05, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
