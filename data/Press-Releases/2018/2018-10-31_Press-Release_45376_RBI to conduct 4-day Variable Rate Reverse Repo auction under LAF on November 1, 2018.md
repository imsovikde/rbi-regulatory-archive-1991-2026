## RBI to conduct 4-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on November 1, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on November 1, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
