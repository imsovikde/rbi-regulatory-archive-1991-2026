## June 04, 2018

**RBI to conduct 7- Day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on June 04, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on June 04, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|500|7|2.30 pm to 3:00 pm|June 11, 2018 (Monday)|
|2.|300|14|12.00 pm to 12:30 pm|June 18, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
