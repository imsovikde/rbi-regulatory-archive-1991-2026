March 28, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable rate Repo Auction under LAF on March 28, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Repo Auction on March 28, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day)|||
|1|500|7|12.30 pm to 1.00 pm|April 04, 2018 (Wednesday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
