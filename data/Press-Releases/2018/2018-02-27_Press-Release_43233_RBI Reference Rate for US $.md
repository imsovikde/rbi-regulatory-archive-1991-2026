# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8514 on February 27, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 26, 2018) was

64.6639. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 26, 2018 February 27, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.6983 79.9683 **1 GBP** 90.6523 90.5844 **100 YEN** 60.69 60.71 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
