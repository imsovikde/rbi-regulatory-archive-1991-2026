**RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on January 1, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 1, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
