## RBI to conduct 3-day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction today (April 03, 2018, Tuesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
