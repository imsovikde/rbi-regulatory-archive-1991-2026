## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on May 15, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on May 15, 2018, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
