# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.3496 on April 12, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 11, 2018) was

65.1272. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 11, 2018 April 12, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.5884 80.7982 **1 GBP** 92.5197 92.7049 **100 YEN** 60.84 61.15 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
