# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7156 on May 17, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 16, 2018) was

67.8276. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
