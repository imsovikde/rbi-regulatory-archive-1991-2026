June 29, 2018 **RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under** **LAF on June 29, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on June 29, 2018, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

||Notified|Tenor|||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Sl. No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|1.|200|3|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|July 02, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
