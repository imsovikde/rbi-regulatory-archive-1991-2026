## RBI to conduct 28-day Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF on September 17, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo Auction on September 17, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
