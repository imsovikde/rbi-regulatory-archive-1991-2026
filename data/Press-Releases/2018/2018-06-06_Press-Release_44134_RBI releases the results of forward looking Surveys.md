June 06, 2018 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys** The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – May 2018</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – May 2018</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Round 52</u>
The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
