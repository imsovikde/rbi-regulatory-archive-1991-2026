## 4 NBFCs surrender their Certificate of Registration to RBI

The following NBFCs have surrendered the Certificate of Registration granted to them by the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has therefore cancelled their Certificate of Registration.

|Sr. No.|Company's Name|Address of Registered Office|Certificate of Registration No.|Issued On|Date of cancellation|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|M/s Tristar Pvt. Ltd.|34, Chittaranjan Avenue, Kolkata-700012|05.02633|June 04, 1998|May 15, 2018|
|2|Kottai Finance & Investments Limited, Pattukottai|Old No 171, New No 182/A, South Kaliyamman Kovil Street, Pattukottai Tamilandu-614601|B-07.00614|June 14, 2001|June 15, 2018|
|3|Rajammal Rangasamy Finance Limited|12/161, G.R.Complex, Theppakulam Street, Tiruchengode, Namakkal Tamilnadu-637211|B-07.00599|July 13, 2016|June 07, 2018|
|4|Gauri Shankar Credit & Leasing Pvt. Ltd.|33, Prakash Apartments, 5 Ansari Road, Darya Ganj, New Delhi-110002|14.01511|September 10, 1999|March 16, 2018|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
