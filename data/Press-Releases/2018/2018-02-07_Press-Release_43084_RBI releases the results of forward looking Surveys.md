## February 07, 2018

## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – December 2017</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – December 2017</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Round 50</u> iv. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS)</u> <u>– Round 39 (Q2:2017-18)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to
the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
