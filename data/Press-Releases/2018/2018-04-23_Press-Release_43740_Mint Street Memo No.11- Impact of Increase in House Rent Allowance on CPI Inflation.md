## Mint Street Memo No.11: Impact of Increase in House Rent Allowance on CPI Inflation

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the eleventh release under the series ‘<u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u> titled “<u>Impact of Increase in House Rent</u> <u>Allowance on CPI Inflation</u>”.

The paper authored by Praggya Das from Monetary Policy Department analyses the impact of increase in House Rent Allowance on headline Consumer Price Index Inflation.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
