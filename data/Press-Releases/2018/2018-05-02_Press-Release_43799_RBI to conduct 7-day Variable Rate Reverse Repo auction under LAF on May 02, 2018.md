May 2, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on May 02, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on May 02, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1.|350|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|May 09, 2018 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
