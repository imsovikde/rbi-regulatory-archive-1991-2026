## Meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee for the Fifth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19

The Fifth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2018-19 will be issued on December 5, 2018. The Monetary Policy Committee has decided to continue the practice of a three-day meeting format commencing on December 3, 2018.
