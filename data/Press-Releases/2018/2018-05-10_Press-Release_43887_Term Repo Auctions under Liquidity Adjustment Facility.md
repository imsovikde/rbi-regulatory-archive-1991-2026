## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|May 11, 2018 (Friday)|225|14|May 25, 2018 (Friday)|
|2|May 15, 2018 (Tuesday)|225|14|May 29, 2018 (Tuesday)|
|3|May 18, 2018 (Friday)|225|14|June 01, 2018 (Friday)|
|4|May 22, 2018 (Tuesday)|220|14|June 05, 2018 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February</u> <u>13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
