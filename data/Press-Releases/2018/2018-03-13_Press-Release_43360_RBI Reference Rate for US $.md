# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9567 on March 13, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 12, 2018) was

65.0199. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 12, 2018 March 13, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.0915 80.0916 **1 GBP** 90.1241 90.2314 **100 YEN** 60.96 60.85 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
