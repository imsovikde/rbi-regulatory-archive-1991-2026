October 03, 2018 **RBI to conduct 2-Day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on October 3, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on October 3, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|

|1.|700|2|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|October 05, 2018 (Friday)|
