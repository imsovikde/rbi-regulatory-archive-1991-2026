# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7183 on January 19, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 18, 2018) was

63.8431. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 18, 2018 January 19, 2018** **1 EUR** 77.8822 78.1441 **1 GBP** 88.2822 88.6449 **100 YEN** 57.41 57.49 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
