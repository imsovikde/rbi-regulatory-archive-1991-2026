## Second Bi-Monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2018-19

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on June 4-6, 2018 for the Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2018-19. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on June 6, 2018.
