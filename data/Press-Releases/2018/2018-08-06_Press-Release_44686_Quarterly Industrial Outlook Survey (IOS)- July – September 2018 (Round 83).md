## Quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS): July – September 2018 (Round 83)

rd The Reserve Bank of India has launched the <u>83 round</u> of the quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) of the Indian manufacturing sector for the reference period July-September 2018 (Q2:2018-19). The survey assesses business sentiment for the current quarter and expectations for the ensuing quarter, based on qualitative responses on a set of indicators pertaining to demand conditions, financial conditions, employment conditions and the price situation. The survey provides useful insight into the performance of the manufacturing sector.

A <u>detailed article</u> presenting quarterly results for 2016-17 was published in the June 2017 issue of the Bank’s Bulletin and the results for the <u>latest round i.e., for</u> <u>Q1:2018-19</u> was published on August 01, 2018.

M/S Hansa Research Group Pvt. Ltd. has been authorised to conduct the survey for July-September, 2018 quarter on behalf of the Bank. While selected manufacturing companies will be approached by this agency, other manufacturing companies, who are not approached, may also participate in this survey by downloading the survey schedule from the Bank’s website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The <u>survey</u> <u>schedule</u> is placed under the head ‘Forms’ (available in the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be e-mailed or faxed as per contact details given in the survey schedule.

Identity of the respondents is not revealed.

In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact us at the following address:
