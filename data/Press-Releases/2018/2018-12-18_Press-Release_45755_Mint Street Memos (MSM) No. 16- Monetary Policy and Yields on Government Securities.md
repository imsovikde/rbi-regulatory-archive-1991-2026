## Mint Street Memos (MSM) No. 16:

## Monetary Policy and Yields on Government Securities

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the sixteenth release under the series ‘Mint Street Memos (MSM)’ titled “Monetary Policy and Yields on Government Securities”.

The paper authored by Muneesh Kapur, Joice John and Pratik Mitra from Monetary Policy Department examines the drivers of government bond yields in India.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
