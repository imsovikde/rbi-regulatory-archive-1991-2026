## September 20 2018

## Mint Street Memo No. 14: Finance-Neutral Output Gap:

## Empirical Estimates for India

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the fourteenth release under the series ‘<u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u>’ titled “<u>Finance-Neutral Output Gap:</u> <u>Empirical Estimates for India</u>”.

The paper authored by Deba Prasad Rath, Pratik Mitra and Joice John from the Monetary Policy Department analyses the finance-neutral output gap (FNOG) of the Indian economy.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
