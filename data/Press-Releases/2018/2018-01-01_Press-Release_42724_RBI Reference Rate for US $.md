# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.6697 on January 01, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 29, 2017) was

63.9273. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 29, 2017 January 01, 2018** **1 EUR** 76.3867 76.4100 **1 GBP** 86.0653 85.8968 **100 YEN** 56.72 56.51 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
