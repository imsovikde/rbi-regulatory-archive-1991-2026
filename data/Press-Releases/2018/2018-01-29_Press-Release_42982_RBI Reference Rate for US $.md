# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.5470 on January 29, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 25, 2018) was

63.4983. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 25, 2018 January 29, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.0681 78.8682 **1 GBP** 90.9169 89.8237 **100 YEN** 58.36 58.38 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
