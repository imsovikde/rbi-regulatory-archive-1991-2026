## RBI releases Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services: January 2018

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of January 2018 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** (US$ Million)

## Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)

|October-17|14,152|8,700|
|---|---|---|
|November-17|15,392|9,647|
|December -17|16,005|9,859|
|January-18|16,337|9,847|

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
