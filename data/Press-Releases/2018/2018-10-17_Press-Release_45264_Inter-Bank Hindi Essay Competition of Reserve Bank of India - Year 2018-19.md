## Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition of Reserve Bank of India-Year 2018-19

With a view to encourage original writing in Hindi on banking subjects, Reserve Bank of India conducts an inter-bank Hindi Essay Competition every year. All Officers and Staff Members (Except Rajbhasha Officers and Translators) of Public Sector Banks (Including RRBs sponsored by Banks), Financial Institutions and Reserve Bank of India can participate in this Competition. Accordingly, three topics selected for the competition to be held for the year 2018-19 are as under: -
