July 25, 2018 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF on July 25, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on July 25, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|150|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|July 26, 2018 (Thursday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
