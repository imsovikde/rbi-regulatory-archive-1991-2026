April 03, 2018 **RBI to conduct 14-day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today** **for** ` **600 billion (instead of** ` **400 billion as announced earlier)** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction today (April 03, 2018, Tuesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Revised Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|600|14|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|April 17, 2018 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
