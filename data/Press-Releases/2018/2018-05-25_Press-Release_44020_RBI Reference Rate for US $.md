# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.2600 on May 25, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 24, 2018) was

68.3872. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **May 24, 2018 May 25, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.1430 79.8847 **1 GBP** 91.4405 91.2158 **100 YEN** 62.40 62.38 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
