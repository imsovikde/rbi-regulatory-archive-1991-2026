## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on January 24, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on January 24, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
