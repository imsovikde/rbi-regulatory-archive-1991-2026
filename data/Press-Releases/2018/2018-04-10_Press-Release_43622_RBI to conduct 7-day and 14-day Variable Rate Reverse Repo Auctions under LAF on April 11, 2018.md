## RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on April 11, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 11, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
