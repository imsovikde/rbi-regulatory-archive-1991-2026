**Expert Committee on high divergence observed in asset classification and provisioning by banks and prevention of frauds**

It may be recalled that in February 2018, the Reserve Bank of India had constituted an Expert Committee to look into the reasons for high divergence observed in asset classification and provisioning by banks and suggest measures for prevention of frauds, under the Chairmanship of Shri Y.H. Malegam, former Member of Board of Directors, Reserve Bank of India. Shri S. Raman, former Chairman & Managing Director, Canara Bank and retired Whole Time Member, Securities and Exchange Board of India, was a member of the Committee. In the wake of certain media reports about action taken in a case by an investigating agency, Shri Raman has, of his own volition, tendered his resignation from the Expert Committee on grounds of good governance practices. The Expert Committee is expected to submit its report shortly.
