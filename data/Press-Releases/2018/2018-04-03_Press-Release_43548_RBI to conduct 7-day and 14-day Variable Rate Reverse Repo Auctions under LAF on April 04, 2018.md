April 03, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on April 04, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on April 04, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
