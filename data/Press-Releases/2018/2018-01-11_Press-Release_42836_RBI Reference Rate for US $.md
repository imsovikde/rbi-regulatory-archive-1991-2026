# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7364 on January 11, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 10, 2018) was

63.8264. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||January 10, 2018|January 11, 2018|

|1 EUR|76.1385|76.1395|
|---|---|---|
|1 GBP|86.2805|86.0123|
|100 YEN|56.81|57.03|
