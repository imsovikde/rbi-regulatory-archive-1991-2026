October 19, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on October 19, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on October 19, 2018, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|450|7|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|October 26, 2018 (Friday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
