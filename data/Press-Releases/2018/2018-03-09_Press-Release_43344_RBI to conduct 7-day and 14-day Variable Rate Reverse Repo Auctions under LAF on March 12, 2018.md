March 09, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on March 12, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on March 12, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|---|---|---|---|---|

2. 400 7 2.30 pm to 3.00 pm
(Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
