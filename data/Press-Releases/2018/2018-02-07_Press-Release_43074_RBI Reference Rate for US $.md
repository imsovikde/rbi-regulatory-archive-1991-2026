# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1377 on February 07, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 06, 2018) was

64.2723. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 06, 2018 February 07, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.5241 79.4345 **1 GBP** 89.7177 89.4977 **100 YEN** 59.05 58.71 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
