## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on

## September 07, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on September 07, 2018, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

||Notified|Tenor|||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Sl. No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|1.|200|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|September 14, 2018 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
