# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.9797 on January 17, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 16, 2018) was

63.7602. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 16, 2018 January 17, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.2019 78.3495 **1 GBP** 87.9126 88.1256 **100 YEN** 57.50 57.74 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
