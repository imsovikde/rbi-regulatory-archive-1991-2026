July 13, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on July 16, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on July 16, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1.|150|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|July 23, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
