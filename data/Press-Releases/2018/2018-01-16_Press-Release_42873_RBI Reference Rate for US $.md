# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7602 on January 16, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 15, 2018) was

63.4125. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 15, 2018 January 16, 2018** **1 EUR** 77.4457 78.2019 **1 GBP** 87.1795 87.9126 **100 YEN** 57.23 57.50 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
