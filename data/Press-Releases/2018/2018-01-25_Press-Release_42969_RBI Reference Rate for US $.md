# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4983 on January 25, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 24, 2018) was

63.6439. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 24, 2018 January 25, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.4538 79.0681 **1 GBP** 89.3242 90.9169 **100 YEN** 57.92 58.36 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
