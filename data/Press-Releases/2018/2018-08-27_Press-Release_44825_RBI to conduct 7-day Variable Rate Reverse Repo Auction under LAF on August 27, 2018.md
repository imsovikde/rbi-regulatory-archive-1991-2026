August 27, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on August 27, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on August 27, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

||Notified|Tenor|||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Sl. No.|Amount (` billion)|(day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|1.|400|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|September 03, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
