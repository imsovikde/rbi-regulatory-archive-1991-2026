January 01, 2018 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo** **Auction under LAF on January 01, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo Auction on January 01, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|300|1|04.00 pm to 04.30 pm|January 02, 2018 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
