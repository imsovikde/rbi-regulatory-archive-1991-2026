March 12, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on March 13, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on March 13, 2018, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Amount Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day/s)|||

2. 500 7 2.30 pm to 3.00 pm
(Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
