## Government of India nominates Ms. Revathy Iyer and Prof. Sachin

## Chaturvedi to the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of the sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Central Government has nominated Ms. Revathy Iyer and Prof. Sachin Chaturvedi (vice Dr. Nachiket Madhusudan Mor) as Directors to the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from September 19, 2018 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
