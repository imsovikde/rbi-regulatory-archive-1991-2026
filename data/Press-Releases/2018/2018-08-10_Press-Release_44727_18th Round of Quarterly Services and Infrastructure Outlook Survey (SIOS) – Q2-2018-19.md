## August 10, 2018

**th** **18 Round of Quarterly Services and Infrastructure** **Outlook Survey (SIOS) – Q2:2018-19**

The Reserve Bank has been conducting the Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS) since 2014-15. The survey is conducted on a quarterly basis. th The <u>18 round</u> of the SIOS seeks an assessment of the business situation for the current quarter (Q2:2018-19, *i.e.*, July-September 2018) from selected companies in the services and infrastructure sectors in India, and their outlook for the ensuing quarter (Q3:2018-19, *i.e.*, October-December 2018).

Companies in the Services and Infrastructure sectors are encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> along with the guidelines from the website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed or faxed as per the contact details given below. The responses of the individual companies will be kept strictly confidential.

In case of any query/clarification, feel free to contact us at:
