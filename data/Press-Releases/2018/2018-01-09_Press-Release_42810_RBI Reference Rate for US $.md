# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4690 on January 09, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 08, 2018) was

63.3482. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 08, 2018 January 09, 2018** **1 EUR** 76.1509 75.9153 **1 GBP** 85.8875 86.1528 **100 YEN** 55.95 56.33 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
