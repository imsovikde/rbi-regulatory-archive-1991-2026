## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on December 6, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on December 6, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
