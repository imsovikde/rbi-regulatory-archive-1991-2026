# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7722 on January 23, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 22, 2018) was

63.8895. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 22, 2018 January 23, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.1241 78.2166 **1 GBP** 88.5956 89.2046 **100 YEN** 57.64 57.57 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
