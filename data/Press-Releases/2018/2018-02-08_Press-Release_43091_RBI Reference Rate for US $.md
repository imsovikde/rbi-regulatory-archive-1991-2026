# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1616 on February 08, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 07, 2018) was

64.1377. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 07, 2018 February 08, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.4345 78.7455 **1 GBP** 89.4977 89.1910 **100 YEN** 58.71 58.50 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
