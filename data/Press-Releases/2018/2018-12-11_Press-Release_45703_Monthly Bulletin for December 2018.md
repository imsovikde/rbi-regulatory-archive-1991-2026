## December 11, 2018

## Monthly Bulletin for December 2018

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>December 2018</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>Fifth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for</u> <u>the Year 2018-19</u>, two Articles, Current Statistics and Indicative Calendar for Bulletin Articles, 2019.
