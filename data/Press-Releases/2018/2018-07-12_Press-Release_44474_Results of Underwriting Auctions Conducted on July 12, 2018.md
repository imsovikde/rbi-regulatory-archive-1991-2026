July 12, 2018 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on July 12, 2018** In the underwriting auctions conducted on July 12, 2018 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

|7.17% GS 2028|4000|2016|1984|4000||0.47|
|7.40% GS 2035|2000|1008|992|2000||1.82|
|7.06% GS 2046|3000|1512|1488|3000||1.73|
