February 02, 2018 **RBI to conduct 3 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on February 02, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on February 02, 2018, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|200|3|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|February 05, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
