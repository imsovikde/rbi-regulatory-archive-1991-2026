## RBI to conduct 8-day Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on September 25, 2018

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on September 25, 2018, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
