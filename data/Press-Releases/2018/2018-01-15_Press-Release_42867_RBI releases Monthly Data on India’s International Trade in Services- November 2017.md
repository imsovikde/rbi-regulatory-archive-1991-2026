January 15, 2018 **RBI releases Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services: November 2017**

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of November 2017 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** **(US$ Million)** **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

October-17 14,152 8,700 November-17 15,392 9,647

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
