August 17, 2018 **Mint Street Memo No.13:** **How have MSME Sector Credit and Exports Fared?**

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the thirteenth release under the series <u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u> titled “<u>How have MSME Sector Credit and</u> <u>Exports Fared?</u>”

The paper authored by Harendra Behera and Garima Wahi from Monetary Policy Department assesses the recent credit dynamics and export performance of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
