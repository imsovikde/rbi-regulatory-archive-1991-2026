# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.6878 on January 31, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 30, 2018) was

63.7534. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 30, 2018 January 31, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.7546 79.2149 **1 GBP** 89.3886 90.3539 **100 YEN** 58.62 58.60 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
