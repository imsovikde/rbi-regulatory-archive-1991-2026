## March 27, 2018

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|March 28, 2018 (Wednesday)|220|16|April 13, 2018 (Friday)|
|2|April 03, 2018 (Tuesday)|220|14|April 17, 2018 (Tuesday)|
|3|April 06, 2018 (Friday)|220|14|April 20, 2018 (Friday)|
|4|April 10, 2018 (Tuesday)|220|14|April 24, 2018 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February</u> 13, 2014. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.

Further, as announced vide our Press Release dated <u>February 15, 2018</u>, Standalone Primary Dealers would be eligible to participate in the auction to be conducted on March 28, 2018, within the notified amount.
