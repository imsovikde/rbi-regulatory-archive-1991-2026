# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0199 on March 12, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 09, 2018) was

65.0784. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 09, 2018 March 12, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.1636 80.0915 **1 GBP** 89.8537 90.1241 **100 YEN** 60.97 60.96 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
