# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.6639 on February 26, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 23, 2018) was

64.8227. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 23, 2018 February 26, 2018** **1 EUR** 79.7643 79.6983 **1 GBP** 90.4017 90.6523 **100 YEN** 60.59 60.69 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
