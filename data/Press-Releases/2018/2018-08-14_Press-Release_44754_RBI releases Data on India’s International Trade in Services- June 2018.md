## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: June 2018

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of June 2018 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** **(US$ Million)** **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

|April-18|17,563|10,915|
|---|---|---|
|May-18|16,173|10,208|
|June-18 Note : Data are provisional.|16,872|10,299|

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
