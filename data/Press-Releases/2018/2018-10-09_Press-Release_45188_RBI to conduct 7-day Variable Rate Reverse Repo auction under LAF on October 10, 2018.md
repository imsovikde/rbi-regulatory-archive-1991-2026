October 09, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on October 10, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on October 10, 2018, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
