December 11, 2018 **RBI to conduct 56-day, 21-day and 14-day Variable Rate** **Repo auctions under LAF**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Repo Auctions as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (` Billion)|
|---|---|
|December 13, 2018 (Thursday)|250|
|December 14, 2018 (Friday)|350|
|December 19, 2018 (Wednesday)|200|

|Sl|Tenor Window Timing|
|---|---|
|No.|(day)|
|1|56|
|2|21|
|3|14|

## Date of Reversal

|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 07, 2019 (Thursday)|
|---|---|
|10.00 am to 10.30 January 04, 2019 am|(Friday)|
|10.00 am to 10.30 January 02, 2019 am|(Wednesday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates.

Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
