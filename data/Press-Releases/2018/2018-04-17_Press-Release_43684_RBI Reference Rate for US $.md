# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.6124 on April 17, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 16, 2018) was

65.4476. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 16, 2018 April 17, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.7362 81.3200 **1 GBP** 93.2759 94.2063 **100 YEN** 61.02 61.35 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
