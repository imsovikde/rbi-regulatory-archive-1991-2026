July 27, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on July 30, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on July 30, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1.|200|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|August 6, 2018 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
