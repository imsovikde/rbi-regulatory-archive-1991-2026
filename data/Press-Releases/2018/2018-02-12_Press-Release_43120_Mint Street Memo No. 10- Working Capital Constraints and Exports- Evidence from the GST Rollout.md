## February 12, 2018

## Mint Street Memo No. 10: Working Capital Constraints and Exports:

## Evidence from the GST Rollout

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the tenth release under the series ‘Mint Street Memos (MSM) titled “<u>Working Capital Constraints and Exports:</u> <u>Evidence from the GST Rollout</u>”

The paper authored by Saurabh Ghosh, Shekhar Tomar and Sankalp Mathur from Strategic Research Unit analyses the impact of Goods and Services Tax on working capital of firms using exports and firm level data.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
