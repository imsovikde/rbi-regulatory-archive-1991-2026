# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0295 on February 05, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 02, 2018) was

64.0781. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 02, 2018 February 05, 2018** **1 EUR** 80.0335 79.7295 **1 GBP** 91.3369 90.4033 **100 YEN** 58.36 58.22 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
