February 15, 2018 **RBI to conduct 7 day Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on February 15, 2018**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on February 15, 2018, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|Amount (` billion)|(day)|||
|1|250|7|11.00 am to 11.30 am|February 22, 2017 (Thursday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates.

Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
