# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0458 on February 22, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 21, 2018) was

64.8161. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||February 21, 2018|February 22, 2018|

|1 EUR|79.9442|79.8307|
|---|---|---|
|1 GBP|90.6583|90.4137|
|100 YEN|60.17|60.55|
