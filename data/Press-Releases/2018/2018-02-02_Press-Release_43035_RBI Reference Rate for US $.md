# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0781 on February 02, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 01, 2018) was

63.6113. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 01, 2018 February 02, 2018** **1 EUR** 78.9480 80.0335 **1 GBP** 90.1754 91.3369 **100 YEN** 58.09 58.36 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
