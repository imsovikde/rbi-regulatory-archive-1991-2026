## October 23, 2018

## Conversion of Government of India (GoI)’s Securities

The Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has converted a security maturing in 2018-19, having a total face value of `4,600 crore, to a longer tenor security maturing in 2020-21.

The transaction has been conducted on October 22, 2018 at the Financial Benchmarks India Private Limited (FBIL) prices with a scheduled commercial bank.
