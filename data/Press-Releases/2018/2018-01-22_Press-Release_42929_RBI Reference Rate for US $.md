# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.8895 on January 22, 2018. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 19, 2018) was

63.7183. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||

||January 19, 2018|January 22, 2018|

|1 EUR|78.1441|78.1241|
|---|---|---|
