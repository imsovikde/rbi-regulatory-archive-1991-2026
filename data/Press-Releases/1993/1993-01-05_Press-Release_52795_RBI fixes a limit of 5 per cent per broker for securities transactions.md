Review of the investment policies recently formulated by the banks on the advice of RBI and with the approval of the banks' boards indicated that while some of the banks had prescribed "broker exposure" limits, others did not specifically do so. The latter deficiency has been sought to be corrected now by prescribing a uniform "broker exposure" limit of *5* per cent. The change will also broadbase the securities business transacted through brokers and will make the system more open and transparent.
