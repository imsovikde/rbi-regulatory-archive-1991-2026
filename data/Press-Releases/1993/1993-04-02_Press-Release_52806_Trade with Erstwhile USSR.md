## May 1993 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Trade with Erstwhile USSR** **(April 2, 1993)** **The Reserve Bank of India has decided in consultation with Government of India to initially** **settle all replenishment/reimbursement claims of the authorised dealers maintaining accounts of the** **Bank for Foreign Economic Aff aiis of the USSR in respect of certain categories of transactions** **with the erstwhile USSR. The decision was taken to resolve the payment problems of the exporters** **to the erstwhile USSR and other Indian residents in respect of their transactions with the erstwhile** **USSR parties. The decision was facilitated by the agreements on trade and economic cooperation** **reached recently between Government of India and the Government of Russian Federation.**

## The categories of transactions which will be settled by the RBI are:

## (i) Goods actually shipped to the erstwhile

## USSR on or before March 31, 1992;

## (ii) Payments due to the Indian project exporters

**up to March 31, 1992, and duly certified for payments by the employers;** **(iii) Retention money released by the importers in the erstwhile USSR in respect of** **shipments made, services rendered on or before March 31, 1992; and** **(iv) Payments due in respect of other non-trade transactions completed upto March 31, 1992.**
