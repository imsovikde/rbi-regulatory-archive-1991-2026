The **revised** structure of deposit rates will apply only to fresh In a communication to scheduled commercial deposits and on renewals of maturing deposits. banks, Dr. C. Rangarajan, Governor of the Reserve Bank of India announced a reduction in interest rates. In this connection the Governor set out the following measures

## (a) Lending Rates

In view of the perceptible reduction in the inflation rate, the Reserve Bank has decided to reduce, effective from March I, I 993, the lending rate on credit limits of over Rs. 2 lakh by one percentage point from 18.0 per cent (minimum) to 17.0 per cent (minimum). Banks have been asked to reduce rates of interest charged for all borrowers under this category by a minimum of one percentage point over the present rates being charged to such borrowers. Between March 1992 and March 1993 the lending rate has been reduced thrice from 20.0 per cent (minimum) to 17.0 per cent (minimum).

(b) **Interest rates on export credit** To provide further support to the export effort, it has been decided to reduce, effective from March 1, 1993, the interest rates on export credit (rupee) by one percentage point across-the-board. ( c) **Deposit rates** To maintain the viability of banks in the context of the reduction in lending rates, the interest rates on deposits (excluding non-resident deposits) are being reduced, effective from March 1, 1993, for maturity of 46 days to three years
