## Modifications in the Liberalised Exchanae Rate Management System (LERMS) (February 27, 1993)

The Liberalised Exchange Rate Management System (LERMS), which came into effect from l st March 1992, has been modified.

The present system of dual exchange rate has worked reasonably well. The premium over the official rate has remained steady at around 17% except in the current month (i. e. February 1993) probably due to certain expectations regarding the modifications in the exchange rate system. The stability of the market value of the rupee has been achieved despite a widening trade deficit. **The** dual exchange rate system was adopted to enable some essential imports to be made at the official rate of exchange which was naturally lower than the market rate. This was done with the intention of keeping the price of essential imports down. However, the system suffers from certain disadvantages. First of all, the exporters feel that they are al a disadvantage as they have to surrender a part of their earnings at the official rate. This is almost regarded as a tax on exports. Besides, there are problems with transactions passing through Asian Oearing Union. In addition, under the system of advance licences for imports, a part of the import entitlement has to be allowed at the official exchange rate.

Since (i) the rate of inflation has been brought under control to 6.6 per cent presently **as against**

12.9 per cent in the·corresponding period of the previous year (ii) the level of foreign exchange reserves has remained fairly steady and (iii) the budget deficit has been brought down significantly, the Government has decided to switch over to a unified system of exchange rate. The Reserve Bank expects the unified exchange rate system to produce desirable effects

**on exports, inward remittances and imports and thus impart stability to the exchange value of the** **rupee. The modified system will also considerably simplify import-export transactions eliminating the** **need for special arrangements/incentives.** **Under the modified system, all foreign exchange transactions will be put through by** **authorised dealers at market determined rates of exchange. Foreign exchange payments/ receipts** **will be subject to exchange control regulations.** **All foreign exchange receipts are generally required to be surrendered by residents to** **authorised dealers as hitherto but there will be no obligation on the part of authorised dealers to** **surrender any portion of such receipts to the** **Reserve Banlc. The entire receipts can be sold by them for permissible transactions at market** **determined rates of exchange. The system of dual exchange rates (i. e. official rate and the market** **rate) has thus been replaced by a unified system of exchange rates.**

**Reserve Banlc will fix its rates of exchange on the basis of prevailing market rates within a** **margin of 5 per cent on either side of the market rate. Reserve Banlc will buy from authorised** **dealers U. S. dollars for spot deliveries. ll will sell to authorised dealers U.S. dollars for spot** **deliveries for such purposes as are approved by the Government of India. Reserve Banlc will not** **ordinarily purchase spot Pound sterling, Deutsche mark or Japanese yen nor will it ordinarily buy** **any currency forward. Reserve Bank will not sell any currency forward but will be prepared to enter** **into swap transactions under which it will buy**

**U.S. dollars spot and sell forward for two to six months.** **Rese Bank will sell exchange at its selling rate for imports against unutilised Exim scrips/** **REP licences and for 40 per cent of the value of imports against advance/imprest/replenishment** **licences as per instructions to be issued by** **Government of India. The purchase of such scrips/ licences valid for imports from GCA countries at** **a reasonable premium may also be considered.**
**Transactions with countries belonging to the** **Asian Oearing Union will be at rates announced by Reserve Banlc which will be determined on lhe** **basis of prevailing market rates.** **Rupee trade and payment arrangements, wherever they exist will continue for the period** **announced by the Reserve Bank from time to time.** **There is no change in the regulations relating to the accounts of non-residents with banks in** **India including FCNR accounts. Reserve Banlc will continue to provide exchange risk cover in** **respect of FCNR deposits. Foreign currency accounts of residents with authorised dealers in** **India will continue to be operated according to the current regulations.** **Money-changers will transact business relating to foreign currency notes and travellers' cheques** **at market related rates.**
