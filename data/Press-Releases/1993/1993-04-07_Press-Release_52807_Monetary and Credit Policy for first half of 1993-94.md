**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

#### The major objective of the monetary and credit

##### policy during the first half of 1993-94, the

##### Governor said, is to enlarge the availability of

credit. This is sought to be achieved by a reduction in **Cash** Reserve Ratio **(CRR)** and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR), he added. Adequate availability of credit will be critical in converting the weak industrial recovery into a strong revival. It is necessary to ensure that the genuine credit requirements of the productive sectors of the economy including agriculture, large and small scale industries and export sector are fully met. The lendable resources of the banks will **be** enhanced as a consequence of reduction in CRR and SLR. Simultaneously, the limits under **the** general line of credit provided by RBI to NABARD for financing short term seasonal agricultural operations is being increased by an additional Rs. 200 crore over and above the increase of Rs.400 crore announced in January

1993. The changes announced in the credit policy measures are:
* SLR reduced The Statutory Liquidity Ratio to be maintained by scheduled commercial banks up to the level of outstanding demand and time liabilities as on April 3, 1992 would be reduced from 37.75 per cent to 36.75 per cent in four phases. This would augment banks' lendable resources by a little over **Rs.** 2,150 crore.
* CRR Reduced
##### The CRR to be maintained by scheduled

commercial banks (excluding RRBs) would be reduced from 15 per cent to 14 per cent in two phases. This will augment lendable resources of banks by about **Rs.** 2800 crore. Certificates of Deposit issued by banks over the pre-May 1992 limit will, however, now **be** subject to CRR.
