# RBI announces policy for Urban Cooperative Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*July 1993* **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**RBI announces policy for Urban Cooperative** **Banks** **(June 5, 1993)** **The Reserve Bank of India has announced the policy for registration, licensing and other allied** **matters following acceptance of the Report on** **Licensing of new urban cooperative Banks** **(Marathe Committee).**

## The Marathe Committee was appointed by Reserve Bank o.f India in September 1991.

**The thrust of the new policy for organisation of urban cooperative banks will be towards a need** **based and healthy growth of such banks.** **Organisation of new urban cooperative banks will be permitted by adopting a liberal approach on** **the criteria of need, potential and prospects of the areas in which such banks are proposed to be** **organised. The-se banks will have to comply with certain entry point nonns and will also have to** **achieve certain viability nonns in a timeframe of three years.**
