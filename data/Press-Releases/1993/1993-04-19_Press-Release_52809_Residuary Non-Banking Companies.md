# Residuary Non-Banking Companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OP INDIA BULLETIN** *J,u,;e 1993*

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Residuary Non-Banking Companies** **(April 19, 1993)** **Reserve Banlc has announced an amendment to the Residuary Non-Banlcing Companies (ReseIVe** **Banlc) Directions, 1987 today.** **In tenns of the Modified directions, a residuary non-banking company cannot take from any** **depositor/subscriber to any schemes run by the company, with or without his consent, any amount** **towards processing or maintenance charges or any such charges, by whatever name called, for** **meeting its revenue expenditure. However, companies may charge a new depositor a one-time** **initial sum not exceeding Rs. 10 towards cost of application f onn, brochures, service charges, etc.**

**It had come to the notice of the Reserve Banlc of India that some residuary non-banlcing** **companies had devised new deposit schemes under which they took from the depositors** **substantial non-refundable amounts towards processing/maintenance charges. This** **circumvented the provisions of the Reserve Bank directions, the validity of which was upheld by** **the Supreme Court in its judgement dated January** **30, 1992. Against this background, the Reserve** **Banlc amended its directions issued to residuary non-banking companies.**
