## RBI appoints a committee for IRDP

## (October 1, 1993)

The Reserve Bank of India has appointed a committee to review the progress of Integrated Rural Development Programme (IRDP) and recommend suitable measures for its improvement.

The committee to be headed by Shri D. R. Mehta, Deputy Governor will consist of:

l. Shri D. R. Mehta Deputy Governor Reserve Bank of India Bombay.
2. Shri B. N. Yugandhar Special Secretary Ministry of Rural Development Government of India New Delhi.
3. Shri R. V. Gupta Additional Secretary Ministry of Finance (Department of Banking) Government of India New Delhi.
4. Shri P. Kotaiah Chairman National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development Bombay.
5. Shri Rashid Jilani Chairman & Managing Director Punjab National Bank New Delhi.
6. Shri T. K. K. Bhagwat Chairman & Managing Director Indian Overseas Bank Madras.
