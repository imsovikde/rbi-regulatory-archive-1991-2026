## Extent or bank participation in term loans revised

**(August 21, 1993)** **The Reserve Bank of India has allowed commercial banks to sanction term loans** **up to Rs. SO crore for an individual project.** **Simultaneously, it has also allowed the commercial banks to relate the sanction of term** **finance to the quantum of the loan rather than to the cost of the project. Earlier a bank could** **sanction term loans for projects costing up to** **Rs. 10 crore only. The banking system as a whole can now sanction loan up to Rs. 200 crore for** **an individual project. Earlier banking system could sanction total loan not exceeding Rs. 1 SO** **crore or 25 per cent of total term loan assistance, whichever was less.** **However, for projects requiring term finance assistance in excess of Rs. 200 crore, banks should** **continue to participate jointly with all-India financial institutions subject to the share of an** **individual bank not exceeding Rs. SO crore and that of the banking system not exceedilng Rs. 200** **crore.** **1be decision was taken after reviewing the extent of bank participation in financing projects** **against the background of nature of bank resources and expertise developed by them in term** **financing. It was felt that if term loans and working capital were made available to the** **borrowers for smaller projects by the same institution, it would improve the quality of pre-** **sanction credit appraisal and post-sanction supervisibn over the borrowing unit**
