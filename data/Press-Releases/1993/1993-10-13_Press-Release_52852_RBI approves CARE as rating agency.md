# RBI approves CARE as rating agency

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## 1S14 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN December 1993

**RBI approvesCARE as rating agency** **(October 13, 1993)** **The Reserve Bank of India has granted the** **Credit Analysis & Research Ltd. (CARE) the status of an approved agency for the purpose of** **rating issue/s of commercial paper. The minimum**

***December 1993* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**credit rating of CARE specified by the Reserve** **Bank of India for issue of commercial paper shall be PR-2. According to RBI directions governing** **issue of commercial papers, the eligible companies issuing commercial paper are required** **to obtain the specified credit rating from an agency approved by the Reserve Bank. 'Credit** **Rating Inf onnation Services of India Ltd.** **(CRISIL) and Investment Information & Credit rating Agency of India Ltd. (ICRA) are the other** **two approved credit rating agencies.**
