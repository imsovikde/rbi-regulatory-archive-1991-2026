## Investment facility to the non-resident of Indian nationality/origin (NRls)

**(April 23, 1993)** **In view of the recent relaxations brought in the policies relating to Exchange Control and the** **Government's intention to invite more foreip investment to the country, it has since been** **decided to extend the facility of opening NRB/** **FCNR accounts as well as making investments in shares and debentures of Indian companies under** **various investment schemes available to NRis to the foreign born husbands of NRis also.** **Accordingly a spouse (not being a national of** **Pakistan and Bangladesh) of a citizen lndii or of of a person of Indian origin will hencefortli be** **deemeda person to be of Indian origin.**
