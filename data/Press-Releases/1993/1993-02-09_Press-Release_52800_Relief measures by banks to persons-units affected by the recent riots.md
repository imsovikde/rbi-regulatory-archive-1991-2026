# Relief measures by banks to persons/units affected by the recent riots

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## April 1993 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN <,05

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Relief measures by banks to persons/units affected by the recent riots** **(February 9, 1993)** **Reserve Bank has advised commercial banks to grant the foil owing facilities to trade and** **industry which have suffered in the recent riots:**

**i) Additional limits to the extent of 25 per cent of existing working capital limits may be** **sanctioned on the merits of each case. Such increased limits may be granted even if the** **concerned borrowers are temporarily not *in* a position to conform to the second method** **of lending.** **ii) The branch managers may be authorised to extend the period for realisation of bills** **purchased/discounted by one month, on merits of each case.**
**iii) The repayment of instalments of existing term loans may be suitably rescheduled in** **deserving cases by extending the period of repayment by one year.** **iv) The power to reject the borrowers' request for additional facilities mentioned above** **should vest in the higher level of regional manager/zonal manager.**

**Reserve Bank of India has also advised banks not to take margin money for credit limits upto** **Rs. 1 lakh and collateral security/third party guarantee upto Rs. 50,000 granted to the victims** **of the recent riots.**
