Outlining the objective of the credit policy, the Governor said that the package of measures was devised to ensure adequate support by the banking sector to the revival of output. He further added that the credit policy **was** designed to ensure that banks have adequate lendable resources. Specific measures to enhance credit limits of borrowers were also being taken.

Elaborating on the specific measures, Dr. Rangarajan said that rationalisation of various stipulations would give banks greater autonomy in determining the credit requirements of borrowers which would help promote larger and more effective flow of credit. According to the Governor, the lendable resources of banks would also enhance significantly by carrying forward of the medium term policy framework relating to reserve requirements.

The highlights of the policy announcement are:

(i) Reduction in SLR **(ii)** Increase in General Line of Credit to NABARD by Rs. 400 crore
(iii) Introduction of a new pre-shipment export credit facility in foreign currencies (iv) Changes in consortium arrangements, commercial paper and lending norms to give greater autonomy to banks and increased access to borrowers
