# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Task Forces for improving Credit Deposit ratio in Kerala and Pondlcherry** **(June 11, 1993)** **1be Reserve Bank of India has constituted task forces to specifically review:**

**(i) the causes for low credit deposit ratio in** the **State of Kerala/Union Territory of** **Pondicherry taking into account the investment deposit ratio;** **(ii) the perfonnance of banks with a relatively larger share of business in the State/Union** **Territory; and**
## (iii) to make recommendations for a time bound improvement in the credit deposit

## ratio in the State/Union Territory.
