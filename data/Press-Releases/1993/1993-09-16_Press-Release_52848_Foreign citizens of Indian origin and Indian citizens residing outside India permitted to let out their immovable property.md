# Foreign citizens of Indian origin and Indian citizens residing outside India permitted to let out their immovable property

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***November 1993*** RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Foreign citizens of Indian origin and Indian** Subsequently, the RBI in May 1993 granted **citizens residing outside India permitted to let** general permission to foreign nationals of Indian **out their immovable property** origin and non-resident Indians to acquire commercial immovable property. **(September 16, 1993)**

The Reserve Bank of India has granted general permission enabling foreign nationals of Indian origin and non-resident Indians to let out their immovable properties. The rental income or proceeds of any investment of such income, however, is not repalriable outside India at any time in future. Such funds should be credited to the owner's Non-Resident (Ordinary) Rupee accoint maintained with authorised banks in India.

For the purpose of this general permission, a foreign citizen shall be deemed to be of Indian origin -

a) if he held an Indian passport at any time, or
b) he or ***his*** father or grandfather ***was*** a citizen of India by virtue of the Constitution of India or the Citizenship Act, 1955 (57 of 1955) Further, the citizens of Pakistan, Bangladesh,
Afghanistan, Bhutan, Nepal and Sri Lanka shall be deemed to be not of Indian origin.

The permission has been granted by the RBI under Section 29 of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1973 by its notification No. FERA. 155/43-RB dated 16th September 1993.

It may be recalled that foreign citizens of Indian origin, whether resident in India or not, and Indian citizens resident outside India were granted in January 1992, general permission to let out residential properties acquired by them in India which are not required for their immediate residential purpose. This facility was not available for 1the purpose of letting out commercial **immovable property.**
