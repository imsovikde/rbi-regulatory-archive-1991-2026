# Immediate credit for local cheques

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***April 1993* RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

**Immediate credit for local cheques** **(March 4, 1993)** **The customers will be able to ask their banlcs to give immediate credit to all local cheques up** **to Rs. 5,000 from 1st April 1993. The Reserve** **B'ank of India, by a circular issued to all banks on 25th February I 993, has asked banks to offer** **this facility to all customers. Earlier, banlcs gave immediate credit to salary cheques up to Rs. 2,500** **issued by government departments/quasi government authorities and public sector** **undertakings.** **Banlcs will ensure proper conduct of the accounts before giving such immediate credit and** **will recover a charge of Rs. 5 per cheque.** **In case such cheques are later dishonoured, customers will have to pay normal rate of interest** **for the period banks were out of funds. In its circular, the Reserve Bank of India has indicated** **that banlcs might want to introduce pay-in-slips with such a clause. U the customer does not wish** **to receive immediate credit, cheques will be credited on realisation through clearing.**
