# RBI approval to HDFC Ltd. and UTI for starting new banking companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## September 1993 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**RBI approval to HDFC Ltd. and UTI for starting new banking companies** **(July 21, 1993)** **The RBI has today'in given principle' approval to HDFC Ltd. and UTI for starting new** **banking companies, subject to certain terms and conditions. Other requests are under various stages** **of processing.** **The proposed bank by HDFC Ltd., will have an initial paid-up capital of Rs. 300 crore. The** **shares of the bank will be widely held among** **HDFC, the shareholders and employees of HDFC and the public. It will provide full range of ·** **banking services and will be fully computerised from the beginning.** **The bank being set up by UTI will initially have a paid-up capital of Rs. 100 crore. It will,** **after a year, go in for a public issue of Rs. 150 crore. UTI, however, will reduce its holding over** **a period of three years. The proposed bank's objectives are to create a 'financial super-market'** **catering to diverse needs of its clientele.** **Both the banks will set up branches in rural, semi-urban and urban areas, fulfil the** **requirements for priority sector and meet the capital adequacy 8 of per cent of the risk weighted** **assets from the beginning.**

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULlEl'IN *September 1993***

**It may be recalled that the RBI had, on** **January 22, 1993, issued guidelines for the entry of new private sector banks. While the importance** **and role of public sector banks were recognised, there was an increasing recognition of the need** **to introduce greater competition which could lead to higher productivity and efficiency of the** **banking system. The guidelines, therefore, aimed at ensuring that the new entrants would be** **financially viable and technologically strong and that there was no concentration of credit,** **monopolisation of economic power, crossholding with industrial groups, etc.**

**Up to the end of May 1993, the RBI had received 123 requests from public limited** **companies, trusts, associations, firms and individuals. Keeping in view the large number of** **requests, the applications from financial institutions were taken up first, recognising the** **past track record of these institutions.**
