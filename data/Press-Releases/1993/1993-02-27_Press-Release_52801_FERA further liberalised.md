- **Authorised dealers will be permitted to allow reduction in invoice value/remittance of quality** **claim, after satisfying themselves with the documentary evidence produced without any** **restriction whatsoever, in cases where the track record of the exporter is satisfactory.**
*** Authorised dealers will be permitted to open foreign currency accounts in the names of** **overseas buyers to be funded by inward remittances in convertible currency for making** **payments to Indian exporters. Overseas companies executing projects in India will be** **allowed by authorised dealers to open foreign currency accounts with funds received from** **abroad in foreign exchange for meeting local expenses in connection with the project** **executed by them.**
*** Authorised dealers will also be permitted to open non-resident accounts in the names of all** **non-residents, i. e., individuals, firms, corporate bodies irrespective of their nationality without** **the approval of the Reserve Bank of India. Any remittances of funds held in these accounts** **will, however, require approval of the Reserve** **Bank of India.**
* **Authorised dealers are also allowed remittance** **of dividends by FERA companies to their non resident shareholders.**
- **General permission will be granted to non resident investors to sell the shares, acquired** **by them under direct investment schemes, through stock exchange through the same bank** **branch through which the original investment was made.**
