# Banks asked to pay interest on delayed collection of outstation instruments

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULlEl'IN *September 1993***

## Banks asked to pay interest on delayed collection or outstation instruments

**(July 22, 1993)** **Banks will have to pay interest at the minimum lending rate stipulated by the Reserve** **Bank of India from time to time for the period of delay in collection of outstation instruments in** **the case of customers who have obtained credit facilities from banks.**

**Conveying this to the scheduled commercial banks, the RBI explained that the direction had** **become necessary as in spite of repeated instructions that remittances must be speedily** **credited to the account holders, inordinate delay had been noticed in collection of outstation** **instruments. The RBI further explained that since the loss of interest to the customers for the delayed** **remittances was at the rate stipulated by banks for the overdraft, cash credit or loan account, they had** **to be compensated suitably. The RBI has also said that in all other cases, the customers will have**

**to** be **compensated at the rate of 2 per cent above** **the savings bank rate, as specified by RBI's instructions given in May 1992.**
