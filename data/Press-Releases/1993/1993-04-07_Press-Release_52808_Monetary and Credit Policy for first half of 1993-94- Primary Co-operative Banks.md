# Monetary and Credit Policy for first half of 1993-94: Primary Co-operative Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*May 1993* **RBSERVB BANK OF INDIA BULI.BllN** *1SS*

**Monetary and Credit Policy for first half of** **1993-94: Primary Co-operative Banks** **(April 7, 1993)** **Conuent upon the announcement of the monetary policy for the first half of 1993-94 for** **Scheduled Commercial Banks, the following changes are made in respect of primary** **cooperative banks effective April 8, 1993:-**

**(i) Non-Resident External Deposit Rates** **Tenn Deposit Rates for NRE Accounts for maturity of 46 days to 3 years and above are** **reduced by one percentage point from the existing** **'not e,:ceeding 13.0% per annum' ro 'not exceeding 12.0% per annum'.** **(ii) Advances against shares and debentures** **The minimum margins for loans against shares and debentures are reduced from 75 per cent to** **50 per cent and the ceiling for such bankwise credit as of October 9, 1991 removed.** **(iii) Selective Credit Control** **The minimum margins on bank advances against paddy/rice, wheat pulses,** **'otherfoodgrains', oilseeds and vegetable oils arc reduced across-the board by 15 per centage points** **and level of credit ceilings raised by 15 percentage points.**
