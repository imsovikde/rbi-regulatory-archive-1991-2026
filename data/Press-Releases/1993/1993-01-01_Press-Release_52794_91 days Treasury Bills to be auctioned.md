# 91 days Treasury Bills to be auctioned

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## February 1993 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BUl.LETIN

**91 days Treasury Bills to be auctioned** **(January 1, 1993)** **With a view to developing the treasury bills market and as a part of the move towards** **market- related rates of interest on Government securities, the Government of India has announced** **a scheme of auction of 91 days treasury bills.** **The auction of 91 days treasury bills will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India at** **Bombay. The date of the auction and the notified amount will be announced by the RBI from time** **to time.** **The bills would be issued in the form of a promissory note/credit to subsidiary general ledger** **account/scrip form and may be purchased by any person, firm, company, corporate body and** **institution in India. State governments and provident/gratuity funds are nol, however, eligible** **to invest in the 91 days treasury bills sold through auction.**

**The bills will be issued at a discount for a minimum subscription of Rs. 25,000 and in** **multiples thereof. There will be no limit on individual bids subject to the notified amount. The** **cut-off rate of discount and the corresponding issue price would be determined at each auction.** **Successful bids up to the minimum rate of discount detennined at the auction will be** **accepted.** **The 91 days treasury bills purchased in auctions will be an eligible security for purposes**

of Statutory Liquidity Ratio under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

The Reserve Bank of India will not provide an automatic rediscounting facility for 91 days treasury bills purchased in auctions. The Reserve Bank may from time to time at its discretion buy or sell the auctioned treasury bills.

The existing schemes of 91 days treasury bills, available on tap, carrying a fixed rate of discount at 4.6 per cent per annum, and the auction of 364 days treasury bills will, however, be continued.
