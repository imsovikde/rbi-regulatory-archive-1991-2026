# Streamlining of sanctioning procedure for loans and advances to export and advances to export sector

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

*January 1994* RESBRVB BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**Streamlining of sanctioning procedure for loans and advances to export and advances to** **export sector** **(December 8, 1993)** **Reserve Bank has advised the scheduled commercial banks to streamline the sanctioning** **procedure for loans and advances to export sector so that the credit limits are sanctioned to** **exponers within the prescribed time limit of 45 days for sanction of fresh/enhanced export credit**

**limit,;,** 30 **days for renewal of limits and** 15 **days** **for ad hoc limits. Banks have to ensure proper delegation of powers to various functionaries** **which should be reviewed periodically. It has** **also been indicated that** the **overseas branches** **should have higher powers to take care of the exporters· needs. Banks will be required to** **introduce a suitable mechanism to deal with exporters' grievances with reference to timely** **sanction of limits and adequacy of the credit. For speedy disposal of their applications, it will be** **necessary for the exporters to furnish the requisite financialdata/operating statements, etc.,** **without any deficiencies. For this purpose banks will assist the various expon promotion councils** **to educate the borrowers by means of seminars and workshops.**

**Reserve Bank has advised the banks that the internal audit and inspection teams of banks** **should specifically comment on the timely sanction of export credit limits. The boards of** **the banks have also been directed to review at quarterly intervals the disposal of applications** **for export credit.**
