## January 11, 2024

## Exchange / Deposit facility of ₹2000 banknotes at Mumbai Regional Office, RBI

## – Non-availability on Friday, January 12, 2024

The facility of exchange / deposit of ₹2000 banknotes at Mumbai Regional Office will not be available on Friday, January 12, 2024, due to operational reasons. The facility will resume on Monday, January 15, 2024, which is the next working day.
