## September 30, 2024

## Sources of Variation in India’s Foreign Exchange Reserves during April-June 2024

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released the <u>balance of payments (BoP) data</u> <u>for the first quarter (Q1), i.e., April-June of 2024-25</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during April-June 2024 are detailed below in Table 1.

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 5.2 billion during April-June 2024 as compared with an accretion of US$ 24.4 billion during April-June 2023. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including valuation effects) increased by US$ 5.6 billion during April-June 2024-25 as compared with an increase of US$ 16.6 billion in the corresponding period of the preceding year (<u>Table 2</u>).

The valuation gain, primarily reflecting the rise in gold price, amounted to US$ 0.4 billion during April-June 2024 as against a valuation loss of US$ 7.8 billion during April-June 2023.
