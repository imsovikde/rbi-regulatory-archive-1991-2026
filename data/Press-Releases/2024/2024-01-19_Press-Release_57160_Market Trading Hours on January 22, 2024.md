January 19, 2024 **Market Trading Hours on January 22, 2024** In view of the half day closing on January 22, 2024 (Monday) announced by the Government of India, the trading hours for the various markets regulated by the Reserve Bank of India will be as under:

||Timings on January|
|---|---|
||22, 2024|
|Call/notice/term money|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|Market repo in Government Securities|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|Tri-party repo in Government Securities|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|Commercial paper and Certificates of Deposit|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|

## Market

|Repo in Corporate Bonds|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|---|---|
|Government Securities (Central Government Securities, State Government Securities and Treasury Bills)|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|Foreign Currency (FCY)/Indian Rupee (INR) Trades including Forex Derivatives*|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|
|Rupee Interest Rate Derivatives*|2:30 PM to 5:00 PM|

*Other than those traded on recognised stock exchanges. Settlement of the auction of Government of India dated securities conducted on January 19, 2024 will take place on January 22, 2024 after the commencement of market trading hours at 2:30 PM. The market trading hours will be restored to the hours indicated in the <u>press release</u> <u>dated December 7, 2022</u> with effect from January 23, 2024.
