## August 05, 2024

## Financial Literacy Ideathon-Winners

The Reserve Bank of India had announced a <u>Financial Literacy Ideathon</u> for post graduate students on the topic “Money Matters for Young Adults: Rethinking Outreach Strategies” as part of its Financial Literacy Week 2024 campaign.

2. A total of 256 entries were received. After a rigorous screening process, we are pleased to announce the following winners:

|st 1 Position:|Shri Gautam Singh|R.D.S., Muzaffarpur, B.R. Ambedkar Bihar University|
|---|---|---|
|nd 2 Position:|Ms. Siddhi Bala Acharya|Institute of Distance and Open Learning, University of Mumbai|
|rd 3 Position:|Shri Ishan Dixit|Indian Institute of Technology, Roorkee|

3. The Reserve Bank extends its congratulations to the winners and also compliments all the other participants for their efforts.
