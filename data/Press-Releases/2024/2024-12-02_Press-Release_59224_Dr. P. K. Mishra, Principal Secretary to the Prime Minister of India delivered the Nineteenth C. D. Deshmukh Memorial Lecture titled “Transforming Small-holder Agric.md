## December 02, 2024

## Dr. P. K. Mishra, Principal Secretary to the Prime Minister of India

## delivered the Nineteenth C. D. Deshmukh Memorial Lecture titled

## “Transforming Small-holder Agriculture in India in the 21

**st** **Century: Challenges and Strategies”**

The Reserve Bank of India hosted the Nineteenth C.D. Deshmukh Memorial Lecture on November 28, 2024 in Mumbai. <u>The lecture</u> was delivered by Dr. P. K. Mishra, Principal Secretary to the Prime Minister of India.

The text of his lecture “<u>Transforming Small-holder Agriculture in India in the 21</u> <u>st</u> <u>Century: Challenges and Strategies</u>” is placed at <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>.
