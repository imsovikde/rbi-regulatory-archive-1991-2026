## March 22, 2024

## Final redemption under Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Scheme-Redemption Price

**for final redemption of SGB 2016 Series II due on March 28, 2024 (March 29, 2024 being a holiday)**

In terms of <u>GOI Notification F.No.4(19) - W&M/2014 dated March 04, 2016</u> (SGB 2016 Series II-Issue date March 29, 2016) on Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme, the Gold Bond shall be repayable on the expiration of eight years from the date of the issue of the Gold Bonds. Accordingly, the final redemption date of the above tranche shall be March 28, 2024 (March 29, 2024 being a holiday).

2. Further, the redemption price of SGB shall be based on the simple average of closing price of gold of 999 purity of the week (Monday-Friday), preceding the date of redemption, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). Accordingly, the redemption price for the final redemption due on March 28, 2024 (March 29, 2024 being a holiday) shall be ₹6601/- (Rupees Six thousand Six hundred and One only) per unit of SGB based on the simple average of closing price of gold for the week March 18-22, 2024.
