## March 28, 2024

## India’s International Investment Position (IIP), December 2023

Today, the Reserve Bank released data relating to <u>India’s International Investment</u> <u>Position</u> for <u>end-December 2023</u> [1].

## Key Features of India’s IIP in end-December 2023:

- Net claims of non-residents on India declined by US$ 12.2 billion during October-December 2023 to US$ 370.4 billion as at end-December 2023.
- The decline in net claims was due to the higher rise in Indian residents’ overseas financial assets (US$ 40.7 billion) as compared to the increase in foreign-owned assets in India (US$ 28.5 billion) (<u>Table 1</u>).
- Reserve assets recorded a significant rise (US$ 34.8 billion) and accounted for over 85 per cent of increase in foreign assets of Indian residents during Q3:2023-24.
- The rise in India’s foreign liabilities was attributed to higher inward direct investments, portfolio investments and loans taken by resident entities; even as trade credits recorded a marginal decline.
- Reserve assets accounted for 63.9 per cent of India’s international financial assets (<u>Table 2</u>).
- The share of debt liabilities in total external liabilities remained at its level a quarter ago (50.4 per cent) (<u>Table 3</u>).
- The ratio of India’s international financial assets to international financial liabilities improved to 72.4 per cent in December 2023 from 70.9 per cent a quarter ago.
