## March 13, 2024

## Conversion/Switch of Government of India Dated Securities

As per the <u>Issuance Calendar for Marketable Dated Securities for H2: FY 2023-24</u> <u>issued on September 26, 2023</u>, auction for switches of government dated securities is scheduled to be conducted on the third Monday of every month. As the budgetary provision for switches for the fiscal year 2023-24 has since been met, it has been decided, in consultation with the Government of India, not to conduct the switch auction scheduled to be held in March 2024.
