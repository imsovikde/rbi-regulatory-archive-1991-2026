## May 03, 2024

## Buyback of Government of India Dated Securities

The Government of India has announced the buyback of its Securities through auction for an aggregate amount of ₹40,000 crore (face value). The following securities will be offered for buyback:

|Sr. No.|Security|Date of Maturity|
|---|---|---|
|1|6.18% GS 2024|November 4, 2024|
|2|9.15% GS 2024|November 14, 2024|
|3 of ₹40,000 crore. • • •|6.89% GS 2025 There is no notified amount for the individual securities within the aggregate ceiling The offers for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on May 09, 2024 (Thursday) between 10:30 a.m. and 11:30 a.m. The result of the auction will be announced on the same day and settlement will take place on May 10, 2024 (Friday). Government of India reserves the right to: Decide on the quantum of buyback of individual securities. Accept more or less than the notified amount of ₹40,000 crore. Accept or reject any or all of the offers, either wholly or partially, without assigning any reason thereof.|January 16, 2025 Auction for securities will be conducted using multiple price method.|
