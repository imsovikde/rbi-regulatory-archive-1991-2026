## March 28, 2024

## Exchange / Deposit facility of ₹2000 banknotes at RBI – Non-availability on

## Monday, April 01, 2024

The facility of exchange / deposit of ₹2000 banknotes will not be available on Monday, April 01, 2024 at the 19 Issue Offices¹ of the Reserve Bank of India due to operations associated with the Annual Closing of Accounts. The facility will resume on Tuesday, April 02, 2024.
