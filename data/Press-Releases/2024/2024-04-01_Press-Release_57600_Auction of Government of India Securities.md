April 01, 2024 **Auction of Government of India Securities** On a review of the prevailing and evolving market conditions, Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has decided that all securities under the market borrowing programme of Government of India shall, henceforth, be auctioned using multiple price auction method. The above arrangement shall be in place till further review.
