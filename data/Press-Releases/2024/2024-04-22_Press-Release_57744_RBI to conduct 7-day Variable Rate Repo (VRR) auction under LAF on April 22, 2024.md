## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction under LAF on April 22, 2024

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to conduct a Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction on April 22, 2024, Monday, as under:

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(₹ crore)|(day)||April 29, 2024|
|1|50,000|7|1:00 PM to 1:30 PM|(Monday)|

2. The operational guidelines for the auction will be same as given in Reserve Bank’s <u>Press Release 2021-2022/1572 dated January 20, 2022</u>.
