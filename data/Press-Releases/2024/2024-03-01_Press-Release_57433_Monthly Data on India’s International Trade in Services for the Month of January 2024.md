## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of January 2024

The value of exports and imports of services during January 2024 is given in the following table.

||International Trade in Services|(US$ Million)|
|---|---|---|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|October – 2023|28,025 (10.8)|13,459 (-0.4)|
|November – 2023|28,085 (4.3)|13,677 (-11.1)|
|December – 2023|31,606 (1.3)|15,627 (-1.2)|
|January – 2024|31,023 (10.8)|14,850 (0.2)|
