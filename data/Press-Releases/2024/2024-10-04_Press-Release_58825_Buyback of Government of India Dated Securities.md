## October 04, 2024

## Buyback of Government of India Dated Securities

The Government of India has announced the buyback of its Securities through auction for an aggregate amount of ₹25,000 crore (face value). The security-wise details of the buyback are as under:

||S.No.|Security|Date of Maturity|

Auction for securities will be conducted using multiple price method.
