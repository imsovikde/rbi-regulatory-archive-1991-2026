October 07, 2024 **Shri M. Rajeshwar Rao re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor** The Central Government has re-appointed Shri M. Rajeshwar Rao as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a period of one year with effect from October 09, 2024, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
