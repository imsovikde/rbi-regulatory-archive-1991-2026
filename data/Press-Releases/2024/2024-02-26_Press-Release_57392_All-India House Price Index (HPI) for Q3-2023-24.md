## February 26, 2024

## All-India House Price Index (HPI) for Q3:2023-24

Today, the Reserve Bank released its quarterly house price index (HPI) 1 (base: 2010- 11=100) for Q3:2023-24, based on transaction-level data received from the registration authorities in ten major cities². Time series data on all-India and city-wise HPIs are available at the Bank’s database on Indian economy (DBIE) portal (<u>[https://cimsdbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/#/dbie/home](https://cimsdbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/#/dbie/home)</u>> Statistics > Real Sector > Prices & Wages).
