The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) system on **May 14, 2024 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to Ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions, subject to a maximum limit of One per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the ‘<u>Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>’.
