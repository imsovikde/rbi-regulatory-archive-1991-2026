## September 20, 2024

## Scheme for writing books originally in Hindi on Economics/Banking/Financial subjects

## Declaration of Result for the year 2022-23

Reserve Bank of India had invited entries under the captioned scheme vide <u>press release</u> <u>dated 29 December 2023</u>. On evaluation of the entries received, it has been decided to award Dr. Bidyanand Choudhary, Assistant Professor, Department of Economics, Jagannath Nagar College, Dhurwa, Ranchi-4, (Ranchi University), Jharkhand under this scheme for his book “झारखंड क अथर्व्यवस्था".
