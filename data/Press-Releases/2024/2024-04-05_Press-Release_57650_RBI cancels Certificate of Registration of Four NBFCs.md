## April 05, 2024

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of Four NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the Certificate of Registration of the following companies.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Registered Office Address|CoR No.|CoR Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Kundles Motor Finance Private Limited|C-41, Kumar Kuter, Near Pancham Diagnosis Centre, Prem Nagar, Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh - 243001|B-12.00454|June 09, 2016|March 12, 2024|
|2|Nithya Finance Limited|"Nithiya Nilayam", 132, Palani Road, S.V. Mills Road, Udumalpet, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu - 642128|B-07.00639|October 14, 2003|March 14, 2024|
|3|Bhatia Hire Purchase Pvt Ltd|V-378, Ganpati Enclave, Bathinda, Punjab - 151001|B-06.00283|June 28, 2000|March 19, 2024|
|4|Jiwanjyoti Deposits and Advances Limited|Jiwan Jyoti Shopping B-06.00490 Complex, Near Petrol Pump, Mehre-Barsar, Teh. Barsar, Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh – 174313||August 29, 2018|March 21, 2024|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
