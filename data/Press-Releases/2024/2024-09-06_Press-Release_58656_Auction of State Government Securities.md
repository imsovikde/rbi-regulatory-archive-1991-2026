The Government Stock up to Ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions, subject to a maximum limit of One per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the ‘<u>Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding</u> <u>Facility</u>’.
