## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹39,000 crore on October 04, 2024

Government of India has announced the sale (issue / re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auctions to be held on **October 04, 2024**.

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **October 04, 2024 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
