September 20, 2024 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on September 20, 2024** In the underwriting auctions conducted on September 20, 2024, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.10% GS 2034|20,000|10,017|9,983|20,000|0.04|
|7.34% GS 2064|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.05|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on September 20, 2024.
