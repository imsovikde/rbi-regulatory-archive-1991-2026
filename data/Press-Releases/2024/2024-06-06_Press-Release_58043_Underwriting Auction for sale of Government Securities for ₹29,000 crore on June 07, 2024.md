## June 6, 2024

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹29,000 crore on June 07, 2024

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auctions to be held on June 07, 2024.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore)

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **June 07, 2024 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **10:30 A.M.** and **11:00 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
