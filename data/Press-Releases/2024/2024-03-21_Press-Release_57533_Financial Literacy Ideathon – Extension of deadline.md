## March 21, 2024

## Financial Literacy Ideathon – Extension of deadline

RBI had announced a <u>Financial Literacy Ideathon</u> for post graduate students on February 26, 2024 with a timeline of March 20, 2024 for submission of entries. In view of requests received for an extension of the timeline, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is pleased to announce the extension of last date of submission, to April 15, 2024.

2. Additionally, to streamline the submission process and maintain consistency, participants are advised that:
i) The submissions should be in typed form and can be submitted online or by post. ii) Each entry must be accompanied by the respective college/institution's ID card.
3. The Financial Literacy Ideathon underscores RBI's commitment to fostering financial literacy and innovation among the youth. By providing an extended timeline we aim to encourage more students to participate actively and contribute their creative solutions to address financial literacy challenges.
4. For further information regarding the event, please refer to the <u>brochure</u>.
