## March 11, 2024

## RBI releases Annual Report of Ombudsman Scheme, 2022-23

The Reserve Bank of India released the <u>Annual Report of the Ombudsman Scheme for</u> <u>the period April 1, 2022 to March 31, 2023</u>. This is the first full-year report after the launch of <u>Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS), 2021</u> in November 2021. The Annual Report covers the activities under the RB-IOS, 2021 as well as major developments during the year in consumer education and protection and the way forward.
