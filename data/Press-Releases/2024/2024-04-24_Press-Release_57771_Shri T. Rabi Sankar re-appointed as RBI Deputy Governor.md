April 24, 2024 **Shri T. Rabi Sankar re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor** The Central Government has re-appointed Shri T. Rabi Sankar as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a period of one year with effect from May 03, 2024, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
