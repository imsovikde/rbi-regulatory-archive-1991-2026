April 12, 2024 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on April 12, 2024** In the underwriting auctions conducted on April 12, 2024 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut- off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.32% GS 2030|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.41|
|New GS 2039|10,000|5,019|4,981|10,000|0.43|
|7.30% GS 2053|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|0.67|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on April 12, 2024.
