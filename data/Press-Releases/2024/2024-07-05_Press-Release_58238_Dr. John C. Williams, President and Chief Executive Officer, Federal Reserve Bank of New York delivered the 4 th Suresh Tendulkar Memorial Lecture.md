## July 05, 2024

## Dr. John C. Williams, President and Chief Executive Officer, Federal Reserve

## Bank of New York delivered the 4

**th** **Suresh Tendulkar Memorial Lecture**

The Reserve Bank of India hosted the 4 th Suresh Tendulkar Memorial Lecture on July 5, 2024 in Mumbai. <u>The lecture</u> was delivered by Dr. John C. Williams, President and Chief Executive Officer, Federal Reserve Bank of New York.

The text of his lecture titled “<u>Managing the Known Unknowns</u>” is available on the RBI website.
