## December 18, 2024

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of Four NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the Certificate of Registration of the following companies.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Registered Office Address|CoR No.|CoR Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Marwah Finance Pvt Ltd|B-512, 5th Floor, Wall Fort Ozone Fafad, Raipur, Chhattisgarh - 492001|B- 03.00189|October 31, 2017|November 11, 2024|
|2|PVP Capital Limited|th No. 2, 9 Floor, KRM Centre, Harington Road, Chetpet, Chennai, Tamil Nadu – 600031|B- 07.00406|May 23, 2002|November 21, 2024|
|3|Rain Bow Finance India Ltd|RMZ Millenia Business Park, Campus 1A, No. 143, Dr. M.G.R. Road, (North Veeranam Salai), Perungudi, Sholinganallur, Kanchipuram, Saidapet, Chennai, Tamil Nadu – 600096|07.00157|March 11, 1998|November 21, 2024|
|4|Ram Alloy Castings Private Limited|KB-33, 2nd Floor, Sector- 14, Kaushambi, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh - 201010|B- 12.00281|December 18, 2000|November 26, 2024|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
