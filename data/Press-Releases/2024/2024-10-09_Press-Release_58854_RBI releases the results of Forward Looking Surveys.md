## October 09, 2024

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i) <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – September 2024</u> ii) <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – September 2024</u>
iii) <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q1:2024-25</u> iv) <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q2:2024-25</u> <u>th</u>

v) <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – Round¹ 90</u> vi) <u>Bank Lending Survey for Q2:2024-25</u>
vii) <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q2:2024-25</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Ajit Prasad

**Press Release: 2024-2025/1256** Deputy General Manager (Communications)

The survey results reflect the respondents’ views, which are not necessarily shared by the Reserve Bank. Results of the previous survey round were released on the Bank’s website on <u>August 8, 2024</u>.
