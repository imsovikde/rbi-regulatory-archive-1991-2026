# Government of Kerala announces the auction of Kerala State Government Loan 2016

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> April 24, 2006

Government of Kerala announces the auction of Kerala State Government Loan 2016

The State Government of Kerala have announced the sale of a 10 year Kerala State Development Loan for a notified amount of Rs.300 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on April 27, 2006 (Thursday).

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, April 27, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by</u>

<u>12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on April 27,2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 28, 2006 (Friday) at Mumbai and Thiruvananthapuram. The stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.
Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1366
