## March 22, 2006

Annual Policy Statement for the year 2006-07 on April 18, 2006

Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will present the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2006-07 on Tuesday, April 18, 2006. He will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks on that date at 11.00 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
