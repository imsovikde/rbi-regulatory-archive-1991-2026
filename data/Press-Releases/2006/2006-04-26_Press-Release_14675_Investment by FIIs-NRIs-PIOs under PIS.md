## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on April 26, 2006 has notified that further purchases of equity shares of Vijaya Bank, on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed without prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that foreign share holdings in Vijaya Bank, has gone down below the limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital.
