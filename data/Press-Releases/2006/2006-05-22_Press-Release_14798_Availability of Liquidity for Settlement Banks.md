## Availability of Liquidity for Settlement Banks

In light of the developments in the stock exchanges, the RBI has been in touch with major settlement banks and both the major stock exchanges to ensure that the payment obligations on the exchanges are met smoothly. Banks can get in touch with the Reserve Bank for any liquidity assistance at Tel.No.22630699 (Shri V.K.Sharma, Executive Director).
