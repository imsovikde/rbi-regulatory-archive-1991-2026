## January 13, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.3500 on January 13, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 12, 2006) were Rs.44.1500 and Rs.53.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
