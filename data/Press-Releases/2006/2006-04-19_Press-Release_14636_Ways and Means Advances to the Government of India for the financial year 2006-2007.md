# Ways and Means Advances to the Government of India for the financial year 2006-2007

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> April 19, 2006 Ways and Means Advances to the Government of India for the financial year 2006-2007

It has been decided in consultation with the Government of India that the following arrangements will be in force for the fiscal year 2006-07.

The WMA limits for the fiscal 2006-07 would be fixed on a quarterly basis as under:

|First Quarter: April – June|Rs. 20,000 crore|
|---|---|
|Second Quarter: July-September|Rs.10,000 crore|
|Third Quarter: October- December|Rs.6,000 crore|
|Fourth Quarter: January-March|Rs.6,000 crore|

The Reserve Bank would retain the flexibility to revise the limits any time in consultation with the Government of India taking into consideration the transitional issues and prevailing circumstances.

The interest rate on WMA will be at Repo Rate and that on Overdraft will be at Repo Rate plus two per cent.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1343
