## May 5, 2006

Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

The Reserve Bank of India on May 5, 2006 has notified that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) with Jagran Prakashan Ltd., has reached the limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that no further purchases of shares of Jagran Prakashan Ltd., should, therefore, be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs/NRIs and PIOs without its prior approval.
