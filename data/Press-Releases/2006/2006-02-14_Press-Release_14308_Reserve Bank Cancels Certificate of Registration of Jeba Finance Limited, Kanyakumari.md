Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of Jeba Finance Limited, Kanyakumari

The Reserve Bank of India, has on January 31, 2006 cancelled the certificate of registration granted to Jeba Finance Limited, having its registered office at 7/60 C, Main Road, Anjugramam, Kanyakumari-629401 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation of registration certificate, Jeba Finance Limited, cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of non-banking financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
