## July 21, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.8300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.59.1700 on July 21, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 20, 2006) were Rs.46.8400 and Rs.59.0000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
