## 55 August 2, 2006

Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : Television Eighteen Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on August 2, 2006 has notified that further purchases of equity shares of Television Eighteen Ltd., on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed only with prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that foreign share holdings in Television Eighteen Ltd., have reached the caution the limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
