## March 28, 2006

Monitoring of Overall Ceiling-Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on March 28, 2006 that the holdings of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the paid up capital of undernoted companies have gone below the caution limit. It will, therefore, be in order for FIIs to purchase the shares of these companies in the secondary market under the portfolio investment scheme. The investments will be subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines in this regard.

1. Reliance Capital Venture Ltd.
2. Reliance Communication Venture Ltd.
3. Reliance Energy Venture Ltd.
4. Reliance Natural Resources Ltd.
