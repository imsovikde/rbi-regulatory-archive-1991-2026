## June 21, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at Re. 0.03 (three paise) and Re 0.08 (eight paise) per Rupees 100 in the underwriting for the auctions of “7.37% Government Stock 2014" and “7.94% Government Stock 2021” respectively, to be held on June 22, 2006.
