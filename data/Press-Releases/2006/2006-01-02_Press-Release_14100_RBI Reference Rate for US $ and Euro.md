## January 02, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.2800 on January 02, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 30, 2005) were Rs.45.0700 and Rs.53.5500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 30, 2005|77.8967|38.44|
|January 02, 2006|77.5153|38.32|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
