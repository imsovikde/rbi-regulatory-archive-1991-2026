## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on February 22, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Jagran Prakashan Limited, under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India without obtaining its prior approval. The Reserve Bank has stated that Jagran Prakashan Limited has reached the caution limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
