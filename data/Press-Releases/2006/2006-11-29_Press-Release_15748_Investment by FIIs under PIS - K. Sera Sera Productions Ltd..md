` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> November 29, 2006

Investment by FIIs under PIS : K. Sera Sera Productions Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on November 29, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of K. Sera Sera Productions Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary and secondary markets in India without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that K. Sera Sera Productions Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release : 2006-2007/736
