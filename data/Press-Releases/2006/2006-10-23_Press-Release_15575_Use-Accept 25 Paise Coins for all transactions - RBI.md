# Use/Accept 25 Paise Coins for all transactions : RBI

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 www.rbi.org.in Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> October 23, 2006 Use/Accept 25 Paise Coins for all transactions : RBI

The Reserve Bank of India continues to receive complaints that traders, shopkeepers and the general public in certain parts of the country are reluctant to accept 25 paise coins. It is clarified and reiterated that 25 paise coins have not been withdrawn from circulation, are legal tender and may be validly used/accepted for all transactions.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release : 2006-2007/562
