## Investment by FIIS/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on November 29, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of the following compaies, through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 100 per cent of its paid up capital, as these companies have passed resolutions to this effect at their Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.

1. AZTEC Software and Technology Services Ltd.
2. Sujana Universal Industries Ltd.
