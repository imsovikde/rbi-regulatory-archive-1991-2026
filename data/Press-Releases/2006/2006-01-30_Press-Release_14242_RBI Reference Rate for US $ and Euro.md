## January 30, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.1400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4300 on January 30, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 27, 2006) were Rs.44.1500 and Rs.53.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 27, 2006|78.5738|37.92|
|January 30, 2006|78.0329|37.61|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
