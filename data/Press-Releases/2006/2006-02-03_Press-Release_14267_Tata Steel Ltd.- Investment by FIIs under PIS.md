## February 3, 2006

Tata Steel Ltd.: Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on February 3, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Tata Steel Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Tata Steel Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
