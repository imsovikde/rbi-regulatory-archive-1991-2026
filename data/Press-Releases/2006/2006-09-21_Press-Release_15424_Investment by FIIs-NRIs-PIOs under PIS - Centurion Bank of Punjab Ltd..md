555 September 21, 2006

Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on September 21, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Porfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through stock exchange/s in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., has reached the limit of 74 per cent of its paid up capital.

P.V.Sadanandan Manager
