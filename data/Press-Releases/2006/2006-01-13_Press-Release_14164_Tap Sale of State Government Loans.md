## January 13, 2006

## Tap Sale of State Government Loans

Eight State Governments, namely, Arunachal Pradesh, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura have offered to sell 7.61 per cent State Development Loan, 2016 having maturity of ten years aggregating around Rs.700 crore on January 16, 2006 by way of tap sale under the approved market borrowing programme. The sale will be held at Mumbai for all the eight states as also at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after close of banking hours on or after January 16, 2006 having regard to the subscription received. The payment by the successful applicants will be made on January 18, 2006 Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Offices of the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the aforesaid office during banking hours while the sale is open.
