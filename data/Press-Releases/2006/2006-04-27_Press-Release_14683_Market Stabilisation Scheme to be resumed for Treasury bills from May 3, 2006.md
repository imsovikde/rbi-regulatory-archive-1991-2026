## April 27, 2006

Market Stabilisation Scheme to be resumed for Treasury bills from May 3, 2006

On a review of the liquidity conditions, it has been decided to resume Treasury bills auctions under the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS) with effect from May 3, 2006. The issuance of Treasury bills under MSS will be undertaken by increasing the notified amount for regular weekly auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills by Rs.1,500 crore and those for the fortnightly 182-day and 364-day Treasury bills auctions by Rs.1,000 crore each. These auctions will continue till further notice.

The auctions would be conducted in accordance with the annual ceiling for 2006-07 fixed at Rs.70,000 crore by the Government of India and the Reserve Bank.

The Reserve Bank would retain the flexibility of reviewing the auctions under the MSS from time to time, in response to evolving circumstances.
