## February 24, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.4500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9900 on February 24, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 23, 2006) were Rs.44.5600 and Rs.53.0700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|February 23, 2006|77.7661|37.92|
|February 24, 2006|77.8786|38.10|
