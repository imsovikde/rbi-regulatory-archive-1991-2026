## January 6, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auctions of "9.39" per cent Government Stock, 2011" and "7.40 per cent Government Stock, 2035" to be held on January 9, 2006 at Re.0.0250 (two point five zero paise) and Re.0.0489 (four point eight nine paise) per Rupees 100 respective.
