## May 19, 2006

RBI expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

The Reserve Bank of India has today further expanded the coverage of its "Database on Indian Economy" to include, among other things, static reports on foreign exchange reserves, foreign exchange spot rates and certain other aspects of financial markets as well as commercial banking.

The additional static reports released on the Database on Indian Economy today are:

(a) Foreign Exchange Reserves – Year End, (b) Foreign Exchange Spot Rate (Daily) - FEDAI, (c) Turnover in Government Securities Market (Face Value), (d) Government of India Treasury Bills Outstanding, (e) Average daily turnover in Call Money Market, (f) Commercial Paper Issued by Companies (At face value), (g) Abridged Balance Sheet of Scheduled Commercial Banks Bank Group wise and (h) Financial Ratios of Selected Public Limited Companies NIC-1998. The additional data query reports now available on the Database on Indian Economy are:
(a) Directory of branches of Scheduled Commercial Banks with Authorised Dealer information, (b) Open Market Operation Sales/Purchases by RBI, (c) Ownership pattern of deposits of Scheduled Commercial Banks –State-wise, (d) Ownership Pattern of deposits in selected metropolitan cities, (e) Composition of deposits by type of deposits, bank group-wise and (f) ownership of deposits with Scheduled Commercial Banks-bank group-wise, sector-wise. With the release of these data series, the Database on Indian Economy would now have 139 static reports (subject and frequency wise), 67 subject wise and 53 frequency wise data query templates covering various sectors of the Indian economy. The Reserve Bank of India's Database on Indian Economy is available on its website (www.rbi.org.in) under the Database Section. This can also be directly accessed through URL [https://cdbmsi.reservebank.org.in/](https://cdbmsi.reservebank.org.in/). The home page of the Database on Indian Economy lists the data series available on it. The coverage of the Database is continuously being expanded on the basis of the feedback received from the users of the database. The present expansion is the tenth such expansion release. Users are requested to provide their feedback through a feedback link on the site so as to enable further improvements in the coverage of data series in the system.
