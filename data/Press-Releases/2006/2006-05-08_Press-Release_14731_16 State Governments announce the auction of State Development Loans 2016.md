# 16 State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans  2016

` PRESS RELEASE

16 State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2016 The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.5598.995 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on May 11, 2006 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, May 11, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on May 11, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on May 12, 2006 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on May 10, 2006 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
