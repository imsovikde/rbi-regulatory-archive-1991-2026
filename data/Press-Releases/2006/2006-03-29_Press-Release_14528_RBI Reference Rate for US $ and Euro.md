## March 29, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.6900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6300 on March 29, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 28, 2006) were Rs.44.6600 and Rs.53.6400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|March 28, 2006|78.0032|38.19|
|March 29, 2006|77.9058|37.91|
