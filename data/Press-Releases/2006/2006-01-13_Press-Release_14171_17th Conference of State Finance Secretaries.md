# 17th Conference of State Finance Secretaries

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

January 13, 2006 th 17 Conference of State Finance Secretaries th The 17 Conference of the State Finance Secretaries was held in the Reserve Bank of India at Mumbai today. Finance Secretaries of 21 States participated. Senior officials from Ministry of Finance, Government of India and representatives from Comptroller and Auditor General of India, the Controller General of Accounts and the Chief Controller of Accounts attended the Conference. Dr. Y. V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India, inaugurated the Conference.

The Reserve Bank has been, since 1997, organising the State Finance Secretaries' Conference twice a year. The Conference all through has provided a very useful forum for interactions among State Finance Secretaries, senior officials of Government of India, Planning Commission, Comptroller and Auditor General, and the Reserve Bank of India. The State Finance Secretaries play a key role in the development process of their respective States and strive to achieve transparency, stability, fairness and efficiency in the conduct of fiscal policies. The deliberations in the past Conferences have served to arrive at a consensus on various issues in furtherance of better financial management and fiscal prudence in the States.

Today's Conference aimed to take this consultative process further. Apart from an assessment of the market borrowing programme of the State Governments and operational issues pertaining to Government transactions, the discussions primarily focused on the recommendations of the Advisory Committee on Ways and Means Advances to the State Governments, issues relating to liquidity of State Government securities, compilation of data on State Government liabilities and computerization of treasuries.
