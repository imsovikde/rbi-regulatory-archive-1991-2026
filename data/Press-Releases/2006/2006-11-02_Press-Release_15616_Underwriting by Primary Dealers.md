## November 2, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 0.87 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “7.40% Government Stock 2012” and at 1.30 paise per Rs. 100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “7.50% Government Stock 2034” to be held on November 3, 2006.
