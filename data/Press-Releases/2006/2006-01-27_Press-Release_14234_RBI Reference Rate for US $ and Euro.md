## January 27, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.1500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9100 on January 27, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 25, 2006) were Rs.44.2900 and Rs.54.3800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
