## January 13, 2006

Shifting of FED – MRO to Bandra-Kurla Complex

The Foreign Exchange Department, Mumbai Regional Office (MRO) housed at 1st and 4th floor of Amar Building, Fort, Mumbai-400001 will be shifting to 5th and 6th floor of C-7 Building at Bandra-Kurla Complex as at the close of business of January 13, 2006. It is, therefore, requested that in future all correspondence meant for Foreign Exchange Department, Mumbai Regional Office may be kindly addressed to :

The General Manager Foreign Exchange Department Mumbai Regional Office Reserve Bank of India C-7 / 5th and 6th Floor Bandra-Kurla Complex Bandra (East) Mumbai-400051
