## June 5, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at Re. 0.08 (eight paise) and Re 0.0238 (two point three eight paise) per Rupees 100 in the underwriting for the auctions of “Government Stock of 30 years’’ and “9.39% Government Stock 2011” respectively, to be held on June 6, 2006.
