## March 24, 2006

## Market Stabilisation Scheme

In accordance with the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) of the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), the annual ceiling for 2006-07 is being fixed at Rs. 70,000 crore while annual ceiling for 2005-06 is Rs.80,000 crore. The threshold level at which the ceiling will be reviewed in 2006-07 has been fixed at Rs. 60,000 crore. The revised ceilings and thresholds have been agreed upon between the Government of India and the Reserve Bank.

It may be recalled that on December 30, 2005, the Reserve Bank had announced its decision to suspend the issue of Treasury Bills and dated securities under the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS). This decision would continue till such time as is necessary. However, within the overall MSS ceiling for 2006-07, the Reserve Bank would retain the flexibility of conducting auctions under the MSS from time to time with sufficient notice to the market, in response to evolving circumstances.
