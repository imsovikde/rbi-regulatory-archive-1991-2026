## Seven State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2016 on July 13, 2006

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.1683.324 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, July 13, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on July 13, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on July 14, 2006 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Andhra Pradesh Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on July 12, 2006 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
