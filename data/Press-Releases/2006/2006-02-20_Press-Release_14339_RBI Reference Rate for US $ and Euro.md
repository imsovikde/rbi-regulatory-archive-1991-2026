## February 20, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.4300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1700 on February 20, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 17, 2006) were Rs.44.4600 and Rs.52.8600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|February 17, 2006|77.1848|37.64|
|February 20, 2006|77.4970|37.58|
