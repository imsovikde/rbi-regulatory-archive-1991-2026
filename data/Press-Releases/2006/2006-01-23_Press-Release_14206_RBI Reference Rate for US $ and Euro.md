## January 23, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.1500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.0600 on January 23, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 20, 2006) were Rs.44.3600 and Rs.53.5600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 20, 2006|77.8806|38.38|
|January 23, 2006|78.6069|38.48|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
