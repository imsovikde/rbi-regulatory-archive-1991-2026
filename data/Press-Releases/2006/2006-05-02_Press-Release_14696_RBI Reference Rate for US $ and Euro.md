## May 2, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.4700 on May 2, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 28, 2006) were Rs.44.9700 and Rs.56.4100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
