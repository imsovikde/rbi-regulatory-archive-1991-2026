September 22, 2006

91-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.500 crore under regular auction, Rs.1500 crore under Mss

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government of

India Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.20o0 crore (Rs. 500 crore under the

regular auction calendar and Rs.15o0 crore under the Market Stabilisation Scheme

(MSS)). The auction will be conducted on September 27, 2006 using "Multiple Price

Auction" method. The allocation to the non-competitive bidders will be outside the

notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and

1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted  in the prescribed form on Wednesday,

September 27, 2006 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening.
