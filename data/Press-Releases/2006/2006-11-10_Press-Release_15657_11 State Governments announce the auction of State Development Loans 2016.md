## November 10, 2006

11 State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2016

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.2,431.225 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

Sr. State Notified Amount No. (Rs. in Crore)

|No.|||
|---|---|---|
|1|Andhra Pradesh|400.000|
|2|Himachal Pradesh|300.000|
|3|Jammu & Kashmir|90.970|
|4|Maharashtra|500.000|
|5|Manipur|42.250|
|6|Mizoram|20.520|
|7|Nagaland|43.190|
|8|Punjab|243.360|
|9|Rajasthan|274.010|
|10|Sikkim|50.260|
|11|West Bengal Total|466.665 2431.225|

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on November 16, 2006 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, November 16,

2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on November 16, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on November 17, 2006 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
