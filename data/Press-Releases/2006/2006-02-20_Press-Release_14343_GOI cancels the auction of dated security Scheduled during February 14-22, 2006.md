## February 20, 2006

GOI cancels the auction of dated security Scheduled during February 14-22, 2006

It may be recalled that as per the calendar for issuance of Government of India dated securities, an auction of dated security with 10 years to 14 years tenure is scheduled during February 14-22, 2006 for Rs.5,000 crore.

On a review of the current status of Central Government finances, the Government of India in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India have decided to cancel the scheduled auction.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1058
