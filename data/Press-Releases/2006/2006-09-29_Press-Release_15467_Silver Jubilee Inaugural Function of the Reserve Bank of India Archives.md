## Silver Jubilee Inaugural Function of the Reserve Bank of India Archives

The Reserve Bank of India Archives (RBIA), Pune celebrated its Silver Jubilee Inaugural function today and assumed the new name (formerly called Central Records and Documentation Centre). Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India unveiled the plaque of the RBIA and presided over the function. Located in the campus of the Reserve Bank of India's College of Agricultural Banking, Pune the RBIA is a centre for preservation and retrieval of non-current permanent records of the Reserve Bank.

The core activities of RBIA are acquisition and scientific preservation of important records of the Reserve Bank in the form of files, registers etc., dating back to April 1, 1935 and even earlier. Photographs covering important events of the Bank, digital records, published material, publications brought out, newspaper clippings and securities covering period as early as 1777 are also preserved. The RBIA has facilities for microfilming and scanning along with scientific repair and rehabilitation techniques. The RBI Archives has been catering to the needs of historical, legal and administrative needs of the Bank as well as other institutions and individuals who require information.
