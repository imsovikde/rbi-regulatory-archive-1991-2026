## December 7, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 0.63 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “7.37% Government Stock 2014” and at 1.50 paise per Rs. 100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “8.33% Government Stock 2036” to be held on December 8, 2006.
