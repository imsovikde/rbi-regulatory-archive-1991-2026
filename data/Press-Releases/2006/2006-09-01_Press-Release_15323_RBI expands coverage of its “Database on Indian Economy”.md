# RBI expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> September 1, 2006

RBI expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

The Reserve Bank of India has today further expanded the coverage of its "Database on Indian Economy" to include, among other things, static reports on turnover details at National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), Central Government Market borrowings and Assets and Liabilities of a few financial institutions.

The additional static reports released on the Database on Indian Economy today are:

(a) Weekly traded volume in Corporate Debt at NSE,
(b) Monthly Turnover and Capitalisation at BSE,
(c) Daily Turnover and Capitalisation at BSE,
(d) Monthly Turnover and Capitalisation at NSE,
(e) Daily Turnover and Capitalisation at NSE,
(f) Month end YTM of SGL transactions in Central Government dated securities,
(g) Details of Central Government Market Borrowing by issue date,
(h) Details of Central Government Market Borrowing by maturity year,
(i) Liabilities and Assets of NABARD,
(j) Liabilities and Assets of UTI and
(k) Certificate of Deposits by SCBs With the release of these data series, the Database on Indian Economy would
now have 150 static reports (subject and frequency wise), 67 subject wise and 53 frequency wise data query templates covering various sectors of the Indian economy.

The Reserve Bank of India's Database on Indian Economy is available on its website (www.rbi.org.in) under the Database Section. This can also be directly accessed through URL [https://cdbmsi.reservebank.org.in/](https://cdbmsi.reservebank.org.in/). The home page of the Database on Indian Economy lists the data series available on it. The coverage of the Database is continuously being expanded on the basis of the feedback received from the users of the database. The present expansion is the eleventh such expansion release.

Users are requested to provide their feedback through a feedback link on the site so as to enable further improvements in the coverage of data series in the system.
