April 24, 2006 Money Market Operations : Overnight Rates and CBLO/Market Repo Volumes

The Reserve Bank of India will, with effect from tomorrow release information on its website on overnight rates and volume for Collateralised Borrowings and Lending Obligations (CBLO) and Market Repo. The information will be provided as part of the daily press release on Money Market Operations.

It may be recalled that the Annual Policy Statement for 2006-07 released on April 18, 2006 announced in paragraph No.118 that the information on overnight rates and volume for this information would be in addition to the call/notice money market provided on the website on a daily basis.

Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release : 2005-2006/1367
