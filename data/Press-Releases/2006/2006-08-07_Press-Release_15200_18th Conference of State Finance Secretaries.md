# 18th Conference of State Finance Secretaries

August 7, 2006. th 18 Conference of State Finance Secretaries th The 18 Conference of the State Finance Secretaries was held in the Reserve Bank of India in Mumbai today. Finance Secretaries of 20 States participated. Senior officials from the Ministry of Finance, Government of India and representatives from Comptroller and Auditor General of India and the Controller General of Accounts attended the Conference. Dr. Y. V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India inaugurated the Conference.

The Reserve Bank has been, since 1997, organising the State Finance Secretaries' Conference twice a year. The Conference all through has provided a very useful forum for interactions among State Finance Secretaries, senior officials of Government of India, Planning Commission, Comptroller and Auditor General, and the Reserve Bank of India. The State Finance Secretaries play a key role in the development process of their respective States and strive to achieve transparency, stability, fairness and efficiency in the conduct of fiscal policies. The deliberations in the past Conferences have served to arrive at a consensus on various issues in furtherance of better financial management and fiscal prudence in the States.

Today's Conference aimed to take this consultative process further. Apart from an assessment of the market borrowing programme of the State Governments and operational issues pertaining to Government transactions, the discussions primarily focussed on issues relating to computerisation of treasuries, underwriting in respect of open market loans and management of open market borrowings of the State Governments.
