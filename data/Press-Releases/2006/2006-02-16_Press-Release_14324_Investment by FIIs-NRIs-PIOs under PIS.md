## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on February 16, 2006 has notified that further purchases of equity shares of Television Eighteen India Ltd., on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed only after obtaining prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that foreign share holdings in Television Eighteen India Ltd., has gone down below the limit of 26 per cent of its paid up capital.
