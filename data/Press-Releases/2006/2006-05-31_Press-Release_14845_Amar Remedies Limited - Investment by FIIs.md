Amar Remedies Limited : Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India on May 30, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Amar Remedies Limited, under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Amar Remedies Limited has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
