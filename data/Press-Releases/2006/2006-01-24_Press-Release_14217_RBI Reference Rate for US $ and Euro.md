## January 24, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3600 on January 24, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 23, 2006) were Rs.44.1500 and Rs.54.0600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 23, 2006|78.6069|38.48|
|January 24, 2006|78.9657|38.57|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
