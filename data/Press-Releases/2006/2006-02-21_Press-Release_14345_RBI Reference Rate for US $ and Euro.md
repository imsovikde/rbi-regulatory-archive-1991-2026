## February 21, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.4000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9500 on February 21, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 20, 2006) were Rs.44.4300 and Rs.53.1700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|February 20, 2006|77.4970|37.58|
|February 21, 2006|77.4203|37.46|
