Investment by FIIs under PIS - The South Indian Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on July 17, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of The South Indian Bank Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary and secondary markets in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that The South Indian Bank Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
