## April 19, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.7800 on April 19, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 18, 2006) were Rs.45.1500 and Rs.55.3400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
