## January 18, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.5100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.7700 on January 18, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 17, 2006) were Rs.44.3100 and Rs.53.7300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
