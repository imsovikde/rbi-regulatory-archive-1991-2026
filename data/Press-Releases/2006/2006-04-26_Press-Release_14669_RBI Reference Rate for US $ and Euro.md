## April 26, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.9200 on April 26, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 25, 2006) were Rs.44.8300 and Rs.55.5000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
