## <u>Auction Calendar – LAF & SLAF</u>

The Reserve Bank of India announced the calendar of auction for Liquidity Adjustment Facility **(LAF)** and Second Liquidity Adjustment Facility **(SLAF)** for the **quarter January-Mar 2007.** There is no change in the existing terms and conditions under which the Liquidity Adjustment Facility auctions are conducted.
