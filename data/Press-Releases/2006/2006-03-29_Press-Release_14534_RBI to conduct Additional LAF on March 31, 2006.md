## March 29, 2006

RBI to conduct Additional LAF on March 31, 2006

At the request of the market participants, the Reserve Bank of India would conduct an additional Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) on March 31, 2006. The bids for this auction would be received between 9.00 p.m. to 9.30 p.m. on that day.

It may be noted that as in the past, on March 31, banking hours for government business, including receipts of tax have been extended. The additional LAF has been put in place to facilitate funds management by banks on account of the year-end closing on March 31, 2006 falling on a reporting Friday. The features of the additional LAF would remain the same as those of the LAF and SLAF in all its operational aspects. The settlement will be conducted separately and on gross basis.

At present, the Reserve Bank of India operates Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) to inject/absorb liquidity through daily repos/reverse repos auctions twice a day-in the forenoon between 9.30 a.m. and 10.30 a.m and in the evening between 3.00 p.m. and 3.45 p.m.
