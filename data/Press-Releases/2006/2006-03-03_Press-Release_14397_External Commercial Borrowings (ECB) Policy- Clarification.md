## March 3, 2006

## External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) Policy: Clarification

The Reserve Bank of India had announced the comprehensive ECB guidelines vide A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 5 dated August 1, 2005. Enquiries have been received on whether airports and companies engaged in telecom equipment manufacturing are eligible to raise ECB.

It is clarified that the companies in airport sector as well as those engaged in telecom equipment manufacturing activity are eligible to raise ECB in accordance with the existing ECB guidelines.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1114
