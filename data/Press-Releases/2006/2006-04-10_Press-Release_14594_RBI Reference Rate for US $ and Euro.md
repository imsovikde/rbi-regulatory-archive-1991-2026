## April 10, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.7400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1400 on April 10, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 7, 2006) were Rs.44.6300 and Rs.54.4800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|April 7, 2006|78.2074|37.91|
