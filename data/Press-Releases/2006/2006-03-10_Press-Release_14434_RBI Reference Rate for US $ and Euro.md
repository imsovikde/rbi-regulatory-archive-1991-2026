## March 10, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.5000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9800 on March 10, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 9, 2006) were Rs.44.5500 and Rs.53.1100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
