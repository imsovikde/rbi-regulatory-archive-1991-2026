# Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 2, 2006 Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies The Reserve Bank of India has notified on February 2, 2006 that the holdings of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) of the following companies under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) has reached the limit of 22 per cent of their paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has further notified that no further purchases of equity shares of these companies on behalf of FIIs will be allowed without obtaining its prior approval as these companies have demerged from Reliance Industries Limited. Since these companies have not passed the requisite resolution for enhancing FIIs limit, their FII limit would remain as 24 per cent. 1. Reliance Communication Ventures Ltd. 2. Reliance Energy Ventures Ltd. 3. Reliance Capital Ventures Ltd. 4. Reliance Natural Resources Ltd. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2005-2006/978
