## March 21, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.3800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.8400 on March 21, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 20, 2006) were Rs.44.4400 and Rs.54.0800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
