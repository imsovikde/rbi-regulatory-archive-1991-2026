## February 1, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.1500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6800 on February 1, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 31, 2006) were Rs.44.0700 and Rs.53.3200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 31, 2006|77.9510|37.50|
|February 1, 2006|78.5693|37.68|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
