## April 27, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.0700 on April 27, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 26, 2006) were Rs.45.0300 and Rs.55.9200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
