` PRESS RELEASE

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

||PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION|, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001||www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in \hindi|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|||Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 Underwriting auction for 7.40 per cent Government Stock 2012 and Government Stock, 2032 The underwriting auctions for the captioned stocks would be held on April 24, 2006. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment and Liquidity Support dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for each auction are as follows:||e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 21, 2006|
|Sr. No.|Nomenclature of Security|Notified Amount|MUC for each PD|ACU for which bidding has to be done by PDs collectively|
|1|7.40 per cent Government Stock, 2012|Rs. 6000 crore|Rs. 177 crore|Rs. 2991 crore|
|2|7.95 per cent Government Stock, 2032|Rs. 4000 crore|Rs. 118 crore|Rs. 1994 crore|

The underwriting auction will be based on multiple price based auction method. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through NDS-PMO module. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with the Reserve Bank on the date of issue of security.
