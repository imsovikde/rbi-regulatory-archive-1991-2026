## March 31, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.6100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.2000 on March 31, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 29, 2006) were Rs.44.6900 and Rs.53.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
