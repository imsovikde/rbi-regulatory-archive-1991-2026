## October 12, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 1.00 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “7.59% Government Stock 2016” and at 1.79 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “8.33% Government Stock 2036” to be held on October 13, 2006.
