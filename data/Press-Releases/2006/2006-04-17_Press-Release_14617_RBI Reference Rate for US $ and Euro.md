## April 17, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.9800 on April 17, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 13, 2006) were Rs.45.3000 and Rs.54.8800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
