## April 24, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.7000 on April 24, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 21, 2006) were Rs.45.0900 and Rs.55.4000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|April 21, 2006|80.1587|38.33|
|April 24, 2006|80.5087|38.99|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
