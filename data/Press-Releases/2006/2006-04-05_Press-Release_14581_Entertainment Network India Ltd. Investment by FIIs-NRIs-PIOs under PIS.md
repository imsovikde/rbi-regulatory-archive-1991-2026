## April 5, 2006

Entertainment Network India Ltd. Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on April 5, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Entertainment Network India Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through stock exchanges in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Entertainment Network India Ltd., has reached the limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital.
