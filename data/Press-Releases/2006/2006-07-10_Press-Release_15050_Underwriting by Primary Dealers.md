## July 10, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 1.87 paise and 2.74 paise per Rupees 100 in the underwriting for the auctions of “7.59% Government Stock 2016" and “7.50% Government Stock 2034” respectively, to be held on July 11, 2006.
