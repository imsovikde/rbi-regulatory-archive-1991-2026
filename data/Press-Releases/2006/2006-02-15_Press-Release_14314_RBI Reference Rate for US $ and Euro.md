## February 15, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.7400 on February 15, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 14, 2006) were Rs.44.2200 and Rs.52.7000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|February 14, 2006|77.0777|37.73|
