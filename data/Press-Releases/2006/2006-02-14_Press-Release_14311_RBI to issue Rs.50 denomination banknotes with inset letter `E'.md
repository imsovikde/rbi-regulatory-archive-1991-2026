## February 14, 2006

RBI to issue Rs.50 denomination banknotes with inset letter `E'

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue Rs.50 denomination banknotes with inset letter `E' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series – 2005 bearing the signature of Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor. Except for the change in inset letter, the design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respects to the banknotes in Mahatma Gandhi Series with additional/new security features issued on August 24, 2005. All banknotes in the denomination of Rs.50 issued by the Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1027
