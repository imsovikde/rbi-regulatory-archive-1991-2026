Monitoring of Investments by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on May 30, 2006 has notified that further purchases of equity shares of the following companies on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed without prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that foreign share holdings in these companies have gone down below the limit of 22 per cent of their paid up capital.

1. The Associated Cement Companies Ltd.
2. Ashok Leyland Ltd.
3. Bharat Heavy Electricals Ltd.
