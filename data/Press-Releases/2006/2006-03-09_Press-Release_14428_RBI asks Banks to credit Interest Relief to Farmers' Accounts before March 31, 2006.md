# RBI asks Banks to credit Interest Relief to Farmers' Accounts before March 31, 2006

RBI asks Banks to credit Interest Relief to Farmers' Accounts before March 31, 2006

The Reserve Bank of India has asked the banks to credit the farmers’ accounts before March 31, 2006 with interest relief of two percentage points in the interest rate on the principal amount upto Rs1 lakh of crop loans availed by the farmers for Kharif and Rabi 2005-06. Banks can seek reimbursement from Reserve Bank after crediting the relief to farmers’ accounts, it said in a circular issued to banks today.

It may be recalled that Paragraph 48 of the Union Budget Speech envisages grant of interest relief of two percentage points in the interest rate on the principal amount up to Rs1 lakh of crop loans availed by the farmers for Kharif and Rabi 2005-06. The amount of the relief is required to be credited to the borrower's account before March 31, 2006.

Detailing the method for calculating the interest relief, the Reserve Bank circular said that:

(a) All crop loans for Kharif and Rabi disbursed to farmers during the financial year 2005-06 will be eligible for the interest relief of two percentage points. However, where each crop loan exceeds Rs.1 lakh, the interest relief will be applicable on the principal amount up to Rs.1 lakh.
(b) Interest relief may be calculated at two percentage points on the amount of the crop loan disbursed from the date of disbursement/drawal up to the date of payment, or upto the date beyond which the outstanding loan becomes overdue, that is, March 31, 2006 for Kharif and June 30, 2006 for Rabi, respectively, whichever is earlier. Banks have been asked to submit their claims to the Reserve Bank of India
and they will be reimbursed by way of credit to their accounts with the Reserve Bank of India within one month of the date of receipt of the claim.

Suitable instructions to cooperative banks and Regional Rural Banks will be issued by National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) separately.
