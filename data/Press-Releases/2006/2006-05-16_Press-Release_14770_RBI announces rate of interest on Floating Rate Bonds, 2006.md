## May 16, 2006

RBI announces rate of interest on Floating Rate Bonds, 2006

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2006 (FRB, 2006) applicable for the half-year May 22, 2006 to November 21, 2006 shall be 6.30 percent per annum.

It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2006 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on November 21, 2001) over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest shall be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the said last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills worked out to be 6.35 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on November 21, 2001 was (-) 0.05 per cent (minus 0.05 per cent). The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
