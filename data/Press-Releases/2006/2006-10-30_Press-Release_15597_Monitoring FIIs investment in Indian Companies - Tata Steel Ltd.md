Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies : Tata Steel Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on October 30, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares of Tata Steel Ltd., through the secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the holdings of FIIs in the company have gone down below 22 per cent limit of its paid up capital.
