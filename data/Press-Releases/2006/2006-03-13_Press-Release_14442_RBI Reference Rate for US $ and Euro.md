## March 13 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.5300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1600 on March 13, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 10, 2006) were Rs.44.5000 and Rs.52.9800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|March 10, 2006|77.2320|37.57|
|March 13, 2006|76.9323|37.46|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
