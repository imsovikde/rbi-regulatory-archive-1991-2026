# RBI invites suggestions on the proposed "Protected Disclosures Scheme for Private Sector and Foreign Banks"

## January 25, 2006

RBI invites suggestions on the proposed "Protected Disclosures Scheme for Private Sector and Foreign Banks"

As a proactive measure to strengthen the financial stability and with a view to enhancing public confidence in the financial sector, the Reserve Bank of India proposes to introduce a scheme called "Protected Disclosures Scheme for Private Sector and Foreign Banks". The draft of the proposed scheme has been placed on the website of the Reserve Bank of India at h<u>ttp://www.rbi.org.in for</u> information of members of the public. Suggestions are invited from all concerned including members of the public on the draft scheme which may be addressed to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Supervision, Central Office, World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Colaba, Mumbai - 400005 or may be sent by e-mail to kvsubbarao@rbi.org.in within two months, i.e., up to March 27, 2006.

It may be noted that the Government of India had passed a resolution on April 21, 2004 authorising the Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) as the ‘Designated Agency’ to receive written complaints or disclosure on any allegation of corruption or of misuse of office and recommend appropriate action. The jurisdiction of the CVC in this regard is restricted to employees of the Central Government or of any corporation established by it or under any Central Act, Government companies, societies or local authorities owned or controlled by the Central Government. All public sector banks and the Reserve Bank of India are already covered by this resolution.

Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager
