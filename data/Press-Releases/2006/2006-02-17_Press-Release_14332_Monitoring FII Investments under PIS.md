## February 17, 2006

## Monitoring FII Investments under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has on February 17, 2006 notified that the Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) holdings in the undernoted companies have reached the limit of 22 per cent of their paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has further notified that further purchases of equity shares of these companies through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed only after obtaining prior clearance of the Reserve Bank till 24 per cent limit is reached.

1. Power Trading Corporation of India
2. Rico Auto Industries Ltd.
