## July 12, 2006

## <u>RBI confirms Normal Functioning</u>

In response to queries from a section of the press in the light of yesterday's developments in Mumbai, the Reserve Bank of India stated that it had made all arrangements yesterday to ensure uninterrupted continuation of normal services rendered by it. The Reserve Bank added that its critical payment systems such as, Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) System and Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) would function normally today and all auctions, including Liquidity Adjustment Facility and treasury bills, would be conducted as scheduled. The Reserve Bank would as usual also continue to closely monitor the financial markets, it assured.

Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager
