## RBI Central Board Reconstituted

The Government of India has reconstituted the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India. The following distinguished personalities have been nominated to be the Members of the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India, for a period of four years with effect from June 27, 2006:

1. Shri Suresh Tendulkar, Economist and Member, Prime Minister's Economic Advisory Council (to represent Eastern Area Local Board).
2. Prof. U. R. Rao, former Chairman ISRO and Chairman Research Council, Physical Research Laboratory, Department of Space (to represent Northern Area Local Board).
3. Shri Lakshmi Chand, IAS (Retd.) (to represent Southern Area Local Board).
4. Shri Azim Premji, Chairman, WIPRO Ltd.
5. Shri Kumar Mangalam Birla, Chairman Aditya Birla Group of Companies.
6. Smt. Shashi Rekha Rajagopalan, Consultant, Co-operatives.
7. Shri Suresh Kumar Neotia, Chairman, Ambuja Cement.
8. Dr. A. Vaidyanathan, Madras Institute of Development Studies.
9. Dr. Man Mohan Sharma, FRS, Former Director, Mumbai University Institute of Chemical Technology.
10. Dr. D. Jayavarthanavelu, Chairman and Managing Director, Laxmi Machine Works Ltd. The following were re-nominated to the Board:
1. Shri Y.H. Malegam, Chartered Accountant, (to represent Western Area Local Board).
2. Shri H. P. Ranina, Supreme Court Advocate.
3. Dr. Ashok S. Ganguly, Member, Investment Commission and Knowledge Commission and Chairman, ICICI OneSource Ltd.

Shri Ratan Tata, Shri N.R. Narayana Murthy, Prof. Mihir Rakshit, Shri K. Madhava Rao, Prof. V. S. Vyas, Shri K.P. Singh, Dr. Amrita Patel, Shri Suresh Krishna retired as Directors of the Central Board of the Bank.
