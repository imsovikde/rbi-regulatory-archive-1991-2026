## January 17, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.3100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.7300 on January 17, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 16, 2006) were Rs.44.1700 and Rs.53.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
