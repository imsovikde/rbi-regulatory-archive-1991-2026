## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on March 9, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of D.S.Kulkarni Dev Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that D.S.Kulkarni Dev Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
