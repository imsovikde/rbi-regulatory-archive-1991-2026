## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on April 17, 2006, has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Union Bank of India should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Union Bank of India has reached the limit of 20 per cent of its paid up capital.
