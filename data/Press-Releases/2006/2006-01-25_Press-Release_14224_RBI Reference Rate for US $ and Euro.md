## January 25, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3800 on January 25, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 24, 2006) were Rs.44.2100 and Rs.54.3600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
