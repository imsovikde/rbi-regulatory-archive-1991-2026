## September 19, 2006

Monitoring of investment limits of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : Andhra Bank

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on September 19, 2006 that the aggregate foreign share holdings by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/ Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in Andhra Bank has reached the limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that further purchases of equity shares on behalf of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs will be allowed only on obtaining its prior approval.
