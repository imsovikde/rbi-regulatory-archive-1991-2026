## June 8, 2006

Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd: Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on June 8, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 72 per cent of its paid up capital.
