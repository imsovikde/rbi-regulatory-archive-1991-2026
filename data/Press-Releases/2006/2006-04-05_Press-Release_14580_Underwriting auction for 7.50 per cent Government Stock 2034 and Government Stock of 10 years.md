# Underwriting auction for 7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034 and Government Stock of 10 years

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

||PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION|, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001||www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in \hindi|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|||Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 Underwriting auction for 7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034 and Government Stock of 10 years The underwriting auctions for the captioned stocks would be held on April 7, 2006. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment and Liquidity Support dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for each auction are as follows:||e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 5, 2006|
|Sr. No.|Nomenclature of Security|Notified Amount|MUC for each PD|ACU for which bidding has to be done by PDs collectively|
|1|7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034|Rs. 3000 crore|Rs. 89 crore|Rs. 1487 crore|
|2|Government Stock of 10 years|Rs. 5000 crore|Rs. 148 crore|Rs. 2484 crore|

The underwriting auction will be based on multiple price based auction method. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through NDS-PMO module. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
