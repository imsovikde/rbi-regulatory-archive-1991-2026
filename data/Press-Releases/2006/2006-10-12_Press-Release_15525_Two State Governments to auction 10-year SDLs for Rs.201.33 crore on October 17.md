## October 12, 2006

## Two State Governments to auction 10-year SDLs for Rs.201.33 crore on October 17

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans (SDLs) for an aggregate amount of Rs.201.33 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## Sr. <u>State</u> Notified Amount

## No. (Rs. in Crore)

1 Arunachal Pradesh 48.10 2 Kerala 153.23 **Total 201.33**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on October 17, 2006 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday October 17, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on October 17, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on October 18, 2006 (Wednesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
