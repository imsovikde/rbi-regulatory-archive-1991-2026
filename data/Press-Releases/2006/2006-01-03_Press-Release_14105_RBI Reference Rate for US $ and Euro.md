## January 3, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4600 on January 3, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 2, 2006) were Rs.45.0500 and Rs.53.2800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 2, 2006|77.5153|38.32|
|January 3, 2006|77.7230|38.34|
