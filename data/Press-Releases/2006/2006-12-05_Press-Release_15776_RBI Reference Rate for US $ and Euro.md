## December 05, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.56 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.59.41 on December 05, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 04, 2006) were Rs.44.59 and Rs.59.40 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
