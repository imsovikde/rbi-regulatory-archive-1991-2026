## May 19, 2006

Investment by FIIs under PIS : Prajay Engineering Work Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on May 19, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Prajay Engineering Work Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary and secondary markets in India without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Prajay Engineering Work Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
