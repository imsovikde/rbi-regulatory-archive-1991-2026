## Monitoring Foreign Investment under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on February 8, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Bank of Baroda, under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Bank of Baroda has reached the overall limit of 20 per cent of its paid up capital.
