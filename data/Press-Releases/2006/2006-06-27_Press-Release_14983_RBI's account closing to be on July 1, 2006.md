RBI's account closing to be on July 1, 2006

The Reserve Bank of India will remain open on Friday June 30, 2006 for public transactions. It will observe Saturday, July 1, 2006 as a holiday for public transactions on account of its annual closing.

P.V.Sadanandan Manager
