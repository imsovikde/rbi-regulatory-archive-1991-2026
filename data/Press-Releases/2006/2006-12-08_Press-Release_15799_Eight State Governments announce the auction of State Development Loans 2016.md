## December 8, 2006

Eight State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2016

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.1,963.236 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State Notified Amount

|Sr.|||
|---|---|---|
|No.||(Rs. crore)|
|1|Andhra Pradesh|408.780|
|2|Assam|166.455|
|3|Jharkhand|192.564|
|4|Kerala|400.000|
|5|Meghalaya|55.437|
|6|Nagaland|40.000|
|7|Rajasthan|300.000|
|8|Tamil Nadu Total|400.000 1963.236|

Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain

additional subscription upto a maximum of Rs.100.00 crore (Nominal) over and above the notified amount of Rs.400.00 crore.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on December 14, 2006 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, December 14, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by</u>

<u>12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on December 14, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on December 15, 2006 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
