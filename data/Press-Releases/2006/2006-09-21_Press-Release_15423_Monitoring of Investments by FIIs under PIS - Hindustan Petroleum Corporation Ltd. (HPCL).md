## September 21, 2006

Monitoring of Investments by FIIs under PIS : Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (HPCL)

The Reserve Bank of India on September 21, 2006 has notified that further purchases of equity shares of Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (HPCL) on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through secondary market in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) would be allowed without prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that foreign share holdings in this company has gone down below the caution limit of its paid up capital.
