## April 24, 2006

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at Re.0.18 (eighteen paise) per Rupees100 and Re.0.024 (two point four paise) per Rupees 100 in the underwriting for the auction of 7.95% Government Stock 2032 and 7.40% Government Scock 2012 respectively, to be held on April 25, 2006.
