## February 22, 2006

Twelve State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2016 on February 27, 2006

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans 2016 for an aggregate amount of Rs.3724.207 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on February 27, 2006 (Monday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400001 should be submitted to that Office on Monday, February 27, 2006. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 p.m. On the basis of bids received, Reserve Bank will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on February 27, 2006 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on February 28, 2006 (Tuesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Goa, Punjab, Uttaranchal and Uttar Pradesh Government Securities under auction may be submitted by the Primary Dealers' up to

12.30 p.m. on February 24, 2006 (Friday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001.
