## April 5, 2006

ICICI Bank Ltd. Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on April 5, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of ICICI Bank Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Porfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that ICICI Bank Ltd., has reached the limit of 74 per cent of its paid up capital.
