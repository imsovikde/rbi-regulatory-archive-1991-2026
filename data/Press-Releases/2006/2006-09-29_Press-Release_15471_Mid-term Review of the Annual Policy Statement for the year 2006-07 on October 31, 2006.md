**Mid-term Review of the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2006-07 on October 31, 2006**

As announced in the First Quarter Review of Annual Statement on Monetary Policy for the year 2006-07, Dr.Y.V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will present the Mid-term Review of Annual Policy Statement for 2006-07 on Tuesday, October 31, 2006. The Governor will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on that day in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
