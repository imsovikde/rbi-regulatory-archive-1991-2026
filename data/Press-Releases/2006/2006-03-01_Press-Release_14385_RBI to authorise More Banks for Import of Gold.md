# RBI to authorise More Banks for Import of Gold

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 1, 2006 RBI to authorise More Banks for Import of Gold To enlarge the list of banks authorised to directly import gold the Reserve Bank of India today invited applications from eligible banks. The Reserve Bank also said that it will apply the eligibility criteria liberally while considering these applications. This, the Reserve Bank said, was being done in order to enhance competition in this sector. Currently, 17 banks are authorised as nominated agencies for import of gold. The current eligibility criteria to apply for authorisation as nominated agency for import of gold are: (a) The applicant entity should be a scheduled commercial bank. (b) It should have unimpaired total capital of Rs.300 crore or more for its business in India. In case the capital of the bank for its business in India falls short of this, its global capital can be taken into consideration for this purpose, provided it has sufficient experience and expertise in the area of gold internationally and is willing to submit a letter of undertaking from its head office/parent to the effect that the head office/parent would stand ready to provide support and assistance to its gold operation in India. (c) Its ratio of total capital to risk-weighted assets for business in India should be 9 per cent or more as per the latest balance sheet. (d) It should have fairly advanced risk-management system in place to be able to identify various risk involved in supply of gold to the eligible categories and evolve control measures. (e) It should have excellent track record both in terms of business reputation and history of compliance vis-à-vis legal and regulatory norms/requirements. (f) It should have previous experience or familiarity in respect of gold-related business activities. In case it does not satisfy this requirement, it should provide sufficient evidence about its intention and ability to forge links with international banks, etc., having long-standing presence in the markets for physical and financial gold so as to be able to develop in-house capabilities in this area. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1101
