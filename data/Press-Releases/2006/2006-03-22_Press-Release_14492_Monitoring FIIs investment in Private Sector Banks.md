Monitoring FIIs investment in Private Sector Banks

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on March 22, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRI)/Persons of Indian Origins (PIOs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of The Federal Bank Ltd., through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 49 per cent limits of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
