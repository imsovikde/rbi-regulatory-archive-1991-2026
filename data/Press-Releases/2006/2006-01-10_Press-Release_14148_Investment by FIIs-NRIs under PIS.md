## Investment by FIIs/NRIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on January 10, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shars of Vijaya Bank should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Vijaya Bank has reached the limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital.
