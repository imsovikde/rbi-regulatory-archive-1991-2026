# Restrictive Practices while accepting Banknotes

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> March 7, 2006 Restrictive Practices while accepting Banknotes

The Reserve Bank of India has been receiving complaints from the public regarding adoption of restrictive practices such as, forcing the customers to note down their names on the banknotes, listing the note numbers on the bills submitted for payment insisting on undertaking while depositing higher denomination banknotes, etc., by certain public and private sector organisations/institutions, thus causing avoidable harassment to the public.

The Reserve Bank, therefore, considers it necessary to emphasise that all government/semi government offices, public/private sector institutions and also any other organisation/office/individual accepting cash should desist from the aforesaid restrictive practices.

For further information and guidance, the regional offices of the Bank may please be approached. The features of genuine Indian currency notes are posted on RBI website www.rbi.org.in

P.V.Sadanandan Manager
