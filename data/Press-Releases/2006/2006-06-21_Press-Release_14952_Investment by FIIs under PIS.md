## Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on June 21, 2006 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of the following companies, should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through primary and secondary markets in India without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that these companies have reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.

1. Cranes Software International Ltd.
2. Power Trading Corporation of India
