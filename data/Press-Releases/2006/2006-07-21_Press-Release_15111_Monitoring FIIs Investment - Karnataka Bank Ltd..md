Monitoring FIIs Investment : Karnataka Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on July 21, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Karnataka Bank Ltd., through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 49 per cent limits of its paid up capital, as the bank has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
