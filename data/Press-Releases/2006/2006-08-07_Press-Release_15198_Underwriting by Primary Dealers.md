## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 2.89 paise per Rupees 100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “9.39% Government Stock 2011" and at 1.99 paise per Rupees100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of "7.59% Government Stock 2016" to be held on August 8, 2006.
