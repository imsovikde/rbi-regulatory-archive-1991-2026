## November 16, 2006

Monitoring FII Investments in Indian Companies : Gateway Distriparks td.

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on November 16, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Gateway Distriparks Ltd., through stock exchanges in India upto 100 per cent of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
