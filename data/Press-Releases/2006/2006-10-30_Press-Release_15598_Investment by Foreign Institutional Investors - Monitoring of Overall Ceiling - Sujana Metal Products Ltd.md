## October 30, 2006

Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling : Sujana Metal Products Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on October 30, 2006 has notified that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in Sujana Metal Products Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that no further purchases of shares of Sujana Metal Products Ltd., should, therefore, be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs.
