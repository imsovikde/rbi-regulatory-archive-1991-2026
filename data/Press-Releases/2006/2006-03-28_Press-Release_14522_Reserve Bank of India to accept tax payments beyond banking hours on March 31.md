# Reserve Bank of India to accept tax payments beyond banking hours on March 31

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u>

March 28, 2006 Reserve Bank of India to accept tax payments <u>beyond banking hours on March 31</u>

For the convenience of taxpayers, the Reserve Bank of India has made special arrangements. At its Mumbai and Navi Mumbai offices, the Reserve Bank will receive cash deposits representing taxes at the counters upto 4.00 p.m. on March 31, 2006. Cheques/other instruments representing payment of taxes to the Government would be received upto 6.00 p.m. on March 31, 2006. Other offices of the Reserve Bank of India which accept government taxes wil also accept tax payments beyond banking hours on March 31, 2006.

Instructions have also been issued to agency banks, viz., State Bank of India and its associates, public sector banks and designated private sector banks to receive taxes beyond normal banking hours on March 31, 2006. Over 890 branches of commercial banks in Mumbai are authorised to receive tax payments.

The Reserve Bank has requested taxpayers to take advantage of these facilities provided for the financial year ending March 31, 2006.

Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1236
