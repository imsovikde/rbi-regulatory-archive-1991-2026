## April 12, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.5800 on April 12, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 10, 2006) were Rs.44.7400 and Rs.54.1400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
