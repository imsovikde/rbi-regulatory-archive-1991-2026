## October 16, 2006

## Monitoring FIIs Investments : Unichem Laboratories Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on October 16, 2006 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Unichem Laboratories Ltd., through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 39 per cent of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
