## April 7, 2006

## <u>Underwriting by Primary Dealers</u>

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at Re. 0.04 (four paise) per Rs. 100 and Re 0.09 (nine paise) per Rs. 100 in the underwriting for the auction of “Government Stock of 10 years’’ and “7.50% GS 2034” respectively, to be held on April 10, 2006.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1299
