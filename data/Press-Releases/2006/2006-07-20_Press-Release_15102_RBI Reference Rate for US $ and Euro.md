## July 20, 2006

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.59.0000 on July 20, 2006. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 19, 2006) were Rs.46.9500 and Rs.58.6900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
