## Results of Underwriting Auctions

In the underwriting auctions conducted on August 7, 2014 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (` crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ` 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.35 per cent GS 2022|2000|1000|950|1950|1.97|
|8.60 per cent GS 2028|7000|3500|2975|6475|4.39|
|9.20 per cent GS 2030|2000|1000|600|1600|4.79|
|8.30 per cent GS 2042|3000|1500|525|2025|4.95|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 08, 2014.
