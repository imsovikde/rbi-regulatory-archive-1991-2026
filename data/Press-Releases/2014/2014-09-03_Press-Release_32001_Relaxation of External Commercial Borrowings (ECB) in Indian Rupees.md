## September 3, 2014

## Relaxation of External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) in Indian Rupees

With a view to providing greater flexibility for structuring of External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) arrangements, it has been decided that all recognised non-resident ECB lenders may extend loans in Indian Rupees by mobilising Indian Rupees through swaps undertaken with an Authorised Dealer Category I bank in India in terms of <u>A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25 dated September 03, 2014</u>. At present all eligible borrowers are eligible to raise ECB in Indian Rupees only from their foreign equity holders.
