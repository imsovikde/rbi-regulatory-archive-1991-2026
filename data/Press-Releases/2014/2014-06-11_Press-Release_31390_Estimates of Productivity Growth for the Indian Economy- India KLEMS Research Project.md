## Estimates of Productivity Growth for the Indian Economy:

## India KLEMS Research Project

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the Report on <u>“Estimates of Productivity Growth for the Indian Economy”</u> along with <u>times series</u> <u>data on productivity</u> for the period 1980-81 to 2008-09, prepared under the India KLEMS [Capital (K), labour (L), energy (E), material (M) and services (S)] research project.
