## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, following term repo auctions will be conducted during the reporting fortnight ending on October 31, 2014 (Friday): Sl.No. Date of Auction Notified Amount Tenor Date of Reversal ( Crore) (days) 1 October 17, 2014 17,500 14 October 31, 2014

2 October 21, 2014 15,500 13 November 03, 2014

3 October 22, 2014 15,500 16 November 07, 2014

4 October 28, 2014 15,000 14 November 11, 2014

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
