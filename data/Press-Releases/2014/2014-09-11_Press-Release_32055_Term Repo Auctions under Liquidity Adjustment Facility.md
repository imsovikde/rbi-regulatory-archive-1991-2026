## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on August 22, 2014, Reserve Bank will conduct (i) 4 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 15,000 crore and (ii) 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 15,500 crore on September 12, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 4 day and 14 day term repos will take place on September 16, 2014 (Tuesday) and September 26, 2014 (Friday) respectively.
