## May 12, 2014

**Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the** **Portfolio Investment Schemes in Indian Companies -** **Incorporation in Ban List-FII/RFPIs : M/s Zen Technologies Ltd.**

The Reserve Bank of India advise that the foreign share holding by Foreign Institutional Investor (FII)/Registered Foreign Portfolio Investors(RFPIs) in defence industry is not permitted under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS.) Therefore, no purchases of shares of <u>M/s Zen Technologies Limited</u> would be allowed through stock exchange in India on behalf of FII/RFPIs.
