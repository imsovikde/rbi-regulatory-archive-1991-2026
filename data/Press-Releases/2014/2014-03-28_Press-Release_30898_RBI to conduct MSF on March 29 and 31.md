## RBI to conduct MSF on March 29 and 31

In order to facilitate smooth and non-disruptive conduct of banking operations during the annual closing of accounts for the financial year 2013-14 and in partial modification of the press release issued on <u>March 20, 2014</u>, the Reserve Bank has decided to keep the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) window open on March 29 (Saturday) and March 31, 2014 (Monday) between 7.00 and 7.30 pm. The reversal of MSF operations conducted on both the days will take place on April 2, 2014 (Wednesday). Other terms and conditions of MSF will remain unchanged.
