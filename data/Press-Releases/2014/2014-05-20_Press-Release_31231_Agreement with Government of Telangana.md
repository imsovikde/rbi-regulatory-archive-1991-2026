## May 20, 2014

## Agreement with Government of Telangana

The Reserve Bank of India has entered into an Agreement under Section 21A of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with the Government of Telangana. Under the agreement, which shall be effective from June 2, 2014, the Reserve Bank of India shall carry on the general banking business of the Government of Telangana, manage its rupee public debt and act as the sole agent for investment of Government’s funds. The agreement is pursuant to the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014.
