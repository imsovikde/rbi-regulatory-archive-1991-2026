## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the PIS in Indian Companies -

## Incorporation in Caution List-FIIs/NRI/PIO/FDI/ADR/GDRs : M/s D B Corp Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign share holding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs)/Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)/American Depository Receipt (ADR)/Global Depository Receipts (GDRs) in <u>M/s D B Corp Ltd</u> has reached the trigger limit. Hence, further purchases of equity shares of M/s D B Corp Ltd would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
