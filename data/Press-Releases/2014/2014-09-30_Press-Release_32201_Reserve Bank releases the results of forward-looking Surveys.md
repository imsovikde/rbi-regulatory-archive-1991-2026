i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey- Q2: 2014-15 (Round 67)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS)</u>
<u>- April- June 2014 (26th Round)</u>
iii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey-September 2014 (Round 20)</u> iv. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households-September 2014 (Round 37)</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators- September</u> <u>2014 (Round 30)</u> The results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the
Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
