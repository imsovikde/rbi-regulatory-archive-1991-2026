## September 4, 2014

# Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct (i) 4 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 46,000 crore and (ii) 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 15,500 crore on September 5, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 4 day and 14 day term repos will take place on September 9, 2014 (Tuesday) and September 19, 2014 (Friday) respectively.

The revised liquidity management framework is being put in place to assure transparency and predictability to liquidity management operations, notwithstanding the prevailing easy liquidity conditions which will continue to be managed by countervailing operations, including variable rate reverse repo auctions.
