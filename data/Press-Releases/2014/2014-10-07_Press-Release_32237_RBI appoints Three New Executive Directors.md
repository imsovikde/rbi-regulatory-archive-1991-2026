October 07, 2014 **RBI appoints Three New Executive Directors**

The Reserve Bank of India today promoted three senior officers as new executive directors-Dr. M.

D. Patra, Shri K. K. Vohra and Shri G. Mahalingam. One promotion is in lieu of an existing vacancy and the other two are against additional vacancies. Simultaneously, the Reserve Bank is also restructuring some departments as part of its reorganisation exercise already underway. Consequently, portfolio reallocation has been done as under: **Shri D.K.Mohanty** 1. Financial Stability Unit
2. Inspection Department
3. Risk Monitoring Department **Shri P. Vijaya Bhaskar** 1. Internal Debt Management Department
2. Department of External Investments and Operations
