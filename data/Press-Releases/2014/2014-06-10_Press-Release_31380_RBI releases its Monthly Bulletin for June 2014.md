June 10, 2014 **RBI releases its Monthly Bulletin for June 2014**

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>June 2014</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. Along with the regular features like speeches of the top management and current statistics, the Bulletin includes two special articles: (1) <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households:</u> 2013-14 and (2) <u>Finances of Non-Government Non-Financial Public Limited companies:</u> <u>2012-13</u>.

## 1. Inflation Expectations Survey of Households: 2013-14

The Reserve Bank’s Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) captures the inflation expectations of 5,000 urban households across 16 cities for the next three-month period and the next one-year period. This article analyses the changes in households’ inflation perceptions and expectations in recent times especially focusing on the four quarters of the year 2013-14 (Q1:2013-14 to Q4:2013-14). The results of the survey are based on replies of the respondents and do not necessarily reflect the perceptions of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Main Findings:

- While double digit inflation expectations persisted throughout the year 2013 -14, it declined marginally in Q4:2013 -14.
- The percentage of respondents expecting general price ‘increase by more than current rate’ decreased from 74.5 per cent (in Q2: 2013-14) to 51.2 per cent (in Q4:2013-14) for three-month ahead period and from 76.6 per cent (in Q2: 2013-14) to
59.0 per cent (in Q4:2013-14) for one-year ahead period.
- The inflation perception and expectations varied across various cities covered in the survey.
## 2. Finances of Non-Government Non-Financial Public Limited companies: 2012-13

The article analyses the financial performance of select 3,014 non-government non-financial public limited companies during the financial year 2012-13, based on their audited annual accounts. It draws a comparative picture over the three year period from 2010-11 to 2012-13 at the aggregate level as well as for sales-size groups and sectors/industries.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

## Main Findings:

- Growth rates in major income and expenditure variables in 2012-13 declined sharply reflecting subdued economic activity.
- Small sized companies performed relatively poorly. The ‘construction’ sector, ‘motor vehicles’ and non-electrical ‘machinery’ industries in the manufacturing sector and ‘real estate’ and ‘transportation’ industries in the services sector were worst affected in terms of sales growth.
- Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) grew marginally, recovering from contraction in 2011-12. However, net profit, *i.e.,* profit after tax continued to decline and profit margins fell for the second consecutive year.
- Lower net profits led to lower accretion to reserves. Correspondingly, the leverage ratio moved upward between 2010-11 and 2012-13. This also led to a gradual fall in the interest coverage ratio.
- Accretion to net assets during 2012-13 was at a lower rate reflecting overall business conditions. Share of funds raised through borrowings as well as share of funds used in fixed assets formation was higher.
- ‘Transportation’ industry continued to be very highly leveraged with interest coverage ratio below 1, mainly impacted by air transport companies. In the ‘iron and steel’ industry, interest coverage ratio fell steeply during 2010-11 to 2012-13 with rise in leverage. The data pertaining to the Finances of Non-Government Non-Financial Public Limited companies for 2012-13 was earlier released on the RBI website vide <u>Press Release No.</u> <u>2209 on May 15, 2014</u>.
