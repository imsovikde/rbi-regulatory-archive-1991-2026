## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: May 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the data on India’s International Trade in Services for May 2014. The value of exports and imports of services during May 2014 was:

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million) **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

## April 2014 13,632 8,058

## May 2014 13,920 8,027

**Note**: Data are provisional.

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The data for April 2014 was released on June 13, 2014. The data being provisional would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released.
