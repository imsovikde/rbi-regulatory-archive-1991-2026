## September 3, 2014

## LAF operations on reporting Fridays

As announced by the Reserve Bank on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, the Revised Liquidity Management Framework will be operationalised with effect from September 5, 2014. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has decided to discontinue with immediate effect the second fixed rate repo window under Liquidity Adjustment Facility, currently available on reporting Fridays between 4.45 PM and 5.15 PM.
