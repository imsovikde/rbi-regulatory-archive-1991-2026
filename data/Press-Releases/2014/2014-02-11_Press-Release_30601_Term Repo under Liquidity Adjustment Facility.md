## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a variable rate auction of 9 day term repo for a notified amount of `10,000 crore on February 12, 2014 (Wednesday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 9 day term repo will take place on February 21, 2014 (Friday).
