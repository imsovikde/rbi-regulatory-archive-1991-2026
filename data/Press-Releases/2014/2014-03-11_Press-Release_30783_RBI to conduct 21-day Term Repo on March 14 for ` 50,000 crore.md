## March 11, 2014

**RBI to conduct 21-day Term Repo on March 14 for** ` **50,000 crore**

As announced on <u>February 18, 2014</u>, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to conduct a 21 -day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 50,000 crore on March 14, 2014 (Friday). The reversal will be on April 4, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. There will be no term repo auction on March 18, 2014 when the existing 28-day term repo of ` 30,004 crore matures.
