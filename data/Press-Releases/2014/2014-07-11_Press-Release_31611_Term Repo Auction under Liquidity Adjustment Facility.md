## Term Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has decided to conduct a 4 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 5,000 crore on July 14, 2014 (Monday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 4 day term repo auction will take place on July 18, 2014 (Friday).
