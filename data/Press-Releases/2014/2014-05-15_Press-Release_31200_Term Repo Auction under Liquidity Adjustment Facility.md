## Term Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 61,000 crores on May 16, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 14 day term repo will take place on May 30, 2014 (Friday).
