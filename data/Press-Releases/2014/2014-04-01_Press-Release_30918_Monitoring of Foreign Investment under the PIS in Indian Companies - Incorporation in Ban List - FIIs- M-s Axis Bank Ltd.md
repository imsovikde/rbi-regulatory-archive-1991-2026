## April 1, 2014

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the PIS in Indian Companies -

## Incorporation in Ban List-FIIs: M/s Axis Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign share holding by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in <u>M/s Axis Bank Ltd</u>. has crossed the overall limit of 49% of its paid-up capital. Therefore no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs.
