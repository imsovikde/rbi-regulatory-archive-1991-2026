August 01, 2014 **Half Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves – March 2014**

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>half yearly report on</u> <u>management of foreign exchange reserves with reference to March, 2014</u>.

The position of foreign exchange reserve as on June 30, 2014 is as under:

## US $ Billion

|Foreign Exchange Reserves (i+ii+iii+iv)|316.13|
|---|---|
|i. Foreign Currency Assets (FCA)|289.32|
|ii. Gold|20.63|
|iii. Special Drawing Rights (SDR)|4.47|
|iv. Reserve Tranche Position (RTP)|1.71|

It may be recalled that in February, 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the domain for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure.
