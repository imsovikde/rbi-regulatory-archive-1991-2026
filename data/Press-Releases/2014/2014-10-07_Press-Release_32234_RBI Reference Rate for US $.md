October 07, 2014 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 61.3595 on October 07, 2014. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 01, 2014) was 61.7511. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross- currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 01, 2014 October 07, 2014** **1 EUR** 77.9546 77.4664 **1 GBP** 100.0677 98.7090 **100 YEN** 56.24 56.50 **Note :** The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.
