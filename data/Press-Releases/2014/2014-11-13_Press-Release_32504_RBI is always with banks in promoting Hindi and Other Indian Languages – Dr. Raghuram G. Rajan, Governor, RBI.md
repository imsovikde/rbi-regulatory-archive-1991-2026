## November 13, 2014

## RBI is always with banks in promoting

## Hindi and Other Indian Languages–

## Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan, Governor, RBI

"Today it is not a mere necessity of Rajbhasha Policy to work in Hindi and other Indian languages. It has become an integral part of better customer service to work in Hindi in the changing economic scenario. That is the reason the Reserve Bank of India is always with you for promoting Hindi and other Indian languages.”

Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India gave this assurance to the banks today at Mumbai while awarding the Rajbhasha Shields for the year <u>2012-2013</u>.

He also remarked that globalisation and IT have changed the socio-economic structure of our country to a large extent today. Those who hesitated to enter bank branches till yesterday are now connected with banking through business correspondents, mobiles or computers. To gain their confidence it is necessary to do their work in their language. Hindi and other Indian languages are like a bridge between the banker and the customer. This is the reason private banks are also using Hindi, although the Rajbhasha Policy is not applicable to them.

On this occasion, Shri S.S. Mundra, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India while congratulating prize winning banks and financial institutions observed that out of the three issues emphasised by the Government of India, it has laid special emphasis on the care of common man’s interests while enacting Rajbhasha policy. That is why most of the targets are made keeping in view the common man’s interests in connection with Rajbhasha. The success of the Rajbhasha policy should be measured by the success in fulfilling the interests of common man and not by achieving the targets from a statutory point of view.

Shri K.K. Vohra, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India welcomed the Chief Executives of banks and financial institutions and Smt. Surekha Marandi, Chief General Manager (Rajbhasha), proposed a vote of thanks. Dr. Ramakant Gupta, General Manager, Rajbhasha Department, Reserve Bank of India conducted the programme.

The function was attended by chairmen and other senior executives of banks and financial institutions.
