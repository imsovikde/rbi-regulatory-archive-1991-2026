## November 10, 2014

## RBI releases its Monthly Bulletin for November 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>November 2014</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. Along with regular features like speeches of the top management and current statistics, the Bulletin includes one special article on <u>Finances of Non-</u> <u>Government Non-Financial Private Limited Companies: 2012-13</u>.

The article analyses the financial performance of select 1,628 non-government non-financial (NGNF) private limited companies during the financial year 2012-13, based on their audited annual accounts.

## Main Findings:

- Growth in sales of select NGNF private limited companies moderated in 2012-13 due to lower increase, especially in the services sector. Both operating expenses and value of production registered a significant decline in 2012-13. Lower growth in earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) was observed due to higher increase in operating expenses than in value of production in 2012-13.
- The increase in EBITDA for most of the industries in manufacturing sector was higher except for ‘machinery and machine tools’ and ‘food product and beverages’ in 2012-13 as compared with the previous year, whereas in the services sector it remained low except for ‘computer and related activities’.
- Total net assets of selected companies grew at a higher rate in 2012-13 as compared to 2011-12. Higher pace of expansion was observed in services and construction sectors while the manufacturing sector witnessed a slower pace of expansion.
- The total borrowings to equity ratio, at the aggregate level, witnessed an increasing trend. Debt to equity ratio also increased in 2012-13.
- The interest coverage ratio (EBIT to interest ratio) at the aggregate level increased marginally in 2012-13. Detailed data on non-Government non-financial private limited
companies for 2012-13 along with explanatory notes were released on the RBI website on October 22, 2014 vide press release No.829 ([http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=32355](http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=32355))
