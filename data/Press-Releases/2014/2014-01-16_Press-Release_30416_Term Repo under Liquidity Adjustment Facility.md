## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a variable rate auction of 28 day term repo for a notified amount of `10,000 crore on January 17, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 28 day term repo will take place on February 14, 2014 (Friday).
