October 01, 2014 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 61.7511 on October 01, 2014. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 30, 2014) was 61.6135. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross- currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 30, 2014 October 01, 2014** **1 EUR** 78.2060 77.9546 **1 GBP** 100.2760 100.0677 **100 YEN** 56.36 56.24 **Note :** The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.
