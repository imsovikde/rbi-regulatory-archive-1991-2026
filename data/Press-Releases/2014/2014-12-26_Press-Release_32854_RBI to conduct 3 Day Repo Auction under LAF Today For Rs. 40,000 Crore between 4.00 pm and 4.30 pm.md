## RBI to conduct 3 Day Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 40,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs. 40,000 crore today (December 26, 2014, Friday). The auction will be conducted between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day repo will take place on December 29, 2014 (Monday).
