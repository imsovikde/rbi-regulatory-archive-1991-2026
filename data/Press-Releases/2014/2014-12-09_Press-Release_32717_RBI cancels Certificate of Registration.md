December 9, 2014 **RBI cancels Certificate of Registration** The Reserve Bank of India have cancelled the certificate of registration of the following non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 as the companies have opted to exit from the business of non-banking financial institution.

|Sr. No.|Company's Name|Address of Registered Office|Certificate of Registration No. & Date|Date of cancellation|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|Rajasthan State Industrial Development & Investment Corporation Ltd.|Udyog Bhawan, Tilak Marg, Jaipur (Rajasthan)-302 005|B-10.00147, December 13, 2000|September 9, 2014|
|2.|Mentor India Ltd.|B-9, Mentor House, Govind Marg, Sethi Colony, Jaipur (Rajasthan)-302 004|B-10.00074, December 30, 2004|September 11, 2014|

Following the cancellation of registration certificate, the companies cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as laid down under clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
