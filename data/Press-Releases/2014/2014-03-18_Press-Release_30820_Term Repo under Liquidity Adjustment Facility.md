## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has decided to conduct a 7 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `10,000 crore on March 19, 2014 (Wednesday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 7 day term repo will take place on March 26, 2014 (Wednesday).
