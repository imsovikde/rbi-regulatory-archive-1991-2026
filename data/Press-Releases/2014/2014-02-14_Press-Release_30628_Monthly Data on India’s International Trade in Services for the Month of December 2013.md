## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of December 2013

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of November 2013 were released on January 15, 2014.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of December 2013 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** **(US$ Million)** **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)** October 2013 12,563 6,964 November 2013 12,317 6,117 December 2013 12,869 6,725

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
