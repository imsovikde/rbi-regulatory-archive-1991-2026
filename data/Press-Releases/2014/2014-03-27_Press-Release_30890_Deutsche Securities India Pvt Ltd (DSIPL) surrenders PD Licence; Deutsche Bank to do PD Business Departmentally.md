## March 27, 2014

## Deutsche Securities India Pvt Ltd (DSIPL) surrenders PD Licence;

## Deutsche Bank to do PD Business Departmentally

In view of the request received from Deutsche Securities India Pvt. Ltd. (DSIPL) to surrender its authorisation for conducting Primary Dealer (PD) business and another request by Deutsche Bank A.G, to commence PD business as one of its departments, it has been decided to cancel the PD authorisation of DSIPL with effect from the close of business of March 28, 2014 and grant approval to Deutsche Bank AG to start PD business departmentally with effect from April 1, 2014.

The cancellation of PD authorisation of DSIPL and entry of Deutsche Bank AG as a Bank PD has been effected in terms of para 18 and para 1.3, respectively, of the Master Circular RBI/2013-14/102 IDMD.PDRD.01/03.64.00/2013-14 dated July 01,

2013.
