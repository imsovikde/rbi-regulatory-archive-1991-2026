## July 3, 2014

## Shri H. R. Khan re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor for Two years

The Government of India today re-appointed Shri Harun Rashid Khan as the Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank of India, for a further period of two years with effect from July 4, 2014 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.

Shri Khan was <u>appointed as Deputy Governor on July 01, 2011</u> for a period of three years from the date of his taking charge or until further orders, whichever was earlier. Shri Khan had taken charge as Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank on July 04, 2011.
