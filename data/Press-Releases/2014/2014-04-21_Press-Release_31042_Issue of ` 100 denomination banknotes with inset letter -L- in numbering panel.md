## April 21, 2014

**Issue of** ` **100 denomination banknotes** **with inset letter "L" in numbering panel**

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue `100 denomination Banknotes incorporating " ` " symbol on the obverse and reverse, with inset letter "L" in both the numbering panels, in the Mahatma Gandhi Series-2005 bearing the signature of Dr. Raghuram G Rajan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India, and the year of printing '2014' printed on the reverse of the Banknote.

The design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respects to the ` 100 Banknotes in Mahatma Gandhi Series-2005 issued earlier.

All the Banknotes in the denomination of ` 100 issued by the Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender.
