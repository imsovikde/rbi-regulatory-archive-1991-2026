## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: August 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the data on India’s International Trade in Services for August 2014. The value of exports and imports of services during August 2014 was:

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million) **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

July 2014 13,344 6,822 August 2014 12,242 6,767

**Note**: Data are provisional.

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The data for July 2014 was released on September 15, 2014. The data being provisional would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released.
