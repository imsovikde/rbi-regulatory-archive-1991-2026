## December 17, 2014

**RBI to conduct Overnight Repo Auction under LAF Today for Rs. 50,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

In partial modification of the announcement made earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to conduct overnight repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs. 50,000 crore today (December 17, 2014, Wednesday) as against earlier announced notified amount of Rs. 40,000 crore. The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the overnight repo will take place on December 18, 2014 (Thursday).
