## January 23, 2014

## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a variable rate auction of 14 day term repo for a notified amount of Rs. 39,000 crore on January 24, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 14 day term repo will take place on February 07, 2014 (Friday).
