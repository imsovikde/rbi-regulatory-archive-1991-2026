## August 27, 2014

**th** **RBI launches 8 Round of Survey on** **Computer Software and Information Technology Enabled Services Exports**

The Reserve Bank of India has launched the <u>8th round</u> of its Survey on Computer Software and Information Technology Enabled Services (ITES) Exports. The reference period for the Survey is 2013-14.

Survey Schedule of the Survey for 2013-14 is required to be filled by the Software and ITES/BPO exporter companies. The soft form of this survey with in-built validation checks is available on the RBI website (<u>[http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_](http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_)</u> <u>ViewForms.aspx</u>) which can be duly filled-in, validated and <u>emailed</u>.

In case of any query / clarification, kindly contact: The Director, External Liabilities and Assets Statistics Division, Department of Statistics and Information Management (DSIM), Reserve Bank of India, C-8, 3rd floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (E), Mumbai-400 051. Phone 022-26578351/ 022-26578348, <u>Email</u>.

The Reserve Bank has been conducting the Survey annually since 2002-03. The survey results are used for compilation of balance of payments (BoP) statistics. The survey collects data on various aspects of computer services exports as well as exports of Information Technology Enabled Services (ITES) and Business Process Outsourcing (BPO).
