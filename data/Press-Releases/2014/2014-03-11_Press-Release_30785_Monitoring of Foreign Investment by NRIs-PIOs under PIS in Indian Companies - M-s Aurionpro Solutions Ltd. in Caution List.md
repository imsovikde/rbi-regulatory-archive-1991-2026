## Monitoring of Foreign Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS in Indian Companies :

## M/s Aurionpro Solutions Ltd. in Caution List

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign share holding in <u>M/s</u> <u>Aurionpro Solutions Ltd.</u> by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) has reached the trigger limit of 8 per cent. Hence, further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
