## Term Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has decided to conduct a 7 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 10,000 crore on June 20, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 7 day term repo auction will take place on June 27, 2014 (Friday).
