## Repurchase of Government Stocks on September 25, 2014

The Government of India had notified the repurchase of its securities through reverse auction for an aggregate amount of `20,000 crore (face value) on September 12, 2014. The first tranche of reverse auction was conducted on September 16, 2014 wherein an amount of `12,761.416 crore was accepted. It has been decided to conduct second tranche of reverse auction on September 25, 2014 for an aggregate amount of `8,000 crore (face value) the security-wise details of which are given as under:

## Nomenclature Date of Maturity

|Sr No|||
|---|---|---|
|1|7.32% GS 2014|20-Oct-2014|
|2|7.56% GS 2014|3-Nov-2014|
|3|6.49% GS 2015|8-Jun-2015|
|4|7.17% GS 2015|14-Jun-2015|
|5|7.38% GS 2015 (conv)|3-Sep-2015|

The repurchase by the Government of India will be undertaken to prematurely redeem the Government Stocks by utilizing surplus cash balances. The above repurchase of the Government Stocks is purely *adhoc* in nature.

Auction for securities will be on price based auction format. The auctions will <u>be conducted using multiple price method</u>. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on September 25, 2014 (Thursday) between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon. The result of the auctions will be announced on the same day.

## RBI reserves the right to:

Ø Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities. Ø Accept less than the aggregate amount of `8,000 crore. Ø Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding off effects. Ø Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially without assigning any reason.
