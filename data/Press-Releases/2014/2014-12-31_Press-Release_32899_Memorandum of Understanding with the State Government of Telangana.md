## December 31, 2014

## Memorandum of Understanding with the State Government of Telangana

The Government of Telangana and the Reserve Bank of India have, on December 30, 2014, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with regard to Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs) in the State of Telangana. Consequent upon this, a State level Task Force for Urban Co-operative Banks (TAFCUB) will be constituted for Telangana. The TAFCUB is entrusted with the task of identifying the potentially viable UCBs and drawing up a time-bound action plan for their revival by setting specific monitorable milestones. The Regional Director for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, Reserve Bank of India, Hyderabad, will be the Chairman of the TAFCUB and Registrar of Co-operative Societies, Government of Telangana would be its Co- Chairman. The other members of the TAFCUB include a representative each from the National Federation for Urban Co-op. Banks (NAFCUB) and the body representing the Urban Co-op Banks in the State of Telangana, a nominee of State Government of Telangana and a representative from the Department of Co-operative Bank Supervision, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai. The first meeting of the TAFCUB will be held shortly.

It may be mentioned that the Reserve Bank of India had, on June 27, 2005, entered into a MoU with the State Government of Andhra Pradesh. The MoU now entered into with the State Government of Telangana has been necessitated consequent upon the bifurcation of the State of Andhra Pradesh into Telangana and Andhra Pradesh on June 2, 2014 as per the provisions of the Andhra Pradesh Reorganization Act, 2014.

Similar MoUs have earlier been signed with the Governments of all other States and the Central Government. With this, all the UCBs in the country are now covered under the MoU.
