## RBI to conduct 7-dayTerm Repo Variable Rate Auction of 15,000 crore under LAF

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has decided to conduct a 7 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 15,000 crore on August 13, 2014 (Wednesday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 7 day term repo auction will take place on August 20, 2014 (Wednesday).
