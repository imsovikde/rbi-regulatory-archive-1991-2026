## Quarterly Data on Weighted Average Lending Rates (WALR) of Banks

The Reserve Bank of India has been releasing information on rupee lending rates of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs) (excluding RRBs) on a quarterly basis since June 2002 based on Special Returns submitted by banks. These data include base rates and interest rates on cash credit, demand loans, term loans, pre-shipment credit and post-shipment credit.

On July 3, 2013, the Reserve Bank also released annual time-series data on the Weighted Average Lending Rate (WALR) of all SCBs (including RRBs) on their outstanding rupee loan portfolio based on *Basic Statistical Return 1 – Part A*.

It has now been decided to release high frequency data on WALR of the SCBs (excluding RRBs) on a quarterly basis, both on outstanding rupee loans and fresh rupee loans sanctioned by banks, which are as follows:
