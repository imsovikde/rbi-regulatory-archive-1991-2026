## July 31, 2014

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment in Indian Companies under PIS:

## M/s Persistent Systems Ltd. - Incorporation in Ban List

The Reserve Bank of India today advised that the foreign shareholding by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) in <u>M/s Persistent Systems Ltd</u>. has crossed the limit of 24% of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs/RFPIs.
