## June 17, 2014

**Three Divisions of RBI’s Foreign Exchange Department to shift to New Delhi from July 15, 2014**

The Reserve Bank of India has today informed banks and other stakeholders that from July 15, 2014, three divisions of Foreign Investment Division (FID) will shift to New Delhi Regional Office of the Reserve Bank of India. The divisions are Liaison/Branch/Project Office (LO/BO/PO) Division, Non Resident Foreign Account Division (NRFAD) and Immovable Property (IP) Division. The address of the FED, CO Cell is:

FED, CO Cell Foreign Exchange Department Reserve Bank of India New Delhi Regional Office 6, Parliament Street New Delhi - 110 001 India

The new FED, CO Cell will be headed by Shri P. Shimrah, General Manager who can be contacted on the official <u>email.</u>

th The divisions are at present located at 11 Floor, FED, Central Office Building, Mumbai 400001. The divisions will continue to be part of Foreign Exchange Department, Central Office, Mumbai.
