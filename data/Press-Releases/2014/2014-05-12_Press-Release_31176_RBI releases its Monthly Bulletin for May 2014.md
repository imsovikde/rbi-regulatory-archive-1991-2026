## May 12, 2014

## RBI releases its Monthly Bulletin for May 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>May 2014</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin contains regular features, *viz*., current statistics and speeches of the top management including a speech by the Governor, Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan titled “<u>Competitive Monetary Easing: Is it yesterday once more?</u>”
