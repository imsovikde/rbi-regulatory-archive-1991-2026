September 18, 2014 **Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility** As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, following term repo auctions will be conducted during the reporting fortnight ending on October 3, 2014 (Friday):

|Sl.No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|||(` Crore)|(days)||
|1|September 19, 2014|16,500|12|October 1, 2014|
|2|September 23, 2014|15,500|14|October 7, 2014|
|3|September 26, 2014|15,500|14|October 10, 2014|
|4|September 30, 2014|15,000|14|October 14, 2014|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
