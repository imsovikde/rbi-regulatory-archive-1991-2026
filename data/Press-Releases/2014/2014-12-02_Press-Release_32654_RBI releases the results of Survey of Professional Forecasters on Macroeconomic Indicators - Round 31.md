## RBI releases the results of Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 31

The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the results of the <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators- (Round 31)</u> conducted during November 2014.

The results of the Survey represent views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
