## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, following term repo auctions will be conducted during the reporting fortnight ending on November 14, 2014 (Friday):

|Sl.No.||Date of Auction|Notified|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
||||Amount (` Crore)|(days)||
|1|October 31, 2014 (Friday)||16,500|14|November 14, 2014|
|2|November 03, 2014 (Monday)||15,500|15|November 18, 2014|
|3|November 07, 2014 (Friday)||15,500|14|November 21, 2014|
|4|November 11, 2014 (Tuesday)||15,000|14|November 25, 2014|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
