## RBI releases Data on India’s Invisibles for Third and Fourth Quarter

## (October-December and January-March) of 2013-14

The Reserve Bank today released <u>data on India’s invisibles</u> as per the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) format for October-December 2013 and January-March 2014 and revised data for April-June 2013 and July-September 2013.
