## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 61.8750 on December 09, 2014.

61.9253. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
