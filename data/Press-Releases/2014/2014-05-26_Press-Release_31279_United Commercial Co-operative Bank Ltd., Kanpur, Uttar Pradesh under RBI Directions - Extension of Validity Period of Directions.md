## May 26, 2014

**United Commercial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh under RBI Directions-Extension of Validity Period of Directions**

It is hereby notified for the information of the public that the validity period of directions imposed on United Commercial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh as on May 24, 2013 has been extended further as per modified directive for a period of six months from June 1, 2014 to November 30, 2014, subject to review. The other terms and conditions remain unchanged. A copy of the directive dated May 12, 2014 notifying the above modification is displayed at the bank’s premises for perusal of the public.
