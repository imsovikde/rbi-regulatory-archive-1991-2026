April 15, 2014 **Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of February 2014**

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of January 2014 were released on March 14, 2014. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of February 2014 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** (US$ Million) <u>Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)</u> January 2014 13,927 7,268 February 2014 13,155 6,386

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
