## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has decided to conduct variable rate auctions of (i) 7 day term repo for a notified amount of ` 15,000 crores and (ii) 14 day term repo for a notified amount of ` 60,000 crores on May 02, 2014 (Friday). Both the auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 7 day and 14 day term repos will take place on May 9, 2014 (Friday) and May 16, 2014 (Friday) respectively.
