August 5, 2014 **Reserve Bank releases the results of forward-looking Surveys** The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey- Q1: 2014-15 (Round 66)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS)</u>
<u>- January-March 2014 (25th Round)</u>
iii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey: June 2014</u> iv. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households: June 2014</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – 29th</u> <u>Round: July 2014</u> The results are based on the feedback received from the Survey-respondents
and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
