## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, following term repo auctions will be conducted during the reporting fortnight ending on October 17, 2014 (Friday):

|Sl.No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|October 01, 2014|(Rs.Crore) 16,000|(days) 16|October 17, 2014|
|2|October 07, 2014|15,500|14|October 21, 2014|
|3|October 10, 2014|15,500|12|October 22, 2014|
|4|October 14, 2014|15,000|14|October 28, 2014|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
