## January 8, 2014

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the

## Portfolio Investment Scheme in Indian Companies -

## Incorporation in Caution List :

## M/s Jubilant FoodWorks Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>M/s Jubilant FoodWorks Limited</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in primary/secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Hence, further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
