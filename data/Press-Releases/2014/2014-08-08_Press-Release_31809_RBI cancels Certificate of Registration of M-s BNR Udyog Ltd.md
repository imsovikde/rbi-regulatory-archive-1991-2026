## August 8, 2014

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of M/s BNR Udyog Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the certificate of registration of the following non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

|Sr.|Company's Name|Address of Registered|Certificate of|Date of|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.||Office|Registration No. & Date|cancellation|
|||nd|||
|1.|M/s BNR Udyog Ltd.|6-3-650, 2 Floor, Maheshwari Chambers, Somajiguda, Hyderabad- 500082|09.00067 March 5, 1998|July 11, 2014|

Following cancellation of registration certificate, the company cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institution as laid down under clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
