## Special Drawing Facility to State Governments -Change in

## Nomenclature of existing Special Ways and Means Advances

It has been decided, in consultation with the State Governments, to change the nomenclature of Special Ways and Means Advances granted to the State Governments under Section 17(5) of the Reserve Bank of India Act 1934, as Special Drawing Facility. This nomenclature change will come into effect from June 23, 2014. Other terms and conditions of the Schemes of Special Ways and Means Advances, Normal Ways and Means Advances and Over Draft to the State Governments would remain unchanged.
