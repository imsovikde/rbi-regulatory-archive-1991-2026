## September 12, 2014

## Repurchase of Government Stocks

The Government of India have notified the repurchase of its securities through reverse auction for an aggregate amount of `20,000 crore (face value). The security- wise details of the repurchase are given as under:

## Nomenclature

|Sr. No.||Date of Maturity|
|---|---|---|
|1|7.32% GS 2014|October 20, 2014|
|2|7.56% GS 2014|November 3, 2014|
|3|6.49% GS 2015|June 8, 2015|
|4|7.17% GS 2015|June 14, 2015|
|5|7.38% GS 2015 (conv)|September 3, 2015|

The repurchase by the Government of India will be undertaken to prematurely redeem the Government Stocks by utilizing surplus cash balances. The above repurchase of the Government Stocks is purely *adhoc* in nature.

Auction for securities will be on price based auction format. The auctions will <u>be conducted using multiple price method</u>. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on September 16, 2014 (Tuesday) between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon. The result of the auctions will be announced on the same day.

## RBI reserves the right to:

Ø Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities. Ø Accept less than the aggregate amount of `20,000 crore. Ø Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding off effects. Ø Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially without assigning any reason.
