## Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during April-September 2014

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released the Balance of Payments (BoP) data for July-September 2014 on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during April- September 2014 have been compiled.

On balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), the foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 18.1 billion during April-September 2014 as against a decline of US$ 10.7 billion during April-September 2013. The foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including the valuation effects) increased by US$ 9.6 billion during April-September 2014 as against a decline of US$ 14.8 billion during the same period of preceding year (Table 2).
