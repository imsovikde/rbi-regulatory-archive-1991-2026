## Shut period in respect of SGL Accounts and Stock Certificates

Following the recommendations of the Working Group on Enhancing Liquidity in the G-sec and Interest Rate Derivatives Markets (Chairman: Shri R. Gandhi), it has been decided to revise the shut period for trading and settlement in G-sec held in SGL Account and Stock certificates as under:

|Sr. No.|Particulars|Shut Period (Existing)|Shut Period (Revised)|
|---|---|---|---|
|1.|SGL Accounts|One working day prior to coupon and redemption dates.|- No shut period prior to coupon date-One working day prior to redemption date.|
|2.|Stock|One week|Four working days|

Certificates

The revised shut period in respect of SGL Account and Stock Certificates will come into force with effect from May 12, 2014.
