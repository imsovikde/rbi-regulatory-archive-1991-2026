## January 9, 2014

## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct variable rate auctions of (i) 7 day term repo for a notified amount of ` 10,000 crore and (ii) 14 day term repo for a notified amount of ` 39,000 crore on January 10, 2014 (Friday). The auctions will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 7 day and 14 day term repos will take place on January 17, 2014 (Friday) and January 24, 2014 (Friday) respectively.
