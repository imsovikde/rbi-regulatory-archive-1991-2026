## RBI to conduct 2 day Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 30,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs. 30,000 crore today (December 24, 2014, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day repo will take place on December 26, 2014 (Friday).
