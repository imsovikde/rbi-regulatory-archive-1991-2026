## March 13, 2014

**st** **21 Half Yearly Report on Management of Foreign** **Exchange Reserves – April–September, 2013** <u>st</u> The Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>21</u> half yearly report on <u>management of foreign exchange reserves with reference to September, 2013</u>.

The position of foreign exchange reserve as on February 28, 2014 is as under:

US $ Billion Foreign Exchange Reserves 294.36 <u>FCA 266.90</u> <u>Gold 20.98</u> <u>SDR 4.47</u> <u>RTP 2.01</u>

It may be recalled that in February, 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the domain for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure.
