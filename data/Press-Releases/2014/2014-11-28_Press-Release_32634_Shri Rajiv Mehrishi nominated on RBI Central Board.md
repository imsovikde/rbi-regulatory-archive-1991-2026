## Shri Rajiv Mehrishi nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Shri Rajiv Mehrishi, Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi as a Director on the Central Board of Directors of Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Arvind Mayaram. Shri Mehrishi’s nomination is effective November 25, 2014 and until further orders.
