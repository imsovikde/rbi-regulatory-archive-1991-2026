## RBI to conduct 3 day Reverse Repo Auction

**of** ` **10,000 crore under LAF Today**

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 3 day reverse repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 10,000 crore today (September 12, 2014, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 3.00 PM and 3.30 PM as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day reverse repo will take place on September 15, 2014 (Monday).
