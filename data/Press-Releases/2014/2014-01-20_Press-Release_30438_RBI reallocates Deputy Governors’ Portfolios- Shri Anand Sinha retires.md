## RBI reallocates Deputy Governors’ Portfolios:

## Shri Anand Sinha retires

The Reserve Bank of India has reallocated the portfolios among the three Deputy Governors from Monday, January 20, 2014. The reallocation was necessitated consequent upon Shri Anand Sinha relinquishing his charge as Deputy Governor. The reallocation of portfolios is: **Dr. K.C. Chakrabarty**

1. Customer Service Department
2. Department of Banking Supervision
3. Department of Currency Management
4. Financial Stability Unit
5. Human Resources Management Department
6. Rural Planning & Credit Department
7. Secretary's Department
8. Premises Department
9. Urban Banks Department
10. Legal Department
11. Co-ordination Work
## Shri H.R. Khan

1. Central Security Cell
2. Department of External Investments & Operations
3. Department of Government & Bank Accounts
4. Department of Payment & Settlement Systems
5. Foreign Exchange Department
6. Internal Debt Management Department
7. Inspection Department
8. Department of Banking Operations & Development
9. Department of Non-Banking Supervision
10. Department of Information Technology
