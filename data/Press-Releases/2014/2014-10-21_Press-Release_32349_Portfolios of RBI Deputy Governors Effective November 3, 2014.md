October 21, 2014 **Portfolios of RBI Deputy Governors Effective November 3, 2014** Consequent on reorganisation of the departments/work areas in the Reserve Bank of India effective November 3, 2014, the portfolios of the Deputy Governors will be redistributed as under:

## Name Cluster/Departments Shri H. R. Khan Financial Markets & Infrastructure

1. Coordination work
2. Department of External Investments and Operations (DEIO)
3. Department of Government and Bank Accounts (DGBA)
4. Department of Information Technology (DIT)
5. Department of Payment and Settlement Systems (DPSS)
6. Financial Markets Regulation Department including Market Intelligence (FMRD/MI)
7. Foreign Exchange Department (FED)
8. Internal Debt Management Department (IDMD)
9. Premises Department (PD)
10. Secretary’s Department (SD)
## Dr. Urjit R. Patel Monetary Policy & Research

1. Department of Communication (DoC)
2. Department of Economic and Policy Research (DEPR)
3. Department of Statistics and Information Management (including Data and Information Management Unit) (DSIM/DIMU)
4. Financial Market Operations Department (FMOD)
5. International Department (Intl. D)
6. Monetary Policy Department including Forecasting & Modelling Unit (MPD/MU)
7. Central Security Cell (CSC)
8. Corporate Strategy and Budget Department (CSBD)
9. Department of Corporate Services including Document Management System (DCS/DMS)

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

## Shri R. Gandhi Regulation & Risk Management

1. Department of Banking Regulation (DBR)
2. Department of Co-operative Banking Regulation (DCBR)
3. Department of Non-Banking Regulation (DNBR)
4. Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC)
5. Financial Stability Unit (FSU)
6. Inspection Department (ID)
7. Risk Monitoring Department (RMD)
8. Department of Currency Management (DCM)
9. Legal Department (LD)
## Shri S. S. Mundra Supervision & Inclusion

1. Consumer Education and Protection Department (CEPD)
2. Department of Banking Supervision (DBS)
3. Department of Co-operative Banking Supervision (DCBS)
4. Department of Non-Banking Supervision (DNBS)
5. Financial Inclusion and Development Department (FIDD)
6. Human Resource Management Department including Central Administrative Processing Unit (HRMD/CAPU)
7. Rajbhasha Department (RD)
8. Right to Information (RIA Division)
It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India is currently restructuring some of its departments as part of its reorganisation exercise.
