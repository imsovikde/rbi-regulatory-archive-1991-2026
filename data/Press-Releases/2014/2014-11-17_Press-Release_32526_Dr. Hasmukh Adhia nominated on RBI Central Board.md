## Dr. Hasmukh Adhia nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Dr. Hasmukh Adhia, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi as a director on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Gurdial Singh Sandhu. Dr. Hasmukh Adhia’s nomination is with effect from November 11, 2014 and until further orders.
