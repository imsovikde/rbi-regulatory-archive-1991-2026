## Market Borrowings of State Governments and Puducherry to be

**around** ` **50,000 crore to** ` **55,000 crore for July-September 2014: RBI**

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), in consultation with the State Governments, has announced that the indicative quantum of total market borrowings by the State Governments and the Union Territory of Puducherry, for the quarter July to September 2014, in the range of ` 50,000 to ` 55,000 crore.

The amount will be raised through auction of State Development Loans (SDLs) generally on the second and fourth Tuesdays of the month. The Reserve Bank would endeavour to conduct the auctions in a calibrated manner and evenly distribute the borrowings throughout the quarter. The exact amount of borrowings would be intimated by way of press release two/three days prior to the actual auction day and they would depend on the requirements of the State Governments, approval from the Government of India under Article 293(3) of the Constitution of India and the market conditions.
