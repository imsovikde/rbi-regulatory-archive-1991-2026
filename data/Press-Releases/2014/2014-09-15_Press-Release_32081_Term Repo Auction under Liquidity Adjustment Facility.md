# Term Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on August 22, 2014, Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `15,000 crore on September 16, 2014 (Tuesday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 14 day term repo will take place on September 30, 2014 (Tuesday).
