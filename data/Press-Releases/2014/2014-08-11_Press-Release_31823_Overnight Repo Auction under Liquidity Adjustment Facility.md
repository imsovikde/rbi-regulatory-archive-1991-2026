## Overnight Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a one day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 5,000 crore today (August 11, 2014, Monday). The auction will be conducted between 2.30PM-3.00PM as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the overnight repo will take place on August 12, 2014 (Tuesday).
