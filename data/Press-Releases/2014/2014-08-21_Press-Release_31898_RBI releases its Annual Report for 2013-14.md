## RBI releases its Annual Report for 2013-14

The Reserve Bank of India today released its <u>Annual Report for 2013-14</u>, a statutory Report of its Central Board of Directors. It covers (i) the assessment of the macroeconomic performance during 2013-14 and the prospects for 2014-15 along with some short to medium term issues (ii) the working and operations of the Reserve Bank and its financial accounts for the year 2013-14. The Report also provides for the first time a chapter on the Reserve Bank's vision and agenda for 2014-15.
