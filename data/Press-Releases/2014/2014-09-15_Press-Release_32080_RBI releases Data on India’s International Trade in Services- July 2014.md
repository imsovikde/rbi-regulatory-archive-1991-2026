## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: July 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the data on India’s International Trade in Services for July 2014. The value of exports and imports of services during July 2014 was:

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|April 2014|13,632|8,058|
|May 2014|13,920|8,027|
|June 2014|12,972|7,194|
|July 2014|13,344|6,822|

**Note**: Data are provisional.

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The data for June 2014 was released on August 14, 2014. The data being provisional would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released.
