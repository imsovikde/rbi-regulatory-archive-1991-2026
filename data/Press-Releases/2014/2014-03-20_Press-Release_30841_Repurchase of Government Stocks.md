## Repurchase of Government Stocks

The Government of India have notified the repurchase of its Securities through reverse auction for an aggregate amount of ` 5,000 crore (face value). The security- wise details of the repurchase are given as under:

|Sr. No.||Date of Maturity|
|---|---|---|
|1|6.07% Government Stock 2014|May 15, 2014|
|2|10.00% Government Stock 2014|May 30, 2014|
|3|7.32% Government Stock 2014|October 20, 2014|
|4|10.50% Government Stock 2014|October 29, 2014|
|5|7.56% Government Stock 2014|November 3, 2014|
|6|11.83% Government Stock 2014|November 12, 2014|
|7|10.47% Government Stock 2015|February 12, 2015|

## Nomenclature

The repurchase by the Government of India will be undertaken to prematurely redeem the Government Stocks by utilizing its surplus cash balances. The above repurchase of the Government Stocks is purely *ad hoc* in nature.

Auction for securities will be on price based auction format. The auctions will be conducted using multiple price method. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on March 24, 2014 (Monday) between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon. The result of the auctions will be announced on the same day and the settlement will take place on March 25, 2014 (Tuesday)

## RBI reserves the right to:

· Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities.

· Accept less than the aggregate amount of ` 5,000 crore.

· Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding off effects.

· Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially without assigning any reason.
