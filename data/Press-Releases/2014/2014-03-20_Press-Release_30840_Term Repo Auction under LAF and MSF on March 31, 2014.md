## Term Repo Auction under LAF and MSF on March 31, 2014

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct a 14-day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `40,000 crore on March 21, 2014 (Friday). The reversal of the 14-day term repo will take place on April 4, 2014 (Friday).

Further, in order to facilitate smooth and non-disruptive conduct of banking operations during the annual closing of accounts for the financial year 2013-14, the Reserve Bank has also decided:

a) to conduct a 5-day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `20,000 crore on March 28, 2014 (Friday) with reversal on April 2, 2014 (Wednesday). As a one-time measure, standalone primary dealers will be allowed to participate in the auction to be conducted on March 28, 2014 within the notified amount of `20,000 crore. Both the term repo auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
b) to offer funds to the banks through the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) on March 31, 2014 (Monday) with reversal on April 2, 2014 (Wednesday). The MSF window will remain open between 5.00 and 5.30 pm on March 31, 2014.
