## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 39,000 crore on March 7, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on February 13, 2014. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 14 day term repo will take place on March 21, 2014 (Friday).
