## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 13-day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 60,000 crores on April 4, 2014 (Friday). The reversal of the 13- day term repo will take place on April 17, 2014 (Thursday).

Further, on an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, Reserve Bank has also decided to conduct a 3-day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 20,000 crores on April 4, 2014 (Friday) with reversal on April 7, 2014 (Monday).

Both the term repo auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
