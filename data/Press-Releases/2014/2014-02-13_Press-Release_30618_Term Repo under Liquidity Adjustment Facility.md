## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 28 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `20,000 crore on February 14, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on March 14, 2014 (Friday).
