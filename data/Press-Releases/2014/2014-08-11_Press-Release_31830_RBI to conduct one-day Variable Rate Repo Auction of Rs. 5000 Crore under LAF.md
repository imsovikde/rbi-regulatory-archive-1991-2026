## RBI to conduct one-day Variable Rate Repo Auction of Rs. 5000 Crore under LAF

On an assessment of the prevailing liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to conduct a one-day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore on August 12, 2014 (Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 10.00 am -10.30 am as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the overnight repo will take place on August 13, 2014 (Wednesday).
