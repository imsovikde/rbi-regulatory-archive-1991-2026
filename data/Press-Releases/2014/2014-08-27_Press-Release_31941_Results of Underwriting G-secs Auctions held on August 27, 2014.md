## Results of Underwriting G-secs Auctions held on August 27, 2014

In the underwriting auctions conducted on August 27, 2014 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

(` crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ` 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.27 per cent GS 2020|2000|1000|1000|2000|0.29|
|8.40 per cent GS 2024|6000|3000|3000|6000|0.34|
|8.32 per cent GS 2032|2000|1000|1000|2000|0.44|
|9.23 per cent GS 2043|2000|1000|1000|2000|0.44|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 28, 2014.
