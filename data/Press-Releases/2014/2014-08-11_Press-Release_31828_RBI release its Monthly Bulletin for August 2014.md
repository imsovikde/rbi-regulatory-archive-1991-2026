## August 11, 2014

## RBI release its Monthly Bulletin for August 2014

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>August 2014</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes a special article on <u>India’s Foreign Trade:</u> <u>2013-14</u>.

## Highlights:

India’s trade performance improved during 2013-14. Modest pick-up in some trade partner economies and depreciation of the rupee helped India’s exports grow in Q2 and Q3 of 2013-14. Imports also moderated since June 2013, largely driven by gold imports and fall in non-oil non-gold imports, reflecting the slowdown in domestic economic activities and fall in international prices of some commodities (*e.g,* metal). This led to a significant correction in India’s trade balance in 2013-14. Major highlights of India’s trade performance during 2013-14 are set out below:

- Indian merchandise exports grew by 4.1 per cent to US$ 312.6 billion in 2013-14 as against a decline of 1.8 per cent at US$ 300.4 billion in 2012-
13.
- Disaggregated commodity-wise data show that the rise in total exports in 2013-14 can be primarily attributed to the turnaround in the exports of manufactured goods, particularly, engineering goods and textile products.
- Destination-wise data reflect that the improved export performance in 2013-14 could be attributed to a renewed export demand from some of the major trade partners, particularly; Belgium, Germany, Italy, UK, USA, Japan, China and Hong Kong.
- India’s merchandise imports at US$ 450.1 billion recorded a decline of 8.3 per cent in 2013-14 as compared with a marginal increase of 0.3 per cent in 2012-13.
- Apart from a decline in gold and silver by about 42 per cent, the import decline during 2013-14 could be attributed to lower imports of capital goods, iron & steel, metalliferous ores and metal scraps, coal and fertilisers. POL import growth also decelerated sharply to 0.7 per cent in 2013-14.
