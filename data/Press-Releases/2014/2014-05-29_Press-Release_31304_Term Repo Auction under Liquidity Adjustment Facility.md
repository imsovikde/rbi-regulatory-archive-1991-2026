## Term Repo Auction under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 60,000 crores on May 30, 2014 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on February 13, 2014. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the 14 day term repo will take place on June 13, 2014 (Friday).
