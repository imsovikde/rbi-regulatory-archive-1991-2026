## December 01, 2014

**th** **RBI launches the 10 Round of Survey on** **Foreign Collaboration in Indian Industry**

The Reserve Bank of India has been conducting the survey on Foreign Collaboration in Indian Industry (FCS) since 1965. The survey collects information on the operations of the Indian companies having foreign participation/technical <u>th</u> collaboration with foreign companies. The <u>10 round of survey with 2012-13 to 2013-</u> <u>14</u> as a reference period is now being launched.

The <u>survey-schedule</u> is required to be filled-in by the Indian companies having technical collaboration with foreign companies. The soft form of this survey schedule with in-built validation checks is available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> –> Forms –> Survey) which can be dully filled-in and sent by <u>email</u>.
