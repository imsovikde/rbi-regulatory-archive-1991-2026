## August 11, 2022

## Re-nomination of Shri Satish Kashinath Marathe and Shri Swaminathan

## Gurumurthy as part-time, Non-official Directors on the Central Board of

## Reserve Bank of India

The Central Government has re-nominated Shri Satish Kashinath Marathe and Shri Swaminathan Gurumurthy as part-time, non-official Directors on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India for a further period of four years with effect from August 11, 2022, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
