The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) system on **January 18, 2022 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to 10% of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of 1% of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the <u>Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>. Individual investors can also place bids as per the non-competitive scheme through the Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).
