## June 16, 2022

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹32,000 crore on June 17, 2022

Government of India has announced the sale (issue/re-issue) of Government Stock detailed below through auctions to be held on June 17, 2022

As per the extant scheme of underwriting notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore)

|Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) amount per PD|Minimum bidding commitment per PD under ACU auction|
|---|---|---|---|
|New GS 2027|9,000|215|215|
|GOI FRB 2034|4,000|96|96|
|7.54% GS 2036|10,000|239|239|
|6.99% GS 2051|9,000|215|215|

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **June 17, 2022 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **09.00 A.M.** and **09.30**

**A.M.** on the date of underwriting auction. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the date of issue of securities.
