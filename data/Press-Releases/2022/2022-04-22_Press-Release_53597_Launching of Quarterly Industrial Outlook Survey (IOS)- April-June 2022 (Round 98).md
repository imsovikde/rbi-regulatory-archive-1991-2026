## April 22, 2022

## Launching of Quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS):

## April-June 2022 (Round 98)

The Reserve Bank has launched the <u>98</u> <u>th</u> <u>round</u> of the quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) of the Indian manufacturing sector for the reference period April-June

2022. The survey assesses business sentiment for the current quarter (Q1:2022-23) and expectations for the ensuing quarter (Q2:2022-23), based on qualitative responses on a set of indicators pertaining to demand conditions, financial conditions, employment conditions and the price situation. The survey provides useful insight into the performance of the manufacturing sector. Owing to continued uncertainty because of the Covid-19 pandemic, an additional block has been included in this survey round for assessing the outlook on key parameters for the two subsequent quarters (Q3:2022-23 and Q4:2022-23).
2. The results of the <u>97</u>
<u>th</u> <u>round i.e., for Q4:2021-22</u> were released in public domain on April 8, 2022.

3. M/s Genesis Management & Market Research Pvt. Ltd. has been authorised to conduct the survey for April-June 2022 on behalf of the Reserve Bank. While the agency will approach selected companies, other manufacturing companies are also encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> from the Bank’s website, which is placed under the head ‘<u>Forms</u>’ (see ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the RBI Homepage) and the sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed as per contact details given therein.
4. Identity of the respondents is not revealed.
5. In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact us at the following address:
The Director, Division of Enterprise Surveys, Department of Statistics and Information Management, Reserve Bank of India, C-8, 2 nd Floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai-400051. Phone: 022-26578386, 022-26572197. Please <u>click here</u> to send email.
