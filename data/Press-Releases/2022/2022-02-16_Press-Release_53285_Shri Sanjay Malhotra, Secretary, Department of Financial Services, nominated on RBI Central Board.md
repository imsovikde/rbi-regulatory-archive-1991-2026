## February 16, 2022

## Shri Sanjay Malhotra, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Shri Sanjay Malhotra, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India as a Director on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Debasish Panda. The nomination of Shri Sanjay Malhotra is effective from February 16, 2022 and until further orders.
