## October 12, 2022

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of four NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45- IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the Certificate of Registration of the following four companies.

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
