## June 24, 2022

## Award scheme for writing books originally in Hindi on

## Economics/Banking/Financial subjects

## Declaration of Result for the Year 2020-21

Reserve Bank of India had invited entries under the captioned scheme vide <u>press</u> <u>release dated 03 June 2021</u>. On evaluation of the entries received, it has been decided to jointly award Professor Renu Jatana, former Dean, Mohanlal Sukhadia University, Udaipur, Rajasthan and Dr. Sagar Sanwariya, Assistant Professor, Government Girl’s College, Nathdwara, Rajasthan under this scheme for their book “िवीय बंध".
