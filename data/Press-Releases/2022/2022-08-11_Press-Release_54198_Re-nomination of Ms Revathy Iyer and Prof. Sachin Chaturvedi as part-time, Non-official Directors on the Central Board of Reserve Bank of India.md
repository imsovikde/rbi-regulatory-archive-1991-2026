## August 11, 2022

## Re-nomination of Ms Revathy Iyer and Prof. Sachin Chaturvedi as part-time,

## Non-official Directors on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India

The Central Government has re-nominated Ms Revathy Iyer and Prof. Sachin Chaturvedi as part-time, non-official Directors on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India for a further period of four years after completion of their present tenure on September 18, 2022, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
