## June 02, 2022

## RBI releases data on ECB/FCCB/RDB for April 2022

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on <u>External Commercial</u> <u>Borrowings (ECB), Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB) and Rupee</u> <u>Denominated Bonds (RDB)</u> both, through Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the month of April 2022.
