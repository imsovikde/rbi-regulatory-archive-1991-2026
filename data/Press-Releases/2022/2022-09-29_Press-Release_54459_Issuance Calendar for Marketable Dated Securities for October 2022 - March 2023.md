## September 29, 2022

## Issuance Calendar for Marketable Dated Securities for October 2022 - March 2023

In order to enable institutional and retail investors to plan their investments efficiently and to provide transparency and stability to the Government Securities Market, Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has decided to notify the indicative calendar for issuance of Government dated securities for the second half of the fiscal year 2022-23 (October 01, 2022 to March 31, 2023).
