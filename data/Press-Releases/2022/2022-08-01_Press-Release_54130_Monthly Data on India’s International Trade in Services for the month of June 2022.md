## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the month of June 2022

## International Trade in Services

(US$ Million) **Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

|Month|||

**Notes**: (i) Data are provisional; and (ii) Figures in brackets are growth rates over the corresponding month of the previous year which have been revised on the basis of balance of payments statistics.

Monthly data on services are provisional and are likely to undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
