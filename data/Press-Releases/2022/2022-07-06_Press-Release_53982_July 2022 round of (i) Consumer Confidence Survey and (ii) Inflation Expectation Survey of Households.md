## July 06, 2022

## July 2022 round of (i) Consumer Confidence Survey and (ii) Inflation

## Expectation Survey of Households

In continuation of the press releases relating to the launch of July 2022 round of the <u>consumer confidence survey (CCS)</u> and <u>inflation expectation survey of</u> <u>households (IESH)</u>, both dated June 30, 2022, it is informed that M/s ‘Hansa Research Group Pvt.Ltd., Mumbai’ has now been engaged to conduct field work for July 2022 round of the two surveys on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India.

Other details relating to the survey as contained in the above-mentioned two press releases remain unchanged.
