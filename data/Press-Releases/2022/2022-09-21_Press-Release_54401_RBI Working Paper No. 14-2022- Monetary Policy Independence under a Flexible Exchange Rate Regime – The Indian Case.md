## September 21, 2022

## RBI Working Paper No. 14/2022: Monetary Policy Independence under a

## Flexible Exchange Rate Regime – The Indian Case

Today the Reserve Bank of India placed on its website a Working Paper titled “<u>Monetary Policy Independence under a Flexible Exchange Rate Regime – The</u> <u>Indian Case</u>” under the Reserve Bank of India Working Paper Series¹. The Paper is co-authored by Harpreet Singh Grewal and Pushpa Trivedi.

In an open economy, central banks confront the impossible challenge of simultaneously preserving exchange rate stability, conducting independent monetary policy, and benefitting from an open capital account.

Against this backdrop, the paper assesses whether the foreign exchange market interventions by the RBI to contain the volatility in INR have had any constraining influence on India’s monetary policy independence during 1991 to 2020.

The major findings of the paper are:

i) There is a high degree of sterilisation of the increase in money supply that results from forex market interventions in India. ii) Forex market interventions during surges in capital flows to contain the INR volatility lead to an increase in M₃, which however is found to be neither inflationary nor elicits a policy rate response by the RBI; this points to RBI’s monetary policy independence not facing much constraints from its exchange rate policy and financial openness.
