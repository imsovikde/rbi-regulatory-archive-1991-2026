## December 07, 2022

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i) <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – November 2022</u> ii) <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – November 2022</u> iii) <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round¹ 79</u>
<u>th</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Ajit Prasad

**Press Release: 2022-2023/1325** Director (Communications)

1 The results of the previous survey round were released on <u>September 30, 2022</u> on the Reserve Bank’s website.
