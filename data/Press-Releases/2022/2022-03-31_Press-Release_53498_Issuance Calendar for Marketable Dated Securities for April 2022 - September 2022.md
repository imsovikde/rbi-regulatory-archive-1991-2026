## March 31, 2022

## Issuance Calendar for Marketable Dated Securities for

## April 2022 - September 2022

In order to enable institutional and retail investors to plan their investments efficiently and provide transparency and stability to the Government Securities market, an indicative calendar for issuance of Government dated securities for the first half of the fiscal year 2022-23 (April 01, 2022 to September 30, 2022) has been prepared in consultation with the Government of India i.
