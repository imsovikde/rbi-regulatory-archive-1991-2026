## May 27, 2022

## Annual Report for the Year 2021-22

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released its <u>Annual Report for 2021-22</u>, a statutory report of its Central Board of Directors. The Report covers the working and functions of the Reserve Bank of India for the period April 2021 - March 2022.
