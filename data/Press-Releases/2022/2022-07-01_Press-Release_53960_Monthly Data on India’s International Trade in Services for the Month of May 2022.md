July 01, 2022 **Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of May 2022** The value of exports and imports of services during May 2022 is given in the following Table.

||International Trade in Services|(US$ Million)|
|---|---|---|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|January – 2022|21,625 (24.5)|13,237 (34.0)|
|February – 2022|21,302 (19.4)|12,971 (25.0)|

|April – 2022|22,594 (25.1)|14,052 (46.1)|

Monthly data for April-May are provisional and are likely to undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
