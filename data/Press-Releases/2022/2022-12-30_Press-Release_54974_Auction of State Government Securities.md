The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **January 03, 2023 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to Ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of One per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the Scheme <u>for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>. Individual investors can place bids as per the non-competitive scheme also through the Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **January 03, 2023** <u>(Tuesday). The competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and 11.30 A.M.</u> <u>and non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and 11.00 A.M.</u>
