November 04, 2022 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on November 04, 2022** In the underwriting auctions conducted on November 04, 2022 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|6.69% GS 2024|4,000|2,016|1,984|4,000|0.87|
|7.10% GS 2029|6,000|3,003|2,997|6,000|0.83|
|7.54% GS 2036|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.58|
|7.40% GS 2062|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|0.67|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on November 04, 2022.
