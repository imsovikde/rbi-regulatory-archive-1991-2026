## December 27, 2022

## RBI releases “Statistical Tables relating to Banks in India: 2021-22”

Today, the Reserve Bank released its web publication entitled ‘<u>Statistical Tables</u> <u>relating to Banks in India: 2021-22</u>’ covering the activities of the Indian banking sector.

The publication presents entity-wise information on major items of liabilities and assets along with their maturity profile; income and expenses; select financial ratios, number of employees and details of priority sector advances for scheduled commercial banks (excluding regional rural banks). In addition, capital to risk weighted asset ratios (CRARs), non-performing assets (NPAs), exposure to sensitive sectors, contingent liabilities and unclaimed deposits are included. State wise distribution of consolidated balance sheet of rural co-operative banks are also presented.

The publication can be accessed on the RBI website through the link ‘<u>Time Series</u> <u>Publications</u>’ under the Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>), where the time series on all variables have been updated up to 2021-22.
