March 31, 2022 **Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during April-December 2021**

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released <u>balance of payments (BoP) data</u> <u>for October-December 2021</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during April-December 2021 are detailed below. **Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves: April-December 2021** During April-December 2021, there was an increase in the foreign exchange reserves, the sources of which are set out in Table 1.

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 63.5 billion during April-December 2021 as compared with US$ 83.9 billion during April-December 2020. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including valuation effects) increased by US$ 56.6 billion during April-December 2021 as compared with US$ 108.0 billion in the corresponding period of the preceding year (<u>Table 2</u>).

The valuation loss, reflecting the appreciation of the US dollar against major currencies, amounted to US$ 6.9 billion during April-December 2021 as against a valuation gain of US$ 24.1 billion during April-December 2020.
