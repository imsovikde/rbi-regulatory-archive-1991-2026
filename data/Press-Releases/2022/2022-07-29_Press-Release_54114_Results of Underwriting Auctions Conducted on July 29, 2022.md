July 29, 2022 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on July 29, 2022** In the underwriting auctions conducted on July 29, 2022, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.38% GS 2027|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|0.22|
|GOI FRB 2028|4,000|2,016|1,984|4,000|3.90|
|7.54% GS 2036|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.22|
|6.99% GS 2051|8,000|4,011|3,989|8,000|0.23|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on July 29, 2022.
