September 16, 2022 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on September 16, 2022** In the underwriting auctions conducted on September 16, 2022 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|6.69% GS 2024|4,000|2,016|1,984|4,000|1.95|
|7.10% GS 2029|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|1.75|
|7.26% GS 2032|13,000|6,510|6,490|13,000|1.10|
|New GS 2062|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|1.33|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on September 16, 2022.
