## Meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee, November 3, 2022

A separate meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) was held on November 3, 2022 to discuss and draft the report to be sent to the Government by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the provisions of <u>Section 45ZN of the RBI Act,</u> <u>1934</u> and <u>Regulation 7 of RBI MPC and Monetary Policy Process Regulations, 2016</u>.

2. The meeting was chaired by Shri Shaktikanta Das, Governor and attended by all MPC members -Dr. Michael Debabrata Patra, Dr. Rajiv Ranjan, Dr. Shashanka Bhide, Dr. Ashima Goyal and Prof. Jayanth R. Varma.
