July 21, 2022 **Meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee – Rescheduling** As announced in the Reserve Bank’s <u>Press Release: 2021-2022/1931 dated</u> <u>March 30, 2022</u>, the next meeting of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) for the financial year 2022-23 is scheduled during August 2-4, 2022. Due to administrative exigencies, it has been decided to reschedule the MPC meeting from August 2-4, 2022 to August 3-5, 2022. This announcement is being made under Section 45ZI(4) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
