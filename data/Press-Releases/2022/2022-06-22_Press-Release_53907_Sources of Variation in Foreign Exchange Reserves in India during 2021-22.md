June 22, 2022 **Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during 2021-22**

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released <u>balance of payments (BoP) data</u> <u>for January-March 2022</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 47.5 billion during 2021-22 as compared with US$ 87.3 billion during 2020-21. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including valuation effects) increased by US$ 30.3 billion during 2021-22 as compared with US$ 99.2 billion during 2020-21 (<u>Table 2</u>).
