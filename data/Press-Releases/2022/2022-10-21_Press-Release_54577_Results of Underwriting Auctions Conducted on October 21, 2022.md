October 21, 2022 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on October 21, 2022** In the underwriting auctions conducted on October 21, 2022 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.38% GS 2027|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|0.58|
|7.26% GS 2032|12,000|6,006|5,994|12,000|0.58|
|7.36% GS 2052|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|0.77|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on October 21, 2022.
