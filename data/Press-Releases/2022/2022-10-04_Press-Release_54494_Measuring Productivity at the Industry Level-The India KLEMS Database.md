## October 04, 2022

## Measuring Productivity at the Industry Level-The India KLEMS Database

Today the Reserve Bank of India placed on its website an update on “<u>Measuring</u> <u>Productivity at the Industry Level-The India KLEMS [capital (K), Labour (L), Energy (E),</u> <u>Material (M) and services (S)] Database</u>” comprising the Data Manual 2021 along with <u>time-series data on productivity for 27 industries covering the period 1980-81 to 2019-20</u>.

The database, which has KLEMS data for an additional year, i.e., 2019-20, could aid the analysis of productivity trends in India at disaggregated industry level.

The <u>India Productivity Report</u> prepared by the India KLEMS team is also released along with the KLEMS dataset for 2019-20.
