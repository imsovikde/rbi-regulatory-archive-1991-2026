## November 10, 2022

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of two NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the Certificate of Registration of the following two companies.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Registered Office Address|CoR No.|CoR Issued on|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Grihastha Finance Limited|Shivpuri, Near Dhrampur High School, Tajpur Road, Samastipur, Bihar - 848101|A-15.00049|May 08, 2002|October 10, 2022|
|2|Pawan Motor and General Finance Private Limited|Office No. S-6, Second Floor, B-46, Sector-63, Noida, Gautam Buddha Nagar, Uttar Pradesh - 201301|B-12.00355|October 23, 2001|October 31, 2022|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
