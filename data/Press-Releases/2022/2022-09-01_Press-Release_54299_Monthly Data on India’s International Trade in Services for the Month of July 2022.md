September 01, 2022 **Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of July 2022**

The value of exports and imports of services during July 2022 is given in the following Table.

||International Trade in Services||
|---|---|---|
|||(US$ Million)|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|

Monthly data on services are provisional and are likely to undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
