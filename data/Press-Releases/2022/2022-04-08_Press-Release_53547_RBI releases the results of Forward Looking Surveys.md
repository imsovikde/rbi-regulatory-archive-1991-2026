## April 08, 2022

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – March 2022</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – March 2022</u>
iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q3:2021-22</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q4:2021-22</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–</u> <u>Round¹ 75th</u> vi. <u>Bank Lending Survey for Q4:2021-22</u>
vii. <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q4:2021-22</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
