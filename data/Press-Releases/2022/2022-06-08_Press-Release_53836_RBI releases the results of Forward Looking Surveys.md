## June 08, 2022

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – May 2022</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – May 2022</u>
iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators –</u> <u>Round¹ 76th</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Rupambara

**Press Release: 2022-2023/338** Director (Communications)

The results of the previous survey round were released on <u>April 08, 2022</u> on the Reserve Bank’s website.
