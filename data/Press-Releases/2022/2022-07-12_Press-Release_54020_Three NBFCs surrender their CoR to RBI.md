## July 12, 2022

## Three NBFCs surrender their CoR to RBI

The following three Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) have surrendered the Certificate of Registration (CoR) granted to them by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The RBI, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has therefore cancelled their CoR.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Registered Office Address|CoR No.|CoR Issued on|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|P.K.C. Credit Pvt. Ltd.|Flat No.18, Trimurti 4, Lower Rawdon Street, Kolkata, West Bengal- 700020|B.05.05093|May 30, 2003|June 01, 2022|
|2|Gajanand Lefin Pvt Ltd|5, Rani Rashmani Road, Kolkata, West Bengal- 700013|B.05.04393|September 18, 2001|June 02, 2022|
|3|Harita Malini Private Limited|No.29(8), Haddows Road, Chennai, Tamil Nadu-600006|B-07.00559|January 09, 2001|June 09, 2022|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
