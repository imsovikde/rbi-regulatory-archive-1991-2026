## November 15, 2022

## Shri Vivek Joshi, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Shri Vivek Joshi, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India as a Director on the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Sanjay Malhotra. The nomination of Shri Vivek Joshi is effective from November 15, 2022 and until further orders.
