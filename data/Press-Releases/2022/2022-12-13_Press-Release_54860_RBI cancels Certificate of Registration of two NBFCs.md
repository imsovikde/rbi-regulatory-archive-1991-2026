## December 13, 2022

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of two NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the Certificate of Registration (CoR) of the following two companies.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Registered Office Address|CoR No.|CoR Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|Nedumpillil Financial Company Limited|41/3793, IInd Floor, Carmel Centre Building, Banerji Road, Kochi, Kerala- 682018|B-16.00124|September 25, 2002|November 17, 2022|
|2|Bhagwan Hire Purchase Limited|Station Road, Ujhani, District Budaun, Uttar Pradesh - 243639|B-12.00176|September 12, 2008|November 29, 2022|

As such, the above companies shall not transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
