## Investments by Foreign Institutional Investors

## December 20, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India on December 20, 2001 has notified that foreign institutional investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of the following companies up to 49 per cent of their paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as these companies have passed resolutions at their Board of Directors and General Body Meetings to this effect.

1. Blue Dart Express Ltd.
2. Panacea Biotec Ltd.
