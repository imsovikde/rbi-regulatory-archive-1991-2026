## List of Securities for Purchase through Auction on September 26

## September 24, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase following dated securities through auction on September 26, 2001 :

|1.|12.50% GOI 2004|
|---|---|
|2.|11.68% GOI 2006|
|3.|11.03% GOI 2012|
|4.|11.43% GOI 2015|

All SGL and Current Account holders with the Reserve Bank of India at Mumbai will be eligible to participate in the auction.

Minimum bid amount should be Rs. one crore (face value) and multiples of Rs.one crore.

Application forms may be obtained from Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India. 16th Floor, Central Office Building, Mumbai or from Public Debt Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office.

Bids should be tendered at the Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai up to 12.00 Noon on September 26, 2001. On the basis of the tenders received, the Reserve Bank of India will determine the maximum price upto which tenders for purchase of Government securities will be accepted at the auction. In other words, the auction will be conducted on multiple price basis. The settlement will be on September 27, 2001.

The Reserve Bank of India has the right to accept or reject any or all the bids either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
