## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## June 28, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based auction of 10-Year Government Stock and priced based auction of 10.45% Government Stock, 2018 to be held on June 29, 2001 was 1.00 paise for both.
