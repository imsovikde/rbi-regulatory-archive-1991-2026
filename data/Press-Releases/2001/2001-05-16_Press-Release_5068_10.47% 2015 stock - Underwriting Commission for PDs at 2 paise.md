## 10.47% 2015 stock :

## Underwriting Commission for PDs at 2 paise

## May 16, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has cut-off the underwriting commission for Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of 10.47% Government Stock, 2015 at two paise. The auction will be held on May 17, 2001.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
