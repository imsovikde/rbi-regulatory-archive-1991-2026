## RBI to open Purchase Window for Government Securities Tomorrow

## September 17, 2001

In view of the extraordinary circumstances in the Government securities market, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to open a purchase window for select government securities on a daily auction basis. The purchase window will open tomorrow and will last upto Friday, September 21, 2001.

The details of the securities to be purchased on auction basis in the window tomorrow will be notified later today.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
