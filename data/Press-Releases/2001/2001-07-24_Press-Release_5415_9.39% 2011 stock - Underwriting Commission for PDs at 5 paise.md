## 9.39% 2011 stock : Underwriting Commission for PDs at 5 paise

## July 24, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has cut-off the underwriting commission for Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of 9.39% Government Stock, 2011 at five paise. The auction will be held on July 25, 2001.
