## RBI announces Guidelines on Margin Trading

## September 22, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has today issued detailed guidelines to banks on margin trading. The guidelines are available on RBI web site [http://www](http://www). rbi. org. in

Earlier, on September 18, 2001 the Reserve Bank of India had announced its decision to permit banks to finance margin trading. It had indicated at that time that it would issue detailed guidelines separately.
