## 10.82 per cent State Development Loans, 2011 oversubscribed

## January 31, 2001

The 10.82 per cent ten year State Development Loans in respect of Thirteen State Governments opened for subscription for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.1567.81 crore on January 30, 2001. The subscriptions received aggregated Rs.1984.20 crore. Some subscriptions received at designated branches of State Bank of India/Associate Banks at District headquarters of the States are still being reported. As the loans of six States, viz. Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttaranchal were oversubscribed, partial allotments ranging from 34.98 per cent to 94.87 per cent have been made. Excess subscription in these cases is being refunded to the applicants.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
