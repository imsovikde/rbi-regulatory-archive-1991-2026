## Underwriting Commission for PDs

## August 8, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the of 9.81% Government Stock, 2013 was 10 paise and that for 18- year Government Stock was 8.75 paise. The auction for both the Securities would be held on August 8, 2001.
