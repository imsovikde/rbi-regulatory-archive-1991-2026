# Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of "10.71 percent Government Stock, 2016" through price based auction

## Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of "10.71 percent Government

## Stock, 2016" through price based auction

Government of India have announced sale (re-issue) by auction of “10.71 percent Government Stock, 2016” for an aggregate amount of Rs 3,000 crore (nominal).

The auction will be <u>price based</u> and will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India at Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on Wednesday, December 5, 2001. Bids in prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted at that Office, on December 5, 2001 during the banking hours. The auction will be conducted on "Multiple- Price basis". On the basis of the bids received, RBI will determine the minimum price up to which tenders for purchase of Government Stock will be accepted at the auction. Bids quoted at rates lower than the minimum price determined by Reserve Bank of India will be rejected. The results of the auction will be announced on December 5, 2001 and payment by successful bidders will be during the banking hours on December 6, 2001.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market’ up to 2.30 p.m. on Tuesday, December 4, 2001 to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.
