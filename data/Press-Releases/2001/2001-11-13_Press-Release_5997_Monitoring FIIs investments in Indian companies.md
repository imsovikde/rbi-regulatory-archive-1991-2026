## Monitoring FIIs investments in Indian companies

## November 13, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Housing Development Finance Corporation Ltd. up to 74 per cent. The company has passed resolution at its Board of Directors ' meeting and Special Resolution in its General Body meeting agreeing for the purchase of their equity shares and convertible debentures by FIIs up to 74 per cent of its paid-up capital through primary/secondary markets in India under the Portfolio Investment Scheme. **N L Rao Asst. Manager**
