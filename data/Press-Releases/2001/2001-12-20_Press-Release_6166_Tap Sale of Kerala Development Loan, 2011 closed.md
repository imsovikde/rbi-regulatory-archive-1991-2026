# Tap Sale of Kerala Development Loan, 2011 closed

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Tap Sale of Kerala Development Loan, 2011 closed

The sale of 8.37 per cent Kerala Development Loan, 2011 which opened for subscription on December 20, 2001 on tap at the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai and Thiruvananthapuram was closed at 11.30 hours today after receipt of approximately Rs.138.96 crore.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
