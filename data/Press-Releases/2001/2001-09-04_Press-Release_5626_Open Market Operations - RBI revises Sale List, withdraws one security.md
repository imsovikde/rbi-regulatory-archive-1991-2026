## Open Market Operations :

## RBI revises Sale List, withdraws one security

## September 4, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India withdrew 9.81% 2013 from the Open Market Operations(OMO) sale window with immediate effect. Sale of 11.75% 2006 continues at the price of Rs.116.13.
