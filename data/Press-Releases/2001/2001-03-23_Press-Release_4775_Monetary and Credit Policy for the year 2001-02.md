## Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2001-02

## March 23, 2001

Dr.Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will hold a meeting with the Chief Executives of major scheduled commercial banks to announce the Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2001-02 on Thursday, April 19, 2001. The meeting will be held at 11.30 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
