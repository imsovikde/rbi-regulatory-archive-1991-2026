## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## October 15, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 14-Year and Price Based Auction of

10.18% Government Stock, 2026 to be held on October 15, 2001 was 14.00 paise and
7.00 paise respectively.
