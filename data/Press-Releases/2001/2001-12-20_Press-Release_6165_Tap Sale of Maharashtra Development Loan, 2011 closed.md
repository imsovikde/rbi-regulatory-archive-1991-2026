## Tap Sale of Maharashtra Development Loan, 2011 closed

## December 20, 2001

The sale of 8.37 per cent Maharashtra Development Loan, 2011 which opened for subscription on December 20, 2001 on tap at the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai was closed at

11.50 hours today after receipt of approximately Rs.141.20 crore.
## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
