## RBI gives final approval to two more Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has granted final approval to Banc of America Securities (India) Pvt. Ltd. and Standard Chartered – UTI Securities India Pvt. Ltd. to operate as Primary Dealers in the Government Securities market.

Banc of America Securities (India) Pvt. Ltd. and Standard Chartered – UTI Securities India Pvt. Ltd. were granted “in principle” approval on March 2, 2001. With the grant of final approval to these entities the number of primary dealers in the Government securities market has now gone up to seventeen (17).
