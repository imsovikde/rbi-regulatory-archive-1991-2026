## RBI Reference Rate for US $ October 24, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0200 on

24.10.2001. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : **Date CURRENCY**
## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

23 October, 2001 48.0100 68.3374 42.7793 39.09 24 October, 2001 48.0200 68.5870 42.8314 39.1800 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
