## Local Boards of RBI reconstituted

## May 31, 2001

The Central Government has reconstituted the four Local Boards of the Reserve Bank of India. The following persons have been appointed as members of Local Boards of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from May 24, 2001:

## <u>Eastern Area Local Board</u>

1. Shri P.D. Chittalangiya, Industrialist, Sarda Plywod Industries Ltd.,
2. Shri A.K. Saikia, Retd. IAS,
3. Shri Sovan Kanungo, Retd. IAS
4. Smt. Kiran Ghai, Reader, Department of Hindi, Patna Women's College Consequent on the above, two existing members, viz., Dr. Subrata Ganguly and Dr. Subir Chowdhury, have ceased to be members on the Eastern Area Local Board.
## <u>Northern Area Local Board</u>

1. Shri Prem Nath Khanna, Chartered Accountant,
2. Dr. Ram Nath, Ex-Professor & Vice Chancellor, CSA University of Agri. &Tech.,
3. Shri Mitha Lal Mehta, Former Chief Secretary, Government of Rajasthan,
4. Dr. Pritam Singh, Director, Indian Institute of Management. Consequent on the above, three existing members viz., Smt. Shobhana Bhartia, Shri Brij Mohan Lall and Shri D.H. Pai Panandikar, have ceased to be members on the Northern Area Local Board.
## <u>Western Area Local Board</u>

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

1. Shri K. Venkatesan, Former Executive Director, Asian Development Bank
2. Shri Dattaraj V. Salgaocar, Managing Director,
V.M. Salgaocar & Bro. Ltd.,
3. Shri Jayanti Lal Bavjibhai Patel, Vice-Principal,
S.B. Garda College
4. Prof. Mahendra Singh Sodha, Director, Institute of Technology and Management. Consequent on the above, four existing members viz., Dr. Bakul H. Dholakia, Shri Pradip Pannalal Shah, Dr. Neelkanth A. Kalyani and Shri Inder Chand Jain, have ceased to be members on the Western Area Local Board.
## <u>Southern Area Local Board</u>

1. Dr. Ashok Kumar Lahiri, National Institute of Public Finance and Policy
2. Shri Nair Chellappan Parameswaran, Retd. Chief Secretary to Government of Kerala
3. Dr. S. Ramachander, Director, Institute for Financial Management and Research
4. Dr. M. Govinda Rao Director, Institute for Social and Economic Change Consequent on the above, two members viz., Shri N. Sankar and Shri D. Seetharamaiah have ceased to be members on the Southern Area Local Board.
2. The above appointments are in addition to the appointment of Prof. Mihir Rakshit, Dr.A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Shri Y.H.Malegam and Shri K. Madhava Rao to the Eastern, Northern, Western and Southern Area Local Boards of the Bank, respectively, with effect from November 27, 2000.
