## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 27, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.01 on

27.06.2001. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : **Date CURRENCY**
## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

26 June, 2001 47.0400 66.4558 40.6261 38.06 27 June, 2001 47.0100 66.6226 40.6002 37.8000 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
