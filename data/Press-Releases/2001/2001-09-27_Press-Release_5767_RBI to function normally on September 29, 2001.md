## RBI to function normally on September 29, 2001

## September 27, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has stated that its offices in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will conduct normal business on Saturday, September 29, 2001. Commercial banks and co-operative banks will remain closed on that day on account of half-yearly closing of their accounts. There will, however, be no clearing on that day and settlement of MICR cheques presented on September 28 will be done on Monday, October 1, 2001.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
