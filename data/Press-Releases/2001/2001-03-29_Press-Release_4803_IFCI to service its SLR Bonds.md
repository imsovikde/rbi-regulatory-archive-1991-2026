## IFCI to service its SLR Bonds

## March 29, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has stated that with effect from May 1, 2001 the IFCI Ltd., will take over the servicing of IFCI Bonds (SLR). As such, payment of interest, redemption proceeds as also other servicing such as splitting, consolidation of bonds will be handled by IFCI Limited. Consequently, the servicing of these bonds by Public Debt Offices of the Reserve Bank of India will be discontinued from May 1, 2001.

The Reserve Bank of India has requested all the bond holders/investors to approach the concerned office of IFCI for servicing of all the IFCI Bonds (SLR) from May 1, 2001. The addresses and telephone numbers of RBI's Public Debt Offices and corresponding IFCI offices are available on the RBI website **www.rbi.org.in**
