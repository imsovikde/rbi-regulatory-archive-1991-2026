## RBI adds one more Institution as non-competitive bidder for 364 day Treasury Bill Auction

## September 14, 2001

It has been decided to permit `Custodian of Enemy Property for India' to participate in the auctions of 364 day Treasury Bills on a non-competitive basis. Presently, State Governments are permitted to bid on a non-competitive basis in the auctions of 364-day Treasury Bills conducted by the Reserve Bank of India.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
