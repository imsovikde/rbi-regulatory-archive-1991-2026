# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## December 6, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.91 on 06.12.2001. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given <u>below :</u> **Date CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 05 December, 2001 47.9100 68.1544 42.7190 38.5800 06 December, 2001 47.9100 67.8118 42.5273 38.4300 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager**
