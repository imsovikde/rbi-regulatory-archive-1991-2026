## Reserve Bank of India offices to remain open on December 31, 2001

## December 28, 2001

The offices of the Reserve Bank of India in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will remain open on December 31/last working day of December and undertake all public transactions.

Earlier, the Reserve Bank offices used to be closed for public transactions on this day for the half-yearly closing of its accounts.

**P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager**
