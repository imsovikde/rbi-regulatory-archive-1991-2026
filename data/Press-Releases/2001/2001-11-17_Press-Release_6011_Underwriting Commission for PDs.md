## Underwriting Commission for PDs

## November 17, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the price based auction of 9.85% Government Stock, 2015 was 1.00 paisa. The auction for the Security would be held on November 19, 2001.
