## RBI cancels licence of The Friends' Co-operative Bank Ltd.

## August 1, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the licence of The Friends' Co- operative Bank Ltd., Mumbai, Maharashtra to carry on banking business in India. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of `banking' as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from public and allow withdrawal by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
