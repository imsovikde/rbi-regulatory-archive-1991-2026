## Shri N. Sadasivan takes over as Executive Director of RBI

## December 10, 2001

Shri N. Sadasivan has today taken over as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India. Shri Sadasivan will be in-charge exclusively of the Department of Non-Banking Supervision. Earlier Shri Sadasivan was Deputy Managing Director of State Bank of India.
