## Underwriting Commission for PDs

## November 20, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Auction of Government of India Floating Rate Bonds, 2006 to be held on November 21, 2001 was 0.50 paisa.
