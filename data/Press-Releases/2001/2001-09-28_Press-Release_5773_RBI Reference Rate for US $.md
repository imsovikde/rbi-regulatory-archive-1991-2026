## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 28, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.8600 on

28.09.2001. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :
