## Report of the Core Group on Voluntary Disclosure Norms for State Governments

## February 2, 2001

In the meeting of the State Finance Secretaries held at the Reserve Bank on June 12, 1999, it was decided to constitute a group to suggest various measures to enhance the transparency in the disclosures of State budgets. Pursuant to this decision, the Core Group on ‘Voluntary Disclosure Norms for State Governments’ was constituted.

The members included – Shri K.V. Bhanujan, Principal Secretary, Government of Gujarat, Shri P.J.Bazeley, Principal Secretary, Government of Meghalaya, Shri Vinod Rai, Principal Secretary, Government of Kerala and Shri Asok Gupta, Principal Secretary, Government of West Bengal, Dr. N.J.Kurien, Adviser, Planning Commission, Shri S.C.Pandey, Director, Government of India, and Smt. Usha Thorat, Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India. Dr. R. Kannan, Adviser, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy, Reserve Bank of India was Member Secretary to the group.

The terms of reference of the group were :

(a) to study the transparency practices in the fiscal operations of State Governments in India with focus on their work-methods and budgetary practices.
(b) to study transparency issues in State budgets *vis-a-vis* Central Government budget.
(c) To study inter-State comparisons of budgetary accounts and reporting operational issues; and
(d) to examine the challenges for State Governments in ensuring better and more transparent practices and an agenda for the future.
The Group submitted its report on January 12, 2001.

The report can be accessed on the Reserve Bank of India Website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>) under "Publication-Reports".
