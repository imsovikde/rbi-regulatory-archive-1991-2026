## Investment by FIIs

## October 19, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 19, 2001 notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd., upto 49 per cent of its issued and paid up capital instead of the earlier 30 per cent through primary/secondary markets in India under portfolio investment scheme (PIS). The company has passed a resolution to this effect at its Board of Directors and a special resolution in its General Body meeting.
