## Underwriting Commission for PDs

## August 13, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 10-year Stock of Government of Andhra Pradesh to be held on August 13, 2001 was 25.00 paise. No bids for underwriting of 10-year Stock of Government of West Bengal have been accepted.
