## RBI-SEBI Committee Report released for Expert Comments

## April 12, 2001

As announced in October 2000 in the Mid-term Review of the Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2000-2001, the RBI-SEBI Technical Committee has reviewed the RBI guidelines on banks' investments in shares, as also, advances against shares and other connected exposures. The Report of the Committee has been submitted to the Reserve Bank of India today.

In making its recommendations, the Committee has taken into account the recent experiences of the banks and the exposures taken by them by way of advances against shares and financial guarantees. The report also contains the executive summary of the main findings and highlights of recommendations.

The Reserve Bank has released the report of the RBI-SEBI Technical Committee along with its executive summary for comments/suggestions by experts/market participants and others. The report has been placed on the RBI website (<u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>). The comments and suggestions on the report may be sent by April 22, 2001 to the Reserve Bank of India by e-mail to <u>rbidboco@bom3.vsnl.net. in</u> or by fax to :

Shri M.R.Srinivasan, Chief General Manager-In-charge Reserve Bank of India Department of Banking Operations & Development Central Office, Mumbai 400 005 Fax : 91-22-218 3785

The Reserve Bank of India proposes to revise the guidelines issued earlier in November 2000 on banks' investments in shares, as also, advances against shares and other connected exposures in the light of the recommendations of the Technical Committee. These new draft guidelines will also be put on the RBI website (http:/www.rbi.org.in) for any comments/suggestions to be received by April 22,

2001. The Reserve Bank proposes to issue the final guidelines in this regard in early May 2001.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
