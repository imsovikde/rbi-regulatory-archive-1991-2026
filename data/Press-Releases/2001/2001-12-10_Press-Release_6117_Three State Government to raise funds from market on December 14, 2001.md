## Three State Government to raise funds from market on December 14, 2001

Three State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year stocks to the public by auction for an aggregate amount of Rs.123.17 crore as detailed below :

|Name of State|Amount Rs. crore|
|---|---|
|Arunachal Pradesh|5.17|
|Jammu & Kashmir|75.00|
|Madhya Pradesh|43.00|

----------

123.17 ----------
The auction will be conducted by Mumbai (Fort) office of the Reserve Bank of India on Friday, December 14, 2001. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Friday, December 14, 2001 before 14.30 hrs. Results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Tuesday, December 18, 2001.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
