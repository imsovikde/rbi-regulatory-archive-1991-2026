## 364 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## November 10, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.750 crore. The allocation to non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F.2(12)-W&M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Tuesday, November 13, 2001 (Wednesday, November 14, 2001 being a holiday) before 1430 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Saturday, November 17, 2001as Friday, November 16, 2001 is a holiday.

**N. L. Rao Asst. Manager**
