## Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India-Summary

## Tables-March 2000

## March 21, 2001

The Department of Statistical Analysis & Computer Services of Reserve Bank of India has brought out the `Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India-Summary Tables-March 2000'. The publication is based on Basic Statistical Returns collected from the offices of scheduled commercial banks. The booklet presents summary tables on number of employees, deposits and credit of scheduled commercial banks as at the end of March 2000.

Copies of the publication are available from the Sales Section (DRRP), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P. M. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.25. The publication is available on RBI Website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>
