## Non-Renewal of Authorisation to act as Primary Dealer

## September 18, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has decided not to renew its authorisation to Ceat Financial Services Ltd. (CFSL) for Primary Dealer business. Accordingly, CFSL ceases to be a Primary Dealer with immediate effect.

## P.V. Sadanandan Assistant Manager
