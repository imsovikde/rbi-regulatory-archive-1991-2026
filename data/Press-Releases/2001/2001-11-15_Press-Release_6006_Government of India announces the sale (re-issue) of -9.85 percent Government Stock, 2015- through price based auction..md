## Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of "9.85 percent Government

## Stock, 2015" through price based auction.

## November 15, 2001

The Government of India have announced sale (re-issue) by auction of “9.85 percent Government Stock, 2015” for an aggregate amount of Rs 4,000 crore (nominal).

The auction will be <u>price based</u> and will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai Office on Monday, November 19, 2001. Bids in prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted at that Office, on November 19, 2001 during the banking hours. The auction will be conducted on "Multiple- Price basis". On the basis of the bids received, RBI will determine the minimum price up to which tenders for purchase of Government Stock will be accepted at the auction. Bids quoted at rates lower than the minimum price determined by the Reserve Bank of India will be rejected. The results of the auction will be announced on November 19, 2001 and payment by successful bidders will be during the banking hours on November 20, 2001.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Security under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market’ up to 12.30 p.m. on Saturday, November 17, 2001 to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
