## CD ROM on Selected Financial Statistics-Public Limited Companies -

## 1974-75 to 1999-2000 (Selected Industries)

## October 1, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has published a compilation of industry-wise time series data on select industries. The data cover non-Government, non-financial public limited companies in the select industries for the period 1974-75 to 1999-2000. The publication is a sequel to the earlier publication "Private Corporate Business Sector in India-Selected Financial Statistics from 1950-51 to 1997-98 (All-Industries)". This publication had presented data at aggregate level for all industries.

The publication on public limited companies consists of two parts - the first part comprising four chapters, briefly describing the scope of the studies, the sources of data, the methodology and the concepts and definitions adopted while the second part consisting of two volumes contains the statistical statements.

The publication is also available on a CD-ROM to facilitate easy recourse to data by researchers.

The studies on financial performance of public limited companies are published in the Reserve Bank of India Bulletin from time to time and these are widely referred to by researchers and other users interested in this area. From time to time the data contained in these studies are consolidated and brought out as special publication.
