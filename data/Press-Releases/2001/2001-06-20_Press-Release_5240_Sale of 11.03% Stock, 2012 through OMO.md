## Sale of 11.03% Stock, 2012 through OMO

## June 20, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India will offer for sale through its Open Market Operations window, "11.03% Government Stock, 2012 (re-issue)" with effect from June 21, 2001. The sale will be at Rs.108.85 per Rs.100.00 (nominal).

The Stock was re-issued by the Government of India and was privately placed with the Reserve Bank at the same price on June 20, 2001. The private placement was done for a notified amount of Rs.4,000 crore (nominal).
