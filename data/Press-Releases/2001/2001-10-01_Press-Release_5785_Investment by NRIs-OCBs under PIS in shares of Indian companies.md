## Investment by NRIs/OCBs under PIS in shares of Indian companies

## October 1, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India on October 1, 2001 has notified that non-resident Indian (NRIs) and overseas corporate bodies (OCBs) can now purchase through stock exchanges equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Garware Shipping Corporation Ltd. upto 24 per cent of its paid up capital under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS), as the company has passed a resolution to this effect in its general body meeting.
