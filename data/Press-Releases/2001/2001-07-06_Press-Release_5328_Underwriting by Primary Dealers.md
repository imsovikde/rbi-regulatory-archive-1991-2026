## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## July 6, 2001

RBI has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based auctions of 9.81% Govt. Stock, 2013 and 10.25% Govt. Stock, 2021 to be held on July 07, 2001 was 1 paise & 5 paise respectively.
