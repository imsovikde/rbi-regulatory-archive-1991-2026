## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## May 28, 2001

RBI has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auctions of 12 Year Govt. Stock and 20 Year Govt. Stock to be held on May 29, 2001 was 1.00 paise and 2.00 paise respectively.
