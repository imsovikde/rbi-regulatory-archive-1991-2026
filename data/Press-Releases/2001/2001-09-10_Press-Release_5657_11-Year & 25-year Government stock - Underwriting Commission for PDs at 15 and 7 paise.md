## 11-Year & 25-year Government stock : Underwriting Commission for PDs at 15 and 7 paise

## September 10, 2001

The Reserve Bank of India has announced cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 11-Year and 25- Year Government Stock to be held on September 10, 2001 was 15.00 and 7.00 paise respectively.

## P.V. Sadanandan Assistant Manager
