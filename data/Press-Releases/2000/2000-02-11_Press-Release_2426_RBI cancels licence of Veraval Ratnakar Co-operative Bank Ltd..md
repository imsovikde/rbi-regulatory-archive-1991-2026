## RBI cancels licence of Veraval Ratnakar Co-operative Bank Ltd.

## February 11, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the licence of the Veraval Ratnakar Co- operative Bank Ltd., Veraval, Dist. Junagadh (Gujarat), to carry on banking business in India. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of "banking" as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (as applicable to Co-operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from the public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
