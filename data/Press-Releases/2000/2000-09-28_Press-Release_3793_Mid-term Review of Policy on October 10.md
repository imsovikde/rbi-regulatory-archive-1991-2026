## Mid-term Review of Policy on October 10

## September 28, 2000

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will meet the Chief Executives of major scheduled commercial banks for a Mid-term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2000-2001 on Tuesday, October 10, 2000.
