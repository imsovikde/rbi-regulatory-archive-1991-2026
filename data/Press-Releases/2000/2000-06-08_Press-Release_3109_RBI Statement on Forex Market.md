## RBI Statement on Forex Market

## June 8, 2000

In response to queries received today from banks on open positions, it is clarified as under :

As clearly indicated in the statement issued this morning, the instruction on open position is applicable only for today. Hence status quo ante is restored with effect from June 9,

2000.
## P.V. Sadanandan Asst. Manager
