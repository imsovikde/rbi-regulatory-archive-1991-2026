## 10.52 per cent State Development Loans, 2010 Oversubscribed

## April 26, 2000

The 10.52 per cent ten-year State Development Loans in respect of twenty-five State Governments opened for subscription for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.4,369 crore on April 25, 2000. The subscriptions received aggregated Rs.5,837.70 crore. Some subscriptions received at designated branches of State Bank of India/Associate Banks at district headquarters of the States are still being reported. The excess subscriptions received by some State Governments will be retained.

## P. V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager
