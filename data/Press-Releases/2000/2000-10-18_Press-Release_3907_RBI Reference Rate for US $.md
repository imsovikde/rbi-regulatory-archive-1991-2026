## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## October 18, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.33 on 18.10.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : **DATE** <u>CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 17 October 2000 46.3600 67.0064 39.3550 42.86 18October 2000 46.3300 67.1646 39.6538 42.8900 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
