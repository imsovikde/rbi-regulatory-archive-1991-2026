## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## November 14, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.78 on 14.11.2000. Based on the reference rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

## Date <u>CURRENCY</u>

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

13 November 2000 46.7600 66.8341 40.4217 43.35 14 November 2000 46.7800 67.3983 40.2425 43.4300

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
