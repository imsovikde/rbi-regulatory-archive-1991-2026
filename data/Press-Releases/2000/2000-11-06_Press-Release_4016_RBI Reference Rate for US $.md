## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## November 6, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.64 on 06.11.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 03 November 2000 46.6900 67.4717 40.1441 43.17 06 November 2000 46.6400 67.4298 40.3786 43.4800 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
