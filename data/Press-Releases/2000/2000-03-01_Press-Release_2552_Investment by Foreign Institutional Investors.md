# Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## March 1, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Himachal Futuristic Communications Ltd., up to 30 per cent of their paid up capital through primary and stock exchanges, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting to this effect. **Alpana Killawala** **General Manager** **Press Release : 1999-2000/1173**
