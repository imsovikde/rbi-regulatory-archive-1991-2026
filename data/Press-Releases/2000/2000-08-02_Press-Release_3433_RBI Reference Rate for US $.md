## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## August 2, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.34 on 02.08.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 01 August 2000 45.1300 67.5190 41.7565 41.22 02 August 2000 45.3400 67.7357 41.5156 41.7200 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
