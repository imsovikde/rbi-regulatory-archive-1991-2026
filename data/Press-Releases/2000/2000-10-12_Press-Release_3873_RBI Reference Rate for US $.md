## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## October 12, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.30 on 12.10.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 11 October, 2000 46.3300 67.5144 40.4785 42.96 12 October, 2000 46.3000 67.6744 40.1328 42.9400

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
