## Publication of Computer Paribhasha Kosh

## March 6, 2000

The Department of Banking Operations and Development of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out a publication in Hindi viz., 'Computer Paribhasha Kosh'. In this dictionary, extensive computer terminology is presented in easy Hindi. This would, therefore, be of great use for reference purpose. Similarly, it would also be useful in preparing training material.

The publication is available from the Sales Section, Division of Reports, Review & Publication, Reserve Bank of India, Amar Bldg., P.M. Road, Fort, Mumbai - 400 001 at a price of Rs.100/-.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
