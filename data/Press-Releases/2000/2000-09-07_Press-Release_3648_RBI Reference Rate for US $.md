# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 7, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.64 on 07.09.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 6 September 2000 45.6800 66.3411 40.3766 43.1000 7 September 2000 45.6400 65.6189 39.6977 42.9400 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P. V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager**
