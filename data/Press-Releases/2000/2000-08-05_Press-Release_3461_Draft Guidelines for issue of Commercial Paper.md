# Draft Guidelines for issue of Commercial Paper

## August 5, 2000

In view of the request received from some market participants to give them more time for sending in their comments/suggestions on the draft guidelines on Commercial Paper (CP), the Reserve Bank of India has decided to issue the final guidelines of Commercial Paper (CP) by the first week of September 2000.

It may be recalled that the draft guidelines for issue of Commercial Paper (CP) were circulated for the comments of market participants on July 6, 2000. It was, at that time, indicated that the final guidelines will be issued by August 7, 2000 after taking into account the suggestions received from market participants.

The suggestions/comments may continue to be sent to Adviser-in-Charge, Monetary Policy th Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office Building, 24 Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Mumbai-400 001; Fax Nos. 270 0850, 265 1685; e-mail <u>mpdrbi4@bom7.vsnl.net.in</u>
