## RBI Reference Rate for US $

June 27, 2000 The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.67 on 27.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 26 June 2000 44.6500 67.0330 41.8058 42.75 27 June 2000 44.6700 67.0028 42.0233 42.3100 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P. V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager**
