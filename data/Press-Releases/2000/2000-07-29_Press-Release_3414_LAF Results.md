## LAF Results

## July 29, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has advanced the timing of announcement of the results of Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) by half-an-hour. From Monday, July 31, 2000, the LAF results will be announced at 12.30 p.m. instead of 1.00 p.m. All other conditions for LAF remain unchanged. LAF results will also be available at 12.30 p.m. on the home page of the RBI website at www.rbi.org.in

**P.V.Sadanandan** **Asstt. Manager**
