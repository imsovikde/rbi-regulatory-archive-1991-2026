## New ED in RBI

## August 31, 2000

Shri D. P. Sarda has been appointed as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India with effect from September 1, 2000. Shri Sarda was earlier Chief General Manager In- charge of Department of Administration and Personnel Management, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
