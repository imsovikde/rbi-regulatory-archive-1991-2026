## Dr. R.Radhakrishna, Vice Chancellor, Andhra University Appointed Director of Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research

## July 27, 2000

Dr. R.Radhakrishna currently Vice Chancellor, Andhra University, Vishakhapatnam has been appointed Director of Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research (IGIDR), Mumbai, on the retirement of the present Director Dr. Kirit Parikh whose term comes to an end on July 31, 2000. IGIDR is the premier economic research institution established by the Reserve Bank of India in 1986.

Dr. Radhakrishna, MA (Economics), M.Sc.(Statistics) and Ph.D (Economics) has been holding positions as professor/director of many reputed academic Institutes at different point of time in his career. He served as the Member Secretary of the Indian council for social Science Research (ICSSR), New Delhi. Dr. Radhakrishna was the recipient of prestigious VKRV Rao Prize in 1986 in Social Science Research for his significant contributions in the field of Economics. He has been consultant to many international Institutions including the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank and served as Member in several expert committees. He has published several books and has contributed widely to reputed National and International Journals.
