## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 24, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.72 on 24.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 21 July 2000 45.0200 68.0680 42.0014 41.59 24 July 2000 44.7200 68.0079 41.8713 41.1700 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
