## Draft Guidelines on Entry of Banks/Financial Institutions in Insurance on RBI Website

## January 10, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India had recently circulated to banks and financial institutions draft guidelines for diversification into insurance business. The draft guidelines were framed with a view to assessing the scope, desirability and eligibility of institutions to take up the insurance business. The draft guidelines have been framed keeping in view that financially sound and strong banks and financial institutions are allowed to undertake insurance business.

In view of the wide interest in the subject and in order to invite suggestions from market participants, the Reserve Bank of India has placed the draft guidelines for entry of banks and financial institutions into insurance business on its website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>.

The Reserve Bank will finalise the guidelines in consultation with the Government of India after receiving comments from banks, financial institutions and other market participants. The comments may be sent to the Chief General Manager-in- Charge, Department of Banking Operations and Development, Reserve Bank of India, World Trade Center, Center I, Post Box No.6089, Cuffe Parade, Colaba, Mumbai-400
