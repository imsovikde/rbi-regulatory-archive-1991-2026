## RBI Annual Report for the year ended June 30, 2000

## August 28, 2000

The Annual Report of the Reserve Bank of India together with its Summary for the period ended June 30, 2000 will be released at 5.00 p.m. today. Both the documents will also be made available on the home page of the RBI Website at the same time. The Annual Report can also be accessed through a special URL: www.annualreport.rbi.org.in
