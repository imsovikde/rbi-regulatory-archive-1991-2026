# Four-day fixed rate repos on January 21 at 6.00 per cent : RBI

## January 20, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has announced four-day fixed rate repos in Government of India dated securities on January 21, 2000 for parties holding SGL Account and current account with it at Mumbai. The repo rate is 6.00 per cent per annum. Eligible banks/institutions may submit their applications indicating the volume of repos (face value) in the prescribed form obtainable at th the Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office Building (16 Floor), Mumbai. The applications should be addressed to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai and submitted to him **before**

**1.30 p.m. on January 21, 2000.** The applications may be accepted fully or partially. Payment by applicants will be on January 21, 2000 up to 2.30 p.m. and the repurchase by the RBI on January 25, 2000.
## Chhaya Raje Manager
