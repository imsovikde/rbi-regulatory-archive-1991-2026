## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 11, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.69 on 11.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 10 July 2000 44.6900 67.7903 42.5695 41.76 11 July 2000 44.6900 67.6986 42.6946 41.8300 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
