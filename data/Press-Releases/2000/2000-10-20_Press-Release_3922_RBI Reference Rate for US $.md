## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## October 20, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.34 on 20.10.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 19 October 2000 46.3300 67.0117 38.9334 43.00 20 October 2000 46.3400 67.0447 39.1828 42.7100 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
