## Clarification regarding suspension of clearing

## July 13, 2000

The following transaction undertaken by RBI viz.,

1. OMO Sales
2. Refinance
3. LAF
4. Credit on account of redemption
5. Auction payments will be put through on July 14, 2000 but will be settled as of July 13, 2000. The transaction for value date 14 July, 2000 will be effected in the normal course tomorrow.
## P.V. Sadanandan Assistant Manager
