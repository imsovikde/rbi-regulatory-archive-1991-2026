## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 19, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.22 on 19.09.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 18 September 2000 45.8500 64.0845 39.1421 42.91 19 September 2000 46.2200 64.9229 39.3887 43.2600 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
