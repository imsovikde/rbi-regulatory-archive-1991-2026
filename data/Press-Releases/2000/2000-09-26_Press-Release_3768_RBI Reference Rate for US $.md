## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 26, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.07 on 26.09.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

## <u>Date</u> CURRENCY

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

25 September 2000 46.0200 67.3227 40.5574 42.69 26 September 2000 46.0700 67.0019 40.2951 42.9200 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
