## Flow of Funds Accounts of the Indian Economy -

## 1951-52 to 1995-96 : Monograph

## August 31, 2000

In response to the many requests of scholars for making available a long time series data on the flow of funds, the Reserve Bank of India has published a monograph bringing together the flow of funds accounts from 1951-52 to 1995-96. Flow of funds accounts represent a systematic record of net transactions involving financial instruments during a given period of time.

Besides providing detailed accounts, the monograph covers several other aspects of the subject, such as, the evolution and the conceptual framework of flow of funds, its relationship with the System of National Accounts 1993, the instrument-wise and sector- wise flow of funds, and the methodology of compilation. The compilation of data for the monograph is done by the Division of National Income, Savings and Flow of Funds of the Department of Economic Analysis and Policy.

Earlier, the Reserve Bank of India has been publishing the flow of funds accounts since December 1964. The flow of funds accounts covering the period 1951-52 through 1993- 94 were published in the various issues of the monthly RBI Bulletin of the Reserve Bank and those for 1994-95 to 1995-96 were published in the "Report on Currency and Finance", Volume II, 1997-98.

The monograph is available on RBI website <u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>
