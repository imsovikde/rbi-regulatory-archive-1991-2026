## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 2, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.65 on

02.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :
## <u>Date CURRENCY</u>

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

01 June 2000 44.5700 66.7881 41.7599 41.11 02 June 2000 44.6500 66.8187 41.7768 40.9700

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
