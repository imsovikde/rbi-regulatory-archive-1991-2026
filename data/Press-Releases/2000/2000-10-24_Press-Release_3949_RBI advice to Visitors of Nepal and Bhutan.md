## RBI advice to Visitors of Nepal and Bhutan

## October 24, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has advised visitors to Nepal and Bhutan not to take or send out to these countries currency notes in the denominations higher than above Rs.100.

It has stated that Regulation 8(i) of the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Currency) Regulations, 2000 provides that :

"A person may take or send out of India to Nepal or Bhutan, currency notes of Government of India and Reserve Bank of India notes (other than notes of denominations of above Rs.100 in either case)".
