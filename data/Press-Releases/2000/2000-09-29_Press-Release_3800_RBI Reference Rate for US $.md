## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 29, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.07 on 29.09.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 28 September 2000 46.1600 67.5852 40.7616 42.91 29 September 2000 46.0700 67.3589 40.4725 42.8400 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
