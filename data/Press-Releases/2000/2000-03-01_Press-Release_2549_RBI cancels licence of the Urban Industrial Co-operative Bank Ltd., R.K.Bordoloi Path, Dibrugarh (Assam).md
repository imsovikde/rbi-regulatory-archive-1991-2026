# RBI cancels licence of the Urban Industrial Co-operative Bank Ltd., R.K.Bordoloi Path, Dibrugarh (Assam)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RBI cancels licence of the

## Urban Industrial Co-operative Bank Ltd.,

## R.K.Bordoloi Path, Dibrugarh (Assam)

**March 1, 2000**

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the licence of the Urban Industrial Co-operative Bank Ltd., R.K.Boradoloi Path, Dibrugarh (Assam) to carry on banking business in India. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of 'banking' as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies). Thus, it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from the public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
