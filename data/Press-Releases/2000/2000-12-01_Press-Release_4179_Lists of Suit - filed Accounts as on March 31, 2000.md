## Lists of Suit-filed Accounts as on March 31, 2000

## December 1, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has compiled the lists of suit filed accounts of (a) Rs. 1 crore and above and (b) willful defaulters of Rs.25 lakhs and above as on March 31,

2000. These are available on the Reserve Bank of India website on a special URL: defaulters.rbi.org.in. These lists in a single booklet form will be available for sale at the Reserve Bank's sales division at Amar Building, Fort, Mumbai, shortly. It may be recalled that since March 1994, the Reserve Bank has been publishing the list of suit filed accounts of Rs.1 crore and above every year. This year, in addition, the list of cases of willful defaulters of Rs.25 lakhs and above where suits have been filed, is also published.
