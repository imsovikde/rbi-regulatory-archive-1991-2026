## Central Board of Directors for RBI reconstituted

## November 28, 2000

The Central Government has reconstituted the Central Board of Directors for the Reserve Bank of India. The following persons have been nominated to be the directors on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India, for a period of four years with effect from November 27, 2000 :

|1.|Shri Kushal Pal Singh, Chairman, DLF Group|
|---|---|
|2.|Prof. Vijay Shankar Vyas, Professor Emeritus, Institute of Development Studies|
|3.|Shri Davinder Singh Brar, CEO and Managing Director, Ranbaxy Laboratories Limited|
|4.|Prof. C. N. R. Rao, Honorary President, Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research|
|5.|Shri Homi Phiroze Ranina, Advocate, Supreme Court of India|
|6.|Shri N. R. Narayana Murthy, Chairman and Managing Director, Infosys Tech Ltd.|
|7.|Shri Ratan N. Tata, Chairman, Tata Industries Ltd.|

|9.|Shri Suresh Krishna, Chairman and Managing Director, Sundaram Fastners Limited|

|11.|Prof. Mihir Rakshit, represent Eastern Area Local Board)|
|12.|Shri Y. H. Malegam, Chartered Accountant, Billimoria & Co. (To represent Western Area Local Board)|
|13.|Shri A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, of India (to represent Northern Area Local Board)|
