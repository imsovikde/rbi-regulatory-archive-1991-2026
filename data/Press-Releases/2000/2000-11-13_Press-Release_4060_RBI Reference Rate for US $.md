## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## November 13, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.76 on 13.11.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

## Date CURRENCY

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

10 November 2000 46.7400 66.8546 40.5166 43.44 13 November 2000 46.7600 66.8341 40.4217 43.3500

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
