## RBI releases Modified Guidelines for Diversification by Banks into Insurance Business for Feedback

## March 16, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India issued draft guidelines in December 1999 setting out the criteria for eligibility of banks for undertaking insurance business. Subsequently, the banks have offered their views on the various parameters suggested in the indicative guidelines. Several useful suggestions were also published in newspapers. The draft guidelines for determining the eligibility of banks into insurance sector were discussed by the Reserve Bank of India with the Chief Executives of several commercial banks as well as with the Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs, Special Secretary (Insurance), Special Secretary (Banking) of the Government of India and the Chairman of Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDA).

On the basis of the discussions with the representatives of banks and the Government and the suggestions received from the Press, the guidelines have been suitably modified. The modified guidelines have been placed on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in)</u>. Banks, financial institutions and the members of public may send their comments to Shri Amalendu Ghosh, Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department of Banking Operations and Development, Reserve Bank of India, World Trade Centre, Centre I, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai 400 005 or on Fax no. 022 218 3785. The Reserve Bank will finalise the guidelines in a week's time after receiving the feedback.

The proposed modified guidelines for the entry of banks into insurance sector are as enclosed.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
