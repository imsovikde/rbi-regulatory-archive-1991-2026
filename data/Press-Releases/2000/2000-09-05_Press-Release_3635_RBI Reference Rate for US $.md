## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## September 5, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.74 on 05.09.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 4 September 2000 45.7300 66.9396 41.1959 43.23 5 September 2000 45.7400 66.6295 40.9990 43.0000 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
