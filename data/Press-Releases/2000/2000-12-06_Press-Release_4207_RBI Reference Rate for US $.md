## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## December 6, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.74 on 06.12.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 5 December 2000 46.8000 67.7313 41.5046 42.14 6 December 2000 46.7400 67.0111 41.1569 42.2100 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
