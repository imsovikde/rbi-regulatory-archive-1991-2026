## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 31, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.99 on 31.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 28 July 2000 44.8900 67.8221 41.8846 41.33 31 July 2000 44.9900 67.6177 41.5775 41.1000 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
