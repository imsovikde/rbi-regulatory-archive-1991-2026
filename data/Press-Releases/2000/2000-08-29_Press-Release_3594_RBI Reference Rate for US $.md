# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## August 29, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.79 on 29.08.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 28 August 2000 45.8400 67.4673 41.4210 43.1300 29 August 2000 45.7900 67.3800 41.2705 42.9400 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**N.S.D. Lobo Asstt. Manager**
