## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## August 25 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.89 on 25.08.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 24 August 2000 45.9200 68.0764 41.3510 42.89 25 August 2000 45.8900 67.8598 41.2574 42.8600 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**N.S.D.Lobo Asstt. Manager**
