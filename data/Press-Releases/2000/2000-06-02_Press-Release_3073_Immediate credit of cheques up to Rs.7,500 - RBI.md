## Immediate credit of cheques up to Rs.7,500 : RBI

## June 2, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has enhanced the amount for immediate credit of outstation and local cheques tendered for collection by bank customers to Rs.7,500 subject to the accounts being conducted satisfactorily. The enhanced limit is effective immediately. The earlier limit was Rs.5,000.

The decision was based on a recommendation of the Regulation Review Authority set up by the Reserve Bank of India in 1998.
