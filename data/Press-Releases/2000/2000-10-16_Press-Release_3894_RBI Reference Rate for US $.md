## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## October 16, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.32 on 16.10.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 13 October 2000 46.3800 68.3386 40.0770 43.11 16October 2000 46.3200 67.1594 39.5573 42.9200 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
