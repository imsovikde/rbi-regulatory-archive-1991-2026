# Information on Money Market available on RBI Website

## February 5, 2000

In the `Mid-Term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy for the Year 1999-2000' announced on October 29, 1999, it was indicated that with a view to facilitating flow of information and transparency in the functioning of money market, Reserve Bank of India would work out, in consultation with market participants, modalities of releasing on a daily basis, data on volumes and rates in call money market as well as some other relevant data.

Accordingly, it has been decided to release data (volumes and interest rates) on call/notice/term money market, liquidity support availed from the Reserve Bank, RBI's open market operations (OMO) (net sales/purchases) and RBI's Repo, on a daily basis, after taking into account suggestions from market participants. The daily data will be available on the RBI website at

10.00 A.M., the next working day. The Reserve Bank of India will start releasing the data with effect from February 7, 2000, with data pertaining to February 5, 2000 (February 6, 2000 being Sunday).
