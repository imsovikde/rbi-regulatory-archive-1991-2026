# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## January 20, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.56 on 20.01.2000 as against Rs.43.57 on 19.01.2000. The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-rupee rate will be based on this rate. Based on the RBI Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12.00 noon, the exchange rates of US dollar, Pound Sterling, EURO and Yen against Rs.100 are : <u>Date Currency per Rs. 100 (Middle Rates)</u> **USD GBP EURO Yen**

January 13, 2000 2.2973 1.3955 2.2309 242.650 January 20, 2000 2.2957 1.3964 2.2699 241.816
