## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 27, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.90 on 27.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 26 July 2000 44.8800 68.1884 42.2725 41.08 27 July 2000 44.9000 68.0617 42.3295 41.2000 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
