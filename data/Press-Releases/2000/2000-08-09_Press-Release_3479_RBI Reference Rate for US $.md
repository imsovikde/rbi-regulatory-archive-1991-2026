## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## August 9, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.64 on 09.08.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 08 August 2000 45.7200 69.0486 41.5069 42.10 09 August 2000 45.6400 68.5809 41.0372 42.1300 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
