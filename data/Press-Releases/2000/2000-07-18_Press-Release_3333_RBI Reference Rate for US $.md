## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 18, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.74 on 18.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 17 July 2000 44.7100 66.9532 41.8821 41.33 18 July 2000 44.7400 66.7677 41.8632 41.3600

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
