# RBI gives final approval to one more primary dealer

## February 2, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has granted its final approval to Corpbank Securities Limited to operate as a primary dealer in the Government Securities Market.

Corpbank Securities was granted "in principle" approval on May 17, 1999. With the grant of final approval to the aforesaid entity, the number of primary dealers in the Government Securities Market has gone up to fifteen.
