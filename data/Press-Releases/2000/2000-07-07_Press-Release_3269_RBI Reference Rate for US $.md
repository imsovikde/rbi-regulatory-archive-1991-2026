## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## July 7, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.73 on 07.07.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 06 July 2000 44.7300 67.4685 42.7104 42.78 07 July 2000 44.7300 67.3589 42.4354 41.5900

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
