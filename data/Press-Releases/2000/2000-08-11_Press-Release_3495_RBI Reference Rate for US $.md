## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## August 11, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.93 on 11.08.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 10 August 2000 45.6800 68.6365 41.2787 42.44 11 August 2000 45.9300 68.8422 41.7871 42.2500 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
