# RBI withdraws Securities from OMO window

## RBI withdraws Securities from OMO window

## June 7, 2000

The sale of security 10.95% 2011 and the six 364 day treasury bills maturing in December 2000, January 2001 and February 2001 under Open Market Operation of RBI has been discontinued with immediate effect.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
