## Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2000-2001

## March 30, 2000

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of india will hold a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks to announce the monetary and credit policy for the year 2000-2001 on Thursday, April 27, 2000. The Governor's meeting with the Commercial banks will be held at 11.30 a. m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai. **Alpana Killawala General Manager**
