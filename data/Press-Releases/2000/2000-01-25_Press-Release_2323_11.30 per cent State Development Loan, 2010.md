# 11.30 per cent State Development Loan, 2010

## January 25, 2000

The 11.30 per cent State Development Loans, 2010 of four State Governments which opened for subscription on January 24, 2000 for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.635.53 crore were closed on the same day. The total subscriptions aggregated Rs.1143.17 crore. All the loans of 4 states, viz. Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh have been oversubscribed, partial allotments ranging from 29.79 per cent to

93.05 per cent have been made. Excess subscription is being refunded to the applicants.
