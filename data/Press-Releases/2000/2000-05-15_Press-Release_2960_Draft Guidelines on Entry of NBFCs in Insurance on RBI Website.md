## Draft Guidelines on Entry of NBFCs in Insurance on RBI Website

## May 15, 2000

In view of the wide interest in the subject and in order to invite suggestions from market participants, the Reserve Bank of India has today published on its website draft guidelines for diversification of Non-Banking Financial Companies into insurance business.

The comments may be sent to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department of Non-Banking Supervision, Reserve Bank of India, World Trade Centre, Centre I, Cuffe Parade, Colaba, Mumbai-400 005. Fax 2162768, E-mail: <u>rbidnbs@vsnl.com</u>
