## Dr. Bimal Jalan re-appointed RBI Governor

## April 26, 2000

The Government of India has reappointed Dr. Bimal Jalan as the Governor of the Reserve Bank of India for a further period of two years commencing November 22, 2000 to November 21, 2002. Dr. Jalan was appointed the Governor of the Reserve Bank in November 1997 for a period of three years. **Alpana Killawala General Manager** **Press Release : 1999-2000/1433**
