## Banking Statistics-Summary Tables-March 1999

## March 6, 2000

The Department of Statistical Analysis and Computer Services of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out the Banking Statistics-Summary Tables-March 1999. The publication is based on Basic Statistical Returns collected from the scheduled commercial banks. The booklet presents summary tables on number of offices, employees, deposits and credit of scheduled commercial banks as at the end of March 1999.

The publication is available for sale from the Sales Section (DRRP), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P.M. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.25. The publication is also available at the Reserve Bank's website (<u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in))</u>.
