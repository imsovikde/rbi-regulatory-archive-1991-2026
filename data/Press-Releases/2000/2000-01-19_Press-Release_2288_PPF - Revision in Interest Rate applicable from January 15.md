# PPF : Revision in Interest Rate applicable from January 15

## January 19, 2000

The Government of India has notified that with effect from January 15, 2000, the rate of interest applicable to the Public Provident Fund (PPF) is revised downwards by one percentage point to eleven percent per annum. The balances at the credit of the subscribers will earn interest at the rate of twelve per cent per annum up to January 14, 2000 and at eleven per cent per annum, thereafter. The collections under the PPF Scheme 1968, which were suspended from January 15, 2000 will be resumed from January 24,

2000.
