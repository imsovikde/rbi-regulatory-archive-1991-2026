## Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## May 12, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on May 11, 2000 the application for certificate of registration submitted by M/s. Osiaji Finance (Chennai) Ltd., No.101, Thirupalli Street, Sowcarpet, Chennai-600 079.

As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

## P. V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager
