## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 1, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.57 on

01.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

31 May 2000 44.5800 66.5869 41.3992 41.81 01 June 2000 44.5700 66.7881 41.7599 41.1100
