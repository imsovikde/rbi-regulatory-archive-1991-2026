## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 23, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.69 on 23.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date CURRENCY</u> **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 22 June 2000 44.6800 66.9262 42.2785 42.41 23 June 2000 44.6900 67.4417 41.9036 42.7100

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
