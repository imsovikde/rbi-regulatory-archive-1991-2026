## Current arrangements for Ways and Means Advances to the

## Government of India to continue for the financial year 2000-2001

## April 7, 2000

The arrangements for the fiscal year 2000-2001 in respect of Ways and Means Advances (WMA) to the Government of India and the rate of interest and the minimum balance required to be maintained with the Reserve Bank of India effective April 1, 2000 would be the same as those for the fiscal year 1999-2000. They are :

(a) The limit for WMA will be Rs.11,000 crore for the first half of the year (April to September) and Rs.7,000 crore for the second half of the year (October to March). When 75 per cent of the limit for WMA is utilised by Government, the Reserve Bank may trigger fresh floatation of market loans depending on market conditions.
(b) The interest rate on WMA will be at Bank Rate and on overdraft at Bank Rate plus two percentage points as under :
Rate per cent per annum

(i) Ways and Means Advances Bank Rate
( 7.0 )*

(ii) Overdrafts beyond the Ways Bank Rate plus two and Means Advances limits percentage points ( 9.0 )*

* Currently applicable w.e.f. close of business on April 1, 2000
(c) The minimum balance required to be maintained by the Government of India with the Reserve Bank of India is not less than Rs.100 crore on Fridays and not less than Rs.10 crore on other days.
(d) As per the provisions of the Agreement dated March 26, 1997 between the Government of India and the Reserve Bank of India, overdrafts beyond ten consecutive working days will not be allowed.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
