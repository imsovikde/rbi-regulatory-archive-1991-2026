# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 21, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.64 on 21.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :
