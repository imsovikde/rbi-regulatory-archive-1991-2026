# RBI REFERENCE RATE FOR US $

## June 7, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.69 on 07.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

# <u>Date</u> CURRENCY

# 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

# 06 June 2000 44.6700 67.7041 42.2869 41.70

# 07 June 2000 44.6900 68.2103 42.7326 42.2100

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P. V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager**
