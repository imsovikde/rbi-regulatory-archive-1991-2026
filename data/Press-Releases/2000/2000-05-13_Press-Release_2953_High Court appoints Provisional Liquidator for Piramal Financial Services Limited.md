## High Court appoints Provisional Liquidator for Piramal Financial Services Limited

## May 13, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India had filed a petition in the High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad under Section 45 MC of the RBI Act, 1934 for winding up of M/s. Piramal Financial Services Limited, an NBFC having its registered office at Ahmedbad.

After hearing the petition on May 10, 2000, Honourable Justice K. M. Mehta has passed an order appointing the Official Liquidator attached to the High Court of Gujarat as Provisional Liquidator in the case. The Court has further ordered an ad-interim injunction restraining the respondents, their agents from encumbering, transferring, alienating and dispossessing any of the assets of the respondent company.
