## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## May 31, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.58 on

31.05.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY**
## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

30 May 2000 44.5800 66.7474 41.4839 41.70 31 May 2000 44.5800 66.5869 41.3992 41.8100

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
