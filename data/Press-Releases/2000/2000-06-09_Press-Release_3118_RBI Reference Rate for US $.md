## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 9, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.76 on 09.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :

## <u>Date</u> CURRENCY

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

08 June 2000 44.7800 68.0432 42.8992 42.25 09 June 2000 44.7600 67.6055 42.6988 42.1800

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
