## RBI cancels licence of Shree Veraval Vibhagiya

## Nagrik Sahakari Bank Ltd.

## August 24, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the licence of Shree Veraval Vibhagiya Nagrik Sahakari Bank Ltd., Veraval, Dist. Junagadh (Gujarat) to carry on banking busines in India. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of ‘banking’ as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (as applicable to Co-operative Societies). Thus, it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from the public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
