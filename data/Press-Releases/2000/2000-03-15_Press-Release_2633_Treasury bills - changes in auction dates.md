## Treasury bills-changes in auction dates

## March 15, 2000

Since **March 17, 2000** has been declared Holiday for **"Bakari-Id"** under Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 by the **Government of Maharashtra,** the dates of auction and payment in respect of Treasury Bills during the week have been revised as shown under :

|Type of||Scheduled||Revised|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Treasury Bill|Date of|Date of|Date of|Date of|
||Acution|Payment|Auction|Payment|
|182 Days|15.3.2000|17.3.2000|15.3.2000|16.3.2000|
|14 Days|17.3.2000|18.3.2000|16.3.2000|18.3.2000|
|91 Days|17.3.2000|18.3.2000|16.3.2000|18.3.2000|
