## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 28, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.67 on 28.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below : <u>Date</u> **CURRENCY** **1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN** 27 June 2000 44.6700 67.0028 42.0233 42.3100 28 June 2000 44.6700 67.2172 42.2288 42.3300

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
