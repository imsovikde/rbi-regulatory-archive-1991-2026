## Monitoring FIIs investments in Indian Companies

## November 15, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Silverline Technologies Ltd. up to 40 per cent of their paid up capital through stock exchanges under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), as this company has passed resolutions at its Board of Directors' Meeting and a special resolution in the General Body Meeting to this effect.
