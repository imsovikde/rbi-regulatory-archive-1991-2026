# High Court appoints Provisional Liquidator for Lalbhai Finance Limited

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## High Court appoints Provisional Liquidator for

## Lalbhai Finance Limited

## April 7, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India had filed a petition in the High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad under Section 45 MC of the RBI Act, 1934 for winding up of M/s. Lalbhai Finance Limited, an NBFC having its registered office at Ahmedbad.

After hearing the petition on March 22, 2000, Honourable Justice K. M. Mehta has passed an order appointing the Official Liquidator attached to the High Court of Gujarat as Provisional Liquidator in the case. The Provisional Liquidator has been ordered to take charge of all assets and properties of the company along with the books of accounts, papers and other accounts of the company. The Court has further ordered an ad-interim injunction restraining the respondents, its agents or servants from encumbering, transferring, alienating and/or dispossessing any of the assets of the respondents.
