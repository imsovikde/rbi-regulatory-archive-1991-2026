## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## June 13, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.78 on

13.06.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :
## <u>Date CURRENCY</u>

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

12 June 2000 44.7800 67.3939 42.5410 41.93 13 June 2000 44.7800 67.8910 42.9082 41.9900

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
