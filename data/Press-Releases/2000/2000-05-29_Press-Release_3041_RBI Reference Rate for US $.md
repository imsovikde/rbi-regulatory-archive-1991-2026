## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## May 29, 2000

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.45 on

29.05.2000. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of major currencies against the rupee are given below :
## <u>Date</u> CURRENCY

## 1 USD 1 GBP 1 EURO 100 YEN

26 May 2000 44.3100 65.4126 40.6013 41.22 29 May 2000 44.4500 66.2083 41.2763 41.5700

Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
