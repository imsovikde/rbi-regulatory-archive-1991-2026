## Report of the Task Force on Empowering RRB Boards for Operational Efficiency

In order to strengthen Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) and make them viable rural financing institutions, various measures have been taken from time to time. With a view to increasing the operational efficiency of RRBs, Reserve Bank set up a Task Force in September 2006 under the chairmanship of Dr K.G.Karmakar, Managing Director, NABARD to deliberate on areas where more autonomy could be given to the Boards of RRBs and suggest measures for empowering the Boards of RRBs for operational efficiency, particularly in matters relating to investment, business development and manpower requirement, etc.

The Task Force submitted its report to Reserve Bank and its recommendations are under examination/ implementation.
