## June 19, 2007

## RBI's Account Closing to be on July 2, 2007

The Reserve Bank of India will remain closed for public transactions on Monday, July 02, 2007 to facilitate annual closing of its accounts. All the departments of the Reserve Bank, which normally work on Saturdays, shall remain open for public transactions on Saturday, June 30, 2007.
