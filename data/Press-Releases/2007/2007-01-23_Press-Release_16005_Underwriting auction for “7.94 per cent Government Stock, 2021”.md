## Underwriting auction for “7.94 per cent Government Stock, 2021”

The Government of India have notified sale (re-issue) of “7.94 per cent Government Stock 2021” for an amount of Rs. 5000 crore on January 25, 2007. The underwriting auction for the stock will be conducted on **January 24, 2007**. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auction are as follows: <u>(Rs.Crore)</u>

|Nomenclature|Notified|MUC for|ACU for which bidding has to be|
|---|---|---|---|
|of the Security|Amount|each PD|done by PDs collectively|
|7.94% GS 2021|5000 148||2484|

The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on January 24, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
