## August 24, 2007

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS :

## ICICI Bank Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase equity shares of ICICI Bank Limited under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), through secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the holdings of FIIs in the company have gone down below the trigger limit of 74 per cent of its paid up capital.
