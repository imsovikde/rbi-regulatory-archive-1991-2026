## March 8, 2007

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 3.17 paise per Rs. 100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “8.07% Government Stock 2017” and at 9.00 paise per Rs. 100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “8.33% Government Stock 2036” to be held on March 9, 2007.
