## New Ferritic Stainless Steel Coins of Re.1 denomination with the theme "UNITY IN DIVERSITY"

The Reserve Bank of India has put into circulation new Ferritic Stainless Steel Coins of Re.1 with the theme "UNITY IN DIVERSITY".

The coins conform to the following dimension, design and composition, namely :

|Denomination of the coin|Shape and|Metal composition|
|---|---|---|
||outside diameter||
||Cicular : 25 millimetres|Ferritic Stainless Steel|
|One Rupee|(Mono-metallic)|Chromium - 17% Iron - 83%|
