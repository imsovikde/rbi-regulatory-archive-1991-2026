## February 07, 2007

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on February 07, 2007 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through primary and secondary markets in India without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that aggregate foreign shareholding of Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., has reduced to less than 74 per cent of its paid up capital.
