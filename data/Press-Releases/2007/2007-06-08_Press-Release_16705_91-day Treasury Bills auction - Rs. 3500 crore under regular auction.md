June 8, 2007 **91-day Treasury Bills auction : Rs. 3500 crore under regular auction**

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government of India Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.3500 crore under regular auction. The auction will be conducted on June 13, 2007 using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation to the non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in st the General Notification No. F.2 (12)-W & M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, June 13, 2007 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by successful bidders will be on Friday, June 15, 2007.
