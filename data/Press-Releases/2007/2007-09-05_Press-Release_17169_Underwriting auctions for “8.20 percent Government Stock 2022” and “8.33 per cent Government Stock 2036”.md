## September 5, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for “8.20 percent Government Stock 2022” and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036”

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “8.20 per cent Government Stock 2022**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) and “8.33 percent Government Stock 2036**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on September 7, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on September 6, 2007.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: Rs. crore **Nomenclature of the Notified MUC amount per ACU amount** **Security Amount PD**

8.20 per cent GS 2022 4000 112 1984
<u>8.33 percent GS 2036 3000 84 1488</u>
The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on September 6, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
