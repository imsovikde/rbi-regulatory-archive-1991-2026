December 11, 2007 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.39.36 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.96 on December 11, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 10, 2007) were Rs.39.38 and Rs.57.66 respectively.
