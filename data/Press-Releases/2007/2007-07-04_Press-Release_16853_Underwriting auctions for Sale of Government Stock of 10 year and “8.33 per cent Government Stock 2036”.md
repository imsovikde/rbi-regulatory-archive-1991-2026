## July 4, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for Sale of Government Stock of 10 year and

## “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of a new 10 year security for an amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal) and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036” for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) on July 6, 2007. The underwriting auctions for the stocks will be conducted on July 5, 2007. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:

## (Rs. crore)

||Nomenclature of|Notified|MUC amount|ACU|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||the Security|Amount|per PD|amount|
|New 10 year Government Security repayable on July 9, 2017||6000|167|2994|
|8.33% GS 2036||4000|112|1984|

The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM and

12.30 PM on July 5, 2007. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
