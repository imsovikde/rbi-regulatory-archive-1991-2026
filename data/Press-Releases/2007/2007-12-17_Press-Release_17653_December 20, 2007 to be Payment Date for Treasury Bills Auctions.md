## December 20, 2007 to be Payment Date for Treasury Bills Auctions

The date of payment for both the auctions – 91-day and 364-day Treasury Bills has been advanced by one day to December 20, 2007 (Thursday) since December 21, 2007 has been declared as a Public Holiday under Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 by the Government of Maharashtra. The date for auctions of 91-Day and 364-Day Treasury Bills will remain unchanged on December 19, 2007 (Wednesday).
