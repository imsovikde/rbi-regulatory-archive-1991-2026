## August 6, 2007

## Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi signs MoU with Reserve Bank of India

The Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi and the Reserve Bank of India have, on August 6, 2007 signed a Memorandum of Understanding with regard to Urban Co-operative Banks in the State of Delhi. Consequent upon this, a State level Task Force on Urban Co-operative Banks (TAFCUB) has been constituted. Shri H. R. Khan Regional Director for Delhi, Reserve Bank of India, is the Chairman of the TAFCUB and Shri U.K.Worah, Registrar of Cooperative Societies is its Co-Chairman. The other members of the TAFCUB are : Shri

K.D.Vora representing the National Federation for Urban Cooperative Banks (NAFCUB), Shri P.R. Mehra representing the State Federation of Urban Cooperative Banks, Joint Secretary, Finance, Government of NCT of Delhi, from the State Government and a representative of Urban Banks Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai. The first meeting of the TAFCUB will be held shortly. MoUs have also been signed with the Governments of the States of Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Uttaranchal, Chattisgarh, Goa, Maharashtra and Haryana. As part of its developmental role, the Reserve Bank would also be assessing the training, computerization needs of the Urban Co-operative Banks in the State of Delhi with the objective of upgrading their human resources skills and technological infrastructure so that they improve their operational efficiency and quality of management information systems. It may be recalled that in the Mid-Term Review of Annual Policy announced in October 2004, the Reserve Bank had announced that a Vision Document for Urban Co-operative Banks would be prepared. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank placed in public domain a draft Vision Document in March 2005. As the Urban Co-operative Banks are subject to dual control by the Reserve Bank and the Government (Central/State), the Vision Document envisaged the signing of MoU between respective Government and the Reserve Bank of India to put in place an arrangement through which there is a convergence on the approach and remedial actions required to be taken for facilitating the development of the sector. The Vision Document also proposed the constitution of a Task Force of Urban Co-operative Banks with representatives of Government, Federation / Association of Urban Cooperative Banks and the Reserve Bank of India as its members, for the purpose of identifying the potentially viable urban co-op. banks and drawing up a time-bound action plan for their revival by setting specific monitorable milestones.
