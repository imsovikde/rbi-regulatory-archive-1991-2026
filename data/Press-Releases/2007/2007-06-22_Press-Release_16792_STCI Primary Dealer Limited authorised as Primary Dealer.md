## June 22, 2007

## STCI Primary Dealer Limited authorised as Primary Dealer

STCI Primary Dealer Limited has been authorised to undertake Primary Dealer business with effect from Monday, June 25, 2007 and, concurrently, the authorisation given to Securities Trading Corporation of India Limited (STCI), as Primary Dealer, stands withdrawn.
