## Underwriting auctions for “7.49 per cent GS 2017” and “8.33 per cent GS 2036”

The Government of India has notified sale (re-issue) of “7.49 per cent Government Stock 2017” for an amount of Rs. 6000 crore (nominal) and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036” for an amount of Rs. 4000 crore (nominal) on May 11,

2007. The underwriting auctions for both the stocks will be conducted on May 10,
2007. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: Rs. crore **Nomenclature of Notified MUC for ACU for which bidding has to be** **the Security Amount each PD done by PDs collectively**
7.49% GS 2017 6000 167 2994
<u>8.33% GS 2036 4000 112 1984</u>
The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on May 10,

2007. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
