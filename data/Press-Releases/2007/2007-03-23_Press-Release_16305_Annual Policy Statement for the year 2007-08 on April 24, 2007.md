## Annual Policy Statement for the year 2007-08 on April 24, 2007

Dr. Y.V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will present the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2007-08 on Tuesday, April 24, 2007. He will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on that day in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
