## Revised Reporting System for

## Direct Investment Overseas by Indian Residents

As announced in the Annual Policy Statement for 2007-08, the Reserve Bank of India has put in place with immediate effect, a revised reporting framework on overseas investments. The circular and the reporting formats are available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>).

The key features of the reporting system are:

Ø A single application cum reporting format replaces the existing six formats.

Ø A comprehensive coverage on the costs of acquisition overseas and the methods of financing forms part of the data.

Ø Simplified Annual Performance Reporting – submission of financial statements has been dispensed with

Ø A new format to capture disinvestments / liquidation, etc. has been introduced.

Online reporting will be introduced once the system stabilises and the accuracy and timeliness of submission improves.
