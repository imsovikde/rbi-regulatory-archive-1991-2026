## November 27, 2007

## Six State Governments announce Auctions of

## State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.5,497.353 on November 30

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of **Rs. 5,497.353 crore** through an yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State Notified Amount

|Sr.|||
|---|---|---|
|No.||(Rs. crore)|
|1|Andhra Pradesh|500.000|
|2|Himachal Pradesh|200.000|
|3|Maharashtra|1,597.353|
|4|Rajasthan|500.000|
|5|Tamil Nadu|600.000|
|6|West Bengal Total|2,100.000 5,497.353|

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional subscription upto a maximum of Rs.150.000 crore over**

## and above their notified amount.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on November 30, 2007 (Friday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Friday, November 30, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on November 30, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on December 03, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on November 29, 2007 (Thursday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
