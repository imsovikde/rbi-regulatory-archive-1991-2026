## January 24, 2007

**th** **19 Conference of State Finance Secretaries**

th The 19 Conference of the State Finance Secretaries was held in Pune today. Finance Secretaries of 19 States participated. Senior officials from the Ministry of Finance, Government of India and representatives from Comptroller and Auditor General of India, Controller General of Accounts and Planning Commission attended the Conference. Smt. Shyamala Gopinath, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India chaired the Conference.

The Reserve Bank has been, since 1997, organising the State Finance Secretaries' Conference twice a year. The Conference all through has provided a very useful forum for interactions among State Finance Secretaries, senior officials of Government of India, Planning Commission, Comptroller and Auditor General, and the Reserve Bank of India. The State Finance Secretaries play a key role in the development process of their respective States and strive to achieve transparency, stability, fairness and efficiency in the conduct of fiscal policies. The deliberations in the past Conferences have served to arrive at a consensus on various issues in furtherance of better financial management and fiscal prudence in the States.

Today's Conference aimed to take the consultative process further. Apart from an assessment of the market borrowing programme of the State Governments and operational issues pertaining to Government transactions, the discussions primarily focussed on issues relating to indicative calendar of State Government open market borrowings, non-competitive bidding in respect of State Development Loans, re-issuance of State Government securities and payment of brokerage/commission on State Development loans.

The Conference was held in Pune this time so that senior functionaries of the State and Central Governments could also participate in the inaugural celebrations of the Joint India-IMF Training Programme (ITP), housed in the campus of the National Institute of Bank Management (NIBM), Pune. The ITP would cater, inter-alia, to the training requirements of officials from the State and Central Governments. Mr. John Lipsky, First Deputy Managing Director, International Monetary Fund and Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India also addressed the participants of the Conference on this occasion.
