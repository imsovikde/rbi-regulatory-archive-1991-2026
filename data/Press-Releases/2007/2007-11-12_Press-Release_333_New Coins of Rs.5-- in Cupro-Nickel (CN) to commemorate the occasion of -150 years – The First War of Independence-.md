New Coins of Rs.5/-in Cupro-Nickel (CN) to commemorate

the occasion of "150 years - The First War of Independence"

The Reserve Bank of India will put into circulation New Cupro-Nickel (CN)

coins of Rs.5/- issued by the Government of India to commemorate the occasion of

"150 Years-The First War of Independence".
