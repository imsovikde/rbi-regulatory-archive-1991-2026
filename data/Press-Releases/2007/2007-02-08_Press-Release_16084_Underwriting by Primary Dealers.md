## February 8, 2007

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at 7.00 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “7.37% Government Stock 2014” and at 3.33 paise per Rs.100 in the underwriting auction conducted for the auction of “8.33% Government Stock 2036” to be held on February 9, 2007.
