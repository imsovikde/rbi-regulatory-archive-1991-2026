## November 12, 2007

# Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s Unitech Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) investors can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s Unitech Limited, through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto the limits as stated below:

(i) FII investment limit upto 100% (ii) NRI/PIO investment limit upto 24% The company has passed resolutions to this effect at the Board of Directors' and the Extra-Ordinary General Meetings.
