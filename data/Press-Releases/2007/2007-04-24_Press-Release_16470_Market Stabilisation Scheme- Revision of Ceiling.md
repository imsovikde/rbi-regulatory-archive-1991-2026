## April 24, 2007

## Market Stabilisation Scheme: Revision of Ceiling

In accordance with the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) of the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), the Government of India and the Reserve Bank have revised the ceiling for the outstandings under the MSS to Rs.95,000 crore with a threshold of Rs.85,000 crore at which the ceiling will be reviewed in future.

In March 2007, the MSS ceiling was fixed at Rs. 80,000 crore, with a threshold level for review of the ceiling at Rs. 70,000 crore. In accordance with the provisions of the MOU, the Reserve Bank of India formally notified the Government of India earlier this month that the outstandings under MSS has crossed the threshold limit of Rs.70,000 crore and requested the Government to increase the ceiling and the threshold to enable it to take recourse to the scheme as and when required. The revision in the ceiling has since been effected. The MSS outstandings currently stand at Rs.75,521 crore.

The Reserve Bank, under the existing arrangements, subject to variations in liquidity, announces every Friday the auctions under the MSS, covering the Treasury Bills and dated securities, if any, for the succeeding week. These arrangements would continue until further notice.
