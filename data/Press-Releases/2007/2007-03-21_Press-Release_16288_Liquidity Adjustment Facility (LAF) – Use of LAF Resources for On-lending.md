March 21, 2007 <u>Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) – Use of LAF Resources for On-lending</u>

There have been some enquiries regarding RBI's indication that, *inter-alia*, resources accessed from the Reserve Bank's LAF window should not be used for on-lending by banks. In this context, it is clarified that the intent of this advice is that the recourse to LAF by market participants should not be persistent in order to fund balance sheets for credit needs of customers, but banks can utilize the funds borrowed under this facility for inter-bank lending. Such, inter-bank lending is part of normal money market functioning that enables daily liquidity management by market participants having temporary mismatches.
