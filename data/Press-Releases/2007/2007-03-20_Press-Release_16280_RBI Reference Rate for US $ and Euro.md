## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs. 43.98 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs. 58.49 on March 20, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 16, 2007) were Rs. 44.17 and Rs. 58.67 respectively.
