## Underwriting auction for “7.38 percent Government Stock, 2015” and “8.33 per cent Government Stock, 2036”

The Government of India has notified sale (re-issue**)** for ‘7.38 percent Government Stock, 2015” for an amount of Rs. 6000 crore (nominal) and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036” for an amount of Rs 4000 crore (nominal) on April 12, 2007. The underwriting auction for the stock will be conducted on April 11, 2007. As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: <u>( Rs.Crore)</u>

|Nomenclature|Notified|MUC for|ACU for which bidding has||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|of the Security|Amount|each PD|to be done by PDs|collectively|
|7.38% GS 2015|6000|167||2994|
|8.33% GS 2036|4000|112||1984|

The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS- PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on April 11, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
