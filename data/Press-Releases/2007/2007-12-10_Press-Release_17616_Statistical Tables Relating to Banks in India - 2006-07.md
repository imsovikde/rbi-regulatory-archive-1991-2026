## STATISTICAL TABLES RELATING TO BANKS IN INDIA : 2006-07

The Reserve Bank of India has today released its Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India : 2006-07.

The publication presents detailed tables on assets and liabilities and earning and expenses of Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) for the year 2006-07. In addition to detailed bank-wise tables, the publication provides summary tables giving aggregated data for bank groups as well as for all banks.

The publication also gives data on important parameters like maturity and ownership pattern of deposits, sector-wise bank credit, investment pattern, state- wise distribution of employees, insured deposits, major items of priority sector lending both at state-wise and bank-wise, monthly data on sector-wise and industry- wise deployment of credit, Non Performing Assets (NPAs), bank-wise data on loans subjected to restructuring, corporate debt restructured and maturity profile of selected items of the balance sheet at bank group level. In addition, the publication also presents the list of amalgamated regional rural banks and bank-wise list of foreign offices of Indian scheduled commercial banks.

The publication is also being brought out in the CD-ROM format. Besides providing all the tables, graphs and maps given in the publication, The CD-ROM, also gives inter-active data querying facility.

The data published in this volume are also made available through Internet at <u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>
