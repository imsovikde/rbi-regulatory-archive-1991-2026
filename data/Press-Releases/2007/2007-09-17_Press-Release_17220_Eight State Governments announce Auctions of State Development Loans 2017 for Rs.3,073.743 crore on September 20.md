# Eight State Governments announce Auctions of State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.3,073.743 crore on September 20

www.rbi.org.in <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>**\**hindi **PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION**, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279

## September 17, 2007

## Eight State Governments announce Auctions of

## State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.3,073.743 crore on September 20

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.3,073.743 crore through an yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State

|Sr.||Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|---|

|5|Kerala||435.995|
|6|Punjab||1,000.000|
|7|Sikkim||112.105|
|8|Tamil Nadu Total||500.000 3,073.743|

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional**

**subscription upto a maximum of Rs.125.00 crore over and above the notified amount.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on September 20, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, September 20,

2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on September 20, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on September 21, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
