## RBI releases Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy 2006-07

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the ninth volume of its annual statistical publication Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy 2006-07. This publication is one of the major initiatives of the Reserve Bank aimed at improving data dissemination by providing a useful storehouse of statistical information at one place. Through this publication, the Reserve Bank has been providing time series data on various economic and financial indicators for the Indian economy.

The print volume contains 254 statistical tables covering national income aggregates, output, prices, money, banking, financial markets, public finances, foreign trade and balance of payments, and select socio-economic indicators. In addition to the tables already contained in the earlier volume, the present volume contains five new tables that provide: (i) yearly information on deposits and credit of scheduled commercial banks according to population groups since 1980-81; (ii) monthly information on broad commodity composition of India’s merchandise exports and imports-oil and non-oil, in US dollar terms, beginning 1997-98; (iii) monthly information on broad commodity composition of India’s merchandise exports and imports-oil and non-oil, in Rupees, beginning 1997-98; (iv) monthly information on stock market price-book value ratios on an aggregate basis beginning 1991; and (v) yearly information on assets under management of mutual funds beginning 1996-97.

The Handbook is brought out in three versions-print, CD-Rom and website. The CD-Rom incorporates intelligent search facilities that allow users to search across tables enabling the user to select one or more data series for a selected period from any table. The web-version can be downloaded from the RBI website: [http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)
