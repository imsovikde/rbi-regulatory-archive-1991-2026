## June 8, 2007

## Government of India to sell dated securities for Rs. 5000 crore on June 12

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.49 per cent Government Stock 2017” for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal). The Government Stock will be sold through price based auction using multiple price method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on June 12, 2007 (Tuesday).

Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of the stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

. Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 and RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on June 12, 2007. The NDS members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30

P.M. The result of the auction will be announced on June 12, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on June 13, 2007 (Wednesday). The Stock will qualify for the ready forward facility. The underwriting of the Government Securities under auction by the ‘Primary Dealers’ will be as per the “Revised Scheme of Underwriting and Liquidity Support” announced by the Reserve Bank vide circular RBI/2005-06/347 dated April 4, 2006. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on June 11, 2007 (Monday) using PMO module of NDS. The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading for a period commencing from June 11, 2007 to June 12, 2007 in accordance with the guidelines on ‘When Issued transactions in Central Government Securities’ issued by the Reserve Bank of India vide circular No. RBI /2006-07/178 dated November 16, 2006. On a review of the borrowing requirements of the Government of India, it has been decided in consultation with the Government of India to schedule this issuance over and above the issuances scheduled in the indicative calendar for marketable dated securities for the first half of the year 2007-08.
