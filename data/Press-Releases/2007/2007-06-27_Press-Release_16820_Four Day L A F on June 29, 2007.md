June 27, 2007 <u>Four Day L A F on June 29, 2007</u>

The Reserve Bank of India would conduct the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) on June 29, 2007 for a period of 4-days, with the reversal on July 3,

2007. This is on account of the Reserve Bank of India remaining close for public transactions on July 2, 2007 to facilitate annual closing of accounts.
