## February 15, 2007

## Seven State Governments announce the sale of 10-year

## State Development Loans for Rs.3399.573 crore on February 22

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.3,399.573 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu the option to retain additional subscription upto a maximum of Rs.125.00 crore**

**(Nominal) over and above the notified amount of Rs.500.00 crore.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on February 22, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, February 22,

2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on February 22, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on February 23, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility. Bids for underwriting of the Andhra Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh Government Stocks under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on February 21, 2007 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400
001.
