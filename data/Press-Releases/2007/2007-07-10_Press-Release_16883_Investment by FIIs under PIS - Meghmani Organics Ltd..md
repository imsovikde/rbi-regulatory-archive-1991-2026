## July 10, 2007

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Meghmani Organics Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares of Meghmani Organics Ltd. under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), through secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the holdings of FIIs in the company have gone down below the caution limit of its paid up capital.
