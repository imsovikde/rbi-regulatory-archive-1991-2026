## Debt Buyback by State Governments on February 22, 2007 – Reference Prices

As announced in the press release dated February 14, 2007 on the buyback of SDLs by two State Governments viz., Orissa and Rajasthan, the reference prices of the specified securities for the buyback auction are given in Annex I. The reference prices are derived by using the prevailing yields on Government of India securities of corresponding residual maturities as at the close of trading hours on February 21, 2007.

It may be noted that the reference prices are purely for the purpose of calculating discount percentages of offers made in the auction and in no way an indication of prices at which offers are to be made by the participants.
