## November 2, 2007

## Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of M/s. Pacific Netinvest Private Limited, New Delhi

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 15, 2007 cancelled the certificate of registration granted to M/s. Pacific Netinvest Private Limited, New Delhi having its registered office at Block E, 2nd Floor, International Trade Tower, Nehru Place, New Delhi-110019 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation of registration certificate, M/s. Pacific Netinvest Private Limited, New Delhi cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-banking financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
