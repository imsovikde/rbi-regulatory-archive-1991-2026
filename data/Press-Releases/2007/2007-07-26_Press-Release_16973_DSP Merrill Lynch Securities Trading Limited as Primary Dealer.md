## July 26, 2007

## DSP Merrill Lynch Securities Trading Limited as Primary Dealer

DSP Merrill Lynch Securities Trading Limited has been authorised to undertake Primary Dealer business with effect from Friday, July 27, 2007. Concurrently, the authorisation given to DSP Merrill Lynch Limited, as Primary Dealer, will stand withdrawn.
