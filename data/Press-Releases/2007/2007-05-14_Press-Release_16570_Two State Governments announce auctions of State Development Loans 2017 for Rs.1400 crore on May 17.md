# Two State Governments announce auctions of State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.1400 crore on May 17

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` **PRESS RELEASE**

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

www.rbi.org.in <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>**\**hindi **PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION**, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279

## May 14, 2007

## Two State Governments announce auctions of State

## Development Loans 2017 for Rs.1400 crore on May 17

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.1400.00 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

|Sr.|State|Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|---|
|No.||(Rs. crore)||
|1|Andhra Pradesh||400.000|
|2|West Bengal Total||1000.000 1400.000|

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on May 17, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, May 17, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on May 17, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on May 18, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on May 16, 2007 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
