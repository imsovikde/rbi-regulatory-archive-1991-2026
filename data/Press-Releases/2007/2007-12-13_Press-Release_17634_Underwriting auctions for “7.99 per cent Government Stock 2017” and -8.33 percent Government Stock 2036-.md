## December 13, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for “7.99 per cent Government Stock 2017” and

## "8.33 percent Government Stock 2036"

In the underwriting auctions for “7.99 per cent Government Stock 2017” and "8.33 percent Government Stock 2036" conducted on December 13, 2007, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below.

|Nomenclature of|Additional Competitive||Underwriting commission||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Security|Underwriting (Rs. crore)||cut-off rate (paise per Rs.100)||
|7.99% GS 2017||2492||0.64|
|8.33% GS 2036||993||0.46|

Auctions for the above securities will be held on December 14, 2007.
