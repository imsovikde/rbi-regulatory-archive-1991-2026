## October 11, 2007

# Investment by FIIs under PIS : Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd., through primary markets and stock exchanges in India without prior permission of Reserve Bank. The purchases can be made upto 49 per cent of the paid up capital of Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd., as it has passed resolutions to this effect at the Board of Directors' and the Annual General Body Meetings.
