## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.42.15 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.22 on April 19, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 18, 2007) were Rs.41.89 and Rs.56.93 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
