## Debt Buyback by Government of Orissa on March 23, 2007 –

## Reference Prices

As announced in the press release dated March 21, 2007 on the buyback of SDLs by Government of Orissa, the reference prices of the specified securities for the buyback auction are given in Annex I. The reference prices are derived by using the prevailing yields on Government of India securities of corresponding residual maturities as at the close of trading hours on March 22, 2007. It may be noted that the reference prices are purely for the purpose of calculating discount percentages of offers made in the auction and in no way an indication of prices at which offers are to be made by the participants.
