## Debt Buyback by Orissa State Government on March 23, 2007

*State Government of Orissa will buy back specified State Development Loans* *(SDLs) issued by them. A Scheme has been formulated for this purpose. The main* *features of the Scheme are as follows:*

1. The State Government will be offering to buyback a set of their securities, the list of which is provided in annex I.
*2.* The buyback of securities will take place through a multi security auction using multiple price auction method. *The auction will be conducted on March 23,* *2007 (Friday) between 10.30 am to 12.30 pm for settlement on March 28,*
*2007.*
3. The auction will be on the basis of the *percentage discount offered by* *participants to the reference price* for each security. Reference price for each security will be announced on March 22, 2007.
4. The offers should be for a minimum of Rs.10000/- and in multiples of Rs.10000/- thereafter for each security.
*5.* The cut-off at the auction will be arrived at on the basis of the amount (in Face Value) targeted for buyback by the State Government *(see Annex I for amount* *and Annex II for illustration).*
6. The State Government retains the right to accept offers for an amount higher than the amount targeted for buyback at its discretion. Also, the state retains the right to reject any or all bids in the auction if the prices are not acceptable to them.
7. Successful participants at the buyback auction will be paid the full consideration (clean price plus accrued interest for the accepted face value) by credit to their current accounts maintained at the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
8. The buyback auction will be limited to members of Negotiated Dealing System (NDS).
9. For convenience, the buyback auction will be conducted on the existing NDS Primary Auction module. � For each security under the buyback, one issue will be created in NDS (similar to that of issuances). � Participants may submit their offers (amount and price for each security) in the normal primary auction bid submission screen. � Multiple bids per security are allowed. � Results, however, will be announced to the successful participants separately.
