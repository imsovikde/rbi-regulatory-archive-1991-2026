## Monitoring of Investment by NRIs and PIOs under PIS :

## Fours Soft Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on February 14, 2007 that Non- Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase equity shares of Fours Soft Ltd. under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) through secondary market in India. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the holdings by NRIs and PIOs in the company have gone below 10 per cent of its paid up capital.
