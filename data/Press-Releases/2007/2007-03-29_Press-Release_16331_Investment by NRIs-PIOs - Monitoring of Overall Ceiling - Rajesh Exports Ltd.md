## March 29, 2007

## Investment by NRIs/PIOs -

## Monitoring of Overall Ceiling : Rajesh Exports Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on March 29, 2007 has notified that the aggregate share holding of Non-Residents Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in Rajesh Exports Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 10 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that no further purchases of shares of Rajesh Exports Ltd., should, therefore, be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of NRIs/PIOs.
