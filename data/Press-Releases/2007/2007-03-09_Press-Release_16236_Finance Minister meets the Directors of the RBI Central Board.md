## Date : 09 Mar 2007

## Finance Minister meets the Directors of the RBI Central Board

The customary post-budget meeting of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India with the Finance Minister was held at New Delhi today. The meeting was also attended by Shri Pavan Kumar Bansal, Minister of State (Expenditure, Banking & Insurance), Shri K M Chandrasekhar, Secretary, Revenue and Shri Sanjiv Mishra, Secretary, Expenditure, Shri Vinod Rai, Secretary, Financial Sector and Dr. Ashok Lahiri, Chief Economic Advisor.

Besides Dr. Y. V. Reddy, Governor of the Reserve Bank, Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Smt. Shyamala Gopinath and Smt. Usha Thorat, Deputy Governors of the Reserve Bank attended the meeting. The other Directors of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India present at the meeting included Shri Y. H. Malegam, Chartered Accountant, Prof. Suresh Tendulkar, Economist and member of PM’s Economic Advisory Council, Prof. U.R.Rao, space scientist and Chairman, Governing Council, Physical Research Laboratory, Ahmedabad, Shri Lakshmi Chand, retired IAS officer of UP cadre, Shri H.

P. Ranina, Advocate, Supreme Court of India, Smt. Shashi Rekha Rajagopalan, consultant with specialization in accounts & finance, voluntary organizations and cooperative legislation, Shri Suresh Kumar Neotia, Chairman of Gujarat Ambuja Cement Co, Dr.A. Vaidyanathan, agricultural economist and Professor Emeritus of Madras Institute of Development Studies, Prof. Man Mohan Sharma, Chemical Scientist and Emeritus Professor of Eminence of Mumbai University Institute of Chemical Technology, Dr.D. Jayavarthanavelu, Chairman and MD of Laxmi Machine Works Ltd. and Shri Sanjay Labroo, Managing Director and CEO of Asahi India Glass Ltd.
## The Governor welcomed the Finance Minister.

In his address to the Reserve Bank Board, the Finance Minister explained that the Budget has been framed in the context of continuing healthy growth in the service sector and recent resurgence and competitiveness of the industrial sector. In view of the low growth observed in the agricultural sector in recent years, on which more than half of the population subsists, the Budget has focussed on measures that can revitalise the agricultural and rural economy. He mentioned that revenues have been buoyant reflecting healthy economic growth in the past year, and that he expected such growth to continue.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

He emphasised the importance of the containing inflation while maintaining high growth. The Reserve Bank has the responsibility of conducting monetary policy so that price stability is maintained while high growth continues. The Directors appreciated the Union Budget and also articulated their views on a range of issues covered by it. The Governor thanked the Finance Minister for addressing the Board.
