## December 14, 2007

## Memorandum of Instructions for Opening and Maintenance of Rupee/

## Foreign Currency Vostro Accounts of Non-resident Exchange Houses

Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website www.rbi.org.in a draft Memorandum of Instructions for opening and maintenance of rupee / foreign currency Vostro accounts of non-resident Exchange Houses for comments from Authorised Dealer Category-I banks.

In view of the increased volume of business being handled by the Exchange Houses and improvements in communication facilities between Exchange Houses and drawee banks, there was a need to revise the existing instructions and the same has been finalised in consultation with a few banks.

Comments on the draft circular may please be addressed to the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Foreign Exchange Department, Forex Markets Division, Central Office, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai- 400001 or through e-mail latest by December 21, 2007.
