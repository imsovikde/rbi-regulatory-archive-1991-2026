## Two new Executive Directors appointed in RBI

Shri G. Gopalakrishna and Shri H.R. Khan have been appointed Executive Directors of the Reserve Bank of India w.e.f. October 26, 2007. Shri

G. Gopalakrishna has been given charge of Department of Non-Banking Supervision, Foreign Exchange Department, Department of Expenditure and Budgetary Control, Premises Department and Customer Service Department. Shri H.R. Khan has the charge of Department of External Investments & Operations, Internal Debt Management Department and Department of Government and Bank Accounts.
