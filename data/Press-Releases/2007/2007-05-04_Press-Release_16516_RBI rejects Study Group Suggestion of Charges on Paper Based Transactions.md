# RBI rejects Study Group Suggestion of Charges on Paper Based Transactions

` **PRESS RELEASE**

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA** www.rbi.org.in <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>**\**hindi **PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION**, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u>

## May 4, 2007

## RBI rejects Study Group Suggestion of Charges on

## Paper Based Transactions

The Reserve Bank of India has decided not to accept Study Group's recommendation of levying a charge for paper based transactions. It may be recalled that an Internal Study Group on Migration from Paper Based Funds Movement to Electronic Funds Transfer had, among other suggestions, recommended levying a charge for paper based transactions to be borne by the bank customers in a bid to encourage them to move to safer and speedier electronic funds transfer. Incidentally, it may be noted that the Reserve Bank had not accepted similar suggestion received twice in the past.

The Reserve Bank of India has further clarified that the recommendations of any study group-internal or external-constituted by the Reserve Bank, are purely recommendatory in nature and do not reflect the views of the Reserve Bank. These reports are placed in the public domain for comments/suggestions.

The Reserve Bank continues to receive comments in respect of other suggestions made by the Study Group which are under consideration.
