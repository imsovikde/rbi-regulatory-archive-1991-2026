## December 6, 2007

## Agreement with Government of Union Territory of Puducherry

The Reserve Bank of India has entered into an Agreement under section 21A of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 with the Government of Union Territory of Puducherry. Under the agreement, which shall be effective from December 17, 2007, the Reserve Bank of India shall carry on the general banking business of the Government of Puducherry, manage its rupee public debt and act as the sole agent for investment of Government’s funds. The agreement is pursuant to the Government of Union Territories (Amendment) Act, 2001, which provides for the creation of a separate Public Account of a Union Territory and enables borrowing upon the security of the Consolidated Fund of the Union Territory, subject to agreed conditions.
