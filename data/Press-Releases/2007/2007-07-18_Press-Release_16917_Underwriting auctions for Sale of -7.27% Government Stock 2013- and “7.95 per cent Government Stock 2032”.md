## July 18, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for Sale of "7.27% Government Stock 2013" and

## “7.95 per cent Government Stock 2032”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of "7.27 per cent Government Stock 2013" for a notified amount of Rs.6,000 crore (nominal) and “7.95 per cent Government Stock 2032” for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal) on July 20, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on July 19, 2007.

As per the revised scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows:

## (Rs. crore)

|Nomenclature of||Notified|MUC amount|ACU|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||the Security|Amount|per PD|amount|
|7.27% GS 2013||6000|167|2994|
|7.95% GS 2032||3000|84|1488|

The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on July 19, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
