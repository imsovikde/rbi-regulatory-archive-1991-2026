## Underwriting by Primary Dealers – Auction of State Development Loan

The Reserve Bank of India has set the underwriting commission cut-off rate at 14 paise per Rs.100 in respect of the auction of West Bengal State Development Loan, 2017, which will be held on May 17, 2007.
