## December 14, 2007

## Report of the Internal Working Group on Currency Futures

The Reserve Bank of India had placed on its website the Report of the Internal Working Group on Currency Futures for public comments on November 16, 2007.

The comments/suggestions from a cross section of market participants have been received. The Reserve Bank will consider the comments/suggestions on the Report that will be received until December 31, 2007, which may please be addressed to the Chief General Manager, Foreign Exchange Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai or may be sent through e-mail.
