## February 22, 2007

## Debt Buyback by State Governments – Results

Auction for buyback of SDLs by two State Governments viz., Orissa and Rajasthan was conducted by the Reserve Bank of India today. The results of the auction are given below.

## Orissa

The total amount of Securities bought back was ***Rs 308.20 crore at a cut-off discount*** ***of 0.3700% to the reference prices.*** A total of 82 bids were received for the eligible securities for an amount of Rs.542.42 crore. The individual security wise details are given in Table I.

## Rajasthan

The total amount of Securities bought back was ***Rs 84.46 crore at a cut-off discount of*** ***0% to the reference prices.*** A total of 25 bids were received for the eligible securities for an amount of Rs.386.16 crore. The individual security wise details are given in Table 2.
