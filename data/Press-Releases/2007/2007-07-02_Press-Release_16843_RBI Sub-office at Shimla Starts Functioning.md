July 2, 2007 **RBI Sub-office at Shimla Starts Functioning**

The Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) Sub-office at Shimla has started functioning from today. The Sub-office has the Rural Planning and Credit Department with Financial Inclusion Cell and will cover the State of Himachal Pradesh.

Hon’ble Chief Minister of Himachal Pradesh, Shri Virbhadra Singh inaugurated the Sub-office on July 1, 2007.

The Sub-office is headed by Shri S. K. Bal, General Manager-in-Charge. The address and contact details are:

Flat No. B-478, Sector IV, New Shimla, Shimla – 171001 (H.P.). Tel. No. 0177-2670480, 0177-2670481 Fax.No.: 0177-2670482.
