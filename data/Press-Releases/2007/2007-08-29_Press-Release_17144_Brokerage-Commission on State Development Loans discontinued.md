## August 29, 2007

## Brokerage/Commission on State Development Loans discontinued

In terms of General Notifications issued by all the State Governments in July 2007, payment of brokerage/commission to banks/other eligible entities has been discontinued in case of State Development Loans issued through auction method under the market borrowing programme.

It may be recalled that the issuances of State Government securities under the market borrowing programme are subject to the terms and conditions stipulated in the General Notification on “Issue of State Government Securities” issued by State Governments and amended from time to time.
