## January 10, 2007

## Shri Sanjay Labroo nominated on RBI Central Board

The Government of India has nominated Shri Sanjay Labroo as Director of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India, for a period of four years with effect from January 2, 2007. Shri Labroo is the Managing Director & CEO of Asahi India Glass Ltd. Shri D.S. Brar retired as Director of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India.
