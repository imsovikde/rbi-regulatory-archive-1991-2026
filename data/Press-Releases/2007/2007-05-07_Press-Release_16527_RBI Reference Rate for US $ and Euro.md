## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs. 40.57 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs 55.19 on May 7, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 4, 2007) were Rs. 40.90 and Rs. 55.47 respectively.
