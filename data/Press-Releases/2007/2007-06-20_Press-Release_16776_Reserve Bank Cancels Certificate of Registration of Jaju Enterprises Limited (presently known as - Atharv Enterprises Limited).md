## June 20, 2007

## Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of Jaju Enterprises Limited

## (presently known as-Atharv Enterprises Limited)

The Reserve Bank of India has on May 29, 2007 cancelled the certificate of registration granted to Jaju Enterprises Limited (presently known as-Atharv Enterprises Limited) having its registered office at 1039 E, Rajaram Road, Kolhapur-416008 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution as the company has opted to exit from the business of a non-banking financial institution.

Following cancellation of registration certificate, Jaju Enterprises Limited, cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
