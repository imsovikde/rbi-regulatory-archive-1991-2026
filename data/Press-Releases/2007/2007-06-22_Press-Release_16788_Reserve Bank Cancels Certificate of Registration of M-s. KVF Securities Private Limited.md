## June 22, 2007

## Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of M/s. KVF Securities Private Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has on May 18, 2007 cancelled the certificate of registration granted to M/s. KVF Securities Private Limited having its registered office at Mahatta Towers, 54, B-Block, Community Centre, Janakpuri, New Delhi – 110058 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation of registration certificate, M/s. KVF Securities Private Limited, cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

Under powers conferred by Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of non-banking financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
