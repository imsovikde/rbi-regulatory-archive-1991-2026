## "7.95 percent Government Stock 2032" : Auction Results

In the underwriting auctions for “7.27 per cent Government Stock 2013” and "7.95 percent Government Stock 2032" conducted on July 19, 2007, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below.

## Nomenclature of Additional Competitive Underwriting commission cut-

## Security Underwriting (Rs. crore) off rate (paise per Rs.100)

7.27% GS 2013 2994 0.87 paise
7.95% GS 2032 1488 0.98 paise
Auctions for the above securities will be held on July 20, 2007.
