## New Ferritic Stainless Steel Coins of Rs. 2 denomination with the theme " NRITYA MUDRA"

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation new Ferritic Stainless Steel Coins of Rs. 2 with the theme " NRITYA MUDRA ". The coins shall conform to the following dimensions, designs and compositions, namely :

|Denomination of the coin|Shape and outside|Metal composition|
|---|---|---|
||diameter||
||Circular|Ferritic Stainless Steel|
|Two Rupees|27 millimeters|containing – Iron – 83%|
||(Mono-Metallic)|Chromium – 17%|
