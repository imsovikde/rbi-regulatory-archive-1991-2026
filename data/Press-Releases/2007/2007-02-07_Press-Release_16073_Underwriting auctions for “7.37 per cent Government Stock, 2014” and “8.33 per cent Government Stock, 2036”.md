## February 7, 2007

**Underwriting auctions for “7.37 per cent Government Stock, 2014” and “8.33 per cent Government Stock, 2036”**

The Government of India has notified sale (re-issue) of “7.37 per cent Government Stock 2014” for an amount of Rs. 6000 crore and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036” for an amount of Rs. 3000 crore on February 9, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the stocks will be conducted on February 8, 2007. As per the revised Scheme of Underwriting Commitment dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: (Rs. Crore)

|Nomenclature of|Notified|MUC for|ACU for which bidding has to||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|the Security|Amount|each PD|be done by PDs collectively||
|7.37% GS 2014|6000|177||2991|
|8.33% GS 2036|3000|89||1487|

The underwriting auctions will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on February 8, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
