## June 15, 2007

## Dr. Rakesh Mohan, RBI Deputy Governor, chairs Working Group on Capital Flows to EMEs

Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Deputy Governor, chaired today the first meeting of the Working Group on Capital Flows to Emerging Market Economies (EMEs) in Paris, hosted by Banque de France.

The Working Group has been constituted by the Committee on the Global Financial System (Chairman : Dr. Donald Kohn, Vice Chairman, Federal Reserve Board of the US) to explore the consequences of capital flows for EMEs, both the macroeconomic effects and the impact on the domestic financial system.

Twenty-seven central banks, both from industrial countries and major emerging market economies, are represented in the Working Group. The Group is expected to submit its report to the Committee on the Global Financial System in mid-2008.
