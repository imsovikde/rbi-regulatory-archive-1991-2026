## Underwriting auction for “8.07 per cent Government Stock, 2017”

The Government of India has notified sale (re-issue) of “8.07 per cent Government Stock 2017” for an amount of Rs. 6000 crore on April 27, 2007. The underwriting auction for the stock will be conducted on April 26, 2007. As per the revised scheme of underwriting dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auction are as follows: (Rs.Crore)

|Nomenclature|Notified|MUC for|ACU for which bidding has to|
|---|---|---|---|
|of the Security|Amount|each PD|be done by PDs collectively|
|8.07% GS 2017|6000|167||

The underwriting auction will be conducted by using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on April 26, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
