## August 10, 2007

## Eight State Governments announce Auctions of

## State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.3,334.439 crore on August 16

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.3,334.439 crore through an yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

## State

|---|---|---|---|

|7|Tamil Nadu||300.000|
|8|West Bengal Total||1,098.062 3,334.439|

**Note: Government of Madhya Pradesh and Tamil Nadu will have the option to**

**retain additional subscription upto a maximum of Rs.150.00 crore and Rs.75.00 crore (Nominal), respectively, over and above their notified amount.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on August 16, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, August 16, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on August 16, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on August 17, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the Primary Dealers up to 12.30 P.M. on August 14, 2007 (Tuesday) using PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
