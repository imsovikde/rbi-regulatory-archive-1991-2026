## September 21, 2007

## Conversion of Nationalised Banks' Recapitalisation Bonds

The Government of India have announced the issue of “8.13 per cent Government Stock 2022” for an aggregate amount of Rs. 2,495.28 crore (nominal), “8.28 per cent Government Stock 2027” for an aggregate amount of Rs.1,252.24 crore (nominal) and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2032” for an aggregate amount of Rs. 1,522.48 crore (nominal) to 19 nationalised banks on September 21, 2007. The Government Stocks are issued in lieu of outstanding amount of the following securities held by these nationalised banks. The Government Stocks are issued at par.
