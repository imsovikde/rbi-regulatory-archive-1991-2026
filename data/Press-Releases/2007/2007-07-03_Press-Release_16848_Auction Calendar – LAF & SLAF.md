## Auction Calendar – LAF & SLAF

The Reserve Bank of India announces the calendar of auction for Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) and Second Liquidity Adjustment Facility (SLAF) for the quarter July-September 2007. There is no change in the existing terms and conditions under which the Liquidity Adjustment Facility auctions are conducted.
