## November 21, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for “7.99 per cent Government Stock 2017” and “8.35 percent Government Stock 2022”

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.99 per cent Government Stock 2017**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal) and “8.35 per cent Government Stock 2022**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on November 23, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on November 22, 2007.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: (Rs. crore) **Nomenclature of the Notified MUC amount Minimum bidding commitment** **Security Amount per PD** **under ACU auction per PD**

7.99 per cent GS 2017 3000 79 79
<u>8.35 percent GS</u> 2022 4000 <u>106 106</u>
The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on November 22, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
