## March 30, 2007

## <u>Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Acceptance of SDLs under Repos</u>

With effect from April 3, 2007, State Development Loans (SDLs) would be treated as eligible securities under the LAF repo operations. The acceptance of SDLs would widen the collateral base for LAF. The operational guidelines for LAF are also being modified through notification to eligible entities.

It may be recalled that, in the Annual Statement on Monetary Policy for the year 2006-07 (paragraph 133) it was announced that the State Development Loans (SDLs) would be treated as eligible securities under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF). Necessary changes have now been made in PDO-NDS to enable the acceptance of the SDLs.
