## January 19, 2007

## Government of India to sell dated securities for Rs. 5,000 crore on January 25, 2007

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.94 per cent Government Stock 2021” for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal) through a <u>price based auction using multiple price method</u>. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on January 25, 2007 (Thursday).

Upto 5% of the notified amount of the sale of the stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 and RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on January 25, 2007. The NDS members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in electronic format <u>using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module</u> of NDS. All bids should be submitted by

<u>12.30 P.M .</u> The result of the auction will be announced on January 25, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on January 29, 2007 (Monday). The Stock will qualify for the ready forward facility. The underwriting of the Government Security under auction by the ‘Primary Dealers’ will be as per the “Revised Scheme of Underwriting and Liquidity Support” announced by the Reserve Bank vide circular RBI/2005-06/347 dated April 4, 2006. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on January 24, 2007 (Wednesday) using PMO module of NDS. The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading for a period commencing from January 22, 2007 to January 25, 2007 in accordance with the guidelines on ‘When Issued transactions in Central Government Securities’ issued by the Reserve Bank of India vide circular No. RBI /2006-07/178 dated November 16, 2006.
