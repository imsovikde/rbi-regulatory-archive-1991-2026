## November 06, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for “8.20 percent Government Stock 2022” and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036”

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “8.20 per cent Government Stock 2022**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal) and “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on November 08, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on November 07, 2007.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: <u>(Rs. Crore)</u> **Nomenclature of Notified MUC amount ACU the Security Amount per PD amount**

8.20 percent GS 2022 5000 132 2492
8.33 per cent GS 2036 3000 79 1499
The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on November 07, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
