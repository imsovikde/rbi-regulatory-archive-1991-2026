## Data on ECB/FCCB from August to December 2006

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB), both Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the months of August, September, October, November and December 2006.
