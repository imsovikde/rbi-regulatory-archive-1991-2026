## March 23, 2007

## Debt Buyback by Government of Orissa – Results

Auction for buyback of SDLs by the Government of Orissa was conducted by the Reserve Bank of India today. The results of the auction are given below.

The total amount of Securities bought back was **Rs 86.412 crore at a cut-off** **discount of 0.2132% to the reference prices.** A total of 34 bids were received for the eligible securities for an amount of Rs.195.1720 crore. The individual security wise details are given in Table I.
