March 14, 2007 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.31 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.58.47 on March 14, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 13, 2007) were Rs.44.22 and Rs.58.24 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are: <u>Currency</u> **Date** **1 GBP 100 YEN** March 13, 2007 85.2738 37.68 <u>March 14, 2007 85.4740 38.17</u> **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
