## February 23, 2007

## RBI expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

The Reserve Bank of India has further expanded the coverage of its "Database on Indian Economy" to include, static reports on Union Government Accounts, Financial Performance of NBFCs, Cheque clearing Data, Electronic Payment Systems and Advances of NABARD to State Co-operative Banks.

The additional reports being released through the Database on Indian Economy are:

|a)|Union Government A/c at a Glance|
|---|---|
|b)|New Capital Issues by Non-Government Public Limited Companies|
|c)|Financial Performance of NBFC|
|d)|Commercial Bank Survey|
|e)|Liquidity Aggregates|
|f)|State Co-op Banks maintaining Accounts with RBI|
|g)|Reserve Bank's Standing Facilities to Scheduled Commercial Banks|
|h)|Cheque clearing Data|
|i)|Retail Electronic Payment Systems (Card payments, EFT, ECS, etc.)|
|j)|Large Value Clearing and Settlement Systems|
|k)|Assistance sanctioned and disbursed by All-India Financial Institutions|
|l)|Advances Outstanding of NABARD to State Co-operative Banks – Yearly|
|m)|Advances of NABARD to State Co-operative Banks – Yearly|
|n)|Advances Outstanding of NABARD to State Co-operative Banks – Monthly|
|o)|Advances of NABARD to State Co-operative Banks – Monthly|

With the release of these data series, the Database on Indian Economy would now have 182 static reports (subject and frequency wise), 71 subject wise and 53 frequency wise data query templates covering various sectors of the Indian economy.

The Reserve Bank of India's Database on Indian Economy is also available on its website. The home page of the Database on Indian Economy, lists the data series available on it. The coverage of the Database is continuously being expanded on the basis of the feedback received from the users of the database.

Users are requested to provide their feedback through a feedback link on the site so as to enable further improvements in the coverage of data series in the system.
