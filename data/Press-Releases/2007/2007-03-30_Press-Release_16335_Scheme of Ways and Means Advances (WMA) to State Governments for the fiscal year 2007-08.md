## March 30, 2007

## Scheme of Ways and Means Advances (WMA) to State Governments for the fiscal year 2007-08

On a review of the State-wise limits of Normal Ways and Means Advances for the year 2006-07, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep these limits unchanged for the year 2007-08. Accordingly, the aggregate Normal WMA limit would be retained at Rs.9,875 crore in 2007-08. Other terms and conditions of the Scheme would also continue to remain unchanged for 2007-08.
