## A PROFILE OF BANKS 2006-07

The publication, "A Profile of Banks 2006-07", is an attempt to provide quick and handy access to bank-wise data on important performance indicators of scheduled commercial banks, excluding regional rural banks. The publication covers 18 important indicators like return on assets, CRAR, business per employee, etc. The publication contains bank-wise data for the years 2002-03 to 2006-07, presented in a bank-centric way. For quick comparison with the peer group, the publication also provides averages at bank group level and also at all banks' level for 2006-07. The publication also gives peer group comparisons for 2006-07 with 2005-06. This publication is the third volume in the series, the first volume being "A Profile of Banks 2004-05". The publication is priced at Rs.90/- (normal) and Rs.120/- (inclusive of postage) for inland and US$55 (inclusive of courier charges) and US$20 (inclusive of registered airmail) for abroad.
