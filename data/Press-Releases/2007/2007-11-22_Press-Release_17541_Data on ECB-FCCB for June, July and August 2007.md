## Data on ECB/FCCB for June, July and August 2007

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB), both Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the months of June 2007, July 2007 and August 2007.
