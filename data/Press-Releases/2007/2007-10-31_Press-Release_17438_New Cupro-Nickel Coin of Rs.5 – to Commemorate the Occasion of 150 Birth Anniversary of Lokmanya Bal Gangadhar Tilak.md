## Security edge for 5 Rupees :

The edge of the coin is milled with serrated or upright milling and Security edge. At the centre of the edge is a shallow groove with a design inside the two sections separated by blank spaces. This design consists of chain of beads in relief and each bead being followed by one inclined line in relief. There are total 30 lines and 30 beads.

The coin is a legal tender as provided in the Coinage Act, 1906. The existing Five Rupee Coins in circulation shall also continue to be legal tender.
