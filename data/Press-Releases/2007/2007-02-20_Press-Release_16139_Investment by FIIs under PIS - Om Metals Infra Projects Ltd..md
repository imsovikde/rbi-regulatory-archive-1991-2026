## February 20, 2007

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India on February 20, 2007 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary and secondary markets in India without obtaining the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Om Metals Infra Projects Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
