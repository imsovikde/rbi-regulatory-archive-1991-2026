## October 24, 2007

## Underwriting auctions for “7.27 per cent Government Stock 2013” and “8.35 percent Government Stock 2022”

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.27 per cent Government Stock 2013**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) and “8.35 per cent Government Stock 2022**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through auctions, to be held on October 26, 2007. The underwriting auctions for both the securities will be conducted on October 25, 2007.

As per the scheme of underwriting dated April 4, 2006, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the above auctions are as follows: Rs. crore

|Nomenclature of the|Notified|MUC amount per|ACU amount|
|---|---|---|---|
|Security|Amount|PD||
|7.27 per cent GS 2013|4000|112|1984|
|8.35 percent GS 2022|4000|112|1984|

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM to 12.30 PM on October 25, 2007.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of security.
