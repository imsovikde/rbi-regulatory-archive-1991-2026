## January 5, 2007

## Government of India to sell dated security for Rs.4000 on January 12, 2007

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “8.33 per cent Government Stock 2036” for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) through a price based auction using multiple price method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on January 12, 2007 (Friday).

Up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale of the stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 and RBI <u>website www.rbi.org.in</u> should be submitted to that Office on January 12, 2007. The NDS members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auction will be announced on January 12, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on January 15, 2007 (Monday).

The Stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.

The underwriting of the Government Securities under auction by the ‘Primary Dealers’ will be as per the “Revised Scheme of Underwriting and Liquidity Support” announced by the Reserve Bank on April 4, 2006. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on January 11, 2007 (Thursday) using PMO module of NDS.

The Stock will be eligible for “When Issued” trading in accordance with the guidelines on ‘When Issued transactions in Central Government Securities’ issued by the Reserve Bank of India on November 16, 2006.

It may be recalled that, as per the indicative calendar for issuance of marketable dated securities, the Government of India was to issue a security with a term of 10-14 years for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore during the period January 5-12, 2007 apart from the above security. However, on a review of the current borrowing requirements, the Government of India, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, has decided to cancel the issuance of the security with a term of 10-14 years for Rs.5,000 crore and to issue only the security with a term of over 20 years for Rs.4,000 crore.
