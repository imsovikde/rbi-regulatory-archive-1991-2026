## RBI Sub-office at Ranchi starts Functioning

The Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) Sub-office at Ranchi has started functioning. The Sub-office has the Rural Planning and Credit Department and Financial Inclusion Cell and will cover the State of Jharkhand.

Hon’ble Chief Minister of Jharkhand, Shri Madhu Koda inaugurated the Sub- office on November 15, 2007.

The Sub-office is headed by Dr. Rabi Mishra, General Manager-in-Charge. The address and contact details are:
