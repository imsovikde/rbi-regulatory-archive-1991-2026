## February 15, 2007

## Conversion of Nationalised banks Recapitalisation Bond

The Government of India have announced the issue of “8.20 per cent Government Stock 2022” for an aggregate amount of Rs. 1,632.33 crore (nominal), “8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027” for an aggregate amount of Rs.4,388.55 crore (nominal) and “8.28 per cent Government Stock 2032” for an aggregate amount of Rs. 2,687.11 crore (nominal) to 19 nationalized banks on February 15, 2007. The Government Stocks are issued in lieu of outstanding amount of “10 per cent Nationalised Banks’ Recapitalisation Bonds, 2006” aggregating to Rs. 4818.78 crore (nominal) and “10 percent Nationalised Banks’ (non-transferable) Special Security, 2006 aggregating to Rs.3889.21 crore (nominal) held by these nationalized banks. The Government Stocks are issued at par.

The Government Stocks will be transferable and eligible for ready forward transactions (Repo). The Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR).
