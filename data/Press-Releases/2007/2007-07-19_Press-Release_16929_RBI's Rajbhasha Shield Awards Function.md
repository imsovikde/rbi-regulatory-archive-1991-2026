## July 19, 2007

## RBI's Rajbhasha Shield Awards Function

Dr. Y. V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India, while awarding the shields for the year 2005-2006, said that to achieve the target of financial inclusion we have to invariably draw on the spread of Hindi as well as other Indian languages. In our country there is a large section of people who do not know English, therefore, it becomes imperative for us to use Indian languages, specially Hindi to converse with our customers. Governor said that he had spoken in Telugu during his recent visit to Andhra Pradesh while explaining the functions of the Reserve Bank so that the public can easily understand when spoken to in their own language.

The annual Reserve Bank Rajbhasha Shield was instituted in 1980 to encourage public sector banks to use Hindi in their day-to-day work. The Reserve Bank also conducts a competition for the house journals of banks and financial institutions. The Reserve Bank has also started an Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition from 2002-03.

Shri V. Leeladhar, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India while complimenting the prize winning banks and financial institutions observed that for the development of rural areas, our banking sector also has to play an important role along with the Government because the problems of rural areas can only be solved by providing loan facilities to rural artisans, businessmen, farmers and people doing smaller jobs. He added that Banks will have to make valuable contribution in providing basic banking to the villagers. It is a matter of concern for all of us that even now the banking services have not reached a greater part of the population in the villages. That is the reason why we are emphasising on extending banking services to rural areas. To comply with the above objective use of Hindi and Indian languages is not only necessary but also unavoidable.

On this occasion the Governor released the new edition of "Banking Glossary".

Earlier, Shri V. S. Das, Executive Director, RBI, welcomed the Chief Executives of banks and financial institutions. Shri H.N.Prasad, Principal Chief General Manager, Department of Administration and Personnel Management, RBI, proposed a vote of thanks. Shri Ramakant Sharma, Deputy General Manager, Rajbhasha Department, RBI conducted the programme.

The function was attended by chairmen and other senior executives of banks and financial institutions. List of prize winners is enclosed.
