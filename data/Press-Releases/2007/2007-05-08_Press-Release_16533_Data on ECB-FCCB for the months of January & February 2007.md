## Data on ECB/FCCB for the months of January & February 2007

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECB) and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB), both Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the months of January and February 2007.
