## January 11, 2007

## Committee on Financial Sector Assessment

In continuation of the Press Release : 2006-2007/378 dated September 14, 2006, regarding constitution of the Committee on Financial Sector Assessment, it has been decided to include Shri Vinod Rai, Secretary (Financial Sector), Government of India and Dr. Arvind Virmani, Principal Adviser, Planning Commission. Accordingly, the composition of the reconstituted Committee on Financial Sector Assessment is as follows :

Dr. Rakesh Mohan Chairman Shri Ashok Jha Co-chairman Deputy Governor Finance Secretary Reserve Bank of India Government of India

Shri Vinod Rai Member Secretary (Financial Sector) Government of India

Dr. Arvind Virmani Member Principal Adviser Planning Commission

Shri Madhusudan Prasad Member Joint Secretary (Fund Bank) Government of India
