## July 20, 2007

## IDBI Gilts Limited authorised as Primary Dealer

IDBI Gilts Limited has been authorised to undertake Primary Dealer business with effect from Tuesday, July 24, 2007 and, concurrently, the authorization given to IDBI Capital Market Services Limited, as Primary Dealer, stands withdrawn.
