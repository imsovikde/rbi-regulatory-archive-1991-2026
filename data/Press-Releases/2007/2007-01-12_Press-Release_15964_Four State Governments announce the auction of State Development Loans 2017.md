## January 12, 2007

## Four State Governments announce the auction of

## State Development Loans 2017

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.1,214.585 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

|Sr.|Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|
|No.|(Rs. crore)||
|1 Andhra Pradesh||300.000|
|2 Goa||100.000|
|3 Kerala||314.585|
|4 Tamil Nadu||500.000|

## State

## Total 1214.585

**Note: Government of Tamil Nadu will have the option to retain additional subscription upto a maximum of Rs.125.00 crore**

**(Nominal) over and above the notified amount of Rs.500.00 crore.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on January 18, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, January 18, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on January 18, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on January 19, 2007 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
