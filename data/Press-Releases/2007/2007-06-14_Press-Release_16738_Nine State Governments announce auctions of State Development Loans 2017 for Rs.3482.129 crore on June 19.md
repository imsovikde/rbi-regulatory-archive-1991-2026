## June 14, 2007

## Nine State Governments announce auctions of

## State Development Loans 2017 for Rs.3482.129 crore on June 19

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.3,482.129 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

Note: Government of Madhya Pradesh will have the option to retain additional

subscription upto a maximum of Rs.125.00 crore (Nominal) over and above the notified amount of Rs.500.00 crore.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on June 19, 2007 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai- 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, June 19, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on June 19, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on June 20, 2007 at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the West Bengal Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 P.M. on June 18, 2007 (Monday) using PMO module of NDS to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001.
