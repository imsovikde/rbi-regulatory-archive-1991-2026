## Greater Transparency in Market Borrowing Programme of

## State Governments - 2007-08

The Annual Policy Statement for the year 2006-07 had proposed, inter- alia, that “States, at their discretion and initiative, would be encouraged to develop an advance indicative open market borrowing calendar”. The proposal was discussed in the Conferences of State Finance Secretaries. It was decided, to begin with, to disseminate information on borrowings of State Governments in order to usher in greater transparency in the market borrowing programme in a progressive manner. The details of the State Governments’ market borrowing programme for the year 2007-08 are:

It is estimated that State Governments will raise Rs.35,113 crore under the market borrowing programme taking into account the net allocation, additional allocation to a few State Governments and repayments during the financial year 2007-08.

So far (up to September 12, 2007), 17 State Governments have raised Rs.12,026 crore through the sale of their State Development Loans (SDLs) through the auction method. Accordingly, State Governments are expected to raise the balance amount of Rs.23,087 crore during the remaining part of the year 2007-08 in a phased manner.

The State Governments/The Reserve Bank will have the flexibility to bring about modifications in the borrowings keeping in view the emerging requirements of the State Governments, market conditions and other relevant factors.
