## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors -Monitoring of Overall Ceiling : M/s SICAL Logistics Limited

The Reserve Bank India has notified on August 7, 2007 that no further purchases of shares of M/s SICAL Logistics Limited, should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS). The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) in M/s SICAL Logistics Limited, has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
