## May 8, 2007

## Government of Kerala announces the auction of

## State Development Loan 2017

The Government of Kerala has announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loan for an aggregate amount of Rs.350.000 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on May 10, 2007 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, May 10, 2007. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on May 10, 2007 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on May 11, 2007 (Friday) at Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai and Thiruvananthapuram. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility. **Ajit Prasad** Manager **Press Release :2006-2007/1522**
