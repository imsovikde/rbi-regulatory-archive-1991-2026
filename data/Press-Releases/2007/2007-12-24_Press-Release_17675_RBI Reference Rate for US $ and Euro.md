December 24, 2007 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.39.49 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.78 on December 24, 2007. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 20, 2007) were Rs.39.57 and Rs.56.85 respectively.
