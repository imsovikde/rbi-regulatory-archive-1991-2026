## June 15, 2007

## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors -Monitoring of Overall Ceiling : Educomp Solutions Ltd.

The Reserve Bank India has notified on June 15, 2007 that no further purchases of shares of Educomp Solutions Ltd., should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) in Educomp Solutions Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
