August 31, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3062 on August 31, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 28, 2015) was 66.0808. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 28, 2015 August 31, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.3673 74.4950 **1 GBP** 101.9230 102.3105 **100 YEN** 54.54 54.75 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
