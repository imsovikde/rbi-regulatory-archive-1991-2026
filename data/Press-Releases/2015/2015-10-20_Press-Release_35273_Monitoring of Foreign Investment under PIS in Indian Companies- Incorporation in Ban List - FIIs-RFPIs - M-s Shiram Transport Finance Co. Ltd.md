## October 20, 2015

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List-FIIs/RFPIs :

## M/s Shiram Transport Finance Co. Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in <u>M/s Shriram Transport Finance Co. Ltd.</u> has crossed threshold ban limit of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchase of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FII/RFPIs.
