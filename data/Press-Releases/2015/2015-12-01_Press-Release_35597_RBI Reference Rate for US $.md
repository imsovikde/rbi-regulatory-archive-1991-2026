December 01, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5180 on December 01, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 30, 2015) was

66.8148. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 30, 2015 December 01, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.6834 70.4359 **1 GBP** 100.3692 100.4355 **100 YEN** 54.41 54.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
