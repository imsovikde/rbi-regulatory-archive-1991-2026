## August 13, 2015

## RBI launches Sixth Pilot Round of Quarterly

## Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS):Q2 2015-16

The Reserve Bank of India has launched the sixth pilot round of the <u>Services &</u> <u>Infrastructure Outlook Survey</u>. The survey seeks to assess the business situation for the current quarter (July-September 2015, that is Q2:2015-16) from the services and infrastructure companies in India, and their perceptions for the ensuing quarter (October- December 2015, that is Q3:2015-16).

The services and infrastructure sector plays a pivotal role in the economic development. Considering the increasing importance of this sector, the Reserve Bank of India has initiated pilot survey to assess the business sentiments in Services & Infrastructure Sector.

The Reserve Bank encourages and welcomes the companies in Services and Infrastructure Sector to download the <u>survey schedule</u> from the website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). Queries/clarifications, if any, and the duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be emailed to or faxed to:

The Director, Division of Enterprise Surveys, Department of Statistics and Information Management, Reserve Bank of India, C-8, 2 nd floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai-400051. Phone-022-26578235, 022-26578234; Fax-022-26571555, <u>E-mail</u>.
