## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on October 27, 2015

**For** ` **200 billion between 10.00 am and 10.30 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `200 billion tomorrow (October 27, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 10.00 am and 10.30 am as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate repo will take place on October 30, 2015 (Friday).
