## Reserve Bank releases the results of Survey of Professional Forecasters on

## Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 32

The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the <u>results of the</u> <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators - (Round 32)</u> conducted during January 2015.

The results of the Survey represent views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
