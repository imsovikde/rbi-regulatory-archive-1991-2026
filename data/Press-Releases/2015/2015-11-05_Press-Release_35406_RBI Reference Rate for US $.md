November 05, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.6755 on November 05, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 04, 2015) was

65.5680. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 04, 2015 November 05, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.7970 71.3499 **1 GBP** 101.1255 101.0221 **100 YEN** 54.17 54.05 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
