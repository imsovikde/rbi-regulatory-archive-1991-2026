## July 28, 2015

## 24th Half Yearly Report on Management of Foreign

## Exchange Reserves – March 2015

th The Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>24 half yearly report on</u> <u>management of foreign exchange reserves</u> with reference to March 2015.

The latest position of foreign exchange reserves as on July 17, 2015 is as under:

## US $ Billion

|Foreign Exchange Reserves (i+ii+iii+iv)|353.33|
|---|---|
|i. Foreign Currency Assets (FCA)|328.93|
|ii. Gold|19.08|
|iii. Special Drawing Rights (SDR)|4.02|
|iv. Reserve Tranche Position (RTP)|1.30|

It may be recalled that in February 2004, the Reserve Bank had started a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the public domain for bringing about more transparency and enhancing the level of disclosure in relation to management of the country’s foreign exchanges reserves.
