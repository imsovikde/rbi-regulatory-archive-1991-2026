## April 08, 2015

## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 20,000 Crore between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 20,000 crore today (April 8, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 pm and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on April 10, 2015 (Friday).
