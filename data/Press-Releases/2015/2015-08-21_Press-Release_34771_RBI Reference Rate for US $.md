August 21, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.8298 on August 21, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 20, 2015) was 65.2385. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 20, 2015 August 21, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.5713 74.2692 **1 GBP** 102.2092 103.3857 **100 YEN** 52.59 53.55 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
