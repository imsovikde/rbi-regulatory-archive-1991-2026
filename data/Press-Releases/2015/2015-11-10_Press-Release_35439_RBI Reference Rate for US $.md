November 10, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3495 on November 10, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 09, 2015) was

66.3378. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 09, 2015 November 10, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.4458 71.2992 **1 GBP** 100.0308 100.2806 **100 YEN** 53.79 53.80 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
