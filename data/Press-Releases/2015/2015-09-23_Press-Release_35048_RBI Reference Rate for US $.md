September 23, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.9696 on September 23, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 22, 2015) was

65.6250. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 22, 2015 September 23, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.2638 73.3780 **1 GBP** 101.7319 101.1908 **100 YEN** 54.50 54.92 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
