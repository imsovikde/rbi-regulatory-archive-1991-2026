October 19, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8178 on October 19, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 16, 2015) was

64.9697. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 16, 2015 October 19, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.9940 73.7497 **1 GBP** 100.5536 100.1176 **100 YEN** 54.54 54.36 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
