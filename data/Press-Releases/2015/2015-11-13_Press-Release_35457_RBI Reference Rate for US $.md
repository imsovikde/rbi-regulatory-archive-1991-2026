November 13,2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.1390 on November 13, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 10, 2015) was

66.3495. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 10, 2015 November 13, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.2992 71.2648 **1 GBP** 100.2806 100.5511 **100 YEN** 53.80 53.90 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
