January 28, 2015 **Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities**

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) the Government Stock through auctions to be held on January 30, 2015. As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (` in crore) **Minimum bidding**

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified|MUC amount per|commitment per|
|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|Primary Dealer|PD under ACU auction|
|8.27 per cent GS 2020|3000|75|75|
|8.40 per cent GS 2024|5000|125|125|
|8.24 per cent GS 2033|2000|50|50|
|8.17 per cent GS 2044 The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction|3000|75|75|

method on January 29, 2015. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between 10.30 a.m. and

12.00 noon on the date of underwriting auction.. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
