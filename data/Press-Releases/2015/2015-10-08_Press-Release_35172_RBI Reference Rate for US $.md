October 08, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.1570 on October 08, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 07, 2015) was

65.2571. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 07, 2015 October 08, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.4664 73.3277 **1 GBP** 99.5301 99.7814 **100 YEN** 54.34 54.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
