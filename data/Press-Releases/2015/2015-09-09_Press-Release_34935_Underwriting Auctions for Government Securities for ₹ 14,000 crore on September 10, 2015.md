## September 9, 2015

## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities for 14,000 crore on September 10, 2015

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of the Government Stock through auctions to be held on September 11, 2015.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer (PD), are as under:

( in crore) **Minimum bidding** **Nomenclature of the Notified MUC amount commitment per PD under** **Security Amount per PD** **ACU auction**

7.68% GS 2023 2,000 48 48
7.72% GS 2025 6,000 143 143
8.24% GS 2033 3,000 72 72
8.13% GS 2045 3,000 72 72 The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction
method on September 10, 2015. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on the date of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
