## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl.No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (Crore)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|April 17, 2015 (Friday)|23,500|13|April 30, 2015 (Thursday)|
|2.|April 21, 2015 (Tuesday)|15,500|14|May 05, 2015 (Tuesday)|
|3.|April 24, 2015 (Friday)|15,500|14|May 08, 2015 (Friday)|
|4.|April 28, 2015 (Tuesday)|15,000|14|May 12, 2015 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.
