August 20, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.2385 on August 20, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 19, 2015) was 65.2525. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 19, 2015 August 20, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.1954 72.5713 **1 GBP** 102.2507 102.2092 **100 YEN** 52.50 52.59 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
