## April 07, 2015

## Reserve Bank releases the results of forward-looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey- Q4: 2014-15 (Round 69)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS) -</u> <u>October- December 2014 (Round 28)</u> iii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey- March 2015</u>
iv. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households-March 2015 (Round 39)</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators - March 2015</u> <u>(Round 33)</u> The results of the Surveys are based on the responses received from the
respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
