September 08, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6060 on September 08, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 07, 2015) was

66.7445. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 07, 2015 September 08, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.2733 74.6054 **1 GBP** 101.3649 102.3002 **100 YEN** 55.93 55.60 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
