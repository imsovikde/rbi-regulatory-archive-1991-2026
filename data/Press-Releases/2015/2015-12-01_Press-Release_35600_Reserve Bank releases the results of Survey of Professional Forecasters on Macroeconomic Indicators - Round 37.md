## December 01, 2015

## Reserve Bank releases the results of Survey of

## Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 37

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators -(Round 37)</u> conducted during November 2015.

The results of the Survey represent views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
