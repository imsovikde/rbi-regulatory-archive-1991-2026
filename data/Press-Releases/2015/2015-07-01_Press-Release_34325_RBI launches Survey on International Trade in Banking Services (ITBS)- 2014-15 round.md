July 01, 2015 **RBI launches Survey on International Trade in** **Banking Services (ITBS): 2014-15 round** The Reserve Bank of India has launched the 2014-15 round of its Survey on <u>International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS)</u>. The survey, conducted annually since 2006-07, provides information on financial services rendered by branches/subsidiaries of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India, based on explicit/implicit fee/commission charged to customers. The Survey Schedule for the 2014-15 round is required to be filled by the branches/subsidiaries/joint venture/associate of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India. The soft form of this <u>survey</u> <u>schedule</u> with in-built validation checks is available on the RBI website under the head ‘Forms’ (available under the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’, which can be duly filled-in, validated and <u>emailed</u> by July 31, 2015. In case of any query / clarification, kindly contact: The Director, External Liabilities and Assets Statistics Division, Department of Statistics and Information Management (DSIM), Reserve Bank of India, C-9, 5 th floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai-400 051. <u>Email:</u> Phone: 022- 26578458/217/351.
