August 17, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.2200 on August 17, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 14, 2015) was 65.1225. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 14, 2015 August 17, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.5595 72.3942 **1 GBP** 101.6171 102.2324 **100 YEN** 52.35 52.42 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
