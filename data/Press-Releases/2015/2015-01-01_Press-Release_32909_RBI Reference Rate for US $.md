## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.3213 on January 1, 2015.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 31, 2014) was

63.3315. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||December 31, 2014|January 1, 2015|
|1 EUR|77.0048|76.5998|
