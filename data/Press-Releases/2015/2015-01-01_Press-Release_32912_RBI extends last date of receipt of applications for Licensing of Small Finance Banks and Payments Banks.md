## RBI extends last date of receipt of applications for Licensing of

## Small Finance Banks and Payments Banks

The Reserve Bank of India received requests seeking extension of time for submission of applications for <u>Small Finance Banks</u> / <u>Payments Banks</u>, the last date for which was January 16, 2015. Keeping in view the difficulties expressed by various parties, it has been decided to extend the last date of receipt of applications to February 2, 2015.

Accordingly, applications will be accepted till the close of business as on February 2, 2015.
