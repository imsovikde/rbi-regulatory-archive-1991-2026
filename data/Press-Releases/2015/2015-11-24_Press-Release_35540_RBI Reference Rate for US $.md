November 24, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3840 on November 24, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 23, 2015) was

66.3490. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 23, 2015 November 24, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.4693 70.5529 **1 GBP** 100.6647 100.4523 **100 YEN** 53.87 54.10 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
