## June 10, 2015

## RBI releases its Monthly Bulletin for June 2015

The Reserve Bank of India today released the June 2015 issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes Governor’s Statement on Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy for the Year 2015-16, Speeches by the Top Management and Current Statistics. It also includes two articles: (1) <u>Industrial Outlook Survey: 2014-15</u> and

(2) <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households: 2014-15</u>.
## 1. Industrial Outlook Survey: 2014-15

The Reserve Bank’s quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey gives insight into the perception of a select group of manufacturing companies about the current business environment and prospects one quarter ahead.

## Main Findings:

- The business sentiments in the manufacturing sector improved during FY: 2014-15. The Business Expectation Index (BEI) based on assessment perked up during the first two quarters of the year. The index which had dipped in Q3:2014-15, bounced back in the Q4: 2014-15 partly due to seasonality. BEI (based on expectation) remained upward during the first three quarters followed by some moderation in the last quarter.
- On an annual average basis, the BEI (both assessment and expectation) recorded marked improvement in FY: 2014-15 as compared to the previous year due to higher optimism in majority of parameters that are used for the computation of BEI.
- Improved sentiments on cost of finance and cost of raw material were observed during FY: 2014-15 along with some moderation in selling price. However, the net response of profit margin, although in negative terrain, improved in FY: 2014-15 over the previous year.
## 2. Inflation Expectations Survey of Households: 2014-15

The Reserve Bank’s Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) captures the inflation expectations of 5,000 urban households across 16 cities for the next three-month and the next one-year period. This article analyses the changes in households’ inflation perceptions and expectations in recent times especially focusing on the four quarters of the year 2014-15 (Q1: 2014-15 to Q4: 2014-15). The results of this survey are based on replies of the respondents and do not necessarily reflect the perceptions of the Reserve Bank of India.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

## Main Findings:

- Inflation perceptions and expectations of households moderated in the last two quarters of 2014-15. The moderation was well spread across the cities and occupation categories of respondents covered in the survey.
- Percentage of respondents expecting general prices to ‘increase more than current rate’ decreased from 41.2 per cent (Q1: 2014-15) to 28.3 per cent (Q4: 2014-15) for three-month ahead period and 47.4 per cent (Q1: 2014-15) to
32.9 per cent (Q4: 2014-15) for one-year ahead period.
- The moderation observed in general price expectations was spread across various product groups.
- Significant variation was observed in inflation perceptions and expectations among various cities.
