## November 24, 2015

## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities for 15,000 crore on November 26, 2015

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of the Government Stock through auctions to be held on November 27, 2015.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer (PD), are as under:

( in crore) **Minimum bidding** **Nomenclature of MUC amount** **Notified Amount** **commitment per PD the Security per PD under ACU auction**

8.17% GS 2044 2,000 48 48
7.72% GS 2055 1,000 24 24 The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction
method on November 26, 2015 (Thursday). PDs may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between

10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on the date of underwriting auction. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
