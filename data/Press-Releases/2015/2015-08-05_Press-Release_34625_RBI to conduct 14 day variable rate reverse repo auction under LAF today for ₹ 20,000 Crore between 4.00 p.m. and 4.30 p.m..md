## August 5, 2015

## RBI to conduct 14 day variable rate reverse repo auction under

## LAF today for 20,000 Crore between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 14 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of 20,000 crore today (August 05, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 14 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on August 19, 2015 (Wednesday).
