## August 17, 2015

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF Operations on August 18, 2015

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep LAF Reverse Repo and MSF windows open on August 18, 2015 (Tuesday). The operations will be conducted between 7.00 p.m. and 7.30 p.m. The reversal will take place on August 19, 2015 (Wednesday).
