September 30, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.7418 on September 30, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 29, 2015) was

66.1843. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 29, 2015 September 30, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.6096 73.7952 **1 GBP** 100.4611 99.5331 **100 YEN** 55.42 54.80 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
