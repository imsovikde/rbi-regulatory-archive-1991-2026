September 02, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.1638 on September 02, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 01, 2015) was

66.2603. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 01, 2015 September 02, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.7880 74.6063 **1 GBP** 101.9945 101.2174 **100 YEN** 55.05 55.09 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
