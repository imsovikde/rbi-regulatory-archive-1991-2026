January 1, 2015 **Results of Underwriting Auctions**

In the underwriting auctions conducted on January 1, 2015 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: ( crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.27 per cent GS 2020|3000|1500|1500|3000|0.19|
|8.60 per cent GS 2028|6000|3000|3000|6000|0.24|
|8.24 per cent GS 2033|2000|1000|1000|2000|0.32|
|8.17 per cent GS 2044|3000|1500|1500|3000|0.37|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on January 2, 2015.
