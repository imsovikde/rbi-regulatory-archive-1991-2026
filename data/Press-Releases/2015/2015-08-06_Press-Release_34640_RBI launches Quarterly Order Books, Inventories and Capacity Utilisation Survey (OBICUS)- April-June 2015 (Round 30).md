## August 06, 2015

## RBI launches Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and

## Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS): April-June 2015 (Round 30)

th The Reserve Bank of India has launched the <u>30 round</u> of its Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS). The survey is for the reference period April-June 2015 (Q1:2015-16).

The Reserve Bank has been conducting the Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS) of the manufacturing sector on a quarterly basis. The survey seeks quantitative information on order books, inventories and capacity utilisation, such as, pending order books, backlog order books, total inventories, finished goods inventories, work in progress inventories, installed capacity, quantity produced, capacity utilisation, value of production, etc., from companies involved in manufacturing activities. The information on installed capacity, quantity produced, value of production, etc., is used for calculating the capacity utilisation at industry as well as at an all India level. The survey has been providing a significant input to the Reserve Bank in monetary policy formulation.

A <u>detailed annual article</u> based on the results for the quarters October- December 2012 to July-September 2013 was published in <u>April 2014</u> issue of the RBI th monthly bulletin. The survey results for the <u>29 round (Q4:2014-15)</u> were released on the RBI website on August 4, 2015.

M/s Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy Pvt. Ltd. (CMIE), Mumbai, has been mandated to conduct the survey for this round on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India. According to the articles of agreement, the term of contract will expire after this round in September 2015. During this quarter the major manufacturing companies will be approached by the agency. Other manufacturing companies that are not approached by the agency may also participate in this survey round by downloading the <u>survey schedule</u> from the Reserve Bank’s website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The survey schedule is placed under the head ‘Forms’ (available under the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘Survey’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be <u>e-mailed</u> or faxed to the agency as per contact details provided in the survey schedule.
