November 26, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5507 on November 26, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 24, 2015) was

66.3840. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 24, 2015 November 26, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.5529 70.6968 **1 GBP** 100.4523 100.6446 **100 YEN** 54.10 54.30 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
