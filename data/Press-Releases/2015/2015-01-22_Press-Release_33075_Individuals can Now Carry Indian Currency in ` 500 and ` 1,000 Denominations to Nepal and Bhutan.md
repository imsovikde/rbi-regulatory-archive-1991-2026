January 22, 2015 **Individuals can Now Carry Indian Currency in** **500 and 1,000 Denominations to Nepal and Bhutan**

The Reserve Bank of India has today announced that an individual can now carry to Nepal and Bhutan Indian currency notes in denominations of `500 and/or `1,000, subject to a limit of `25,000. Earlier, individuals travelling to Nepal and Bhutan were allowed to carry Indian currency without any limit in denomination of only up to `100. This has been relaxed to mitigate the hardship faced by individuals travelling to Nepal and Bhutan from India.

The Reserve Bank of India has suitably amended the <u>regulations</u> to incorporate the relaxation.
