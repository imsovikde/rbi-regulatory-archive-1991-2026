November 17, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.9805 on November 17, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 16, 2015) was

66.1690. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 16, 2015 November 17, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.9729 70.3484 **1 GBP** 100.6563 100.1056 **100 YEN** 54.05 53.48 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
