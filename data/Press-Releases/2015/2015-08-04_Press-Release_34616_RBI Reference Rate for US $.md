August 04, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.9315 on August 04, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 3, 2015) was 63.9623. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 03, 2015 August 04, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.1602 70.0497 **1 GBP** 99.9347 99.8226 **100 YEN** 51.56 51.59 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
