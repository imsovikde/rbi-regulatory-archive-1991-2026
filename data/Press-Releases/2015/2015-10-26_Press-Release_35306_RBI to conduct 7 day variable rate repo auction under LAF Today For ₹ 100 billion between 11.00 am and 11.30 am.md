## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **100 billion between 11.00 am and 11.30 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 100 billion today (October 26, 2015, Monday). The auction will be conducted between **11.00 am and 11.30 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on November 02, 2015 (Monday).
