February 13, 2015 **RBI to conduct 7 day Variable rate Repo Auction under LAF Today** **For** ` **25,000 Crore between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 25,000 crore today (February 13, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between **3.45 pm and 4.15 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on February 20, 2015 (Friday).
