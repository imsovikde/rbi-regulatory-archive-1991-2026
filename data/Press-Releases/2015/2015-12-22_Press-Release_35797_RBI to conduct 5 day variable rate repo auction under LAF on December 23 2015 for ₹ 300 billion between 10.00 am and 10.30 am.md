## RBI to conduct 5 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on December 23,

**2015 for** ` **300 billion between 10.00 am and 10.30 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 5 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 300 billion on December 23, 2015, Wednesday. The auction will be conducted between **10.00 am and 10.30 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 5 day variable rate repo will take place on December 28, 2015 (Monday).
