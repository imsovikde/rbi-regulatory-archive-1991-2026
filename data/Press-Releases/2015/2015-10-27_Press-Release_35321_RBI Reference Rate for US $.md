October 27, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9553 on October 27, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 26, 2015) was

64.9603. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 26, 2015 October 27, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.6447 71.8406 **1 GBP** 99.5452 99.7324 **100 YEN** 53.63 53.83 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
