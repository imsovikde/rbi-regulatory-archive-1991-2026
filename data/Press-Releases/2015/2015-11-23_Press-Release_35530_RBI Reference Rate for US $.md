November 23, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3490 on November 23, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 20, 2015) was

66.0940. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 20, 2015 November 23, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.8594 70.4693 **1 GBP** 101.0379 100.6647 **100 YEN** 53.82 53.87 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
