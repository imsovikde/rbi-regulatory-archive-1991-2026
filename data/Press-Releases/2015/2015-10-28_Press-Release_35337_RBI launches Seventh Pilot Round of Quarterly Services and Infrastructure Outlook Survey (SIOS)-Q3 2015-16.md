## October 28, 2015

## RBI launches Seventh Pilot Round of Quarterly Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS):Q3 2015-16

The services and infrastructure sector plays a pivotal role in the economic development. Considering the increasing importance of this sector, the Reserve Bank of India has initiated the pilot survey to assess the business sentiments in Services th and Infrastructure Sector. The Reserve Bank of India has now launched the 7 pilot round of the <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey</u>, seeking assessment of the business situation for the current quarter (October-December 2015 i.e. Q3:2015-16) from the services and infrastructure companies in India and their perceptions for the ensuing quarter (January-March 2016 i.e. Q4:2015-16).

Services and Infrastructure Sector companies are encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey schedule</u> from the Reserve Bank’s website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be e-mailed or faxed as per contact details given in the survey schedule. The responses of the individual companies will be kept strictly confidential.

In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact at the following address:
