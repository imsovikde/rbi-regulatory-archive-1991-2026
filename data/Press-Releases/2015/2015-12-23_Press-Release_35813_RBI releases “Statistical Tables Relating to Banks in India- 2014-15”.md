## December 23, 2015

## RBI releases “Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India: 2014-15”

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website ‘<u>Statistical Tables</u> <u>Relating to Banks in India: 2014-15</u>’ – an annual publication that provides key information on banks.

The publication includes bank-wise information on major items pertaining to liabilities and assets, income and expenses, non-performing assets, financial ratios, spatial distribution of offices, number of employees and details of priority sector advances etc. In addition, bank-wise yearly data on capital adequacy ratio (CRAR), movement of non-performing assets (NPAs) and unclaimed deposits etc. are also released in the publication. Monthly data on some major indicators, such as, aggregate deposits, bank credit, liabilities to the banking system, assets with the banking system, investments, and sector-wise and industry-wise gross bank credit are also included.

Data are published in time series format covering a period of 11 years from 2004-05 to 2014-15. Primary source of data in this publication is the audited annual accounts of the banks.

The publication is available only in electronic form on the RBI website through the link for Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).
