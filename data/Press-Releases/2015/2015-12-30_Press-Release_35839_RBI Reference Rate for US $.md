December 30, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4243 on December 30, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 29, 2015) was

66.3700. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 29, 2015 December 30, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.8477 72.6018 **1 GBP** 98.9112 98.5471 **100 YEN** 55.16 55.17 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
