## September 24, 2015

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF Operations on September 25, 2015

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep LAF Reverse Repo and MSF windows open on September 25, 2015 (Friday). The operations will be conducted between 7.00 p.m. and 7.30 p.m. The reversal will take place on September 28, 2015 (Monday).
