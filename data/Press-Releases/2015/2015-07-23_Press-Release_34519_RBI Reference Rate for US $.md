July 23, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7138 on July 23, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 22, 2015) was 63.5283. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 22, 2015 July 23, 2015** **1 EUR** 69.6016 69.6264 **1 GBP** 99.1931 99.5719 **100 YEN** 51.38 51.37 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
