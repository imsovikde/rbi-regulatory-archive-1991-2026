## March 31, 2015

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF Operations on April 2, 2015

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep LAF Reverse Repo and MSF windows open on April 2, 2015 (Thursday). The operations will be conducted between 7.00 PM and 7.30 PM. The reversal will take place on April 6, 2015 (Monday).
