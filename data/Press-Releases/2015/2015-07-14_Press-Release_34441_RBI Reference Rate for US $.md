July 14, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4978 on July 14, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 13, 2015) was 63.4655. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 13, 2015 July 14, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.6942 69.7269 **1 GBP** 98.4731 98.1993 **100 YEN** 51.77 51.48 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
