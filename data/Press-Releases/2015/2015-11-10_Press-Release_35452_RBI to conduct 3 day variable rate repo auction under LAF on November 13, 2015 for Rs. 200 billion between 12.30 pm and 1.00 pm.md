## November 10, 2015

## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on November 13,

## 2015 for Rs. 200 billion between 12.30 pm and 1.00 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 200 billion on November 13, 2015 (Friday). The auction will be conducted between **12.30 pm and 1.00 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate repo will take place on November 16, 2015 (Monday).
