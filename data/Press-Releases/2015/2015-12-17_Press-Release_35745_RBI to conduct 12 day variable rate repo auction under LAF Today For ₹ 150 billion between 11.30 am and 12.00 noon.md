## RBI to conduct 12 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **150 billion between 11.30 am and 12.00 noon**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 12 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `150 billion today (December 17, 2015, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between **11.30 am and 12.00 noon** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 12 day variable rate repo will take place on December 29, 2015 (Tuesday).
