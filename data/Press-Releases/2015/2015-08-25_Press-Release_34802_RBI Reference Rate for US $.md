August 25, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7090 on August 25, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 24, 2015) was 66.5093. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 24, 2015 August 25, 2015** **1 EUR** 76.2729 77.1089 **1 GBP** 104.0072 105.1000 **100 YEN** 54.97 56.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
