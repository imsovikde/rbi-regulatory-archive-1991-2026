## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.2878 on January 02, 2015.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 01, 2015) was

63.3213. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
