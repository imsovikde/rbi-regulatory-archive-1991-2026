July 20, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.5510 on July 20, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 17, 2015) was 63.4928. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 17, 2015 July 20, 2015** **1 EUR** 69.1437 68.8321 **1 GBP** 99.4297 99.1014 **100 YEN** 51.20 51.20 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
