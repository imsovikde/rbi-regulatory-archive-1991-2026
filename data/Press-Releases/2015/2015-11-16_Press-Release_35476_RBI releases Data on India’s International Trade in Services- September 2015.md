## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: September 2015

The Reserve Bank of India today released the data on India’s International Trade in Services for September 2015. The value of exports and imports of services during September 2015 are given in the table below:

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|July 2015|13,386|7,502|
|August 2015|13,580|7,773|
|September 2015|13,321|7,457|

<u>Note: Data are provisional.</u>

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The data being provisional would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released.
