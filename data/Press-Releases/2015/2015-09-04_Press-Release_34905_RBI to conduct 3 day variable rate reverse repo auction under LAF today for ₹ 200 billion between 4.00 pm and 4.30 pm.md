## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate reverse repo auction under

**LAF today for** ` **200 billion between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of ` 200 billion today (September 4, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on September 7, 2015 (Monday).
