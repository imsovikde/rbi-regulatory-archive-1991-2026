July 02, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.5705 on July 02, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 01, 2015) was 63.6223. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 01, 2015 July 02, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.7671 70.3535 **1 GBP** 99.8934 99.2018 **100 YEN** 51.85 51.52 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
