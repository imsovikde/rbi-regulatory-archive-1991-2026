December 03, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7450 on December 03, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 02, 2015) was

66.6158. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 02, 2015 December 03, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.6660 70.6696 **1 GBP** 100.3434 99.6102 **100 YEN** 54.12 54.08 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
