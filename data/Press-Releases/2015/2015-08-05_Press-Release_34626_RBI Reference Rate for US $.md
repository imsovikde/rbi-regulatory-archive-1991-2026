August 05, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is `63.8159 on August 05, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 4, 2015) was `63.9315. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 04, 2015 August 05, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.0497 69.3104 **1 GBP** 99.8226 99.1508 **100 YEN** 51.59 51.29 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
