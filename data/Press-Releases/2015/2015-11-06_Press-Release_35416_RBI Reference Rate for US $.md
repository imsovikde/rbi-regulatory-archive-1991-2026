November 06, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.7910 on November 06, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 05, 2015) was

65.6755. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 05, 2015 November 06, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.3499 71.5477 **1 GBP** 101.0221 99.9497 **100 YEN** 54.05 54.00 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
