## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate repo auction under

**LAF today for** ` **150 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 150 billion today (November 18, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m**.** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day variable rate repo will take place on November 20, 2015 (Friday).
