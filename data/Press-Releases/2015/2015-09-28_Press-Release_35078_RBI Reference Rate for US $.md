September 28, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.0805 on September 28, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 24, 2015) was

66.0993. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 24, 2015 September 28, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.9585 73.8119 **1 GBP** 100.8874 100.3168 **100 YEN** 55.08 54.92 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
