## Results of Underwriting Auctions

In the underwriting auctions conducted on May 7, 2015 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

( crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.68 per cent GS 2023|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|0.68|
|New security maturing on March 19, 2030|7,000|3,500|3,500|7,000|0.75|
|8.24 per cent GS 2033|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|1.20|
|8.17 per cent GS 2044|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|1.20|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on May 8, 2015.
