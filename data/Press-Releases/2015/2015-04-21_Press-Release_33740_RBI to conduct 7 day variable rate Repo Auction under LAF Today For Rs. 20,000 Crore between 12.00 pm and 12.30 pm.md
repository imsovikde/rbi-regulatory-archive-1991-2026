## April 21, 2015

## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 20,000 Crore between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 20,000 crore today (April 21, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 pm and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on April 28, 2015 (Tuesday).
