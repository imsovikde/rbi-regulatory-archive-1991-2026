October 14, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.1123 on October 14, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 13, 2015) was

65.0215. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 13, 2015 October 14, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.9164 74.2410 **1 GBP** 99.8860 99.4981 **100 YEN** 54.26 54.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
