## November 23, 2015

## RBI to conduct Overnight variable rate repo auction under

## LAF today for 100 billion between 1.00 p.m. and 1.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct Overnight variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 100 billion today (November 23, 2015, Monday). The auction will be conducted between 1.00 p.m. and 1.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight variable rate repo will take place on November 24, 2015 (Tuesday).
