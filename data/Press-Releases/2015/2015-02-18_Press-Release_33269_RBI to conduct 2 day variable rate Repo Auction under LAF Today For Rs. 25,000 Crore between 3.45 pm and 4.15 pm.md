## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 25,000 Crore between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 25,000 crore today (February 18, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day repo will take place on February 20, 2015 (Friday).
