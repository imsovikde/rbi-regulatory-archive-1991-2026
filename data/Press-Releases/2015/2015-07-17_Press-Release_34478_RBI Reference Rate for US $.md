July 17, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.4928 on July 17, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 16, 2015) was 63.4955. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 16, 2015 July 17, 2015** **1 EUR** 69.3942 69.1437 **1 GBP** 99.1736 99.4297 **100 YEN** 51.28 51.20 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
