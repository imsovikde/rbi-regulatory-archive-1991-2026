## RBI to conduct 5 day Reverse Repo Auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **20,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 5 day reverse repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 20,000 crore today (January 2, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00pm and 4.30pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 5 day reverse repo will take place on January 7, 2015 (Wednesday).
