## December 21, 2015

## RBI withdraws Directions issued to Shree Govardhansinghji Raghuvanshi Sahakari Bank Ltd., Nandurbar, Maharashtra

The Reserve Bank of India has withdrawn the All Inclusive Directions issued on September 9, 2015 to Shree Govardhansinghji Raghuvanshi Sahakari Bank Ltd., Nandurbar, Maharashtra, with effect from the close of business on December 19,

2015. The withdrawal of Directions is in exercise of powers vested in the Reserve Bank under sub-section (2) of Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies). A copy of the order is displayed on the banks premises for perusal by interested members of public. The bank will continue to undertake regular banking business hereafter.
