## March 20, 2015

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF Operations on

## March 21 and March 28, 2015

As announced on <u>February 20, 2015</u>, the Reserve Bank of India will conduct Reverse Repo and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) operations on March 21 and 28, 2015 between 2.00 p.m. and 2.30 p.m.
