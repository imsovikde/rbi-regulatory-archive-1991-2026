## Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on June 25, 2015

In the underwriting auctions conducted on June 25, 2015 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

( crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.35 per cent GS 2024|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|3.81|
|7.88 per cent GS 2030|6,000|3,000|3,000|6,000|4.20|
|7.95 per cent GS 2032|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|6.20|
|8.17 per cent GS 2044|3,000|1,500|1,500|3,000|6.20|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on June 26, 2015.
