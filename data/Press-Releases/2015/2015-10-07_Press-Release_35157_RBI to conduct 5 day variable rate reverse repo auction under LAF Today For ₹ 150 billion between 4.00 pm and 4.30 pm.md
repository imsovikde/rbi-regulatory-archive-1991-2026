## October 07, 2015

## RBI to conduct 5 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **150 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 5 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of ` 150 billion today (October 7, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 5 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on October 12, 2015 (Monday).
