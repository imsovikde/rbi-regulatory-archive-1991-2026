## September 16, 2015

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF Operations on September 17, 2015

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to keep LAF Reverse Repo and MSF windows open on September 17, 2015 (Thursday). The operations will be conducted between 7.00 PM and 7.30 PM. The reversal will take place on September 18, 2015 (Friday).
