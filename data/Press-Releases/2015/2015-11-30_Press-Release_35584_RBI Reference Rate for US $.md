November 30, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8148 on November 30, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 27, 2015) was

66.7503. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 27, 2015 November 30, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.8755 70.6834 **1 GBP** 100.6795 100.3692 **100 YEN** 54.53 54.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
