November 18, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.1138 on November 18, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 17, 2015) was

65.9805. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 17, 2015 November 18, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.3484 70.3914 **1 GBP** 100.1056 100.5128 **100 YEN** 53.48 53.63 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
