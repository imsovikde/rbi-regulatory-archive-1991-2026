## December 17, 2015

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Removal from the Caution List-FIIs/RFPIs

## M/s Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign share holding by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in M/s Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. have gone below the prescribed threshold caution limit stipulated under the extant FDI Policy. Hence, the restrictions placed on the purchase of shares of the above company are withdrawn with immediate effect. Therefore equity shares of M/s Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd. can now be purchased through primary market and stock exchanges.
