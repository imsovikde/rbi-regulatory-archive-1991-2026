September 14, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3708 on September 14, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 11, 2015) was

66.3866. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 11, 2015 September 14, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.8974 75.3242 **1 GBP** 102.5939 102.5429 **100 YEN** 55.00 55.18 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
