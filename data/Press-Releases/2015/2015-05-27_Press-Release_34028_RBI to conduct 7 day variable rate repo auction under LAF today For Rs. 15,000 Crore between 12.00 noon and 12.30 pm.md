## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF today

## For Rs. 15,000 Crore between 12.00 noon and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 15,000 crore today (May 27, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on June 3, 2015 (Wednesday).
