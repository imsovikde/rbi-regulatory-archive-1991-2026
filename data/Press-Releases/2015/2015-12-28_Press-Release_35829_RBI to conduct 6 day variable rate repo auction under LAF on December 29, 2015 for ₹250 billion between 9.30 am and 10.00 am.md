## December 28, 2015

## RBI to conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on

## December 29, 2015 for 250 billion between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 250 billion on December 29, 2015, Tuesday. The auction will be conducted between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 6 day variable rate repo will take place on January 04, 2016 (Monday).
