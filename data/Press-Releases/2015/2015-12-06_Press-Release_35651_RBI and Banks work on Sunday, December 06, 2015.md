## RBI and Banks work on Sunday, December 06, 2015

In order to help the people of Chennai in particular and Tamil Nadu in general, banks will be functioning on Sunday, December 06, 2015. The Reserve Bank-as part of its measures aimed at providing assistance to the flood hit parts of the country

- would also be open on Sunday to facilitate banks in their cash based transactions for their customers and also by supporting cash availability for banks from the currency chests.
