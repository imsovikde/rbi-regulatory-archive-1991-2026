## October 30, 2015

## Shri Shaktikanta Das nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Shri Shaktikanta Das, Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi as a Director on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Ajay Tyagi. The nomination of Shri Shaktikanta Das is effective October 30, 2015 and until further orders.
