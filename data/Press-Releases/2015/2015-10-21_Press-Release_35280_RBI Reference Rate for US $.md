October 21, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.1513 on October 21, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 20, 2015) was

64.8908. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 20, 2015 October 21, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.5213 73.9728 **1 GBP** 100.3926 100.5350 **100 YEN** 54.33 54.31 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
