July 28, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0270 on July 28, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 27, 2015) was 64.0028. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 27, 2015 July 28, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.6143 70.8843 **1 GBP** 99.4348 99.7156 **100 YEN** 51.85 51.84 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
