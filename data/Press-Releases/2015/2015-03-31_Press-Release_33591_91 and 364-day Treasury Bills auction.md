## 91 and 364-day Treasury Bills auction

The Reserve Bank had on March 27, 2015 announced vide <u>press release</u> <u>No.2041</u> and <u>2042</u> regarding the auction of 91 and 364 day Treasury Bill respectively scheduled on April 01, 2015. Keeping in view bunching of holidays and other technical reasons, it has been decided in consultation with the Government of India to reschedule the Treasury Bill auction from April 01, 2015 to April 06, 2015.

The competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system between

10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on **Monday, April 06, 2015**. The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 11.30 a.m. Results will be announced on the same day. Payments by successful bidders will be on **Tuesday, April 7, 2015.**
