December 07, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6263 on December 07, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 04, 2015) was

66.8365. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 04, 2015 December 07, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.0456 72.4028 **1 GBP** 101.0434 100.6524 **100 YEN** 54.51 54.05 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
