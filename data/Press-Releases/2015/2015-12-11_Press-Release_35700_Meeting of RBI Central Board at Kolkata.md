## December 11, 2015

**Meeting of RBI Central Board at Kolkata**

<u>Photographs</u>

The Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India met on Friday, December 11, 2015 at Kolkata. Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India, chaired the meeting. Besides Deputy Governors Shri Harun R. Khan, Dr. Urjit R. Patel, Shri

R. Gandhi and Shri S.S. Mundra, the other Directors of the Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India who attended the meeting were Dr. Nachiket Mor, Shri Y.C. Deveshwar and Prof. Damodar Acharya. The Board reviewed the current economic situation, global and domestic challenges and policy responses. The Board also reviewed the functioning of the Foreign Exchange Department, Financial Inclusion and Development Department, Department of Co-operative Banking Supervision, Department of Non-Banking Regulation and Department of Supervision. The Board also discussed the Financial Stability Report and the Trend and Progress for the half year ended December 2015. The plan for a coherent national strategy for Financial Literacy involving all the stakeholders and improving the effectiveness of the Financial Literacy and Credit Counselling (FLCC) centres was also discussed. The Central Board meets at least once every quarter. Apart from holding meetings in Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and in New Delhi after the Union Budget which is addressed by the Finance Minister, the rest of the meetings are held in other state capitals or cities by rotation. The main function of the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank is to provide overall direction to the Reserve Bank’s affairs.
