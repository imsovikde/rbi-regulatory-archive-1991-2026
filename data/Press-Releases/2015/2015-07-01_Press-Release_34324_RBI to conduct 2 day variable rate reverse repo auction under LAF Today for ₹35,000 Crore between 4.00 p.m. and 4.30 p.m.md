## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction under

**LAF Today for** ` **35,000 Crore between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of ` 35,000 crore today (July 1, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on July 3, 2015 (Friday).
