## RBI releases the Monthly Bulletin for August 2015

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>August 2015</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The bulletin includes <u>Third Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement; 2015-16</u> released on August 4, 2015, Speeches by the Top Management and Current Statistics.
