## May 12, 2015

## RBI to conduct 10 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 20,000 Crore between 12.00 noon and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 10 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 20,000 crore today (May 12, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 10 day variable rate repo will take place on May 22, 2015 (Friday).
