October 15, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8240 on October 15, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 14, 2015) was

65.1123. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 14, 2015 October 15, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.2410 74.5022 **1 GBP** 99.4981 100.4124 **100 YEN** 54.41 54.61 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
