## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities

**for** ` **15,000 crore on October 08, 2015**

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of the Government Stock through auctions to be held on October 9, 2015.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (` in crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|MUC PD|Minimum bidding amount per commitment per PD under ACU auction|
|---|---|---|---|
|7.68% GS 2023|2000|48|48|
|7.72% GS 2025|7000|167|167|
|New GS maturing on December 19, 2034|3000|72|72|
|8.17% GS 2044|3000|72|72|

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method on October 8, 2015. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on the date of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
