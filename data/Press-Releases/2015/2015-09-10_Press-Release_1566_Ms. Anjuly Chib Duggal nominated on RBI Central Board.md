## September 10, 2015

## Ms. Anjuly Chib Duggal nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Ms. Anjuly Chib Duggal, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi as a Director on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India vice Dr. Hasmukh Adhia. Ms. Anjuly Chib Duggal’s nomination is with effect from September 3, 2015 and until further orders.
