**Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility**

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

## *August 18, 2015 being holiday

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids.

In view of sizable surplus liquidity conditions prevailing all through July 2015 so far, market participants’ recourse to the Reserve Bank’s liquidity facilities has been muted. As against 14-day normal term repo auctions of about 67,000 crore (equivalent to 0.75 per cent of NDTL), the daily average amount of liquidity actually accessed by banks has been lower at about 46,200 crore. Under the fixed rate overnight repo facility, the daily average access by banks has been of the order of 1,300 crore as against the availability of about 22,300 crore (0.25 per cent of NDTL). These signals from the market suggest that the system level demand for liquidity remains below the amounts of assured liquidity provided by the Reserve Bank. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated October 8, 2013, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor. Accordingly, RBI will use this option as part of its liquidity management framework going forward.
