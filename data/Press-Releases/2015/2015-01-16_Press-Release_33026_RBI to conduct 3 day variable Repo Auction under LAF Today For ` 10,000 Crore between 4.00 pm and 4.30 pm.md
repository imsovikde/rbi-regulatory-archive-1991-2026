## RBI to conduct 3 day variable Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For 10,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of 10,000 crore today (January 16, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day repo will take place on January 19, 2015 (Monday).
