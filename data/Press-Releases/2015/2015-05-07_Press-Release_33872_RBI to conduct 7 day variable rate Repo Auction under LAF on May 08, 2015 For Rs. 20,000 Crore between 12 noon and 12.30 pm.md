May 07, 2015 **RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate Repo Auction under LAF on May 08, 2015** **For Rs. 20,000 Crore between 12 noon and 12.30 pm** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 20,000 crore on May 08, 2015, Friday. The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on May 15, 2015 (Friday).
