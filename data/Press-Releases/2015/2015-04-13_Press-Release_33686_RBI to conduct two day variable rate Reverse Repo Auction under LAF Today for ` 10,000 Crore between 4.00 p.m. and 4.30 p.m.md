## April 13, 2015

## RBI to conduct two day variable rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF Today

## for 10,000 Crore between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct two day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of 10,000 crore today (April 13, 2015, Monday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the two day variable rate reverse repo will take place on April 15, 2015 (Wednesday).
