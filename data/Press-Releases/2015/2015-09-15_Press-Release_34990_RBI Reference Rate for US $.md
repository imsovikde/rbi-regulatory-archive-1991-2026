September 15, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4383 on September 15, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 14, 2015) was

66.3708. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 14, 2015 September 15, 2015** **1 EUR** 75.3242 75.0487 **1 GBP** 102.5429 102.4080 **100 YEN** 55.18 55.55 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
