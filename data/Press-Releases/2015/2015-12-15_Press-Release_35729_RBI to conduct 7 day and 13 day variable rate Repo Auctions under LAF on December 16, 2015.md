## RBI to conduct 7 day and 13 day variable rate Repo Auctions under LAF on

## December 16, 2015

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate repo auctions tomorrow (December 16, 2015, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (Rs. Billion)|Tenor|Window Timings|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|7|9.30 am – 10.00 am|December 23, 2015 (Wednesday)|
|2|200|13|11.00 am – 11.30 am|December 29, 2015 (Tuesday)|

Successful bids will get the allotment at their respective rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
