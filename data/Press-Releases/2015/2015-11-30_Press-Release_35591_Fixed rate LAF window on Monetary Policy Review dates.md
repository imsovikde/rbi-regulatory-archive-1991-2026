## November 30, 2015

## Fixed rate LAF window on Monetary Policy Review dates

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the timings of the fixed rate LAF and MSF windows with effect from November 30, 2015 in order to give eligible market participants more flexibility to operate.

Further, it has now been decided to keep the LAF Repo window open from

9.00 a.m. to 10.30 a.m. on the days of the Monetary Policy Review starting from December 1, 2015. On other days, the window will continue to be operated upon between 9.00 a.m. and 3.00 p.m.
