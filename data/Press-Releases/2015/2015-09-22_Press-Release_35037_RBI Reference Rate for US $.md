September 22, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.6250 on September 22, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 21, 2015) was

65.7531. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 21, 2015 September 22, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.3799 73.2638 **1 GBP** 102.1672 101.7319 **100 YEN** 54.83 54.50 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
