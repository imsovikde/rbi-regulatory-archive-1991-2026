## April 29, 2015

## RBI to conduct 6 day variable rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

## For Rs. 10,000 Crore between 11.00 am and 11.30 am

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of Rs. 10,000 crore today (April 29, 2015, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **11.00 am and 11.30 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 6 day variable rate repo will take place on May 5, 2015 (Tuesday).
