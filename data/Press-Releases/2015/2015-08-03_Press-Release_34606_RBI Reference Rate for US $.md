August 03, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.9623 on August 03, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 31, 2015) was 64.0054. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 31, 2015 August 03, 2015** **1 EUR** 70.1627 70.1602 **1 GBP** 99.8356 99.9347 **100 YEN** 51.55 51.56 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
