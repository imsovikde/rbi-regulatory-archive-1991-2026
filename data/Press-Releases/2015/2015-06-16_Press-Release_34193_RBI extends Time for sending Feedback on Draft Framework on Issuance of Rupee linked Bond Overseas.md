## June 16, 2015

## RBI extends Time for sending Feedback on

## Draft Framework on Issuance of Rupee linked Bond Overseas

The Reserve Bank of India has extended up to June 22, 2015, the timeline for receipt of comments/feedback on the draft framework on issuance of Rupee linked Bond Overseas. The Reserve Bank had published <u>the draft framework on its website</u> <u>on June 09, 2015</u>.
