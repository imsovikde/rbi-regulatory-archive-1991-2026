October 07, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.2571 on October 07, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 06, 2015) was

65.3908. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 06, 2015 October 07, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.1919 73.4664 **1 GBP** 99.0540 99.5301 **100 YEN** 54.41 54.34 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
