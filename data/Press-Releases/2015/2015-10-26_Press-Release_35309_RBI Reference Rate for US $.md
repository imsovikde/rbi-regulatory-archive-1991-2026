October 26, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9603 on October 26, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 23, 2015) was

64.8815. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 23, 2015 October 26, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.0639 71.6447 **1 GBP** 99.8851 99.5452 **100 YEN** 53.79 53.63 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
