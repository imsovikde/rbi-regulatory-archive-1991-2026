## August 25, 2015

## RBI to conduct 20 day variable rate reverse repo auction

## under LAF today for 100 billion between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 20 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of 100 billion today (August 25, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 20 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on September 14, 2015 (Monday).
