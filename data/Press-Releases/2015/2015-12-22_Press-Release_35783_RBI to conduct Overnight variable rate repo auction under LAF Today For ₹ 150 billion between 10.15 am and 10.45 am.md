## RBI to conduct Overnight variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **150 billion between 10.15 am and 10.45 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct Overnight variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `150 billion today (December 22, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **10.15 am and 10.45 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight variable rate repo will take place on December 23, 2015 (Wednesday).
