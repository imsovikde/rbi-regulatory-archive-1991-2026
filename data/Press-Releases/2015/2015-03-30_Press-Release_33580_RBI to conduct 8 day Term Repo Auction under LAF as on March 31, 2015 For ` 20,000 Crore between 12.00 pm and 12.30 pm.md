## March 30, 2015

## RBI to conduct 8 day Term Repo Auction under LAF as on March 31, 2015

## For 20,000 Crore between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 8 day Term Repo auction for a notified amount of 20,000 crore on March 31, 2015 (Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 8 day Term Repo will take place on April 8, 2015 (Wednesday).
