## November 10, 2015

## RBI to conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction under LAF

## today for 300 billion between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 300 billion today (November 10, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 6 day variable rate repo will take place on November 16, 2015 (Monday).
