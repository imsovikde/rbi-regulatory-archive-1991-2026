## December 15, 2015

## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

## For 150 billion between 12.30 p.m. and 1.00 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 150 billion today (December 15, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 12.30 p.m. and 1.00 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate repo will take place on December 18, 2015 (Friday).
