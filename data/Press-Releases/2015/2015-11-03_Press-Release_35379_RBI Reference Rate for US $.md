November 03, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.4510 on November 03, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 02, 2015) was

65.4793. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 02, 2015 November 03, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.2171 72.1008 **1 GBP** 101.0608 100.9647 **100 YEN** 54.38 54.24 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
