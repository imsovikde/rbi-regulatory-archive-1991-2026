## April 21, 2015

## MSS Ceiling for Fiscal 2015-16 fixed at Rs. 50,000 Crore

In accordance with the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), the ceiling for the outstanding balance under the MSS for the fiscal year 2015-16 has been fixed at ` 50,000 crore. This ceiling will be reviewed when the outstanding balance reaches the threshold limit of ` 35,000 crore. The current MSS outstanding balance is nil.
