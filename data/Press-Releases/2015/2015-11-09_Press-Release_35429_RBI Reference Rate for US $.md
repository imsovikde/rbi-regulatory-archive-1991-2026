November 09, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3378 on November 09, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 06, 2015) was

65.7910. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 06, 2015 November 09, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.5477 71.4458 **1 GBP** 99.9497 100.0308 **100 YEN** 54.00 53.79 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
