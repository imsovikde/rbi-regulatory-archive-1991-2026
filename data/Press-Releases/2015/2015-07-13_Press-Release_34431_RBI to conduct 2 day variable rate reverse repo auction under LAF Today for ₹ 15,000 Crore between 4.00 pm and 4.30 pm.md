## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

**for** ` **15,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of `15,000 crore today (July 13, 2015, Monday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on July 15, 2015 (Wednesday).
