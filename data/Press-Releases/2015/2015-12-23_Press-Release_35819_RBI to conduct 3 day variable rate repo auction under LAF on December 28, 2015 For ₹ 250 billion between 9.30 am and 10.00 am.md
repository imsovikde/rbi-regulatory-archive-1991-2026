## December 23, 2015

## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on December 28,

**2015 For** ` **250 billion between 9.30 am and 10.00 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 250 billion on December 28, 2015, Monday. The auction will be conducted between **9.30 am and 10.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate repo will take place on December 31, 2015 (Thursday).
