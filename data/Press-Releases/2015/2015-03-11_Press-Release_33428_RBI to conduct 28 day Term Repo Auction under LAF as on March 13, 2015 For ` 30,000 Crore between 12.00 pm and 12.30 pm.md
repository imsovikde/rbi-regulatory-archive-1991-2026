## March 11, 2015

## RBI to conduct 28 day Term Repo Auction under LAF as on March 13, 2015

## For 30,000 Crore between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 28 day Term Repo auction for a notified amount of 30,000 crore on March 13, 2015, (Friday). The auction will be conducted between 12.00 pm and 12.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 28 day Term Repo will take place on April 10, 2015 (Friday).
