October 01, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.5540 on October 01, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 30, 2015) was

65.7418. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 30, 2015 October 01, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.7952 73.0796 **1 GBP** 99.5331 99.1701 **100 YEN** 54.80 54.54 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
