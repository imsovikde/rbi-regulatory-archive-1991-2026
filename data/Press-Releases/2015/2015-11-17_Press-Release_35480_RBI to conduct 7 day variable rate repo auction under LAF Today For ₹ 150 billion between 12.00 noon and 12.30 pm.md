## November 17, 2015

## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **150 billion between 12.00 noon and 12.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 150 billion today (November 17, 2015, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on November 24, 2015 (Tuesday).
