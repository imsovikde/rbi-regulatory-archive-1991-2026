June 30, 2015 **RBI to conduct Overnight and 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under** **LAF Today** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (June 30, 2015, Tuesday) between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Date of Reversal**

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor||
|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(Crore)|(Days)||
|1.|40,000|1|July 1, 2015 (Wednesday)|
|2.|30,000|3|July 3, 2015 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
