## July 29, 2015

## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities for 14,000 crore on July 30, 2015

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of the Government Stock through auctions to be held on July 31, 2015.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer (PD), are as under:

## ( in crore)

**MUC amount per**

|Nomenclature of the|Notified||commitment per PD under Minimum bidding||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Security|Amount|Primary Dealer|ACU auction||
|7.68% GS 2023|2,000|48||48|
|7.72% GS 2025|6,000|143||143|
|8.24% GS 2033|3,000|72||72|
|8.13% GS 2045|3,000|72||72|

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method on July 30, 2015. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on the date of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
