## June 03, 2015

## Reserve Bank releases the results of Survey of Professional

## Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 34

The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the results of the <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators -(Round 34)</u> conducted during May 2015.

The results of the Survey represent views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
