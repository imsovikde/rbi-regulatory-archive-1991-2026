December 21, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3240 on December 21, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 18, 2015) was

66.4235. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 18, 2015 December 21, 2015** **1 EUR** 72.1359 72.1340 **1 GBP** 99.1703 98.9886 **100 YEN** 54.48 54.67 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
