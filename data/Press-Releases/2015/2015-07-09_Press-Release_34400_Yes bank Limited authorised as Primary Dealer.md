## July 9, 2015

## Yes bank Limited authorised as Primary Dealer

The Reserve Bank has authorised Yes bank Limited to undertake Primary Dealership business departmentally with effect from July 13, 2015. Yes bank Limited had earlier requested Reserve Bank of India to grant it Primary Dealership authorisation.
