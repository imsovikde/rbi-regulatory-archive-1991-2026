July 21, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.6506 on July 21, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 20, 2015) was 63.5510. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 20, 2015 July 21, 2015** **1 EUR** 68.8321 68.9081 **1 GBP** 99.1014 99.1358 **100 YEN** 51.20 51.17 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
