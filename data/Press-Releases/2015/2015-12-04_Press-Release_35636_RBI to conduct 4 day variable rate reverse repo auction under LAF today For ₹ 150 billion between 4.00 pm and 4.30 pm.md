## December 04, 2015

## RBI to conduct 4 day variable rate reverse repo auction under

## LAF today For 150 billion between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 4 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of 150 billion today (December 04, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m**.** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 4 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on December 8, 2015 (Tuesday).
