## March 20, 2015

## RBI to conduct 17 day Term Repo Auction under LAF as on March 20, 2015

## For 15,000 Crore between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 17 day Term Repo auction for a notified amount of 15,000 crore on March 20, 2015 (Friday). The auction will be conducted between 3.45 pm and 4.15 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 17 day Term Repo will take place on April 6, 2015 (Monday).
