October 09, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.7848 on October 09, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 08, 2015) was

65.1570. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 08, 2015 October 09, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.3277 73.0837 **1 GBP** 99.7814 99.5418 **100 YEN** 54.41 53.96 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
