October 16, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9697 on October 16, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 15, 2015) was

64.8240. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 15, 2015 October 16, 2015** **1 EUR** 74.5022 73.9940 **1 GBP** 100.4124 100.5536 **100 YEN** 54.61 54.54 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
