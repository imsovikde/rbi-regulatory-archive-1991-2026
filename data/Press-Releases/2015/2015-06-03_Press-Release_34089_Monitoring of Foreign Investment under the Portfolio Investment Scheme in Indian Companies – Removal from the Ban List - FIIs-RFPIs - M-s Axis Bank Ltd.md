## June 03, 2015

**Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the** **Portfolio Investment Scheme in Indian Companies –** **Removal from the Ban List -FIIs/RFPIs-M/s Axis Bank Ltd.**

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in M/s Axis Bank Ltd. has gone below the threshold limit. Hence the restrictions placed on the purchase of shares of the above company are withdrawn with immediate effect.
