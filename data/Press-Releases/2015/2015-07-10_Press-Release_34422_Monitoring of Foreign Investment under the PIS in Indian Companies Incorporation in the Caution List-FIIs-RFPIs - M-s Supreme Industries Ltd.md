## July 10, 2015

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under the PIS in Indian Companies

## Incorporation in the Caution List-FIIs/RFPIs-M/s Supreme Industries Ltd

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that the foreign shareholding by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in <u>M/s Supreme Industries Limited</u> has reached the trigger limit. Hence, further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
