## May 22, 2015

## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF today

## For 10,000 Crore between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 10,000 crore today (May 22, 2015, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on May 29, 2015 (Friday).
