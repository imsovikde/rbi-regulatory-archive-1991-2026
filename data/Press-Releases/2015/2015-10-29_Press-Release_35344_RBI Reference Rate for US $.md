October 29, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.1515 on October 29, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 28, 2015) was

65.0408. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 28, 2015 October 29, 2015** **1 EUR** 71.7530 71.2497 **1 GBP** 99.5840 99.3626 **100 YEN** 54.01 53.93 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
