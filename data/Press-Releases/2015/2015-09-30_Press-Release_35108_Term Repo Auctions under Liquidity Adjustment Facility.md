## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August</u> <u>22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 8, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons there for.
