December 14, 2015 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9940 on December 14, 2015. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 11, 2015) was

66.7860. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 11, 2015 December 14, 2015** **1 EUR** 73.1040 73.4656 **1 GBP** 101.1006 101.7840 **100 YEN** 54.75 55.29 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
