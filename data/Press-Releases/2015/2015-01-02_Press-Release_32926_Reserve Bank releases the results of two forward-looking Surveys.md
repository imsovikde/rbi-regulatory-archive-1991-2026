## Reserve Bank releases the results of two forward-looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the results of the following two Surveys:

i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey- Q3: 2014-15 (Round 68)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey</u> <u>(OBICUS) - July- September 2014 (27th Round)</u> The results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the
Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
