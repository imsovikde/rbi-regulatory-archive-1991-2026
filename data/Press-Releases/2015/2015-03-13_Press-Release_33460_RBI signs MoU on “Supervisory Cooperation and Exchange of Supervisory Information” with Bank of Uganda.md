## March 13, 2015

## RBI signs MoU on “Supervisory Cooperation and Exchange of Supervisory Information” with Bank of Uganda

The Reserve Bank of India, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on “Supervisory Cooperation and Exchange of Supervisory Information” with Bank of Uganda (BoU). The MoU was inked by Prof Emmanuel Tumusiime-Mutebile, Governor, Bank of Uganda and Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India.

The Reserve Bank has been signing the Memorandum of Understanding/Exchange of Letters (EoL)/Statement of Co-operation (SoC) with supervisors of other countries to promote greater co-operation and sharing of supervisory information among the authorities. With this RBI has signed 24 such MoUs, one Letter for Supervisory Co-operation and one Statement of Co-operation, with overseas regulators/supervisors.
