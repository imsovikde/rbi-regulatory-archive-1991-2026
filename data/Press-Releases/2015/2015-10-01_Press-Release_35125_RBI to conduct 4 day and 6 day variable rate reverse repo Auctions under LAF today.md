## October 01, 2015

## RBI to conduct 4 day and 6 day variable rate reverse repo

## Auctions under LAF today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (October 01, 2015, Thursday) between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
