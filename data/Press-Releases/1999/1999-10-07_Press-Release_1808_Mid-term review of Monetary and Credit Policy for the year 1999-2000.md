## Mid-term review of Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 1999-2000

## October 7, 1999

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will hold a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks to announce the mid-term review of monetary and credit policy for the year 199-2000 on Friday, October 29, 1999. The meeting will be held at 11.30 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.

## Rupambara Padhi

## Manager
