# Reserve Bank Rajbhasha Shield 1997-98 Prize Distribution

## August 6, 1999

In order to make the common man aware of the economic development of the nation, there is a need to understand the nature of the Official Language and it would be possible only when the work is originally performed in Hindi instead of depending on translation". Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor of Reserve Bank of India has stated this while addressing the representatives of public sector banks and financial institutions present in the prize distribution function of the Reserve Bank Rajbhasha Shield and House Journal competition held today. He further stated that if the nation is to realise the real prosperity, we have to take recourse of Hindi and other Indian languages. He also appealed that Hindi should be used in right spirit in various fields in this golden jubilee year of official language Hindi.

The function was being held under the Chairmanship of Deputy Governor, Shri Jagdish Capoor for awarding the Rajbhasha Shield to the banks/financial institutions who have worked extensively in the field of Hindi and have also excelled in producing house journals in Hindi. Shri S.P. Talwar, Deputy Governor, the senior executives and the senior officers of RBI were present in the function. the meeting was also attended by the senior executives of the public sector banks and financial institutions. Prizes were also awarded to the winners of inter-bank Hindi essay competition that was conducted for the first time in 1998-99 by the Reserve Bank. The essay competition was conducted on the subject "Nature of Indian Banking in the next decade".

Shri Jagdish Capoor in his speech said that in contrast to the early days there is a wide awareness in the banking sector towards the official language Hindi. He further stated that today's banking relates to all the sections of the society. Hence there is a need for doing the banking functions in the public language so as to get full co-operation from every section of the society. This would ultimately enhance the banking business.

Welcoming the Governor, Deputy Governor and representatives of banks/financial institutions, Shri B.S. Sharma, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India said that the year being the golden jubilee year of official language Hindi, this function has lot of significance. He also said that Hindi as an official language is now growing and under these circumstances, organising such competitions would help in developing healthy competition among the banks and financial institutions.

Shri M.L. Jain, Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Reserve Bank of India proposed a vote of thanks to the Governor, Deputy Governor and the representatives of public sector banks and financial institutions. Dr. Shrinivas Dwivei, General Manger (Officer-in-Charge) of Rajbhasha Department of Reserve Bank of India had conducted the programme.

A list of winner banks and financial institutions and of the winners of the essay competition is enclosed.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
