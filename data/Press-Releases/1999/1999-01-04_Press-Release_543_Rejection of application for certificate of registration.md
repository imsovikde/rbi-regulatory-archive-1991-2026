# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## January 4, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on January 2, 1999, the application for certificate of registration submitted by the financial company Jyothirmayi Finance Ltd., 1-2-365/26, R. K. Mutt Road, Damalguda, Hyderabad-500 029. As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
