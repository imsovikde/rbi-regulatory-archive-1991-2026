# Seven-year Government of India Stock Auction Announced

## April 8, 1999

The Government of India has announced an auction of Seven-year Stock for Rs.3,000 crore. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai of Friday, April 9, 1999. Bids, in the prescribed form obtainable from the Chief General Manager, RBI, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted on Friday, April 9, 1999, during banking hours. On the basis of the bids received, the RBI will determine the maximum rate of yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on April 9, 1999, and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 10, 1999. The new stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.
