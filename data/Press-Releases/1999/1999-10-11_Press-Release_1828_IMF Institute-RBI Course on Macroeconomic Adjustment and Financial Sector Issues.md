## IMF Institute/RBI Course on Macroeconomic Adjustment and Financial Sector

## Issues

## October 11, 1999

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) Institute and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) have jointly sponsored a course on Macroeconomic Adjustment and Financial Sector Issues in India. The course is being held between October 11-22, 1999, at the Reserve Bank of India's College of Agricultural Banking, Pune. The course is the first of its kind in India and represents a unique experiment in collaboration between the IMF and the RBI.

The Course is directed at senior-and middle-level official of the Government of India, State Governments and the Reserve Bank of India who are involved with the design and implementation of macroeconomic policies and financial sector reforms. The objective of the course is to familiarise participants with macroeconomic management, interlinkages between the different sectors of the economy, the rationale and modalities of financial sector reform, the development and monitoring of financial markets and the role of the regulatory and supervisory function for policy makers in the rapidly changing, liberalised environment. The course is being conducted through lectures, group discussions and case studies in which participants will develop a macroeconomic policy programme for India.

The course was inaugurated this morning at the College of Agricultural Banking by Dr.

A. Vasudevan, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India, Speaking on the occasion, Dr.
A. Vasudevan set out the rationale for organising the course, the emphasis placed on both, the design and the quality of participation and the country-specific flavour imparted to the course which distinguishes it from other forms of technical support provided elsewhere by the IMF Institute. Earlier, Shri S.K. Newlay, Principal, College of Agricultural Banking extended a warm and hearty welcome to the members of the faculty of the IMF Institute and the participants and assured them of all possible assistance to make the course a success. Mr. Reza Vaez-Zadeh, Chief of the Asian Division, IMF Institute and head of the IMF Institute team, gave details about the course content and noted that the interactions would greatly help them in gaining insights about the policy implementation in developing countries.
