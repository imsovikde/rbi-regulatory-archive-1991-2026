# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## July 19, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on July 15, 1999 the application for Certificate of Registration submitted by M/s. Jagpriya Finance & Investment Ltd., a Non-Banking Financial Company having its Registered Office at B-207, II Floor, Manpasand Complex, Bapunagar, Ahmedabad.

As such, the company cannot transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934.
