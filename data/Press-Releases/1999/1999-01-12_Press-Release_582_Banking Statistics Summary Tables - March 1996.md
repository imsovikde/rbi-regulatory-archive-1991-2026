# Banking Statistics Summary Tables-March 1996

## January 12, 1999

The Department of Statistical Analysis and Computer Services of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out a publication, viz. Banking Statistics (Vol 25)- March 1996. The publication gives comprehensive data on number of offices, employees, deposits and credit of scheduled commercial banks as at the end of March 1996.

The publication is available from the Sales Section (Division of Reports, Reviews and Publications), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P.M. Road, Mumbai-400 001 at a price of Rs.220. The publication will be also available at the Reserve Bank's website (URL: [http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)).
