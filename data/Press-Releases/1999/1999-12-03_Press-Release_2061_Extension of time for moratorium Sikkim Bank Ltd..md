# Extension of time for moratorium Sikkim Bank Ltd.

## December 3, 1999

The Central Government have, on the recommendation of the Reserve Bank of India and in partial modification of the their earlier Notifcation dated September 4, 1999, issued a Notification on December 2, 1999 extending the period of moratorium in respect of Sikkim Bank Ltd., for a futher period upto and inclusive of 3 March 2000.

## Rupambara Padhi

## Manager
