# Special Training Programme for NBFC Personnel

## April 16, 1999

The College of Agricultural Banking of the Reserve Bank of India, Pune, will conduct on a regular basis a special training programme for the benefit of the personnel working in non-banking financial companies (NBFCs). The objective of the training programme is to familiarize the dealing officials of the NBFCs with the regulatory and statutory framework prescribed by the Reserve Bank and to provide the necessary skills to ensure compliance with the new regulatory framework of the Reserve Bank.

The contents of the programme would include a session on Chapter III-B of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. It would also familiarize the participants with the Reserve Bank directions on acceptance of deposits for equipment leasing companies, hire-purchase finance companies, loan companies, investment companies and residuary non-banking companies. Other sessions would include subjects, such as, RBI guidelines on income recognition, asset classification, provisioning norms for bad and doubtful debts, capital adequacy and concentration of credit and investments, asset/ liability and risk management, securitization of assets, returns to be submitted to the Reserve Bank for its off-site surveillance, funds management and corporate governance.

The programme will be offered to personnel of NBFCs who are directly involved in attending to the work connected with compliance of statutory framework of the Reserve Bank, including submission of various returns/statements.

NBFCs interest in participating in the programme may send their nominations by FAX on 020-5537089.

It may be recalled that the College of Agricultural Banking, Pune, conducted two special training programmes for NBFC personnel in January/March this year. Apropos the excellent feed-back received from the participants in these programmes it has now been decided to offer the programme on a regular basis.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
