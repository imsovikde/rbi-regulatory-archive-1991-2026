# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## January 29, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.42.50 on 29.01.1999 as against Rs.42.50 on 28.01.1999. The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-rupee rate will be based on this rate. Based on the RBI Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12.00 noon, the exchange rates of US dollar, Pound Sterling, EURO and Yen against Rs.100 are : <u>Date Currency per Rs. 100 (Middle Rates)</u> **USD GBP EURO Yen**

January 22, 1999 2.3524 1.4236 2.0270 267.710 January 29, 1999 2.3529 1.4284 2.0606 273.059
