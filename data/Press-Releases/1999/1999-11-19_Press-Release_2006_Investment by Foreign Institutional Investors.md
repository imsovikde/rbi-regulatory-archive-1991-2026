# Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## November 19, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Thiru Arooran Sugars Ltd., up to 30 per cent of their paid up capital through primary and stock exchanges, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting to this effect.
