**Government of India announces the sale of 11.99 per cent Government Stock, 2009 through price based auction**

## August 24, 1999

The Government of India has announced auction of 11.99 per cent Government Stock, 2009 for an aggregate amount for Rs.2,500 crore.

The auction will be price based and will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on Friday, August 27, 1999. Bids, in the prescribed form obtainable from Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted on Friday, August 27, 1999 during banking hours. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank of India will determine the minimum price at which offers will be accepted. The results for the auction will be announced on August 27, 1999 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on August 28, 1999. The stock will qualify for ready forward facility.
