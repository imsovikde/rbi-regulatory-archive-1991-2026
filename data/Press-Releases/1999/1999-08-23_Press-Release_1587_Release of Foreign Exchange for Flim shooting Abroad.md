# Release of Foreign Exchange for Flim shooting Abroad

## August 21, 1999

The procedure for release of foreign exchange was modified in May 1998, and persons seeking release of foreign exchange towards shooting of films abroad, including production of news related films are not required to approach the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. It is reiterated that such applicants are required to approach the concerned Regional Office of the Reserve Bank of India with an estimate together with proforma invoices, etc., received from the overseas parties.
