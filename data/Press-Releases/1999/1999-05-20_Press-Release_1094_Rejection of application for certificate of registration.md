# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## May 20, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on May 18, 1999, the application for Certificate of Registration submitted by M/s. Contact Securities Ltd., E-7/L-369, Arera Colony, Bhopal-462 016 for carrying on the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution. As such, the company cannot transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/1433**
