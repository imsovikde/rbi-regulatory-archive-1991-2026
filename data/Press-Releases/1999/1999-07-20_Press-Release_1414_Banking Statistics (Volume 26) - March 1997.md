# Banking Statistics (Volume 26) - March 1997

## July 20, 1999

The Department of Statistical Analysis & Computer Services of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out a publication, viz., Banking Statistics (Volume 26) - March 1997. The publication is based on the basic statistical returns collected from scheduled commercial banks. The publication presents comprehensive data on number of offices, employees, deposits and outstanding credit of scheduled commercial banks according to various characteristics at the end of March 1997.

The publication is available for sale from the Sale Section (DRRP), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P.M. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.220/-. The publication is also available on the Reserve Bank's website (URL : <u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in))</u>.
