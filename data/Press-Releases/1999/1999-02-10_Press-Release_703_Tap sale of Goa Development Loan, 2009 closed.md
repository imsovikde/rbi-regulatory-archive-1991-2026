# Tap sale of Goa Development Loan, 2009 closed

## February 10, 1999

The sale of 12.50 per cent Goa State Development Loan, 2009 which opened for subscription on February 10, 1999 on tap at Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai was closed at 12.00 Hrs. today.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
