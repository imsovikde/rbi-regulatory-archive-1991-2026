# Price-based auction for Government Securities on May 11

## May 7, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the first-ever price-based auction of two Government of India securities on Tuesday, May 11, 1999. The securities to be auctioned for an aggregate amount of Rs.5,000 crore are:

## (Rs. in crore)

11.19% Government Stock 2005 3,000
12.32% Government Stock 2011 <u>2,000</u>
<u>5,000</u>

In price-based auction, the coupon of the security is predetermined (generally existing securities are offered for subscription on a reissue basis) and the bidders have to quote the price per Rs.100.00 per cent face value of the stock, (based on their yield expectations) at which they desire to purchase the stock. The cut-off price or the minimum offer price is decided by the Reserve Bank of India. Bids below this cut-off or minimum offer price are rejected.

The auction will be conducted at Mumbai. Prescribed form for bids can be obtained from the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai. Bids should be submitted on Tuesday, May 11, 1999 during banking hours.

On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank of India will determine the minimum price for acceptance of offers. The results of the auction will be announced on May 11, 1999 itself and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on May 12,

1999. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
