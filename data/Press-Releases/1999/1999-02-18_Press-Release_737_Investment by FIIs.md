# Investment by FIIs

## February 18, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on February 18, 1999 that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares of M/s. Essel Packaging Ltd. by foreign institutional investors (FIIs) in the primary/secondary markets has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. No further purchases of equity shares of M/s.Essel Packaging Ltd. will, therefore, be allowed on behalf of the FIIs without obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
