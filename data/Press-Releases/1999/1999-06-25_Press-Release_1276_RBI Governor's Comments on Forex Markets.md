# RBI Governor's Comments on Forex Markets

## June 25, 1999

Following is the text of comments made by Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India about the effect of developments in Kargil on the foreign exchange markets. These remarks were made by Dr. Jalan in response to questions by press reporters after the inaugural session of the meeting of the Central Level Co-ordinating Committee on Rural Development in Mumbai on 25 June 1999.

"RBI is keeping a close watch. In view of developments in Kargil, there is obviously some uncertainty in the market. In March, April and May of the current year, RBI was buying dollars from the market. In fact, we bought as much as US $ 2.5 billion during these three months. Last week, however, RBI sold dollars to SBI in order to meet some extra demand. Our policy is to keep forex markets orderly and meet, as necessary, temporary demand-supply gaps that emerge from time to time. We have deliberately followed a policy of keeping our reserves at a very high level in order to take care of such situations.

Except for some temporary hiccups, financial markets are functioning normally and overall conditions are satisfactory. RBI will continue to monitor the position and, as in the past, take appropriate measures when necessary."

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
