# Exchange Control Manual on RBI Website

## April 22, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has released the Exchange Control Manual on its Website (URL: <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). The Exchange Control Manual is updated till December 1998. The ADMA circulars issued since January 1999 are also available on the website. For the first time the Reserve Bank has also made available on the website all the forms required for exchange control purposes. The Exchange Control Manual can be directly accessed through URL: <u>www.ecm.rbi.org.in</u>.

The ADMA/GP circulars as well as forms issued since January 1999 are also separately available on the RBI Website under `Notifications' and `Forms', respectively.
