# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## October 18, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on October 18, 1999 the application for certificate of registration submitted by the financial company Mark Leasing and Finance Ltd., 93, Mahavar Towers, 9th Floor, Nr.Mahalaxmi Char Rasta, Paldi, Ahmedabad-380 007. As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1999-2000/537**
