# Foreign Direct Investment under the 'automatic route of RBI' - Further liberalisation

## February 1, 1999

As a measure of further liberalisation, the general permission granted under the 'automatic route of RBI' for issue of shares in terms of Notification No.FERA. 180/98-RB dated January 13, 1998 has been extended to cover issue of shares upto 100% in respect of the activities, viz., "construction and maintenance of roads, highways, vehicular bridges, toll roads, vehicular tunnels, port and harbours", as added in Part 'D' of Annexure III list in terms of Ministry of Industry Press Note No. 1 dated January 4, 1999.

Notification No.FERA 191/99-RB dated January 30, 1999 issued in this regard will be available on the internet at <u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>.
