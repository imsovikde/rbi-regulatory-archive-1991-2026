# Tap sale of AP & UP Development Loan, 2009 closed

## February 12, 1999

The sale of 12.50 per cent Andhra Pradesh and Uttar Pradesh State Development Loan, 2009 which opened for subscription on February 10, 1999 on tap at Reserve Bank of India offices was closed at 12.30 Hrs. today.

## Chhaya Raje Manager
