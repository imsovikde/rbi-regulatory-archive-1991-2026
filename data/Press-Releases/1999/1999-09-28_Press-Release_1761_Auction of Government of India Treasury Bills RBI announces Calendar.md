# Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills RBI announces Calendar

## Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills

## RBI announces Calendar

## September 28, 1999

<u>Calendar</u>

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the following calendar of Treasury Bills issuance. <u>This calendar is valid till March 2000.</u>

|Type of|Periodicity|Notified Amount|Day of Auction|Day of|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|T-Bills||(Rs. Crore)||Payment|
|14-day|Weekly|100|Every Friday|Following Saturday|
|91-day|Weekly|100|Every Friday|Following Saturday|
|182-day|Fortnightly|100|Wednesdays preceding the non-reporting Fridays|Following Thursday|
|364-day|Fortnightly|500|Wednesdays preceding the|Following Thursday|

reporting Fridays

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if circumstances warrant without assigning any reasons. The calendar is, thus, subject to changes, if circumstances warrant including for reasons, such as, intervening holidays and such changes will be communicated through regular press releases.
