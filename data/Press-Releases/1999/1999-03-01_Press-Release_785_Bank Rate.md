# Bank Rate

## March 1, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to reduce the Bank Rate by one percentage point, i.e. from 9 per cent per annum to 8 per cent per annum with effect from the close of business on March 1, 1999.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
