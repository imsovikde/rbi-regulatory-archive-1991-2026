# Exchange of Soiled Notes : RBI adds One More Centre

## June 8, 1999

In an effort to reach out the public, the Reserve Bank of India will now also extend the facility of making available coins of Re.1, Rs. 2, and Rs. 5 and exchanging soiled notes in Chembur. Members of the public in Chembur area may avail this facility at Sindhi st Society Gymkhana, Nichaldas Market, 1 floor, Little Malabar Hill, near Bhakti Bhavan, Mumbai-400 071 on every Thursday effective from June 10, 1999 between 10.30 a.m. to

1.30 p.m. (excluding public holidays). They may also utilise this opportunity for exchange of soiled notes of Re. 1, Rs. 2, and Rs.5 into coins. It may be recalled that in order to mitigate the hardship faced by the members of the public in obtaining coins, the Reserve Bank of India has made special arrangements to make available coins upto Rs. 800 packed in 'Potlas' of 100 pieces each of Re. 1, Rs. 2, and Rs. 5 in exchange at various centres in Mumbai.
## Place/address Day Time

1. Central Bank of India, On every Monday 10.30 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. Plot No.4, St. Mary's Road, Mazagaon, Mumbai-400 010.
2. Lions Club at 2, Mufaddar, On every 10.30 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. Church Road, Wednesday, Saifee Park, marol, Andheri (East), Mumbai- 400 059.
3. Sindhi Society Gymkhana, On every Thursday 10.30 a.m. to 1.30 p.m. Nichaldas Market, st 1 Floor, Little Malabar Hill, Near Bhakti Bhavan, Mumbai-400 071.
4. Central Bank of India, On every Friday 10.00 a.m. to 1.00 p.m. Khodadad Circle Branch, Jame Jamshed Charity Bldg., Dadar T.T., Mumbai-400 014
