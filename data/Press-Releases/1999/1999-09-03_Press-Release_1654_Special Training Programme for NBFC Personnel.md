# Special Training Programme for NBFC Personnel

## September 3, 1999

The college of Agricultural Banking (CAB), Pune has drawn up a calendar of training programmes for personnel of Non-Banking Finance Companies (NBFCs). NBFC officials who are directly involved with the work connected with compliance of statutory framework and submission of various returns/statements to the Reserve Bank of India can participate in the programmes.

As per the calendar, CAB will conduct nine programmes between October 1999 and June 2000 at the college premises in Pune. Each programme will be of three-and-a-half day duration. The programmes will aim to familiarise NBFC personnel with the statutory and regulatory framework prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India and to provide necessary skills for compliance as also to impart knowledge in operational areas for achieving sound and efficient functioning.

The contents of the programme would include lectures and discussions on :

(i) Regulatory framework for NBFCs-Chapter III(B) of RBI Act, 1934; (ii) RBI directions-acceptance of deposits-equipment leasing companies-hire-purchase finance companies, loan companies investment companies and residuary non-banking companies;
(iii) RBI guidelines on income recognition, asset classification, provisioning norms for bad and doubtful debts, capital adequacy and concentration of credit investments; (iv) Asset, liability and risk management/funds management/portfolio management;

(v) Securitization of assets and (vi) Off-site surveillance-reports to be submitted to RBI-Discussion and clarifications. CAB was established by RBI to train senior and middle level banks officials concerned with rural credit. The participation fee for this residential programme is Rs 5,500 per participant.
