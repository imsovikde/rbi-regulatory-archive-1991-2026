# Application for grant of Certificate of Registration rejected

## January 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected the applications for grant of certificates of registration submitted by the undernoted non-banking financial companies: **Sr.No. Name of the Company Address of the Registered Office Rejected on** <u>of the Company</u>

1. M/s. S. M. Finance Ltd. No.6, Upper Ground Floor, Nalanda 09.01.1999
Apartment, Somajiguda, Hyderabad-500 082

2. M/s. Madhusudan Leasing & 6/A-2, Court Chambers, 35, New Marine Lines, 11.01.1999 Finance Ltd. Mumbai 400 020. As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institutions as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
