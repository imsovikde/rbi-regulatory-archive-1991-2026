# Four more Primary Dealers in Government Securities Market Operationalised

## February 5, 1998

The Reserve Bank of India has granted its final approval to four more entities to operate as primary dealers in the Government securities market. These are :

|1|J. P. Morgan Securities India Private Ltd.|
|---|---|
|2.|ABN AMRO Securities (India) Pvt. Ltd.|
|3|Tata Finance Securities Ltd.|
|4.|Ceat Financial Services Ltd.|

These entities were granted 'in-principle' approval on October 30,1998. With the grant of approval to four more primary dealers, the number of primary dealers in the Government securities market has gone up to ten.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
