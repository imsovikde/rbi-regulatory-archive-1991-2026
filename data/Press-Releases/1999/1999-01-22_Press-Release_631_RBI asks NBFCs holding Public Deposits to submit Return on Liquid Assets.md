# RBI asks NBFCs holding Public Deposits to submit Return on Liquid Assets

The provisions of the Reserve Bank of India Act require the non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) including those having net own fund (NoF) below Rs. 25 lakh holding public deposits to maintain liquid assets as a certain percentage of the public deposits and furnish to the Reserve Bank of India a statutory return on quarterly basis in the prescribed format.

The Reserve Bank has reviewed the position of statutory compliance by such companies. It has found that many companies, more particularly the companies having NoF of less than Rs.25 lakh, have not submitted this return to the Reserve bank despite holding public deposits although the return is statutory. The Reserve bank has taken a serious view of the non-compliance of the law by these NBFCs.

The Reserve Bank has, however, decided to give a last opportunity to these companies to comply with the legal provisions. In order to enable them to do so, the Reserve Bank will be separately mailing to each NBFC, a format of the return for immediate submission. The Reserve bank has cautioned that appropriate penal action, including rejection of their application for registration and cancellation of the certificate if already issued, will be taken against the non-compliant companies. The NBFCs have been advised to invariably file such returns at the prescribed periodicity. Further, although the Reserve Bank had earlier clarified that the companies not holding public deposits need not file this return, in view of the large scale non-compliance of law, it has considered necessary to ask these companies also to file a 'Nil' return in order to comply with the provisions of law.

The Reserve Bank has also clarified that the applications of NBFCs having NoF below Rs.25 lakh for issue of a certificate of registration have been kept in abeyance because they have been given three years' time for attainment of the requisite level of NoF. During the intervening period, however, they have been precluded from accepting further public deposits, as also, from renewing such maturing deposits. They should repay such deposits as and when they mature. In the meantime, it is obligatory on their part to file the Statutory Return on Liquid Assets on quarterly basis to the Reserve Bank.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
