# 364 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## February 6, 1999

The Reserve Bankof India has announced the auction of 364 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.750 crore. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, February 10, 1999 before 1430 hours. Results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, February 12, 1999.

## Maureen Shankar
