# RBI-Prices of Treasury Bills

## August 28, 1999

The price list effective from August 30, 1999 is as under :

|Sr. No.|Type of Treasury Bill|Date of Maturity|Yield rate (%) for sale|
|---|---|---|---|
|1.|364 Day|25 Feb. 2000|9.87|
|2.|364 Day|24 Mar. 2000|9.94|

2. Deals will be for a minimum amount of Rs.1 lakh and multiples of Rs.1 lakh thereafter. The dated securities will continue to be on the sale list.
