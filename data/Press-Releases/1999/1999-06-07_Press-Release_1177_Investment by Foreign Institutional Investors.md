# Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## June 7, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India on June 7, 1999 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Vikas WSP Ltd., upto 30 per cent of their paid up capital through primary and stock exchanges, as the company has passed a resolution at its General Body Meeting to this effect. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/1517**
