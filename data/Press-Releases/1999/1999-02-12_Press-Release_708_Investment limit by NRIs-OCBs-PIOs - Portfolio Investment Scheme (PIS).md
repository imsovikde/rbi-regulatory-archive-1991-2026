# Investment limit by NRIs/OCBs/PIOs-Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS)

# February 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has withdrawn the restriction placed on the purchase of shares of M/s. Bharati Telecom Ltd. The Reserve Bank of India has advised that the percentage of Non- Resident Indians (NRIs)/Overseas Corporate Bodies (OCBs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) investments to the paid up capital of M/s. Bharati Telecom Ltd. has gone down below 8 per cent as on February 11, 1999. It would, therefore, be in order for NRIs/OCBs/PIOs to invest in the shares of this company under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) subject to the existing ceiling and guidelines.

The Reserve Bank has also advised that the aggregate net purchase of equity shares of the following companies by NRIs/OCBs/PIOs in the secondary markets have reached the limit of 8 per cent of their paid up capital :

i) Bank of Punjab ii) NALCO Chemicals India Ltd iii) National Steel Industries Ltd iv) South Indian Shipping Corporation Ltd
No further purchases of equity shares of the companies on behalf of NRIs/OCBs/PIOs would be allowed without obtained prior clearance of the Reserve Bank.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
