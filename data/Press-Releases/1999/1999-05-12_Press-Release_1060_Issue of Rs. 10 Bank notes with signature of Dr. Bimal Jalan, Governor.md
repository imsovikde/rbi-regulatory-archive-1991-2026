# Issue of Rs. 10 Bank notes with signature of Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor

## May 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly be issuing Rs.10 denomination notes in Mahatma Gandhi Series bearing the signature of Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor with capital letter 'P' inset in both numbering panels. Except for this change, the design of Rs. 10 notes being issued now are similar to those issued earlier in the existing design. All Rs.10 notes issued by the Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender.

## Rupambara Padhi

## Manager
