## Chinese Delegation visits RBI

## December 24, 1999

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India today welcomed the high level delegation from the People's Republic of China which visited the Reserve Bank. The delegation was led by the Chinese First Vice Minister for Finance, Mr. Lou Jiwei. After preliminary discussions with the Governor, the delegation had a detailed discussion with the other Top Executives of the RBI, Securities and Exchange Board of India, banks and financial institutions. Shri G.G. Vaidya, Chairman, State Bank of India, Shri G.P. Gupta, Chairman, Industrial Development Bank of India, Shri P.S.Subramaniam, Chairman, Unit Trust of India, Shri A.T.Pannirselvam, Chairman, Indian Banks' Association and Shri O.P.Gehrotra, Executive Director, Securities and Exchange Board of India were present in the discussions led by Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Deputy Governor of RBI.

The Chinese First Vice Minister, in his introductory remarks, drew similarities between the experiences of China and India especially in the area of economic reform. He hoped that the two countries would be able to share their expertise and experiences in financial policies. He also gave an overview of the current economic situation in China and in particular the interface between the fiscal and the monetary policies.

The Deputy Governor gave a brief overview of the macro-economic conditions in India and the progress made in the financial sector reforms so far. He stated that India had given priority to financial sector reform in the sequence of reforms in the economy. The recent experience has shown that sustained growth and price stability play a very vital role in attracting international investment.

The heads of financial institutions present at the meeting gave a brief account of their respective areas and their reform agenda.
