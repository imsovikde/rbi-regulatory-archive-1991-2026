## Monitoring FIIs Investments in Indian Companies

## October 5, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India on October 5, 1999 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares of M/s. Satyam Computers Services Ltd. in the secondary market under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), as the FIIs investments to the paid up capital of the company have gone down below the 26 per cent level.
