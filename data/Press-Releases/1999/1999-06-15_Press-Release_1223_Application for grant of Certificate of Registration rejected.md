# Application for grant of Certificate of Registration rejected

## June 15, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected the applications for grant of certificates of registration submitted by the undernoted financial companies: <u>Sr.No. Name of the Company Rejected on</u>

1. Himachal Housing & Fiscal Services Ltd., 14.6.1999 Neelam Bhawan, Sanjauli, Shimla - 171 006.
2. Bachan Leasing & Auto Finance Limited, 14.6.1999 Diamond Complex, Opp. Bus Stand, Ludhiana - 141 002. As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institutions as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
## Rupambara Padhi

## Manager
