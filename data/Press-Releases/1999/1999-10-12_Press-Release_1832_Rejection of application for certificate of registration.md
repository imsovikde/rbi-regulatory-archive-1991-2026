# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## October 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on October 11, 1999, the application for Certificate of Registration submitted by M/s. Krittika Finco & Investment Ltd., having its Registered Office at 114/C, Garfa Main Road, Calcutta - 700 075. As such, the company cannot transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45-I of the RBI Act, 1934. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1999-2000/509**
