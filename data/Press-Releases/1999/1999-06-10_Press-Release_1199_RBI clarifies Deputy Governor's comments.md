# RBI clarifies Deputy Governor's comments

## June 10, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India's attention has been drawn to the story appearing in one of the news agencies to the effect that the Deputy Governor Shri Jagdish Capoor's reported comments at Basle were intended to convey the Reserve Bank of India's intention to weaken the rupee. It has been officially clarified that the Reserve Bank has no such intention and the interpretation of the comment is without any basis or foundation whatsoever.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
