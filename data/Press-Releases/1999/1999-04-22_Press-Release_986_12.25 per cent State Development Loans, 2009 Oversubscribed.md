# 12.25 per cent State Development Loans, 2009 Oversubscribed

## April 22, 1999

The 12.25 per cent Ten-year State Development Loans in respect of twenty-four State Governments opened for subscription for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.3,440.00 crore on April 21, 1999. The subscriptions received aggregated Rs.6,933.15 crore. Some subscriptions received at designated branches of State Bank of India/associate banks at district headquarters of the States are still being reported. The excess subscriptions received by some State Governments will be retained.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
