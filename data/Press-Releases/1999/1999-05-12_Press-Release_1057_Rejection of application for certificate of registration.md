# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

## May 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on May 11, 1999, the application for certificate of registration submitted by M/s Farrukhabad Investment (India) Ltd., a Non-Banking financial Comapny having its Registered Office at (near) I. T. I. Chauraha, Farrukhabad, Uttar Pradesh. As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution, as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/1397**
