# Ten-year Government of India Stock Auction Announced

## April 5, 1999

The Government of India has announced an auction of ten year stock for Rs.3,000 crore. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on Tuesday, April 6,

1999. Bids, in the prescribed form obtainable from the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted on Tuesday, April 6, 1999 during banking hours. On the basis of the bids received, RBI will determine the maximum rate of yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on April 6, 1999 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 7, 1999. The new stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.
## Chhaya Raje Manager
