# RBI asks banks to waive service charges for funds remitted for Orissa cyclone relief

## November 18, 1999

The Reserve Bank has today issued a circular to all commercial banks advising them to waive service charges/commission in respect of funds being remitted for credit to the Prime Minister's Relief Fund, Orissa Cyclone Relief Fund and also other contribution being remitted to provide relief to cyclone affected people in the Orissa State.

The Reserve Bank has also advised banks to immediately issue necessary instructions to their controlling offices and branches.
