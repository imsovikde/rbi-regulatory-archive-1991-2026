# Monitoring FIIs Investments in Indian Companies

## October 18, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India on October 18, 1999 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares of Bombay Oxygen Corporation Ltd. in the secondary market under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), as the FIIs investments to the paid up capital of the company have gone down below the 22 per cent level. **Rupambara Padhi** **Manager** **Press Release : 1999-2000/536**
