# New ED at RBI

## June 30, 1999

Shri M.G. Srivastava, Chief General Manager-in-charge, Industrial & Export Credit Department has appointed as Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India with effect from July 1, 1999.

Shri S. M. Taqi Hussaini has laid down office as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India as at the close of business today.
