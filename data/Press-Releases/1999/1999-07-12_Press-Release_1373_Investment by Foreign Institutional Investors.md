# Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## July 12, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India on July 12, 1999 has notified that no further purchases of shares of M/s. Satyam Computer Services Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s. Satyam Computer Services Ltd., has reached the overall ceiling of 30 per cent of its paid up capital.
