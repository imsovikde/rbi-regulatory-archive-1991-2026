# Seven-year Government Stock, 2006-Auction Results

**April 9, 1999** The results of the auction of Seven-year Government Stock, 2006 held on April 9, 1999 are: **Auction Results**

I. Notified amount : Rs.3000.00 crore II. Bids received
(i) Number : 222 (ii) Amount : Rs.8987.00 crore
III. Cut-off Yield (Coupon Rate) : 11.68 per cent

IV. Bids Accepted

(i) Number : 16 (ii) Amount : Rs.3000.00 crore
V. **Partial Allotment Percentage** : 71.11 VI. **Devolvement on RBI** : Nil VII. **Devolvement on Primary** **Dealers**
: Nil (in fulfillment of underwriting commitment)

VIII. Weighted Average Yield : 11.67 per cent
