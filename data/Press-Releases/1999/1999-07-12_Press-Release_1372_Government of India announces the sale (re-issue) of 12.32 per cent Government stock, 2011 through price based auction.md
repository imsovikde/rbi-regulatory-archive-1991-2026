**Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of 12.32 per cent Government stock, 2011 through price based auction**

## July 12, 1999

Government of India has announced the sale (reissue) of 12.32 per cent Government Stock, 2011 for a notified amount of Rs.2,500 crore through price based auction. The auction will be conduced by Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai on July 15, 1999 and the successful bidders have to make payment on July 16, 1999. The stock is eligible for ready forward facility.
