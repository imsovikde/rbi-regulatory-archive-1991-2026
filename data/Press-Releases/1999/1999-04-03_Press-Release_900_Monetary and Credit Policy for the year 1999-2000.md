# Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 1999-2000

## April 3, 1999

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will hold a meeting with the Chief Executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.30 a.m. on April 20, 1999 to announce the Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 1999-2000.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
