# 12.40 per cent Government Stock, 2013 on 'private placement-cum-tap sales' basis

## April 7, 1999

The 12.40 per cent Government Stock, 2013 for an amount of Rs.3,000 crore privately placed with RBI today and put on sale simultaneously through the Open Market window was sold out during the day and the tap was closed.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager
