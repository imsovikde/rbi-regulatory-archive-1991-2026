# Banking Statistics Summary Tables-March 1998

## September 16, 1999

The Department of Statistical Analysis and Computer Services of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out a publication, viz., Banking Statistics-Summary Tables-March 1998. The publication gives comprehensive data on number of offices, employees deposits and credit of scheduled commercial banks as at the end of March 1998.

The publication is available from the Sales Section (Division of Reports, Reviews and Publications), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P.M. Road, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.25. The publication will be also available on the Reserve Bank's website (URL: <u>http//www.rbi.org.in)</u>.

## P.V. Sadanandan Asstt. Manager
