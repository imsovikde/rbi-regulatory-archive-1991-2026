# RBI Employees' Contribution to Armed Forces

## June 25, 1999

All categories of the employees of the Reserve Bank of India have decided to contribute a minimum of one day of their salary to the welfare of the families of the Armed Forces. **Alpana Killawala** **General Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/1619**
