# RBI grants 12 new licences to foreign banks in 1998-99

## June 15, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has issued further five licences for the year 1998-99 for opening branches by foreign banks in India as under

|Name of bank|Centre|
|---|---|
|1. Citibank N A|Ahmedabad|
|2. American Express Bank|Bangalore|
|3. ANZ Grindlays Bank PIc|Pune|
|4. Bank of Nova Scotia|Hyderabad|
|5. Standard Chartered Bank|Bangalore|

The Reserve Bank of India had earlier during the year issued 7 licences which included two maiden branches of new foreign banks and 5 licences for additional branches of existing banks as follows

## Maiden branches of new foreign banks

|1. Morgan Guaranty Trust Company of New York, USA|Mumbai|
|---|---|
|2. KBC Bank NV. Belgium Expansion by existing banks|Mumbai|
|1. Citibank N A|Hyderabad|
|2. ABN Amro Bank|Indore|
|3. Banque Nationale de Paris|Secunderabad|
|4. Bank of Ceylon|Coimbatore|
|5. Hong-kong & Shanghai Banking Corporation|Hyderabad|
