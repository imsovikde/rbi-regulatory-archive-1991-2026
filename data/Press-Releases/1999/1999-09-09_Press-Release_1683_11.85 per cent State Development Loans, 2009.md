# 11.85 per cent State Development Loans, 2009

## September 9, 1999

The 11.85 per cent State Development Loans, 2009 of twenty three State Governments which opened for subscription on September 8, 1999 for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.3,250.08 crore were closed on the same day. The total subscription aggregated Rs.4,230.70 crore. As the loans of 9 states, viz. Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Punjab and Tamil Nadu have been oversubscribed, partial allotments ranging from 22.81 per cent to 89.80 per cent have been made. Excess subscription is being refunded to the applicants.
