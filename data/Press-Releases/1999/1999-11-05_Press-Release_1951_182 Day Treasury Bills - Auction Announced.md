# 182 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## November 5, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 182 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.100 crore on November 9, 1999. In view of November 9, 1999 having been declared as a Public Holiday instead of the November 8, 1999, by the Government of Maharashtra, the Reserve Bank has decided to conduct the auction of 182-Day Bills on November 8, 1999. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on <u>Monday, November</u> <u>8, 1999 before 1430 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by</u> <u>successful bidders will be on Thursday, November 11, 1999.</u>
