# New ED at RBI

## July 31, 1999

Shri G.P. Muniappan, Regional Director, Tamilnadu has been appointed as Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India with effect from August 1, 1999. Consequent on the elevation of Shir Muniappan as Executive Director, he will look after the following departments :

1. Department of Banking Operations and Development
2. Department of Banking Supervision
3. Secretary’s Department
4. Financial Institutions Division Shri S. Gurumurthy has laid down office as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India as at the close of business today.
