# RBI gives final approval to one more primary dealer

## September 23, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has granted its final approval to IDBI Capital Market Services Limited to operate as a primary dealer in the government securities market.

IDBI Capital Market Services Limited was granted "in-principle" approval on August 7,

1999. With the grant of final approval to the aforesaid entity, the number of primary dealers in the government securities market has gone up to fourteen.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
