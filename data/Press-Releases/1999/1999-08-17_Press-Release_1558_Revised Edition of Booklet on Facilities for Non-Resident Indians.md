# Revised Edition of Booklet on Facilities for Non-Resident Indians

## August 17, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has published the revised edition of booklet on Facilities for Non-Resident Indians. The booklet explains various facilities for investments and maintenance of bank accounts in India, available to Non-Resident Indians/Overseas Corporate Bodies in question answer form. The booklet is amended up to end of May

1999. Initially brought out in June 1992, the publication is available for sale at the Sales Section, Division of Report, Review and publications, Department of Economic Analysis & Policy, Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, Ground Floor, Sir P.M.Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.35. It is also available on the Reserve Bank of India website (URL : <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>) under a special URL : <u>www.faqs.rbi.org.in</u>
