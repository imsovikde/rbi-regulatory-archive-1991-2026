# Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills RBI announces Calendar, will issue 182-day Tbills

## May 21, 1999

## Calendar

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the following calendar of Treasury Bills issuance. <u>This calendar is valid till September, 1999.</u>

Type of Periodicity Notified Amount Day of Day of T-Bills (Rs.Crore) Auction Payment

|T-Bills||(Rs.Crore)|Auction|Payment|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|14-day|Weekly|100|Every Friday|Following Saturday|
|91-day|Weekly|100|Every Friday|Following Saturday|
|182-day|Fortnightly|100|Wednesdays preceding the non-reporting Fridays|Following Thursday|
|364-day|Fortnightly|500|Wednesdays preceding the|Following Thursday|

non-reporting Fridays

It may be recalled that in the "Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 1999-2000," the Reserve Bank of India had proposed to announce a calendar of issue of Treasury Bills of various maturities for the year 1999-2000 in order to facilitate the market participants to plan their investments.

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if circumstances warrant without assigning any reasons. The calendar is, thus, subject to change if circumstances warrant including for reasons, such as, intervening holidays, and such changes will be communicated through regular press releases.

**182-day tbills**

In order to promote a six-month benchmark rate and provide additional instrument for asset liability management, it has been decided to issue 182-day Treasury Bills every fortnight in addition to other maturities already being issued. The first auction of 182-day tbills will be held on Wednesday, May 26, 1999. The Reserve Bank will notify the details of the auction separately.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

Introduction of 182-day Treasury bills was announced in the Monetary and Credit Policy for the first half of 1998-99, but was not introduced for various reasons.
