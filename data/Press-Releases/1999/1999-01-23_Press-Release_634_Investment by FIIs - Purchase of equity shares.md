# Investment by FIIs-Purchase of equity shares

## January 23, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that overall ceiling of 24 per cent of paid up equity capital permitted to be held by all registered Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) has been reached in M/s. Pentafour Software Ltd. No further purchases of shares of this company should, therefore, be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs.

The Reserve Bank has also advised that as per the resolution passed by the Board and in General Body meetings of M/s. NIIT Ltd. FIIs can now purchase its equity shares and convertible debentures up to 30 per cent of the paid up capital of M/s. NIIT Ltd., through primary and stock exchanges. As such, the earlier RBI advice of not purchasing equity shares of this company on behalf of FII/NRI/OCB clients from the secondary market, as the overall ceiling of 24 per cent had been reached, stands withdrawn.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
