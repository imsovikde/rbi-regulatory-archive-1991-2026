# Calendar for Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills-Revision

## November 16, 1999

The Reserve Bank of India had announced on September 28, 1999 a calendar of Treasury Bills auctions. As a part of the contingency plan in respect of Year 2000 readiness and the decision that the banks shall remain closed for public transactions on January 1, 2000, it has been decided to revise the dates of auctions of Treasury Bills falling during the second half of December, 1999 as under :

|Type of|Scheduled|||Revised||
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Treasury|Date of|Date of|Date of|Date of|Date of|
|Bills|Auction|Payment|Auction|Payment|Repayment|
|14-Day|17.12.1999|18.12.1999|21.12.1999|22.12.1999|05.01.2000|
|14-Day|24.12.1999|27.12.1999|24.12.1999|27.12.1999|10.01.2000*|
|14-Day|31.12.1999|01.01.2000|30.12.1999|04.01.2000|18.01.2000|
|91-Day|31.12.1999|01.01.2000|30.12.1999|04.01.2000|04.04.2000|
|364-Day|29.12.1999|30.12.1999|28.12.1999|29.12.1999|27.12.2000|

* There will be no change in respect of auction of 14 Day Treasury Bills as on December 24, 1999.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
