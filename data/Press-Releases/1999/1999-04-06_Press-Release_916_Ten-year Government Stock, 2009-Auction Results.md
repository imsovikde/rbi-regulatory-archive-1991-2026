# Ten-year Government Stock, 2009-Auction Results

**April 6, 1999** The results of the auction of Ten-year Government Stock, 2009 held on April 6, 1999 are: **Auction Results**

I. Notified amount : Rs.3000.00 crore II. Bids received
(i) Number : 188 (ii) Amount : Rs.7180.35 crore
III. Cut-off Yield (Coupon Rate) : 11.99 per cent

IV. Bids Accepted

(i) Number : 93 (ii) Amount : Rs.3000.00 crore
V. **Partial Allotment Percentage** : 31 VI. **Devolvement on RBI** : Nil VII. **Devolvement on Primary** **Dealers**
: Nil (in fulfillment of underwriting commitment)

VIII. Weighted Average Yield : 11.97 per cent
