# Banking Statistics : March 1998

## November 16, 1999

The Department of Statistical Analysis & Computer Services of the Reserve Bank of India has brought out a publication, viz., Banking Statistics (Volume 27) - March 1998. The publication is based on the Basic Statistical Returns collected from the scheduled commercial banks. The publication presents comprehensive data on number of offices, employees, deposits and outstanding credit of scheduled commercial banks according to various characteristics as at the end of March 1998.

The publication is available for sale from the Sales Section (DRRP), Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P. M. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 at a price of Rs.220. The publication is also be available at the Reserve Bank's website www.rbi.org.in.
