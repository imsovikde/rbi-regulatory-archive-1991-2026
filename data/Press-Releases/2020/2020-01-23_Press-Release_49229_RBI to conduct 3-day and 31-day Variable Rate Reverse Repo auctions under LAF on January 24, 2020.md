## RBI to conduct 3-day and 31-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under

## LAF on January 24, 2020

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 24, 2020, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` crore)|(day)|||

2 1,30,000 3 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Monday)

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
