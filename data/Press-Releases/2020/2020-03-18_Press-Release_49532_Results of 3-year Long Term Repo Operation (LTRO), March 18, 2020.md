## March 18, 2020

|Results of 3-year Long Term Repo Operation (LTRO), March 18, 2020 Today, the Reserve Bank conducted long term repo operations (LTROs) for a notified amount of `25,000 crore with a 3-year tenor in pursuance of the announcement vide press release dated March 16, 2020. 2. The total bids received amounted to `27,096 crore, implying a bid to cover ratio (i.e ., the amount of bids received relative to the notified amount) of 1.1. 3. Allotment was done for all bids on a pro rata basis as given below:||
