## April 17, 2020

## Review of Limits of Way and Means Advances (WMA) of States/UTs

RBI had announced an increase in the WMA limit of the States on <u>April 1, 2020</u>. With a view to providing greater comfort to the States to undertake COVID-19 containment and mitigation efforts and enable them to better plan their market borrowings, it has been decided to

- increase the WMA limit of the States by 60% over and above the level as on March 31, 2020
- The increased limit will be available till September 30, 2020.
