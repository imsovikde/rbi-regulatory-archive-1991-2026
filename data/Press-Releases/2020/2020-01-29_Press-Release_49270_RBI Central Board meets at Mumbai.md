## January 29, 2020

## RBI Central Board meets at Mumbai

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Central Board had its 581 st meeting today in Mumbai under the Chairmanship of Shri Shaktikanta Das, Governor.

The Board nominated Dr. Janak Raj, Executive Director on the Monetary Policy Committee as an ex officio member.

Deputy Governors Shri B. P. Kanungo, Shri Mahesh Kumar Jain and Dr. M. D. Patra and other Directors of the Central Board – Shri N. Chandrasekaran, Shri Bharat N. Doshi, Shri Sudhir Mankad, Dr. Ashok Gulati, Shri Manish Sabharwal, Dr. Prasanna Kumar Mohanty, Shri Satish K. Marathe, Ms. Revathy Iyer and Prof. Sachin Chaturvedi attended the meeting.
