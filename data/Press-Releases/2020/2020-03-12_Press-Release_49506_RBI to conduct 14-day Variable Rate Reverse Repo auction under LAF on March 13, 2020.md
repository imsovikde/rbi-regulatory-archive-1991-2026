## RBI to conduct 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on March 13, 2020

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on March 13, 2020, Friday, under the <u>revised Liquidity Management Framework</u> <u>issued on February 6, 2020</u>. **Notified Amount Tenor** **Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(**` **crore) (day)** March 27, 2020 1 3,80,000 14 2:30 pm to 3:00 pm (Friday)

The operational guidelines for the auction as given in the Reserve Bank’s <u>Press</u> <u>Release 2019-2020/1947 dated February 13, 2020</u> will remain the same.
