April 09, 2020 **Result of 3-year Targeted Long Term Repo Operation (TLTRO), April 9, 2020** Today, the Reserve Bank conducted the third Targeted Long Term Repo Operation (TLTRO) for a notified amount of ₹ 25,000 crore with a 3-year tenor in pursuance of the TLTRO announced *vide* <u>press release dated April 3, 2020</u>.

2. The total bids that were received amounted to ₹ 1,13,470 crore, implying a bid to cover ratio (*i.e*., the amount of bids received relative to the notified amount) of 4.5.
3. Allotment was done for all bids on a *pro-rata* basis as given below:

|Tenor|3-year|
|---|---|
|Date of Reversal|April 7, 2023|
|Notified Amount (in ₹ crore)|25,000|
|No of bids received|18|
|Total amount of bids received (in ₹ crore)|1,13,470|
|Amount allotted (in ₹ crore)|25,016|
|Pro-rata Allotment Percentage (%)|22.04 (Yogesh Dayal)|
