## February 14, 2020

## Conversion/Switch of Government of India (GoI)’s Securities

As per the Issuance Calendar for Marketable Dated Securities, auction for switches are scheduled on the third Monday of every month. However, keeping in view the bunching of holidays in the third week of February 2020, the switch auction has been rescheduled to Monday, February 24, 2020. The press release indicating the details of the auction will be issued in due course.
