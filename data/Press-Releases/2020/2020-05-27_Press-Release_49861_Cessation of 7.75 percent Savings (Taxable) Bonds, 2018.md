## May 27, 2020

## Cessation of 7.75 percent Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018

The Government of India (GoI), vide <u>Notification F.No.4(28)-(W&M)/2017 dated May</u> 27, 2020**,** hereby announces that 7.75% Savings (Taxable) Bonds, 2018 shall cease <u>for subscription</u> with effect from the close of banking business on Thursday, the 28 th of May, 2020.
