The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **April 17, 2020 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between **09.00 A.M. and 09.30 A.M.** **on the date of underwriting auction**.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the date of issue of securities.
