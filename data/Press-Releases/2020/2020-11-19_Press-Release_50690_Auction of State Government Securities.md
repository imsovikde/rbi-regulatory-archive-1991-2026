The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) system on **November 24, 2020 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to 10% of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of 1% of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the <u>Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>.
