## August 28, 2020

## Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2020-21 Series VI-Issue Price

In terms of <u>GoI notification F.No.4(4)-B(W&M)/2020</u> and <u>RBI circular</u> <u>IDMD.CDD.No.2729/14.04.050/2019-20 dated April 13, 2020</u>, the Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2020-21-Series VI will be opened for subscription for the period from Aug 31, 2020 to Sep 04, 2020. The nominal value of the bond based on the simple average closing price [published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA)] for gold of 999 purity of the last three business days of the week preceding the subscription period, i.e. Aug 26 – Aug 28, 2020 works out to ₹5,117/- (Rupees five thousand one hundred and seventeen only) per gram of gold.

Government of India, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, has decided to offer a discount of ₹50/- per gram less than the nominal value to those investors applying online and the payment against the application is made through digital mode. For such investors, the issue price of Gold Bond will be ₹5,067/- (Rupees five thousand and sixty seven only) per gram of gold.
