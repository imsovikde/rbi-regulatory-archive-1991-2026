## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – July 2020</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – July 2020</u> iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q4:2019-20</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q1:2020-21</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round 65</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to
