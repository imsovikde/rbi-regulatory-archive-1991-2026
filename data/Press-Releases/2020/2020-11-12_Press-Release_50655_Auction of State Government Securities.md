The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **November 17, 2020 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to 10% of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of 1% of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the <u>Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>.

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on <u>November 17, 2020 (Tuesday). The non-competitive bids should be submitted</u> <u>between 10.30 A.M. and 11.00 A.M. and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and 11.30 A.M.</u>
