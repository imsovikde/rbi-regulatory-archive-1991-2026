October 12, 2020 **RBI appoints Shri Jayant Kumar Dash as new ED** The Reserve Bank of India has appointed Shri Jayant Kumar Dash as Executive Director (ED) with effect from October 12, 2020. Prior to being promoted as ED, Shri Jayant Kumar Dash was Chief General Manager- in-Charge of Department of Supervision, Central Office. Shri Dash has, over a span of three decades, served in currency and debt management, deposit insurance and banking / non-banking supervision areas in the Reserve Bank. As Executive Director, Shri Dash will look after Department of Regulation II. Shri Dash holds a Master’s degree in Economics besides having earned professional qualifications in advance management, treasury management, fraud examination, data analytics, cyber law and tackling financial corruption.
