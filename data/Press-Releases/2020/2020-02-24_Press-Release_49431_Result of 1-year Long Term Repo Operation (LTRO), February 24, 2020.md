## February 24, 2020

|Result of 1-year Long Term Repo Operation (LTRO), February 24, 2020 Today, the Reserve Bank conducted the second long term repo operation (LTRO) for a notified amount of `25,000 crore with a 1-year tenor in pursuance of the LTROs announced vide press release dated February 7, 2020. 2. The total bids that were received amounted to `1,23,154 crore, implying a bid to cover ratio (i.e ., the amount of bids received relative to the notified amount) of 4.9. 3.
