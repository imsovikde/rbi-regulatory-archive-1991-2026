## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – January 2020</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – January 2020</u> iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q2:2019-20</u> iv. Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q3:2019-20
v. Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–
<u>nd</u> <u>Round¹ 62</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
