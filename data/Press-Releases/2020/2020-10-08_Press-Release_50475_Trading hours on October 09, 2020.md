## Trading hours on October 09, 2020

A reference is invited to the <u>press release dated October 6, 2020</u> on auction of Government of India dated securities scheduled on October 9, 2020. The time for submission of competitive bids for the auction will be between 12 noon and 1 PM. Accordingly, it has been decided to extend the trading hours on October 09, 2020 for various markets regulated by the Bank as under:
