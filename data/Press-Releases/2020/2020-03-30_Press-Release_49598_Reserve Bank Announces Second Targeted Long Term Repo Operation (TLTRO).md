March 30, 2020 **Reserve Bank Announces Second Targeted Long Term Repo Operation (TLTRO)** As announced in the Statement of Developmental and Regulatory Policies on March 27, 2020, the Reserve Bank will conduct TLTROs of up to three years tenor of appropriate sizes for a total amount of up to ` 1,00,000 crore. The first tranche of TLTRO for ` 25,000 crores was conducted on March 27, 2020. It has now been decided to conduct another TLTRO for ` 25,000 crore. The details of the operation are as under:
