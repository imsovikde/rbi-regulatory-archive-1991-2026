## April 23, 2020

## Result of 3-year Targeted Long Term Repo Operation (TLTRO) 2.0-April 23, 2020

Today, the Reserve Bank conducted the first tranche of Targeted Long Term Repo Operation (TLTRO) 2.0 for a notified amount of ₹25,000 crore with a 3-year tenor in pursuance of the announcement made *vide* <u>press release 2237/2019-2020 dated April</u> <u>17, 2020</u>.

2. The total bids that were received amounted to ₹12,850 crore, implying a bid to cover ratio (*i.e*., the amount of bids received relative to the notified amount) of 0.5.
3. Allotment was done for all bids as given below:

|Tenor|3-year|
|---|---|
|Date of Reversal|April 21, 2023|
|Notified Amount (in ₹ crore)|25,000|
|No of bids received|14|
|Total amount of bids received (in ₹ crore)|12,850|
|Amount allotted (in ₹ crore)|12,850|
|Pro-rata Allotment Percentage (%)|NA|

4. The RBI will review the auction results and take a view in the matter.
