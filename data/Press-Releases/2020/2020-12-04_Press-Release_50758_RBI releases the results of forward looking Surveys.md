## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – November 2020</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – November 2020</u> iii. Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–
<u>th</u> <u>Round¹ 67</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Ajit Prasad

**Press Release: 2020-2021/731** Director

The results of the previous survey round were released on <u>October 09, 2020</u> on the Reserve Bank’s website.
