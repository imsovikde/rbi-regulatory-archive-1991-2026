## Directions under Section 35 A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation

## Act, 1949, (AACS) – Vasantdada Nagari Sahakari Bank Ltd Dist. Osmanabad,

## Maharashtra-Extension of period

Reserve Bank of India, in the public interest, had issued directions to **Vasantdada** **Nagari Sahakari Bank Ltd Dist. Osmanabad, Maharashtra** in exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35 A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (AACS) from the close of business on November 13, 2017. These directions were modified from time to time, the validity of which was last extended upto December 31, 2020. These directions shall continue to apply to the bank **for a further period of three months from January 1, 20**2**1 to March 31,** **2021, subject to review**. The Directions stipulate certain restrictions and / or ceiling on withdrawal / acceptance of deposits. A copy of Directions is displayed at the bank’s premises for interested members of public to peruse. Reserve Bank of India may consider modifications of the Directions depending upon the circumstances. The issue of Directions should not per se be construed as cancellation of banking license by the Reserve Bank of India. The bank will continue to undertake banking business with restrictions till its financial position improves.
