January 13, 2020 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on January 14, 2020**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on January 14, 2020, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

1 2,10,000 1 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Wednesday)
