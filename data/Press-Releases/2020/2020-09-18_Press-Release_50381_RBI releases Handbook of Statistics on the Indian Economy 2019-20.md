## RBI releases Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy 2019-20

Today, the Reserve Bank released its annual publication titled “<u>Handbook of</u> <u>Statistics on the Indian Economy, 2019-20</u>” (HBS). This publication, the 22nd in the series, disseminates time series data on various economic and financial indicators relating to the Indian economy. The current volume contains 240 statistical tables covering national income aggregates, output, prices, money, banking, financial markets, public finances, foreign trade and balance of payments and select socio- economic indicators.

Longer time-series are also available on the Reserve Bank’s Data Warehouse portal "<u>Database on Indian Economy (DBIE)</u>” (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>), where all data series are updated periodically.

Feedback/comments on the HBS are welcome and may be sent (i) through the feedback button available on DBIE portal (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>); or (ii) to the <u>email</u>; or (iii) to the Director, Data Management and Dissemination Division, Department of Statistics and Information Management, Reserve Bank of India, C-9, Bandra- Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400 051.
