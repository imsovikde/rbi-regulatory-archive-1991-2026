## December 29, 2020

## RBI releases “Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India: 2019-20”

Today, the Reserve Bank released its web publication entitled ‘<u>Statistical Tables</u> <u>Relating to Banks in India: 2019-20</u>’ covering the activities of scheduled commercial banks and rural co-operative banks.

For scheduled commercial banks, entity-wise information on major items of liabilities and assets; income and expenses; selected financial ratios; number of employees and details of priority sector advances (for Public sector banks) are provided. In addition, capital adequacy ratios (CRAR), non-performing assets (NPAs), exposure to sensitive sectors, contingent liabilities and unclaimed deposits are included.

Time series on all variables have been updated up to 2019-20. The publication can be accessed on the RBI website through the link ‘<u>Time Series Publications</u>’ under the Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).
