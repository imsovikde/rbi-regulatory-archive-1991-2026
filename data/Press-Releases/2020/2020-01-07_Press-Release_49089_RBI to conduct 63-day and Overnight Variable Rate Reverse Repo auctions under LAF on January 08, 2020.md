## January 07, 2020

**RBI to conduct 63-day and Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on January 08, 2020**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 08, 2020, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
