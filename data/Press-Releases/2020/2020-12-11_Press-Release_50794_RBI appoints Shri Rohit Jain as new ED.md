## December 11, 2020

## RBI appoints Shri Rohit Jain as new ED

The Reserve Bank of India has appointed Shri Rohit Jain as Executive Director (ED) with effect from December 11, 2020.

Prior to being promoted as ED, Shri Rohit Jain was Chief General Manager-in- Charge, Department of Supervision.

Shri Jain has, over a span of about three decades, served in supervisory, human resource management, banking and other areas in the Reserve Bank.

As Executive Director, Shri Jain will look after Department of Supervision (Risk, Analytics and Vulnerability Assessment).

Shri Jain holds a Master’s degree in Commerce and a Master’s in Business Administration besides having earned professional qualifications including International Certificate in Banking Risk and Regulation (ICBRR), Certified Associate of Indian Institute of Banking and Finance (CAIIB) and Certified Bank Trainer.
