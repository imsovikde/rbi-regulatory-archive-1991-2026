April 30, 2020 **RBI extends Fixed Rate Reverse Repo and MSF window**

Reserve Bank had vide <u>Press Release 2019-2020/2147 dated March 30, 2020</u> extended the window timings of Fixed Rate Reverse Repo and MSF operations.

In view of the continuing disruptions caused by COVID-19, it has been decided to continue with the revised timings till further notice.
