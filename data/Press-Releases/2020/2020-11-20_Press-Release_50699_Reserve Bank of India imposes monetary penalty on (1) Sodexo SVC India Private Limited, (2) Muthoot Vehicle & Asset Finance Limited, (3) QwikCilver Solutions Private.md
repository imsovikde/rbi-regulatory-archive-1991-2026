## November 20, 2020

**Reserve Bank of India imposes monetary penalty on (1) Sodexo SVC India** **Private Limited, (2) Muthoot Vehicle & Asset Finance Limited, (3) QwikCilver** **Solutions Private Limited, (4) Phonepe Private Limited, (5) Delhi Metro Rail** **Corporation Limited and (6) Punjab National Bank**

In exercise of powers vested under Section 30 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, the Reserve Bank of India has imposed monetary penalty on the following entities for non-compliance of regulatory guidelines.

## Sr. Speaking Amount of

|No.|Name of the Entity|Order dated|Penalty (₹ lakh)||
|---|---|---|---|---|
||Non-bank Prepaid Payment Instrument Issuers||||
|1.|Sodexo SVC India Private Limited|03-08-2020||200.00|
|2.|Muthoot Vehicle & Asset Finance Ltd.|12-08-2020||34.55|
|3.|QwikCilver Solutions Private Limited|15-10-2020||100.00|
|4.|Phonepe Private Limited|04-11-2020||139.00|
|5.|Delhi Metro Rail Corporation Limited ATM Network Operator|13-11-2020||5.00|
|6.|Punjab National Bank|13-11-2020||100.00|
