April 09, 2020 **State Government Borrowings-Clarification** There are certain media reports that the Government of India has allowed States to avail up to 50% of their borrowing requirement for the Financial Year 2020-21, in April 2020 itself.

In this connection, the communication issued by Government of India on the subject is available at the following link:

<u>[https://doe.gov.in/whatsnew/goi-consent-under-art-293-3-raising-omb-states-during-](https://doe.gov.in/whatsnew/goi-consent-under-art-293-3-raising-omb-states-during-)</u> <u>2020-21</u>
