## 91 day, 182 day and 364 day Treasury Bills auction – Revised

Reserve Bank of India announces the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills as per the following details:

## Notified

**Sr** **Treasury Bill Amount Auction Date Settlement date** **No** **(in** ` **crore)** 1 91 Day 5,000 March 18, 2020 March 19, 2020 2 182 Day 10,000 (Wednesday) (Thursday) 3 364 Day 10,000 **Total 25,000**

The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the <u>General</u> <u>Notification F.No.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated March 27, 2018</u> along with the <u>Amendment</u> <u>Notification No.F.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated April 05, 2018</u>, issued by Government of India, as amended from time to time. State governments, eligible Provident Funds in India, designated Foreign Central Banks and any person or institution specified by the Bank in this regard, can participate on non-competitive basis, the allocation for which will be outside the notified amount. Individuals can also participate on non-competitive basis as retail investors. For retail investors, the allocation will be restricted to a maximum of 5 percent of the notified amount.

The auction will be **Price based** using **multiple price method**. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India’s Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **Wednesday, March 18, 2020,** during the below given timings:

|Category|Timing|
|---|---|
|Competitive bids|10:30 am - 12:00 pm|
|Non-Competitive bids|10:30 am - 11:30 am|

Results will be announced on the day of the auction.

Payment by successful bidders to be made on **Thursday, March 19, 2020.**

Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to the Public Debt Office (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22632527, 022-22701299) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website
