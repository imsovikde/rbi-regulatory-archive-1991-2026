## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 30, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.4700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1200 on June 30, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 27, 2003) were Rs.46.4900 and Rs.53.1300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 27, 2003|77.1269|38.85|
|June 30, 2003|76.7545|38.7800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
