## RBI resets rate of interest on FRBs 2006 at 5.29%

## May 17, 2003

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2006 (FRB, 2006) applicable for the half year May 22, 2003-November 21, 2003 shall be 5.29 per cent per annum. It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2006 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on November 21, 2001) over and above the variable base rate. It was indicated that the variable base rate for payment of interest would be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of the Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of the Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to May 17, 2003 works out to 5.34 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on November 21, 2001 was (-)0.05 (minus 0.05). The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
