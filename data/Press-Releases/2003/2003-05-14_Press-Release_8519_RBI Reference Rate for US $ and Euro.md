## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.1400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1600 on May 14, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 13, 2003) were Rs.47.1700 and Rs.54.2700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|May 13, 2003|75.8659|40.45|
|May 14, 2003|75.8200|40.4300|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
