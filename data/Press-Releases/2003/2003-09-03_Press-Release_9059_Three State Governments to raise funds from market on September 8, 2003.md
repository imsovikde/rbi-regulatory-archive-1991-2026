## Three State Governments to raise funds from market on September 8, 2003

## September 3, 2003

Three State Governments have announced the sale of 10 year Stock to the public by auction for an aggregate amount Rs.955.00 crore as detailed below: (Rs. in crore)

|Sr. No.|Sate|Notified Amount|
|---|---|---|
|1|Maharashtra|400.00|
|2|Madhya Pradesh|220.00|
|3|West Bengal Total|335.00 955.00|

The auction will be conducted by Mumbai (Fort) Office of the Reserve Bank of India on Monday, September 8, 2003. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Monday, September 8, 2003 before 12.30 hrs. NDS members shall submit their applications through NDS only. Results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Tuesday, September 9, 2003.
