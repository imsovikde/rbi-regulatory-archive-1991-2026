## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 10, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.3500 on January 10, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 9, 2003) were Rs.47.9700 and Rs.50.4300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
