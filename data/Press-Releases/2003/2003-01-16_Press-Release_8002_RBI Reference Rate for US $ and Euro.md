## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 16, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.7000 on January 16, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 15, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9400 and Rs.50.5400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
