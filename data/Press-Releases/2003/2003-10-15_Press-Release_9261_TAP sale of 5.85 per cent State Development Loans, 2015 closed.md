## TAP sale of 5.85 per cent State Development Loans, 2015 closed

## October 15, 2003

The sale on tap of 5.85 per cent State Loans, 2015 in respect of three States viz., Assam, Bihar, and Uttar Pradesh has been closed on October 15, 2003.

**P.V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
