## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 28, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.6900 on August 28, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 27, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8400 and Rs.49.8100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 27, 2003|72.0284|38.98|
|August 28, 2003|71.8248|39.0000|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
