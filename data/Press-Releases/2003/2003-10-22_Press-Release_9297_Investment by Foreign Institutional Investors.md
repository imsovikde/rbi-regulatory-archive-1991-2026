## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## October 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on October 22, 2003 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of I-flex Solutions Ltd., up to 60 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting.
