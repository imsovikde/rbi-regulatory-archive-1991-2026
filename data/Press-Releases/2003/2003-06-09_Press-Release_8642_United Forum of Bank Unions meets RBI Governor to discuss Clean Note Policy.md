## United Forum of Bank Unions meets RBI Governor to discuss Clean Note Policy

## June 9, 2003

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India met the United Forum of Bank Unions here today. The meeting was held at the request of the United Forum of Bank Unions on the implementation of the Reserve Bank of India's clean note policy. A free exchange of views took place at the meeting between the Forum and the Reserve Bank. The Governor pointed out that the Reserve Bank was keen to implement its clean note policy for the benefit of members of the public who deserved clean notes. It was, however, not intended to cause any problem or difficulty to the bank staff or bank customers. The Forum expressed the difficulties faced by the bank staff while implementing the clean note policy. It was agreed that the bank management, the unions and the Reserve Bank of India will work together to supply good clean notes to members of the public.
