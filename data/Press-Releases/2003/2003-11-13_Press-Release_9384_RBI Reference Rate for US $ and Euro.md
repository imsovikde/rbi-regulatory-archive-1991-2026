## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8600 on November 13, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 12, 2003) were Rs.45.3200 and Rs.52.4100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
