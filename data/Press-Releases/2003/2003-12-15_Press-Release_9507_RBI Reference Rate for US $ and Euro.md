## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 15, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.5600 on December 15, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 12, 2003) were Rs.45.5300 and Rs.55.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
