## Calendar for Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills

## March 28, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the calendar of Treasury Bills issuance for the period April 1, 2003 to September 30, 2003.

## Type of Periodicity Notified Amount Day of Day of <u>of T-Bills (Rs. Crore) Auction Payment</u>

91-Day Weekly 500 Every Following Wednesday Friday 364-Day Fortnightly 1000 Wednesday Reporting Preceding the Friday <u>Reporting Friday</u>

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if emerging circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons such as intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)- W&M/97 dated March 31, 1998 issued by the Government of India. **Alpana Killawala General Manager**
