## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.2000 on August 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 11, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9600 and Rs.51.8400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
