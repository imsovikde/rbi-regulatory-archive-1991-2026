## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 3, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.3800 on March 3, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 28, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6700 and Rs.51.2500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
