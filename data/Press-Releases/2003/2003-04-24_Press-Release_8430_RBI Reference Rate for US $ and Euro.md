## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.3400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.1600 on April 24, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 23,

2003) were Rs.47.3300 and Rs.51.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date||Currency|
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|April 23, 2003|74.5992|39.39|
|April 24, 2003|75.2659|39.5300|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P. V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
