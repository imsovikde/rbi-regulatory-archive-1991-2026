## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.6800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.9100 on June 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 11, 2003) were Rs.46.7300 and Rs.54.7600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 11, 2003|77.3615|39.66|
|June 12, 2003|77.1527|39.6500|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
