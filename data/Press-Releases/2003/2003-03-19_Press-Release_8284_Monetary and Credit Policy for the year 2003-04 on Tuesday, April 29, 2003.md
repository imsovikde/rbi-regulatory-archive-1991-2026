## Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2003-04 on Tuesday, April 29, 2003

## March 19, 2003

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2003-04 on Tuesday, April 29, 2003. He will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks for announcing the Policy which will take place at 11.00 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.

## P. V. Sadanandan
