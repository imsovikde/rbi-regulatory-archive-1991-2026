## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 15, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.5400 on January 15, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 14, 2003) were Rs.47.9000 and Rs.50.5700 respectively.
