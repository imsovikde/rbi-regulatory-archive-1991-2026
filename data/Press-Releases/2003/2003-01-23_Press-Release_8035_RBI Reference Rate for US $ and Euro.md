## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 23, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.3800 on January 23, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 22, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9100 and Rs.51.4100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
