## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## August 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on August 13, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of ACC Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) without prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that ACC Ltd., has reached the overall ceiling of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
