## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8400 on October 14, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 13, 2003 ) were Rs.45.3500 and Rs.53.3700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
