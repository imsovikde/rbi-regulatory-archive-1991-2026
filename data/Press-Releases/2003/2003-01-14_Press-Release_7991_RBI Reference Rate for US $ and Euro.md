## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.5700 on January 14, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 13, 2003) were Rs.47.9500 and Rs.50.6500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
