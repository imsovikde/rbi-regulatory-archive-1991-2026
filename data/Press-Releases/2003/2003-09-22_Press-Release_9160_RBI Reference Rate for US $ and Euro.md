## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.3200 on September 22, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 19, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9300 and Rs.51.7400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|September 19, 2003|74.2849|39.84|
|September 22, 2003|75.2673|40.7300|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
