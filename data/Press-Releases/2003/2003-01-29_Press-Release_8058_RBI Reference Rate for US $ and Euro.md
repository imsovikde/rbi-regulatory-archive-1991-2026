## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 29, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.8600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.0400 on January 29, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 28, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9200 and Rs.51.8600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
