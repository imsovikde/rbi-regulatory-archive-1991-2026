December 29, 2003 **Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) Quarterly Auction Calendar**

The Reserve Bank of India announces the calendar for the daily Repo, Reverse Repo and fortnightly Repo for the last quarter of the financial year under Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF). There is no change in the existing terms and conditions under which the LAF auctions are conducted.
