## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## October 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the "5.69% Government Stock, 2018" to be held on October 21, 2003 at Re.0.01 (1 paisa) per Rupees 100.
