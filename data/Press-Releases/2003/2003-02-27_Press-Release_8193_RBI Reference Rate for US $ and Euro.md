## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.4600 on February 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 26, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6900 and Rs.51.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
