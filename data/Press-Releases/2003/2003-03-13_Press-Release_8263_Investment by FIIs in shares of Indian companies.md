## Investment by FIIs in shares of Indian companies

## March 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on March 13, 2003 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Punjab Tractors Ltd., up to 64 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings to this effect.
