## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 3, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.4100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.0900 on April 3, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 31, 2003 ) were Rs.47.5000 and Rs.51.4400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
