## Investment by NRIs/PIOs in shares of Indian companies

## March 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on March 12, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Ltd. should be made on behalf of non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) without obtaining prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that the net purchases of equity shares of the company in the secondary markets have reached the limit of 8 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
