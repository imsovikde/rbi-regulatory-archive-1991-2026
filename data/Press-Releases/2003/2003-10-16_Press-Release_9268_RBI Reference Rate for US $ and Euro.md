## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 16, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6500 on October 16, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 15, 2003 ) were Rs.45.7100 and Rs.53.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
