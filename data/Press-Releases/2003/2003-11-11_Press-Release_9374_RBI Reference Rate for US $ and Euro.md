## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 11, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.9800 on November 11, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 10, 2003) were Rs.45.2700 and Rs.52.1700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
