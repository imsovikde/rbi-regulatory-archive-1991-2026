# Government not to pay interest on matured Relief Bonds

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Government not to pay interest on matured Relief Bonds

## April 26, 2003

The Government of India had notified that it will not pay any interest on the relief bonds that had matured but not encashed after February 28, 2003. The Reserve Bank of India has advised investors to present the matured relief bonds under the various Relief Bonds Schemes for encashment to the offices that had issued the relief bonds. This applies to 9 per cent Relief Bond 1987, 10 per cent Relief bond 1993, 9 per cent Relief Bond, 1993 and 10 per cent Relief Bond 1995. The Reserve Bank offices have also issued advices to all individual investors holding matured bonds for immediate encashment.
