## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 29, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.3700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.9800 on April 29, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 28, 2003) were Rs.47.3600 and Rs.52.4200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
