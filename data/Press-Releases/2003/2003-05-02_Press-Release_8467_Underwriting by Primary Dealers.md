## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## May 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers at Re.0.02 (2 paise) per Rupees 100 for both the price based auctions of "6.30% Government Stock, 2023" and "7.27% Government Stock, 2013" to be held on 3rd May 2003.
