## Auction of 91 day Treasury Bills-Reduction in the Notified Amount

## September 25, 2003

A reference is invited to our Press Release dated August 4, 2003 announcing enhancement in the notified amount for auctions of 91 day Treasury Bills from the Rs. 500 crore to Rs. 1,500 crore for eight auctions covering the period August 6, 2003 to September 24, 2003.

It has been decided to reduce the notified amount to Rs. 500 crore for the auctions of 91 Day Treasury Bills with effect from October 1, 2003.

The notified amount of 364 day Treasury Bills will remain unchanged.

The format for auction of 91 day Treasury Bills and 364 day Treasury Bills will continue to be multiple price auction format.

Accordingly, the revised Calendar for Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills from October 1, 2003 to March 31, 2004 is as under:

**Type of Periodicity Notified Amount Day of** Day of **of T-Bills (Rs. Crore) Auction** Payment 91-Day Weekly 500 Every Following Wednesday Friday 364-Day Fortnightly 1000 Wednesday Reporting Preceding the Friday Reporting Friday

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if emerging circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons such as intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)- W&M/97dated March 31, 1998 issued by Government of India.
