## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 10, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.6200 on December 10, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 9, 2003) were Rs.45.5400 and Rs.55.6900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
