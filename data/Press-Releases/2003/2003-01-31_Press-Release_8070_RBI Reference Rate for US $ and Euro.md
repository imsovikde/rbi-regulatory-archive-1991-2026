## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 31, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.8000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.6300 on January 31, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 30, 2003 ) were Rs.47.8200 and Rs.51.7900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
