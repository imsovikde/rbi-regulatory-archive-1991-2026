# Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on December 31, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Tata Motors Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in the primary/secondary markets without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Tata Motors Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
