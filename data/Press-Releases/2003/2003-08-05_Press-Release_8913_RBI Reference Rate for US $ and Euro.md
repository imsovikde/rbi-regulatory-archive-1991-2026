## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 5, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.1200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4100 on August 5, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 4, 2003 ) were Rs.46.1100 and Rs.51.9800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
