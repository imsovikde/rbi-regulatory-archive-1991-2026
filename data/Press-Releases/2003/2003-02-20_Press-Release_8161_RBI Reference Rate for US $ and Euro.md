## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.3500 on February 20, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 19, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7000 and Rs.51.1700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
