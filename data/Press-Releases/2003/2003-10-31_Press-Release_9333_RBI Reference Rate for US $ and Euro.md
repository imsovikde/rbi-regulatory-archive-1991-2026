## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 31, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6600 on October 31, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 30, 2003) were Rs.45.3200 and Rs.52.8600 respectively.
