## State Governments to raise funds from market on October 13

## October 3, 2003

All the State Governments except Manipur and Punjab will be raising market borrowings for an aggregate amount of about Rs.6400 crore as the second trance of their regular market borrowing programme on October 13, 2003. The exact amount of the loans, coupon rate and other details will be notified later.
