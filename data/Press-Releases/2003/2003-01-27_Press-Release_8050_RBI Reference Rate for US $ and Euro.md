## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.8900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.9000 on January 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 24, 2003 ) were Rs.47.8700 and Rs.51.5500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
