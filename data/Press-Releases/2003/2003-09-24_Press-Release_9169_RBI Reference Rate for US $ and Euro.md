## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4300 on September 24, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 23, 2003 ) were Rs.45.7500 and Rs.52.4900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|September 23, 2003|75.3891|40.86|
|September 24, 2003|75.5466|40.8300|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
