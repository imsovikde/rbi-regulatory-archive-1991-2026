## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 19, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.1700 on February 19, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 18, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7400 and Rs.51.2100 respectively.
