## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## October 31, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on October 31, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of Moser Baer Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Moser Baer Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
