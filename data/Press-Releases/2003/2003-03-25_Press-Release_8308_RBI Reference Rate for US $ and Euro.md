## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 25, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.9600 on March 25, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 24, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6600 and Rs.50.6100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
