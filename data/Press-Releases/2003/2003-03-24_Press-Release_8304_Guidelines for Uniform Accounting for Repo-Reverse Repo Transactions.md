# Guidelines for Uniform Accounting for Repo/Reverse Repo Transactions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## Guidelines for Uniform Accounting for Repo/Reverse Repo Transactions

The Reserve Bank of India has, in consultation with Fixed Income Money Markets and Derivatives Association of India (FIMMDA), issued guidelines on uniform accounting policies to be followed by banks, primary dealers and other entities regulated by the Reserve Bank. In the accounting guidelines, the legal character of the repo transaction under the current law, viz., as outright purchase and outright sale transactions, has been kept intact while reflecting the substance of repo as a funding transaction. The Reserve Bank has, however, clarified that, for the present, these norms would not apply to repo/reverse repo transactions under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) with the Reserve Bank.

The Reserve Bank has also requested other regulatory agencies, viz., Security and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDA) and National Housing Bank (NHB) to advise entities regulated by them to follow similar accounting policies. The Reserve Bank has also advised the regulated entities to consider entering into bilateral Master Repo Agreement as per the documentation finalised by FIMMDA to obviate disputes arising out of repo transactions.

Currently there are divergent practices prevailing among the market participants for accounting of repo/reverse repo transactions. In order to ensure uniform accounting treatment in this regard and to impart transparency so as to widen and broaden the repo market, it was decided to lay down uniform accounting principles for repo/reverse repo transactions undertaken by all the regulated entities.

A copy of the circular issued in this regard is available in the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>).
