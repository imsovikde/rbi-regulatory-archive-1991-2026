## RBI releases LAF Report and Sterilisation Paper For Public Comment

## December 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India today placed the executive summary of the report of the Internal Group on Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) on its website. The Reserve Bank has also placed the summary of the report of the Working Group on Instruments of Sterilisation on its website.

It may be recalled that in the Mid term Review of the Monetary and Credit Policy for 2003-2004 released on November 3, 2003, it was indicated that the report of the Internal Group on Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) would be placed on the RBI website for wider dissemination and comments.

The Reserve Bank has stated that comments on the report of the Internal Group on Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) may be sent to the Principal Monetary Policy Adviser, th Monetary policy Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 24 Floor, Central Office Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 by post or by email to helpmpd@rbi.org.in by December 31, 2003. Comments on the Report on Instruments of Sterilisation may be sent to Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Internal Debt Management th Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 16 Floor, Central Office Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort, Mumbai 400 001 by post or by email to cgmidmc@rbi.org.in also by December 31, 2003.
