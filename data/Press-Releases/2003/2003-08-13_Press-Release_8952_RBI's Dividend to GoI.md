## RBI's Dividend to GoI

## August 13, 2003

In response to query, the RBI spokesperson confirmed that the Reserve Bank Board has, at its meeting held today, decided to transfer a dividend of Rs.8,834 crore to the Government of India out of its surplus profit for the year ended June 30, 2003.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
