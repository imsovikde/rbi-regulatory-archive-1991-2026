## State Development Loans get Overwhelming Response

## July 30, 2003

The sale `on tap' of 6.20 per cent ten year State Development Loans, 2013 in respect of 28 State Governments received overwhelming response of about Rs.18,500 crore. The issue opened for subscription on July 30, 2003 for a target amount of Rs.8000 crore.

The tap sale has been closed today. Excess subscriptions are being refunded to the applicants after making partial allotment in respect of states whose loans are oversubscribed.

## P. V. Sadanandan
