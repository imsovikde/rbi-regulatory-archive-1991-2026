## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.6400 on November 24, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 21, 2003) were Rs.45.8400 and Rs.54.5200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
