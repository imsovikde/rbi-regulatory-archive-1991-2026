# GoI to sell three dated securities through price based auctions; Increases notified amount by Rs. 4000 crore for next auction

## GoI to sell three dated securities through price based auctions;

## Increases notified amount by Rs. 4000 crore for next auction

## June 26, 2003

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.37 per cent Government Stock 2014” for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal), “6.05 per cent Government Stock 2019” for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) and “6.13 per cent Government Stock 2028” for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal). The Government Stocks will be sold through <u>price-based auctions</u> using <u>multiple price method</u>. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on Tuesday, July 1, 2003.

Up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale of **all the three stocks** will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, July 1, 2003. Competitive bids by the members of the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS, while all the non- competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 p.m</u>. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank will determine the minimum price up to which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auctions will be announced on July 1, 2003 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on July 2, 2003 (Wednesday). All the three stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the Primary Dealers up to 12.30 p.m. on Monday, June 30, 2003 to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

It may be recalled that as per the calendar for issuance of Government securities the notified amounts and the tenure of the securities for the auction scheduled during July 1-7, 2003 were Rs. 5,000 crore for a security with 10 year to 14 years tenure and Rs. 4,000 crore for a security with 15 year to 19 year tenure. Taking into account all relevant factors, the Government of India has, however, decided to sale (re-issue) a 25-year security for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore in addition to the securities notified in the calendar.

The Government of India has also decided to increase the notified amount for the auctions scheduled during July 14-21, 2003 by Rs. 4,000 crore to Rs. 9,000 crore as against Rs. 5,000 crore announced earlier. The details in this regard would be announced later.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
