## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.8700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.5500 on January 24, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 23, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9100 and Rs.51.3800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
