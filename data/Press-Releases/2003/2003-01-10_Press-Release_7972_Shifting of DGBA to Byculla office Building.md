## Shifting of DGBA to Byculla office Building

## January 10, 2003

The Department of Government and Bank Accounts housed at 1st, 2nd and 3rd floor of C-7, Reserve Bank of India, Bandra-Kurla Complex (Administration Division, Government Accounts Division and Central Debt Division) will be shifting to 4th floor of Byculla Office Building as at close of business of January 11, 2003. It is therefore requested that in future all communications meant for DGBA in respect of above divisions may be kindly addressed to:

The Chief General Manager Department of Government & Bank Accounts Reserve Bank of India Central Office 4th Floor, Byculla Office Building Opp. Mumbai Central Station Byculla, Mumbai-400008. New Fax Number :
