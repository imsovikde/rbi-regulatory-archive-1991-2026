## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.4900 on November 20, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 19, 2003) were Rs.45.5400 and Rs.54.4700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
