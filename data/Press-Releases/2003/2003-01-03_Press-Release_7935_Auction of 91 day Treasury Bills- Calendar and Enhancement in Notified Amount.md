## Auction of 91 day Treasury Bills: Calendar and Enhancement in Notified Amount

## January 3, 2003

It has been decided to continue the enhanced notified amount for auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills at Rs.1000 crore in the next four auctions of these bills starting from January 8, 2003 to January 29, 2003. These four auctions would also be conducted by using multiple price auction format. The position will be reviewed thereafter.

The Reserve Bank had, on December 6, 2002, announced enhancement in the notified amount for the auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills. The amount was raised from Rs.250 crore to Rs.1000 crore for four auctions conducted during the period from December 11, 2002 to January 1, 2003**.** While announcing the enhancement in the amount, the Reserve Bank had said that it would review the position after the four auctions.

The experience has been reviewed and it has been decided to keep the auction amount for these bills at Rs. 1000 crore.

The notified amount and the present format of auction of 364 day Treasury Bills will remain unchanged.

## Reporting Friday

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is, thus, subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons, such as, intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)- W&M/97dated March 31, 1998 issued by the Government of India.
