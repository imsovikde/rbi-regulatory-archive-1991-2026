## TAP sale of 6.75 per cent State Development Loans, 2013 closed

## March 17, 2003

The sale on tap of 6.75 per cent State Loans, 2013 in respect of three States viz., Assam, Rajasthan and West Bengal has been closed on March 17, 2003.
