## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 7, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.1200 on January 7, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 6, 2003) were Rs.48.0000 and Rs.50.1100 respectively.
