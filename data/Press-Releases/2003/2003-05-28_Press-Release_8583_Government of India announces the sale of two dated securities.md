## Government of India announces the sale of two dated securities

## May 28, 2003

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of “6.85 per cent Government Stock 2012” for a notified amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal) through a <u>price based auction</u> using <u>multiple price method</u>. Government of India have also announced the sale of a new 25 year Stock for a notified amount of Rs 3,000 crore (nominal) through a <u>yield based auction</u> using <u>multiple price method</u>. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on June 3, 2003 (Tuesday).

Up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale of **both the Stocks** will be allotted to eligible individuals and Institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, June 3, 2003. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. The non-competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form and deposited in the appropriate tender box at RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>.

The results of the auction will be announced on June 3, 2003 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on June 4, 2003 (Wednesday). Both the stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ by 12.30 p.m. on June 2, 2003 (Monday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
