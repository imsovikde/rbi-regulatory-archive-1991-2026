## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.2900 on October 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 24, 2003 ) were Rs.45.3300 and Rs.53.5100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
