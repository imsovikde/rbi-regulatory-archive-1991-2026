## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## July 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.0600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.9000 on July 14, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 11, 2003) were Rs.46.1400 and Rs.52.2800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|July 11, 2003|75.3697|39.14|
|July 14, 2003|74.9903|39.1200|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
