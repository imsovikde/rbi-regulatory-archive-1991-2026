## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.2700 on January 2, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 1, 2003) were Rs.47.9900 and Rs.50.3100 respectively.
