## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 18, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.4800 on August 18, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 14, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9100 and Rs.51.9700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
