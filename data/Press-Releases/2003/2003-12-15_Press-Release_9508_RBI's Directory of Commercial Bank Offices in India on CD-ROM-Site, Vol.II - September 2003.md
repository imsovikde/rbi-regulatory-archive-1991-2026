## RBI's Directory of Commercial Bank Offices in India on CD-ROM/Site, Vol.II-September 2003

## December 15, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India's Department of Statistical Analysis and Computer Services has brought out the Directory of Commercial Bank Offices in India. The Directory is available on CD-ROM as well as on the RBI Website. Based on latest available information on bank branches, the Directory contains information on various functional and locational details of 68,723 branches/offices of 295 commercial banks as on September 30, 2003. The Directory can be accessed through geographical region, state, district, centre; as well as through bank group/bank. It also provides details of branches/offices of banks functioning in a particular locality within a centre authorised dealers category-wise and type of business activity-wise details of branches of banks also can be accessed. The price of the CD is Rs.200 each in India and US$ 40 abroad. Prices are inclusive of postage. Drafts/cheques/pay-orders should be drawn in favour of "Reserve Bank of India", payable in Mumbai. CDs are available for sale from the Sale Section, Division of Reports, Review and Publications, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy, Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, Ground Floor, P. M. Road, Fort, Mumbai-400001.
