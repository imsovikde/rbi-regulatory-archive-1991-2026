## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.3400 on February 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 11, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7100 and Rs.51.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
