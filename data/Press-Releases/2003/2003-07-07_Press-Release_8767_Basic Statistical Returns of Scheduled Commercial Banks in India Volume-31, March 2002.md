## Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India Volume-31, March

## July 7, 2003

The Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India-Volume 31, March 2002 is available for sale. The Department of Statistical Analysis and Computer Services of Reserve Bank of India brings out this publication annually. The Volume, thirty- first in the series, presents comprehensive data on Number of Offices, Employees, Deposits and Credit based on Basic Statistical Returns in respect of the offices of the scheduled commercial banks in India as at the end of March 2002. The Volume is divided into five different Sections disseminating credit and deposit data of scheduled commercial banks with reference to various classificatory characteristics. Section 1 presents general information on commercial banking as also the summary data on deposits and credit at all-India and State level. Section 2 gives State/District-wise distribution of deposits and outstanding credit classified according to population groups and bank groups. Data on deposits by type of deposits is presented in Section 3. Classification of outstanding credit according to different categories such as size of credit limit, rate of interest, type of organisation, type of account, bank group, State and population group, etc. are given in Section 4. In Section 5, these are further classified according to occupation of borrower. District and occupation-wise distribution of outstanding credit is also given in the final section.

The inland price of this publication is Rs. 225/- (inclusive of postage) and for overseas buyers the price is US $70 (inclusive of airmail charges).

The publication is also available on CD at inland price of Rs. 225/- (inclusive of postage) and US $70 (inclusive of postage) for overseas buyers. Index/key word search is provided in the CD for easy navigation. The tables are in MS Excel formats, which can be easily downloaded/copied.
