## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 26, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.7700 on August 26, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 25, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8600 and Rs.49.8800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
