## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 18, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.2100 on February 18, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 17, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7600 and Rs.51.2000 respectively.
