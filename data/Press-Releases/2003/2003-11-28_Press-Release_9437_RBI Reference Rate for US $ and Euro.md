## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 28, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.8000 on November 28, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 27, 2003) were Rs.45.8900 and Rs.54.7300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
