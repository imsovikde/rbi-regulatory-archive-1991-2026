## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.7700 on December 2, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 1, 2003) were Rs.45.7800 and Rs.54.8200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
