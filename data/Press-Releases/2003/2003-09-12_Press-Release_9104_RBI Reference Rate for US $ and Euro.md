## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.1800 on September 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 11, 2003 ) were Rs.45.7200 and Rs.51.4400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
