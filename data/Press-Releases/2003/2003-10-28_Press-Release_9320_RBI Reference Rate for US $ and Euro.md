## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 28 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1700 on October 28, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 27, 2003) were Rs.45.3400 and Rs.53.2900 respectively.
