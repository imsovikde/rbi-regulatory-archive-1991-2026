## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 8, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.0100 on January 8, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 7, 2003) were Rs.47.9900 and Rs.50.1200 respectively.
