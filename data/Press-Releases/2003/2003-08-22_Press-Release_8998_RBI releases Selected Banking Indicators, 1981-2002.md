## RBI releases Selected Banking Indicators, 1981-2002

## August 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has released Selected Banking Indicators - 1981-2002, both in the form of a book and CD-Rom. The publication presents data on selected important banking indicators, such as deposits, credit and investments of all scheduled banks on a fortnightly basis from 1981 to 2002. The data are presented for six bank groups, viz., State Bank of India and its Associates, Nationalised Banks, Foreign Banks operating in India, Regional Rural Banks, other Indian Scheduled Commercial Banks and Co-operative banks. These data are primarily compiled from the statutory returns submitted by scheduled banks under Section 42(2) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. In addition, data on monthly excess reserves maintained by all scheduled commercial banks (excluding RRBs) with the Reserve Bank over and above the statutory minimum balance for the purpose of cash reserve ratio (CRR) are also presented.

The hard copy of the publication is priced at Rs.320 (Rs.460 by post) and US$ 75 (by airmail). The soft copy of the publication is priced at Rs.250 (Rs.300 by post) and US$ 75 (by airmail).
