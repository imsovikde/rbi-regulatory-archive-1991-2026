## Government of India announces issue of Floating Rate Bonds, 2012

## November 5, 2003

The Government of India have announced sale of "Floating Rate Bonds, 2012" for an aggregate amount of Rs 5,000 crore (nominal) byauction using <u>uniform price format</u>.

Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of the bonds will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on **Friday, November 7, 2003**. Applicants who wish to bid for the bonds will have to clearly indicate in the prescribed form (obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai) the nominal value of the bonds applied for, and the 'spread’ on the base rate' up to two decimal places expected by them. Bids in prescribed form should be deposited at RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai, on November 7, 2003 in the appropriate tender box. However, the competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. The non-competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form and deposited in the appropriate tender box at RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai. All bids should be submitted on Friday, November 7, 2003 <u>by 12.30 pm</u>. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum spread for the Floating Rate Bonds at which offers will be accepted. The auction will be conducted by using the Uniform Price Method, where bids offered up to and including the maximum spread as determined by Reserve Bank of India will be accepted. Bids quoted at spreads higher than the spread determined by the Reserve Bank of India will be rejected.

The result of the auction will be announced on November 7, 2003 and payment by successful bidders will be during the banking hours on **Monday, November 10, 2003**. The bonds will be repaid at par on November 10, 2012.

Bids for underwriting of the Bonds under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market’ by 12.30 p.m. on **Thursday, November 6,** **2003** to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

The Floating Rate Bonds will be of 9-year tenure and will be issued at par. (i.e., at Rs100.00 per cent). The bonds will carry an interest rate, which is calculated by adding a **'spread'** to a **variable base rate**. The 'spread' **will be decided in the auction** to be conducted by Reserve Bank of India and will remain unchanged during the currency of the bonds. The variable base rate will be the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices emerging in the immediate previous three auctions of ‘Government of India 364 day Treasury Bills’ held prior to the relative annual coupon reset period. The interest will be paid every half year. The <u>base rate</u> for calculation of interest for the first coupon period commencing from November 10, 2003 to November 9, 2004 as per the aforesaid formula is 4.53 per cent (please see enclosed illustration). The base rate for the subsequent annual coupon periods, worked out as per the aforesaid formula, will be announced by RBI on or before the commencement of the respective coupon period.

There will be no floor or cap on the interest rate on the Floating Rate Bonds.

In the unlikely event of 364-day Treasury Bill auctions being discontinued during the currency of the FRB, the applicable variable base rate will be the average of the Yield to Maturity (YTM) rate prevailing for one year Government of India Security as on last three reporting Fridays prior to the commencement of the relative annual coupon period worked

out in consultation with the Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association of India (FIMMDA). In case particular Friday/s is/are holiday/s, the yield to maturity rates on the previous working day shall be taken. This rate will be announced by RBI on or before the commencement of the relative annual coupon period.

The Floating Rate Bonds are being issued by the Government of India, on auction basis for the sixth time. The bonds provide hedge against interest rate risk, offering returns linked to short term yield.

For any further clarifications, interested investors may approach Chief General Manager-in- Charge, Reserve Bank of India, IDM Department, Central Office, Mumbai.
