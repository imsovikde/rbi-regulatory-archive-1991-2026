## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 30, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8600 on October 30, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 29, 2003) were Rs.45.3100 and Rs.52.9900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|October 29, 2003|77.0927|41.96|
|October 30, 2003|77.0327|41.9200|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
