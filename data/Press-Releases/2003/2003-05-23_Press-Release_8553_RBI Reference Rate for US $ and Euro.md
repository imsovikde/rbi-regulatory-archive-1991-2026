## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 23, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.8100 on May 23, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 22, 2003) were Rs.46.9100 and Rs.54.5800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|May 22, 2003|76.5853|39.8700|
|May 23, 2003|76.6178|40.0100|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR- Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
