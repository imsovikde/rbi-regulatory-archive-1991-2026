## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.9700 on August 14, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 13, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9200 and Rs.51.6400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
