## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 3, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1600 on October 3, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 1, 2003 ) were Rs.45.6800 and Rs.53.3500 respectively.
