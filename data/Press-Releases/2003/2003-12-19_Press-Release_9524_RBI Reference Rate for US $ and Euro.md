## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 19, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.5400 on December 19, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 18, 2003) were Rs.45.5200 and Rs.56.4500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

## Currency

|Date|1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 18, 2003|80.4384|42.31|
|December 19, 2003|80.6296|42.2800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
