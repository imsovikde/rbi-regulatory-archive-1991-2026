## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 9, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.6900 on December 9, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 8, 2003) were Rs.45.5600 and Rs.55.4100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
