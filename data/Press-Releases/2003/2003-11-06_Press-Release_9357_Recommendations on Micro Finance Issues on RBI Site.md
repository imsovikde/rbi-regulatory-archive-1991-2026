## <u>Recommendations on Micro Finance Issues on RBI Site</u>

## November 6, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has placed on its website, the recommendations of the informal groups on micro finance which studied issues relating to (i) Structure and Sustainability; (ii) Funding; (iii) Regulations; and (iv) Capacity Building for greater public debate.

Comments may please be sent to Chief General Manager, Rural Planning and Credit Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Mumbai 400 001 (fax: 022-22658276 / email: <u>cgmincrpcd@rbi.org.in</u>).

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank had in October 2002 constituted four informal groups to further stimulate the process of constructive dialogue amongst the major players in the area of micro finance. The four groups studied issues relating to (i) Structure and Sustainability; (ii) Funding; (iii) Regulations; and (iv) Capacity Building. Shri Vepa Kamesam, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India on August 6, 2003 chaired a high level meeting to discuss the recommendations of the groups. It was decided at that meeting to circulate the recommendations of the groups for greater public debate.
