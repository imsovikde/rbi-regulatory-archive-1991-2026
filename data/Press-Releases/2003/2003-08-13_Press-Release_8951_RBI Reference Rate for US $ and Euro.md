## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.6400 on August 13, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 12, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9300 and Rs.52.2000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
