## States to Sell 6.35 per cent 2013 Loans for Rs.7000 crore on June 12

## June 4, 2003

All the State Governments have offered to sell on tap 6.35 per cent State Development Loan, 2013 having maturity of ten years for an aggregate target amount of Rs.7000 crore on account of debt swap scheme. The sale will be held at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all the 28 States) on June 12, 2003. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the banking hours on or after June 12, 2003 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received.

Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Office at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the aforesaid Offices during banking hours while the sale is open.

It may be recalled that the market participants were informed on May 28, 2003 that the State Governments would be accessing the market on June 12, 2003 as the first tranche on account of debt-swap scheme for the fiscal 2003-04.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
