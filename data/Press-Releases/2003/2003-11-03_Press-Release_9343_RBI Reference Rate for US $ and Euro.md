## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 3, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4700 on November 3, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 31, 2003) were Rs.45.3200 and Rs.52.6600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
