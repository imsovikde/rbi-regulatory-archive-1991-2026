## RBI releases the Report of the Working Group on instruments of Sterilization for public comments

## December 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India today placed the full text of the Report of the Working Group on Instruments of Sterilisation on its website.

It may be recalled that a summary of the Report was placed on the Bank's website on December 2, 2003 for wider dissemination and comments. Comments on the Report on Instruments of Sterlisation may be sent to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 16th Floor, Central Office Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 by post or by e-mail to cgmidmc@rbi.org.in by December 31, 2003.

## P. V. Sadanandan
