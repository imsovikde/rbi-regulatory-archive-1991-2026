## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 3, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.7600 on January 3, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 2, 2003) were Rs.48.0100 and Rs.50.2700 respectively.
