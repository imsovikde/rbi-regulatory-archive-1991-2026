## Two New Executive Directors appointed in RBI

## June 12, 2003

Shri N. H. Siddiqui, Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Bhubaneshwar has been appointed as Executive Director of Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation. Shri A.

V. Sardesai, Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Rural Planning and Credit Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai has been appointed as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India. Both appointments are effective from June 12, 2003.
