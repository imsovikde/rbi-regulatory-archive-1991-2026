## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4100 on November 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 11, 2003) were Rs.45.2900 and Rs.51.9800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

## Currency

|Date|1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 11, 2003|75.5800|41.52|
|November 12, 2003|75.7275|41.6800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
