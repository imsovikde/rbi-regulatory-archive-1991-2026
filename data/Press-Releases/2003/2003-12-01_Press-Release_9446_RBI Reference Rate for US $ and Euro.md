## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 1, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.8200 on December 1, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 28, 2003) were Rs.45.9400 and Rs.54.8000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

## Currency

|Date|1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 28, 2003|78.7825|42.02|
|December 01, 2003|78.8332|41.8500|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
