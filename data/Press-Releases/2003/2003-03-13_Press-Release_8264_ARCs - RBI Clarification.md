## ARCs-RBI Clarification

## March 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has clarified that the last date of March 20, 2003 for asset reconstruction companies (ARCs) to make an application to the Reserve Bank for registration is applicable only to companies existing as on June 21, 2002. This is in terms of the second proviso to paragraph 3(1) of the Securitisation and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcement of Security Interest Act, 2002. This deadline is not applicable to companies incorporated after that date.
