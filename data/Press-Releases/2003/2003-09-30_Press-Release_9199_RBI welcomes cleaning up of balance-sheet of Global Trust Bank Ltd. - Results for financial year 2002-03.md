**RBI welcomes cleaning up of balance-sheet of Global Trust Bank Ltd. - Results for financial year 2002-03**

## September 30, 2003

The just announced audited financial results of Global Trust Bank Ltd., for the year ended 2002-03 indicate that the present management had made special efforts in recovery of non-performing assets relating to the previous years and have also made necessary provisions in accordance with Reserve Bank guidelines. The Reserve Bank of India has noted that even though the financial statements show an overall loss, the bank has made an operating profit for the year 2002-03.

The Reserve Bank of India welcomes the decision taken by the Global Trust Bank Ltd., and its Board of Directors to clean up the balance sheet. The cleaning up of the balance sheet is essential for sound functioning of the bank and for the good health of the financial system.
