## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors -

## Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## October 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on October 24, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of Television Eighteen India Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Television Eighteen India Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
