## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 26, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.2900 on February 26, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 25, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7600 and Rs.51.5700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
