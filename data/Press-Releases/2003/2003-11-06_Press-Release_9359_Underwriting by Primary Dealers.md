# Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## November 6, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the "GOI Floating Rate Bonds, 2012" to be held on October 21, 2003 at Re.0.01 (1 paisa) per Rupees 100.
