## Auction of 91 day Treasury Bills-Enhancement of the Notified Amount

## August 4, 2003

In view of the prevailing liquidity conditions, it has been decided to enhance the notified amount in the auctions of 91 day Treasury Bills from the present Rs.500 crore to Rs.1,500 crore for eight auctions starting from August 6, 2003 to September 24, 2003. The position will be reviewed thereafter.

The notified amount of 364 day Treasury Bills will remain unchanged.

The format for auction of 91 day Treasury Bills and 364 day Treasury Bills will continue to be multiple price auction format.

Accordingly, the revised Calendar for Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills from August 6, 2003 to September 24, 2003 is as under:

|Type of T-Bills|Periodicity|Notified Amount (Rs.Crore)|Day of Auction|Day of Payment|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|91-Day|Weekly|1,500|Every Wednesday|Following Friday|
|364-Day|Fortnightly|1000|Wednesday Preceding the Reporting Friday|Reporting Friday|

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if emerging circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons such as intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F 2(12)-W&M/97 dated March 31, 1998 issued by Government of India.

## P.V.Sadanandan Manager
