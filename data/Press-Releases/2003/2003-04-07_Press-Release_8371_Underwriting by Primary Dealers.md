## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## April 7, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate at Re.0.01 (1 paisa) for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the price based auction of `7.37% Government Stock, 2014' as well as for the yield based auction of 20-year Government Stock, both to be held on April 8, 2003.
