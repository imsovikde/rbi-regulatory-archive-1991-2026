## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## July 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on July 22, 2003 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Satyam Computer Services Ltd., up to 60 per cent of its paid up capital, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting.
