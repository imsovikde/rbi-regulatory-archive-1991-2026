## State Government Loans on Tap to be sold on May 12

## May 6, 2003

All the State Governments except Chhattisgarh have offered to sell on tap 6.40 per cent State Development Loan, 2013 having maturity of ten years for an aggregate target amount of Rs.6747 crore, with an option to retain excess subscription up to 30 per cent. The sale will be held at the respective Regional offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all the 27 States) on May 12, 2003. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the business hours on or after May 12, 2003 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received.

Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Office at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the aforesaid offices during business hours while the sale is open.

It may be recalled that the market participants were informed on April 30, 2003 that the State Governments would be accessing the market on May 12, 2003 for part of their borrowing requirements for the fiscal 2003-04.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
