## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 18, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6200 on November 18, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 17, 2003) were Rs.45.4100 and Rs.53.5000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
