## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 21, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.5800 on March 21, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 20, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7900 and Rs.50.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
