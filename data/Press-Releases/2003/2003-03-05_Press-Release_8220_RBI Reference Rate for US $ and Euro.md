## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 5, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.2400 on March 5, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 4, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6500 and Rs.52.0400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
