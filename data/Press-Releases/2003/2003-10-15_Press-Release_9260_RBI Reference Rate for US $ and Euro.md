## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 15, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6200 on October 15, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 14, 2003 ) were Rs.45.5900 and Rs.52.8400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
