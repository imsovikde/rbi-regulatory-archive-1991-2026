## On Tap Sale of State Government Loans on February 25, 2003

## February 20, 2003

The first tranche of the market borrowings for Rs.7,325 crore will be on February 25,

2003. The ten-year securities will carry a coupon rate of 6.95 per cent. In case of oversubscription, the States will have the option to retain an additional amount up to 25 per cent of the target amount. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the business hours on or after February 25, 2003 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. The sale for all the 27 States will be held at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office at the Reserve Bank of India and will be received at the concerned Regional Offices during office hours while the sale is open. Market participants were informed on February 19, 2003 that the State Governments would be accessing the market for additional borrowings for an amount of Rs.14,151 crore in two tranches in the current financial year. The amount includes Rs.10,000 crore for the debt swap scheme mutually agreed between the Central Government and the State governments towards repayment of high cost debt owed by the States to the Centre.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
