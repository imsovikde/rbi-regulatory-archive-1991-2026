## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 8, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.0300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.3100 on August 8, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 7, 2003 ) were Rs.46.0200 and Rs.52.1000 respectively.
