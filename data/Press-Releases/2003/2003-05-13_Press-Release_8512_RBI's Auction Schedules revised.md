## RBI's Auction Schedules revised

## May 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India today revised the schedule for various auctions it would conduct today and tomorrow. As per the revised schedule:

(i) Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) auction on May 14, 2003 will be of five days; (ii) The payments for successful bids in the 91/364 day treasury bill auctions will be on Saturday, May 17, 2003. Earlier today, the Reserve Bank conducted the LAF auction of one-day after extending
the bidding time to 11.30 am from 10.30 am. It also conducted the 91/364 day treasury bill auctions today as announced earlier but extended the bid submission time up to 12.30 pm from

11.30 am. The auction schedules were revised following the Government of Maharashtra shifting the holiday to May 15, 2003 from May 14, 2003. The Government of Maharashtra declared May 15, 2003 to be holiday under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.
