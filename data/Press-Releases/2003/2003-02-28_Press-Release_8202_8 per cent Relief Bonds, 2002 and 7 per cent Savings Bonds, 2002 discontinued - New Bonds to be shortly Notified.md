# 8 per cent Relief Bonds, 2002 and 7 per cent Savings Bonds, 2002 discontinued : New Bonds to be shortly Notified

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## 8 per cent Relief Bonds, 2002 and 7 per cent Savings Bonds, 2002 discontinued :

## New Bonds to be shortly Notified

## February 28, 2003

As notified today by the Government of India, 8 per cent Relief Bonds, 2002 will cease with effect from the close of business on February 28, 2003.

Similarly, the issue of 7 per cent Savings Bonds, 2002 will also cease with effect from the close of business on February 28, 2003.

The issue of new bonds with revised coupon rates etc., will be notified by the Government in due course.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
