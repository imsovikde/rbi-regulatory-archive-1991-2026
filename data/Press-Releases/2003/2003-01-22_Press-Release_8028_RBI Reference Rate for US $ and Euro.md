## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.4100 on January 22, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 21, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9500 and Rs.51.0500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
