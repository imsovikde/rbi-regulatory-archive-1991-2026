## Two New Executive Directors appointed in RBI

## March 3, 2003

Shri Y.S.P. Thorat, Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai and Shri P.K. Biswas, Principal, Reserve Bank Staff College, Chennai have been appointed as Executive Directors of the Reserve Bank with effect from March 1, 2003.
