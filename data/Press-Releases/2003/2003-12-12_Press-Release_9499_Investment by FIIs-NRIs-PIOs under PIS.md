## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

## December 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 12, 2003 that the holdings of foreign institutional investors (FIIs)/non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the paid up capital of Bharti Tele Ventures Ltd., have gone below 48.5 per cent. The Reserve Bank has further notified that it will be in order for FIIs/NRIs and PIOs to purchase the shares of the company in the secondary market under the portfolio investment scheme subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines in this regard.
