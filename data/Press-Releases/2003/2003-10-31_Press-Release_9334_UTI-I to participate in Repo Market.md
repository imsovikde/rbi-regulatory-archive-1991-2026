## UTI-I to participate in Repo Market

## October 31, 2003

On application from the Administrator of the Specified Undertaking of the Unit Trust of India (UTI-I), the Reserve Bank of India has decided to permit them to participate in the Government Securities Repo market with immediate effect, in accordance with the guidelines issued in this behalf.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
