## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 4, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.8800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.9200 on June 4, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 3, 2003) were Rs.47.0300 and Rs.55.3000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 3, 2003|77.0281|39.71|
|June 4, 2003|76.3863|39.5000|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
