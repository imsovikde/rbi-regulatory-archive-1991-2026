## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## August 19, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on August 13, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of M/s. Mphasis BFL Software Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s. Mphasis BFL Software Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
