## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 17, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.2000 on February 17, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 14, 2003 ) were Rs.47.8600 and Rs.51.8800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
