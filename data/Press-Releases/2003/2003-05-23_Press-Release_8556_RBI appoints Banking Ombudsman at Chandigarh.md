# RBI appoints Banking Ombudsman at Chandigarh

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RBI appoints Banking Ombudsman at Chandigarh** **May 23, 2003** The Reserve Bank of India has appointed Shri S. Govindarajan as the Banking Ombudsman for the states of Himachal Pradesh, Punjab and Union Territory of Chandigarh with effect from May 22, 2003. As Banking Ombudsman, Shri Govindarajan will have his office at the Reserve Bank of India Building, New Office Building, Sector 17, Central Vista, Chandigarh-160017. The Banking Ombudsman Scheme was introduced in June 1995 with a view to providing to the bank customers quick and low-cost redressal of grievances relating to banking services. Its scope of authority and functions include redressal of grievances against deficiency in banking services, concerning loans and advances and other specified matters and arbitration for specified disputes. All commercial banks, regional rural banks and scheduled primary co-operative banks are covered under the Banking Ombudsman Scheme.
