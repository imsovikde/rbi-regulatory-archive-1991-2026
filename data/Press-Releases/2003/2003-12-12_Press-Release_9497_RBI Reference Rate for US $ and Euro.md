## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.6200 on December 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 11, 2003) were Rs.45.5400 and Rs.55.5100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
