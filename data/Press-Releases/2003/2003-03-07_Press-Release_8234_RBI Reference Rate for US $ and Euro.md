## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 7, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4100 on March 7, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 6, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6400 and Rs.52.2400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
