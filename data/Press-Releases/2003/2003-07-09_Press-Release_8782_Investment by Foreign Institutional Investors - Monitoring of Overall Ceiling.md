## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## July 9, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on July 9, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of shares of M/s. Satyam Computer Services Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s. Satyam Computer Services Ltd., has reached the overall ceiling of 49 per cent of its paid up capital.
