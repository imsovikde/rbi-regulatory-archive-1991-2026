## Bank Rate

## April 29, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has reduced the Bank Rate by one quarter of one percentage point i.e. from 6.25 per cent per annum to 6.0 per cent per annum with effect from the close of business today, April 29, 2003.

## P. V. Sadanandan

## Manager
