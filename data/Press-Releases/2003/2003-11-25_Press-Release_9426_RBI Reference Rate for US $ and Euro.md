## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 25, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1800 on November 25, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 24, 2003) were Rs.45.8700 and Rs.54.6400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
