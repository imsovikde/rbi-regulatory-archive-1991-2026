## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 30, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.3500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.7100 on April 30, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 29, 2003) were Rs.47.3700 and Rs.51.9800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
