## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.9800 on January 20, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 17, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9400 and Rs.50.9000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
