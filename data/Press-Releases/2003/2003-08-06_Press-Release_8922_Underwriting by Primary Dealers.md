## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## August 6, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the new "25 year Government Stock" and "Floating rate Bond 2011" to be held on August 7, 2003 at Re.0.01 (1 paisa) and at Re.0.02 (2 Paise) per Rupees 100, respectively.
