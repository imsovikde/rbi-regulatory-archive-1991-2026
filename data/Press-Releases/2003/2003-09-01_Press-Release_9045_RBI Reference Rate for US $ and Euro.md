## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 1, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.4400 on September 1, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 29, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8500 and Rs.49.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
