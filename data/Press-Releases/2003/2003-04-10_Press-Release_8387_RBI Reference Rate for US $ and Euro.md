## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 10, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.4100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.1500 on April 10, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 9, 2003) were Rs.47.4600 and Rs.51.0000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
