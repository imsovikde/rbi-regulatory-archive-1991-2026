## ICICI Securities Limited

## May 9, 2003

Consequent upon change in the name of M/s. ICICI Securities and Finance Company Ltd., to `ICICI Securities Limited', the authorisation granted to the company to act as a Primary Dealer in Government Securities Market, will be valid under its new name, i.e. **ICICI Securities Limited.**

## P. V. Sadanandan
