## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 21, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.5200 on November 21, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 20, 2003) were Rs.45.7100 and Rs.54.4900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
