## December 26, 2003

## RBI's Department of Government and Bank Accounts in Byculla

The Division of Bank Accounts of the Department of Government and Bank Accounts has shifted to Byculla Office. The Division has started functioning from the new premises with effect from December 22, 2003. The address for communication with the Division is:

The Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department of Government and Bank Accounts, Reserve Banak of India, th Central Office, 4 Floor, Byculla Office Building, Opp. Mumbai Central Station, Mumbai 400 008

Fax No: 2300 8764 / 2301 6072 / Telephone No: 2301 6214 / 2301 8040

The Division was earlier situated at the Main Building (2nd Floor) of the Main Office, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Mumbai 400 001
