## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## July 14, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the price based auction of 7.95% Government Stock, 2032 and 6.35% Government Stock, 2020" to be held on July 15, 2003 at Re.0.02 (2 paise) and at Re.0.015 (1.5 Paise) per Rupees 100, respectively.
