## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.3600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1800 on May 2, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 30, 2003 ) were Rs.47.3500 and Rs.52.7100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
