## GoI announces the sale (re-issue) of two dated securities through price based auctions

## July 8, 2003

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of “6.35 percent Government Stock 2020” for a notified amount of Rs. 6,000 crore and of “7.95 percent Government Stock 2032” for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore. Both the securities have been offered for sale through <u>price-based auction using multiple price auction method</u>. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) will conduct the auctions at Mumbai on Tuesday, July 15, 2003.

Up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale of both the stocks will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, July 15, 2003. The competitive bids by the members of the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS, while all the non-competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 p.m</u>. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank will determine the minimum price up to which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auctions will be announced on July 15, 2003 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on July 16, 2003 (Wednesday). Both the stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 p.m. on July 14, 2003 (Monday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

It may be recalled that the Government had, through its Press Release dated June 26, 2003 had announced that the aggregated notified amount for the auctions scheduled during July 14-21, 2003 would be Rs. 9,000 crore instead of Rs. 5,000 crore announced in the calendar.
