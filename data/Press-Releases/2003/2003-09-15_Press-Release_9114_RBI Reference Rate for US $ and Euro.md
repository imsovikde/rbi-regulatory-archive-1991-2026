## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 15, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.5100 on September 15, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 12, 2003 ) were Rs.45.7800 and Rs.51.1800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|September 12, 2003|72.9962|39.11|
|September 15, 2003|73.4746|39.0000|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
