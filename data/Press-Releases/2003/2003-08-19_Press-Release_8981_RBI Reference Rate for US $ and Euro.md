## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 19, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.9700 on August 19, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 18, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8600 and Rs.51.4800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
