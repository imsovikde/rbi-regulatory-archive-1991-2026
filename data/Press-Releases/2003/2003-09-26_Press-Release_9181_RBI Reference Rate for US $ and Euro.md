## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 26, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6300 on September 26, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 25, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8200 and Rs.52.6800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
