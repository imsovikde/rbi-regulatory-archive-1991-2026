## RBI resets rate of interest on FRBs 2009 at 5.16 per cent

## June 6, 2003

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2009 (FRB, 2009) applicable for the half year June 6, 2003-December 5, 2003 shall be 5.16 per cent per annum.

As decided in the auction of FRB, 2009 held on December 5, 2001, the rate of interest on the FRB, 2009 was set at a mark-up over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest was to the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of the Government of India's 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half- yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut- off prices of the last six auctions of the Government of India's 364-day Treasury Bills held up to June 5, 2003 works out to 5.17 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on December 5, 2001 was (-) 0.01 (minus 0.01). The coupon rate has thus been fixed at 5.16 per cent.
