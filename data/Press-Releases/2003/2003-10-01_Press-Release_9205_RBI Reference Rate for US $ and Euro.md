## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 1, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.6800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.3500 on October 1, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 29, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8500 and Rs.52.5400 respectively.
