## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## May 17, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers at Re.0.02 (two paise) per Rupees100 for Auction of Floating Rate Bonds, 2014 to be held on May 19, 2003.
