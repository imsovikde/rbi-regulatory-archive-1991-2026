## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 17, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.5000 on October 17, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 16, 2003 ) were Rs.45.2900 and Rs.52.6500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
