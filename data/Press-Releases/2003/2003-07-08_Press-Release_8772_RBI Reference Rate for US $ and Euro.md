## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## July 8, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.2900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4600 on July 8, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 7, 2003) were Rs.46.2800 and Rs.53.0100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|July 07, 2003|77.1233|39.25|
|July 08, 2003|76.3392|39.2400|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
