## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 9, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.4300 on January 9, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 8, 2003) were Rs.47.9800 and Rs.50.0100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
