## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 24, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.6100 on March 24, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 21, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7000 and Rs.50.5800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
