## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.6200 on March 20, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 19, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6700 and Rs.50.6600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
