## Underwriting by Primary Dealers September 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the "6.17% Government Stock, 2023" to be held on September 3, 2003 at Re.0.02 (2 paise) per Rupees 100.
