## State Tap Sale for Debt Swaps

## August 13, 2003

In consultation with the Government of India it has been decided to conduct a tap sale of State Government securities for Rs.8,000 crores on August 25, 2003 as the third tranche of on- going State Government debt swap programme in the current year. Operational details regarding the tap sale will be announced nearer the date of the sale.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
