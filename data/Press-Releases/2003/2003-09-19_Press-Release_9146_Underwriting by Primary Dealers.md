## Underwriting by Primary Dealers September 19, 2003

RBI has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the “10-year Punjab State Government Stock” to be held on September 20, 2003 at Re.0.02 (two paise) per Rupees hundred.
