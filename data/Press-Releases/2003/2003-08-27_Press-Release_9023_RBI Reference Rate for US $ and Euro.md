## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.8100 on August 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 26, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8700 and Rs.49.7700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
