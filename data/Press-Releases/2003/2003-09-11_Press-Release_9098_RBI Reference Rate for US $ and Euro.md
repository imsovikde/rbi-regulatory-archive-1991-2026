## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 11, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.4400 on September 11, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 10, 2003 ) were Rs.45.7700 and Rs.51.3600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
