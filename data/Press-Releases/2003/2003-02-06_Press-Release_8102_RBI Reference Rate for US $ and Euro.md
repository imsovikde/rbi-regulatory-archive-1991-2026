## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## February 6, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.7300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.5100 on February 6, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 5, 2003 ) were Rs.47.7600 and Rs.52.0300 respectively.
