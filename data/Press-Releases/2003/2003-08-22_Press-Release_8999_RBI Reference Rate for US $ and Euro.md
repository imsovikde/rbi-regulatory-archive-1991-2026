## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 22, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.0100 on August 22, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 21, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8400 and Rs.50.7200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
