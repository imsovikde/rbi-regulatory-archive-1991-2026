## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 29, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.9100 on August 29, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 28, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8300 and Rs.49.6900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
