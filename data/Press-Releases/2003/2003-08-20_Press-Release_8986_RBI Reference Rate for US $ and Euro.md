## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 20, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.9300 on August 20, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 19, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8400 and Rs.50.9700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
