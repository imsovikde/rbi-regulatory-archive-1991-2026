## Tap sale of 6.75 per cent SDL 2013

## March 15, 2003

The sale on tap of 6.75 per cent State Loans, 2013 in respect of 3 States, viz., Assam, Rajasthan and West Bengal will be kept open on March 17, 2003 to collect the balance target amount. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai Office (for all the three States).

Applications alongwith the payment will be received at these offices during banking hours while the tap is open. Applicants, submitting the applications on March 17, 2003 would also have to pay interest for five days at 6.75 per cent per annum for the securities they apply for. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the tap sale during the banking hours on or after March 17, 2003 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received.
