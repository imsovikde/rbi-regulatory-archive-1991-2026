## Government of India’s Debt Buyback Programme Auction:

## Prices of securities to be bought back

## July 18, 2003

The Government of India has announced buy back of high cost, illiquid securities from banks entirely on a voluntary basis. The debt buyback auction has been scheduled between 11.00

A.M. to 2.00 P.M. on July 19, 2003 (Saturday). The Government will accept a minimum discount (as a percentage to market price) of 7.5 per cent that must be offered by eligible institutions. The market price of the 19 securities offered for buyback are:
