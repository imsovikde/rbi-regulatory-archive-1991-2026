## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 13, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.6500 on January 13, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 10, 2003) were Rs.47.9600 and Rs.50.3500 respectively.
