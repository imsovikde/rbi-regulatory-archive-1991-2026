## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 17, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.6400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.3400 on June 17, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 16, 2003) were Rs.46.6300 and Rs.55.3300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 16, 2003|78.0889|39.62|
|June 17, 2003|78.5091|39.6600|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
