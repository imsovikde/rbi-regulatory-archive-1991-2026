## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## July 31, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on July 31, 2003 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of United Breweries Ltd., up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting.
