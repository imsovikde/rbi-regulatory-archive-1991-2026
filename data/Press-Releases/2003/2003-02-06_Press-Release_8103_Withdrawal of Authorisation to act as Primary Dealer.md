## Withdrawal of Authorisation to act as Primary Dealer

## February 6, 2003

M/s. Tata TD Waterhouse Securities Private Limited, Mumbai have requested the Reserve Bank of India that they may be allowed to exit from their Primary Dealership activities as their company proposes to focus on their other business activities. The Reserve Bank of India have acceded to their request and accordingly, Tata TD Waterhouse Securities Private Limited ceases to be a Primary Dealer with effect from close of business on Thursday, February 6, 2003.
