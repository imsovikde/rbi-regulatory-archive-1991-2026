## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 4, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.6500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.0400 on March 4, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 3, 2003 ) were Rs.47.6600 and Rs.51.3800 respectively.
