## RBI offices to remain closed for public on June 30, 2003

## June 25, 2003

The offices of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will remain closed on Monday, June 30, 2003 for public transaction on account of annual closing of its accounts.
