## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

## August 21, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on August 21, 2003 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase shares of M/s. Mphasis BFL Software Ltd., in the secondary market, as the holdings of FIIs in the company have gone down below 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
