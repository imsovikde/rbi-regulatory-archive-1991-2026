# 91 day Treasury Bills- RBI reduces Notified Amount for Next Eight Auctions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## 91 day Treasury Bills-

## RBI reduces Notified Amount for Next Eight Auctions

## February 1, 2003

The notified amount for the next eight auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills to be conducted from February 5, 2003 to March 26, 2003 has been reduced from Rs.1000 crore to Rs. 500 crore. These eight auctions would also be conducted by using multiple price auction format. The position will be reviewed once again after these auctions. The notified amount and the present format of auction of 364-day Treasury Bills will remain unchanged.

Accordingly, the Calendar for Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills from **February 5, 2003 to March 26, 2003** would be:

## Type of Periodicity Notified Amount Day of Day of of T-Bills (Rs. Crore) Auction Payment

91-Day Weekly 500 Every Following Wednesday Friday

364-Day Fortnightly 1000 Wednesday Following Preceding the Friday <u>Reporting Friday</u>

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons such as intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank had, on January 3, 2003, announced continuation of the enhanced notified amount of Rs. 1,000 crore for four auctions of 91- day Treasury Bills to be conducted during the period from January 8, 2003 to January 29,

2003**.** The experience in this regard was to be reviewed. The amount has been reduced to Rs. 500 crore after the review was undertaken. The auctions of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)- W&M/97dated March 31, 1998 issued by the Government of India.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
