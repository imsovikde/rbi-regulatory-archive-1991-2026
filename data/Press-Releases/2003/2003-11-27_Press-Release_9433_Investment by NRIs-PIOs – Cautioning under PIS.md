## Investment by NRIs/PIOs – Cautioning under PIS

## November 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India on November 27, 2003 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of M/s. Heg Ltd., should be made on behalf of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origins (PIOs) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s. Heg Ltd., has reached limit of 8 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
