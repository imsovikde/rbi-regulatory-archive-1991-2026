## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 16, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.1900 on December 16, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 15, 2003) were Rs.45.5600 and Rs.55.5600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
