## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## April 16, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.3600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.2000 on April 16, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 10, 2003) were Rs.47.4100 and Rs.51.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
