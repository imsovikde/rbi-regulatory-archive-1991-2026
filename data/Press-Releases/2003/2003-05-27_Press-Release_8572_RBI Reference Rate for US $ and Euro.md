## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.4700 on May 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 26, 2003) were Rs.46.9000 and Rs.55.4100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|May 26, 2003|76.8785|40.15|
|May 27, 2003|76.7661|40.03|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
