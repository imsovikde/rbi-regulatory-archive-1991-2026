## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

September 18, 2003 The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.0800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.0000 on September 18, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 17, 2003 ) were Rs.45.9400 and Rs.51.2500 respectively.
