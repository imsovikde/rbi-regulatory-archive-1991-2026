## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 4, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.7400 on September 4, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 3, 2003 ) were Rs.45.8800 and Rs.49.5000 respectively.
