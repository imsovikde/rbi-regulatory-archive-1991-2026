## Tap sale of 6.60 per cent State Development Loans, 2013 closed

## February 4, 2003

The sale `on tap' of 6.60 per cent State Development Loans closed today in respect of all three States viz., Andhra Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh and Jammu & Kashmir, as the subscriptions received exceeded the target amount of the respective States.

## P. V. Sadanandan

## Manager
