## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 19, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.5400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.4700 on November 19, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 18, 2003) were Rs.45.5400 and Rs.53.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
