## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.7300 on November 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 25, 2003) were Rs.45.9300 and Rs.54.1800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
