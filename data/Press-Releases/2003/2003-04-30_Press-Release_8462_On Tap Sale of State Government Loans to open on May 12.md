## On Tap Sale of State Government Loans to open on May 12

## April 30, 2003

State Governments will be entering the market for raising market loans carrying tenure of 10 years by way of tap sale on May 12, 2003. The aggregate target amount would be around Rs.7000 crore, with an option to retain excess subscription up to 30 per cent. The coupon rate and other details of the tap issue will be notified later.
