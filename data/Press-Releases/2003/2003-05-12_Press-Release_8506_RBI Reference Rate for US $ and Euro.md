## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 12, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.1600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.5300 on May 12, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 9, 2003) were Rs.47.1900 and Rs.54.2200 respectively.
