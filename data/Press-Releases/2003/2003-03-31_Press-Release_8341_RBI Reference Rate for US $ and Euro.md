## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 31, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.5000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.4400 on March 31, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 28, 2003 ) were Rs.47.5500 and Rs.50.8100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
