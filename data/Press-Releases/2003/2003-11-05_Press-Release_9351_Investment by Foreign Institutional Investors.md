## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## November 5, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on November 5, 2003 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Moser Baer India Ltd., up to 40 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board and General Body Meeting.
