## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 21, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.9500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.51.0500 on January 21, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 20, 2003 ) were Rs.47.9100 and Rs.50.9800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
