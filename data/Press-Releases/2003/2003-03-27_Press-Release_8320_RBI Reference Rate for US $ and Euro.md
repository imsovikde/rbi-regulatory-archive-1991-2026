## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## March 27, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.47.5400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.8600 on March 27, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 26, 2003 ) were Rs.47.5800 and Rs.50.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
