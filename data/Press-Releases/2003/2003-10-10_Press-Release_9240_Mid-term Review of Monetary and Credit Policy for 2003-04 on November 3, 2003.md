## Mid-term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy for 2003-04 on November 3, 2003

## October 10, 2003

Dr. Y.V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Mid-term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy for 2003-04 on Monday, November 3, 2003. He will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks for announcing the policy which will take place at 11.00 am in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
