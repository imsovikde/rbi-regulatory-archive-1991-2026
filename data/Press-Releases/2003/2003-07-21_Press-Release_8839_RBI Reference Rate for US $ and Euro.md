## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## July 21, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.2600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.1700 on July 21, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 18, 2003) were Rs.46.2300 and Rs.51.9300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|July 18, 2003|73.7530|38.83|
|July 21, 2003|73.1556|39.0800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
