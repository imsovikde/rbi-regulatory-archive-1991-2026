## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 23, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.5900 on October 23, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 22, 2003 ) were Rs.45.3700 and Rs.53.0700 respectively.
