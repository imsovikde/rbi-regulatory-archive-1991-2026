## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 29, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9900 on October 29, 2003. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 28, 2003) were Rs.45.4000 and Rs.53.1700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
