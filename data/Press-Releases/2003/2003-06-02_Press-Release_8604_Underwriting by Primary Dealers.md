## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## June 2, 2003

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the price based auction of 6.85% Government Stock 2012 and yield based auction of new 25 Year Government Stock to be held on June 3, 2003 at Re.0.02 (2 paise) and at Re.0.01 (1 Paisa) per Rupees 100, respectively.
