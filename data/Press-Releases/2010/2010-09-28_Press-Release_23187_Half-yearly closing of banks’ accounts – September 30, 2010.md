## Half-yearly closing of banks’ accounts – September 30, 2010

The Government of Maharashtra has declared Thursday, September 30, 2010 as public holiday under Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, on account of half-yearly closing of banks’ accounts. The above holiday will not be applicable for the Reserve Bank of India and its offices in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will therefore function normally on September 30, 2010.
