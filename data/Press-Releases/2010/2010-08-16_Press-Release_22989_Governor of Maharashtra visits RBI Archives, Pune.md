## <u>Governor of Maharashtra visits RBI Archives, Pune</u>

His Excellency, Shri K. Sankaranarayanan, the Governor of Maharashtra visited the Reserve Bank of India’s Archives Museum, Pune, on August 14, 2010.

Welcoming His Excellency, Shri Deepak Mohanty, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India said that the archives not only nurtured the sentiments for culture and history of an organisation but also acted as a bridge between past, present and future. It was more so for the Reserve Bank as the financial and the economic history of the country was intertwined with the history of the Reserve Bank. He also said that in order to create public awareness, the Reserve Bank had recently set up the Archives Museum. The Museum was becoming an important attraction of Pune.

His Excellency, Shri K. Sankaranarayanan remarked, “I am so happy and delighted to be here in this institution. It (Museum) says that this is the history of our great country, very much useful to the students of the colleges in India and abroad”. He emphasised the need for making the present generation aware of the heritage of the country and therefore, urged for a wide publicity to be given to the RBI Archives Museum.

Ms. Kamala Rajan, Principal of the College of Agricultural Banking where Archives Museum is located, proposed a vote of thanks.

Senior officers of Government of Maharashtra and the Reserve Bank and the College of Agricultural Banking were present at the event.
