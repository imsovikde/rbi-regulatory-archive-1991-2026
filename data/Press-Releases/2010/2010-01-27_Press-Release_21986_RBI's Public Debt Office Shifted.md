## RBI's Public Debt Office Shifted

The Public Debt Office (PDO) of the Reserve Bank of India, Byculla will be functioning from Reserve Bank of India, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai - 400 001 with effect from January 28, 2010. All correspondence relating to Relief/Savings Bond and other schemes may henceforth be addressed to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Mumbai Office, Main Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Fort, Mumbai - 400 001.
