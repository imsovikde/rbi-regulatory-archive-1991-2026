## October 11, 2010

**Issue of new Coins of** ` **5 and** ` **2 with the theme** **"XIX COMMONWEALTH GAMES, 2010-DELHI"**

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation coins of ` 5 and ` 2 with the theme "XIX COMMONWEALTH GAMES, 2010-DELHI"

The coins of above denominations shall conform to the following dimension, design and composition, namely :

## Denomination of the coin Shape and outside diameter Metal composition

Circular Nickel Brass Five Rupees 23 millimeters Copper - 75% Zinc - 20% Nickel - 5% Circular Ferritic Stainless Steel Two Rupees 27 millimeters Iron - 83% <u>Chromium-17%</u>
