## January 5, 2010

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Maruti Suzuki

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>Maruti Suzuki</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit of their paid up capital. Therefore, further purchases of equity shares in the primary/secondary markets of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
