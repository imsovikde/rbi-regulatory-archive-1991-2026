## Monetary Policy 2010-11 on April 20, 2010

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Monetary Policy for 2010-11 on Tuesday, April 20, 2010. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00

a.m. on April 20, 2010 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
