## Reserve Bank extends

## Second LAF (SLAF) on a Daily Basis

On an assessment of current overall liquidity conditions and with a view to providing flexibility to scheduled commercial banks and primary dealers in their liquidity management, it has been decided to extend the second liquidity adjustment facility (SLAF) on a daily basis till July 30, 2010.
