## April 9, 2010

## Five State Governments announce Auction of State Development Loans 2020 on April 12, 2010

In continuation of the Press Release No. 2009-2010/1366 dated April 8, 2010 announcing the auction of State Development Loans of four State Governments on April 12, 2010, Government of West Bengal has also announced the sale of its 10- year State Development Loans for Rs. 2,000.000 crore through yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

Accordingly, the aggregate amount of State Development Loans to be sold by five State Governments on April 12, 2010 is Rs. 5,800.000 crore.
