## Smt. Usha Thorat lays down office as Deputy Governor

## Smt. Shyamala Gopinath to hold temporary charge of her portfolios

Smt. Usha Thorat, Deputy Governor relinquished charge of her office at the close of business on November 09, 2010 after completing her 5 year term. The portfolios handled by her will temporarily be looked after by Deputy Governor, Smt. Shyamala Gopinath until further orders.
