## Regulatory Framework for Core Investment Companies (CICs)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the final guidelines on '<u>Regulatory Framework for Core Investment Companies (CICs)</u>. The Reserve Bank had, on April 21, 2010, issued draft guidelines on regulatory framework for core investment companies for feedback from market participants. The guidelines have been finalised after considering the feedback received.
