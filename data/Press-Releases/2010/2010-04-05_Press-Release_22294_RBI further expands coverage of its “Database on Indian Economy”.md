## April 5, 2010

## RBI further expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

The Reserve Bank of India has further expanded the coverage of its "Database on Indian Economy", an on-line database built on the data warehousing technology with enhanced functionality, to include static reports on Urban Cooperative Banks and Rural Cooperative Banks. With the release of these data series, the Database on Indian Economy would now have 451 static reports (subject and frequency wise), 74 subject wise and 80 frequency wise data query templates covering various sectors of the Indian economy. The Reserve Bank of India's Database on Indian Economy is available on its website <u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u> under the Database Section. This can also be directly accessed through URL <u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>. The coverage of the Database is continuously being expanded on the basis of the feedback received from the users of the database. The details of additional data are:

(a) Static reports being released through the Database on Indian Economy in this
release are:

1. Liabilities and Assets of Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks ( Sch. UCBs)
2. Liabilities and Assets of Non-Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks ( Non- Sch. UCBs)
3. Liabilities and Assets of Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)
4. Profile of Rural Co-operative Banks (RCBs)
5. Liabilities and Assets of State Co-operative Banks (StCBs)
6. Liabilities and Assets of District Central Co-operative Banks (DCCBs)
7. Select Balance Sheet Indicators of Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)
8. Liabilities and Assets of State Co-operative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks (SCARDBs)
9. Liabilities and Assets of Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks (PCARDBs)
10. Financial Performance of State Co-operative Banks (StCBs)
11. Financial Performance of District Central Co-operative Banks (DCCBs)
12. Financial Performance of State Co-operative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks (SCARDBs)
13. Financial Performance of Primary Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Banks (PCARDBs)
14. Growth Rate of Select Balance sheet items of Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)
