## Branches of Bank of Rajasthan Ltd. to be

## Branches of ICICI Bank Ltd. from August 13

All branches of Bank of Rajasthan Ltd. will function as branches of ICICI Bank Ltd. with effect from August 13, 2010. This is consequent upon the Reserve Bank of India sanctioning the Scheme of Amalgamation of Bank of Rajasthan Ltd. with ICICI Bank Ltd. The Scheme has been sanctioned in exercise of the powers contained in Sub-section (4) of Section 44A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The Scheme will come into force with effect from close of business on August 12, 2010.
