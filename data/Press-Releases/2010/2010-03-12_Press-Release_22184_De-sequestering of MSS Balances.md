## De-sequestering of MSS Balances

On a review of its cash position, the Government of India, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, decided to transfer an amount of Rs.5,000 crore from the MSS cash account to the normal cash account of the Government of India on March 11, 2010. An equivalent amount of Government securities would accordingly form part of the normal borrowing of the Government of India for the fiscal year 2009-10.

It may be recalled that following the amendment to the Memorandum of Understanding on the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS) on February 26 2009, it was decided to transfer in installments an amount of Rs.45,000 crore from the MSS cash account to the normal cash account of the Government of India by March 31,

2009. Subsequently, Rs.12,000 crore of MSS balances was de-sequestered on March 4, 2009. It was then decided that Rs.33,000 crore of MSS balances would be de-sequestered against the approved market borrowing programme or bought back in the fiscal year 2009-10. On a review of the cash position of the Government, an amount of Rs.28,000 crore of MSS balances was de-sequestered on May 2, 2009. The MSS outstanding as on March 11, 2010 is Rs. 2,737 crore (Face Value).
