## RBI's Rajbhasha Shield Awards Ceremony

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India while awarding the <u>Rajbhasha Shields for the year 2008-2009</u>, said that no banking business can progress without Hindi or Indian languages. Banking services can be provided in a better way if conducted in Hindi and Regional Languages. Hindi can play a greater role in reaching out to common man and improve banking business.

Dr. K.C. Chakrabarty, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India while complimenting the prize winning banks and financial institutions said that we are taking banking to the doors of the common man and in this way the language whether it is regional or Hindi-can play an important role. Last year we have deeply felt this during the outreach programmes in Platinum Jubilee Year of Reserve Bank. He emphasised that to promote Hindi is not only a statutory but also a moral responsibility. To promote Hindi is essential as the banking backwardness is prevalent mostly in Hindi speaking regions.

The annual Reserve Bank Rajbhasha Shield was instituted in 1980 to encourage public sector banks to use Hindi in their day-to-day work. The Reserve Bank also conducts a competition for the house journals of banks and financial institutions. The Reserve Bank started an Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition from 2002-03.

Earlier, Shri V. S. Das, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India welcomed the Chief Executives of banks and financial institutions. Shri Prabal Sen, Principal Chief General Manager, Department of Administration and Personnel Management, Reserve Bank of India, proposed a vote of thanks. Ms. Roopam Misra, General Manager, Rajbhasha Department, Reserve Bank of India conducted the programme.

The function was attended by chairmen and other senior executives of banks and financial institutions.
