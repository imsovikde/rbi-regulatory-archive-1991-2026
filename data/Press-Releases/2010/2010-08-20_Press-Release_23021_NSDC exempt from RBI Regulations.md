## NSDC exempt from RBI Regulations

The Reserve Bank of India has communicated that the provisions of Sections 45IA, 45IB and 45IC of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934) will not apply to National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC). The Reserve Bank has stated that NSDC is a company registered as non-profit company under Section 25 of the Companies Act, 1956 and regulatory oversight to NSDC is provided through the board of trustees of National Skill Development Fund (NSDF).

The exemption is subject to the conditions that NSDC will :

(i) confine its activities to the specific objectives for which it has been set up and not issue loans and advances except for the specific purpose; (ii) not accept or hold public deposits; and (iii) not carry on any other financial activity except as (i) above.
