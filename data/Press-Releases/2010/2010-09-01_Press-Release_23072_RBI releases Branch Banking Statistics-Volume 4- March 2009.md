## September 1, 2010

## RBI releases Branch Banking Statistics-Volume 4: March 2009

The Reserve Bank of India (Department of Statistics and Information Management) today published its "<u>Branch Banking Statistics-Volume 4: March</u> <u>2009</u>". The publication is based on the latest available information on bank branches. The volume contains 25 tables providing the number of branches/offices of all commercial banks in India, at different levels of aggregation, namely, region, state/union territory, district, population group, authorised dealer category, bank group and bank, as on March 31, 2009. The publication also provides information on number of branches/offices of commercial banks opened and closed during the last three years, state/district wise list of lead banks and bank and country-wise number of branches of Indian Banks functioning at overseas centres.

The publication is available on RBI Website ([http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)).
