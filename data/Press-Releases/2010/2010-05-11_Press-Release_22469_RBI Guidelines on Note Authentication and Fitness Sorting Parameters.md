## May 11, 2010

## RBI Guidelines on Note Authentication and Fitness Sorting Parameters

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the <u>guidelines on Note</u> <u>Authentication and Fitness Sorting Parameters</u>.

The objective of these guidelines is to ensure uniform determination of the quality of banknotes by standardisation of the performance parameters of note sorting machines.

It may be recalled that the draft guidelines were placed on RBI's website on December 23, 2009. The guidelines have been finalised after taking into account the comments received from various stakeholders including banks, equipment manufacturers and members of public.
