**Issue of new Coins of** ` **5 with the** **theme "125th Birth Anniversary of Dr. Rajendra Prasad"**

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation coins of ` 5 with the theme "125th Birth Anniversary of Dr. Rajendra Prasad".
