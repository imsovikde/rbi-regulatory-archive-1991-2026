## RBI’s Department of Banking Operations and Development shifts to Fort, Mumbai

Major divisions of Department of Banking Operations & Development (DBOD), th Reserve Bank of India, Centre Office have re-located to the 13 floor (in addition to the th divisions already located in 12 floor) of Central Office Building, Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 from World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Colaba, Mumbai. This re-location has been effected from August 30, 2010..

Any correspondence meant for DBOD may please be addressed to :

The Chief General Manger-in-Charge Department of Banking Operations & Development Reserve Bank of India Central Office th Central Office Building, 12 Floor Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Fort Mumbai-400 001.
