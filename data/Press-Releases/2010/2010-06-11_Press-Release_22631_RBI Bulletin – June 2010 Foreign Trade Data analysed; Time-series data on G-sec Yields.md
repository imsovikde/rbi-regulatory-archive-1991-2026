## June 11, 2010

## RBI Bulletin – June 2010

## Foreign Trade Data analysed; Time-series data on G-sec Yields

The Reserve Bank of India today published <u>June 2010 issue of its monthly</u> <u>RBI Bulletin</u>. Apart from the regular features, such as, speeches of top executives, RBI press releases, regulatory and other measures, foreign exchange developments and current statistics, the June issue of the RBI bulletin carries an article titled Foreign Trade: 2009-10 (April-March)*.* The article reviews India’s merchandise trade performance during financial year 2009-10 on the basis of data released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and Directorate General of Commercial Intelligence and Statistics (DGCI&S). The article also covers disaggregated commodity-wise and direction-wise analysis of India’s trade during the period April- October 2009.

The article observes that during 2009-10, India’s merchandise exports at US$

176.6 billion posted a decline of 4.7 per cent over the year as against a growth of
13.7 per cent during 2008-09. Merchandise imports at US$ 278.7 billion recorded a decline of 8.2 per cent over the year as against a growth of 20.8 per cent during 2008-09. The oil and non-oil imports of India also registered a decline of 8.7 per cent and 8.0 per cent in 2009-10, respectively, as against a growth of 17.6 per cent and
22.3 per cent in 2008-09. Further, the disaggregated commodity level and direction level data reveals that trade of all major commodity groups (namely, primary products, manufactured goods, petroleum products and bulk and non bulk import items) and with major trading partners (namely, OECD, OPEC, Eastern Europe and Developing Countries) registered a decline during April-October 2009 over the corresponding period of previous year.
## Time Series data on Month-end Secondary Market Yield on G-Secs

The June issue of the RBI Bulletin also carries, for the first time, time-series data on ‘Month-end Secondary Market Yield on Government of India Securities’ (Table No. 27 under ‘Current Statistics’). The data is based on FIMMDA/ PDAI month-end valuation of Government of India Securities. The table provides comparative data on security-wise month-end yield of all outstanding Government of India Dated Securities. Besides month-end yield data, the table also provides year- end data (as on March 31) for two latest years. Since all market participants extensively use the FIMMDA valuation, it is expected to be useful to the Bulletin readers to get the FIMMDA/ PDAI yield data in a time-series form.
