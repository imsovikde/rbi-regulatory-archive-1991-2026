## Certificate of Registration of

## M/s. Rakuja Investments Private Limited-Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has on October 7, 2010 cancelled the certificate of registration granted on October 11, 2002 to M/s. Rakuja Investments Private Limited rd having its registered office at U-203, 3 Floor, Shakarpur, Delhi-110092 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation of the registration certificate the company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.

By the powers conferred under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of a non-banking financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
