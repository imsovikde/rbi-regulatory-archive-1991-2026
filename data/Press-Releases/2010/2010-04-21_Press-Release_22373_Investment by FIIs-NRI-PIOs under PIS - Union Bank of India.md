## April 21, 2010

## Investment by FIIs/NRI/PIOs under PIS : Union Bank of India

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holdings in <u>Union Bank of India</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit of their paid up capital. Therefore, further purchases of equity shares in the primary/secondary market of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
