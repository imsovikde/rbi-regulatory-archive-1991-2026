## Issue of new Commemorative Circulation Coin of Rs.10/- denomination

## (bi-metallic) on the occasion of "HOMI BHABHA BIRTH CENTENARY YEAR"

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation new commemorative circulation coins of Rs.10/- denomination (bi-metallic) issued by Government of India on the occasion of " HOMI BHABHA BIRTH CENTENARY YEAR".
