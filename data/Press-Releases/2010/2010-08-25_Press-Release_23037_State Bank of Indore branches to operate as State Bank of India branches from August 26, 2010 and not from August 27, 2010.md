## State Bank of Indore branches to operate as State Bank of India branches

## from August 26, 2010 and not from August 27, 2010

All the branches of State Bank of Indore will function as branches of the State Bank of India from August 26, 2010 and **not** August 27, 2010 as mentioned in the Reserve Bank press release no 287 issued on August 24, 2010. This is due to the fact that the Order of Acquisition will be effective on and from the close of business on August 25, 2010 and not August 26, 2010 as announced earlier.
