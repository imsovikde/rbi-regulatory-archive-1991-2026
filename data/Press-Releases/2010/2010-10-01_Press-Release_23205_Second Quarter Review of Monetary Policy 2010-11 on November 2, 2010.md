## Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2010-11 on November 2, 2010

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy for 2010-11 on Tuesday, November 2,

2010. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on November 2, 2010 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
