# RBI re-sets rate of interest on FRBs 2006

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 22, 2004 RBI re-sets rate of interest on FRBs 2006 The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2006 (FRB, 2006) applicable for the half-year November 22, 2004 to May 21, 2005 shall be 5.37 per cent per annum. It may be recalled that in the auction held on November 21, 2001, the rate of interest on the FRB, 2006 was set at a mark-up over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest was to be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half-yearly coupon period. The mark-up decided in the auction held on November 21, 2001 was (-) 0.05 (minus 0.05). The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills worked out to be 5.42 per cent. The coupon rate has, thus, worked out to be 5.37 per cent. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/536
