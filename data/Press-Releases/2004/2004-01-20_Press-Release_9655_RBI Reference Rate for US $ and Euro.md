## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.2900 on January 20, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 19, 2004) were Rs.45.4100 and Rs.56.2500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
