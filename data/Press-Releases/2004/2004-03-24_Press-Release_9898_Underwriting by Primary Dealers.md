## March 24, 2004

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the "10-year Kerala State Government Stock" to be held on March 25, 2004 at Re.0.02 (two paise) per rupees hundred.
