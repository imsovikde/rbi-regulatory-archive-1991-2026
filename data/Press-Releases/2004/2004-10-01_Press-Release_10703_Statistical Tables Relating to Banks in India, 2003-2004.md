# Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India, 2003-2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in October 1, 2004 Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India, 2003-2004 The Reserve Bank of India has published “Statistical Tables Relating to Banks in India 2003-2004”. This is 59th edition of the volume. The publication contains detailed tables relating to assets and liabilities and earnings and expenses of Scheduled Commercial Banks (excluding RRBs) in India for the year 2003-2004.  In addition to detailed bank-wise tables, the publication provides summary tables giving aggregated data for bank groups as well as for all banks. The publication also gives data on important parameters like maturity and ownership pattern of deposits, sector-wise bank credit, investment pattern, state- wise distribution of employees, insured deposits, risk-weighted assets and NPAs. Tables giving important ratios like return on assets, cost of funds, capital adequacy ratio and business per employee have also been included. The tables have been supplemented with graphs and maps. The CD-ROM, besides providing all the tables, graphs and maps given in the publication, gives inter-active data querying facility. It also provides facility to calculate growth rates and ratios.
