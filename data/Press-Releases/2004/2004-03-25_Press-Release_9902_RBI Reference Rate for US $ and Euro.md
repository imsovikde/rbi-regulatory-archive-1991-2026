## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.7500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3200 on March 25, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 24, 2004) were Rs.44.7600 and Rs.55.1900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
