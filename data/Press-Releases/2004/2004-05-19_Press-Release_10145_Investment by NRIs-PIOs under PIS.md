# Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 19, 2004 Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve of India has notified on May 19, 2004 that the holdings of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of India Origin (PIOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the paid up capital of the following companies, have gone below 8 per cent. The Reserve Bank has further notified that it will be in order for NRIs and PIOs to purchase the shares of the following companies in the secondary market under the portfolio investment scheme subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines in this regard. i) Astra IDL Ltd. ii) IDL Industries Ltd. iii) Seirra Optima Ltd. iv) The Baroda Rayon Corporation Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1354
