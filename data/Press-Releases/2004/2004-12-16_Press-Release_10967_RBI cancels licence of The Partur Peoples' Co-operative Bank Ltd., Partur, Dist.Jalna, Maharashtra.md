# RBI cancels licence of The Partur Peoples' Co-operative Bank Ltd., Partur, Dist.Jalna, Maharashtra

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in December 16, 2004 RBI cancels licence of The Partur Peoples' Co-operative Bank Ltd., Partur, Dist.Jalna, Maharashtra The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the licence of The Partur Peoples' Co-operative Bank Ltd., Partur, Dist.Jalna, Maharashtra to carry on banking business. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of `banking' as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co- operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise. The Reserve Bank has cancelled the licence under Section 22 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (as applicable to Co-operative Societies). P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/630
