# RBI to respond to Developments in Measured Manner : RBI Governor reassures

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in August 11, 2004 RBI to respond to Developments in Measured Manner: RBI Governor reassures In response to press queries, Dr. Y V Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India reiterated, "The Reserve Bank is monitoring the developments carefully. In particular, the Reserve Bank is analysing how the imported oil shock is turning out to be globally and how is it absorbed domestically. The Governor reassured that the Reserve Bank will respond to the developments in a measured manner on an ongoing basis with a view to maintaining price stability over the year." Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/166
