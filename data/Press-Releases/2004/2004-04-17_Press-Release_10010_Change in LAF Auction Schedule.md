# Change in LAF Auction Schedule

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` **PRESS RELEASE**

**PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION**, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA** Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 www.rbi.org.in <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>**\**hindi <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> April 17, 2004 **Change in LAF Auction Schedule** The Reserve Bank of India today announced the following changes in the repo auction calendar :

**1. Calendar for 7 day Repo Auctions**
Repo Period (days)

|Date of Auction|Date of Reversal|Revised Date of|
|---|---|---|
||(Notified)|Reversal|
|April 19, 2004|April 26, 2004|April 23, 2004|

**2. Calendar for Overnight Reverse Repo Auctions**
Repo Period (days)

|Date of Auction|Date of Reversal|Revised Date of|
|---|---|---|
||(Notified)|Reversal|
|April 23, 2004|April 26, 2004|April 27, 2004|

3. No auction will be conducted under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility on April 26, 2004. The changes have been announced in view of the Government of Maharashtra declaring April 26, 2004 as a holiday under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 due to elections. The Reserve Bank had, on March 25, 2004, issued a press release (2003-2004/1129) announcing the Calendar for auctions to be conducted under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility for the quarter April-June 2004.
