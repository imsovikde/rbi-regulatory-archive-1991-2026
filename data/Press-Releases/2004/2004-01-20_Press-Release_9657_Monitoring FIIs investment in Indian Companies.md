## Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on January 20, 2004 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of the following companies, through primary markets and stock exchanges in India up to the limits indicated against them, as these companies have passed resolutions to this effect at their Board of Director’s and General Body Meetings.

1. Alok Industries Ltd., - 30 per cent.
2. Welspun Gujarat Stahal Rohren Ltd., - 40 per cent.
