## March 31, 2004

RBI's Annual Policy Statement (2004-05) on May 18, 2004

Dr. Y. V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2004-05 on Tuesday, May 18, 2004. He will announce the Policy at a meeting of the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks on that date at 11.00 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
