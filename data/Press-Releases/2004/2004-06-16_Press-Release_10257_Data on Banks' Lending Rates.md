# Data on Banks' Lending Rates

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in June 16, 2004 Data on Banks' Lending Rates The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website (www.rbi.org.in) the data on lending rates of scheduled commercial banks as on March 31, 2004. The data include the range of actual and median lending rates of credit of above Rs.2 lakh as well as actual and median lending rates for export credit in rupee terms. Both these categories of data are available bank-group wise. An additional table presenting the lending rates of individual banks for credit above Rs.2 lakh and for export credit in rupee terms is also presented. It may be recalled that to enhance transparency in banks' lending rates, the Reserve Bank of India started publishing the data on its website in February 2003. Since then it has been regularly updating the data on a quarterly basis. This was in pursuance of its intention stated in Monetary  and Credit Policy for 2002-2003 announced in April 2002 of collecting and publishing lending rates by banks. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1464
