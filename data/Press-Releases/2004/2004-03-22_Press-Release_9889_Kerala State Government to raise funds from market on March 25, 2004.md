## March 22, 2004

## Kerala State Government to raise funds from market on March 25, 2004

The State Government of Kerala have announced the sale of 10-year stock to the public by auction for an aggregate amount of Rs.200 crore. The auction will be conducted by Mumbai (Fort) office of the Reserve Bank of India on Thursday, March 25, 2004. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Thursday, March 25, 2004 before 12.30 hrs. NDS members shall submit their applications through NDS only. Results will be announced the same day. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, March 26, 2004.
