## January 5, 2004

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of the "7.38% Government Stock, 2015" to be held on January 6, 2004 at Re.0.0075 (0.75 paisa) per Rupees 100.
