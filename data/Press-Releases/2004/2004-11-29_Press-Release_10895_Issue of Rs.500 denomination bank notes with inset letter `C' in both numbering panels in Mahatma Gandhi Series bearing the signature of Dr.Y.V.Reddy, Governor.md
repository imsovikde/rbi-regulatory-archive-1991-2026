# Issue of Rs.500 denomination bank notes with inset letter `C' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series bearing the signature of Dr.Y.V.Reddy, Governor

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 29, 2004 Issue of Rs.500 denomination bank notes with inset letter `C' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series bearing the signature of Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue Rs.500 denomination banknotes with inset letter `C' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series bearing the signature of Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor. Except for the change in the inset letter, the design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respect to those issued earlier in the Mahatma Gandhi Series. All notes in the denomination of Rs.500 issued by the Bank since September 1987 will continue to be legal tender. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/561
