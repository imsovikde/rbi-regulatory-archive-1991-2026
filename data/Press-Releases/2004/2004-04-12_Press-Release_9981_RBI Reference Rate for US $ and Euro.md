## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.6500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6500 on April 12, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 08, 2004) were Rs.43.6200 and Rs.53.1900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
