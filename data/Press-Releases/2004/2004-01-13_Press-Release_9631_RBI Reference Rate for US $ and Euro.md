# RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

January 13, 2004 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro**

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.9800 on January 13,

2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 12, 2004) were Rs.45.4200 and Rs.58.3000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
## Currency

|Date|1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 12, 2004|83.8862|42.67|
|January 13, 2004|84.0251|42.6400|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
