## February 25, 2004

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers at Re. 0.01 (one paisa) per Rupees 100 for the auctions of 5.64 per cent Government Stock, 2019 to be held on February 26, 2004.
