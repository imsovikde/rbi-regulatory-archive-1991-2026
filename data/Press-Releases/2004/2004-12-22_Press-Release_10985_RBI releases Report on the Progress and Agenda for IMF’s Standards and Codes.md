# RBI releases Report on the Progress and Agenda for IMF’s Standards and Codes

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in RBI releases Report on the Progress and Agenda for IMF’s Standards and Codes The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) today, placed on its website, the "Review of the Recommendations of the Advisory Groups Constituted by the Standing Committee on International Financial Standards And Codes: Report on the Progress and Agenda Ahead ". The Reserve Bank has invited views on the Report to helpmpd@rbi.org.in or to Adviser-in-Charge, Monetary Policy Department (MPD) on fax no. 022-22651685 or 022- 22693204. The Report reviews the progress, provides the current status on the implementation and captures new developments in the field of international financial standards and codes. The objective of the review is to stimulate further discussions and improve awareness about the international standards and codes among various stakeholders. It may be recalled that in May 2002, the Reports of the eleven Advisory/Technical Groups were published and also placed on the RBI website, along with the Report of the Standing Committee that included a synthesis report prepared by an outside expert. The Advisory/Technical Groups constituted by the Standing Committee on International Financial Standards and Codes, consisted of members who were almost wholly experts from outside the Reserve Bank as well as outside the Government, The Mid-Term Review of Annual Policy Statement for 2004-05 had indicated that the Report of the progress made on the implementation of the recommendations of these eleven Groups would be placed in the public domain for wider dissemination. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/646
