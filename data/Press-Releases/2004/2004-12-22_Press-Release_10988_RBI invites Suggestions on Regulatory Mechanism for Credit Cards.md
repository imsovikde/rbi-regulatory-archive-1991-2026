# RBI invites Suggestions on Regulatory Mechanism for Credit Cards

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in December 22, 2004 RBI invites Suggestions on Regulatory Mechanism for Credit Cards In order to receive constructive inputs from the public at large on a suitable regulatory mechanism for credit cards, debit cards and smart cards, the Reserve Bank of India has invited members of public to offer their suggestions and comments on the subject. Suggestions could be sent directly to cardsgroupdit@rbi.org.in It may be recalled that the Governor, Reserve Bank of India, in his mid term review of the Annual Policy for 2004-2005 in October 2004, had announced the setting up of a Working Group for Regulatory Mechanism for Cards. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank has constituted a Working Group for Regulatory Mechanism for Cards to address various issues relating to regulatory requirements for credit cards. The Working Group has been set up by drawing representatives from the banking industry and the regulatory departments of the Reserve Bank. The Working Group would also look into various aspects relating to customer protection and to suggest measures for usage of credit cards in a safe, secure and customer-friendly manner. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/650
