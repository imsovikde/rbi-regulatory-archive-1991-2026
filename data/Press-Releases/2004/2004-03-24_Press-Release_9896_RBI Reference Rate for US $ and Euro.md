## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.7600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.1900 on March 24, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 23, 2004) were Rs.44.9300 and Rs.55.5100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
