# RBI relaxes Service Area Norms

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in December 9, 2004 RBI relaxes Service Area Norms To give easy access to rural borrowers to institutional credit from any bank of their choice at a competitive price and to provide banks, public and private, with a level playing field, the Reserve Bank has relaxed the service area norms. Now commercial banks and regional rural banks (RRBs) would be free to lend in any rural and semi-urban area and borrowers too would have the choice of approaching any branch for their credit requirements. The allocation of villages among rural and semi- urban branches of banks shall, however, continue to be applicable for lending under Government sponsored schemes. The Reserve Bank has also dispensed with the requirement of non-service area branches obtaining a ‘no due certificate’ from the service area branch prior to lending.  Banks have, however, been advised to take necessary steps to avoid multiple financing. It may be recalled that the Service Area Approach (SAA) was introduced in April 1989, to bring about an orderly and planned development of the rural and semi- urban areas of the country. Under the SAA, all rural and semi-urban branches of banks were allocated specific villages, generally in geographical contiguous areas. The respective branches were required to take care of the overall development and credit needs of the villages allocated to them. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/605
