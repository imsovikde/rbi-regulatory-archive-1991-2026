` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 16, 2004 RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.58.2100 on November 16, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 11, 2004) were Rs.45.2000 and Rs.58.2400 respectively.
