# Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA RBI On Internet: http://www.rbi.org.in http://www.rbi.org.in\hindi Email: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 22, 2004 Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India has notified on April 22, 2004 that the holdings of foreign institutional investors (FIIs)/non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in  the paid up capital of ICICI Bank Ltd., have gone below 72 per cent. The Reserve Bank has further notified that it will be in order for FIIs/NRIs and PIOs to purchase the shares of ICICI Bank Ltd. in the secondary market under the portfolio investment scheme subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines in this regard. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1254
