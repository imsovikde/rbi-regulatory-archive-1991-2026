## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.0000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.0500 on April 21, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 20, 2004) were Rs.44.0000 and Rs.52.4700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
