# RBI relaxes loan impairment norm for UCBs

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in September 4, 2004 RBI relaxes loan impairment norm for UCBs The Reserve Bank of India had advised Urban Co-operative Banks to apply 90 day impairment norm to gold loans and small loans upto Rs.One lakh with effect from financial year ending March 31, 2005 for the purpose of assets classification, income recognition and provisioning. Taking into consideration representations received from Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs), it has been decided that these loans will be governed by the 90 day impairment norm from the financial year ending March 31, 2007. Till then, these loans will be governed by 180 day norm as hitherto. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2004-05/258
