` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 29, 2004 RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.59.6600 on November 29, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 25, 2004) were Rs.45.0300 and Rs.59.3500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are: Currency Date 1 GBP 100 YEN November 25, 2004 84.7420 43.79 November 29, 2004 85.1260 43.75 Note : The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
