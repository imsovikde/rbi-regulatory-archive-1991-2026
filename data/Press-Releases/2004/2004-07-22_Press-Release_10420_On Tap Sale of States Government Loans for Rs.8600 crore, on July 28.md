# On Tap Sale of States Government Loans for Rs.8600 crore, on July 28

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 22, 2004 On Tap Sale of States Government Loans for Rs.8600 crore, on July 28 Twenty State Governments have offered to sell on tap 6.35% State Development Loan 2013 (II Series) by way of tap sale on July 28, 2004 for an aggregate amount of about Rs.8600 crore. Of this, nineteen States will tap the market for an aggregate amount of about Rs.8000 crore as the second tranche of additional market borrowing on account of Debt Swap Scheme in the current financial year and three states will raise an aggregate amount of Rs.638.45 crore as part of their normal market borrowings. The States that will tap the market are, viz., Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal. States going in for normal market borrowings are Madhya Pradesh, Nagaland and Punjab. The States will have an option to retain up to 20 per cent of excess subscription, on account of debt swap scheme in the current financial year. The sale will be held at the respective regional offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all the 20 States).  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the banking hours on or after July 28, 2004 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. The date of payment will be July 30, 2004. Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Office at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the Reserve Bank offices during business hours while the sale is open. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/89
