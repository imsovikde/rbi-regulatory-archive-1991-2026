PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in June 30, 2004 Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors - Monitoring of Overall Ceiling The Reserve Bank of India on June 30, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of shares of The Arvind Mills Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank has stated that The Arvind Mills Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1517
