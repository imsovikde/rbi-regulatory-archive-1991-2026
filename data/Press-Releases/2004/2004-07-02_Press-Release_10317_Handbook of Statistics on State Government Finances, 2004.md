# Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

1 ` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July  2, 2004 Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances, 2004 The Reserve Bank today released a new publication entitled Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances, today, as part of its persistent endeavour towards better data dissemination. With the objective of facilitating academic and policy research, the Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances provides, for the first time, detailed time-series data on State-wise budgetary variables. This Handbook is being released in three versions viz., printed, CD ROM and web-based (www.rbi.org.in). The CD ROM, incorporating intelligent search features, would be released shortly. The data in this Handbook are presented in two broad parts. The first part covers State-wise data from 1980-81 onwards on major fiscal indicators. The second part contains State-wise detailed data from 1990-91 onwards on budgetary transactions in the revenue and capital accounts. It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank has been publishing The Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy since 1998. This handbook provides time series data on annual, quarterly, monthly and higher frequencies in respect of the real, monetary, fiscal, banking, financial and external sectors of the Indian Economy. The Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances would supplement The Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy by providing detailed State-wise budgetary data. Comments on the Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances may be sent to the Principal Adviser, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy (DEAP), Reserve Bank of India, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Mumbai 400001 or via email to narendrajadhav@rbi.org.in
