## February 20, 2004

## Tap sale of 5.85 per cent State Development Loans 2015 (Second Series) closed

The sale `on tap' of 5.85 per cent State Development Loan 2015 (Second Series) in respect of the remaining nine State Governments viz. Arunachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Orissa, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened on February 19, 2004 for 17 State Governments for a target amount of Rs.4220 crore and the sale was closed for eight States on February 19,

2004. Of the total subscriptions of Rs.4982 crore received on both the days, Rs.4036 crore has been retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on February 21, 2004 in respect of subscriptions received both on February 19 and February 20, 2004.
