## January 20, 2004

## Tap sale of 5.90 per cent SDL 2017 closed

The sale `on tap' of 5.90 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 in respect of the remaining 14 State Governments has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened on January 19, 2004 for 27 State Governments for a target amount of Rs.8050 crore and the sale was closed for 13 States on January 19, 2004.
