## March 19, 2004

## New Executive Director appointed in RBI

Shri P. V. Subba Rao has today taken over as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India. He will look after Industrial & Export Credit Department, Inspection Department and Secretary’s Department.

Prior to this, Shri Subba Rao was head of Department of Banking Supervision.
