February 6, 2004 **Borrowings by the Government of India and State Governments**

The Government of India has decided to postpone the auction scheduled to be held during the period February 2-9, 2004. The auction is now proposed to be held between February 23-27, 2004. It may be recalled that in the Issuance Calendar for Marketable Dated Securities released on September 29, 2003, it was indicated that the calendar is subject to variations depending on market conditions and other relevant factors. Eighteen States will also raise around Rs. 4,300 crore in the third week of February 2004 by way of tap sale towards the additional market borrowing as also for pre-payment of high cost debt to the Government of India under the Debt Swap Scheme. The exact dates of auction/tap sale and other details will be notified later.
