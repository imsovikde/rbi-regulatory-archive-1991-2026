# Three State Governments to raise funds from market on August 24, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in August 19, 2004 Three State Governments to raise funds from market on August 24, 2004 Three state Governments have announced the sale of 10-year stock to the public by auction for an aggregate amount of about Rs.1186 crore as detailed below: State                       Amount (Rs.crore) Kerala 355.66 Tamilnadu 450.00 West Bengal            380.00 Total   1185.66 The auction will be yield based under multiple yield format and will be conducted by Mumbai (Fort) office of the Reserve Bank of India on Tuesday,  August 24, 2004. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Tuesday, August 24, 2004 before 12.30 p.m. NDS members shall submit their applications through NDS only. Results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Wednesday, August 25, 2004. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/203
