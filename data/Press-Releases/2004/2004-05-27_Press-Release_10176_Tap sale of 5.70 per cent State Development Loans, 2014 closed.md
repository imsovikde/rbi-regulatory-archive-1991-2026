# Tap sale of 5.70 per cent State Development Loans, 2014 closed

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 27, 2004 Tap sale of 5.70 per cent State Development Loans, 2014 closed The sale ‘on tap’ of 5.70 per cent State Development Loan, 2014 in respect of the remaining eight State Governments viz., Assam, Bihar, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Orissa, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened on May 26, 2004 for 19 State Governments for a target amount of Rs. 8000 crore on account of Debt Swap Scheme with an option to retain additional subscription up to 20% and Rs.380 crore on account of normal market borrowings. Total subscription received on both the days aggregated Rs. 9350 crore, of which Rs.8471 crore have been retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on May 28, 2004 in respect of subscriptions received both on May 26 and May 27, 2004. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1390
