# RBI to issue Rs.50 denomination bank notes with inset letter `E' in numbering panel in Mahatma Gandhi series bearing the signature of Dr.Y.V.Reddy, Governor

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA RBI On Internet: http://www.rbi.org.in http://www.rbi.org.in\hindi Email: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 01, 2004 RBI to issue Rs.50 denomination bank notes with inset letter `E' in numbering panel in Mahatma Gandhi series bearing the signature of Dr.Y.V.Reddy, Governor The Reserve Bank of India will shortly be issuing Rs.50 denomination bank notes with inset letter `E'  in numbering panel in Mahatma Gandhi Series bearing the  signature of Dr.Y.V. Reddy, Governor.  Except for the change in inset letter, the design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respects to those issued earlier in Mahatma Gandhi Series. All notes in the denomination of Rs.50 issued by the Reserve Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/4
