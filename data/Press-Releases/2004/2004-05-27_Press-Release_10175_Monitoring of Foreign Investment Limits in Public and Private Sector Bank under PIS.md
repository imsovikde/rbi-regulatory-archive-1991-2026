# Monitoring of Foreign Investment Limits in Public and Private Sector Bank under PIS

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 27, 2004 Monitoring of Foreign Investment Limits in Public and Private Sector Bank under PIS The Reserve Bank of India has notified on May 27, 2004 that the percentage of foreign holdings under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the following banks have gone below 18 percent and 72 per cent, respectively of their paid up capital. 1. Oriental Bank of Commerce 2. Centurion Bank The Reserve Bank has further notified that it will be in order for Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) to purchase the shares of the banks in the secondary market under the PIS subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines in this regard.
