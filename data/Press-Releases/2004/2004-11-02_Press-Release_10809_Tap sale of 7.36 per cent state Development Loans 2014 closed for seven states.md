# Tap sale of 7.36 per cent state Development Loans 2014 closed for seven states

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 2, 2004 Tap sale of 7.36 per cent state Development Loans 2014 closed for seven states The sale 'on tap' of 7.36 per cent ten years State Development Loans, 2014 has closed in respect of seven States, i.e. Goa, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal on receipt of the respective target amounts.  Amounts of allotment in respect of these states are being advised to the investors concerned. The sale on tap in respect of loans for remaining eighteen States, viz., Andra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh,  Assam, Bihar, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagalanad, Orissa, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and Uttaranchal will be kept open on November 3, 2004 to collect the balance target amount. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai Office. Applications will be received at these offices during banking hours while the tap sale is open.  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the tap sale during the banking hours on November 3, 2004 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on November 4, 2004 in respect of subscriptions received / allotted both on November 2 and November 3, 2004. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/473
