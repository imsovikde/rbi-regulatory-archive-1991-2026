## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.5300 on April 07, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 06, 2004) were Rs.43.7800 and Rs.52.6100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
