# Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of M/s. Perfect Holdings Limited, Surendranagar

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in December 20, 2004 Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of M/s. Perfect Holdings Limited, Surendranagar The Reserve Bank of India, has on December 17, 2004 cancelled the certificate of registration issued to M/s. Perfect Holdings Ltd., having its registered office at 4/2, Yogi Shopping Centre, Main Road, Surendranagar-363 001 for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Under powers conferred by Section 45IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Reserve Bank can cancel the registration certificate of non-banking financial company. The business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/640
