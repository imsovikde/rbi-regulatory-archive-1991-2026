# Bank of Baroda Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 27, 2004 Bank of Baroda Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on July 27, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Bank of Baroda should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through Stock Exchange/s in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Bank of Baroda  has reached the trigger limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/109
