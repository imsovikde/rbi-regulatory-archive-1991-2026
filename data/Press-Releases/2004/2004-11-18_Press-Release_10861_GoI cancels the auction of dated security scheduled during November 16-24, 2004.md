# GoI cancels the auction of dated security scheduled during November 16-24, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 18, 2004 GoI cancels the auction of dated security scheduled during November 16-24, 2004 Taking into account all relevant factors, the Government of India, in consultation with the Reserve Bank of India, has decided to cancel the auction of dated security for Rs. 5,000 crore to be held during November 16-24, 2004. It may be recalled that as per the indicative calendar for issuance of Government securities issued on September 20, 2004, the Government of India had scheduled one auction of dated security for Rs. 5,000 crore during the period November 16-24, 2004. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-05/527
