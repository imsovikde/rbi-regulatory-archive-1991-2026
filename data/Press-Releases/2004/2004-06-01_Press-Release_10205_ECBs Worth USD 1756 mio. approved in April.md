# ECBs Worth USD 1756 mio. approved in April

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in June 1, 2004 ECBs Worth USD 1756 mio. approved in April The Reserve Bank of India today released, on its website, the data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) (both under the Automatic Route and the Approval Route) for April  2004. According to the data, a total of 73 proposals for ECBs went through the automatic route. The amount raised through these proposals totalled USD 1356 million. Under the approval route, two proposals, viz., Exim Bank and Power Finance Corporation were approved for a maturity period of five years each. The total amount under the approval route was USD 400 million. The total amount of External Commercial borrowings for the month of April 2004 was USD 1756 million. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1409
