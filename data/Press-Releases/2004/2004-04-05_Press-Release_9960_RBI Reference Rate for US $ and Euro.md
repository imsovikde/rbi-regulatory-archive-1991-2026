## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.6700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.7800 on April 05, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 02, 2004) were Rs.43.7700 and Rs.53.9600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
