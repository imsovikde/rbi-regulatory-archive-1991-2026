# Investment by NRIs/PIOs-Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 19, 2004 Investment by NRIs/PIOs- Monitoring of Overall Ceiling The Reserve Bank of India on May 19, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of shares of the following companies, should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of NRIs/PIOs under PIS Scheme. The Reserve Bank has stated that the NRIs/PIOs holdings of the following companies have reached the overall ceiling of 10 per cent of its paid up capital. i) Chandraprabhu Housing Ltd. ii) Coxswain Technology Ltd. (Kaveri Biotech Ltd.) iii) Dev Sugars Ltd. iv) Dharendra Industries LTD. v) Fintech Communications vi) Rama Phosphates Ltd. vii) SquareD Biotech Ltd.
