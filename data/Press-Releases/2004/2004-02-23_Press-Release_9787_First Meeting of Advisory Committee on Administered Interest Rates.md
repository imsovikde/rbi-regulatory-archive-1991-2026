## February 23, 2004

## First Meeting of

## Advisory Committee on Administered Interest Rates

The first meeting of the Advisory Committee on Administered Interest Rates was held in the Reserve Bank of India today under the Chairmanship of Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Deputy Governor. The members of the Committee who attended the meeting were Dr. Ashok Lahiri, Chief Economic Advisor, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, Shri D. Swarup, Secretary (Expenditure & Budget), Ministry of Finance, Government of India, Dr. Suman Bery, Director General, National Council for Applied Economic Research and Prof. Mihir Rakshit, Director, Monetary Research Project, Investment Information and Credit Rating Agency of India.

The Committee reviewed the progress of implementation of the recommendations of the earlier Expert Committee on Administered Interest Rates. The Committee noted that considerable progress has been made in the implementation of the earlier Committee's recommendations which were accepted in principle by the Government. As per the recommendation of the earlier Committee, now the entire proceeds of small savings are being transferred to the State Governments. Interest rates on small savings have increasingly been aligned to market interest rates.

The Committee decided to deliberate on rationalisation of the number of small savings instruments offered by the Government at present and on tax treatment on such schemes with a view to coming out with a roadmap.

The Committee will meet again to continue its deliberations.
