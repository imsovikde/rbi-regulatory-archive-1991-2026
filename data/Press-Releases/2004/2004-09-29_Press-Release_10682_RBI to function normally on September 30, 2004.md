# RBI to function normally on September 30, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA RBI On Internet: http://www.rbi.org.in Email: helpprd@rbi.org.in September 29, 2004 RBI to function normally on September 30, 2004 The Reserve Bank of India has stated that its offices in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will conduct normal business on Thursday, September 30, 2004. Commercial banks and co-operative banks will, however, remain closed for public on that day on account of half-yearly closing of their accounts. There will, therefore, be no clearing on that day at Brihanmumbai Bankers' Clearing House, National Clearing Centres at Nariman Point and Bandra Kurla Complex and SBI MiCR Cheque Processing Centre, Worli. The settlement of MICR cheques presented on September 29 will be done on Friday, October 1, 2004. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/350
