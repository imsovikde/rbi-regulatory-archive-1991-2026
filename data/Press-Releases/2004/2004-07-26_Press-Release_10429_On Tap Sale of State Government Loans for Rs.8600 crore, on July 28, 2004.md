# On Tap Sale of State Government Loans for Rs.8600 crore, on July 28, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 26, 2004 On Tap Sale of State Government Loans for Rs.8600 crore, on July 28, 2004 Chhattisgarh will not be participating in the tap sale for the State Development Loans to be floated on July 28, 2004. Thus, the target amount will be reduced to about Rs.8500 crore. Other terms and conditions of the tap sale will remain same. It was announced earlier that 20 State Governments will be raising market loans for about Rs.8600 crore with coupon rate of 6.35 per cent per annum for a tenor of nine years on July 28, 2004 with payment being made on July 30, 2004. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/104
