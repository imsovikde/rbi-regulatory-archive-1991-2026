## February 10, 2004

## Tap Sale of State Government Loans

Eighteen State Governments have offered to sell 5.85 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 having maturity of eleven years aggregating about Rs.4300 crore on February 19, 2004 by way of tap sale. Of this, nine States, viz., Chattisgarh, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal will be raising Rs.3500 crore as additional market borrowings and other nine States, viz., Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura and Uttar Pradesh will be raising about Rs.800 crore under the Debt Swap Scheme. The sale will be held at the respective regional offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all the 18 States) on February 19, 2004. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after close of banking hours on or after February 19, 2004 having regard to the subscriptions received. The payment by the successful bidders will be made on February 21, 2004.

Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Office at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the aforesaid offices during banking hours while the sale is open.
