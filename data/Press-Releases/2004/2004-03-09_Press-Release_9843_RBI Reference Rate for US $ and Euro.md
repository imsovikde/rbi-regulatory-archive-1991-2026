## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.2700 on March 09, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 08, 2004) were Rs.45.2500 and Rs.56.0400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
