Reserve Bank Cancels Certificate of Registration of

M/s. Sandeep Traders and Investments Ltd., Ahmedabad

The Reserve Bank of India, has on December 22, 2004 cancelled the certificate of

registration issued to M/s Sandeep Traders and Investments Limited, having its registered

business of a non-banking financial institution.

business of a non-banking financial institution is defined in clause (a) of Section 45lA of the

Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
