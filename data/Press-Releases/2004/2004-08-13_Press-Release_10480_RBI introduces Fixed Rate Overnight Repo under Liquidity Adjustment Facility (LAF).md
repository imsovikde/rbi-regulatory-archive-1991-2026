# RBI introduces Fixed Rate Overnight Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in August 13, 2004 RBI introduces Fixed Rate Overnight Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) While introducing the revised scheme of Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) with effect from March 29, 2004, it was indicated that the Reserve Bank will continue to have the discretion to conduct overnight Repo or longer term Repo auctions at fixed rate or at variable rates depending on market conditions and other relevant factors. On an assessment of the current situation, it has been decided, until further notice, to introduce overnight fixed rate Repo at 4.50 per cent under LAF with effect from August 16, 2004 in addition to the existing overnight fixed rate Reverse Repo at 6.00 per cent.  The current practice of 7-day Repo and 14-day Repo will also continue. The time limit for submission of bids will continue to be 10.30 a.m. as now.  The auction results would be announced by 12 noon as at present.  The bids will continue to be submitted electronically in the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS).  The other operational guidelines and terms and conditions of the auction under the LAF would be applicable as at present. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/174
