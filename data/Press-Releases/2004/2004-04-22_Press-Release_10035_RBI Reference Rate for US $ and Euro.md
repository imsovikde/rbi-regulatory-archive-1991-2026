## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.0400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.0200 on April 22, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 21, 2004) were Rs.44.0000 and Rs.52.0500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
