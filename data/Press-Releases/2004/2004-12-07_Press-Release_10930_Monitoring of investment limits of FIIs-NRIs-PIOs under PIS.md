# Monitoring of investment limits of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in 1 ‚ December 07, 2004 Monitoring of investment limits of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 07, 2004 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares of Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd., through the secondary markets in  India.  The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the holdings of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs in the company have gone down below 48 per cent limit of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/593
