# Defaulters' lists now on CIBIL website

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 20, 2004 Defaulters' lists now on CIBIL website The Reserve Bank of India has entrusted the work of publishing the lists of defaulters (suit filed accounts) of Rs. 1 crore and above, as also, willful defaulters (suit filed accounts) of Rs.25 lakh and above, to the Credit Information Bureau (India) Ltd. (CIBIL). CIBIL, and not the Reserve Bank, will disseminate these as on March 31, 2003 and onwards. The lists will, therefore, be displayed on the CIBIL website (www.cibil.com) and not on the Reserve Bank website. Correspondence, if any, relating to both the defaulters' list may, in future, be addressed to CIBIL/concerned banks/financial institutions. The Reserve Bank of India will, however, continue to deal with the data relating to non-suit filed accounts of the defaulters lists of Rs.1 crore and above and willful defaulters of Rs.25 lakh and above, which are disseminated only to banks/financial institutions for their confidential use. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2003-2004/1360
