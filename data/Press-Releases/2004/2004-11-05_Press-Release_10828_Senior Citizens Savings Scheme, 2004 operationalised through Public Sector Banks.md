# Senior Citizens Savings Scheme, 2004 operationalised through Public Sector Banks

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 5, 2004 Senior Citizens Savings Scheme, 2004 operationalised through Public Sector Banks The Government of India had introduced Senior Citizens Savings Scheme, 2004 (SCSS) with effect from August 2, 2004 through designated Post Offices. The Government has now decided to operate the scheme also through designated branches of 23 public sector banks (presently operating Public Provident Fund Scheme, 1968) with effect from November 1, 2004. The Reserve Bank of India has suitably advised the agency banks, viz., State Bank of India, Associate banks, 14 banks nationalised in 1969 and Corporation Bank in this regard. Members of public may contact the officials of designated branches of public sector banks for investment in the scheme/guidance. For convenience of the senior citizens/investors, the details/general rules, etc., of the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme, 2004 have been placed on the RBI web site (http://www.goisb.rbi.org.in). P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/493
