## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.6400 on March 10, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 09, 2004) were Rs.45.2100 and Rs.56.2700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
