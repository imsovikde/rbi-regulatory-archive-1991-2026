# RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

January 15, 2004 **RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro**

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.5200 on January 15,

2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 14, 2004) were Rs.45.4100 and Rs.57.8100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
## Currency

|Date|1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|January 14, 2004|83.7951|42.76|
|January 15, 2004|83.2664|42.7700|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
