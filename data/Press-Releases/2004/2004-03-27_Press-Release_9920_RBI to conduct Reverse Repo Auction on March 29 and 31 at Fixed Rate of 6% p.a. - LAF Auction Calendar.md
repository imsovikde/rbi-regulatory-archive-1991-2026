## March 27, 2004

## RBI to conduct Reverse Repo Auction on March 29 and 31 at

## Fixed Rate of 6% p.a. - LAF Auction Calendar

The Reserve Bank of India has clarified that the reverse repo auctions to be conducted on March 29, 2004 and March 31, 2004 will be at fixed rate of 6% p.a. As such, there will be no variable reverse repo auction on March 29 and 31, 2004.

|Date of Auction|Date of Reversal|Period (Days)|Rate of Interest (% p.a.)|
|---|---|---|---|
|March 29, 2004|March 31, 2004|2|6 (fixed)|
|March 31, 2004|April 1, 2004|1|6 (fixed)|

It may be recalled that on March 25, 2004, the Reserve Bank had notified the revised Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) Scheme. The revised scheme is to be effective from March 29,

2004. Calendar for LAF auctions was also notified on March 25, 2004. The detailed operational instructions for Revised LAF Scheme are contained in RBI circular 115/2004 dated March 25, 2004 which is available on the RBI website.
