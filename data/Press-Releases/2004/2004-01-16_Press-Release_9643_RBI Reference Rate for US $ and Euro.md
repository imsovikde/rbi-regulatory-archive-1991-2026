# RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## January 16,2004

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.2100 on January 16,2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 15,2004) were Rs.45.4300 and Rs.57.5200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date||Currency|
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|January 15,2004|83.2664|42.77|
|January 16,2004|82.8894|42.8300|
