# RBI releases Payment Vision Document for Public Comment

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in December 25, 2004 RBI releases Payment Vision Document for Public Comment The Reserve Bank of India today released a vision document for Payment Systems in India on its website. Feedback/comments may be mailed to the Reserve Bank at psvision@rbi.org.in or faxed to the Chief general manager-in-charge, Department of Information Technology, Reserve Bank of India at 022 2269 1557.
