# Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in September 15, 2004 Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies The Reserve Bank of India has notified on September 15, 2004 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of The Jammu & Kashmir Bank Ltd. through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 33 per cent limits of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolution to this effect at its Board of Directors' meeting and a special resolution in its General Body Meeting. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/300
