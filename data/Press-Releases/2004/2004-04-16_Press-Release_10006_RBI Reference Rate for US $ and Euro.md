## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.8900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6300 on April 16, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 15, 2004) were Rs.43.9000 and Rs.52.4700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
