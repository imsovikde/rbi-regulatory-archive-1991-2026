## February 10, 2004

## Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India on February 10, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of The Associated Cement Company Ltd., (ACC Ltd.) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that The Associated Cement Company Ltd., has reached the overall ceiling of 24 per cent of its paid up equity capital to be held by all registered FIIs.
