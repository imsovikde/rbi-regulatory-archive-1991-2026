## Guidelines for acknowledgement of transfer/allotment of shares in private banks

RBI has formulated guidelines for acknowledgement of transfer/ allotment of shares in private banks. These guidelines seek to make transparent the broad criteria, which will be followed for issuing such acknowledgements to avoid uncertainty for potential investors. The intensity and comprehensiveness of the due diligence processes at various thresholds of shareholding in banks have been evolved to protect depositors’ interest as also to ensure soundness of the financial system.

The guidelines are available on our website www.rbi.org.in
