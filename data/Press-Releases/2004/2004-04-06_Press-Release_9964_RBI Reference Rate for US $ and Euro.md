## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.7800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6100 on April 06, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 05, 2004) were Rs.43.6700 and Rs.52.7800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
