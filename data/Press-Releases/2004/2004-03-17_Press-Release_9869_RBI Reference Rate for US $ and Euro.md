## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.5000 on March 17, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 16, 2004) were Rs.45.2400 and Rs.55.5700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
