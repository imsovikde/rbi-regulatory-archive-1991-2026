## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.6700 on February 10, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 09, 2004) were Rs.45.2700 and Rs.57.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
