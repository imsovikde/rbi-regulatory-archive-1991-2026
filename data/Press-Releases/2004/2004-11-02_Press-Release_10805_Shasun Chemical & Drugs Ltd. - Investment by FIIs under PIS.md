# Shasun Chemical & Drugs Ltd. - Investment by FIIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 2, 2004 Shasun Chemical & Drugs Ltd. - Investment by FIIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on November 2, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Shasun Chemical & Drugs Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Shasun Chemical & Drugs Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release :2004-2005/470
