# Aftek Infosys Ltd. - Investment by FIIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in November 1, 2004 Aftek Infosys Ltd. - Investment by FIIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on November 1, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Aftek Infosys Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Aftek Infosys Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/465
