## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.5700 on March 16, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 15, 2004) were Rs.45.2500 and Rs.55.3800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
