## <u>RBI asks Banks to increase Margins for Advances against Shares</u>

The Reserve Bank of India, on a review, has today decided to increase, with immediate effect, margins on all advances against shares/IPOs/issue of guarantees from 40% to 50%. It has also advised banks to raise the minimum cash margin of 20% to 25%. It may be recalled that the margin in respect of advance against shares/financing of IPOs/issue of guarantees was reduced from the level of 50% to 40% and the minimum cash margin from 25% to 20% in May 2004.
