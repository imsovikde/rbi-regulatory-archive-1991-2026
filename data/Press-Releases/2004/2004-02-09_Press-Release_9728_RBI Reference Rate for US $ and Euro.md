## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.6300 on February 9, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 06, 2004) were Rs.45.2800 and Rs.56.7900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
