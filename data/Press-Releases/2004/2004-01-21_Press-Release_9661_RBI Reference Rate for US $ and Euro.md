## January 21, 2004

## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.1500 on January 21, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 20, 2004) were Rs.45.4200 and Rs.56.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
