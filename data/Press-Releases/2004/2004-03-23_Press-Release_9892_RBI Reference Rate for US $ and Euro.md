## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.5100 on March 23, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 22, 2004) were Rs.45.1500 and Rs.55.3800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
