# On Tap Sale of State Development Loans for Rs. 6200 crore, on November 2, 2004

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in October 27, 2004 On Tap Sale of State Development Loans for Rs. 6200 crore, on November 2, 2004 The Governments of 25 States have offered to sell 7.36% State Development Loan, 2014 having maturity of ten years, aggregating about Rs. 6200 crore, on November 2, 2004 by way of tap sale under the normal market borrowing programme. The States which are not participating in the tap sale are Chhattisgarh, Kerala and Punjab. The sale will be held at the respective regional offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all the 25 States).  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the banking hours on or after November 2, 2004 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. The date of payment will be November 4, 2004. Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Offices at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the Reserve Bank offices during banking hours while the sale is open. Suraj Prakash Manager Press Release:2004-05/448
