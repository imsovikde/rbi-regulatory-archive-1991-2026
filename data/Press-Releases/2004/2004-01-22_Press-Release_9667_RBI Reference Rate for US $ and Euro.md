## January 22, 2004

## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.4000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.4900 on January 22, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 21, 2004) were Rs.45.3900 and Rs.57.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
