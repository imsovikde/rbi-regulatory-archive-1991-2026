## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.7600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6000 on April 13, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 12, 2004) were Rs.43.6500 and Rs.52.6500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
