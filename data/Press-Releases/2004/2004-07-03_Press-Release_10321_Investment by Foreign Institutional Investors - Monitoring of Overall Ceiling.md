# Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors - Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 3, 2004 Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors - Monitoring of Overall Ceiling The Reserve Bank of India on July 3, 2004 has notified that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in Arvind Mills Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that no further purchases of shares of Arvind Mills Ltd., should, therefore, be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/17
