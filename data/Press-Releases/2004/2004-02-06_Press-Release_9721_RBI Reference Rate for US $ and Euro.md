## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.56.7900 on February 6, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 05, 2004) were Rs.45.2700 and Rs.56.7400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
