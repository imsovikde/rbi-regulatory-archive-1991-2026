## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.3200 on March 05, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 04, 2004) were Rs.45.3000 and Rs.55.2400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
