## January 28, 2004

## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.57.3100 on January 28, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 27, 2004) were Rs.45.3600 and Rs.56.5700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
