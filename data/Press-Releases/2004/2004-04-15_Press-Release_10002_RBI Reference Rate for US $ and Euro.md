## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4700 on April 15, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 13, 2004) were Rs.43.7600 and Rs.52.6000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
