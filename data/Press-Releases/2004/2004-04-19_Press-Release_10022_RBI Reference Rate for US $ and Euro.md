## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.8600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8100 on April 19, 2004. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 16, 2004) were Rs.43.8900 and Rs.52.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
