## March 3, 2004

## Television Eighteen India Ltd. : Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on March 3, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of **Television Eighteen India Ltd.,** should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Person Indian Origins (PIOs) through Stock Exchange/s in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Television Eighteen-India Ltd. has reached the trigger limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
