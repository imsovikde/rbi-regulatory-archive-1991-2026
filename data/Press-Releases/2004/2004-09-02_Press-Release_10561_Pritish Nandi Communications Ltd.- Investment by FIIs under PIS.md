# Pritish Nandi Communications Ltd.: Investment by FIIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in September 2, 2004 Pritish Nandi Communications Ltd.: Investment by FIIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on September 2, 2004 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Pritish Nandi Communications Ltd. should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary and secondary markets in India without prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Pritish Nandi Communications Ltd. has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/250
