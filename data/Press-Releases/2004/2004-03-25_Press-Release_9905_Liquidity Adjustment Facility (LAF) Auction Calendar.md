# Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) Auction Calendar

The Reserve Bank of India announces the calendar for LAF consequent upon the introduction of the revised LAF Scheme, which comes into effect from March 29, 2004. A separate Press Release and circular containing details of the revised scheme have already been issued in this regard today. Accordingly, the following calendars are being notified for the information of the market participants.
