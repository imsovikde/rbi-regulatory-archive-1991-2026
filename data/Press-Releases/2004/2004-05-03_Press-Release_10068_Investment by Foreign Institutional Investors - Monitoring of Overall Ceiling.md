May 3, 2004

Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors -

Monitoring of Overall Ceiling

The Reserve Bank of India on May 3, 2004 has notified that no further

purchases of shares of IClCl Bank Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign

Institutional Investors (Flls) without the prior approval of the Reserve Bank of

India. The Reserve Bank has stated that IClCl Bank Ltd., has reached the

trigger limit of 47 per cent of its paid up capital.
