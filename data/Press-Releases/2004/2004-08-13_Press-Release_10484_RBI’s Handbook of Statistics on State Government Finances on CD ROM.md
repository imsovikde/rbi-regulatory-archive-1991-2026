# RBI’s Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances on CD ROM

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in August 13, 2004 RBI’s Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances on CD ROM The Reserve Bank of India today released a CD ROM on Handbook of Statistics on State Government Finances, as a part of its persistent endeavour towards better data dissemination. The Reserve Bank had released the print and the web versions of the Handbook on July 2, 2004. The Handbook covers State-wise data from 1980-81 onwards on major fiscal indicators and State-wise detailed data from 1990-91 onwards on budgetary transactions in the revenue and capital accounts. The CD ROM incorporates intelligent search features. The search features can be used, among other things, to obtain data in MS-EXCEL format by selecting the desired budgetary variable(s), State Government(s) and year(s).
