## Investment by FIIs/ NRIs/ PIOs

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on February 17, 2004 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origins (PIOs) can now purchase equity shares of Television Eighteen India Ltd., through stock exchanges in India as the aggregate net purchase of equity shares of Television Eighteen Indian Ltd. has gone down below 24 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
