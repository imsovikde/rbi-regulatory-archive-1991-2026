During April 1996 to February 1997, the RBI's direct support to Government in primary issues of dated securities and treasury bills amounted to around Rs.12,000 crore whereas the net RBI credit to Central Government was only of the order of Rs.5,000 crore. This is primarily on account of the fact that RBI undertook active open market operations. The repos operations by RBI similarly resulted in sales of about Rs.1,500 crore at the end of this period.
