InvestmentbyForeignInstitutional Investors

The overall ceiling of 30 per cent of paid up equity capital permitted to be held by all registered Foreign

Institutional Investors together withpurchases madeby all NRls and OCBs under thePortfolioInvestment

Scheme has already been reached in the case of Infosys Technologies Limited. No further purchases of

shares of this company should therefore,be made throgh stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign

InstitutionalInvestors/NRls/OCBs

(Alpana Killawala)

General Manager
