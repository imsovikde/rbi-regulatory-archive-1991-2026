Portfolio Investment by Flls/NRls and OCBs

Infosys Technologies Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has withdrawn the restrictions on further purchase of shares

of Infosys Technologies Ltd., Bangalore on behalf foreign institutional investors (Flls)/

non-residents of Indian nationality/origin (NRls) and overseas corporate bodies owned to

the percentage of investments by Flls/NRls/OCBs to the paid up equity capital of the

company has come down below 30 per cent. However, as the percentage of

investments of Flls/NRls/OCBs in the company has reached 26 per cent of its paid up

capital, further purchases of equity shares of the company on behalf of Flls/NRls/OCBs

will be allowed only after obtaining the prior clearance from the Reserve Bank of India.

Satyam Computer Services Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has also permitted purchase of equity shares and convertible

debentures of Satyam Computer Services Ltd. under the portfolio investment scheme

on behalf of Flls/NRls/OCBs subject to certain conditions. This follows passing of a

resolution by the company agreeing for the purchase of its equity shares and convertible

debentures by Flls/NRls/OCBs up to 30 per cent of its issued and paid up capital

through stock exchanges under the portfolio investment scheme. This was done in

1997 raising the portfolio investment ceiling for all Flls/NRls/OCBs in Indian companies

from 24 per cent to 30 per cent of their issued and paid-up capital. In May 1996 the

Reserve Bank had prohibited purchase of equity shares of this company on behalf of

Flls/NRls/OCBs from the secondary market, as the then overall ceiling of 24 per cent of

its issued and paid up capital had been reached.

(Alpana Killawala)

General Manager
