In terms of the extant guidelines, the amount of investment exceeding Us$ 15

million has to be raised through GDRs. This condition will not apply to Rupee

investments in Nepal.

il.

At present, an Indian promoter company is required to repatriate all its

entitlements by way of dividend, royalty, technical know-how fees, etc., in free

foreign exchange. The returns on Rupee investments in Nepal shall, however,

now be allowed to be repatriated in Indian Rupees.

All other existing terms and conditions applicable to over seas investment in joint

ventures and subsidiaries will continue to apply to investments in Nepal.
