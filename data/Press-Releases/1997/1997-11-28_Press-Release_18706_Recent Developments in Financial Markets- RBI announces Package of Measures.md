## Interest Rate on Post Shipment Rupee Export Credit

It has been decided that with effect from December 15, 1997, the interest rate of 15 per cent per annum would be applicable from the date of advance. Exporters are expected to take advan- tage of the time gap available upto December 15 by expediting realisation of their export proceeds.

It may be recalled that the interest rate on post shipment rupee export credit on usance bills for total period (comprising usance period of export bills transit period as specified by Foreign Exchange Dealers' Association of India and grace period wherever applicable) beyond 90 days and upto six months from the date of shipment was increased to 15.0 per cent per annum on November 26, 1997. It was also indicated that this rate would be applicable only for the period beyond 90 days, and not from the date of advance.

## Cancellations of Forward Contracts

It has been decided to reintroduce the system of monitoring the cancellations of forward contracts beyond US $ 500,000. Information on such cancellations from November 1, 1997 is imme- diately being called for from the banks. The recent scrutiny of banks by the Reserve Bank inspectors has revealed that of late, there has been a spurt in cancellations and rebooking of forward contracts. The Reserve Bank is closely monitoring the situation and will take appropriate action wherever warranted.
