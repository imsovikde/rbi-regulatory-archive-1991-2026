Portfolio Investment by Foreign Investors - Purchase up to 30 per cent

Apropos the Government of India raising the ceiling for portfolio investment for foreign

investors in Indian companiesfrom 24 per cent to 30per cent, the Reserve Bank of India

has allowed purchase of equity shares and convertible debentures of Infosys

Technologies Limited up to 30% of its paid up capital.

It may be recalled that the Government of India by its press release No.14/11/96-NRl

dated April 4, 1997 raised the portfolio investment ceiling for all foreign institutional

investors (Flls)/non-residents of Indian nationality/origin (NRls) and overseas corporate

the board of directors of the concerned company and a special resolution passed by the

general body of that company.

The Reserve Bank of India monitors the foreign investments in companies on a daily

basis. For effective monitoring of the foreign investment ceiling limits in Indian

companies, the Re serve Bank alerts all the designated bank branches at a cut-off point

limit lower than the ceiling limit after which the desig nated bank branches are required

toobtaintheReserveBankofIndia'spriorclearancebeforepurchasingfurthershares

on behalf of their Fll/NRl/OCB clients. Once foreign investments in a company reach the

ceiling limit is reached.While the alert limit is 20 per cent for ceiling limits of 24 per cent

foreign investments, the alert limit is 26 per cent for the revised foreign investment limit

of 30 per cent.

The present investment limits for an individual Fll, NRl or OCB remain unaltered. That is,

investment made on repatriation basis by any single NRl/OCB in equity shares and

debentures issued by the company. The purchase of equity shares by a single Fll/SEBI

approved sub-account of a registered Fll in the company should not exceed 10 per cent

group to which the applicant belongs are counted as the holdings of a single Fll.

1. Infosys Technologies Limited
2. Mastek Ltd.
**Companies in which 24 per cent limit has been reached and no fresh purchases are allowed**

1. Shivalik Projects Ltd.
2. Zee Telefilms Ltd.
3. Housing Development Finance Corporation Ltd.
4. Satyam Computers Services Ltd.
5. Polyplex Corporation Ltd.
6. Nagarjuna Constructions Ltd.
7. Patheja Forgings Auto Parts Manufacturing Ltd.
8. NIIT Ltd.
9. Max India Ltd.
The Reserve Bank has, on June 14, 1997, allowed Infosys Technologies Ltd., which had earlier reached the limit of 24 per cent, to raise the aggregate limit to 30 per cent. **Companies in which 20 per cent limit has been reached and require prior RBI clearance for further purchases**

1. Essel Packaging Ltd.
2. State Bank of India
3. Carrier Aircon Ltd.
In the case of State Bank of India, though only 20 per cent limit has been reached, no further purchases will be allowed in view of the condition stipulated by the Government of India while approving their GDR issue.
