Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors-Monitoring ceiling

The Reserve Bank of India has withdrawn the restrictions placed on further purchase of shares of M/s. Infosys

further purchases of equity shares of the company on behalf of Flls/NRls/OCBs will be allowed only after

obtaining prior clearance from the Reserve Bank of India. The holdings of Flls/NRls/OCBs in this company have

placed the restriction on further purchases of equity shares of Infosys Technologies Ltd. by Flls/NRls/OCBs/ in

August, 1997.

(Alpana Killawala)

General Manager
