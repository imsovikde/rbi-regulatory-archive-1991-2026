ClarificationsonRBlGuidelinesforPrimaryDealersandSatelliteDealers

The Reserve Bank of India (RBl) announced the "Guidelines for Primary Dealers in Government Securities Market" on

March 29, 1995 with the last date set for receiving the applications as April 30, 1995. Based on the applications received,

undertaking primary dealership. STCl, DFHI and I-SEC and the bank subsidiaries set up later, viz., ^SBI Gilts', ^PNB Gilts'

and ^Gilt Securities Trading Corporation of India' were given primary dealer authorisation in two stages in February 1996

and May 1996.

TheRBl announced the"GuidelinesforSatelliteDealers inGovernmentSecuritiesMarket"onDecember31,1996with the

last date set for receiving the applications as January 31, 1997. The applications received for satellite dealership are under

variousstages ofdisposal.

The RBl now clarifies that the receipt of application and approval for both primary dealership and satellite dealership will be

dealership/satellitedealership,asthecasemaybe.

The RBl also clarifies that in the case of scheduled commercial banks and all India financial institutions intending to set up

India financial institution may proceed to set up the subsidiary, which in turn will be allowed to directly apply to the RBl for

grant of authorisation.
