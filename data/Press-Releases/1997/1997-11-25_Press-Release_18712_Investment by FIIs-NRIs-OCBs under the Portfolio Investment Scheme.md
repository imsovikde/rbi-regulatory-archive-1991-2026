Investment byFlls/NRls/OcBs under thePortfolioInvestment Scheme

No further purchases of equity shares of Satyam Computer Services Ltd. should be made on

behalf of foreign institutional investors (Flls)/Non-Resident Indians (NRls)/Overseas Corporate

Bank of India. The aggregate net purchases of equity shares of this company by Flls in the

Clearance will be given by the Reserve Bank on first-come-first-served basis after taking into

account the purchasesproposed tobe madeby all designated banks till the 30per cent limit is

reached.
