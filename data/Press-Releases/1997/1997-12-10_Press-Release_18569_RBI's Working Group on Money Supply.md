RBl's Working Group on Money Supply

It has been decided to set up a Working Group on ^Money Supply : Analytics and Methodology of Compilation' to re-

examine the analytical aspects of monetary survey in view of the changing dimensions and depth of the financial sector with

theintroductionoffinancialsectorreforms.

The terms of reference of the Working Group are :

from the relevant economic entities;

dissemination of data to the recommended system;

centralbanks;and

e.toconsider anyotherrelated matters.

The Working Group is expected to submit its report within three months.

Chaired by Dr.Y.V.Reddy, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India, the Working Group will have Dr.A.Vasudevan,

Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India as its Vice-Chairman. Prof. Ganti Subrahmanyam, National Institute of Bank

Management, Prof. Romar Correa,University of Bombay and Dr.B.K.Bhoi, Director,Monetary Modelling Unit, Department

of Economic Analysis and Policy, Reserve Bank of India will be the members of the Working Group. Shri Deepak Mohanty,

Director, Division of Money and Banking, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy, Reserve Bank of India will be the

Member-Secretary.

The Chief General Managers of the relevant departments of the Reserve Bank would be associated with the Working

Group as special invitees.
