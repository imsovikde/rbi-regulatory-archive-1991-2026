## July 18, 2019

## RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under

## LAF on July 19, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 19, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
