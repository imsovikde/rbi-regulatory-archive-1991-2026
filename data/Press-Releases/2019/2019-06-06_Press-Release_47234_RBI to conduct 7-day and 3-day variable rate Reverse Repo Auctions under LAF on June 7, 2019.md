## June 06, 2019

**RBI to conduct 7-day and 3-day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on June 7, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on June 7, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
