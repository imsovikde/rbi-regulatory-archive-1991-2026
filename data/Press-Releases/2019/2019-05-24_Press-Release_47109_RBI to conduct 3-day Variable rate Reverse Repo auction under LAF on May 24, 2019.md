May 24, 2019 **RBI to conduct 3-day Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on May 24, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on May 24, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|300|3|1.30 pm to 2.00 pm|May 27, 2019 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
