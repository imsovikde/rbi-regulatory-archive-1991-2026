## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.||Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|||(in ₹ Crore)|(days)||
|1|December 20, 2019 (Friday)|26,000|14|January 3, 2020 (Friday)|
|2|December 24, 2019 (Tuesday)|26,000|14|January 7, 2020 (Tuesday)|
|3|December 27, 2019 (Friday)|26,000|14|January 10, 2020 (Friday)|
|4|December 31, 2019 (Tuesday)|26,000|14|January 14, 2020 (Tuesday)|

**Date of Auction**

2. The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February</u> <u>13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
