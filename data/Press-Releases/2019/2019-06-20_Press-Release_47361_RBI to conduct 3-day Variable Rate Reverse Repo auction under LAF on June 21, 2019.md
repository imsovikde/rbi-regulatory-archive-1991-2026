## RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on June 21, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on June 21, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February</u> <u>13, 2014</u>.
