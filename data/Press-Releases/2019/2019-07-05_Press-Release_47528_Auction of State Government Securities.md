The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **July 09, 2019 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to 10% of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of 1% of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility (available on RBI website).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **July 09, 2019 (Tuesday)**. The non-competitive bids should be submitted
