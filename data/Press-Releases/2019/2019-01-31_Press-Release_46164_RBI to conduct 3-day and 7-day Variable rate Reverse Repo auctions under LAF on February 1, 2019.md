January 31, 2019 **RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on February 1, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on February 1, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
