## RBI to conduct 7-day Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on February 20, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on February 20, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
