June 17, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on June 17, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on June 17, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February</u> <u>13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|500|1|3.30 pm to 4.00 pm|June 18, 2019 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
