## LAF on November 4, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on November 4, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo <u>Auctions</u> issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified Amount Tenor** **Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(₹ crore) (day)** November 5, 2019 1 3,00,000 1 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
