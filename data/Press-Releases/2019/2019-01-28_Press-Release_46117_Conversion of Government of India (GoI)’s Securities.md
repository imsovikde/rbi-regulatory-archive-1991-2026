## January 28, 2019

## Conversion of Government of India (GoI)’s Securities

The Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has converted a security maturing in 2019-20, having a total face value of `9,876 crore, to a longer tenor security maturing in 2027-28. The transaction was conducted on January 25, 2019 with a scheduled commercial bank.
