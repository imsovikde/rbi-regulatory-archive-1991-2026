## RBI to conduct longer term Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions

The Reserve Bank monitors the system liquidity on an ongoing basis and conducts liquidity management operations based on an assessment of the evolving liquidity conditions and the requirements of the system.

Based on an assessment of the current and evolving liquidity conditions, RBI has decided to conduct two Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions as under:
