## Mint Street Memo No. 20: Impact of Prudential Regulations for Unrated

## Exposures on the Rating Behaviour of Large Borrowers

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the twentieth release under the series ‘<u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u> titled “<u>Impact of Prudential Regulations</u> <u>for Unrated Exposures on the Rating Behaviour of Large Borrowers</u>”.

The paper authored by Pallavi Chavan, Director and S.K. Ritadhi, Manager (Research) from Department of Banking Supervision, analyses the impact of prudential regulations on the rating behaviour of large borrowers.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
