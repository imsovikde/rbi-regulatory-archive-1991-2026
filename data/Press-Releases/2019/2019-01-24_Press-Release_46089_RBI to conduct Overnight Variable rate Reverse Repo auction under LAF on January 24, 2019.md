January 24, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on January 24, 2019** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on January 24, 2019, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
