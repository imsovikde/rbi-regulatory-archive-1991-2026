## April 1, 2019

## Auction of State Development Loans

It is advised that the first auction of State Development Loans for the financial year 2019-20 will not be held on April 02, 2019, as announced earlier vide our <u>press</u> <u>release dated March 29, 2019</u>. The date of the next auction will be announced in due course in consultation with the Governments concerned.
