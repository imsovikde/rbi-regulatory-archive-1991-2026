January 7, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on January 8, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on January 8, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
