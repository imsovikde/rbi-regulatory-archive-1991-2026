## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of January 2019

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of January 2019 are given in the following Table.

||Table: International Trade in Services||
|---|---|---|
|||(US$ Million)|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|October-2018|16,815|10,099|
|November-2018|16,677|10,109|
|December-2018|17,927|11,376|
|January-2019|17,745|11,027|
|Note : Data are provisional.|||

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
