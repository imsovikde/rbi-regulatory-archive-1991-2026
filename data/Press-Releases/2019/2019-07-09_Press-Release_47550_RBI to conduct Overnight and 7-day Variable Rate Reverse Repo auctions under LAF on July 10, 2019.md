## July 09, 2019

**RBI to conduct Overnight and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 10, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 10, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
