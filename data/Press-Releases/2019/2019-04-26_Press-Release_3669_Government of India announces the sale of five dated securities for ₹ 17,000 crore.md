April 26, 2019

**Government of India announces the sale of five dated securities for ₹ 17,000 crore**

Government of India (GoI) has announced the sale (issue/re-issue) of five dated securities as per the following details:

## Notified

## Sr Settlement

## Security Amount Auction Date

## No date

## (in ₹ crore)

1 7.00% GS 2021 3,000

2 7.27% GS 2026 3,000 May 03, 2019 May 06, 2019 3 GOI FRB 2031 5,000 (Friday) (Monday) 4 7.62% GS 2039 2,000

5 New GS 2059 4,000 **Total 17,000**

Subject to the limit of ₹ 17,000 Cr being total notified amount, GoI will have the option to retain additional subscription up to ₹ 1000 Cr each against any one or more of the above securities. The auction be <u>yield based</u> (for new securities) and <u>price based</u> (for other securities), **using multiple price method for both**. Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of the stocks will be allotted to eligible individuals and Institutions as per the <u>Scheme</u> <u>for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility</u> in the Auction of Government Securities.

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **May 03, 2019**. The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 11.30 a.m. and

12.00 noon and the competitive bids should be submitted between 11.30 a.m. and 12.30 pm. The result of the auctions will be announced on **May 03, 2019**. Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to the Public Debt Office (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22632527, 022- 22701299) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) before the auction timing ends. In case of technical difficulties, Core Banking Operations Team should be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-27595414, 022-27595666). For other auction related difficulties, IDMD auction team can be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22702431, 022-22705125).

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

The underwriting of the Government Securities under auctions by the ‘Primary Dealers’ will be as per the **“Revised Scheme of Underwriting Commitment and Liquidity** **Support”** announced by the Reserve Bank vide <u>circular RBI/2007-08/186 dated November</u> <u>14, 2007</u>. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by ‘Primary Dealers’ from 9.00 AM up to 9.45 AM on **May 03, 2019** (Friday) on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system.

The Stocks will be eligible for “When Issued” trading for a period commencing from **April 30, 2019 – May 03, 2019** in accordance with the guidelines on **‘When Issued** **transactions in Central Government Securities’** issued by the Reserve Bank of India vide <u>circular No. RBI/2018-19/25 dated July 24, 2018</u> as amended from time to time.
