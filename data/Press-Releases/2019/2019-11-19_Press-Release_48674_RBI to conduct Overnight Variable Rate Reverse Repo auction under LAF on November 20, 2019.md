November 19, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on November 20, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on November 20, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
