December 02, 2019 **Fifth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2019-20**

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet during December 3 to 5, 2019 for the Fifth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2019-20. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 11.45 AM on December 5, 2019.
