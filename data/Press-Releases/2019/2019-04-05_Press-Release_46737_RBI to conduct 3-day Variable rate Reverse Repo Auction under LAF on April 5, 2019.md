April 05, 2019 **RBI to conduct 3-day Variable rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on** **April 5, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on April 5, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|300|3|3.30 pm to 4.00 pm|April 8, 2019 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
