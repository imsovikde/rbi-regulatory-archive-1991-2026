March 13, 2019 **Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility** As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Notified Amount (in ₹ Billion)|Tenor (days)|
|---|---|
|240|14|
|240|14|
|245|14|
|245|14|

|No. Sl.|Date of Auction|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|
|1|March 15, 2019 (Friday)|March 29, 2019 (Friday)|
|2|March 19, 2019 (Tuesday)|April 2, 2019 (Tuesday)|
|3|March 22, 2019 (Friday)|April 5, 2019 (Friday)|
|4|March 26, 2019 (Tuesday)|April 9, 2019 (Tuesday)|

2. The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>
<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08,</u> <u>2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
3. Further, as announced vide our <u>Press Release dated March 13, 2019</u>, Standalone Primary Dealers would be eligible to participate in the auction to be conducted on March 26, 2019, along with other eligible participants, within the notified amount.
