December 04, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on December 5, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on December 5, 2019, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified** **Tenor** **Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(day)** **(₹ crore)** December 6, 2019 1 2,90,000 1 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Friday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
