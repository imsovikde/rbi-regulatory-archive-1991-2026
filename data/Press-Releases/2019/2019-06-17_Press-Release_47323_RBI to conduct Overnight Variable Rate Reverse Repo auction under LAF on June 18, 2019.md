June 17, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on June 18, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on June 18, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified Amount Tenor** **Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(**` **billion) (day)** June 19, 2019 1 700 1 1.30 pm to 2.00 pm (Wednesday)
