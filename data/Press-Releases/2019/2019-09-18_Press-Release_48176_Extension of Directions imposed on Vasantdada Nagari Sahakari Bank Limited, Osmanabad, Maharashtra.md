## September 18, 2019

## Extension of Directions imposed on Vasantdada Nagari Sahakari Bank Limited,

## Osmanabad, Maharashtra

Reserve Bank of India, in the public interest, had issued directions to **Vasantdada Nagari Sahakari Bank Ltd, Osmanabad, Maharashtra** in exercise of powers vested in it under sub-section (1) of Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (AACS) from the close of business on November 13, 2017 which were subsequently extended up to September 13, 2019. Reserve Bank of India has now further extended the directions for one month from September 14, 2019 to October 13, 2019. The Directions stipulate certain restrictions and / or ceiling on withdrawal / acceptance of deposits. The detailed Directions are displayed on the bank’s premises for interested members of public to peruse. Reserve Bank of India may consider modifications of the Directions depending upon the circumstances. The issue of Directions should not per se be construed as cancellation of banking license by the Reserve Bank of India. The bank will continue to undertake banking business with restrictions till its financial position improves.
