## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on February 14, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on February 14, 2019, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
