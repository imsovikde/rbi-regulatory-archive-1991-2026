## July 05, 2019

**RBI to conduct Overnight and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on July 8, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 8, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified Amount Tenor

## Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(**` **billion) (day)**

July 9, 2019 1 700 1 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Tuesday)

July 15, 2019 2 200 7 1:30 pm to 2:00 pm (Monday) July 9, 2019 3 200 1 3:30 pm to 4:00 pm (Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
