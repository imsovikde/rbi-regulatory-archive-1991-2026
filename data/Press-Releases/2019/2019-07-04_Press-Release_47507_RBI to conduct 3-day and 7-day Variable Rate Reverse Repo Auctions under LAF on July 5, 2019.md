## July 04, 2019

## RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under

## LAF on July 5, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on July 5, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
