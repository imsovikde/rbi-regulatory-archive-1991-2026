## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on March 13, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on March 13, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
