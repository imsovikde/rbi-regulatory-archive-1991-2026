## November 08, 2019

## Talk by Shri Vinod Rai on “Promoting Integrity in Public Sector Organisations”

The Reserve Bank of India celebrated the Vigilance Awareness Week this year with the theme “Integrity – A way of Life”. As part of the celebrations, various events were organised at central office and regional office levels.

As a sequel, the Reserve Bank of India today organised a talk on “Promoting Integrity in Public Sector Organizations” by Shri Vinod Rai, former CAG of India. In his introductory address, Governor, Shri Shaktikanta Das highlighted the commitment of the Reserve Bank of India to transparent, accountable and ethics driven internal governance.

Shri Vinod Rai, in his address said that promoting integrity entails developing and maintaining an organisational culture or environment that supports ethical behaviour. It involves making expectations about individual conduct clear and ensuring the public authority has robust systems, policies and processes in place to support ethical behaviour. Importantly, public authorities need to focus on both the ethical conduct of the individual employees and good organisational governance.
