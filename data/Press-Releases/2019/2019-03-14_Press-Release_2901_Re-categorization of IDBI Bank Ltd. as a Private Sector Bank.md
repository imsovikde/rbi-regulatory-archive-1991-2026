March 14, 2019 **Re-categorization of IDBI Bank Ltd. as a Private Sector Bank**

IDBI Bank Limited has been categorized as a ‘<u>Private Sector Bank</u>’ for regulatory purposes by Reserve Bank of India with effect from January 21, 2019 consequent upon Life Insurance Corporation of India acquiring 51% of the total paid-up equity share capital of the bank.
