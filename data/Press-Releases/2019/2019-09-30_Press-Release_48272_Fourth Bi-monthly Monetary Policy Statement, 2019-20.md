## September 30, 2019

## Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2019-20

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet during October 1, 3 and 4, 2019 for the Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2019-20. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 11.45 AM on October 4, 2019.
