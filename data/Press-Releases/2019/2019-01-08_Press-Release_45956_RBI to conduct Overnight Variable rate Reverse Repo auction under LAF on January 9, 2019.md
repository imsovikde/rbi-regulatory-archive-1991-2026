## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on January 9, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on January 9, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
