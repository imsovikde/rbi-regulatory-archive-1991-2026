April 24, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on April 24, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on April 24, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
