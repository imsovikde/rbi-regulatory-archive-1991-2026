## November 02, 2019

## United States Secretary of the Treasury visits Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai

The United States Secretary of the Treasury Mr. Steven Mnuchin along with United States Ambassador to India, Mr. Kenneth Juster, Consul General of the United States in Mumbai Mr. David J. Ranz and other officials visited Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai and held discussions with Shri Shaktikanta Das, Governor and other senior officials of Reserve Bank of India, today. The discussions covered global and domestic macro-economic scenario in both countries and regulatory developments.
