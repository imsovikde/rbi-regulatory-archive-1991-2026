## January 29, 2019

## RBI to inject durable liquidity through OMO purchase auctions

As announced vide <u>Press Release dated December 18, 2018</u>, RBI has been monitoring the evolving liquidity conditions and durable liquidity requirements of the system, based on sustained changes in the behaviour of Currency in Circulation, the magnitude of sterilisation operations for RBI’s forex operations and other relevant factors.

Accordingly, it has been decided to conduct purchase of Government securities under Open Market Operations (OMOs) for an aggregate amount of `375 billion in the month of February 2019 through three auctions of `125 billion each during the nd rd th 2, 3 and 4 week. There will be no auction during the first week due to the scheduled MPC meeting.

The auction dates and the Government securities to be purchased in the respective auctions would be communicated in due course. The OMO amount stated above is indicative and RBI retains the flexibility to change it, depending on the evolving liquidity and market conditions.
