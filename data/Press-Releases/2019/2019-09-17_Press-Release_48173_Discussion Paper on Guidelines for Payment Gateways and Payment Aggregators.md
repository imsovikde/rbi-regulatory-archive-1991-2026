## Discussion Paper on Guidelines for Payment Gateways and Payment

## Aggregators

The Reserve Bank has today placed on its website a <u>discussion paper</u> on ‘Guidelines for Payment Gateways and Payment Aggregators’ for public comments.

In the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies</u> released with the Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2018-19, the Reserve Bank had announced that a discussion paper will be placed in the public domain for consultation with the stakeholders on comprehensive guidelines covering payments related activities of payment gateway providers and payment aggregators.

The Reserve Bank invites comments on the policy paper from all stakeholders and members of public by October 17, 2019. The comments may be sent by post to the Chief General Manager, Department of Payment and Settlement Systems, th Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 14 Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai-400001, or by <u>email</u>.
