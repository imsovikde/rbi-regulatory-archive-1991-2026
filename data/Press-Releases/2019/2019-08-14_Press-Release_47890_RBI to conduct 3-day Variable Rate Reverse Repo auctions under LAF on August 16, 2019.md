## August 14, 2019

## RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under

## LAF on August 16, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on August 16, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
