## July 19, 2019

**RBI to conduct Overnight and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 22, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 22, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified** **Tenor** **Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(day)** **(**` **billion)** July 23, 2019 1 700 1 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Tuesday)

July 29, 2019 2 200 7 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
