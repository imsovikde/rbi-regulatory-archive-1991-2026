## RBI to conduct Overnight and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse

## Repo auctions under LAF on April 8, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 8, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Window Timing Date of Reversal

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
