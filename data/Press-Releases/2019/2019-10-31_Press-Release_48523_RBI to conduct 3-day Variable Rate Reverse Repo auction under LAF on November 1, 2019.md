October 31, 2019 **RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on November 1, 2019** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on November 1, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(` crore)|(day)|||
|1|3,00,000|3|4:00 pm to 4:30 pm|November 4, 2019 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
