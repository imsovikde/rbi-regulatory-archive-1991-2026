**RBI to conduct 7-day and Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 2, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 2, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
