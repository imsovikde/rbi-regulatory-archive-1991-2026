## July 12, 2019

**RBI to conduct Overnight and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 15, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 15, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified Amount Tenor

## Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(**` **billion) (day)**

July 16, 2019 1 1200 1 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Tuesday)

July 22, 2019 2 300 7 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
