## August 01, 2019

**RBI to conduct 7-day and Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on August 2, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on August 2, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified Amount Tenor

## Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(**` **billion) (day)**

August 5, 2019 1 800 3 10.30 am to 11.00 am (Monday) August 9, 2019 2 200 7 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Friday) August 5, 2019 3 800 3 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
