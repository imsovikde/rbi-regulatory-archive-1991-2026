## June 28, 2019

**RBI to conduct 7-day and Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 1, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 1, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified Amount Tenor

## Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(**` **billion) (day)**

July 8, 2019 1 800 7 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Monday) July 2, 2019 2 1000 1 1.30 pm to 2.00 pm (Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
