## RBI releases Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy 2018-19

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released its annual publication titled “<u>Handbook of Statistics (HBS) on the Indian Economy 2018-19</u>”. This publication, the 21st in the series, disseminates time series data on various economic and financial indicators relating to the Indian economy. The current volume contains 240 statistical tables covering national income aggregates, output, prices, money, banking, financial markets, public finances, foreign trade and balance of payments and select socio-economic indicators.

Longer time-series are also available on our online portal "Database on Indian Economy (DBIE)”, the RBI’s Data Warehouse (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>), where all data series are updated on a real time basis.
