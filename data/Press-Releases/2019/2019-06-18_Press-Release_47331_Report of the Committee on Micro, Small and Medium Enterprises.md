The Reserve Bank of India had constituted an ‘<u>Expert Committee on Micro, Small</u> <u>and Medium Enterprises</u>’ under the Chairmanship of Shri U K Sinha in January 2019 to suggest long-term measures for the economic and financial sustainability of the MSME sector. The committee included:

|1.|Shri U.K. Sinha Former Chairman, Securities and Exchange Board of India|Chairman|
|---|---|---|
|2.|Shri Ram Mohan Mishra Additional Secretary, Development Commissioner MSME|Member|
|3.|Shri Pankaj Jain Additional Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance|Member|
|4.|Shri P. K. Gupta Managing Director, SBI|Member|
|5.|Shri Anup Bagchi Executive Director, ICICI Bank|Member|
|6.|Shri Abhiman Das Professor, IIM-Ahmedabad|Member|
|7.|Shri Sharad Sharma Co-Founder, iSPIRT Foundation|Member|
|8.|Ms. Bindu Ananth Chair, Dvara Trust|Member|

The committee held its deliberations including consultations with various stakeholders. The committee has submitted its <u>report</u> to the Governor, Reserve Bank of India today.
