## August 23, 2019

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on August 26, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on August 26, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified Amount Tenor

## Sl. No. Window Timing Date of Reversal

## (₹ billion) (day)

August 27, 2019 1 800 1 10.30 am to 11.00 am (Tuesday)

August 27, 2019 2 500 1 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
