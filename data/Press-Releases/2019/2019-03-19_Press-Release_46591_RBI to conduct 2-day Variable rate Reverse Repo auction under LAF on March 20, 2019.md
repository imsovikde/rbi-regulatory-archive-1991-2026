March 19, 2019 **RBI to conduct 2-day Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under** **LAF on March 20, 2019** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on March 20, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|No.|(` billion)|(day)|March 22, 2019|

## Window Timing

1 1000 2 1.30 pm to 2.00 pm (Friday) Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
