## May 06, 2019

## RBI announces rate of interest on Floating Rate Bond, 2024

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bond, 2024 (FRB 2024) applicable for the half year May 07, 2019 to November 06, 2019 shall be 6.47 percent per annum.

It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2024 is set at average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at the cut-off prices of the last three auctions of Government of India 182 day Treasury Bills held up to period preceding the coupon reset date, which is May 07. The implicit yields will be computed by reckoning 365 days in a year. The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
