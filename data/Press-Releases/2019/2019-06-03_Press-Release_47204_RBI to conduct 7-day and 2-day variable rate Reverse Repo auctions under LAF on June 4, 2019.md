**RBI to conduct 7-day and 2-day variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on June 4, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on June 4, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified** **Tenor** **Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal** **(day)** **(**` **billion)** June 11, 2019 1 300 7 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Tuesday) June 6, 2019 2 500 2 3.30 pm to 4.00 pm (Thursday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
