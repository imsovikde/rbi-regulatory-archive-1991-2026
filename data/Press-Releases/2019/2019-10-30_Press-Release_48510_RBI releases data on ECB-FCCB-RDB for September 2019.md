## RBI releases data on ECB / FCCB/RDB for September 2019

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on <u>External</u> <u>Commercial Borrowings (ECB), Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB) and</u> <u>Rupee Denominated Bonds (RDB)</u> both, through Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the month of September 2019.
