January 21, 2019 **Reserve Bank of India invites comments on Authorisation** **of New Retail Payment Systems**

The Reserve Bank has today placed on its website a <u>policy paper on</u> <u>Authorisation of New Retail Payment Systems</u> for public comments.

In the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies</u> released with the <u>Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2018-19</u>, the Reserve Bank had announced that it would encourage more players to participate in and promote pan- India payment platforms and would bring out a policy paper for public consultation by September 30, 2018. The objective was to minimise the concentration risk in the retail payments market, from a financial stability perspective and to give a fillip to innovation and competition.

The Reserve Bank invites comments on the policy paper from all stakeholders and members of public by February 20, 2019. The comments may be sent by post to the Chief General Manager, Department of Payment and Settlement Systems, th Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, 14 Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai-400001, or by <u>email</u>.
