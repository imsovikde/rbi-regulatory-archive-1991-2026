## July 03, 2019

**RBI to conduct Overnight, 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 4, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 4, 2019, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|
|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|
|1|600|1|
|2|600|1|
|3|200|7|
|4|200|14|

## Window Timing Date of Reversal

|3:30 pm to 4:00 pm|July 5, 2019 (Friday)|
|---|---|
|1:30 pm to 2:00 pm|July 5, 2019 (Friday)|
|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|July 11, 2019 (Thursday)|
|11.00 am to 11.30 am|July 18, 2019 (Thursday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
