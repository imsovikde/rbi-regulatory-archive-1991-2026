## July 02, 2019

## Shri N.S. Vishwanathan re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor

The Central Government has re-appointed Shri N.S. Vishwanathan as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a further period of one year beyond his <u>currently</u> <u>notified period</u> of appointment of three years that expires on July 3, 2019, i.e., till July 3, 2020, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
