## May 24, 2019

## RBI to inject durable liquidity through OMO purchase auction in June 2019

Based on a review of the evolving liquidity conditions and assessment of the durable liquidity needs going forward, RBI has decided to conduct purchase of Government securities under Open Market Operation (OMO) for `150 billion on June 13, 2019. The Government securities to be purchased in the auction would be communicated in due course.
