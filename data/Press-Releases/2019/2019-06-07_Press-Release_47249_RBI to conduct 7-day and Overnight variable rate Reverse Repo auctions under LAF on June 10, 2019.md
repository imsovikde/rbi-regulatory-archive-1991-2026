## June 7, 2019

**RBI to conduct 7-day and Overnight variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on June 10, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on June 10, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

June 11, 2019 2 500 1 3.30 pm to 4.00 pm (Tuesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
