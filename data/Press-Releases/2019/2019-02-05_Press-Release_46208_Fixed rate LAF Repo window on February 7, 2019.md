## February 05, 2019

## Fixed rate LAF Repo window on February 7, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has decided to keep the fixed rate LAF Repo window open from 9:00 a.m. to 11:15 a.m. on February 7, 2019 in view of the Sixth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement 2018-19. On other days, the window will continue to be operated upon between 9.00 a.m. and 4.00 p.m.
