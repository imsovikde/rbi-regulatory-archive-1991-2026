September 9, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on September 11, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on September 11, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term <u>Repo A</u>uctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
