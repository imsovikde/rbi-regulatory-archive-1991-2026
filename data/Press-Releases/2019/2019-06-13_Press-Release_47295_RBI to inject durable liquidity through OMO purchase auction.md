## June 13, 2019

## RBI to inject durable liquidity through OMO purchase auction

Based on a review of the evolving liquidity conditions and assessment of the durable liquidity needs going forward, RBI has decided to conduct purchase of Government securities under Open Market Operation (OMO) for `125 billion on June 20, 2019. The Government securities to be purchased in the auction would be communicated in due course.
