### Reserve Bank of India Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition 2018-19 –

### Declaration of Results

With a view to encourage writing originally in Hindi on banking subjects, Reserve Bank of India conducted an Inter-Bank Hindi Essay competition for the year 2018-19 in which staff members (Other than Rajbhasha Officers and Translators) of RBI, PSBs and FIs have participated. The results of the above competition are as under:
