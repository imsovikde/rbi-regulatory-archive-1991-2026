## Monthly Bulletin for October 2019

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>October 2019</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement</u> <u>for the Year 2019-20</u>, <u>Monetary Policy Report – October 2019</u>, two Speeches by the Top Management, two Articles, <u>Press Release of one Working Paper</u> and Current Statistics.
