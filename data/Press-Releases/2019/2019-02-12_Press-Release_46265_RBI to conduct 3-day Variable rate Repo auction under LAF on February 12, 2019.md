February 12, 2019 **RBI to conduct 3-day Variable rate Repo auction under LAF on February 12, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Repo auction on February 12, 2019, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||
|1|200|3|12:00 to 12:30 pm|February 15, 2019 (Friday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
