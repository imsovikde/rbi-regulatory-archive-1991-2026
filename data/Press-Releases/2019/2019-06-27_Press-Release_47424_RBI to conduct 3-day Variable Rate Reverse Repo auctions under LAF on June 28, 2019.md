## June 27, 2019

## RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on June 28, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on June 28, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
