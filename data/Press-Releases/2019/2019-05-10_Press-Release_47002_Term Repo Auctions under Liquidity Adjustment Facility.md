## May 10, 2019

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14-day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|May 10, 2019 (Friday)|250|14|May 24, 2019 (Friday)|
|2|May 14, 2019 (Tuesday)|245|14|May 28, 2019 (Tuesday)|
|3|May 17, 2019 (Friday)|245|14|May 31, 2019 (Friday)|
|4|May 21, 2019 (Tuesday)|245|14|June 4, 2019 (Tuesday)|

2. The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February</u> <u>13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
