## July 25, 2019

**RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 26, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 26, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Window Timing Date of Reversal

July 29, 2019 1 900 3 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Monday)

August 2, 2019 2 150 7 4:00 pm to 4:30 pm (Friday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
