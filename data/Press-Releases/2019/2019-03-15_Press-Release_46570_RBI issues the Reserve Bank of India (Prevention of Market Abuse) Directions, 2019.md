## March 15, 2019

## RBI issues the Reserve Bank of India (Prevention of Market Abuse)

## Directions, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India had, in its <u>Statement on Developmental and</u> <u>Regulatory Policies of the Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy dated June 06, 2018</u>, announced that it will introduce regulations, in line with the best global practices, to prevent abuse in markets regulated by the Reserve Bank. Accordingly, <u>draft</u> <u>directions</u> on Prohibition of Market Abuse were issued inviting comments from market participants and other interested parties. Based on comments received, the <u>Reserve</u> <u>Bank of India (Prevention of Market Abuse) Directions, 2019</u> have been finalized and issued today.
