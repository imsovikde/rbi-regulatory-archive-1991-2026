June 4, 2019 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on June 6, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on June 6, 2019, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified** **Sl. Tenor** **Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal** **No. (day)** **(**` **billion)**

1 350 1 1.30 pm to 2.00 pm June 7, 2019 (Friday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
