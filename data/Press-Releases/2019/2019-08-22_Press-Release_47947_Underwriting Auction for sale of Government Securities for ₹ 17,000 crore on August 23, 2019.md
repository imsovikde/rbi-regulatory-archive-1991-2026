## August 22, 2019

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities

**for** `**17,000 crore on August 23, 2019**

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Stock detailed below through auctions to be held on August 23, 2019.

As per revised scheme of underwriting notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (` in crore) **Minimum Minimum** **Underwriting bidding** **Notified** **Security Commitment commitment** **Amount** **(MUC) amount per PD under per PD ACU auction**

6.17% GS 2021 3,000 72 72
7.27% GS 2026 3,000 72 72 GoI FRB 2031 5,000 120 120
7.62% GS 2039 2,000 48 48
7.63% GS 2059 4,000 96 96
The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **August 23, 2019 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **09.00**

# A.M. and 09.45 A.M. on the date of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the date of issue of securities.
