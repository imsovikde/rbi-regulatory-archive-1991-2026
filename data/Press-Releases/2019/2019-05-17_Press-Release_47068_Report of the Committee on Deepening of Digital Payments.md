## May 17, 2019

## Report of the Committee on Deepening of Digital Payments

The Reserve Bank of India had constituted a <u>High-Level Committee on Deepening</u> <u>of Digital Payments</u> under the Chairmanship of Shri Nandan Nilekani, former Chairman, UIDAI, in January 2019.

The Committee held its deliberations including consultations with various stakeholders and has today submitted its <u>report</u> to the Governor, Reserve Bank of India.

The Reserve Bank of India will examine the recommendations of the Committee and will dovetail the action points, wherever necessary, in its <u>Payment Systems</u> <u>Vision 2021 published on May 15, 2019</u>, for implementation.
