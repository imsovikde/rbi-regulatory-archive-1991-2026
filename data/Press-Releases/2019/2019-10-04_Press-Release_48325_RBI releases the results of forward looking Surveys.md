## October 4, 2019

## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – September 2019</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – September 2019</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Round 60</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q2:2019-20</u>
v. <u>OBICUS Survey on the Manufacturing sector – Q1:2019-20</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to
the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
