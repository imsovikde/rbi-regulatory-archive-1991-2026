## LAF on October 14, 2019

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on October 14, 2019, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(` crore)|(day)|||

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
