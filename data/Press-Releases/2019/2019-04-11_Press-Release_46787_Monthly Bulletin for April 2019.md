April 11, 2019 **Monthly Bulletin for April 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>April 2019</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for the</u> <u>Year 2019-20</u>, <u>Monetary Policy Report – April 2019</u>, two speeches by the Top management, three Articles, Press Release of Working Paper and Current Statistics.
