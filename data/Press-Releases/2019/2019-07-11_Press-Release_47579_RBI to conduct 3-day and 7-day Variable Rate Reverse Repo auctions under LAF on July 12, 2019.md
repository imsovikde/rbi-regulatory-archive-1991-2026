## July 11, 2019

**RBI to conduct 3-day and 7-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on July 12, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on July 12, 2019, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
