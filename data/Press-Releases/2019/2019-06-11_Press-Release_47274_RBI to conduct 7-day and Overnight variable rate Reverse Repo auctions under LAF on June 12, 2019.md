June 11, 2019 **RBI to conduct 7-day and Overnight variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on June 12, 2019**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on June 12, 2019, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified

## Tenor

## Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(day)** **(**` **billion)** June 19, 2019 1 200 7 12.00 pm to 12.30 pm (Wednesday)

June 13, 2019 2 400 1 3.30 pm to 4.00 pm (Thursday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
