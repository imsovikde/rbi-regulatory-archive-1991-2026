# RBI advises raising of FII investment limits in Punjab Tractors to 30%

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now invest up to 30 per cent of M/s Punjab Tractors Ltd. M/s. Punjab Tractors Ltd., has passed a resolution at its board and general body meetings agreeing for the purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by FIIs up to 30 per cent of their paid-up capital through primary and stock exchanges. The aggregate ceiling for FIIs' investment in this company, thus, has been raised to 30 per cent from the earlier 24 per cent.
