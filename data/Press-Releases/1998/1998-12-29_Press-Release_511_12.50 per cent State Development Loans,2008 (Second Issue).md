# 12.50 per cent State Development Loans,2008 (Second Issue)

## December 29, 1998

The 12.50 per cent State Development Loans,2008 (Second Issue) of fifteen State Governments which opened for subscription on December 28, 1998 for an aggregate notified amount of Rs. 1,612.06 crore were closed on the same day. The total subscriptions aggregated Rs. 2,817.01 crore. Excess subscription has been refunded to the applicants today.

## S. R. Singh Manager
