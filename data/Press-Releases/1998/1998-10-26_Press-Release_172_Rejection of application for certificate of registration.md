# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

*October 26, 1998* The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on October 16, 1998 the application for certificate of registration submitted by M/s. Linker's Securities Ltd., 22, Vaishall Nagar, Kotra Sultanabad, Bhopal-462 003. As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
