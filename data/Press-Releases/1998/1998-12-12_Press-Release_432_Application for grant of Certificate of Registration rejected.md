1. Nandy Investments Ltd. 101, Buliding No.9, Somdutt 11-12-1998
Chamber-II, Bhikaji Cama Place, New Delhi As such, the above company cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. **Maureen Shankar** **Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/775**
