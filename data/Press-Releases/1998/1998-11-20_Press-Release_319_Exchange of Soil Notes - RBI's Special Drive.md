# Exchange of Soil Notes-RBI's Special Drive

*November 20, 1998* The Byculla Office of the Reserve Bank of India is launching a special drive from December 1, 1998 for exchange of soiled, cut and mutilated notes in the denomination of Re.1, Rs.2 and Rs.5. The admissible exchange value in accordance with RBI Note Refund Rules 1975 will be paid in the form of coins across the counter. The members of public can avail of this facility during banking hours at the Banking Hall at Reserve Bank of India's Byculla Office in Mumbai.

## S. R. Singh Manager
