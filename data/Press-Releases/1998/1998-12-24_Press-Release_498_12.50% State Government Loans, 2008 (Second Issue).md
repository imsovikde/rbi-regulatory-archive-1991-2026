# 12.50% State Government Loans, 2008 (Second Issue)

## December 24, 1998

In Partial modification of our press release dated December 21, 1998 regarding issue of loans of fifteen State Governments, it has now been decided, at the instance of the Government of Bihar, that the Government of Bihar will be raising an amount of Rs. 214.00 crore only as against Rs.321.44 crore announced earlier. Accordingly, the aggregate amount of loans to be raised stands reduced to Rs. 1612.06 crore.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
