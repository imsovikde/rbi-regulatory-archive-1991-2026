# Application for grant of Certificate of Registration rejected

*November 2, 1998*

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected the applications for grant of a certificate of registration submitted by the undernoted non-banking financial companies:

|Sr.No.|Name of the Company|Address of the Registered|Rejected on|
|---|---|---|---|
|||Office of the Company||
|1.|M/s. Chandan Finlease Limited|67/2, Chanakyapuri Society, Near Railway Crossing, Ghatlodia, Ahmedabad-380 061|30.10.1998|
|2.|M/s. Koluthara Finance &|CC 40/4844-E, Basin|31.10.1998|
||Investments Private Limited|Road,||

Ernakulam, Cochin-682 031

As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institutions as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
