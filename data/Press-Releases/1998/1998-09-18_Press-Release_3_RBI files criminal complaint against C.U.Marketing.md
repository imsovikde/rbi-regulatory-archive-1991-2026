# RBI files criminal complaint against C.U.Marketing

The Reserve Bank of India filed a criminal complaint before the Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate, against C.U.Marketing, Mumbai for luring the public to deposit monies in the guise of 'Advance for goods' in violation of the provisions of Section 45S of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. The learned Magistrate passed an order on September 17, 1998 referring the complaint to police authorities for investigation and to submit the report to the court before October 5, 1998.

*(Alpana Killawala)* **General Manager**
