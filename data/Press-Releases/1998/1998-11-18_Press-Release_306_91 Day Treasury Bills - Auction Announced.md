# 91 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## November 18, 1998

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.400 crore. ***The auction will be conducted using*** ***"Uniform Price Auction" method.*** The allocation to non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Friday, November 20, 1998 before 1430 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Saturday, November 21, 1998.

## S. R. Singh Manager
