# Rejection of application for certificate of registration

*October 29, 1998* The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected on October 28, 1998 the application for certificate of registration submitted by the financial company M/s. Bainda Leasing & Finance (P) Ltd., 743, Pocket A, Santa Vihar, New Delhi. As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
