## October 13, 1998

# 12.50 per cent State Development Loans, 2008 Oversubscribed

The 12.50 per cent ten-year State Development Loans in respect of twenty-five State Governments opened for subscription for an aggregate notified amount of Rs.3,100.28 crore on October 12, 1998. The subscriptions received aggregated Rs. 4,462.40 crore. Some subscriptions received at designated branches of State Bank of India/Associate Banks at district headquarters of the States are still being reported. The excess subscriptions received by some State Governments will be retained.

Alpana Killawala General Manager Press Release : 1998-99/458
