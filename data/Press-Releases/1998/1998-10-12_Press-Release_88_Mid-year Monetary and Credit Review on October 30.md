# Mid-year Monetary and Credit Review on October 30

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks on Friday, October 30, 1998 for a mid-year review of monetary and credit policy developments during 1998-99.
