# India's Foreign Currency Assets increase to a record level at the end of December 1998

## December 31, 1998

According to the Weekly Statistical Supplement of the Reserve Bank of India, to be released on Saturday (January 2, 1999), India's total Foreign Currency Assets reached the level of US $ 26, 843 million on December 24, 1998. This is the last weekly data for the year ending today. The level of foreign currency assets on December 24, was the highest ever recorded. The peak level of foreign currency assets before the current year was reached on August 29, 1997. Foreign currency assets on that date were US $ 26, 431 million.

Compared with end-December 1997, India's foreign currency assets showed an increase of US $ 2, 878 million on December 24, 1998.

The level of Foreign Exchange Reserves, which include gold and SDR, was US $ 29, 967 million on December 24, 1998. This was slightly lower than the previous peak of US $ 30, 240 million reached on August 29, 1997. The increase in foreign exchange reserves during the year 1998 (up to December 24) was US $ 2, 612 million, which was slightly lower than the increase in foreign currency assets during the same period. This is explained by the net change in the value of assets held in the form of gold and SDRs in the reserves during the period. **Maureen Shankar** **Manager** **Press Release : 1998-99/867**
