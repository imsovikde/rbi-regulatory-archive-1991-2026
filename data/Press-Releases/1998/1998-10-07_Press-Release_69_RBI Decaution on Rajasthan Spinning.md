## October 7, 1998

# RBI Decaution on Rajasthan Spinning

The Reserve Bank of India has notified that the Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Overseas Corporate Bodies (OCBs)/Persons of Indian Origins (PIOs) holdings in M/s. Rajasthan Spinning and Weaving Mills Ltd. have gone below 8 per cent of its paid up capital. The restriction placed on the purchase of shares of this company on behalf of NRIs/OCBs/PIOs therefore, has been withdrawn.
