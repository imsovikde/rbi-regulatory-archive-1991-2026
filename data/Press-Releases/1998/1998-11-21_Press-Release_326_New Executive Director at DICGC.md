## November 21, 1998

# New Executive Director at DICGC

Shri A. Chandramouliswaran has been appointed as Executive Director of Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), with effect from November 3, 1998. Shri Chandramouliswaran was the Chief General Manager, Industrial and Export Credit Department, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai prior to his appointment as Executive Director of DICGC.
