RBI modifiesprudential normsforNBFCs

The Reserve Bank had prescribed prudential norms for Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) in

January 1998.Inresponse toadvicereceived from theInstitute of CharteredAccountants of India(ICAl)and

in consultation with them, it has been decided tointroduce certain modifications in the norms for accounting

bring the prudential norms on the above two aspects in alignment with the accounting standard issued by the

ICAl.ThemodificationsareeffectivefromJanuary31,1998.

(i)Accountingforinvestments

Asadvisedby theICAl,theearlierbifurcationof longterminvestmentsintoquoted andunquoted investments

has been done away with. Under the modified norms, the unquoted equity shares in the nature of current

investments only are required to be valued at cost or break up value whichever is lower. The companies also

(ii)Provisioningnorms for hire purchase and lease assets

The earlier norms did not take into account the value of underlying asset for the purpose of provisions against

charges/income not taken to the credit of profit and loss account was to be fully provided for. Due credit has

NPAs in the category of above assets.

(ii) Risk weights on exposures to the PFls and the bonds issued by PSBs

Investments by NBFCs in the bonds of all public sector banks (PSBs) and fixed deposits/certificates of

deposit/bonds issued by public financial institutions (PFls) as also investment in the units of Unit Trust of India

(iv)Reversalofincomeinrespectofloansandothercreditfacilities

The NBFCs have been asked to reverse, with immediate effect, the income including interest/discount or any

theassetbecameNPA.

**2. Prohibition to make loans and investments in case of default in repayment of public deposits** With a view to further protecting the interests of the depositors, the NBFCs have been directed, with immediate effect, not to grant any loan or other credit facility or make investment or create any other asset as long as the default in repayment of public deposits in accordance with the provisions of Section 45QA(1) of the RBI Act exists. The detailed notification is available on the RBI website at [http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in) Circular: DNBS PD NO.CC 06/02.01/97-98 dated May 12, 1998 issued to to All Non- Banking Financial Companies and Residuary Non-Banking Companies (having Net Owned Fund of Rs.25 lakh and above)
