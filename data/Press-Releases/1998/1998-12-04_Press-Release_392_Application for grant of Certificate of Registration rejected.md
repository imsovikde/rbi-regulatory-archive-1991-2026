# Application for grant of Certificate of Registration rejected

## December 4, 1998

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has rejected the applications for grant of a certificate of registration submitted by the undernoted financial companies:

## Sr.No. Name of the Company Rejected on

1. Sri Vikhyat Finance & Leasing Ltd. 1.12.1998 Shop No.3,4 & 5, Jagadamba Complex Main Road, Manchirial Adilabad (Dis.) Andhra Pradesh
2. M/s. Om Samaradhi Leasing & Housing Finance Ltd., 03.12.1998 958, Seth Sadan Complex Ruseel Chowk, Napier Town, Jabalpur.(M.P.)
3. M/s. Rukmini Leasing Ltd., 1.12.1998 317-320, Model House, Punjagutta, Hyderabad-500482
4. Gemini Commericial Credits Ltd., 2.12.1998 Cottage No.4, Bangalore Palace, Bangalore-560 052.
5. Kakatiya Finance & Leasing Company Ltd., 1.12.1998 1-10-140/1, 'Gurukrupa', Ashok Nagar, Hyderabad. As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of non-banking financial institutions as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
## Maureen Shankar

## Manager
