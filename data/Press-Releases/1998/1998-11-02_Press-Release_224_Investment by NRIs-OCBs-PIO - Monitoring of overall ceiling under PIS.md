## November 2, 1998

# Investment by NRIs/OCBs/PIO-Monitoring of overall ceiling under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that no further purchases of shares of M/s. Bharti Telecom Ltd. and Madras Aluminium Co. Ltd. should be made on behalf of non-resident Indians/overseas corporate bodies/Persons of Indian Origin. The overall ceiling of 10 per cent of paid up equity capital permitted to be held by NRIs/OCBs/PIO under the Portfolio Investment Scheme has already been reached in the case of these companies.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
