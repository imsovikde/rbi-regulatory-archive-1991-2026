# New ED at RBI

*November 28, 1998* Shri I.D. Agarwal, Chief General Manager & Secretary, Secretary's Department has been appointed as Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India with effect from December 1, 1998. Shri C. Harikumar will lay-down office as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India on November 30, 1998.
