# Tap sale of 12.60 per cent Government Stock, 2018 closed

The sale of 12.60 per cent Government Stock, 2018 which opened for subscription on November 23, 1998 on tap at Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai was closed at the close of banking hours today. In all, 13 applications for Rs.1,131.88 crore (nominal), were received. All the applications were accepted.
