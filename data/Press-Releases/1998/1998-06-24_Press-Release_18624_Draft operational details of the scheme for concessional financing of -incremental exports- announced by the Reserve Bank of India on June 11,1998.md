Assume that the exporter increases his/her exports to Rs.30 crore, Rs. 35 crore and Rs. 40 crore in each of the quarters of sub-period I. Under the new scheme, the exporter will be entitled to concessional credit at 6.5 per cent for incremental exports of Rs.5 crore, Rs.10 crore and Rs. 15 crore in each of the quarters of sub-period I. Similarly, in sub-period II, if exports increase to Rs.45 crore, Rs. 50 crore and Rs. 55 crore, he/she will be entitled to a concessional credit at 6.5 per cent for incremental exports of Rs. 20 crore, Rs. 25 crore and Rs.30 crore in each quarter of sub-period II.

## (d) Period of validity of the scheme

As announced earlier, the above scheme is a temporary facility and will cease to be in operation on December 31,

1999. It will not be renewed thereafter. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that it would call a meeting of representative bodies of exporters to discuss the draft scheme in order to identify any operational problems that might arise in implementation of the scheme. A meeting of bankers is also proposed to be convened for the same purpose. The Reserve Bank expects to finalise and issue the scheme to banks by July 10, 1998 after discussions with representative bodies of exporters and bankers.
