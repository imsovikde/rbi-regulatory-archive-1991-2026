# New ED at RBI

Shri Khizer Ahmed has been appointed as Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India with effect from November 1, 1998. Shri Ahmed was Chief General Manager, Exchange Control Department before taking over as Executive Director. As Executive Director, Shri Ahmed will look after Department of External Investments and Operations, Internal Debt Management Cell, Industrial and Export Credit Department, Department of Expenditure and Budgetary Control and Department of Government and Bank Accounts.

Shri V. Subrahmanyam laid down office as Executive Director of the Reserve Bank of India on October 31, 1998.
