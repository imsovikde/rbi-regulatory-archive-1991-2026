# RBI Reference Rate for US $

## October 28, 1998

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar today is Rs.42.27 on

28.10.1998 as against Rs.42.26 on 27.10.1998. The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-rupee rate will be based on this rate.
