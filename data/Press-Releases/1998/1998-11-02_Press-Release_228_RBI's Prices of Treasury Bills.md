# RBI's Prices of Treasury Bills

*November 2, 1998*

The price list effective from November 3, 1998 is as under :

|Type of Treasury Bill|Date of Maturity|Yield rate (%) for sale|
|---|---|---|
|91 Day|November 28, 98|8.93|
|91 Day|December 05, 98|9.04|
|91 Day|January 23, 99|9.76|

1. All sales will be made in Mumbai only.
2. Deals will be for a minimum amount of Rs.1 lakh and multiples of Rs.1 lakh thereafter.
