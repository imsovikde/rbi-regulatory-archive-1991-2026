# 91 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

*November 3, 1998*

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs. 200 crore. ***The auction will be conducted*** ***using "Uniform Price Auction" method.*** The allocation to non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Friday, November 6, 1998 before

14.30 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Saturday, November 7, 1998.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
