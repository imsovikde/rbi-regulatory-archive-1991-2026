## November 10, 1998

# Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India - 1997-98

The Reserve Bank has released its Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India for 1997-98. The copies are available from the Director, DRRP, Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, Sales Section, Sir P. M. Road, Fort, Mumbai-400 001. The Report is priced at Rs. 120/-. The Report will also be released on the RBI Website (<u>URL:[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>) at

7.00 a.m. on November 11, 1998.
## Alpana Killawala General Manager
