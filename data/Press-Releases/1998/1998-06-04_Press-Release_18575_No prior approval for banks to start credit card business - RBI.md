No prior approval for banks to start credit card business : RBI

The Reserve Bank of India has done away with the requirement of prior approval for banks to undertake credit card

business. In a circular issued yesterday, the Reserve Bank advised banks that it has been decided to allow banks freedom

to undertake credit card business either independently or in tie-up arrangements with other card issuing banks. Banks
