Investment Limits by NRls/PiOs/OCBs in Indian Companies through Secondary

Markets enhanced, excluded from Portfolio Investments Limits for Flls

The Reserve Bank of India has modified NRls/PlOs/OCBs investment ceilling for

puchase of shares in a company through stock exchanges under the Portfolio

Investment Scheme.

Now, the investment limit by a single Non-resident Indian (NRl)/Person of Indian Origin

cent (OcB) in a company has been enhanced from the existing level of 1 per cent to 5

NRls/PlOs/OCBs investment through the secondary market in a company has been

The individual and aggregate ceilling limits for portfolio investments by NRls/OCBs/PIOs

would be exclusive of the aggregate portfolio investment ceiling for Foreign Institutional

Investors (Flls).

To facilitate speedy approval, the Reserve Bank has also delegated to the designated

banks, powers for renewal of general permission already granted by the Reserve Bank

of India to individual NRls for purchase of shares through stock exchanges. Such RBl

permission granted through designated banks to NRls/PIOs/OCBs for secondary market

transactions is valid for five years.

The Reserve Bank has stated that the earlier facility of aggregate investment by all

NRls/PlOs/OCBs through stock exchanges upto the enhanced limit of 24 per cent

subject to the general body resolution of a company would continue to be operative. Fll

investments would also continue to be subject to a separate aggregate ceilling of 24 per

cent/30 per cent of the paid up equity capital of a company.

The Reserve Bank modifications are in pursuance of the announcement made by the

Finance Minister in his budget speech for 1998-99.
