## January 25, 2017

**RBI to conduct 3 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions for 1000 Billion under LAF on January 27, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 27, 2017, Friday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (B illion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|500|3|10.00 am to 10.30 am|January 30, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|500|3|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|January 30, 2017 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
