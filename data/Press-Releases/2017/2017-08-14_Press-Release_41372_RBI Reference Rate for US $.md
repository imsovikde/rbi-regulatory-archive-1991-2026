August 14, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0253 on August 14, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 11, 2017) was 64.1693. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **August 11, 2017 August 14, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.4567 75.7035 **1 GBP** 83.2661 83.2777 **100 YEN** 58.76 58.43 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
