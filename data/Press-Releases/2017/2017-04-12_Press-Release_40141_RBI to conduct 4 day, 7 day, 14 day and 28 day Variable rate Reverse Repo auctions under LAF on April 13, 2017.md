## April 12, 2017

**RBI to conduct 4 day, 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable rate Reverse Repo** **auctions under LAF on April 13, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 13, 2017, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|250|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|May 11, 2017 (Thursday)|
|2|250|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 27, 2017 (Thursday)|
|3|300|7|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 20, 2017 (Thursday)|
|4|300|4|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|April 17, 2017 (Monday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
