64.1164. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 05, 2017 September 06, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.2921 76.5438 **1 GBP** 82.8192 83.6395 **100 YEN** 58.63 59.04 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
