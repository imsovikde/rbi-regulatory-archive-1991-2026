## FIIs/FPIs investment under PIS-Incorporation in Caution List –

## M/s Niyogin Fintech Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolios Investors (FPIs) in <u>M/s Niyogin Fintech Ltd</u>. has reached the trigger limit. Hence further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
