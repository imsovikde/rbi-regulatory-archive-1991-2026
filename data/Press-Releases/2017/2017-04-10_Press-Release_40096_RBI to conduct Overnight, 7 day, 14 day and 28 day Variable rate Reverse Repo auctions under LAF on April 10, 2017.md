## RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable rate Reverse Repo

## auctions under LAF on April 10, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 10, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|250|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|May 08, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|250|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 24, 2017 (Monday)|
|3|250|7|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 17, 2017 (Monday)|
|4|300|1|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|April 11, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
