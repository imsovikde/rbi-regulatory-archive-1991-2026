September 19, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1769 on September 19, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 18, 2017) was

64.0371. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 18, 2017 September 19, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.4923 77.0187 **1 GBP** 87.0841 86.8121 **100 YEN** 57.57 57.45 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
