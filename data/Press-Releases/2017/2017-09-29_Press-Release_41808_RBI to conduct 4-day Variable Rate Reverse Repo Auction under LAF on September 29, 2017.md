## RBI to conduct 4-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on

## September 29, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following 4-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction on September 29, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|100|4|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|October 3, 2017 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
