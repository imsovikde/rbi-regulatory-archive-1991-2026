# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9338 on October 30, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 27, 2017) was

65.0931. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 27, 2017 October 30, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.6837 75.4271 **1 GBP** 85.3826 85.3750 **100 YEN** 57.00 57.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
