February 08, 2017 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys**

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey</u> <u>(OBICUS) – July–September 2016 (Round 35)</u> ii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – December 2016</u>
iii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – December 2016</u> <u>(Round 46)</u> iv. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators –</u> <u>January 2017 (Round 44)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
