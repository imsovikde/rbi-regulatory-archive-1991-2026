## April 01, 2017

**Applicable Average Base Rate to be charged by NBFC-MFIs for the Quarter Beginning April 01, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India has today communicated that the applicable average base rate to be charged by Non-Banking Financial Company – Micro Finance Institutions (NBFC-MFIs) to their borrowers for the quarter beginning April 01, 2017 will be 9.35 per cent.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank had, in its <u>circular dated February 7,</u> <u>2014</u>, issued to NBFC-MFIs regarding pricing of credit, stated that it will, on the last working day of every quarter, advise the average of the base rates of the five largest commercial banks for the purpose of arriving at the interest rates to be charged by NBFC-MFIs to its borrowers in the ensuing quarter.
