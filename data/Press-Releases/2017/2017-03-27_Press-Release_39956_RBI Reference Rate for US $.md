March 27, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0892 on March 27, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 24, 2017) was

65.4581. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **March 24, 2017 March 27, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.4656 70.6739 **1 GBP** 81.6721 81.6479 **100 YEN** 58.74 59.08 **Note :** The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.
