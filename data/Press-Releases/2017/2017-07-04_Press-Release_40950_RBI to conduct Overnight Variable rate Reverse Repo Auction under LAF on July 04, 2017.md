July 04, 2017 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on July 04, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on July 04, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day)|||
|1|250|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|July 5, 2017 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
