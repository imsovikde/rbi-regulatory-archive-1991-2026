September 21, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5256 on September 21, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 20, 2017) was

64.3637. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 20, 2017 September 21, 2017** **1 EUR** 77.2493 76.7403 **1 GBP** 87.0262 87.1547 **100 YEN** 57.76 57.30 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
