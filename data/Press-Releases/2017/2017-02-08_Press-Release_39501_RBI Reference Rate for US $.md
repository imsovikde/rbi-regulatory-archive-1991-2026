February 08, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3058 on February 08, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 07, 2017) was

67.3692. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 07, 2017 February 08, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.0716 71.8759 **1 GBP** 83.9488 84.1659 **100 YEN** 60.26 59.94 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
