# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0386 on November 21, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 20, 2017) was

65.0565. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 20, 2017 November 21, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.3633 76.3878 **1 GBP** 85.9331 86.1892 **100 YEN** 58.08 57.82 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
