## April 19, 2017

## Au Small Finance Bank Limited commences operations

Au Small Finance Bank Limited has commenced operations as a small finance bank with effect from April 19, 2017. The Reserve Bank has issued a licence to the bank under Section 22 (1) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 to carry on the business of small finance bank in India.

Au Financiers (India) Limited, Jaipur was one of the ten applicants which were issued in-principle approval for setting up a small finance bank, as announced in the <u>press release on September 16, 2015</u>.
