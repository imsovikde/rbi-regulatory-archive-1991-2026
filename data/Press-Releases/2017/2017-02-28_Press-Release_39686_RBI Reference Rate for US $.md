February 28, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7375 on February 28, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 27, 2017) was

66.7249. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **February 27, 2017 February 28, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.5149 70.7151 **1 GBP** 82.8923 83.0548 **100 YEN** 59.51 59.35 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
