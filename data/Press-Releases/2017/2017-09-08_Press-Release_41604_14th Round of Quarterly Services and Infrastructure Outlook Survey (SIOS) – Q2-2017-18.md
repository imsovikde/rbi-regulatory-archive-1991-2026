## September 08, 2017

**14** **th** **Round of Quarterly Services and Infrastructure** **Outlook Survey (SIOS) – Q2:2017-18**

The Reserve Bank has been conducting the Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS) since 2014-15. The survey is conducted on a quarterly th basis. The 14 round of the SIOS seeks an assessment of the business situation for the current quarter (Q2:2017-18, *i.e.*, July - September 2017) from selected companies in the services and infrastructure sectors in India, and their outlook for the ensuing quarter (Q3:2017-18, *i.e.*, October-December 2017).

2. The survey questionnaire has undergone some changes and salient features of the revised questionnaire are:
1. Block 1: Additional information about company size
2. Block 2: Identification of the sector/major activity of the company
3. Block 3: Response to be collected on some more business parameters, such as full-time employees, salary/wages, selling price, technical service capacity and physical investment, as applicable
4. Block 4: Top 3 business enablers and constraints
5. Block 5: Assessment/Outlook on the overall economy
3. Companies in the Services and Infrastructure sectors are encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> along with the guidelines from the website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed or faxed as per the contact details given below. The responses of the individual companies will be kept strictly confidential.
4. In case of any query/clarification, feel free to contact us at: The Director, Division of Enterprise Surveys, Department of Statistics and
