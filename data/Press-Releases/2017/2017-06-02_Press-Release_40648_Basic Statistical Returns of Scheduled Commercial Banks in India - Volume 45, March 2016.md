## June 02, 2017

## Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India -

## Volume 45, March 2016

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released the web publication *‘*<u>Basic Statistical</u> <u>Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) in India – Volume 45</u>’, which provides data on different dimensions of deposits and credit of the banking sector. The information is collected at branch level through Basic Statistical Returns - 1 & 2 (BSR- 1 1&2) annually. Under BSR-1, information on type of account, organisation, occupation/activity and category of the borrower, district and population group of place of utilisation of credit, rate of interest, credit limit and amount outstanding is collected. Under BSR-2, branch-wise data on type of deposits, maturity pattern of term deposits as well as number of employees are collected.

This data can be accessed at <u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/dbie.rbi?site=](https://dbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/dbie.rbi?site=)</u> <u>publications#!9</u>
