## May 24,2017

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List–M/s Claris lifesciences Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding by Foreign institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) in <u>M/s Claris</u> <u>lifesciences Ltd</u> has crossed the overall limit of 24% of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs).
