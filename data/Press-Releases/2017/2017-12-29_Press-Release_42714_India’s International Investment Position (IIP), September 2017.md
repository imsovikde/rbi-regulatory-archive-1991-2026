December 29, 2017 **1** **India’s International Investment Position (IIP), September 2017**

Today, the Reserve Bank released data relating to <u>India’s international</u> <u>investment position (IIP)</u> as at <u>end-September 2017</u>.

## Highlights:

- Net claims of non-residents on India declined by US$ 1.9 billion over the previous quarter due to higher increase in Indian residents’ overseas financial assets as compared with foreign- owned assets in India (Table 1).
- Indian residents’ financial assets abroad exhibited an increase of US$ 18.6 billion over the level in the previous quarter, a substantial portion of which came from increase in reserve assets (Tables 1 and 2).
- Foreign-owned assets in India increased by US$ 16.7 billion during the quarter, mainly due to rise in foreign direct investment (FDI), trade credit and portfolio investment in debt securities; overseas investment in portfolio equity, however, declined during the quarter.
- Reserve assets had over two-thirds share in India’s international assets, followed by overseas direct investment; debt and non- debt liabilities had nearly equal shares in total liabilities to non- residents. (Table 3).
- The ratio of India’s international financial assets to international financial liabilities stood at 59.5 per cent in September 2017 (58.6 per cent in June-2017).
India’s quarterly IIP is being disseminated with one quarter lag and the previous quarterly IIP as at end-June 2017 was placed in the public domain on <u>September 29, 2017</u>.
