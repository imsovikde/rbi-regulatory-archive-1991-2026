March 07, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6347 on March 07, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 06, 2017) was

66.7559. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 06, 2017 March 07, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.7346 70.5595 **1 GBP** 81.9762 81.5342 **100 YEN** 58.67 58.48 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
