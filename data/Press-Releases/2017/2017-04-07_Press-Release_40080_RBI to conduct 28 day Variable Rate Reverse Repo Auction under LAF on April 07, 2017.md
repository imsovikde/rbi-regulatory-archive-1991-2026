April 07, 2017 **RBI to conduct 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on April 07, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction today (April 7, 2017, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|250|28|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|May 5, 2017 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
