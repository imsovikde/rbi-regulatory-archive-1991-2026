March 09, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7707 on March 09, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 08, 2017) was

66.6362. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 08, 2017 March 09, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.4011 70.3229 **1 GBP** 81.3162 81.1665 **100 YEN** 58.61 58.33 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
