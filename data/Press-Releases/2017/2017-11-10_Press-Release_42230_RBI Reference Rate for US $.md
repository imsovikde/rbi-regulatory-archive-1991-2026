# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0147 on November 10, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 09, 2017) was

64.8967. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
