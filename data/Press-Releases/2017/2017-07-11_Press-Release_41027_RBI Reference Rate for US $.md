July 11, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5025 on July 11, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 10, 2017) was

64.5405. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 10, 2017 July 11, 2017** **1 EUR** 73.5826 73.4361 **1 GBP** 83.2766 83.0857 **100 YEN** 56.53 56.38 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
