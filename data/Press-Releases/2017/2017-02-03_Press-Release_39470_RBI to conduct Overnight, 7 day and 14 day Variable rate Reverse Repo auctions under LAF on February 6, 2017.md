## February 03, 2017

**RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on February 6, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on February 6, 2017, Monday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|300|7|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 13, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|500|14|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 20, 2017 (Monday)|
|3|500|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|February 7, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|4|500|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|February 7, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
