## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5883 on June 16, 2017.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 15, 2017) was

64.2801. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
