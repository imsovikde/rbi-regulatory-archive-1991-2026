January 09, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.1863 on January 09, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 06, 2017) was

67.9522. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 06, 2017 January 09, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.8662 71.8206 **1 GBP** 84.0976 83.1259 **100 YEN** 58.50 58.10 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
