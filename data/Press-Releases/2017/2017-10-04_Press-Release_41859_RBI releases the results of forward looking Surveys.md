October 04, 2017 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys** The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – September, 2017</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – September, 2017</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – Round 48</u> iv. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS) –</u> <u>Round 38 (Q1:2017-18)</u>
v. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Round 79 (Q2:2017-18)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the
Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India..
