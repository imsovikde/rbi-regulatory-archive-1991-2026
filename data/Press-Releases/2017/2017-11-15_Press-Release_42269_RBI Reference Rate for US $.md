# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.3689 on November 15, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 14, 2017) was

65.5171. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 14, 2017 November 15, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.4781 77.0634 **1 GBP** 85.9388 85.9143 **100 YEN** 57.65 57.76 **Note :** The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.
