July 08, 2017 **Corrigendum**

The Reserve Bank of India had issued a <u>Press Release on June 13, 2017</u> <u>bearing reference number 2016-2017/3363</u> (“**Press Release**”) titled 'RBI identifies Accounts for Reference by Banks under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC)’.

The third line of paragraph no. 5 of the Press Release, which reads as follows:

“*5. ...Such cases will be accorded priority by the National Company Law* *Tribunal (NCLT).*” stands deleted.

The remaining contents of the Press Release remain unchanged.
