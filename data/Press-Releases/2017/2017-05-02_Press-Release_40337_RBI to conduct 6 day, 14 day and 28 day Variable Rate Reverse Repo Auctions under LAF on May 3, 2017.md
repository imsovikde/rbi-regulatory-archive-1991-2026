May 02, 2017 **RBI to conduct 6 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions** **under LAF on May 3, 2017** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on May 3, 2017, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|200|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|May 31, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|2|400|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|May 17, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|3|400|6|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|May 09, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
