## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.6458 on February 01, 2017.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 31, 2017) was

67.8125. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
