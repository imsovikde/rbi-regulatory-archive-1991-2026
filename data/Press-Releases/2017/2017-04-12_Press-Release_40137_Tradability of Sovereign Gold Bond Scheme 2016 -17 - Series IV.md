## Tradability of Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2016 -17 - Series IV

(Issued on March 17, 2017)

Sovereign Gold Bonds, issued on March 17, 2017, held in dematerialised form shall be eligible for trading (in terms of Para 17 of Government of India notification) with effect from April 13, 2017 (Thursday) on stock exchanges recognised by the Government of India under the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956. <u>Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2016-17 -Series IV</u> was announced by the Government of India on February 23, 2017.
