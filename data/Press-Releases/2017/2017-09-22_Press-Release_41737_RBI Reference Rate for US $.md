64.5256. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 21, 2017 September 22, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.7403 77.7566 **1 GBP** 87.1547 88.3061 **100 YEN** 57.30 57.99 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
