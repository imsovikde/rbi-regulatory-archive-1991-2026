# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.3764 on December 05, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 04, 2017) was

64.3799. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 04, 2017 December 05, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.3739 76.3762 **1 GBP** 86.6360 86.6378 **100 YEN** 57.04 57.15 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
