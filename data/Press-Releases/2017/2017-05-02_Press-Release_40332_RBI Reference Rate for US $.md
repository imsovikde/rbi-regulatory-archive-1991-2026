## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.2072 on May 02, 2017.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 28, 2017) was

64.2170. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
