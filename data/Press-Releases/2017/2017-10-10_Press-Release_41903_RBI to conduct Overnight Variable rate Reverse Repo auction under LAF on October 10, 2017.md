October 10, 2017 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on** **October 10, 2017** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on October 10, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day)|||
|1|100|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|October 11, 2017 (Wednesday)|
