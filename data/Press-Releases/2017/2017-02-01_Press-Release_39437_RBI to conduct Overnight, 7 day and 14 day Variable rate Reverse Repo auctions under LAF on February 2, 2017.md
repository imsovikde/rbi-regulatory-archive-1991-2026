**RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on February 2, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on February 2, 2017, Thursday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
