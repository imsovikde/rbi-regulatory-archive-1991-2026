## April 03, 2017

## RBI to disseminate Interest Rate Data on Monthly Basis from April 2017

With a view to improving the frequency and timeliness of release of <u>interest rate data</u>, it has been decided that the following four tables will now be released on a monthly basis beginning April 2017:

1) Bank group-wise WALR on outstanding rupee loans;
2) Bank group-wise WALR on fresh rupee loans sanctioned;
3) Individual bank-wise 1- year MCLR; and
4) Bank group-wise 1- year median MCLR The tables are at present being released on a quarterly basis. The Reserve Bank has been publishing data on lending rates of banks
on its website ( <u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/rbi-](https://www.rbi.org.in/rbi-)</u> <u>sourcefiles/lendingrate/LendingRates.aspx#)</u> every quarter since the quarter ended June 2002.

A new table on bank-group-wise weighted average domestic term deposit rates has also been introduced.

The new tables on a monthly basis could be accessed at <u>[https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/Pr_DataRelease.aspx?SectionID=369&DateFilter=Year](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/Pr_DataRelease.aspx?SectionID=369&DateFilter=Year)</u>.
