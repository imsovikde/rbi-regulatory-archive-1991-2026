August 21, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0285 on August 21, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 18, 2017) was

64.1048. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 18, 2017 August 21, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.2078 75.2143 **1 GBP** 82.6183 82.3983 **100 YEN** 58.63 58.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
