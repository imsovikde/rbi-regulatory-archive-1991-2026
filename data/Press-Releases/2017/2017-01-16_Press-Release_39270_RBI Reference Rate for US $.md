January 16, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.1558 on January 16, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 13, 2017) was

68.2310. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 13, 2017 January 16, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.4750 72.4155 **1 GBP** 82.9757 81.9982 **100 YEN** 59.40 59.79 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
