## Dissemination of Unit-level Data on Inflation Expectations

## Survey of Households (IESH)

The Reserve Bank of India has been conducting the Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) since 2008 and the summary data on IESH are released in the Bank’s website after finalisation of each round. In order to encourage research, it has been decided to disseminate unit-level data on the IESH through the Reserve Bank’s data warehouse, the ‘Database on Indian Economy’ (<u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>), with a lag of one quarter, after the release of the consolidated results of each round of the survey. With this press release, unit-level data are being released for the March 2014 round onwards, which will be followed up by data prior to March 2014. Meta data on various items are also provided.

## It may be noted that:

i) responses of households on price and inflation may be based on their own consumption baskets; and ii) households’ assessments are subjective, guided by their own understanding of the subject, and may lack internal consistency
