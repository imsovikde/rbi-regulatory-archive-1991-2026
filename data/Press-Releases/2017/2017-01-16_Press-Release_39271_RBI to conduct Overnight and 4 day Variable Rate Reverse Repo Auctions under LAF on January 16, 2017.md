## January 16, 2017

**RBI to conduct Overnight and 4 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on January 16, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (January 16, 2017, Monday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
