February 10, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9367 on February 10, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 09, 2017) was

67.0123. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 09, 2017 February 10, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.5423 71.3344 **1 GBP** 83.8190 83.7713 **100 YEN** 59.71 58.89 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
