## Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on November 29, 2017

In the underwriting auctions conducted on November 29, 2017 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

||||||(|crore)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten||ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|
|GoI FRB 2024|3,000|1512|1488|3,000||0.13|
|6.79% GS 2027|8,000|4011|3989|8,000||0.19|
|7.73% GS 2034|2,000|1008|992|2,000||0.54|
|7.06% GS 2046|2,000|1008|992|2,000||0.74|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on November 30, 2017.
