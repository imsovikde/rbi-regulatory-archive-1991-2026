## August 02, 2017

# RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – June 2017</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – June 2017</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Round 47</u> iv. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS) –</u> <u>Round 37 (Q4:2016-17)</u>
v. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Round 78 (Q1:2017-18)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the
Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
