# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8064 on November 07, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 06, 2017) was

64.7267. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 06, 2017 November 07, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.1801 75.2273 **1 GBP** 84.6366 85.3371 **100 YEN** 56.66 56.85 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
