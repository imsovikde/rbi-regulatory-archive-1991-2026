October 13, 2017 **RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: August 2017** The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of August 2017 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** **(US$ Million)** **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)** July-17 13,178 7,334 August-17 13,701 8,658

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
