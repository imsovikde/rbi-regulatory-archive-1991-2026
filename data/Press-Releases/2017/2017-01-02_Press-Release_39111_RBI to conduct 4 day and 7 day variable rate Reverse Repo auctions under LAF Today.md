## January 02, 2017

**RBI to conduct 4 day and 7 day variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (January 2, 2017, Monday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified

## Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal

1 500 4 11.00 am to 11.30 am January 6, 2017 (Friday)

2 500 7 11.00 am to 11.30 am January 9, 2017 (Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
