## April 06, 2017

**RBI to conduct 3 day, 6 day, 14 day and 56 day Variable rate Reverse Repo** **auctions under LAF on April 7, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 7, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
