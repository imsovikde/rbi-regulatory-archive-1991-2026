## RBI releases Report on “Measuring Productivity at the Industry Level:

## The India KLEMS Database (Version 3)

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the Report on “<u>Measuring Productivity at the Industry Level: The India KLEMS Database-Data</u> <u>Manual 2016 (Version 3)</u> along with <u>time series data on productivity for 27 industries</u> covering the period 1980-81 to 2014-15, prepared under the India KLEMS [capital

(K), labour (L), energy (E), material (M) and services (S)] research project.
