## RBI to conduct 3 day, 7 day and 34 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on January 20, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions tomorrow (January 20, 2017, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|34|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 23, 2017 (Thursday)|
|2|400|7|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|January 27, 2017 (Friday)|
|3|400|3|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|January 23, 2017 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
