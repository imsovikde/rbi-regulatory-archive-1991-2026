February 03, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3825 on February 03, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 02, 2017) was

67.4480. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 02, 2017 February 03, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.7966 72.4429 **1 GBP** 85.4903 84.3225 **100 YEN** 59.91 59.60 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
