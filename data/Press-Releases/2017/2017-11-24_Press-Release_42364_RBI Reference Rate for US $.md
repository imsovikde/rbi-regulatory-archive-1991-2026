# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.7328 on November 24, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 23, 2017) was

64.7949. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 23, 2017 November 24, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.6264 76.7213 **1 GBP** 86.3327 86.0364 **100 YEN** 58.22 58.07 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
