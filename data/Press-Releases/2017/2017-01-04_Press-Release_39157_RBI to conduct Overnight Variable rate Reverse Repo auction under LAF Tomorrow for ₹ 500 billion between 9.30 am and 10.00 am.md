## January 04, 2017

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF

## Tomorrow for 500 billion between 9.30 am and 10.00 am

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction for a notified amount of 500 billion tomorrow (January 05, 2017, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between 9.30 am and 10.00 am as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo will take place on January 06, 2017 (Friday).
