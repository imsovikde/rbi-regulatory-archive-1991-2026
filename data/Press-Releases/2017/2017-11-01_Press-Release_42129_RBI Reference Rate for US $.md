# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5256 on November 1, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 31, 2017) was

64.7745. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 31, 2017 November 1, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.4234 75.0755 **1 GBP** 85.5995 85.6706 **100 YEN** 57.27 56.70 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
