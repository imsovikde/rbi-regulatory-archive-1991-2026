March 16, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.3836 on March 16, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 15, 2017) was

65.5146. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **March 15, 2017 March 16, 2017** **1 EUR** 69.6224 70.1893 **1 GBP** 80.0654 80.2649 **100 YEN** 57.08 57.73 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
