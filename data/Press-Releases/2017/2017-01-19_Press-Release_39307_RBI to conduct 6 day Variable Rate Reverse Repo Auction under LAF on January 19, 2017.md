January 19, 2017 **RBI to conduct 6 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under** **LAF on January 19, 2017**

1.The Reserve Bank of India will conduct a 6 day variable rate Reverse Repo
auction today (January 19, 2017, Thursday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

||Notified|Tenor|||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Sl. No.|Amount (billion)|(day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|1|400|6|11.00 am to 11.30 am|January 25, 2017 (Wednesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
