# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1065 on December 18, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 15, 2017) was

64.0958. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 15, 2017 December 18, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.5241 75.4277 **1 GBP** 86.1319 85.5437 **100 YEN** 57.13 56.90 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
