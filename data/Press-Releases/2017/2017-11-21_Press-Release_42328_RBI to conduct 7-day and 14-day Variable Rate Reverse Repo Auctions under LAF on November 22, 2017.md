November 21, 2017 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo** **Auctions under LAF on November 22, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on November 22, 2017, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
