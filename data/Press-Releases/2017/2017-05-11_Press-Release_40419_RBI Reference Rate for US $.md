May 11, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.4380 on May 11, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 09, 2017) was

64.5065. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 09, 2017 May 11, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.4669 70.1472 **1 GBP** 83.5295 83.4150 **100 YEN** 56.90 56.48 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
