## October 24, 2017

**th** **15 Round of Quarterly Services and Infrastructure** **Outlook Survey (SIOS) – Q3:2017-18**

The Reserve Bank has been conducting the Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS) since 2014-15. The survey is th conducted on a quarterly basis. The <u>15 round</u> of the SIOS seeks an assessment of the business situation for the current quarter (Q3:2017-18,

*i.e.*, October-December 2017) from selected companies in the services and infrastructure sectors in India, and their outlook for the ensuing quarter (Q4:2017-18, *i.e.*, January-March 2018).
2. Companies in the Services and Infrastructure sectors are encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> along with the guidelines from the website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The duly authenticated filled- in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed or faxed as per the contact details given below. The responses of the individual companies will be kept strictly confidential.
