August 23, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1272 on August 23, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 22, 2017) was

64.1099. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **August 22, 2017 August 23, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.6497 75.4072 **1 GBP** 82.5992 82.2303 **100 YEN** 58.69 58.58 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
