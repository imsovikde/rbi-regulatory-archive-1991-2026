August 16, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.2428 on August 16, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 14, 2017) was

64.0253. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 14, 2017 August 16, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.7035 75.3761 **1 GBP** 83.2777 82.6162 **100 YEN** 58.43 58.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
