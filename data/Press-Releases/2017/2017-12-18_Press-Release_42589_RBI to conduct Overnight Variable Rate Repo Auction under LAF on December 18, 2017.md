December 18, 2017 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF on December 18, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Repo Auction on December 18, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified**

|Sl.|Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(` billion)|(day/s)|||
|1|300|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|December 19, 2017 (Tuesday)|

Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
