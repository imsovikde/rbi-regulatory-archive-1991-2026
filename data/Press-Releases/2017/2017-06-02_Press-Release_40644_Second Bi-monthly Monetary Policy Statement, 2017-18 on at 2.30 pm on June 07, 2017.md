June 02, 2017 **Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18 on at 2.30 pm on June 07, 2017**

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on June 6 and 7, 2017 for the Second Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2017-18. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on June 7, 2017.
