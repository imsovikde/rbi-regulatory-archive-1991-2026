## November 17, 2017

## RBI withdraws OMO Sale Auction of Government of India Dated Securities

In view of the recent market developments and based on a fresh review of the current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to withdraw the <u>Open</u> <u>Market Sale Operations scheduled for November 23, 2017 (Thursday)</u>.
