## RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo

## auctions under LAF on April 10, 2017 – Revised

With reference to the <u>Press Release dated April 10, 2017</u> on the captioned subject, it has been decided to revise the notified amounts of the Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions as under:

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 24, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|250|7|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 17, 2017 (Monday)|
|3|450|1|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|April 11, 2017 (Tuesday)|
