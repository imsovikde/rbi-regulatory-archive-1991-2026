## March 24, 2017

## All Agency Banks and select offices of RBI to remain open on all days from

## March 25, 2017 to April 1, 2017

To facilitate government receipt and payment functions, all <u>Agency Banks</u> <u>have been advised</u> to keep all their bank branches dealing with government business open on all days in the current financial year and on April 1, 2017 (including Saturday, Sunday and all holidays).

The concerned departments of the Reserve Bank undertaking government business will also remain open on the above days.
