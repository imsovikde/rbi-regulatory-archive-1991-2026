# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5764 on November 03, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 02, 2017) was

64.5930. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 02, 2017 November 03, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.2831 75.3348 **1 GBP** 85.7860 84.4272 **100 YEN** 56.72 56.66 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
