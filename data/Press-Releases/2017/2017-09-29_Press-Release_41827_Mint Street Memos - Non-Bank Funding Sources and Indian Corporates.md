## September 29, 2017

## Mint Street Memos-Non-Bank Funding Sources and Indian Corporates

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the sixth release under the series `<u>Mint Street Memos (MSM)</u>’ titled “<u>Non-Bank Funding Sources and Indian</u> <u>Corporates</u>”.

The paper authored by Dr. Apoorva Javadekar from the Centre for Advanced Financial Research and Learning (CAFRAL) analyses the non-bank funding sources for Indian corporate sector.

The views and opinions expressed in MSM series are those of the authors and do not necessarily represent the views of the RBI.
