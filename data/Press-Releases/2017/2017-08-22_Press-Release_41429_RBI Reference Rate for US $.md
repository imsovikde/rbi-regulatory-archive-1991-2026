August 22, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1099 on August 22, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 21, 2017) was

64.0285. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 21, 2017 August 22, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.2143 75.6497 **1 GBP** 82.3983 82.5992 **100 YEN** 58.68 58.69 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
