## RBI to conduct 2 day and 14 day Variable rate reverse repo auctions under

## LAF on January 25, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 25, 2017, Wednesday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|300|2|10.00 am to 10.30 am|January 27, 2017 (Friday)|
|2|300|14|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 08, 2017 (Wednesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
