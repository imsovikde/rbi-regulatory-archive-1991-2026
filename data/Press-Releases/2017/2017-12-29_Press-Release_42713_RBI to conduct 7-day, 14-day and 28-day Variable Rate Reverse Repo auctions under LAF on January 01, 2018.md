December 29, 2017 **RBI to conduct 7-day, 14-day and 28-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on January 01, 2018** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 01, 2018, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
