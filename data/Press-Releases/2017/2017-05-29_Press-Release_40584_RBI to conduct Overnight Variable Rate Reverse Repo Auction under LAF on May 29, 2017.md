## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on May 29, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct an Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on May 29, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
