## July 14, 2017

## Shri Subhash Chandra Garg nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Shri Subhash Chandra Garg, Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, New Delhi as a Director on the Central Board of Directors of Reserve Bank of India vice Shri Shaktikanta Das. The nomination of Shri Subhash Chandra Garg is effective from July 12, 2017 and until further orders.
