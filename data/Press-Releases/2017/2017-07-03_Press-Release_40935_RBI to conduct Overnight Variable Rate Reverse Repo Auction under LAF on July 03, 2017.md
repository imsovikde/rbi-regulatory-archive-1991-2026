July 03, 2017 **RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on July 03, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on July 03, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(` billion)|(day)||Reversal|
|1|200|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|July 4, 2017 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
