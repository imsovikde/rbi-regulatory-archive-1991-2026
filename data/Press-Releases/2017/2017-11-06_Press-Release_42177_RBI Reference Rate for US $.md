# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.7267 on November 06, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 03, 2017) was

64.5764. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 03, 2017 November 06, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.3348 75.1801 **1 GBP** 84.4272 84.6366 **100 YEN** 56.66 56.66 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
