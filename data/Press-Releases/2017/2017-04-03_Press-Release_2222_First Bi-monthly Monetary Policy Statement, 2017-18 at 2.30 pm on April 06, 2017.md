April 03, 2017 **First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18 at 2.30 pm on April 06, 2017**

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on April 5 and 6, 2017 for the First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2017-18. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on April 6, 2017.
