## September 29, 2017

## WMA Limit for Government of India for the second half of the Financial Year

## 2017-18 (October 2017 to March 2018)

It has been decided, in consultation with the Government of India, that the limit for Ways and Means Advances (WMA) for the second half of the financial year 2017- 18 (October 2017 to March 2018) will be ` 25,000 crore.

The Reserve Bank may trigger fresh floatation of market loans when the Government of India utilises 75 per cent of the WMA limit.

The Reserve Bank retains the flexibility to revise the limit at any time, in consultation with the Government of India, taking into consideration the prevailing circumstances.
