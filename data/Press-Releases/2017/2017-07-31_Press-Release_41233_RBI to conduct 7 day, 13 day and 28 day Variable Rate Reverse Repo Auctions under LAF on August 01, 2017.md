## July 31, 2017

## RBI to conduct 7 day, 13 day and 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on August 01, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on August 01, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
