## May 23, 2017

## RBI to conduct 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on May 24, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on May 24, 2017, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
