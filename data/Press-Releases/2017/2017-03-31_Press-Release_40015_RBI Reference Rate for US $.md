March 31, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8386 on March 31, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 30, 2017) was

64.9325. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 30, 2017 March 31, 2017** **1 EUR** 69.8739 69.2476 **1 GBP** 80.7501 80.8797 **100 YEN** 58.43 57.96 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
