June 30, 2017 **RBI launches the Survey on Foreign Liabilities and Assets of Mutual Fund Companies: 2016-17 round**

The Reserve Bank of India conducts the annual survey of ‘Foreign Liabilities and Assets of Mutual Funds and Asset Management Companies’. The 2016-17 round of the survey is now being launched.

Asset Management companies are required to submit annual returns on <u>Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA)</u> in the format (MS-Excel template) available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> –> Forms –> <u>Foreign Exchange Management Act</u> <u>Forms</u>) via <u>e-mail</u> by July 15, 2017.

In addition, <u>Mutual Fund companies are required to fill the survey schedule</u> <u>(Schedule-4)</u>, which is also available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u> –> Forms –> Survey) and send via <u>e-mail</u>.

Both <u>Hindi</u> and <u>English formats</u> are available and reporting companies may use either of them. In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact the following address:

The Director, External Liabilities and Assets Statistics Division (ELASD), Department of Statistics and Information Management (DSIM), Reserve Bank of India, C9-5th floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai-400051.

Phone: (91-22) 26571265 / 26578348 / 26578340 Fax: (91-22) 2657 0848 <u>Email</u>.
