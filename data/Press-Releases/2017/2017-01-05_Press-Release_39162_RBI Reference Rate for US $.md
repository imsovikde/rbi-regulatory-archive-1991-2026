January 05, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7884 on January 05, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 04, 2017) was

68.1791. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 04, 2017 January 05, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.9949 71.5574 **1 GBP** 83.5330 83.7051 **100 YEN** 57.81 58.33 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
