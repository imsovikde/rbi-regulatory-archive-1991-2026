## RBI’s Monthly Bulletin for April 2017

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>April 2017</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for the</u> <u>Year 2017-18, Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC), Reserve Bank of</u> <u>India</u>; <u>Monetary Policy Report-April 2017</u>. Apart from the regular data tables, this issue of the Bulletin also carries data on Small Savings, Ownership Pattern of Central and State Governments Securities and Combined Receipts and Disbursements of the Central and State Governments in its Current Statistics section.
