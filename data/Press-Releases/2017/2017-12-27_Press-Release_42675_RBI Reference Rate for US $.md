# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1577 on December 27, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 26, 2017) was

64.0538. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **December 26, 2017 December 27, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.0255 76.2258 **1 GBP** 85.6720 85.8366 **100 YEN** 56.53 56.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
