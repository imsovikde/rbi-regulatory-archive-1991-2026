August 11, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.1693 on August 11, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 10, 2017) was

63.9437. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 10, 2017 August 11, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.0635 75.4567 **1 GBP** 83.0053 83.2661 **100 YEN** 58.15 58.76 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
