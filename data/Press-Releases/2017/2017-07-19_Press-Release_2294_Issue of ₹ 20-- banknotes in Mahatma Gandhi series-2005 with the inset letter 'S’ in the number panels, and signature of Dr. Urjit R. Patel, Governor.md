## Issue of 20/- banknotes in Mahatma Gandhi series-2005 with the inset letter

## 'S’ in the number panels, and signature of Dr. Urjit R. Patel, Governor,

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue 20/- denomination banknotes in the Mahatma Gandhi Series, with the inset letter 'S' in both the number panels.

The design of these banknotes to be issued now is similar in all respects to the 20/- banknotes in the same series issued earlier (For details- refer <u>Press Release</u> <u>No. 2016-2017/678 dated September 15, 2016</u>).

All the banknotes in the denomination of 20/- issued by the Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender.
