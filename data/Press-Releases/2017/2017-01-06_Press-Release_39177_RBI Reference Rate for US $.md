January 06, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.9522 on January 06, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 05, 2017) was

67.7884. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 05, 2017 January 06, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.5574 71.8662 **1 GBP** 83.7051 84.0976 **100 YEN** 58.33 58.50 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
