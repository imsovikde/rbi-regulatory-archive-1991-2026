## RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under

## LAF on October 31, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on October 31, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|100|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|November 14, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|2|150|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|November 07, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
