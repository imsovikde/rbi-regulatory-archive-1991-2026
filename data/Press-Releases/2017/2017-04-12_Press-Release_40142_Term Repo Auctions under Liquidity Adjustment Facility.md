## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on August 22, <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount (Rs.Billion)|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|April 13, 2017 (Thursday)|215|15|April 28, 2017 (Friday)|
|2|April 18, 2017 (Tuesday)|210|14|May 02, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|3|April 21, 2017 (Friday)|210|14|May 05, 2017 (Friday)|
|4|April 25, 2017 (Tuesday)|210|14|May 09, 2017 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
