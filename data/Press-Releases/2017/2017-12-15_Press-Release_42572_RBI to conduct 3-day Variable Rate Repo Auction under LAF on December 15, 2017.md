## RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF on December 15, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Repo Auction on December 15, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
