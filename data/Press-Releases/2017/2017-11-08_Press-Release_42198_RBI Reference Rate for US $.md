# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0592 on November 08, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 07, 2017) was

64.8064. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 07, 2017 November 08, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.2273 75.3841 **1 GBP** 85.3371 85.5724 **100 YEN** 56.85 57.12 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
