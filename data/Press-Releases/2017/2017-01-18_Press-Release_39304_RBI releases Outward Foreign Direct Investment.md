## January 18, 2017

## RBI releases Outward Foreign Direct Investment

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on <u>Outward Foreign</u> <u>Direct Investment (OFDI)</u>, both under Automatic Route and the Approval Route,

(i) for the month of December 2016; (ii) comparative position for the month of November and December 2016; (iii) for the month of December 2015 and December 2016; and (iv) country wise OFDI data for the quarter October-December 2016.
