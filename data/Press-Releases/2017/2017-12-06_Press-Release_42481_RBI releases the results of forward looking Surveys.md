## December 06, 2017

## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) –November 2017</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – November 2017</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators–Round 49</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to
the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
