## January 24, 2017

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under

## LAF on January 24, 2017 for 600 billion

## (instead of 400 billion as announced earlier)

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction today (January 24, 2017, Tuesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

Revised Notified Tenor Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal (day) ( billion) 1 600 1 4.00 pm to 4.30 pm January 25, 2017 (Wednesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
