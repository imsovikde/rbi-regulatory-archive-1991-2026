## March 15, 2017

## Appointment of member on Local Board of Reserve Bank of India

In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 9 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (2 of 1934), the Central Government has appointed Shri Dilip S. Shanghvi as Member on the Western Area Local Board of Reserve Bank of India, for a period of four years with effect from March 11, 2017 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
