January 18, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.9364 on January 18, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 17, 2017) was

68.0509. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 17, 2017 January 18, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.5014 72.5968 **1 GBP** 82.4573 83.8403 **100 YEN** 59.98 59.97 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
