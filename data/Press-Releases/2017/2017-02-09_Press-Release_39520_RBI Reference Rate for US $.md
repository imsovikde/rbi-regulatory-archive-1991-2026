February 09, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.0123 on February 09, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 08, 2017) was

67.3058. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 08, 2017 February 09, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.8759 71.5423 **1 GBP** 84.1659 83.8190 **100 YEN** 59.94 59.71 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
