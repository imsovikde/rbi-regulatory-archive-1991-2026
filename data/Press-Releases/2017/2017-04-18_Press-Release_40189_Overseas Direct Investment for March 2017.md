## April 18, 2017

## Overseas Direct Investment for March 2017

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on <u>Outward Foreign</u> <u>Direct Investment (OFDI)</u> indicating the comparative position for the month of February 2017 and March 2017 and for the month of March 2016 and March 2017 both under Automatic Route and the Approval Route, for the month of March 2017.
