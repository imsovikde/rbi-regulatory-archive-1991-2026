February 23, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8351 on February 23, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 22, 2017) was

66.9644. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 22, 2017 February 23, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.5403 70.5444 **1 GBP** 83.6921 83.1228 **100 YEN** 58.98 59.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
