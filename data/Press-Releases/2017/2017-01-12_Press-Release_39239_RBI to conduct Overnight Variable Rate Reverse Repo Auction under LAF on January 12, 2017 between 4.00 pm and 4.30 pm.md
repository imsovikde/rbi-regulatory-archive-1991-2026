## January 12, 2017

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF

## on January 12, 2017 between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auction today (January 12, 2017, Thursday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
