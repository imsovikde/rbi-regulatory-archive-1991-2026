August 03, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.6314 on August 03, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 02, 2017) was

64.0690. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 02, 2017 August 03, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.7488 75.3905 **1 GBP** 84.6416 84.1589 **100 YEN** 57.86 57.53 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
