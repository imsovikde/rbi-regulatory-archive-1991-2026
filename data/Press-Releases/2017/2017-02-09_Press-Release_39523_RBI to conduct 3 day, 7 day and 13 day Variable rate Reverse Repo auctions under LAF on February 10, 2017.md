## RBI to conduct 3 day, 7 day and 13 day Variable rate Reverse Repo

## auctions under LAF on February 10, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on February 10, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|200|7|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 17, 2017 (Friday)|
|2|300|13|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 23, 2017 (Thursday)|
|3|200|3|12.00 noon to 12.30 pm|February 13, 2017 (Monday)|
|4|300|3|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|February 13, 2017 (Monday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
