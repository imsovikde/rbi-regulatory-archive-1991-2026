March 16, 2017 **RBI to conduct 3 day, 7 day and 14 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo** **Auctions under LAF on March 17, 2017** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on March 17, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)||||
|1|400|3|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 20, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|300|7|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 24, 2017 (Friday)|
|3|350|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|March 31, 2017 (Friday)|
|4|400|3|3.45 pm to 4.15 pm|March 20, 2017 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
