## Issuance of T- Bills under Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS)

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following auctions of T-Bills under the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS) using "Multiple Price Auction" method.

|Date of Issuance|Instrument|Amount (billion)|Maturity|
|---|---|---|---|
|April 17, 2017 (Monday)|329 Days T-bill|250|March 12, 2018 (Monday)|
|April 24, 2017 (Monday)|323 Days T-bill|250|March 13, 2018 (Tuesday)|
|May 2, 2017 (Tuesday)|317 Days T-bill|250|March 15, 2018 (Thursday)|
|May 8, 2017 (Monday)|312 Days T-bill|250|March 16, 2018 (Friday)|

The bids for the auctions should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system between 10.00 am and 11.00 am. Result will be announced on the same day. Payment by successful bidders will be on T+0 basis.

The sale of T-Bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the <u>General Notification. F.No 4 (8)-W & M/2015 dated May 26, 2016</u> issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time. The Non-Competitive Bidding Scheme is not extended to these T-Bill auctions.
