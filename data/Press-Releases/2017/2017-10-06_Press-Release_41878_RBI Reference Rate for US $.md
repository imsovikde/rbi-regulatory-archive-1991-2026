# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.2276 on October 06, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 05, 2017) was

65.1758. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
