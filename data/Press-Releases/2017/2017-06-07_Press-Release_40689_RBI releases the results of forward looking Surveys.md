June 07, 2017 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys** The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

I. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – May 2017</u> II. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – May 2017</u>
III. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – May 2017</u> <u>(Round 46)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
