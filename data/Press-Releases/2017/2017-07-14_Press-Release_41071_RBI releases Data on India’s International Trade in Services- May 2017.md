## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: May 2017

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of May 2017 are given in the following Table.

||Table: International Trade in Services|(US$ Million)|
|---|---|---|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|April-2017|12,904|7,222|
|May-2017 Note : Data are provisional.|13,430|7,615|

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
