# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9226 on October 17, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 16, 2017) was

64.7603. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 16, 2017 October 17, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.3912 76.4204 **1 GBP** 86.0082 86.0354 **100 YEN** 57.93 57.87 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
