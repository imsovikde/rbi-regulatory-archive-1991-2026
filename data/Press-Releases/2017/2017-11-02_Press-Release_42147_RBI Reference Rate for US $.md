# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5930 on November 02, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 01, 2017) was

64.5256. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 01, 2017 November 02, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.0755 75.2831 **1 GBP** 85.6706 85.7860 **100 YEN** 56.70 56.72 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
