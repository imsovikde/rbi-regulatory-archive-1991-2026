## January 12, 2017

## RBI to conduct 3 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF

## on January 13, 2017 between 9.30 am and 10.00 am

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auction tomorrow (January 13, 2017, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
