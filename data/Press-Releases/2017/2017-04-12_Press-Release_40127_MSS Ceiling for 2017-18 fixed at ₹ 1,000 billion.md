## MSS Ceiling for 2017-18 fixed at 1,000 billion

In accordance with the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), the ceiling for gross issuance under the MSS for the fiscal year 2017-18 has been fixed at 1,000 billion. The current MSS outstanding balance is ‘Nil’.
