## January 20, 2017

**RBI to conduct Overnight, 11 day and 28 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on January 23, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 23, 2017, Monday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 20, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|400|11|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|February 3, 2017 (Friday)|
|3|400|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|January 24, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
