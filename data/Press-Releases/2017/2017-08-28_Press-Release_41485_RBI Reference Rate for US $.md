64.0676. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 24, 2017 August 28, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.5805 76.2162 **1 GBP** 81.9361 82.3605 **100 YEN** 58.67 58.53 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
