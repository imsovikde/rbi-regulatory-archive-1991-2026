## RBI launches Survey on International Trade in Banking

## Services (ITBS): 2016-17 round

The Reserve Bank of India has launched the 2016-17 round of its <u>Survey on</u> <u>International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS)</u>.

The survey, conducted annually since 2006-07, provides information on financial services rendered by branches / subsidiaries of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India, based on explicit / implicit fee / commission charged to customers.
