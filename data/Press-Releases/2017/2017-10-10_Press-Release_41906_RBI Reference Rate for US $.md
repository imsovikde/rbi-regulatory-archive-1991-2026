# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.2652 on October 10, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 09, 2017) was

65.3074. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 09, 2017 October 10, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.5990 76.8302 **1 GBP** 85.6311 85.9412 **100 YEN** 57.99 57.95 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
