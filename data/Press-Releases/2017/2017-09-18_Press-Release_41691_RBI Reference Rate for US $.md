September 18, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0371 on September 18, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 15, 2017) was

64.0774. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 15, 2017 September 18, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.3995 76.4923 **1 GBP** 86.1200 87.0841 **100 YEN** 57.92 57.57 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
