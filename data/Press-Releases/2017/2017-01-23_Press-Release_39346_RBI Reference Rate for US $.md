## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.0843 on January 23, 2017.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 20, 2017) was

68.0883. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
