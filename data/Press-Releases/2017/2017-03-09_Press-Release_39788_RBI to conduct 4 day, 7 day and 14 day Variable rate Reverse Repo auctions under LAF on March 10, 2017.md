## RBI to conduct 4 day, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions

## under LAF on March 10, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on March 10, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|700|4|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 14, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|2|400|7|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 17, 2017 (Friday)|
|3|200|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|March 24, 2017 (Friday)|
|4|500|4|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|March 14, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
