September 28, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.7604 on September 28, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 27, 2017) was

65.6947. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 27, 2017 September 28, 2017** **1 EUR** 77.3686 77.1764 **1 GBP** 88.1426 87.9808 **100 YEN** 58.40 58.18 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
