## RBI to conduct Overnight, 8 day and 15 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on March 21, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on March 21, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|1|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 22, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|2|500|8|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 29, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|3|300|15|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|April 5, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|4|500|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|March 22, 2017 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
