June 13, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.4453 on June 13, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 12, 2017) was

64.3407. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **June 12, 2017 June 13, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.1324 72.1465 **1 GBP** 82.0859 81.7424 **100 YEN** 58.36 58.59 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
