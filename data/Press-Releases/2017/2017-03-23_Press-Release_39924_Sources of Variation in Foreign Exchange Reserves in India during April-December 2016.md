## March 23, 2017

## Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during April-December 2016

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released <u>balance of payments (BoP)</u> <u>data for October-December 2016</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during April- December 2016 have been compiled.

## Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves: April-December 2016

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), the foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 14.2 billion during April-December 2016 as compared with an increase of US$ 14.6 billion during April-December 2015. The foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including the valuation effects) decreased by US$ 1.3 billion during April-December 2016 as against an increase of US$ 8.7 billion during the same period of the preceding year (Table 2).

The valuation loss, mainly reflecting the appreciation of the US dollar against major currencies, amounted to US$ 15.5 billion during April-December 2016 as compared with US$ 5.9 billion during the same period of the preceding year.
