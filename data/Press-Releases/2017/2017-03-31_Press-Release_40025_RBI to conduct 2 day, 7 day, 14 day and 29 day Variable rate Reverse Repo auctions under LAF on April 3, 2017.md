## March 31, 2017

**RBI to conduct 2 day, 7 day, 14 day and 29 day Variable rate Reverse Repo** **auctions under LAF on April 3, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 3, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
