July 21, 2017 **RBI to conduct 7 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under LAF on July 24, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on July 24, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
