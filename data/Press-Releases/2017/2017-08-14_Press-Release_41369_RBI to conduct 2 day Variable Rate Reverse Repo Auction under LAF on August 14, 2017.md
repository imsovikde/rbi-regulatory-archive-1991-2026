## RBI to conduct 2 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auction under LAF on August 14, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on August 14, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Notified Amount|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day)|||
|1|100|2|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|August 16, 2017 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
