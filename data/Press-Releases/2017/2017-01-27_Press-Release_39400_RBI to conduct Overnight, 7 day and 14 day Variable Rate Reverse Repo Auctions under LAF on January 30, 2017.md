## RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on January 30, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on January 30, 2017, Monday as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Amount Notified|Tenor|
|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day)|
|1|400|7|
|2|400|14|
|3|400|1|
|4|400|1|

## Window Timing Date of Reversal

|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 6, 2017 (Monday)|
|---|---|
|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 13, 2017 (Monday)|
|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|January 31, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|January 31, 2017 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
