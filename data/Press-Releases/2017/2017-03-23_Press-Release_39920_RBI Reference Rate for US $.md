March 23, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.4220 on March 23, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 22, 2017) was

65.4881. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 22, 2017 March 23, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.7206 70.6688 **1 GBP** 81.6833 81.8495 **100 YEN** 58.70 58.81 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
