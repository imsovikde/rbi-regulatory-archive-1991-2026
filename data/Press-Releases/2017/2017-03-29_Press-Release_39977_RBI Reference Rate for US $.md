March 29, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9552 on March 29, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 27, 2017) was

65.0892. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **March 27, 2017 March 29, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.6739 70.1061 **1 GBP** 81.6479 80.4795 **100 YEN** 59.08 58.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
