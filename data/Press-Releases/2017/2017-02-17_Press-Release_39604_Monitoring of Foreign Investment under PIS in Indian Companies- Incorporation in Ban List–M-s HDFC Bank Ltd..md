## February 17,2017

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List–M/s HDFC Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding by American Depository Receipts (ADR)/Global Depository Receipts (GDR)/ Foreign institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs)/ Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/ Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs**)** in <u>M/s HDFC Bank Ltd</u> has crossed the overall limit of 74% of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs)/ Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/ Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs**)**.
