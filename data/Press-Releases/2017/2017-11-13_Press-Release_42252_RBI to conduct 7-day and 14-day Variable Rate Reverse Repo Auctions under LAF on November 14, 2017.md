November 13, 2017 **RBI to conduct 7-day and 14-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF on November 14, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on November 14, 2017, Tuesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl.|Amount Notified|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(billion)|(day/s)|||
|1|100|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|November 28, 2017 (Tuesday) November 21, 2017|

2 150 7 2.30 pm to 3.00 pm (Tuesday)

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
