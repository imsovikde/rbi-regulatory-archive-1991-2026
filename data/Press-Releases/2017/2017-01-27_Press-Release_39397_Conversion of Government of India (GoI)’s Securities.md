## January 27, 2017

## Conversion of Government of India (GoI)’s Securities

The Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with the Government of India, has converted four securities from its portfolio maturing in 2017-18 having total face value of about 37,078 crore to longer tenor securities maturing in 2024-25 and 2029-30. The transaction has been conducted on January 25, 2017 at the Fixed Income Money Market and Derivatives Association of India (FIMMDA) prices.
