## February 03, 2017

**FIIs/FPIs can now invest from 24 per cent to 36 per cent under PIS in M/s Cera Sanitaryware Limited**

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Foreign Portfolios Investors (FPIs) can now invest from 24% to 36 per cent of the paid up capital of <u>M/s Cera Sanitaryware Limited</u> under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS).

The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s Cera Sanitaryware Limited has passed necessary resolutions at its Board of Directors’ level and a special resolution by its general body agreeing for enhancing the limit from 24% to 36%.

The Reserve Bank has notified this under FEMA 1999.
