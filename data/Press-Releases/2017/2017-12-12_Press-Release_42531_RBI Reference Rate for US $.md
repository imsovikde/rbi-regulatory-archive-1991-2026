# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.4834 on December 12, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 11, 2017) was

64.3616. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 11, 2017 December 12, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.8051 75.9292 **1 GBP** 86.3282 86.0466 **100 YEN** 56.68 56.84 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
