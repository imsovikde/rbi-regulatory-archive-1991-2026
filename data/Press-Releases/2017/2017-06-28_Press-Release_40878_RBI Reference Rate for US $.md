June 28, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.5288 on June 28, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 27, 2017) was

64.4572. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 27, 2017 June 28, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.1921 73.3757 **1 GBP** 82.0798 82.6614 **100 YEN** 57.71 57.44 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
