February 13, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9663 on February 13, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 10, 2017) was

66.9367. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 10, 2017 February 13, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.3344 71.1718 **1 GBP** 83.7713 83.7614 **100 YEN** 58.89 58.89 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
