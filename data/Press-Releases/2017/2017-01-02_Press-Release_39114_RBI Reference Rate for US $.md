January 02, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.0225 on January 02, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 30, 2016) was

67.9547. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 30, 2016 January 02, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.6175 71.5121 **1 GBP** 83.4212 83.9670 **100 YEN** 58.22 58.19 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
