## Bilingual/Hindi House Magazine Competition for

## Public Sector Banks/ Financial Institutions – (2016-17)- Inviting Entries

The Reserve Bank of India has been conducting a Bilingual/Hindi House Magazine Competition for Public Sector Banks and Financial Institutions to encourage the use of Hindi (Rajbhasha). All PSBs/FIs are invited to send six copies each of Bilingual/Hindi House Magazine published during April 01, 2016 to March 31, 2017 alongwith the prescribed format duly filled in. The house magazines will be adjudged by an evaluation committee and Rajbhasha Department of Reserve Bank of India will award shield/certificates to the winners.

Last date of receipt of entries is November 06, 2017.

The entries are to be sent at the following address:

Deputy General Manager In-Charge Reserve Bank of India Rajbhasha Department, Central Office C-9, Second Floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex Bandra(East), Mumbai 400 051.

The criteria of the competition are given in <u>Annex `A’</u>. The prescribed format for submitting entries is given in <u>Annex `B’</u>.
