# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.9301 on October 13, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 12, 2017) was

65.1003. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 12, 2017 October 13, 2017** **1 EUR** 77.2741 76.8772 **1 GBP** 86.2319 86.3051 **100 YEN** 57.98 58.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
