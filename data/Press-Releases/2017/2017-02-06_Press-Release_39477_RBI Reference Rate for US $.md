February 06, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.1958 on February 06, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 03, 2017) was

67.3825. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 03, 2017 February 06, 2017** **1 EUR** 72.4429 72.4035 **1 GBP** 84.3225 83.9208 **100 YEN** 59.60 59.73 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
