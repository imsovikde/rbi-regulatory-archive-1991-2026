## RBI to conduct Overnight, 14 day and 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on January 24, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions tomorrow (January 24, 2017, Tuesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|400|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 21, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|2|400|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|February 7, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|3|400|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|January 25, 2017 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.

Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
