## RBI to conduct 3 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction under

## LAF on January 17, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct a 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo auction today (January 17, 2017, Tuesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|1|750|3|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|(Friday)|
