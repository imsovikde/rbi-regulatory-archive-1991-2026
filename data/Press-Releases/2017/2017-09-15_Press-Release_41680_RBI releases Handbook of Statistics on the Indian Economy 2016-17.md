## September 15, 2017

## RBI releases Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy 2016-17

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released the nineteenth volume of its annual publication titled “<u>Handbook of Statistics (HBS) on the Indian Economy 2016-17</u>”. This publication is aimed at improving data dissemination by providing time series on various economic and financial indicators relating to the Indian economy.

The current volume contains 248 statistical tables covering national income aggregates, output, prices, money, banking, financial markets, public finances, foreign trade and balance of payments and select socio-economic indicators.

The electronic form of the Handbook can be accessed through (URL <u>[https://dbie.rbi.org.in](https://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>) "Data Base on Indian Economy (DBIE): Reserve Bank’s Data Warehouse." The tables in DBIE are updated regularly on a near-real time basis.
