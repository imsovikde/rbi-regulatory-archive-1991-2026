## RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on February 13, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on February 13, 2017, Monday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|200|7|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 20, 2017 (Monday)|
|2|300|14|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 27, 2017 (Monday)|
|3|200|1|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|February 14, 2017 (Tuesday)|
|4|400|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|February 14, 2017 (Tuesday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
