## February 07, 2017

**RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on February 8, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on February 8, 2017, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|200|7|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 15, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|2|300|14|10.00 am to 10.30 am|February 22, 2017 (Wednesday)|
|3|400|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|February 9, 2017 (Thursday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
