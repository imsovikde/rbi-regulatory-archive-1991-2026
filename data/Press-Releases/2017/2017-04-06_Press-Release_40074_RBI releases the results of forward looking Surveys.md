April 06, 2017 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys**

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey</u> <u>(OBICUS) – October-December (Round 36)</u> ii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – March 2017</u>
iii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – March 2017</u> iv. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators –</u> <u>March 2017 (Round 45)</u>

v. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q4:2016-17</u>
The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
