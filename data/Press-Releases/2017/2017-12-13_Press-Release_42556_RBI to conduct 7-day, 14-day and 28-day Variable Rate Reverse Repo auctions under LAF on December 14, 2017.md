## December 13, 2017

**RBI to conduct 7-day, 14-day and 28-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on December 14, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions on December 14, 2017, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day/s)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|100|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|January 11, 2018 (Thursday)|
|2|150|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|December 28, 2017 (Thursday)|
|3|200|7|2.30 pm to 3.00 pm|December 21, 2017 (Thursday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
