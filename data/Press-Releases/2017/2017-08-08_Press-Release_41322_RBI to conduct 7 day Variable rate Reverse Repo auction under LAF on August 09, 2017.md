August 08, 2017 **RBI to conduct 7 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF on August 09, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auction on August 09, 2017, Wednesday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|No.|(` billion)|(day)|||

3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
