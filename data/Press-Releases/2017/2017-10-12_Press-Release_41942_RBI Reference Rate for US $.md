# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.1003 on October 12, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 11, 2017) was

65.2691. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 11, 2017 October 12, 2017** **1 EUR** 77.1677 77.2741 **1 GBP** 86.1161 86.2319 **100 YEN** 58.08 57.98 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
