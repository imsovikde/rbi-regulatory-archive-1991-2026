## September 29, 2017

## Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2017-18

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on October 3 and 4, 2017 for the Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for 2017-18. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on October 4, 2017.
