February 22, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9644 on February 22, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 20, 2017) was

66.9771. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **February 20, 2017 February 22, 2017** **1 EUR** 71.0560 70.5403 **1 GBP** 83.1923 83.6921 **100 YEN** 59.16 58.98 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
