## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.4704 on June 01, 2017.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 31, 2017) was

64.5459. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
