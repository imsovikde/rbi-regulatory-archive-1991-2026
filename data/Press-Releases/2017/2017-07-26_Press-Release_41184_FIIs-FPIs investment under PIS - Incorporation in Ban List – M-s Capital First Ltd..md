## July 26, 2017

## FIIs/FPIs investment under PIS-Incorporation in Ban List –

## M/s Capital First Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Foreign Portfolios Investors (FPIs) in <u>M/s Capital First Ltd</u>. has crossed limit of 24% of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchase of shares of this company would be allowed through Stock Exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs/FPIs.
