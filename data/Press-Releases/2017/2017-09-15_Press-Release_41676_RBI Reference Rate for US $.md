September 15, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0774 on September 15, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 14, 2017) was

64.0692. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 14, 2017 September 15, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.1398 76.3995 **1 GBP** 84.6867 86.1200 **100 YEN** 58.02 57.92 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
