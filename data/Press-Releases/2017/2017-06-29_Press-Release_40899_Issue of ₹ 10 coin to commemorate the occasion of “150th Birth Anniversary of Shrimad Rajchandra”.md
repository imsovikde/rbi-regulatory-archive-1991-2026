## June 29, 2017

**Issue of** ` **10 coin to commemorate the occasion of** **th** **"150 Birth Anniversary of Shrimad Rajchandra”**

The Government of India has minted the above mentioned coin which the Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation. The coin has been released by the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India.

The design details of the coin as notified in *The Gazette of India-* *Extraordinary- Part II-Section 3-Sub-section (i) – G.S.R.641(E) dated June 23, 2017* *issued by* the Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs, New Delhi are as follows—

<u>Obverse</u>-

The face of the coin shall bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the centre with the legend **"सत्यमेि जयते"** inscribed below, flanked on the left periphery with the

word **"भारत"** in Devnagri script and on the right periphery with the word "INDIA" in English, and it shall also bear the Rupee symbol “`**”** and denominational value “10" in the International Numerals below the Lion Capitol.

## <u>Reverse-</u>

This face of the coin shall bear the portrait of “SHRIMAD RAJCHANDRA” in the centre. The inscription “श्रीमद राजचुंद्र” and “150 वीुं जयुंती” shall be written on upper left and lower left periphery of the coin in Devnagri script. The inscription TH “SHRIMAD RAJCHANDRA” and “150 Birth Anniversary” shall be written on the upper right and lower right periphery of the coin respectively. The year “1867” and “1901” in international numerals shall be written on the left and right side of the portrait of Shrimad Rajchandra.

The coin is legal tender as provided in The Coinage Act 2011. The existing coins in this denomination shall also continue to be legal tender.
