## March 08, 2017

**RBI to conduct Overnight, 7 day and 14 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on March 09, 2017**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on March 09, 2017, Thursday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (` billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|800|1|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 10, 2017 (Friday)|
|2|400|7|9.30 am to 10.00 am|March 16, 2017 (Thursday)|
|3|200|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|March 23, 2017 (Thursday)|
|4|500|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|March 10, 2017 (Friday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
