# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.3074 on October 09, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 06, 2017) was

65.2276. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 06, 2017 October 09, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.2641 76.5990 **1 GBP** 85.3112 85.6311 **100 YEN** 57.72 57.99 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
