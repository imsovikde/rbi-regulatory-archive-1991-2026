## RBI to conduct 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on May 12, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
