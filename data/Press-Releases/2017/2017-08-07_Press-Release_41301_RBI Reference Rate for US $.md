August 07, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 63.7375 on August 07, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 04, 2017) was

63.7091. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 04, 2017 August 07, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.6609 75.1019 **1 GBP** 83.7074 83.1583 **100 YEN** 57.85 57.55 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
