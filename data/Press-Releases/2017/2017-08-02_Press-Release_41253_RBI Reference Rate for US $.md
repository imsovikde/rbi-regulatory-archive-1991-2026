August 02, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0690 on August 02, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 01, 2017) was 64.0683. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 01, 2017 August 02, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.7415 75.7488 **1 GBP** 84.6214 84.6416 **100 YEN** 58.12 57.86 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
