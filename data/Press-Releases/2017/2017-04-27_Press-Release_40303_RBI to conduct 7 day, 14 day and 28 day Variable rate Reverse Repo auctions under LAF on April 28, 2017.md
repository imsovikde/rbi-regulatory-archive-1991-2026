## April 27, 2017

## RBI to conduct 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions

## under LAF on April 28, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on April 28, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
