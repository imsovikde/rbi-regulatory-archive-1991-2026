## RBI to conduct 7 day, 14 day and 28 day Variable Rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF on May 5, 2017

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auctions on May 5, 2017, Friday, as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|100|28|10.00 am to 10.30 am|June 02, 2017 (Friday)|
|2|250|14|12.00 pm to 12.30 pm|May 19, 2017 (Friday)|
|3|250|7|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|May 12, 2017 (Friday)|

2. Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates.
3. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
