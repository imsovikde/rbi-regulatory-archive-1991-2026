# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.3799 on December 04, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 30, 2017) was

64.4332. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 30, 2017 December 04, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.4887 76.3739 **1 GBP** 86.7980 86.6360 **100 YEN** 57.46 57.04 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
