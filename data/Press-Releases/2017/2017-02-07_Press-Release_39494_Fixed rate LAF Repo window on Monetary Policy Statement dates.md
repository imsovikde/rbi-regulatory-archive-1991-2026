## February 07, 2017

## Fixed rate LAF Repo window on Monetary Policy Statement dates

In continuation of the <u>Press Release dated November 30, 2015</u>, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has decided to keep the fixed rate LAF Repo window open from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. on the days of the Monetary Policy Statement starting from February 08, 2017. On other days, the window will continue to be operated upon between 9.00 a.m. and 3.00 p.m.
