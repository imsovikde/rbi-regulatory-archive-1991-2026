March 15, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.5146 on March 15, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 14, 2017) was

66.1800. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 14, 2017 March 15, 2017** **1 EUR** 70.4354 69.6224 **1 GBP** 80.5014 80.0654 **100 YEN** 57.57 57.08 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
