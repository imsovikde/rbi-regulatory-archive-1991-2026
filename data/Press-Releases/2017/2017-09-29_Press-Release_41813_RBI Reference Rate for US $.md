September 29, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.3552 on September 29, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 28, 2017) was

65.7604. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 28, 2017 September 29, 2017** **1 EUR** 77.1764 77.0603 **1 GBP** 87.9808 87.7067 **100 YEN** 58.18 58.03 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
