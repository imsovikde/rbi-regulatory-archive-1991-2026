August 01, 2017 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.0683 on August 01, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 31, 2017) was

64.0773. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 31, 2017 August 01, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.2203 75.7415 **1 GBP** 84.1591 84.6214 **100 YEN** 57.98 58.12 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
