# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.5171 on November 14, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 13, 2017) was

65.4272. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 13, 2017 November 14, 2017** **1 EUR** 76.2161 76.4781 **1 GBP** 85.8209 85.9388 **100 YEN** 57.68 57.65 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
