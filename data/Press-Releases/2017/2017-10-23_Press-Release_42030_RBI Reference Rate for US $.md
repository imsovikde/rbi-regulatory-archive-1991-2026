# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.0239 on October 23, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 18, 2017) was

65.0649. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
