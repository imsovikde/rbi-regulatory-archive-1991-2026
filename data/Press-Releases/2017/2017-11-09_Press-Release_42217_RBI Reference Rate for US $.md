# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 64.8967 on November 09, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 08, 2017) was

65.0592. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 08, 2017 November 09, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.3841 75.3061 **1 GBP** 85.5724 85.2548 **100 YEN** 57.12 57.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
