# RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 65.4272 on November 13, 2017. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 10, 2017) was

65.0147. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-
currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 10, 2017 November 13, 2017** **1 EUR** 75.7356 76.2161 **1 GBP** 85.5463 85.8209 **100 YEN** 57.34 57.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
