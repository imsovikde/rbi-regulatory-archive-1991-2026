RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India's Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.51.58 and

the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.66.59 on March 16, 2009. The corresponding rates

for the previous day (March 13, 2009) were Rs.51.67 and Rs.66.73 respectively.

Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency

quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee
