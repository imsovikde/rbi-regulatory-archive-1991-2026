## May 22, 2009

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : Punjab National Bank

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate foreign share holding in the <u>Punjab National Bank</u> had reached 18% of its paid up capital i.e., less than 2% of the sectoral cap of 20% for the public sector banks (PSBs). Therefore, any further purchases of equity shares on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) who have permission under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) will now be allowed after obtaining prior approval of Reserve Bank of India.
