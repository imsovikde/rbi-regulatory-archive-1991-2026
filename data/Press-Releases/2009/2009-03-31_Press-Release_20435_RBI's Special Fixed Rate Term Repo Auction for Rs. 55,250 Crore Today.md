## March 31, 2009

## RBI's Special Fixed Rate Term Repo Auction for Rs. 55,250 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 5.0 per cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs. 55,250 crore today i.e, March 31, 2009, due for reversal on April 15, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. Of the outstanding amount of Rs. 4,780 crore as on March 30, 2009, Rs.30 crore is due for redemption today.

The special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility is available till September 30, 2009 up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,000 crore on outstanding basis to banks exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs.
