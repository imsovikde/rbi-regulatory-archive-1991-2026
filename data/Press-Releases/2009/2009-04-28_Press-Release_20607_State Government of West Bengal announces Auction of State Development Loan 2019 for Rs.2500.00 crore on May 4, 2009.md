## April 28, 2009

## State Government of West Bengal announces Auction of

## State Development Loan 2019 for Rs.2500.00 crore on May 4, 2009

The State Government of West Bengal has announced the sale of its 10-year State Development Loan for an aggregate amount of Rs.2500.00 crore through yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on May 4, 2009 (Monday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai - 400001 should be submitted to that Office on Monday, May 4, 2009. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by

12.30 P.M on May 4, 2009. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auction will be announced on May 4, 2009 and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on May 5, 2009 (Tuesday) at Mumbai and at Kolkata Regional Office of Reserve Bank of India. The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
