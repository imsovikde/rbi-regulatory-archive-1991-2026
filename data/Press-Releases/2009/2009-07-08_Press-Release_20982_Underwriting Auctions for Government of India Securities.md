## July 08, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government of India Securities

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of “6.49 per cent Government Stock 2015” for a notified amount of Rs.5,000 crore (nominal), sale of ”New Government Stock of 10 years” for a notified amount of Rs.6,000 crore (nominal), sale (reissue) of “7.35 per cent Government Stock 2024” for a notified amount of Rs.2,000 crore (nominal) and sale(reissue) of “7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034**”** for a notified amount of Rs.2,000 crore (nominal), through auctions to be held on July 10, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on July 9, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the Revised Scheme of Underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore)

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) between 10.30 AM and

12.30 PM on July 9, 2009 (Thursday). The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
