The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS), Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of <u>M/s Orbit Corporation Limited</u>, through primary markets and stock exchanges in India. The purchases can be made up to 100 per cent of the paid up capital of the company, as it has passed a resolution to this effect at the Board of Directors' level and at the Extra-Ordinary General Body Meeting. The purchases of equity shares by a single FII/Security and Exchange Board of India approved sub-account of a registered FII in the above company should, however, not exceed 10 per cent (ten per cent) of the paid-up equity capital of the companies.

The revised FII limit of 100 per cent is within the overall 100 per cent FDI limit applicable to the company.
