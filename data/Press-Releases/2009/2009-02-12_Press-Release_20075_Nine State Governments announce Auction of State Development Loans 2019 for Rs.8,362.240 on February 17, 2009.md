## February 12, 2009

## Nine State Governments announce Auction of State

## Development Loans 2019 for Rs.8,362.240 on February 17, 2009

The following State Governments have announced the sale of their 10-year State Development Loans (SDLs) for an aggregate amount of Rs. 8,362.240 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on February 17, 2009 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai - 400001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, February 17, 2009. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. on February 17, 2009. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on February 17, 2009 and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on February 18, 2009 (Wednesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India.

The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
