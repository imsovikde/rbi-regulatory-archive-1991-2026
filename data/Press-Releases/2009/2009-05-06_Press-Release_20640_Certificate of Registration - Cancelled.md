## Certificate of Registration-Cancelled

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the certificate of registration granted to the following companies, having their registered offices at the address shown against them, for carrying on the business of a non-banking financial institution. Following cancellation of the registration certificate the companies cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution.
