## RBI Offices to Function As Usual on April 01, 2009

All the offices of the Reserve Bank of India, including its Mumbai and Navi Mumbai offices, will function as usual on Wednesday, April 01, 2009. Commercial banks will remain closed for public transactions on April 01, 2009 for their yearly closing of accounts as it is declared a public holiday under the Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 by the Government of Maharashtra.
