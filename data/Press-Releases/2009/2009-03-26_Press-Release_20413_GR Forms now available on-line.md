## March 26, 2009

## GR Forms now available on-line

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its webs<u>ite www.rbi.org.in</u> the on-line printing of GR forms to facilitate submission of information by the exporter community.

The Foreign Exchange Management Act requires exporters to complete GR forms and submit them in duplicate to the specified authority for exports, including export of software in physical form, i.e., magnetic tapes/discs and paper media but not for exports done through post. At present, exporters are required to purchase the GR forms from the Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank at a cost of one rupee.

Exporters can now print the GR form on-line and submit it to the specified authority. The GR number will be automatically allotted while printing the form.

The present facility of purchasing of GR forms from the Reserve Bank will be phased out within a period of one year after consultation with trade bodies.
