## July 07, 2009

**Auction based OMO-Purchase of Dated securities of Rs 7500 crore on July 9, 2009**

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.7500 crore through multi-security auction using the multiple price method on July 9, 2009 (Thursday). The settlement would be on July 10, 2009 (Friday).

## <u>List of Eligible securities</u>

|Sr. No|Security|Maturity date|
|---|---|---|
|1.|6.05% GS 2019 (Feb)|02-Feb-2019|
|2.|6.30% GS 2023|09-Apr-2023|
|3.|7.95% GS 2032|28-Aug-2032|

Market participants may take note of the following:

There is an overall aggregate ceiling amount of Rs.7500 crore for all the securities in the basket put together. There is no security-wise notified amount.

## RBI reserves the right to:

- Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities
- Accept less than the aggregate amount of Rs.7500 crore
- Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding effects. Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning reason.
The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on July 9, 2009 (Thursday).

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on July 9, 2009. The amount and price of offer in the physical offer tender forms should be indicated both in number and words. The NDS members should submit their competitive offers in electronic format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auction will be announced on July 9, 2009 and payment to successful offerers will be made during banking hours on July 10, 2009 (Friday). Successful offerers may note to make available the requisite amount of securities in their SGL account in the forenoon on July 10, 2009 (Friday).
