## February 18, 2009

## RBI’s Appeal to Public

The United Forum of Reserve Bank Officers’ and Employees has given a call to its members to apply for mass casual leave on Friday, February 20, 2009. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that such action on their part amounts to cessation of work and concerted refusal to work.

The Reserve Bank has also stated that this agitational programme is likely to cause some disruption of normal work of the Reserve Bank on that day, including the payment and settlement system. While it will endeavor to maintain normal services, the Reserve Bank has advised the members of public to complete their transactions, if any, with the Reserve Bank on February 19, 2009 itself.
