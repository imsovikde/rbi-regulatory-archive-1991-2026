The roll over of the additional amounts of T-Bills which become due for redemption from the second quarter onwards will be decided by the Reserve Bank of India in consultation with the Government of India. It may be noted that the notified amounts for issuance of 91 day T-Bills as also 182 day T-Bills and 364 day T-Bills have been fixed at Rs.500 crore and Rs.1,000 crore respectively since the year 2003. There have been requests from the market participants and experts to increase the notified regular amounts of the T-Bills keeping in view the market demand, increased turnover in Government securities, etc. Accordingly, it has been decided, in consultation with the Government of India, to increase the notified amounts of T-Bills as indicated above, with effect from the second quarter of the fiscal year 2009-10. The Reserve Bank of India/Government of India will continue to have the flexibility to modify the notified amount and timing for auction of Treasury Bills keeping in view the emerging requirements of the Government, market conditions and other relevant factors. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons, such as, intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of Treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)-W&M/97, dated March 31, 1998 issued by the Government of India, as amended from time to time.
