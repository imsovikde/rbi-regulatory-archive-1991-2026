## February 18, 2009

## RBI to conduct its LAF for Five Days on February 19, 2009

To take care of the possibility that the normal work of the Reserve Bank, including the payment and settlement systems, might get disrupted on account of the call for mass casual leave given by the United Forum of Reserve Bank Officers and Employees, the Reserve Bank has decided to conduct its daily Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) auctions tomorrow, i.e., Thursday, February 19, 2009, for five days, due for reversal on February 24, 2009.
