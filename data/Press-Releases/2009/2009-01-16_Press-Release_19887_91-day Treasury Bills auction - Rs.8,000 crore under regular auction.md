91-day Treasury Bills auction :

Rs.8,oo0 croreunder regular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 91-day Government of India

Treasury Bills for notified amount of Rs.8,ooo crore. The auction will be conducted on January

21, 2009 using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation to the non-competitive bidders

will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the

terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F.2 (12)-W & M/97 dated 31st

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday,

January 21,

2009 by 12.30 P.M. Results will be announced on the same evening. Payments by successful

bidderswillbeonFriday,January23,2009.
