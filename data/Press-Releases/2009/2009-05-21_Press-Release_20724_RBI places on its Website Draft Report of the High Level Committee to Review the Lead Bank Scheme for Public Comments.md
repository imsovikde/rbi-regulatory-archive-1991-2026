## May 21, 2009

## RBI places on its Website Draft Report of the High Level Committee

## to Review the Lead Bank Scheme for Public Comments

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> the <u>Draft Report of the High Level Committee to Review the Lead Bank Scheme</u> for public comments. Comments may be addressed either by post to the Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Rural Planning and Credit Department, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai - 400 001 or sent by Fax to 022-22610948 or sent by <u>e-mail</u> on or before June 20, 2009.
