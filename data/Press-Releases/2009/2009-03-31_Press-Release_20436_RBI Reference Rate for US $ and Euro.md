RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India's Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.50.95 and

the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.67.48 on March 31, 2009. The corresponding rates

for the previous day (March 30, 2009) were Rs.51.08 and Rs.67.52 respectively.
