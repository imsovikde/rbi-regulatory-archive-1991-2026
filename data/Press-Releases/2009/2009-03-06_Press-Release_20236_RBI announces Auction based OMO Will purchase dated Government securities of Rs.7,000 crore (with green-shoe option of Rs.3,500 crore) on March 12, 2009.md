## March 06, 2009

## RBI announces Auction based OMO

## Will purchase dated Government securities of Rs.7,000 crore

## (with green-shoe option of Rs.3,500 crore) on March 12, 2009

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.7,000 crore with a green-shoe option to purchase up to 50 per cent of the notified amount through a price-based auction using the uniform price method on March 12, 2009. The settlement would be on March 13, 2009. The other details of the auction such as individual securities, modalities etc. would be announced at market close on March 9, 2009 (Monday).
