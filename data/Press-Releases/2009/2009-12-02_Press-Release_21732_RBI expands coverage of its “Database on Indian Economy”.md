## RBI expands coverage of its “Database on Indian Economy”

The Reserve Bank of India has further expanded the coverage of its "Database on Indian Economy", an on line database built on the data warehousing technology with enhanced functionality, to include static reports on Directory of Authorised Dealers Branches of Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) & Cooperative Banks, List of SLR securities. Business of SCBs State-wise distribution of deposit and credit of SCBs in India, Population group-wise distribution of deposit and credit of SCBs, External Commercial Borrowings – Annual Flows and Outstanding, etc.

Simple Query (frequency wise) data on FEDAI Spot Rates ( Rupees), LIBOR Rates-Overnight – USD, EUR, BPG, JPY, Weighted Average-Call Money Rates, REPO / Reverse REPO under LAF (subject wise), Central Govt. Market Borrowings, etc., also provided.

With the release of these data series, the Database on Indian Economy would now have 425 static reports (subject and frequency wise), 74 subject wise and 67 frequency wise data query templates covering various sectors of the Indian economy.

The Reserve Bank of India's Database on Indian Economy is available on its website [http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in) under the Database Section. This can also be directly accessed through URL [http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in). The coverage of the Database is continuously being expanded on the basis of the feedback received from the users of the database.

Users are requested to provide their feedback through a feedback link on the site so as to enable further improvements in coverage of data series in the system.
