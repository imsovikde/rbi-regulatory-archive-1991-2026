## Open Market Operations – Auction based

## Purchase of dated Government security of Rs 6000 crore on September 17,

## 2009

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to **purchase** dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of **Rs 6,000 crore** through **multi-security** auction using the **multiple price** method on **September 17, 2009 (Thursday)**. The settlement would be on **September 18, 2009 (Friday).**

## <u>List of Eligible securities</u>

|Sr. No|Security|Maturity|
|---|---|---|
|1.|7.37% GS 2014|16-Apr-2014|
|2.|8.07% GS 2017|15-Jan-2017|
|3.|7.95% GS 2032|28-Aug-2032|

## Date

Market participants may take note of the following:

There is an overall aggregate ceiling amount of **Rs 6,000 crore** for all the securities in the basket put together. There is no security-wise notified amount.

## RBI reserves the right to:

- Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities
- Accept less than the aggregate amount of **Rs 6,000 crore**
- Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding effects.
- Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning reason.
The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on **September 17, 2009 (Thursday).**

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> should be submitted to that Office on **September 17, 2009**. The amount and price of offer in the physical offer tender forms should be indicated both in number and
