## March 18, 2009

**Open Market Operations – Auction based Purchase of dated Government security of Rs 10,000 crore on March 19, 2009**

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase the following dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.10,000 crore through price- based auction using multiple price method on March 19, 2009 (Thursday).

**Sr.** **Notified Amount (Rs. crore)**

|No|Security||
|---|---|---|
|1|7.37% GS 2014|3000|
|2|7.56% GS 2014|3000|
|3|7.99% GS 2017|4000|

The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on March 19, 2009 (Thursday).

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on March 19, 2009. The NDS members should submit their competitive offers in electronic format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be submitted by 12.30

P.M. The result of the auction will be announced on March 19, 2009 and payment to successful offerers will be made during banking hours on March 23, 2009 (Monday). Reserve Bank has the right to accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.
