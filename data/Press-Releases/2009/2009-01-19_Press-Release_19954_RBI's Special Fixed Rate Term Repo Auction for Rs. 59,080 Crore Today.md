RBl's Special Fixed Rate Term RepoAuction for Rs. 59,080 Crore Today

TheReserveBank ofIndia shallconductaspecial fixedratetermrepo at 5.5percent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs. 59,080 crore on January 19, 2009, due for reversal

on February 02, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of

Mutual Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance

Companies (HFCs)either on incremental or on rollover basis. The outstanding amount under

this facility as on January 16,2009 stood at Rs.920 crore.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30,

2009up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,0o0 crore on outstanding basis and that additional

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks

exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs.
