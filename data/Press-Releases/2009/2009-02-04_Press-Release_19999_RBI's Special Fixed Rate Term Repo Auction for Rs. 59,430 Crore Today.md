## RBI's Special Fixed Rate Term Repo Auction for Rs. 59,430 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 5.5 per cent per annum against eligible securities for Rs.59,430 crore on February 4, 2009, due for reversal on February 18, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of Mutual Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. The outstanding amount under this facility as on February 03, 2009 stood at Rs.570 crore.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30, 2009 up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,000 crore on outstanding basis and that additional liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs. It has now been decided to extend this facility up to September 30,

2009.
