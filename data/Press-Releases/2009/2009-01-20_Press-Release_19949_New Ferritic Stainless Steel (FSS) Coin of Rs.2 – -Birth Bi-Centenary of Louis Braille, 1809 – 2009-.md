New Ferritic Stainless Steel (FSS) Coin of Rs.2 -

"Birth Bi-Centenary of Louis Braille, 1809-2009"

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly put into circulation new Feritic Stainless Steel

coins of Rs.2 issued by Government of India to commemorate "Birth Bi-Centenary of Louis

Braille,1809-2009".

The coins shall conform to the following dimensions, designs and compositions namely:

Obverse : The face of coin is divided into three portions with two horizontal lines. The left

centre portion shall bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar with the legend "rq d"inscribed

below, and right centre portion denominational value "2" in International Numeral. In the upper

portion the word "Ra" in Hindi and "INDIA" in English and lower portion the word "su " in Hindi

and"Rupees"in English.

Reverse : The face of the coin shall contain the portrait of "LOUIS BRAILLE" in the centre. Left

upper periphery the word "v " in Hindi and right upper periphery the word " LOUIS BRAILLE

International Numerals. Below the portrait the word "LOUIS BRAILLE" in Braille Language.

The two Rupee coin is a legal tender as provided in the Coinage Act, 1906. The existing
