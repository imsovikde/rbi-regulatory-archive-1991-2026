## Monitoring of Ceiling for Investment by FIIs under PIS : Bajaj Auto Ltd.

The Reserve Bank India has notified on June 16, 2009 that no further purchases of shares of <u>Bajaj Auto Ltd</u>., should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs. The Reserve Bank of India has stated that the aggregate share holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) in Bajaj Auto Ltd, has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
