## March 09, 2009

## Auction based OMO

## Purchase of Dated securities of Rs 7000 crore (with green-shoe option to

## purchase up to 50% of notified amount) on March 12, 2009

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase the following dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.7000 crore (with green-shoe option to purchase up to 50% of notified amount) through price-based auction using uniform price method on March 12, 2009 (Thursday).

## Sr. Notified Amount

## No Security (Rs. crore) Remark

1 7.49% GS 2017 4000 RBI may exercise Green Shoe 2 7.94% GS 2021 1000 option to additionally purchase 3 7.95% GS 2032 1000 up to 50% of the notified <u>4 7.40% GS 2035 1000 amount in each security</u>

The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on March 12, 2009 (Thursday).

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on March 12, 2009. The NDS members should submit their competitive offers in electronic format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be submitted by 12.30

P.M. The result of the auction will be announced on March 12, 2009 and payment to successful offerers will be made during banking hours on March 13, 2009 (Friday). Reserve Bank has the right to accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.
