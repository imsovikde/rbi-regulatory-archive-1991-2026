## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities –

## “6.07 per cent Government Stock 2014”, “6.35 per cent Government Stock 2020”,

## “8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027” and “7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of ”6.07 per cent Government Stock 2014” for a notified amount of Rs.6,000 crore (nominal), ”6.35 per cent Government Stock 2020” for a notified amount of Rs.5,000 crore (nominal), ”8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027” for a notified amount of Rs.2,000 crore (nominal) and “7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034**”** for a notified amount of Rs.2,000 crore (nominal), through auctions to be held on May 22, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on May 21, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore) **Minimum bidding** **Nomenclature of the Notified MUC amount per commitment per PD** **Security Amount Primary Dealer under ACU auction**

6.07 per cent GS 2014 6000 167 167
6.35 per cent GS 2020 5000 139 139
8.24 per cent GS 2027 2000 56 56
<u>7.50 per cent GS 2034 2000 56 56</u>
The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on May 21, 2009 (Thursday).

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
