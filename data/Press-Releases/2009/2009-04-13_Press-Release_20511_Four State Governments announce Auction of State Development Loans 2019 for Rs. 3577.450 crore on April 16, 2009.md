## April 13, 2009

## Four State Governments announce Auction of

## State Development Loans 2019 for Rs. 3577.450 crore on April 16, 2009

The following four State Governments have announced the sale of their 10- year State Development Loans (SDLs) for an aggregate amount of Rs.3577.45 crore through yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

|Sr.|State Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|
|No.||(Rs. crore)|
|1|Andhra Pradesh *|800.000|
|2 Jharkhand||517.450|
|3 Nagaland||260.000|
|2 West Bengal||2000.000|

## Total 3577.450

***Government of Andhra Pradesh will have the option to retain additional subscription to a maximum of Rs.200.00 crore over and**

**above its notified amount.**

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on April 16, 2009 (Thursday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai - 400001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, April 16, 2009. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M on April 16, 2009. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on April 16, 2009 and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on April 17, 2009 (Friday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India.

The investment in Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
