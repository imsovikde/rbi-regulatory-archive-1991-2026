## February 4, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities – “7.46 per cent Government

## Stock 2017” and “6.30 per cent Government Stock 2023”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of securities “7.46 per cent Government Stock 2017**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 5,000 crore (nominal) and "6.30% Government Stock 2023" for a notified amount of Rs.2000 crore (nominal) respectively, through auctions to be held on February 6, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on February 5, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore)

|Nomenclature of the|Notified|MUC amount per||commitment per PD Minimum bidding||
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|Primary Dealer||under ACU auction||
|7.46 per cent GS 2017|5000||139||139|
|6.30 per cent GS 2023|2000||56||56|

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PDO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on February 5, 2009 (Thursday).

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
