## Reserve Bank Platinum Jubilee Celebrations -Panel Discussion on Challenges for Central Banks

The Reserve Bank of India today arranged a panel discussion on `Challenges for the Central Banks' in its Platinum Jubilee Celebrations programme. The panelists were Shri M. Narasimham, Dr. C. Rangarajan and Dr. Y.V. Reddy, all former Governors of the Reserve Bank and Dr. D. Subbarao, present Governor of the Reserve Bank.

Dr. Shankar Acharya, Member, Board of Governors and Honorary Professor, Indian Council for Research on International Economic Relations (ICRIER) moderated the discussion. The panel discussion was held in Hyderabad. Eminent personalities from state government, research institutions, former and current Reserve Bank employees and media attended the discussion.

The web-cast of the discussion is available at the <u>url:</u> <u>[http://24framesdigital.com/rbi/140809](http://24framesdigital.com/rbi/140809)</u>
