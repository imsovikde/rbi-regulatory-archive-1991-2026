## Open Market Operations – Auction based

## Purchase of dated Government security of Rs 10000 crore on March 16, 2009

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to <u>purchase 8.24% GS 2018</u> for an aggregate amount of Rs.10, 000 crore through price-based auction using <u>uniform</u> price method on **March 16, 2009** (Monday).

The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on March 16, 2009 (Monday).

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on March 16, 2009. The NDS members should submit their competitive offers in electronic format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auction will be announced on March 16, 2009 and payment to successful offerers will be made during banking hours on March 17, 2009 (Tuesday).

Reserve Bank has the right to accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.
