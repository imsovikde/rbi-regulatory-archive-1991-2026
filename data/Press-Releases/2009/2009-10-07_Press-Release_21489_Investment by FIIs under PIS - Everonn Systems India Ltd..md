## October 7, 2009

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Everonn Systems India Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holdings in <u>Everonn Systems India Ltd</u>. by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit of their paid up capital. Therefore, further purchases of equity shares in the primary/secondary market of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
