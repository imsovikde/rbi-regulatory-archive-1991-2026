Report of the Task Force set up to look into the problems

faced by the Diamond Industry in Gujarat

A Task Force convened by the Reserve Bankof India to look into the distressed

which include, fresh financing of existing borrowal accounts as per the Reserve Bank's

guidelines, financing diamond sector units not financed earlier, re-training/re-skilling/

workers. These measures were recommended by the Task Force based on discussions

with various stakeholders and deliberations in the meetings.

The Task Force was convened pursuant to the deliberations at a meeting held by

the RBl Governor with the Minister of Finance, the Minister of State for Finance,

Government of Gujarat, and senior officials of government and banks on February 11,

The Task Force comprised officials of the Gujarat state government, banks, the Small

Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBl) and the State Level Bankers' Committee

(SLBC) convenor.
