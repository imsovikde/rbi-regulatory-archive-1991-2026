The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBl) at Mumbai on

January 13, 2009 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional

Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Main Building, Fort, Mumbai- 400

001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, January 13, 2009. The competitive

bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using Primary

Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M.

on January 13, 2009. On the basis of bids received, RBl will determine the maximum

yield at which offers will be accepted.
