# RBI announces Auction based OMO Will purchase Dated Securities of Rs 6,000 crore (with green-shoe option of Rs.3,000 crore) on March 5, 2009

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## February 27, 2009

**RBI announces Auction based OMO** **Will purchase Dated Securities of Rs 6,000 crore** **(with green-shoe option of Rs.3,000 crore) on March 5, 2009**

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to **purchase** dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.6,000 crore with a green-shoe option to purchase up to 50 per cent of the notified amount through a price-based auction using the <u>uniform price</u> <u>method</u> on **March 5, 2009.** The settlement would be on March 6, 2009. The other details of the auction such as individual securities, modalities, etc., would be announced at market close on March 4, 2009.

Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager **Press Release: 2008-2009/1403**
