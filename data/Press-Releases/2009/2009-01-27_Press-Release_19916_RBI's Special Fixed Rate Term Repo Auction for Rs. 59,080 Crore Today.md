RBl's Special Fixed Rate TermRepo Auction for Rs. 59,080 Crore Today

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 5.5 per cent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs.59,080 crore on January 27, 2009, due for reversal

on February 10, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of

Mutual Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance

Companies (HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. The outstanding amount under

this facility as on January 23, 2009 stood at Rs.920 crore.

Itmayberecalled that theReserveBankofIndiahadannouncedearlier that itwould

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30,

2009uptoacumulativeamountofRs.60,0o0croreonoutstandingbasisandthatadditional

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks

exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs. It has now

been decided to extend this facility up to September 30, 2009.
