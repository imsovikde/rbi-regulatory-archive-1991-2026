The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.6000 crore through multi-security auction using the uniform price method on May 21, 2009 (Thursday). The settlement would be on May 22, 2009 (Friday).

## List of Eligible securities

|Sr. No|Security|Maturity date|
|---|---|---|
|1.|8.24% GS 2018|April 22, 2018|
|2.|8.35% GS 2022|May 14, 2022|
|3.|8.28% GS 2032|February 15, 2032|
|4.|8.33% GS 2036|June 7, 2036|

Market participants may take note of the following:

There is an overall aggregate ceiling amount of Rs.6000 crore for all the securities in the basket put together. There is no security-wise notified amount.

## RBI reserves the right to:

- Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities
- Accept less than the aggregate amount of Rs.6000 crore
- Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding effects
- Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning reason.
- The auction shall be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on May 21, 2009 (Thursday).
Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400001 and/or RBI website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on May 21, 2009. The amount and price of offer in the physical offer tender forms should be indicated both in number and words. The NDS members should submit their competitive offers in electronic format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auction will be announced on May 21, 2009 and payment to successful offerers will be made during banking hours on May 22, 2009 (Friday). Successful offerers may note to make available the requisite amount of securities in their SGL account in the forenoon on May 22, 2009 (Friday). **Ajit Prasad** **Press Release: 2008-2009/1887** Manager
