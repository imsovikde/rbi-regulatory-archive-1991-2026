## Reserve Bank to conduct the next auction-based OMO on July 9, 2009

The Reserve Bank will conduct the next purchase auction under Open Market Operations (OMO) on July 9, 2009 (Thursday).

It may be recalled that on March 26, 2009, the Reserve Bank had issued an indicative calendar for auction-based open market purchases of Government securities amounting to Rs.80,000 crore for the first half of the fiscal year 2009-10. It was stated, *inter-alia*, that the OMO purchase auctions would normally be conducted every alternate week on Thursdays but the Reserve Bank maintains the right to alter the frequency and day of auction as may be necessary according to market conditions.
