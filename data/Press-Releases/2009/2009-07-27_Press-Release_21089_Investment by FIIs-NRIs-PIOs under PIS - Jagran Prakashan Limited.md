## July 27, 2009

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS :

## Jagran Prakashan Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holdings in Jagran Prakashan Limited by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have gone below the prescribed threshold limit stipulated under the extant FDI Policy. Therefore, the restrictions placed on the purchase of shares of the above company may be treated as withdrawn with immediate effect.
