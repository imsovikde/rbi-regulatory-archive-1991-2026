## February 05, 2009

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holding in Punjab National Bank by <u>Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)</u>/Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) has gone below stipulated limit of 20 per cent of their paid up capital. Further purchases of equity shares of the bank would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
