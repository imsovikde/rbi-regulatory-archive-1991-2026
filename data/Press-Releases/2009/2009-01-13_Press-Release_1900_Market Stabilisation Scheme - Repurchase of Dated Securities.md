MarketStabilisationScheme-

Repurchase of Dated Securities

Under the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), “6.57 % Government Stock

2011" for Rs.3,0o0 crore (nominal) will be repurchased through price based auction

using multiple price method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of

India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on January 15, 2009 (Thursday).

Offers in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve

Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-40oo01 and RBI

website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on January 15, 2009

(Thursday). The NDS members should submit the competitive offers in electronic

format using Open Market Operation (OMO) module of NDS. All offers should be

submitted by 12.30 P.M.

The result of the auction will be announced on January 15, 20o9 (Thursday)

and thepayment to successful offererswill be made in the forenoon on January 19,

2009(Monday). Successful offerers may note to make available the requisite amount

of securities in their SGL account in the forenoon on January 19, 2009 (Monday).
