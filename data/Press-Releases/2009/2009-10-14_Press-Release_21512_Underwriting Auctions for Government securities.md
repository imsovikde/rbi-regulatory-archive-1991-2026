## October 14, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities

The Government of India have announced the sale of “New Government Stock of 5 years“ for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal), “6.35 per cent Government Stock 2020” (reissue) for a notified amount of Rs.4,000 crore (nominal) and "7.35 per cent Government Stock 2024"(reissue) for a notified amount of Rs.3,000 crore (nominal) respectively, through auctions to be held on October 16, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on October 15, 2009 (Thursday).

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore)

|Nomenclature of the|Notified|MUC amount per||commitment per PD Minimum bidding||
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|Primary Dealer||under ACU auction||
|New GS of 5 Years|3000||79||79|
|6.35 per cent GS 2020|4000||106||106|
|7.35 per cent GS 2024|3000||79||79|

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on October 15, 2009 (Thursday).

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
