## September 15, 2009

## Release of Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy 2008-09

The Reserve Bank of India today released the eleventh volume of its annual statistical publication <u>Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (HBS) 2008-09</u>. This publication is one of the major initiatives of the Reserve Bank aimed at improving data dissemination by providing statistical information at one place. Through this publication, the Reserve Bank has been providing time series data on various economic and financial indicators for the Indian economy. To mark the Platinum Jubilee of the Bank, this year’s Handbook is also being released in electronic form on the Data Base on Indian Economy (DBIE): Reserve Bank’s Data Warehouse, <u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u> which would facilitate more frequent updating of information.

The current volume contains 251 statistical tables covering national income aggregates, output, prices, money, banking, financial markets, public finances, foreign trade and balance of payments and select socio-economic indicators.

The Handbook is brought out in four versions – print, CD-Rom, website and on the DBIE platform. This year's Handbook is a landmark as it provides for the dynamic updation of Handbook making it almost real time. This is an outcome of joint efforts of two research departments of the Bank, Department of Economic Analysis and Policy and Department of Statistics and Information Management. Data tables from the CD-Rom and DBIE platform can be saved in the Microsoft excel format in order to facilitate their use for modeling and forecasting by various statistical and econometric packages.
