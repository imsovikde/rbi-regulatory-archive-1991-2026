## September 14, 2009

## Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund Rules) 2009

Reserve Bank of India has framed a new set of Note Refund Rules known as <u>Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund Rules) 2009</u> and has been notified in the Gazette of India on August 04, 2009 and came into force with immediate effect .The new set of rules are simplified and easy to comprehend. These rules are also available on RBI website www. rbi.org.in.
