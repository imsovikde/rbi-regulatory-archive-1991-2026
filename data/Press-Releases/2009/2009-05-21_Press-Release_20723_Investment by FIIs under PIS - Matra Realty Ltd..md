## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Matra Realty Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holding in <u>Matra Realty Ltd</u>. by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) in the primary/secondary markets has reached the limit of 22 per cent of their paid up capital and no further purchases of equity shares of the above company on behalf of FIIs would be allowed without obtaining prior clearance of the Reserve Bank of India.
