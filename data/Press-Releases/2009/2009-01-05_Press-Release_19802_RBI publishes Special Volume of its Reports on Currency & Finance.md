## Volume II: The Evolution of Central Banking in India

The second volume covers the theme "The Evolution of Central Banking in India" and documents the evolution of central banking in India over its seventy years of existence.

## Volume III: Development of Financial Markets and Role of the Central Bank

The third volume focuses on Development of Financial Markets and Role of the Central Bank. It covers various segments of financial markets: money, credit, foreign exchange, government securities, equity and corporate debt markets and also the whole range of issues relating to financial market integration.

## Volumes IV & V: The Banking Sector in India: Emerging Issues and Challenges

The fourth and fifth volumes attempt an in-depth analysis of the various aspects of Indian banking, such as, management of resource mobilisation; management of risk and capital, lending and investment operations of banks; financial inclusion; consolidation and competition; efficiency, productivity and soundness; and regulatory and supervisory challenges.
