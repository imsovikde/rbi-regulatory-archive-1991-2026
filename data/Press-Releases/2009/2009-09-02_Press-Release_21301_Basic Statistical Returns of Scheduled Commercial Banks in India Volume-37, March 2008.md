### September 2, 2009

### Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India

### Volume-37, March 2008

The Department of Statistics and Information Management of Reserve Bank of India brings out the publication “<u>Basic Statistical Returns of Scheduled Commercial Banks in India</u>” annually. th The Volume, for March 2008, 37 in the series, provides granular details on credit and deposits of scheduled commercial banks collected from bank branches.

Some salient features based on data published in the publication are set out below:

¾ At the end of March 2008 gross outstanding credit of scheduled commercial banks amounted to Rs. 24,16,593 crore registering an increase of 24.1 per cent as against an increase of 28.6 per cent in the previous year.

¾ Aggregate deposits amounted to Rs. 32,21,305 crore registering a growth of 24.0 per cent in 2008 as against 24.2 per cent a year ago.

¾ The number of borrowal accounts increased to 10.65 crore in 2008 from 9.44 crore in 2007,

i.e. by 12.8 per cent.
¾ The number of deposit accounts in 2008 increased by 11.9 per cent to 58.10 crore from about 51.91 crore in March 2007*.*

¾ The number of small borrowal accounts (with credit limit up to Rs 2 lakh) contributed 88.4 per cent of total number of accounts as against 89.3 per cent in 2007, while the share of outstanding credit of small borrowal accounts was 13.7 per cent as compared to 14.4 in

2007.
¾ The distribution of outstanding credit according to interest rate ranges (accounts each with credit limit of over Rs.2 lakh) revealed that 30.5 per cent of outstanding amount was in the interest rate range of 10 – 12 %.

¾ The weighted average interest rate in respect of all loans and advances with credit limit of over Rs.2 lakh worked out to be 12.34 per cent as at the end of March 2008 as compared to 11.92 per cent in the previous year.

¾ The share of term deposits in total deposits increased to 62.2 per cent in 2008 from 61.5 per cent in 2007. The shares of current deposits and saving deposits were at 12.8 per cent and 24.9 per cent, respectively in 2008 as against 12.4 and 26.1 per cent in 2007.

¾ The weighted average interest rate of term deposits outstanding in 2008 worked out to 8.66 per cent, as compared to 8.22 per cent as at end-March 2007.

¾ The interest rate spread on bank credit (large borrowal account with credit limit over Rs 2 lakhs) over term deposits was at 3.68 per cent in 2008 compared to 3.70 per cent in 2007.
