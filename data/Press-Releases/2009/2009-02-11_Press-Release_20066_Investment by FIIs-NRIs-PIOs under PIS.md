## 133February 11, 2009

### Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate share holdings in (1) <u>Bank of Baroda</u> and (2) <u>Union Bank of India</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/ Non Resident Indians (NRIs) / Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have gone below the stipulated caution limit of 20% of their paid up capital. FIIs/NRIs/PIOs who have obtained permission to invest under the Portfolio Investment Scheme would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India to purchase equity shares of the banks in the primary/secondary market.
