Investment byFlls under PIS :M/s Dagger-Forst Tools Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that under Portfolio Investment Scheme

debentures of M/s. Dagger-Forst Tools Limited, through primary market and stock exchanges

in India up to 74% of the total paid-up equity capital and total paid-up value of each series of

convertible debentures of the company, as it has passed resolutions to this effect at the Board

of Directors' level.

The totalpurchases of all Flls should not exceed the applicableoverall ceilinglimits of

debentures, of the company.

The purchases of equity shares by a single Fll/SEBl approved sub-account of a

registered Fll in the company should not exceed 10% (ten per cent) of the paid-up equity

capital of the company.
