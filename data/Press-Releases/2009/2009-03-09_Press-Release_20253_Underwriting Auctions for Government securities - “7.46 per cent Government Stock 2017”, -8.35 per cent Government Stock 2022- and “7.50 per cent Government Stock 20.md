## March 9, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities - “7.46 per cent Government

## Stock 2017”, "8.35 per cent Government Stock 2022" and

## “7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of securities “7.46 per cent Government Stock 2017” for a notified amount of Rs. 8,000 crore (nominal), “8.35 per cent Government Stock 2022” for a notified amount of Rs. 2,000 crore (nominal) and "7.50 per cent Government Stock 2034" for a notified amount of Rs.2,000 crore (nominal) respectively, through auctions to be held on March 13, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on March 12, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under:

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PDO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on March 12, 2009

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
