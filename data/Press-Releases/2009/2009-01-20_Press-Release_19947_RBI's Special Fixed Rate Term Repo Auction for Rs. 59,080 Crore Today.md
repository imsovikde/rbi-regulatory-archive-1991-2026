RBl'sSpecial FixedRateTermRepoAuction for Rs.59,080 CroreToday

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 5.5 per cent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs.59,080 crore on January 20, 2009, due for

reversal on February 03, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity

outstanding amountunder this facility as onJanuary 19,2009 stood at Rs.920 crore.

ItmayberecalledthattheReserveBankofIndiahadannouncedearlierthatitwould

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30,

2009 up to a cumulative amount of Rs.60,0o0 crore on outstanding basis and that additional

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks

exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs.
