## Annual Policy Statement for 2009-10 on April 21, 2009

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will present the Annual Policy Statement for 2009-10 on Tuesday, April 21, 2009. The presentation will be made at a meeting of the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on that day in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.
