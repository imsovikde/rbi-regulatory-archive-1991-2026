## February 3, 2009

## CGFS Report on Capital Flows and Emerging Market Economies released

The Committee on the Global Financial System (CGFS) of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) has today released a report entitled <u>Capital Flows and</u> <u>Emerging Market Economies</u> prepared by a working group chaired by Dr Rakesh Mohan, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India.

The Report can be accessed at the BIS website <u>[http://www.bis.org/publ/cgfs33.pdf?noframes=1](http://www.bis.org/publ/cgfs33.pdf?noframes=1)</u>
