## May 6, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities –

## “6.05 per cent Government Stock 2019”, “8.24 per cent Government

## Stock 2027” and "6.83 per cent Government Stock 2039"

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of securities “6.05 per cent Government Stock 2019” for a notified amount of Rs. 6,000 crore (nominal), “8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027” for a notified amount of Rs. 3,000 crore (nominal) and "6.83 per cent Government Stock 2039" for a notified amount of Rs.3000 crore (nominal) respectively, through auctions to be held on May 8, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on May 7, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore)

Nomenclature of the Notified MUC amount per commitment per PD Minimum bidding
Amount Primary Dealer under ACU auction
6.05 per cent GS 2019 6000 167	167
8.24 per cent GS 2027 3000 84	84
6.83 per cent GS 2039 3000 84	84

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PMO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on May 7, 2009 (Thursday).

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
