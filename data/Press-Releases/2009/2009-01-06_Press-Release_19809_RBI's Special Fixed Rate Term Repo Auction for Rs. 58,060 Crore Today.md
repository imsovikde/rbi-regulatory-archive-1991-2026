RBl's Special Fixed RateTermRepoAuction for Rs.58,060 CroreToday

The Reserve Bank of India shall conduct a special fixed rate term repo at 5.5 per cent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs. 58,060 crore on January 06, 2009, due for reversal

on January 20, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of Mutual

Funds (MFs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance Companies

(HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. Of the outstanding amount of Rs.2,015 crore

as on January 05, 2009, Rs.75 crore is due for redemption today.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30,

2009uptoacumulativeamountofRs.60,o00croreonoutstandingbasisandthatadditional

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks

exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs.
