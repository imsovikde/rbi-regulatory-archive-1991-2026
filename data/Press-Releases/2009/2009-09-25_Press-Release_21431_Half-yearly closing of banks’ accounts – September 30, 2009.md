## September 25, 2009

## Half-yearly closing of banks’ accounts – September 30, 2009

The Government of Maharashtra has declared Wednesday, September 30, 2009 as public holiday under Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, on account of half-yearly closing of banks’ accounts. The above holiday will not be applicable for the Reserve Bank of India and its offices in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will therefore function normally on September 30, 2009.
