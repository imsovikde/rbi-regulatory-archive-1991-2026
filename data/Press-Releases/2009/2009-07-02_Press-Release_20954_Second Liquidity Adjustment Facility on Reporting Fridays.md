## SECOND LIQUIDITY ADJUSTMENT FACILITY ON

## REPORTING FRIDAYS

In response to suggestions from the market participants, the Reserve Bank of India will now conduct the auctions under the Second Liquidity Adjustment Facility (SLAF) between 4.15 PM and 4.45 PM from July 3, 2009.

It may be recalled that Reserve Bank of India has been conducting SLAF between 4.00 PM and 4.30 PM on reporting Fridays or the immediate preceding working day in case the reporting Friday is a Bank holiday in Mumbai with effect from August 1,

2008.
