## July 16, 2009

## Half-yearly Report on Forex Reserves as at end March 2009

The Reserve Bank of India has today placed on its website, the 12th report on management of <u>foreign exchange reserves with reference to March 31, 2009</u>.

It may be recalled that in February 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the public domain with a lag of about three months for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure. The <u>first such report</u> with reference to September 30, 2003 was released on February 3, 2004.
