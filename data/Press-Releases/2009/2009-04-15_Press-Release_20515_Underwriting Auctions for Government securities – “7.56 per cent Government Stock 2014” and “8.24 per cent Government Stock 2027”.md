## April 15, 2009

## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities –

## “7.56 per cent Government Stock 2014” and

## “8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027”

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of securities “7.56 per cent Government Stock 2014**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 8,000 crore (nominal) and “8.24 per cent Government Stock 2027**”** for a notified amount of Rs. 4,000 crore (nominal) respectively, through auctions to be held on April 17, 2009. The underwriting auctions for the captioned securities will be conducted on April 16, 2009 (Thursday).

As per the revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (Rs. in crore)

|Nomenclature of the|Notified|MUC amount per||commitment per PD Minimum bidding||
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|Primary Dealer||under ACU auction||
|7.56 per cent GS 2014|8000||223||223|
|8.24 per cent GS 2027|4000||112||112|

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through NDS-PDO module between 10.30 AM and 12.30 PM on April 16, 2009 (Thursday).

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
