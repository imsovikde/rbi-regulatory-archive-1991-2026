RBI Announces Auction-based OMO purchases of Government Securities

Until recently, as part of its Open Market Operations (OMO), the Reserve Bank of India has ordinarily been buying Government securities through NDS-OM. For more effective liquidity management, it has now been decided to widen the scope of such OMO by including purchases of Government securities through an auction-based mechanism in addition to operations through NDS-OM, as hitherto.

Accordingly, the first such auction will be held on February 19, 2009, with settlement on February 20, 2009.

The other details of the auction including the size, timing, securities, etc. would be announced separately.
