RBl's Special Fixed RateTermRepoAuction for Rs.59,170 CroreToday

TheReserveBankof India shallconduct a specialfixedratetermrepo at5.5per cent

per annum against eligible securities for Rs.59,170 crore on January 23, 2009, due for reversal

on February 06, 2009, with a view to enabling banks to meet the liquidity requirements of

Mutual Funds (MFs),Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) and Housing Finance

Companies (HFCs) either on incremental or on rollover basis. Of the outstanding amount of

Rs.920 crore as on January 22, 2009, Rs.90 crore is due for redemption today.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had announced earlier that it would

conduct the special fixed rate term repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility daily till June 30,

2009uptoacumulativeamountofRs.60,oo0croreonoutstandingbasisandthatadditional

liquidity support to the extent of up to 1.5 per cent of their NDTL, can be availed by banks

exclusively for the purpose of meeting requirements of MFs, NBFCs and HFCs.
