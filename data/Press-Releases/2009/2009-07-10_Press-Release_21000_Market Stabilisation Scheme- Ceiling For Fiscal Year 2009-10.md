## Market Stabilisation Scheme: Ceiling For Fiscal Year 2009-10

In accordance with the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on the Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS), the ceiling for the outstandings under the MSS for the fiscal year 2009-10 has been fixed at Rs.50,000 crore. The threshold at which this ceiling will be reviewed is when the outstandings reach Rs.35,000 crore. The ceiling and the threshold have been agreed between the Government of India and the Reserve Bank.

The current MSS outstanding balance (face value) is Rs.23,273 crore. MSS redemptions of Rs.15,536 crore are due during the remaining part of the current fiscal year.
