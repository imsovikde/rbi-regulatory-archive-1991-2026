## March 21, 2009

## RBI to conduct Auction-based OMO on March 25, 2009; will purchase

## Dated Government Securities of Rs 10,000 crore

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to purchase dated Government securities for an aggregate amount of Rs.10,000 crore through a price-based auction on March 25, 2009 (Wednesday). The settlement would be on March 26, 2009 (Thursday). The other details of the auction, such as, individual securities, modalities, etc., would be announced at market close on March 23, 2009.
