## December 04, 2009

## Ten State Governments/Union Territory announce Auction of State

## Development Loans 2019 for Rs. 6,507.190 crore on December 08, 2009

In continuation of the <u>Press Release No. 2009-2010/796</u> dated December 03, 2009 announcing the auction of State Development Loans of Nine State Governments/Union Territory on December 08, 2009, Government of Andhra Pradesh has also announced the sale of its 10-year State Development Loans for Rs. 500.000 crore through yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

Accordingly, the aggregate amount of State Development Loans to be sold by ten State Governments/UT on December 8, 2009 is Rs. 6,507.190 crore.
