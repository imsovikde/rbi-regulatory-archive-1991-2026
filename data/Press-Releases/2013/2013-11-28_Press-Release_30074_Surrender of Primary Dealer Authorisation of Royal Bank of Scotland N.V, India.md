## November 28, 2013

## Surrender of Primary Dealer Authorisation of Royal Bank of Scotland N.V, India

In view of the request received from Royal Bank of Scotland N.V, India to surrender its authorisation for conducting Primary Dealer (PD) business, Reserve Bank of India has cancelled the said authorisation of the bank with effect from December 02, 2013. The cancellation of authorisation has been effected in terms of para 18 of the Master Circular RBI/2013-14/102 IDMD.PDRD.01/03.64.00/2013-14 dated July 01, 2013.

The Royal Bank of Scotland N.V, India was authorised to carry out PD business in terms of RBI authorisation issued in May 2008. Consequent to the cancellation of authorisation, The Royal Bank of Scotland N.V, India will stop undertaking business as PD in Government Securities Market.
