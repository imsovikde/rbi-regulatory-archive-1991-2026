## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is

59.7340 and the Reference Rate for Euro is 78.2286 on June 24, 2013. The
corresponding rates for the previous day (June 21, 2013) were 59.3505 and

78.5930 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the
middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||June 21, 2013|June 24, 2013|
|1 GBP|92.1120|91.8171|
|100 YEN|60.72|60.66|
