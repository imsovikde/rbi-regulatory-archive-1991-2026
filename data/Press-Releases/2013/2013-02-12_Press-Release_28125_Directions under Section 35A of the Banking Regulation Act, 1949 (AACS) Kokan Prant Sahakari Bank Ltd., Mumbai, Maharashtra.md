## Directions under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (AACS)

## Kokan Prant Sahakari Bank Ltd., Mumbai, Maharashtra

It is hereby notified for the information of the public that validity period of directions imposed on Kokan Prant Sahakari Bank Ltd., Mumbai, Maharashtra vide our directive UBD.CO.BSD-I.No.D-04/12.22.114/2012-13 dated August 06, 2012, has been extended further vide modified directive UBD.CO.BSD I/D-25/12.22.114/2012- 13 dated January 28, 2013 for a period of six months i.e. from February 07, 2013 to August 06, 2013. The other terms and conditions remain unchanged. A copy of the direction dated January 28, 2013 notifying the above modification is displayed at the bank’s premises for the perusal of public.

The aforesaid modification by the Reserve Bank of India should not per-se be construed to imply that Reserve Bank of India is satisfied of substantive improvement in the financial position of the bank.
