## Cut-off rates in underwriting auctions

In the auctions conducted on June 06, 2013 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the captioned securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: ( Crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per Rs. 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.12 % GS 2020|3000|1512|1488|3000|0.21|
|8.33 % GS 2026|6000|3003|2997|6000|0.24|
|8.32 % GS 2032|2000|1008|992|2000|0.34|
|8.30 % GS 2042|3000|1512|1488|3000|0.36|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on June 07, 2013.
