## November 13, 2013

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS -

## Incorporation in Ban List :

## M/s Carnation Industries Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>M/s Carnation Industries Limited</u> by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in the primary/secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Accordingly, no further purchases of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges on behalf of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs). The above company has been included in the Ban List of the Reserve Bank of India.
