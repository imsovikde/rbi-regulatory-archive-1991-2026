## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced on <u>October 7, 2013</u>, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of `19,500 crore on October 18, 2013 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the operational guidelines issued in this regard on <u>October 8, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on November 1, 2013 (Friday).
