## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `54.2415 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `71.3620 on May 09, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 08, 2013) were `54.1600 and `70.9980 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
