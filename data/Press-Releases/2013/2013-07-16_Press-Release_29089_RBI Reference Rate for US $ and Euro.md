## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is

59.3925 and the Reference Rate for Euro is 77.7250 on July 16, 2013. The
corresponding rates for the previous day (July 15, 2013) were 60.0510 and

78.4705 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the
middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||July 15, 2013|July 16, 2013|
|1 GBP|90.7130|89.8965|
|100 YEN|60.51|59.54|
