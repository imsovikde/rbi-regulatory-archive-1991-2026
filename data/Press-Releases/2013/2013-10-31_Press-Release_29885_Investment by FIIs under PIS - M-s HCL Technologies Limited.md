## Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s HCL Technologies Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s HCL Technologies Limited</u> has passed resolutions at their board of directors' level and a special resolution by the shareholders, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), through the primary market and stock exchanges, under the Portfolio Investment Scheme, up to 49 per cent (revised from earlier limit of 30 per cent) of its paid up capital.
