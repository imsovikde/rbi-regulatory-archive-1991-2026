## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : M/s. Indiabulls Housing Finance Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under the Portfolio Investment Scheme equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Indiabulls Housing Finance Limited as under:

- FIIs through primary market and stock exchanges up to <u>100 per cent</u> of the paid up capital of the company.
- NRIs/PIOs through primary market and stock exchanges up to <u>24 per</u> <u>cent</u> of the paid up capital of the company. M/s. Indiabulls Housing Finance Limited has passed resolutions at the board
of directors' level and a special resolution by the shareholders, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures.
