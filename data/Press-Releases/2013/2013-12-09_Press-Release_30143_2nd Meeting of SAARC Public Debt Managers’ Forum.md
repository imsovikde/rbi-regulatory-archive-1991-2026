## December 9, 2013

**nd** **2 Meeting of SAARC Public Debt Managers’ Forum**

The Reserve Bank of India, in collaboration with the South Asian Association for Regional Co-operation (SAARC) Secretariat, organized the second meeting of the SAARC Debt Managers’ Forum at Jaipur on December 6 & 7, 2013. The SAARC Debt Managers’ Forum serves as a platform for senior government debt mangers from the member countries to exchange their views, experiences and policies in the field of public debt management including best practices, techniques, strategies, operations and statistics. Governor, Deputy Governors and Senior Officials of the Central Banks and the Ministries of Finance of SAARC member countries attended the meeting.

Mr. R. Gandhi, Executive Director, Reserve Bank of India welcomed the participants and highlighted the importance of the Forum as also the context of choosing the theme as “Sovereign Debt Management in Emerging Markets”. He also highlighted the importance of macro-economic development, the strategies for debt management in varying market conditions and debt market infrastructure in Emerging Market and Developing Economies (EMDEs) particularly, in SAARC countries. In his key note address, Mr. Harun R. Khan, Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India, observed the growing inter-connectedness with the global economy, the challenges posed to policy making for EMDEs in debt management area and the way forward to manage the uncertainties that emerge by an inter-connected world. He highlighted the challenges faced by debt managers as greater integration of EMDEs in international capital markets would transmit the contagion to the domestic debt markets very fast. Further, strong capital inflows may expand the demand for Government debt but such flows also pose the sudden-stop risk. He underlined the challenge faced by policy makers to maintain a fine balance between various objectives, viz. debt sustainability, price stability, financial stability and steady growth and termed this challenge as new “Quadrilemma”.

The debt managers from SAARC countries shared their country practices, polices, latest developments in debt management, market infrastructure, institutional mechanism, etc. The experts in the debt management field drawn from international and domestic institutions, such as, the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), the International Monetary Fund (IMF), the World Bank, Clearing Corporation of India Ltd. (CCIL), and Morgan Stanley addressed the three technical sessions covering topics, viz. Public Debt Management and Macroeconomic Stability, Global Developments and its Impact on Public Debt Management of Small Emerging Market Economies, Public Debt Management Strategies and its relevance to SAARC countries, Market Infrastructure in G-Sec market and Product innovations and issues and challenges for floatation of Sovereign Foreign Currency bonds. As the meeting of the Forum provided some very useful lessons and takeaways for the SAARC member countries in the area of public debt management, members resolved to continue such structured interactions on ongoing basis.
