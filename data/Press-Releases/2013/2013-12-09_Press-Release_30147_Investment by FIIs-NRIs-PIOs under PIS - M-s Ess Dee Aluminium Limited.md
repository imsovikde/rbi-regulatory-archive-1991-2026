## December 9, 2013

## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS -M/s Ess Dee Aluminium Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s Ess Dee Aluminium Limited</u> has passed resolutions at the board of directors' level and a special resolution by the shareholders, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by FIIs through primary market and stock exchanges up to 100 per cent and by NRIs/PIOs up to 24 per cent of its paid up capital, under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS).
