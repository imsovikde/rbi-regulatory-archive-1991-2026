## RBI announces additional LAF- Repo on March 28, 2013 and

## LAF and MSF on March 30 and 31, 2013

In view of the anticipated large volume of banking transactions during the annual closing of accounts for Financial Year 2012-13, the Reserve Bank of India, in order to facilitate smooth and non-disruptive conduct of banking operations, has decided to conduct additional liquidity operations as under;

|Date|Operation|Timing|
|---|---|---|
|28 March 2013 (Thursday)|Additional LAF Repo|4:45 PM – 5:15 PM|
|30 March 2013|LAF Repo, Reverse|5:30 PM – 6:00 PM|
|(Saturday)|Repo & MSF||
|31 March 2013|LAF Repo, Reverse|5:30 PM – 6:00 PM|
|(Sunday)|Repo & MSF||

th

th

st

The reversal of all the above operations shall be on April 2, 2013 (Tuesday).

The features of the additional LAF and MSF would remain the same as regular LAF and MSF in all aspects.
