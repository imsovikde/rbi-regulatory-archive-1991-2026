## August 22, 2013

## RBI releases its Annual Report for 2012-13

The Reserve Bank of India today released its <u>Annual Report for 2012-13</u>, a statutory Report of its Central Board of Directors. It covers (i) the assessment of the macroeconomic performance during 2012-13 and the prospects for 2013-14, and (ii) the working and operations of the Reserve Bank and its financial accounts for the year 2012-13.
