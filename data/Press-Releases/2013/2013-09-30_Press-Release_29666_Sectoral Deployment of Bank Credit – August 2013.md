## Sectoral Deployment of Bank Credit – August 2013

Data for the month of August 2013 on sectoral deployment of credit collected from select 47 scheduled commercial banks, accounting for about 95 per cent of the total non-food credit deployed by all scheduled commercial banks, are set out in <u>Statements I</u> and <u>II</u>. These data are also available in the Real-Time Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).

Highlights of the data are given below:

� On a year-on-year (y-o-y) basis, non-food bank credit increased by 17.0 per cent in August 2013 as compared with the increase of 15.9 per cent in August 2012.

� Credit to agriculture increased by 12.1 per cent in August 2013 as compared with the increase of 18.7 per cent in August 2012.

� Credit to industry increased by 17.3 per cent in August 2013 same as in August

2012. Acceleration in credit growth to industry was observed in all the major sub- sectors, barring mining & quarrying, engineering, vehicles, rubber & rubber products, construction and glass & glassware.
� Credit to the services sector increased by 18.4 per cent in August 2013 as compared with the increase of 14.2 per cent in August 2012.

� Credit to NBFCs increased by 18.0 per cent in August 2013 as compared with the increase of 32.5 per cent in August 2012.

� Personal loans increased by 17.8 per cent in August 2013 as compared with the increase of 13.0 per cent in August 2012.
