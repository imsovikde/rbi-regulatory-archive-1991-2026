## Mid-Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2012-2013 :

## To be released at 11.00 a.m. on RBI Website on March 19, 2013

The Reserve Bank of India will release the next Mid-Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2012-13 at 11.00 a.m. on March 19, 2013. The Review will be by way of a press release and will be placed on the Reserve Bank's website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>).
