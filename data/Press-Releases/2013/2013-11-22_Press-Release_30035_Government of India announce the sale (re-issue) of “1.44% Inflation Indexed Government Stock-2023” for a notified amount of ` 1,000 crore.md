## Government of India announce the sale (re-issue) of “1.44% Inflation Indexed

**Government Stock-2023” for a notified amount of** ` **1,000 crore**

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “<u>1.44%</u> <u>Inflation Indexed Government Stock-2023</u>” for a notified amount of `1,000 crore through price based auction. The auction will be conducted using uniform price method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai on November 26, 2013 (Tuesday).

Up to 20% of the notified amount of the sale of the stocks will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on November 26, 2013. The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 11.30 a.m**.** and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon.

The result of the auctions will be announced on November 26, 2013 and payment by successful bidders will be on November 27, 2013 (Wednesday)**.**

The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

The underwriting of the Government Securities under auctions by the ‘Primary Dealers’ will be as per the “<u>Revised Scheme of Underwriting Commitment and</u> <u>Liquidity Support</u>” announced by the Reserve Bank vide circular RBI/2007-08/186 dated November 14, 2007. Bids for underwriting of the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) portion can be submitted by ‘Primary Dealers’ from 10:30 AM up to 12.00 Noon on November 25, 2013 (Monday) on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system.
