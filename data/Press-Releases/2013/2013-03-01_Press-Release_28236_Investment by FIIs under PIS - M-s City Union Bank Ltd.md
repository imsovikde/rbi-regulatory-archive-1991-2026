## March 1, 2013

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s City Union Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s City Union Bank Ltd</u>. has crossed the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of share of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors.
