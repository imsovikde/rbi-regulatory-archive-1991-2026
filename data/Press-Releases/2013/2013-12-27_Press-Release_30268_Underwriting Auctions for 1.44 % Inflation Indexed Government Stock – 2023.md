## Underwriting Auctions for 1.44 % Inflation Indexed

## Government Stock – 2023

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “1.44 % Inflation Indexed Government Stock - 2023” for a notified amount of ` 500 crore through auctions to be held on December 31, 2013.

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method on December 30, 2013. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 p.m. on the date of auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, and Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
