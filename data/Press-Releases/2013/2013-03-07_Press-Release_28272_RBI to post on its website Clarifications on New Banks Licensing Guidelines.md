## RBI to post on its website Clarifications on New Banks Licensing Guidelines

The Reserve Bank of India has been receiving queries from intending applicants seeking clarifications on <u>the guidelines</u> on Licensing of New Banks in the Private Sector. The guidelines were issued on February 22, 2013.

Considering that the clarifications sought would be of wider interest and use for all intending applicants, the Reserve Bank has decided to post the clarifications on its website. The identity of those who raise queries would be kept confidential.

The queries may be sent by April 10, 2013 to Chief General Manager-in- Charge, Department of Banking Operations and Development, Reserve Bank of th India, Central Office, 12 Floor, Central Office Building, Fort, Mumbai–400 001 through mail or <u>email</u>.
