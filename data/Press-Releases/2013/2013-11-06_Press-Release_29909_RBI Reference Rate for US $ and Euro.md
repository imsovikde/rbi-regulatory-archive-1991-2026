## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is ` 61.9225 and the Reference Rate for Euro is ` 83.6375 on November 6, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 5, 2013) were ` 61.7880 and ` 83.4190 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
