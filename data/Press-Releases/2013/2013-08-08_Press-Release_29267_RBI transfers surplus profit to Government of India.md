## August 8, 2013

## RBI transfers surplus profit to Government of India

The Reserve Bank's Central Board, at its meeting held today, approved the transfer of surplus profit to the Government of India amounting to ` 330.10 billion for the year ended June 30, 2013 as against ` 160.10 billion for the year ended June 30, 2012.
