## November 22, 2013

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies -

## Incorporation in Caution List : M/s Axis Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign share holding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs)/Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)/American Depository Receipt (ADR)/Global Depository Receipts (GDRs) in M/s Axis Bank Ltd. has reached the trigger limit. Accordingly, further purchases of equity shares of <u>M/s Axis Bank Ltd</u>. would be allowed only after obtaining the Reserve Bank's prior approval.
