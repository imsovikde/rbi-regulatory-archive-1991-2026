## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs 38,500 crore on November 01, 2013 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on November 15, 2013 (Friday).
