## May 22, 2013

## Government of India nominates Dr. Nachiket M. Mor on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Dr. Nachiket Madhusudan Mor as a Member of the Local Board, Eastern Area of Reserve Bank of India, for a period of th four years with effect from 16 May 2013.

Further, the Central Government has also nominated Dr. Nachiket Mor to be a Director of the Central Board of Reserve Bank of India.

Dr. Nachiket Mor holds a Post-Graduate Diploma in Finance Management from the Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad and a Doctorate in Finance and Econometrics from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, USA. He is a Yale World Fellow (2004).
