## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is

61.9580 and the Reference Rate for Euro is 85.3345 on December 17, 2013.
The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 16, 2013) were

62.1024 and 85.4284 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US
dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||

|1 GBP|101.2704|101.1402|
