## Results of Underwriting Auctions

In the auctions conducted on September 26, 2013 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

(` crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ` 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.12 % GS 2020|3000|1512|1488|3000|9.40|
|8.28 % GS 2027|6000|3003|2997|6000|20.00|
|New 17-yr GS 2030|2000|1008|992|2000|9.45|
|8.83 % GS 2041|3000|1512|1488|3000|40.00|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on September 27, 2013.
