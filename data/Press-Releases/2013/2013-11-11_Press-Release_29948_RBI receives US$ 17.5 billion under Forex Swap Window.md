## November 11, 2013

## RBI receives US$ 17.5 billion under Forex Swap Window

The Reserve Bank has received till date USD 17.5 billion under the special concessional window for swapping Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Banks) Deposits and Overseas Foreign Currency Borrowings. The Reserve Bank of India had announced these schemes on September 4, 2013.
