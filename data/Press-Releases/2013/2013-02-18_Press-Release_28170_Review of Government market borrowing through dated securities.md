## February 18, 2013

## Review of Government market borrowing through dated securities

On review of the Government of India’s cash position and funding requirement, it has been decided, in consultation with Government of India, to reduce the government market borrowing through dated securities by Rs. 12,000 crore for the current financial year. Accordingly, the auction for dated securities scheduled on February 22, 2013 for Rs. 12,000 crore has been cancelled.
