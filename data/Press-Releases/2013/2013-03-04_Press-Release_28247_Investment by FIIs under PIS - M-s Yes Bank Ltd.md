## March 4, 2013

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s Yes Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s Yes Bank Ltd.</u> has crossed the overall limit of 49 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of share of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors.
