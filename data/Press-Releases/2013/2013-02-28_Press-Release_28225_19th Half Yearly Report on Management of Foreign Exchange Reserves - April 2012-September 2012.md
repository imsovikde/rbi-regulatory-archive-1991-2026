## February 28, 2013

**th** **19 Half Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves -** **April 2012-September 2012**

<u>th</u> The Reserve Bank of India has released the <u>19 half-yearly report on</u> <u>management of foreign exchange reserves with reference to September 2012</u>.

It may be recalled that in February 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the public domain for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure.
