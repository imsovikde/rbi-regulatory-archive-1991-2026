## October 8, 2013

## Investment by FIIs under PIS-Incorporation in Ban List :

## M/s The Great Eastern Shipping Co. Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s The Great Eastern Shipping</u> <u>Co. Ltd.</u> has crossed the limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors.
