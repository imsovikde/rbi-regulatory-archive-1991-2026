## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `54.4800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `72.2318 on February 21, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (February 20, 2013) were `54.0510 and `72.5355 respectively.
