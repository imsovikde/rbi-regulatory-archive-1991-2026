## RBI and Bank Branches to remain open on September 14 - 15, 2013

With a view to facilitating collection of advance taxes for Income Tax and Corporate Tax and meeting the probable rush of tax-payers, all designated branches of agency banks conducting Government business and Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) will keep their counters open on Saturday, September 14, 2013 and Sunday, September 15, 2013 effectively making both these days normal working days.
