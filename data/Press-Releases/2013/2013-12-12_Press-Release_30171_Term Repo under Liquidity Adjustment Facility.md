## December 12, 2013

## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced on <u>December 11, 2013</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of Rs. 48,500 crore on December 13, 2013 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on <u>October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on December 27, 2013 (Friday).
