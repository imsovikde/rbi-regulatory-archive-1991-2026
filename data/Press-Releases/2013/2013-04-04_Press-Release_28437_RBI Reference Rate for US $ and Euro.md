## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `54.6460 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `70.0795 on April 4, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 3, 2013) were `54.3885 and `69.5881 respectively.
