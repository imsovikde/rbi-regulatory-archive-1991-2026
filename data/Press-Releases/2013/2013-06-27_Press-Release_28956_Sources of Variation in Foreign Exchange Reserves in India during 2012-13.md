## Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during 2012-13

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released the Balance of Payments (BoP) data for 2012-13 on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during 2012-13 have been compiled.

## Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves: 2012-13

During 2012-13, there was a decline in the foreign exchange reserves. The sources of variation in the foreign exchange reserves are set out in Table 1.

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), the foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 3.8 billion during 2012-13 as against a decline of US$ 12.8 billion during 2011-12. The foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including the valuation effects) declined by US$ 2.4 billion during 2012-13 as compared to a decline of US$ 10.4 billion during the previous year (Table 2).

The valuation loss, reflecting the appreciation of the US dollar against major currencies, amounted to US$ 6.2 billion during 2012-13 as against a valuation gain of US$ 2.4 billion during the preceding year.
