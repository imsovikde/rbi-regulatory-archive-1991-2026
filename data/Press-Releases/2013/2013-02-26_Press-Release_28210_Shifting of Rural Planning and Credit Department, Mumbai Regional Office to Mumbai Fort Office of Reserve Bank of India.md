## February 26, 2013

**Shifting of Rural Planning and Credit Department, Mumbai Regional Office to Mumbai Fort Office of Reserve Bank of India**

Rural Planning and Credit Department, Mumbai Regional Office housed at st Garment House Worli, Mumbai-400018, has shifted to 1 Floor, Main Building, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Regional Office, Mumbai -400 001, at the close of business of February 07, 2013. It is, therefore, requested that in future all correspondences meant for Rural Planning and Credit Department, Mumbai Regional Office, Reserve Bank of India may now be addressed to:-

The Regional Director(Maharashtra & Goa) Reserve Bank of India, Rural Planning & Credit Department, Mumbai Regional Office, st 1 Floor, Main Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Mumbai-400 001.
