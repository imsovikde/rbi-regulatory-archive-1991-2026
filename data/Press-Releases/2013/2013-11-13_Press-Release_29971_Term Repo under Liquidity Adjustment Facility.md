## November 13, 2013

## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The reversal of the Term Repo auction held on November 1, 2013 will now take place on November 18, 2013 (Monday) instead of November 15, 2013(Friday). Accordingly, interest will be adjusted for the revised tenor of the Term Repo at the rates at which the successful bidders had placed their bids in the auction.

Further, the Reserve Bank will conduct an 11 day Term Repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 39,000 crore on November 18, 2013 (Monday). The auction will be conducted as per the guidelines issued on October 29, 2013. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on November 29, 2013 (Friday).

The above change has been effected in view of the Government of Maharashtra cancelling the earlier declared holiday of November 14, 2013 to November 15, 2013 (Friday) as holiday on account of Moharrum. The holiday has been declared under the Negotiable Instrument Act, 1881.
