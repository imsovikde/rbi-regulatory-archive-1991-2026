## November 8, 2013

## Revision of Ways and Means Advances to State Governments

The aggregate Normal Ways and Means Advances (WMA) limit for the State Governments inclusive of Union Territory of Puducherry is currently placed at `10,240 crore for the year 2013-14. On a review, it has been decided to increase the WMA limit for the State Governments by 50 per cent to ` 15,360 crore with effect from November 11, 2013. Other terms and conditions of the WMA scheme would remain unchanged.
