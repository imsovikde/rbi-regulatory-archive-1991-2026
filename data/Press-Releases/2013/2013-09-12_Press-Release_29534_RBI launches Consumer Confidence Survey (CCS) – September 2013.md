## September 12, 2013

## RBI launches Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – September 2013

The Reserve Bank of India has been conducting Consumer Confidence Survey of Households (CCS) on a quarterly basis since June 2010. The survey aims at capturing subjective assessments of around 5,400 households across 6 metropolitan cities viz., Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and New Delhi. The results of this Survey are being used by Reserve Bank as one of the important inputs to the monetary policy formulation. The latest round of the Survey with September 2013 as the reference period has been launched today. The Survey seeks qualitative responses on questions pertaining to economic conditions, and household circumstances, income & spending, perceptions on prices, employment prospects and major outlays such as gold, car, house, durable goods etc.

The agency M/s Sigma Research & Consulting Pvt. Ltd. has been engaged to conduct the field work for this round of the Survey on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India. For this purpose, the respondents will be approached by the agency and the selected respondents are requested to provide their response. Other individuals who are not approached by the agency can also participate in this survey round by downloading the survey schedule from <u>the link</u>. The filled in survey schedule may be e-mailed to us as per contact details given below. In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact at the following address:
