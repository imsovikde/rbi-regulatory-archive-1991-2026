## RBI’s Special Repo Window for Liquidity Requirements of Mutual Funds

On July 17, 2013, Reserve Bank had opened a special 3-day repo window to meet the liquidity requirements of mutual funds.

As announced in the <u>Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2013-14</u> on October 29, 2013, Reserve Bank has decided to close the window with immediate effect.
