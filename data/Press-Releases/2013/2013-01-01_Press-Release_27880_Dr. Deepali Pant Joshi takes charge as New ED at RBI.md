## Dr. Deepali Pant Joshi takes charge as New ED at RBI

<u>Photograph</u>

Dr. Deepali Pant Joshi has taken over as the new Executive Director at the Reserve Bank of India today. She will look after Customer Service Department and Rural Planning and Credit Department. Dr. Joshi will also be the Alternate Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act.

Prior to her appointment as Executive Director, Dr. Joshi was the Regional Director of Rajasthan.

A doctorate in political economy of development of South and South East Asia 1975-1980 from University of Allahabad, Dr. Joshi also holds degrees in law and management. She has written extensively on rural credit and financial inclusion.
