## November 6, 2013

## RBI receives US$ 15.2 billion under Forex Swap Window

The Reserve Bank has received till date USD 15.2 billion under the special concessional window for swapping Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Banks) Deposits and Overseas Foreign Currency Borrowings. The Reserve Bank of India had announced these schemes on September 4, 2013.
