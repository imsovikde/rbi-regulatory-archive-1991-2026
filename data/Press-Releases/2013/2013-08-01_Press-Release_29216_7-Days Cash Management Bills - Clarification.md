## August 1, 2013

## 7-Days Cash Management Bills-Clarification

This is to clarify/ inform that holding period of the 7-Days Cash Management Bills auctioned today for ` 3,000 crore is 6 days due to August 9, 2013 being holiday. Accordingly, the effective holding period return on these bills emerged in today’s auction is 10.3593 per cent.
