## Monetary Policy Statement 2013-14 on May 3, 2013

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Monetary Policy Statement 2013-14 on Friday, May 3, 2013. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00

a.m. on May 3, 2013 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
