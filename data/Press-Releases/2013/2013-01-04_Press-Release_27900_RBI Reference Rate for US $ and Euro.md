## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `54.8458 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `71.5405 on January 4, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (January 3, 2013) were `54.4153 and `71.4945 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
