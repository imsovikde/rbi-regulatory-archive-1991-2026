## Foreign Trade Statistics

The Reserve Bank of India today released <u>four statements</u> providing disaggregated monthly data on India’s Exports and Imports (Commodity-wise and Destination-wise) for April-June 2013-14 and revised data for 2011-12 and 2012-13.
