## RBI Releases Disaggregated Data on India’s Invisibles for Third Quarter (October-December) of 2012-13

The Reserve Bank today released <u>data on India’s invisibles</u> as per the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) format for October-December 2012 and revised data for April-June 2012 and July-September 2012 along with corresponding data for previous year.

In order to enhance data dissemination, the disaggregated data on India’s Invisibles would be released on a quarterly basis on the RBI website with about one month’s lag from the date of press release of BoP. These data would also be available on RBI’s DBIE database. (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/dbie.rbi?site=home](http://dbie.rbi.org.in/DBIE/dbie.rbi?site=home)</u>)
