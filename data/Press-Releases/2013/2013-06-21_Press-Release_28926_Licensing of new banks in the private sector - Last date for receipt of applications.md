## Licensing of new banks in the private sector-Last date for receipt of applications

The Reserve Bank of India released the <u>Guidelines for Licensing of New</u> <u>Banks in the Private Sector</u> on its website on February 22, 2013.

As indicated in the above Press Release, applications for setting up new banks in the private sector should reach the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Department of Banking Operations and Development, Reserve Bank of India, Central th Office, 12 Floor, Central Office Building, Mumbai 400 001 on or before July 1, 2013. Applications received after close of business (i.e. 17.45 hrs) on July 1, 2013 or incomplete applications will not be considered.
