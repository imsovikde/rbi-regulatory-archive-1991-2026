## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of January 2013

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of December 2012 were released on February 15, 2013.

The exports and imports of services during the month of January 2013 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

## (US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|October 2012|12,348|6,703|
|November 2012|12,029|6,618|
|December 2012|12,881|6,761|
|January 2013 Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the|13,899|7,522|

Note: Data are provisional.

Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis. Revised data on services receipts and payments on quarterly basis (July-September 2012 and Partially Revised data for the First Quarter (April-June 2012) are available in the press release on 'Developments in India's Balance of Payments during the Second Quarter (July-September 2012) and Partially Revised data for the First Quarter (April- June 2012) of 2012-13' placed on RBI website on December 31, 2012.
