## RBI receives US$ 22.7 billion under Forex Swap Window

The Reserve Bank has received till date USD 22.7 billion under the special concessional window for swapping Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Banks) Deposits and Overseas Foreign Currency Borrowings. The Reserve Bank of India had announced these schemes on September 4, 2013.
