## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `55.5220 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `71.6725 on May 22, 2013. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 21, 2013) were `55.0395 and `70.9269 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
