## July 03, 2013

## RBI releases data on ‘Weighted Average Lending Rate (WALR)' of Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs)

The Reserve Bank today released annual time-series <u>data</u> on ‘Weighted <u>Average Lending Rate</u>’ of Scheduled Commercial Banks for the period 1992 to 2012 on its website. The data is based on data reported under Basic Statistical Return 1 - Part A (BSR-1A), in which granular data on borrowal accounts with credit limit more than ` 25,000 till 1998 and more than ` 200,000 thereafter are collected from SCBs.

These data will be available on RBI’s DBIE database (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).
