## Investment by FIIs under PIS-Incorporation in Ban List :

## M/s Axis Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that foreign share holding by Global Depository Receipt (GDR)/American Depository Receipt (ADR)/Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)/Non Resident Indian (NRI)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIO)/Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in <u>M/s Axis Bank Ltd</u>. has crossed the overall limit of 49 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of share of this bank would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of GDR/ADR/FDI/NRI/PIO/FIIs.
