## June 6, 2013

## Investment by FIIs/ NRIs/PIOs under PIS :

## ING Vysya Bank Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>ING Vysya Bank Ltd</u>. by Foreign Institutional Investors(FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians(NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origins(PIOs) and through American Depository Receipt(ADR) /Global Depository Receipt(GDR)/ Foreign Direct Investment(FDI) in the primary/secondary markets have reached the trigger limit. Therefore purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
