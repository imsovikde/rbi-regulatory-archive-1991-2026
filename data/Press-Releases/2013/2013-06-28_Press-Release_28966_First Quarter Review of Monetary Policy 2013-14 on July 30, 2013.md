## First Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2013-14 on July 30, 2013

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the First Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2013-14 on Tuesday, July 30, 2013. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at

11.00 a.m. on July 30, 2013 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
