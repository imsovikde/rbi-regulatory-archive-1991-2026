## Inflation Indexed National Saving Securities- Cumulative (IINSS-C)

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India, in consultation with Government of India launched Inflation Indexed National Saving Securities- Cumulative by opening the issuance on December 23, 2013 and closing the same on December 31, 2013. On review, now it has been decided to extend the issuance of Inflation Indexed National Saving Securities- Cumulative till March 31, 2014. The issuance can be closed earlier than March 31, 2014 with a prior notice.
