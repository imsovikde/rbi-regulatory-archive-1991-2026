## Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

The Reserve Bank will conduct a 14 day term repo variable rate auction for a notified amount of ` 39,000 crore on December 27, 2013 (Friday). The auction will be conducted as per the <u>guidelines issued on October 29, 2013</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. The reversal of the term repo will take place on January 10, 2014 (Friday).
