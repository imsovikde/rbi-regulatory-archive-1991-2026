## Directions under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (AACS)

## United Commercial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh -

## Extension of Validity Period of Directions

It is hereby notified for the information of the public that the validity period of directions imposed on United Commercial Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh vide our directive UBD.CO.BSD IV No.D-46/12.28.055/2012-13 dated May 24, 2013 has been extended further vide directive UBD.CO.BSD-IV No.D-18/

12.28.055/2013-14 dated November 27, 2013 for a period of six months i.e. from December 01, 2013 to May 31, 2014. All the terms and conditions excepting the validity period as above remain unchanged. A copy of the directive dated November 27, 2013 notifying the above modification is displayed at the bank’s premises for perusal of the public.
