## RBI holds Annual Statistics Conference 2013

The Annual Statistics Conference 2013 of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was organised by the Department of Statistics and Information Management during March 22-23, 2013 in Mumbai. The conference provided a platform to the statisticians of the Reserve Bank to present their research before external experts and receive their feedback in order to make the analysis more meaningful for policy and research.

Dr. Urjit R. Patel, Deputy Governor inaugurated the conference. Shri Deepak Mohanty, Executive Director; heads of select central office departments of the Reserve Bank; renowned statisticians and economists from the academia; and the statisticians of the Reserve Bank participated in the conference.

The conference highlighted some broad features of statistical activity in the Reserve Bank. Three important and key takeaways were:

· The use of statistical tools for modelling and forecasting macroeconomic variables of critical importance to the Reserve Bank needs to be constantly improved.

· The initiatives to externally validate statistical research, surveys and other statistical activities are useful and should be continued.

· Focussed approach to research across various central office departments and the regional offices should be pursued to accomplish the research agenda of the department encompassing both policy and operational areas in a timely manner.

Building on the initiatives of the department in managing statistical databases, providing research inputs, undertaking forward looking surveys and data dissemination, some areas for further work were identified. These included, among other things, taking forward G-20 data gaps initiatives, developing a full-fledged forecasting suite including Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE) models, undertaking surveys on financial inclusion and bringing out a revised edition of the Manual on Banking and Financial Statistics.

The technical sessions of the conference deliberated upon, among other things, analytical issues in modelling and forecasting, external sector developments, banking and corporate sector, surveys and economic measurements. The conference also deliberated upon issues in strengthening micro-level, granular and consistent statistical information system on banking statistics to enhance its use for policy.
