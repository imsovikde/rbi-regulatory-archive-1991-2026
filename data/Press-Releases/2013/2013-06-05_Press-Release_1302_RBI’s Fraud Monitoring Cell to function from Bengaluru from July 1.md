## RBI’s Fraud Monitoring Cell to function from Bengaluru from July 1

The Reserve Bank of India’s Fraud Monitoring Cell attached to Department of nd Banking Supervision, Central Office has shifted from the present location at 2 Floor, World Trade Centre-1, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai - 400005 to Bengaluru Regional Office of the Reserve Bank. The Central Fraud monitoring Cell will continue to be part of Department of Banking Supervision, Central Office Mumbai and will start functioning from the new location at Bengaluru from July 01, 2013. All the banks/ other stakeholders are requested to take note of the address of the Central Fraud Monitoring Cell at the new location:

Central Fraud Monitoring Cell Department of Banking Supervision, Reserve Bank of India, 10/3/8, Nruputhunga Road,

P.B. No. 5467
Bengaluru – 560001. Phone No: - +91 80 22244120 Fax No.: +91 80 22127754 <u>Email</u>
