## RBI announces auction of two 48-days Government of India Cash Management Bills

The Reserve Bank of India announced the auction of two 48-days Government of India Cash Management Bills as per the following details:

||Auction Date|Settlement/Issue Date|Date of Maturity|Notified Amount|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|August 26, 2013|August 27, 2013|October 14, 2013|` 11,000 crore|
|2|August 27, 2013|August 28, 2013|October 15, 2013 Total|` 11,000 crore ` 22,000 crore|

The auctions will be conducted using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) between 10.30 a.m. and

12.00 noon. Result of the auctions will be announced on the same day. The Cash Management Bills will have the generic character of Treasury Bills and their sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F.2 (12)-W&M/97 dated March 31, 1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.
