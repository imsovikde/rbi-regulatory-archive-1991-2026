# Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of February 2013

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of January 2013 were released on March 15, 2013.

The exports and imports of services during the month of February 2013 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

## (US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|January 2013|13,899|7,522|
|February 2013|12,287|6,349|

<u>Note: Data are provisional.</u>

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis. Revised data on services receipts and payments on quarterly basis (October-December 2012 and Partially Revised data for the First Quarter (April-June 2012) and Second Quarter (July-September 2012) are available in the press release on 'Developments in India’s Balance of Payments during the Third Quarter (October-December 2012)' placed on RBI website on March 28, 2013.
