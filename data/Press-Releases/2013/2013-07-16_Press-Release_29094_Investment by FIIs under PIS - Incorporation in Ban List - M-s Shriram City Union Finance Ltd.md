## July 16, 2013

## Investment by FIIs under PIS-Incorporation in Ban List :

## M/s Shriram City Union Finance Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s Shriram City Union Finance</u> <u>Ltd</u>. has crossed the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors.
