## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is

59.8280 and the Reference Rate for Euro is 79.3277 on July 30, 2013. The
corresponding rates for the previous day (July 29, 2013) were 59.2970 and

78.7129 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and the
middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Currency|Date||
|---|---|---|
||July 29, 2013|July 30, 2013|
|1 GBP|91.1810|91.7582|
|100 YEN|60.58|60.84|
