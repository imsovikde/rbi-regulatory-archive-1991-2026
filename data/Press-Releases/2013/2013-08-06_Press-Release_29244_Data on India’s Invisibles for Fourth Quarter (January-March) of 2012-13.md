## August 6, 2013

## Data on India’s Invisibles for Fourth Quarter

## (January-March) of 2012-13

The Reserve Bank today released <u>data on India’s invisibles</u> as per the IMF’s Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual (BPM6) format for January-March 2013 and revised data for April-June 2012 and July-September 2012 and October-December 2012 along with corresponding data for previous year.

In order to enhance data dissemination, the disaggregated data on India’s Invisibles are released on a quarterly basis on the RBI website with about one month’s lag from the date of press release of BoP.
