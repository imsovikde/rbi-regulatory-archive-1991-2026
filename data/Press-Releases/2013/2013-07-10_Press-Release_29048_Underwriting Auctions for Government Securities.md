## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of following Government Stock through auctions to be held on July 12, 2013.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under:

(` in crore) **Minimum**

|Nomenclature of the|Notified|MUC amount per||bidding|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|Primary Dealer||PD under ACU auction|
|8.12 per cent GS 2020|3000||72|72|
|8.33 per cent GS 2026|6000||143|143|
|8.32 per cent GS 2032|3000||72|72|
|7.40 per cent GS 2035|3000||72|72|

**commitment per** **Security**

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method on July 11, 2013. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Core Banking Solution (E- Kuber) System between

10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on the date of auction. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, and Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
