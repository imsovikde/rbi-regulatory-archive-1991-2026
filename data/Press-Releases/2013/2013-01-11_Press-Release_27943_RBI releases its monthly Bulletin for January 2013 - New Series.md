financial results of 2,832 listed non-government non-financial (NGNF) companies, the article provides a brief analysis of the performance at the aggregate level as well as by size and major industries. It captures the evolving trend in sales, expenditure and profit margins of the corporate sector over a longer horizon. The detailed data used in the analysis in the article were released separately on the Reserve Bank website on January 9, 2013.

## Highlights:

i) Sales growth of the private (non-financial) corporate business sector declined further in the first half of 2012-13. While there was a modest growth in operating profits, net profits increased, reversing the declining trend observed in the previous two half years. ii) The decline in sales growth was broad-based across manufacturing, IT and non-IT services sectors. While the net profit margin of the IT sector was maintained, the non-IT services registered a fall in margin. iii) Performance of smaller companies was relatively weaker as compared with that of larger companies.
