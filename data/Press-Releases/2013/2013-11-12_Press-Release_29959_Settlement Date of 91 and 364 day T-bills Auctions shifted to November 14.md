## Settlement Date of 91 and 364 day T-bills Auctions shifted to November 14

The settlement date for auctions of 91 and 364 Day Treasury Bills has been advanced to November 14, 2013 from the earlier November 15, 2013. The auctions will be conducted on November 13, 2014. Other terms and conditions of the auctions remain the same.

The change has been effected in view of the Government of Maharashtra cancelling the earlier declared holiday of November 14, 2013 to November 15, 2013 (Friday) as holiday on account of Moharrum. The holiday has been declared under the Negotiable Instrument Act, 1881.
