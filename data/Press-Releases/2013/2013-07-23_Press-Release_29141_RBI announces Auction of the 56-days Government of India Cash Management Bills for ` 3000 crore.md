## July 23, 2013

## RBI announces Auction of the 56-days Government of India

**Cash Management Bills for** ` **3000 crore**

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of the 56-days Government of India Cash Management Bills for a notified amount of ` 3,000 crore. The auction will be conducted on July 25, 2013 (Thursday) using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The Cash Management Bills will be repaid at par on September 20,

2013. The competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) between 10.30 a.m. and
12.00noon on Thursday, July 25, 2013. Results will be announced on the same day. Payments by successful bidders will be on Friday, July 26, 2013. The Cash Management Bills will have the generic character of Treasury Bills and their sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F.2 (12)-W&M/97 dated March 31, 1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.
