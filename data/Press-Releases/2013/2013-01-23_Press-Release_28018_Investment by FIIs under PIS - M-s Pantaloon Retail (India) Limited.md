## Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s Pantaloon Retail (India) Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s Pantaloon Retail (India)</u> <u>Limited</u> has crossed the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of share of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors.
