## May 13, 2013

## RBI releases its monthly Bulletin for May 2013

The Reserve Bank today released the <u>May 2013</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin contains the Governor’s Statement on Monetary Policy 2013-14, Macroeconomic and Monetary Developments in 2012-13, the first Prof. Tendulkar Memorial Oration by Professor Abhijit Banerjee and regular features, *viz.,* speeches of the top management and current statistics.
