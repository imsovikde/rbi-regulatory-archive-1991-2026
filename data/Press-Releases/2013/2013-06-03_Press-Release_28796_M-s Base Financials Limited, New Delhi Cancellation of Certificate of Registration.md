## M/s Base Financials Limited, New Delhi Cancellation of Certificate of Registration

It is hereby notified for the information of the public that the Reserve Bank of India in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA(6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled on May 6, 2013, the Certificate of Registration bearing No.14.00679 dated April 24, 1998 issued to M/s Base Financials Limited, New Delhi (having its Registered Office at 904-A, Chiranjiv Tower, 43, Nehru Place, New Delhi - 110019), to carry on the business of a Non- Banking Financial Institutions.

As such, the above company cannot transact the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution, as defined in clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
