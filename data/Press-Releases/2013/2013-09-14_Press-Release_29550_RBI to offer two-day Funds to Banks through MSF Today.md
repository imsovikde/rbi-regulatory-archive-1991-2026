## September 14, 2013

## RBI to offer two-day Funds to Banks through MSF Today

Given the market conditions, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has decided to offer two-day funds to banks through the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF). The MSF window will be open today between 5 pm and 5.30 pm. Funds will be repayable on Monday, September 16, 2013.
