## September 21, 2023

**Reserve Bank of India (Prudential Regulations on Basel III Capital Framework,** **Exposure Norms, Significant Investments, Classification, Valuation and** **Operation of Investment Portfolio Norms and Resource Raising Norms for All** **India Financial Institutions) Directions, 2023**

The Reserve Bank has today issued the <u>Reserve Bank of India (Prudential</u> <u>Regulations on Basel III Capital Framework, Exposure Norms, Significant</u> <u>Investments, Classification, Valuation and Operation of Investment Portfolio Norms</u> <u>and Resource Raising Norms for All India Financial Institutions) Directions, 2023</u>. These Directions shall be applicable to the five All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs) viz., EXIM Bank, NABARD, NABFID, NHB, and SIDBI.

The draft Directions were released for public comments on <u>October 22, 2021</u>. The final Directions are being issued today considering, *inter-alia*, the feedback received. These Directions shall be applicable latest w.e.f. April 1, 2024.
