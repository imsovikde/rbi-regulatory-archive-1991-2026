## October 12, 2023

## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of One HFC

The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 29A (6) of the National Housing Bank Act, 1987, has cancelled the CoR of following company.

**Sr. Name of the Registered Office CoR Cancellation** **CoR No.** **No. Company Address** <u>Issued On</u> **Order Date** 1 Ind Bank Housing 3rd Floor, 480 Anna 01.0023.01 August 16, September Ltd Salai, Nandanam, 2001 21, 2023 Chennai, Tamil Nadu – 600035

As such, the above company shall transact the business neither of a Housing Finance Institution nor of a Non-Banking Financial Institution as defined in National Housing Bank Act, 1987 and Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, respectively.
