## September 25, 2023

## Measuring Productivity at the Industry Level-The India KLEMS Database

Today, the Reserve Bank of India placed on its website an update on “<u>Measuring</u> <u>Productivity at the Industry Level-The India KLEMS [capital (K), Labour (L), Energy</u>

<u>(E), Material (M) and services (S)] Database</u>” comprising the Data Manual 2022 along with <u>time series on productivity for 27 industries covering the period 1980-81 to</u> <u>2020-21</u>.
