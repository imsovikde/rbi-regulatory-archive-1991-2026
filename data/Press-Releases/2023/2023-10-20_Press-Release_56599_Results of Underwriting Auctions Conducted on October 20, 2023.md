October 20, 2023 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on October 20, 2023** In the underwriting auctions conducted on October 20, 2023 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut- off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|New GS 2028|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|0.28|
|7.18% GS 2033|13,000|6,510|6,490|13,000|0.35|
|7.30% GS 2053|10,000|5,019|4,981|10,000|0.48|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on October 20, 2023.
