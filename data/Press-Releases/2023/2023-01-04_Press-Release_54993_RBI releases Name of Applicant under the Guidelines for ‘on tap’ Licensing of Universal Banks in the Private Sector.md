## RBI releases Name of Applicant under the Guidelines for ‘on tap’ Licensing of

## Universal Banks in the Private Sector

During the quarter ended December 31, 2022, the Reserve Bank of India has received one application under the <u>Guidelines for ‘on tap’ Licensing of Universal</u> <u>Banks in the Private Sector dated August 1, 2016</u> from Annapurna Finance Private Limited, Bhubaneswar.
