January 10, 2023 **Dr. Michael Debabrata Patra re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor** The Central Government has re-appointed Dr. Michael Debabrata Patra as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a further period of one year with effect from January 15, 2023, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
