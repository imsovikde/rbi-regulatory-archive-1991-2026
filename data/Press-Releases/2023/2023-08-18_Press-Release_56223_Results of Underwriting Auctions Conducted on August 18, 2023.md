August 18, 2023 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on August 18, 2023** In the underwriting auctions conducted on August 18, 2023 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.17% GS 2030|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|0.30|
|7.18% GS 2037|12,000|6,006|5,994|12,000|0.34|
|7.25% GS 2063|12,000|6,006|5,994|12,000|0.54|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 18, 2023.
