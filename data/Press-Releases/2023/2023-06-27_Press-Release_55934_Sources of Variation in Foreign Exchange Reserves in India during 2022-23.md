June 27, 2023 **Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves in India during 2022-23**

Earlier today, the Reserve Bank of India released <u>balance of payments (BoP) data</u> <u>for January-March 2023</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during 2022-23 are detailed below: **Sources of Variation in Foreign Exchange Reserves: 2022-23** During 2022-23, there was a decline in the foreign exchange reserves, the sources of which are set out in <u>Table 1</u>.

On a BoP basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects) foreign exchange reserves decreased by US$ 9.1 billion during 2022-23 as against an accretion of US$ 47.5 billion during 2021-22. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (including valuation effects) decreased by US$ 28.9 billion during 2022-23 as against an increase of US$

30.3 billion in the corresponding period of the preceding year (<u>Table 2</u>).

The valuation loss, primarily reflecting the appreciation of the US dollar against major currencies, amounted to US$ 19.7 billion during 2022-23 as compared with a valuation loss of US$ 17.2 billion during 2021-22.
