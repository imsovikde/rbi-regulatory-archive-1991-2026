## October 05, 2023

**Onboarding of more banks on** उद्गम **UDGAM portal**

Reserve Bank of India launched a Centralised Web Portal उद्गम **UDGAM** (**U**nclaimed **D**eposits – **G**ateway to **A**ccess infor**M**ation) on <u>August 17, 2023</u> for the public to facilitate and make it easier for them to search their unclaimed deposits across multiple banks at one place.

The search facility was initially made available on the portal for seven banks and it was intimated to public that the search facility for remaining banks on the portal would be made available in a phased manner by October 15, 2023. The members of public are hereby informed that, the search facility for <u>30 banks</u> has been made available on the portal on September 28, 2023, which covers around 90% of such unclaimed deposits (in value terms) in Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund.
