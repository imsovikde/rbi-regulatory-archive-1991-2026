## February 08, 2023

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – January 2023</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – January 2023</u>
iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q2:2022-23</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q3:2022-23</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round</u> <u>th</u> 1 <u>80</u> vi. <u>Bank Lending Survey for Q3:2022-23</u>
vii. <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q3:2022-23</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Ajit Prasad

**Press Release: 2022-2023/1686** Director (Communications)

The results of the previous survey round were released on <u>December 07, 2022</u> on the Reserve Bank’s website.
