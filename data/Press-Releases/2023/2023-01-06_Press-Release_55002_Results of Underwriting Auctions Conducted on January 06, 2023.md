January 06, 2023 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on January 06, 2023** In the underwriting auctions conducted on January 06, 2023 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut- off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.38% GS 2027|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|0.14|
|7.26% GS 2032|12,000|6,006|5,994|12,000|0.14|
|7.36% GS 2052|9,000|4,515|4,485|9,000|0.22|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on January 06, 2023.
