Key Features of India’s IIP in end-September 2023: Net claims of non-residents on India rose marginally during Q2:2023-24 to US$ 380.9 billion in September 2023.|

- Foreign-owned financial assets in India recorded higher increase (US$ 7.4 billion) *vis-‡-vis* Indian residents’ overseas financial assets (US$ 6.0 billion), which led to the increase in net claims of non-residents (<u>Table 1</u>).
- Indian residents’ foreign assets increased during July-September 2023 due to rise in currency and deposits as well as overseas direct investment, even though reserve assets and loans declined during the quarter.
- Despite some reduction in direct investment liabilities, India’s total foreign liabilities rose during Q2:2023-24 due to increase in portfolio and other investments.
- Reserve assets accounted for 63.0 per cent of India’s international financial assets at end-September 2023 (<u>Table 2</u>).
- Variation in the exchange rate of the Indian rupee *vis-‡-vis* other currencies impacted the change in liabilities, when valued in US dollar terms.
- The ratio of India’s international financial assets to international financial liabilities improved to 71.0 per cent in September 2023 (68.7 per cent a year ago).
- The share of debt liabilities in total external liabilities rose marginally to 50.3 per cent in September 2023 (49.8 per cent a year ago) (<u>Table 3</u>)
