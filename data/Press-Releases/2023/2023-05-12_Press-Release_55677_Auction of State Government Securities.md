## May 12, 2023

## Auction of State Government Securities

The following State Governments have offered to sell stock by way of auction, for an aggregate amount of **₹8,500 Crore** (Face Value).

2 Assam 1000-10 Yield 3 Gujarat 1500-8 Yield 4 Tamil Nadu 2000-10 Yield 5 Telangana 2000-25 Yield **TOTAL 8,500**

The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **May 16, 2023 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to Ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of One per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the Scheme <u>for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>. Individual investors can place bids as per the non-competitive scheme also through the Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on <u>May 16, 2023</u> <u>(Tuesday). The competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and 11.30 A.M.</u> <u>and non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and 11.00 A.M.</u>

In case of technical difficulties, Core Banking Operations Team (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022- 27595666, 022-27595415, 022-27523516) may be contacted.

For other auction related difficulties, IDMD Auction Team can be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22702431, 022-22705125).
