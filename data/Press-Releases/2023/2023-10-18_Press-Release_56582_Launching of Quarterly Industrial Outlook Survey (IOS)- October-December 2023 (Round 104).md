## October 18, 2023

## Launching of Quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS):

## October-December 2023 (Round 104)

The Reserve Bank has launched the <u>104</u> <u>th</u> <u>round</u> of the quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) of the Indian manufacturing sector for the reference period October- December 2023. The survey assesses business sentiment for the current quarter (Q3:2023-24) and expectations for the ensuing quarter (Q4:2023-24), based on qualitative responses on a set of indicators pertaining to demand conditions, financial conditions, employment conditions and the price situation. The outlook on key parameters for the two subsequent quarters (Q1:2024-25 and Q2:2024-25) are also covered.

2. <u>The survey findings are released on the website of the Bank regularly</u>.
3. M/s Genesis Management & Market Research Pvt. Ltd. has been authorised to conduct the survey for October-December 2023 on behalf of the Reserve Bank. While the agency will approach selected companies, other manufacturing companies are also encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> from the Bank’s website, which is placed under the head ‘<u>Forms</u>’ (see ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the RBI Homepage) and the sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed as per contact details given therein.
4. Identity of the respondents is not revealed.
