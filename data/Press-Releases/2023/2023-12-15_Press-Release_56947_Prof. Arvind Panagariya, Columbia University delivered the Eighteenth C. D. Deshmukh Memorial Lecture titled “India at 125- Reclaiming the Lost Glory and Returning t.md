## December 15, 2023

## Prof. Arvind Panagariya, Columbia University delivered the Eighteenth C. D.

## Deshmukh Memorial Lecture titled “India at 125: Reclaiming the Lost Glory and

## Returning the Global Economy to the Old Normal”

The Reserve Bank of India hosted the Eighteenth C.D. Deshmukh Memorial Lecture on December 15, 2023 in Mumbai. <u>The lecture</u> was delivered by Professor Arvind Panagariya, Professor of Economics and the Jagdish Bhagwati Professor of Indian Political Economy at Columbia University.

The text of his lecture “<u>India at 125: Reclaiming the Lost Glory and Returning the</u> <u>Global Economy to the Old Normal</u>” is placed at <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>.
