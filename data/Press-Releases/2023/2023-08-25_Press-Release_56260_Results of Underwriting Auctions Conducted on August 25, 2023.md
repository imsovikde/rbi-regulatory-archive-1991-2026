August 25, 2023 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on August 25, 2023** In the underwriting auctions conducted on August 25, 2023 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|7.06% GS 2028|8,000|4,011|3,989|8,000|0.22|
|7.18% GS 2033|14,000|7,014|6,986|14,000|0.22|
|7.30% GS 2053|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.42|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on August 25, 2023.
