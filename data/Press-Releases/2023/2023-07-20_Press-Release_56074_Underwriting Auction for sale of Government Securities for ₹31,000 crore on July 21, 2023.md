## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹31,000 crore on July 21, 2023

Government of India has announced the sale (issue/re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auctions to be held on July 21, 2023.

**A.M.** and **09.30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
