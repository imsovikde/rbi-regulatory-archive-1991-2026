April 06, 2023 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on April 06, 2023** In the underwriting auctions conducted on April 06, 2023 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut- off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|New GS 2028|8,000|4,011|3,989|8,000|0.44|
|7.26% GS 2033|14,000|7,014|6,986|14,000|0.50|
|7.36% GS 2052|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|0.88|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on April 06, 2023.
