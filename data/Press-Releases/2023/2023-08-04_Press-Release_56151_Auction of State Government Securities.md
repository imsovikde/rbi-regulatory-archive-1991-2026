## August 04, 2023

## Auction of State Government Securities

The following State Governments have offered to sell stock by way of auction, for an aggregate amount of **₹5,250 Crore** (Face Value).

1 Andhra Pradesh 1000-9 Yield 2 <u>Jammu and Kashmir</u> 500-30 Yield 3 Punjab 750-25 Yield 4 Tamil Nadu 1000-10 Yield 1000-9 Yield 5 Telangana 1000-18 Yield **TOTAL 5,250**

The auction will be conducted on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **August 08, 2023 (Tuesday)**. The Government Stock up to Ten per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions, subject to a maximum limit of One per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the ‘<u>Scheme</u> <u>for Non-competitive Bidding Facility</u>’. Individual investors can also place bids as per the non- competitive scheme through the Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **August 08, 2023** <u>(Tuesday). The competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 A.M. and 11:30 A.M.</u> <u>and non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10:30 A.M. and 11:00 A.M.</u>

In case of technical difficulties, Core Banking Operations Team may be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-27595666, 022-27595415, 022-27523516).

For other auction related difficulties, IDMD Auction Team can be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22702431, 022-22705125).

Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to the Public Debt Office (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22632527, 022-22701299) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) before the auction timing ends.
