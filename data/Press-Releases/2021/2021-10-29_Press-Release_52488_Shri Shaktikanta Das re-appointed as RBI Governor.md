October 29, 2021 **Shri Shaktikanta Das re-appointed as RBI Governor** Central Government has re-appointed Shri Shaktikanta Das as Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a further period of three years beyond the 10 th day of December 2021, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
