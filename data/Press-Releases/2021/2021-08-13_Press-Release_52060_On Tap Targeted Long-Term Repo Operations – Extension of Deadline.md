August 13, 2021 **On Tap Targeted Long-Term Repo Operations – Extension of Deadline** As announced in the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies on</u> <u>August 06, 2021</u>, given the nascent and fragile economic recovery, the on Tap TLTRO Scheme, which was made available up to September 30, 2021, is now being further extended by a period of three months i.e., up to December 31, 2021.

2. All other terms and conditions of the scheme remain unchanged.
