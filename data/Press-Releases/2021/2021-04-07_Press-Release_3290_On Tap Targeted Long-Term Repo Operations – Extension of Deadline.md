## April 07, 2021

## On Tap Targeted Long-Term Repo Operations – Extension of Deadline

As announced in the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies on</u> <u>April 07, 2021</u>, the on Tap TLTRO Scheme, which was made available up to March 31, 2021, is now being further extended by a period of six months i.e., up to September 30, 2021 with a view to increasing the focus of liquidity measures on revival of activity in specific sectors.

2**.** All other terms and conditions of the scheme remain unchanged.
