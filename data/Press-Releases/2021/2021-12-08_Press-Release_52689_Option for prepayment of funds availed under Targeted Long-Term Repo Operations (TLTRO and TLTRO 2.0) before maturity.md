## December 08, 2021

## Option for prepayment of funds availed under Targeted Long-Term Repo

## Operations (TLTRO and TLTRO 2.0) before maturity

As announced in the Governor’s Statement of December 08, 2021, banks which had availed of funds under TLTRO and TLTRO 2.0 will be provided with one more option to prepay the outstanding amount.

2. Banks desirous of exercising the above option are advised to submit their requests *via* <u>email</u> to the Financial Markets Operations Department (Ph: 022-2263 0982 / 2263 4925) in the format enclosed at <u>Annex 1</u> of this press release on or before December 17, 2021. No request for extension of the time window will be entertained.
