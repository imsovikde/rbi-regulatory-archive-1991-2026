## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – July 2021</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – July 2021</u> iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q4:2020-21</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q1:2021-22</u>
