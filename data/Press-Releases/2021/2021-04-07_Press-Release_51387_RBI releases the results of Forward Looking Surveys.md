## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – March 2021</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – March 2021</u> iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q3:2020-21</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q4:2020-21</u>
v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round¹ 69</u>
<u>th</u> vi. <u>Bank Lending Survey for Q4:2020-21</u> vii. <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q4:2020-21</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
