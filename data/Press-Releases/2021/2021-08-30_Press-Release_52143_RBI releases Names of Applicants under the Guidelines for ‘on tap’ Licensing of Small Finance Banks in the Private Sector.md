August 30, 2021 **RBI releases Names of Applicants under the Guidelines for ‘on tap’ Licensing of Small Finance Banks in the Private Sector**

In continuation to the <u>press release dated April 15, 2021</u>, the Reserve Bank of India has received applications under the <u>Guidelines for ‘on tap’ Licensing of Small</u> <u>Finance Banks dated December 5, 2019</u> from the following applicants –

1. Cosmea Financial Holdings Private Limited
2. Tally Solutions Private Limited
