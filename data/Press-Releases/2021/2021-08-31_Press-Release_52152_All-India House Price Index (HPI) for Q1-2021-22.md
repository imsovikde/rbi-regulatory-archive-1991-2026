|---|---|
||compared with 2.7 per cent growth in the previous quarter and 2.8 per cent a year ago; HPI growth varied widely across the cities- it ranged from an expansion of 8.8 per cent (Ahmedabad) to a contraction of (-) 5.1 per cent (Chennai).|
|•|On a sequential (q-o-q) basis, the all-India HPI registered a 0.5 per cent growth in Q1:2021-22; Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kanpur and Lucknow recorded a sequential decline in HPI, whereas it increased in other five cities.|
