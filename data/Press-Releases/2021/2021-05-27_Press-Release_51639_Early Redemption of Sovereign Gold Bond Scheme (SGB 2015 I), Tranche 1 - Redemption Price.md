## May 27, 2021

## Early Redemption of Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme (SGB 2015 I),

## Tranche 1 - Redemption Price

In terms of <u>GOI notification [F.No.4(19)-W&M/2014]</u> and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) <u>circular IDMD.CDD.No.939/14.04.050/2015-16, dated October 30, 2015</u> the redemption price of Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) is based on the simple average closing gold price of 999 purity [published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA)] of the week (Monday-Friday) preceding the date of redemption.

Accordingly, the redemption price for the early redemption due on May 30, 2021 shall be ₹4837/-per unit of SGB and payable on May 29, 2021 (May 30, 2021 being a Sunday).
