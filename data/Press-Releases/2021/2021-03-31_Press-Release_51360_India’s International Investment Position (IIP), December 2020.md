## March 31, 2021

**1** **India’s International Investment Position (IIP), December 2020**

Today, the Reserve Bank released data relating to <u>India’s International</u> <u>Investment Position</u> at <u>end-December 2020</u>.

## Key Features of India’s IIP in December 2020

- Net claims of non-residents on India increased by US$ 3.1 billion from their level at the end of the previous quarter (<u>Table 1</u>).
- Higher increase in foreign-owned assets in India (US$ 52.3 billion) *vis-à-vis* Indian residents’ overseas financial assets (US$ 49.2 billion) resulted in the rise in India’s net foreign liabilities during the quarter.
- Reserve assets accounted for over two-thirds of India’s international financial assets (<u>Table 2</u>).
- Foreign direct investment (FDI) and portfolio equity investment dominated the rise in India’s foreign liabilities; the share of non-debt liabilities in total external liabilities increased to 52.4 per cent (50.8 per cent at end-September 2020) (<u>Table 3</u>).
- The ratio of India’s international financial assets to international financial liabilities improved to 71.4 per cent in December 2020 from 62.1 per cent a year ago.
## (Yogesh Dayal)

**Press Release: 2020-2021/1332** Chief General Manager

India’s quarterly IIP is disseminated with a quarter lag. The IIP for end-September 2020 was placed in the public domain on <u>December 30, 2020</u>.
