## December 17, 2021

## 91 days, 182 days and 364 days Treasury Bills auction

Reserve Bank of India announces the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills as per the following details:

## Sr. Notified Amount

## Treasury Bill Auction Date Settlement Date

## No (in ₹ crore)

1 91 Days 10,000 December 22, December 23, 2 182 Days 3,000 2021 2021 (Wednesday) (Thursday) 3 364 Days 7,000

## Total 20,000

The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the <u>General</u> <u>Notification F.No.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated March 27, 2018</u> along with the <u>Amendment</u> <u>Notification No.F.4(2)-W&M/2018 dated April 05, 2018</u>, issued by Government of India, as amended from time to time. State Governments, eligible Provident Funds in India, designated Foreign Central Banks and any person or institution specified by the Bank in this regard, can participate on non-competitive basis, the allocation for which will be outside the notified amount. Individuals can also participate on <u>non-competitive</u> basis as retail investors. For retail investors, the allocation will be restricted to a maximum of 5 percent of the notified amount.

The auction will be **Price based** using **multiple price method**. Bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India’s Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system on **Wednesday, December 22, 2021,** during the below given timings:

|Category|Timing|
|---|---|
|Competitive bids|10:30 am - 11:30 am|
|Non-Competitive bids|10:30 am - 11:00 am|

Individual investors can also place bids as per the non-competitive scheme through Retail Direct portal (<u>[https://rbiretaildirect.org.in](https://rbiretaildirect.org.in)</u>).

Results will be announced on the day of the auction.

Payment by successful bidders to be made on **Thursday, December 23, 2021.**

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

Only in the event of system failure, physical bids would be accepted. Such physical bids should be submitted to the Public Debt Office (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-22632527, 022-22701299) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (<u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)</u>) before the auction timing ends. In case of technical difficulties, Core Banking Operations Team should be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022-27595666, 022-27595415, 022-27523516). For other auction related difficulties, IDMD auction team can be contacted (<u>email</u>; Phone no: 022- 22702431, 022-22705125).
