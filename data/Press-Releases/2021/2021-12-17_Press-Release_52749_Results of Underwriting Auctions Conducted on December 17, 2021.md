December 17, 2021 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on December 17, 2021** In the underwriting auctions conducted on December 17, 2021 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|6.10% GS 2031|13,000|6,510|6,490|13,000|0.39|
|GOI FRB 2034|4,000|2,016|1,984|4,000|0.43|
|6.95% GS 2061|7,000|3,507|3,493|7,000|0.38|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on December 17, 2021.
