## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – September 2021</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – September 2021</u> iii. <u>OBICUS Survey on manufacturing sector – Q1:2021-22</u> iv. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey of the Manufacturing Sector for Q2:2021-22</u>
<u>nd</u>

v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round¹ 72</u> vi. <u>Bank Lending Survey for Q2:2021-22</u> vii. <u>Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey for Q2:2021-22</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to
the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
