June 04, 2021 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys**

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – May 2021</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – May 2021</u>
<u>th</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators– Round¹ 70</u>
