## March 22, 2021

## Cancellation of G-Sec auction of Government of India Dated Securities

As per the <u>revised Issuance Calendar dated February 01, 2021</u> for Government of India Dated Securities, the next auction is scheduled to be held on March 26, 2021. On review of position of cash balance, the Government of India has decided to cancel the above scheduled auction.
