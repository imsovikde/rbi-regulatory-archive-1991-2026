## Highlights:

- All-India HPI recorded 2.6 per cent growth (y-o-y) in Q2:2021-22 as compared with
2.0 per cent growth in the previous quarter and 1.1 per cent a year ago; HPI growth varied widely across the cities-it ranged from an expansion of 10.0 per cent (Kochi) to a contraction of (-) 4.9 per cent (Delhi).
- On a sequential (q-o-q) basis, the all-India HPI contracted marginally by (-) 0.5 per in Q2:2021-22; Delhi, Bengaluru and Ahmedabad recorded a sequential decline in HPI, whereas it increased in other cities.
