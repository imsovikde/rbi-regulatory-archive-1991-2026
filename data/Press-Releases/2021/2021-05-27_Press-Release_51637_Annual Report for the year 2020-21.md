## May 27, 2021

## Annual Report for the year 2020-21

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released its <u>Annual Report for 2020-21</u>, a statutory report of its Central Board of Directors. The Report covers the working and functions of the Reserve Bank for the transition period of nine months (July 2020 - March 2021) following the decision to change its accounting year from July-June to April-March.
