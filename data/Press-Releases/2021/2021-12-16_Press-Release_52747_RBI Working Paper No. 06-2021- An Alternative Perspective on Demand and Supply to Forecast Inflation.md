## December 16, 2021

## RBI Working Paper No. 06/2021:

## An Alternative Perspective on Demand and Supply to Forecast Inflation

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website a Working Paper titled “<u>An Alternative Perspective on Demand and Supply to Forecast Inflation</u>” under the Reserve Bank of India Working Paper Series¹. The Paper is authored by Saurabh Sharma and Ipsita Padhi.

The paper develops a new method to construct a measure of demand-supply imbalance in the economy using a Bayesian Dynamic Factor Model. The estimated index of demand-supply mismatch is found to have a causal relationship with headline inflation. It is also found to be a competing predictor of headline inflation, as compared to other conventional measures of slack such as the output gap and level of capacity utilisation.
