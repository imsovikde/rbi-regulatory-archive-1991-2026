December 24, 2021 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on December 24, 2021** In the underwriting auctions conducted on December 24, 2021 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (₹ crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ₹100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|4.56% GS 2023|2,000|1008|992|2,000|0.62|
|5.74% GS 2026|6,000|3003|2997|6,000|1.43|
|6.67% GS 2035|9,000|4515|4485|9,000|1.87|
|6.99% GS 2051|7,000|3507|3493|7,000|3.29|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on December 24, 2021.
