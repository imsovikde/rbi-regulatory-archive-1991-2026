## December 08, 2021

## RBI releases the results of Forward Looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) – November 2021</u> ii. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) – November 2021</u> iii. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round¹ 73</u>
<u>rd</u>

The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.

## Ajit Prasad

**Press Release: 2021-2022/1330** Director (Communications)

The results of the previous survey round were released on <u>October 08, 2021</u> on the Reserve Bank’s website.
