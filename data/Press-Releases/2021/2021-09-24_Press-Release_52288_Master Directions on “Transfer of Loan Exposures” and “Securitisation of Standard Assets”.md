September 24, 2021 **Master Directions on “Transfer of Loan Exposures” and** **“Securitisation of Standard Assets”**

The Reserve Bank of India has today issued the following Master Directions:

(i) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Transfer of Loan Exposures) Directions, 2021</u> (ii) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Securitisation of Standard Assets) Directions, 2021</u> It may be recalled that the draft guidelines on the above were released for public
comments on <u>June 8, 2020</u>. The final Directions issued today have been prepared taking into account *inter alia* the comments received. The Directions come into effect immediately and all extant related circulars, as listed in the Directions, have been repealed.
