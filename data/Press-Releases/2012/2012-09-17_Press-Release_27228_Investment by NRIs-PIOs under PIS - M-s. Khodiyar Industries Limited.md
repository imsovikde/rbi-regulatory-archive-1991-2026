## September 17, 2012

## Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS : M/s. Khodiyar Industries Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>M/s. Khodiyar Industries Limited</u> by Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origins (PIOs) in primary/secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Therefore, no further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges.
