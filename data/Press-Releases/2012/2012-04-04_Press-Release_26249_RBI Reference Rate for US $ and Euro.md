## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `51.0465 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.3936 on April 4, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 3, 2012) were `50.5645 and `67.4854 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
