## Investment by FIIs under PIS :

## M/s. Housing Development Finance Corporation Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s. Housing Development</u> <u>Finance Corporation Limited</u> has passed resolutions at the board of directors' level, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs), through primary market and stock exchanges, under the Portfolio Investment Scheme up to 100 per cent of its paid up capital.
