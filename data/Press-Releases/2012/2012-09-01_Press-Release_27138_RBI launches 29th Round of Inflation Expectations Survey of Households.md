September 1, 2012 **th** **RBI launches 29 Round of** **Inflation Expectations Survey of Households** th The Reserve Bank of India has today launched 29 round of the Survey with July- September 2012 as the current reference period. The Survey seeks qualitative responses from households on price changes (general prices as well as prices of specific product groups) in the next three months as well as in the next one year and quantitative responses on current, three-month ahead and one-year ahead inflation rates.

The Reserve Bank has been conducting Inflation Expectations Survey of Households (IESH) on a quarterly basis since September 2005. It uses the results of this Survey as an important input to the monetary policy formulation.

Inflation expectations are subjective assessments of around 4,000 households across 12 cities proposed to be covered in the Survey round and are based on households’ individual consumption baskets. M/s Beaumont Consultancy Services, Mumbai has been engaged to conduct this round of the Survey on behalf of the Reserve Bank. The agency will approach the households for this purpose during the quarter. Selected households are requested to provide their response to the agency. In case of any query/clarification, households may contact The Director, Division of Household Surveys, Department of Statistics and Information Management, Reserve Bank of India, C-8, 6th Floor, Bandra-Kurla Complex, Bandra (East), Mumbai-400051; Phone: 022-26578398, 022-26578332, Fax: 022-26571327, 022-26571555; <u>e-mail</u>.
