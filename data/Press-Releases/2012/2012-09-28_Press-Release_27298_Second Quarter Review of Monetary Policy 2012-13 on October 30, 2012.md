## Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2012-13 on October 30, 2012

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Second Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2012-13 on Tuesday, October 30, 2012. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on October 30, 2012 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
