## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `55.0860 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `72.7730 on December 21, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 20, 2012) were `54.8420 and `72.4667 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
