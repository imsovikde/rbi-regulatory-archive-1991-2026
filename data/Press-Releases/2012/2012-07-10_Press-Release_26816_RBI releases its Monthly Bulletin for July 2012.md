## July 10, 2012

## RBI releases its Monthly Bulletin for July 2012

The Reserve Bank today released the July 2012 issue of its monthly RBI Bulletin. The Bulletin contains regular features, such as, speeches of top management, current statistics, press releases, regulatory and other measures and foreign exchange developments. The July issue of the Bulletin also has the Financial Stability Report (FSR) as a supplement.
