## RBI Releases Draft Supplementary Guidance on

## Treatment of Illiquid Positions

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website, Draft Supplementary Guidance on “<u>Treatment of Illiquid Positions</u>” for comments and feedback.

Comments/feedback on the draft supplementary guidance may be sent latest by October 19, 2012 by mail to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Operations and Development, Central Office, 12th floor, Central Office Building, Shahid Bhagat Singh Marg, Mumbai-400001 or through <u>e-mail</u>.
