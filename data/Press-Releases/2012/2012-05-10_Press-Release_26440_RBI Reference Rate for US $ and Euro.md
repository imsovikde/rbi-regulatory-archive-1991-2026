## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `53.3375 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `69.0680 on May 10, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 9, 2012) were `53.4600 and `69.4005 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
