## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `50.9055 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.4035 on March 22, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 21, 2012) were `50.5935 and `67.1366 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
