## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `53.6410 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `69.2880 on May 11, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 10, 2012) were `53.3375 and `69.0680 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
