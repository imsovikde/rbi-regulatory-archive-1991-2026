## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS : M/s. Indiabulls Infrastructure and Power Limited

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) can now purchase under the Portfolio Investment Scheme equity shares and convertible debentures of <u>M/s. Indiabulls Infrastructure and Power Limited</u> as under:

- FIIs through primary market and stock exchanges up to 100 per cent of the paid up capital of the company.
- NRIs/PIOs through primary market and stock exchanges up to 24 per cent of the paid up capital of the company. M/s. Indiabulls Infrastructure and Power Limited has passed resolutions at the
board of directors' level and a special resolution by the shareholders, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by these investors.
