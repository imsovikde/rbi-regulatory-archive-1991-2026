## Underwriting Auctions for Government Securities

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of (i) "8.19 per cent Government Stock 2020” for a notified amount of ` 4,000 crore (nominal), (ii) "9.15 per cent Government Stock 2024” for a notified amount of `.7,000 crore (nominal), (iii) "8.28 per cent Government Stock 2027" for a notified amount of `.2,000 crore (nominal) and (iv) "8.83 per cent Government Stock 2041" for a notified amount of `.2,000 crore (nominal) through auctions to be held on June 22, 2012.

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under:

|---|---|---|---|---|
||Amount|per Primary Dealer|commitment per PD under ACU auction||
|8.19 per cent GS 2020|4000|96||96|
|9.15 per cent GS 2024|7000|167||167|
|8.28 per cent GS 2027|2000|48||48|
|8.83 per cent GS 2041|2000|48||48|

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method on June 21, 2012. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) between

10.30 a.m. and 12.00 p.m. on the date of auction. The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
