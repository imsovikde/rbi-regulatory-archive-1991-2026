## April 9, 2012

## RBI releases its monthly Bulletin for April 2012

The Reserve Bank has released the <u>April 2012 issue of its monthly RBI</u> <u>Bulletin</u>. The Bulletin contains regular features, such as, Speeches of top management, Current Statistics, press releases, regulatory and other measures and foreign exchange developments.
