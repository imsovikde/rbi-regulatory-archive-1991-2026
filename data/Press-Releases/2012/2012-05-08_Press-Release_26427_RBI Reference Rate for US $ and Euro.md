## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `52.8550 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `68.8078 on May 8, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 7, 2012) were `53.3420 and `69.3655 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
