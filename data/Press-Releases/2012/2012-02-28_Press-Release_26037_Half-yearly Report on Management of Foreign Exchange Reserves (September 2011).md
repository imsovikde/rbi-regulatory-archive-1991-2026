## Half-yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves (September 2011)

The Reserve Bank of India has released the 17th <u>half-yearly report on</u> <u>management of foreign exchange reserves with reference to September 2011</u>.

It may be recalled that in February 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the public domain with a lag of about three months for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure.

The <u>first such report for the half year ended September 30, 2003</u> was released on February 3, 2004.
