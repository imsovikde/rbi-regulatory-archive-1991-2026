## Monetary Policy Statement 2012-13 on April 17, 2012

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Monetary Policy Statement 2012-13 on Tuesday, April 17, 2012. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00

a.m. on April 17, 2012 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
