## October 16, 2012

## Prof. Damodar Acharya nominated on RBI Central Board

The Central Government has nominated Prof. Damodar Acharya as a director on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from October 12, 2012. The nomination has been made in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
