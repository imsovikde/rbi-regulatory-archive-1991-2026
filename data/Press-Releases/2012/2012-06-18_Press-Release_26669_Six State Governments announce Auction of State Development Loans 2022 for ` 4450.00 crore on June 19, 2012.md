## Six State Governments announce Auction of State Development

**Loans 2022 for** ` **4450.00 crore on June 19, 2012**

In partial revision to our earlier <u>Press Release: 2011-2012/2005 dated June</u> <u>15, 2012</u>, the Government of West Bengal has also announced the sale of its 10- year State Development Loan (SDL) for an aggregate amount of ` 500.00 crore. Accordingly, now the following Six State Governments will be selling their 10-year State Development Loans (SDLs) for an aggregate amount of ` 4450.00 crore (face value) through yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

* Government of Tamil Nadu will also have an option to retain additional subscription up to a maximum of Rs.150 crore (nominal) over and above the notified amount The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai
on June 19, 2012 (Tuesday). The Government Stock upto 10 per cent of the notified amount of the sale of each of the stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions subject to a maximum limit of one per cent of its notified amount for a single bid per stock as per the Scheme for Non-competitive Bidding Facility (available in RBI website).

Both competitive and non-competitive bids for the auction should be submitted in electronic format on the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) on June 19, 2012 (Tuesday). The non-competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and

11.30 A.M. and the competitive bids should be submitted between 10.30 A.M. and
12.00 P.M.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

The yield percent per annum expected by the bidder should be expressed upto two decimal points. An investor can submit more than one competitive bid at different prices in electronic format on the NDS. However, the aggregate amount of bids submitted by a bidder should not exceed the notified amount for each State.

The Reserve Bank of India will determine the maximum yield at which bids will be accepted. Securities will be issued for a minimum nominal amount of ` 10,000.00 and multiples of ` 10,000.00 thereafter.

The results of the auction will be announced on June 19, 2012 (Tuesday) and payment by successful bidders will be made during banking hours on June 20, 2012 (Wednesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of RBI.

The State Government Stocks will bear interest at the rates determined by RBI at the auctions. Interest will be paid half yearly on December 20 and June 20 of each year till maturity. The Stocks will be governed by the provisions of the Government Securities Act, 2006 and Government Securities Regulations, 2007.

The investment in State Government Stocks will be reckoned as an eligible investment in Government Securities by banks for the purpose of Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
