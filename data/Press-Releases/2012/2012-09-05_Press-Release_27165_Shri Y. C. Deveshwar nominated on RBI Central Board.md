## September 5, 2012

## Shri Y. C. Deveshwar nominated on RBI Central Board

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Central Government has nominated Shri Y. C. Deveshwar, as a director on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from September 3, 2012.
