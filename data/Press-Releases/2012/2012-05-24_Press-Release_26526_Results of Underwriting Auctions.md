## Results of Underwriting Auctions

In the auctions conducted on May 24, 2012 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (` crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ` 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

|8.28% GS 2027|2000|1008|992|2000|1.84|
|8.33% GS 2036|3000|1512|1488|3000|2.00|
