## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `50.3130 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `65.8262 on March 16, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 15, 2012) were `50.2225 and `65.5355 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
