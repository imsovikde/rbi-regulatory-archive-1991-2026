## August 16, 2012

**th** **18 Half Yearly Report on Management of Foreign Exchange Reserves -** **October 2011-March 2012**

th The Reserve Bank of India has released the 18 half-yearly report on <u>management of foreign exchange reserves with reference to March 2012</u>.

It may be recalled that in February 2004, the Reserve Bank had commenced a process of compiling half yearly reports and placing them in the public domain for bringing about more transparency and also for enhancing the level of disclosure.
