## September 28, 2012

**WMA Limit for the Government of India fixed for the Second Half Year of Financial Year 2012-13**

The Reserve Bank of India has decided in consultation with the Government of India to fix the limit for Ways and Means Advances (WMA) to the Central Governmentat `20,000 crore for October 01, 2012 to March 31, 2013.

The other terms and conditions of the provision of WMA will remain unchanged.
