## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `51.4175 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.6685 on April 13, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 12, 2012) were `51.4420 and `67.6087 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
