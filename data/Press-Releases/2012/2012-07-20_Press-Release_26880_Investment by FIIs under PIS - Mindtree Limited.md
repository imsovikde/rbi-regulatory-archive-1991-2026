## July 20, 2012

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : Mindtree Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>Mindtree Limited</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in the primary/secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Therefore, further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
