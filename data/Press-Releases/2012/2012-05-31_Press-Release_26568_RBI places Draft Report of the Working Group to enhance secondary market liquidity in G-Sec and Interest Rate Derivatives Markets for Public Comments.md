## 31 May 2012

## RBI places Draft Report of the Working Group to enhance secondary market

## liquidity in G-Sec and Interest Rate Derivatives Markets for Public Comments

The Reserve Bank of India has, today, placed on its website, the ‘<u>Draft Report of</u> <u>the Working Group on Enhancing Liquidity in the Government Securities and Interest</u> <u>Rate Derivatives Markets</u>’ for public comments. Comments on the draft report may be forwarded, latest by June 22, 2012, to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt rd Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office Building, 23 Floor,

S.B.S. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400001 or <u>emailed</u>. It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank had announced in the Monetary Policy Statement 2012-13 that the Draft Report of the Working Group (Chairman: Shri R Gandhi, ED) would be placed on the Reserve Bank’s website by end-May 2012.
