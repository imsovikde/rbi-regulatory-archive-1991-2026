## RBI signs MoU with the Central Bank of the Russian Federation (CBRF)

<u>Photograph</u> The Reserve Bank of India and the Central Bank of the Russian Federation (CBRF/The Bank of Russia), on August 3, 2012 concluded a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to promote greater co-operation and sharing of supervisory information between the two supervisors. The MoU was signed at CBRF headquarters, Moscow by Shri G. Jaganmohan Rao, CGM-in-Charge, DBS, RBI and Shri Mikhail I. Sukhov, Deputy Chairman, CBRF. DR. K.C. Chakrabarty, DG, th Reserve Bank of India was also present. This was the 10 MoU signed by the Reserve Bank with Central Bank of the Russian Federation (CBRF/The Bank of Russia).
