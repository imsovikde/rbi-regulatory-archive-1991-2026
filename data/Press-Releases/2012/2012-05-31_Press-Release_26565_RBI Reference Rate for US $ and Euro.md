## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `56.4225 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `69.9513 on May 31, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 30, 2012) were `56.0060 and `69.8495 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
