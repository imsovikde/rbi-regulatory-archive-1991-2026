## March 28, 2012

## Buyback of State Development Loans (SDLs) by the Government of Mizoram

The State Government of Mizoram proposes to buyback SDLs, as listed below, for an aggregate amount of ` 35 crore during the year 2011-12. The purchases will be carried out by the Reserve Bank of India, Internal Debt Management Department on behalf of the State Government.

|Security|Maturity Date|Amount to be Purchased|
|---|---|---|
|||(` Crore)|
|9.44% Mizoram SDL 2018|August 27, 2018|35|

Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India will purchase the SDLs from the secondary market in Government securities from March 28, 2012. The deals would settle as part of the overall secondary market trades through the Clearing Corporation of India Ltd. (CCIL).

The specific details of the securities and amounts bought back for the year 2011-12, will be announced to the market, by way of a press release, at the end of the operations.
