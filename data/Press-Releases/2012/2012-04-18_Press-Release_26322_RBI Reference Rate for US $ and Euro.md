## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `51.5035 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.5415 on April 18, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 17, 2012) were `51.6265 and `67.6546 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
