## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `50.5935 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.1366 on March 21, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (March 20, 2012) were `50.2545 and `66.5226 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
