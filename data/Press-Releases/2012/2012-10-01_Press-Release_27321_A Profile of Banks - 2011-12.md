## October 1, 2012

## A Profile of Banks : 2011-12

The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website the publication ‘<u>A</u> <u>Profile of Banks: 2011-12</u>’. The publication, being eighth volume in the series, provides bank-wise and bank group-wise information on important performance indicators of all scheduled commercial banks (SCBs), excluding regional rural banks, for the period 2007-08 to 2011-12. The publication covers 18 important indicators, including return on assets (RoA), capital to risk weighted assets ratio (CRAR), business per employee, and profit per employee.

## Highlights

- Employee productivity in terms of both business per employee and profit per employee in all SCBs at aggregate level increased continuously in all the financial years during the period 2007-08 to 2011-12.
- Profitability in terms of RoA of all SCBs at the aggregate level declined in 2011- 12 as compared to the previous financial year whereas the cost of funds and net NPA ratio increased.
- CRAR of all SCBs increased at the end of 2011-12.
- Bank group wise analysis revealed that business per employee across all bank groups increased during 2011-12 vis-à-vis that in 2010-11. Profit per employee also increased across all bank groups except in respect of ‘nationalised banks’ group during 2011-12. For ‘nationalised banks’ group, profit per employee in 2011-12 remained the same as that in 2010-11.
- RoA increased in all bank groups in 2011-12, except for ‘nationalised banks’.
- Cost of funds increased in all the bank groups in 2011-12.
- CRAR of all bank groups at aggregate level declined at the end of 2011-12 except in ‘SBI and its associate bank’ group.
- Net NPA ratios in all bank groups increased in 2011-12 except in ‘new private sector banks’ and ‘foreign banks’ groups.
