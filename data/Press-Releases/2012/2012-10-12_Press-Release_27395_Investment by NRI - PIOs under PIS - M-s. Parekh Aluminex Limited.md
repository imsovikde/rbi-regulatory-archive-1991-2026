## October 12, 2012

## Investment by NRI / PIOs under PIS : M/s. Parekh Aluminex Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>M/s. Parekh Aluminex Limited</u> by Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in primary / secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges on behalf of NRIs / PIOs.
