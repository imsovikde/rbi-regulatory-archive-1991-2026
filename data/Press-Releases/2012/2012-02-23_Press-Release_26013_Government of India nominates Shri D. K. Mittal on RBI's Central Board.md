## Government of India nominates Shri D. K. Mittal on RBI's Central Board

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (d) of sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Central Government has nominated Shri D. K. Mittal, Secretary, Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, as a director on the Central Board of Directors of Reserve Bank of India with effect from February 7, 2012 and until further orders.

It may be recalled that clause (d) of sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 was amended recently to allow the Central Government to nominate two Government Officials to the Central Board of Reserve Bank instead of one.
