## Draft National Strategy for Financial Education: Date for receiving Comments extended

The Reserve Bank of India, on July 16, 2012, released on its website the draft National Strategy for Financial Education for comments and feedback (<u>[http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=26847](http://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=26847)</u>). To enable a large number of comments and feedback on the paper, the date for receiving comments has been extended till September 07, 2012. Comments may be <u>emailed</u>.
