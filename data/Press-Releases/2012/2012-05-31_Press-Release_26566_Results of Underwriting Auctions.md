## Results of Underwriting Auctions

In the auctions conducted on May 31, 2012 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below: (` crore)

|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten|ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per ` 100)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|8.19% GS 2020|4000|2016|1984|4000|0.78|
|9.15% GS 2024|7000|3507|3493|7000|0.72|
|8.28% GS 2032|2000|1008|992|2000|1.24|
|8.83% GS 2041|2000|1008|992|2000|0.90|
