## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `55.2025 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `70.4255 on November 21, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 20, 2012) were `54.9100 and `70.2380 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
