## Revision in the Notified Amount of Government of India Treasury Bills for the

## Auction Scheduled on April 4, 2012

In view of the improved cash position of Government of India, it has been decided, in consultation with Government of India, to reduce the notified amount of 91 days Treasury Bills from ` 9,000 crore to ` 6,000 crore for the auction scheduled on April 4, 2012. The notified amount of 364 days Government of India Treasury Bills, however, will remain unchanged at ` 5,000 crore.
