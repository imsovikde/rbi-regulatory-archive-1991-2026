## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `52.3750 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `67.9895 on October 9, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 8, 2012) were `52.2145 and `67.7515 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:

|Date||Currency|
