## Dr. K. C. Chakrabarty re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor

Government of India has re-appointed Dr. K. C. Chakrabarty as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India, for a period up to June 30, 2014 i.e. till he attains the age of 62 years or until further orders, whichever is earlier. As per the earlier Government's notification, his three months term would have come to an end on September 14, 2012.
