## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `54.8755 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `69.4885 on May 18, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 17, 2012) were `54.3875 and `69.3003 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
