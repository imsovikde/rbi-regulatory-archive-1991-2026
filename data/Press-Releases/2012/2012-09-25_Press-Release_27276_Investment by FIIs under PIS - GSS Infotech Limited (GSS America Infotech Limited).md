## Investment by FIIs under PIS :

## GSS Infotech Limited (GSS America Infotech Limited)

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares in <u>GSS Infotech Limited (GSS America Infotech Limited)</u> by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) in primary / secondary markets under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) have reached the trigger limit. Therefore, further purchases of equity shares of this company would be allowed only after obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank of India.
