## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is `52.7910 and the Reference Rate for Euro is `69.5325 on April 24, 2012. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 23, 2012) were `52.2295 and `68.8375 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for the US dollar and middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
