## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of March 2012

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India's international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of February 2012 were released on April 13, 2012.

The exports and imports of services during the month of March 2012 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|January 2012|11,027|7,332|
|February 2012|11,226|6,750|
|March 2012|12,891|9,080|

**Note**: Data are provisional.

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis. Revised data on services receipts and payments on quarterly basis (April-June 2011, July-September 2011 and October-December 2011) are available in the press release on 'Developments in India's Balance of Payments during October-December 2011' placed on RBI website on March 30, 2012.
