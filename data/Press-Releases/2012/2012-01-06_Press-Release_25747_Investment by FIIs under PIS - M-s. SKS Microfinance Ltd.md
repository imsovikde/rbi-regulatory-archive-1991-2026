## January 6, 2012

## Investment by FIIs under PIS : M/s. SKS Microfinance Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India today notified that <u>M/s. SKS Microfinance Ltd</u>. has passed resolutions at the board of directors' level and a special resolution by the shareholders agreeing, to enhance the limit for purchase of its equity shares and convertible debentures by Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary market and stock exchanges upto 74 per cent of its paid up capital, under the Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS).
