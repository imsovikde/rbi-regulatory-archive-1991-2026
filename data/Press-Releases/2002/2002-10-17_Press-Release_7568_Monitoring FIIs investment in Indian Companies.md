## Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

## October 17, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on October 17, 2002 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of HDFC Bank Ltd., up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary/secondary markets in India, as the HDFC Bank Ltd., has passed a resolution to this effect at its Board of Directors' meeting and a special resolution in its General Body Meeting.

**P.V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
