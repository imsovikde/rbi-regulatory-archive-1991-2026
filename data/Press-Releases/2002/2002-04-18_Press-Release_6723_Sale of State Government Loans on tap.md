## Sale of State Government Loans on tap

## April 18, 2002

All the State Governments (except Kerala) have offered to sell on tap 7.80 per cent State Development Loan, 2012 having a maturity of ten years. The sale will be held at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai (for all 27 States), on April 23, 2002. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the business hours on or after April 23, 2002 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Office of the concerned offices of the Reserve Bank of India will be received at these offices during business hours while the sale is open. The Reserve Bank expects to mobilise about Rs.4100 crore in the `tap' issue. This is the first tranche of the Market Borrowing Programme for the State Governments in the current fiscal.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
