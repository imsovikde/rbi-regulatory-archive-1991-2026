## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## March 5, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on March 5, 2002 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Soffia Software Ltd., up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board of Directors' Meeting and a special resolution in its General Body Meeting to this effect.
