# 15 Year Government Stock : Underwriting Commission for PDs at 5 paise

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## 15 Year Government Stock :

## Underwriting Commission for PDs at 5 paise

## April 12, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 15 year Government Stock to be held on April 15, 2002, was Re.0.05 (five paise).
