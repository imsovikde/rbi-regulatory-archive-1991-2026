## RBI rejects licence application of Manamadurai Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd.

## October 1, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has rejected the licence application of the Manamadurai Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd., Manamadurai, Tamil Nadu to carry on banking business. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of `banking' as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from public and allow withdrawal by cheques, draft, order or otherwise.
