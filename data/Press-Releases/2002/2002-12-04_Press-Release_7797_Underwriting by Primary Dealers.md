## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## December 4, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the price based auction of "8.35% Government Stock, 2022" to be held on December 5, 2002 at Re.0.0075 (0.75 paisa).
