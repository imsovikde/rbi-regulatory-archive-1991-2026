## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## October 29, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on October 29, 2002 has notified that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Hindustan Lever Ltd., up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meeting to this effect.
