## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs in shares of Indian companies

## February 18, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on February 18, 2002 has notified that no further purchases of shares of Bharti Tele Ventures Ltd., should be made on behalf of foreign institutional investors (FIIs)/ non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) without obtaining prior clearance of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that the net purchases of equity shares of the company in the secondary markets have reached the limit of 47 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
