## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## July 23, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.7000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.6100 on July 23, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 22, 2002) were Rs.48.7000 and Rs.49.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|July 22, 2002|76.7731|41.93|
|July 23, 2002|76.5004|41.5700|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
