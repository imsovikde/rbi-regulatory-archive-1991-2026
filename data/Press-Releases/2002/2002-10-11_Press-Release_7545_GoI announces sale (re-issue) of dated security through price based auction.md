## GoI announces sale (re-issue) of dated security through price based auction

The Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.46 percent Government Stock 2017” for a notified amount of Rs.4,000 crore (nominal) through a <u>price based auction</u> <u>using multiple price auction method</u>.

Up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale the Stock will be allotted to eligible individuals and Institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on Wednesday, October 16, 2002. Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai should be submitted to that office on Wednesday, October 16, 2002. The competitive bids by the Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS, while all the non- competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form. All bids should be submitted <u>by 12.30 P.M</u>. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank will determine the minimum price at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on October 16, 2002 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on October 17, 2002 (Thursday). The stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market’ up to 2.30 p.m. on October 14, 2002 (Monday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.
