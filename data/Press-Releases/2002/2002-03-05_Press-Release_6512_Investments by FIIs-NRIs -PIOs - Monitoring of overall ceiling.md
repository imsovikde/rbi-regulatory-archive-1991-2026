## Investments by FIIs/NRIs /PIOs - Monitoring of overall ceiling

## March 5, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on March 5, 2002 has notified that no further purchases of shares of State Bank of India should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of foreign institutional investors (FIIs)/ non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) as the holdings by FIIs / NRIs and PIOs have reached the overall ceiling of 20 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
