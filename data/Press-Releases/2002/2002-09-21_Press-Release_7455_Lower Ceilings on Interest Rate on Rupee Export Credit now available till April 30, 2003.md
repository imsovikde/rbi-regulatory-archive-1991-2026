## Lower Ceilings on Interest Rate on Rupee Export Credit now available till April 30, 2003

## September 21, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has today extended the dispensation of lower ceiling on interest rates charged by scheduled commercial banks for export credit. The dispensation will now be valid till April 30, 2003.

The ceiling on interest rates charged by scheduled commercial banks on rupee export credit was reduced by one percentage point across the board with effect from September 26, 2001. The reduction was applicable to both pre-shipment and post- shipment credits. It had, at that time, stated that the concession would be available up to March 31, 2002. The concession was extended till September 30, 2002 in March this year.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
