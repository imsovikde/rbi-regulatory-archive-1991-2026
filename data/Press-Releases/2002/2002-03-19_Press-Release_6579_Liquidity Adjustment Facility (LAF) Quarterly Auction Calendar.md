## Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) Quarterly Auction Calendar

## March 19, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced further calendars for the daily repo and reverse repo and fortnightly repo auctions under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) for the April- June 2002 quarter. There is no other change in the existing terms and conditions under which the LAF auctions are conducted.
