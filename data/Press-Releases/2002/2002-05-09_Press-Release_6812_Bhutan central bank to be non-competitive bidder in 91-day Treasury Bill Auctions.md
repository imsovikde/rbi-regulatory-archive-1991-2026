## Bhutan central bank to be non-competitive bidder in 91-day Treasury Bill Auctions

## May 9, 2002

It has been decided to permit the Royal Monetary Authority of Bhutan to participate in the auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills on a non-competitive basis. Presently, state governments, eligible provident funds and Nepal Rashtra Bank are permitted to bid on non- competitive basis in the auctions of 91-day Treasury Bills conducted by the Reserve Bank of India.
