# On tap sale of 8.30 per cent State Development Loans, 2012

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## On tap sale of 8.30 per cent State Development Loans, 2012

The sale 'on tap' 8.30 per cent State Loans, 2012 was closed in respect of Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal pradesh, Chhatisgarh, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Manipur, Mizoram, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu and Uttaranchal, on receipt of the respective target amounts.

The sale in respect of the loans offered by States of Assam, Bihar, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Orissa, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal will continue on January 29, 2002. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after the close of banking hours on or after January 29, 2002 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai (for all States).

Applications alongwith the payment will be received at those offices during office hours while the sale is open. Applicants, submitting the bids on January 29, 2002 would also have to pay interest for one day at 8.30 per cent per annum for the securities they apply for.

It may be recalled that the Governments of 26 States offered to sell `on tap' 8.30 per cent State Loans, 2012. The loans opened for subscription on January 28, 2002 at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India and at Mumbai for 26 States.
