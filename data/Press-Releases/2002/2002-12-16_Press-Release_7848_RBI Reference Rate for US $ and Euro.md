## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 16, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.1600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.2200 on December 16, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 13, 2002) were Rs.48.1800 and Rs.49.0400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
