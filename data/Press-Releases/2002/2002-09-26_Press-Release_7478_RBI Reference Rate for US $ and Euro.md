## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 26, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.3400 on September 26, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 25, 2002) were Rs.48.4100 and Rs.47.5100 respectively.
