## Tap sale of 7.80 per cent State Development Loans 2012

## April 23, 2002

The sale `on tap' of 7.80 per cent State Loans, 2012 was closed in respect of ten States i.e., Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Manipur, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and Uttaranchal on April 23, 2002 on receipt of the respective target amounts.

The sale in respect of remaining 16 States (Kerala & Sikkim not included) will continue on April 24, 2002. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after April 24, 2002 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai ( for all States).

Applications along with the payment will be received at those offices during business hours while sale is open. Applicants, submitting the bids on April 24, 2002 would also have to pay interest for one day at 7.80 per cent per annum for the securities they apply for.
