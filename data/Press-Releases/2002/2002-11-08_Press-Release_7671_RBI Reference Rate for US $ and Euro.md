## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 8, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.6600 on November 8, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 7, 2002) were Rs.48.3200 and Rs.48.5400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
