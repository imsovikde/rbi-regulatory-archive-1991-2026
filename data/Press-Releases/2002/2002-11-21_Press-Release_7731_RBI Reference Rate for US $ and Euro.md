## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 21, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.2400 on November 21, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 20, 2002) were Rs.48.2100 and Rs.48.3300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
