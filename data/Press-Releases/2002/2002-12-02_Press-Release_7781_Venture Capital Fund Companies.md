## Venture Capital Fund Companies

## December 2, 2002

In order to obviate the need for dual regulation over venture capital fund companies, the Reserve Bank of India has, in consultation with the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), exempted venture capital fund companies holding a certificate of registration obtained from SEBI under Section 12 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 and are not holding or accepting public deposits, from the requirement of registration with the Reserve Bank of India, maintenance of liquid assets, creation of special reserve fund under Sections 45IA, 45-IB and 45IC, respectively of the Reserve Bank of India Act, as also from the Directions of Reserve Bank on acceptance of public deposits and prudential norms for Non-Banking Financial Companies.

The relative notification and circular are available on RBI website at www.rbi.org.in
