## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 30, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.5400 on October 30, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 29, 2002) were Rs.48.4500 and Rs.47.6800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|October 29, 2002|75.5408|39.23|
|October 30, 2002|75.3142|39.2900|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
