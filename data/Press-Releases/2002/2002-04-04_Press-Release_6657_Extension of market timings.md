## Extension of market timings

## April 4, 2002

In order to facilitate the market participants for trading in Government Securities after the announcement of result of Government securities auctions being held today, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to extend the market timings for both the NDS members and non-NDS members upto 7.30 p.m. today, i.e. April 4, 2002.
