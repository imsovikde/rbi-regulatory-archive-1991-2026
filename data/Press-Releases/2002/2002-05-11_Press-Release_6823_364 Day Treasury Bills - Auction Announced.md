## 364 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## May 11, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.1000 crore. The allocation to non- competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F.2(12)-W&M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, May 15, 2002 (Wednesday, May 1, 2002 being a holiday), before 1430 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, May 17, 2002.

**P.V.Sadanandan Manager**
