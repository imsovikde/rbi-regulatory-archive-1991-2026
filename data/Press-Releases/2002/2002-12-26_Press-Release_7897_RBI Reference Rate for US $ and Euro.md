## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 26, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.5700 on December 26, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 24, 2002) were Rs.48.0100 and Rs.49.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
