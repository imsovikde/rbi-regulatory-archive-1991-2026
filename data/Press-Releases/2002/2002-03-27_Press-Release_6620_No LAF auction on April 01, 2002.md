## No LAF auction on April 01, 2002

## March 27, 2002

With reference to Press Release No-2001-2002/1046, dated March 19, 2002, giving a calendar for Repo/Reverse Repo auctions under LAF for the quarter April 01 to June 30, 2002, RBI clarifies that in view of modifications in the Repo period for the Repo/Reverse Repo auction on March 28, 2002 (5 days instead of 4 and maturing on April 02, 2002 instead of April 01, 2002 as announced vide Press Release dated March 26, 2002), there will <u>not</u> be any Repo/Reverse Repo auction under LAF on <u>April 01, 2002</u>.

## P.V.Sadanandan Manager
