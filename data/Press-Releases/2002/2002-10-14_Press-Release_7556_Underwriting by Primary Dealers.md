## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## October 14, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of "7.46% Government Stock, 2017" to be auctioned on October 16, 2002 at Re.0.009 (0.90 paisa).
