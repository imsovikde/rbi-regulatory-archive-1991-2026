## RBI offices to remain closed for public on June 29, 2002

## June 26, 2002

The offices of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will remain closed on Saturday, June 29, 2002 for public transaction on account of annual closing of its accounts.
