## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 13, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.1800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.0400 on December 13, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 12, 2002) were Rs.48.2200 and Rs.48.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
