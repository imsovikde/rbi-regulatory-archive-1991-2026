## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## May 27, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.98 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.45.09 on May 27, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (May 24, 2002) were Rs.49.0100 and Rs.45.1100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|May 24, 2002|71.2556|39.22|
|May 27, 2002|71.3467|39.2600|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
