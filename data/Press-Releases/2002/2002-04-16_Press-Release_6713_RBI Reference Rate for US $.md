## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## April 16, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.92 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.43.08 on April 16, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 15, 2002) were Rs. 48.92 and Rs. 43.10 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are :

|Date|Currency|
|---|---|
||1 GBP 100 YEN|
|April 15, 2002|70.2834 37.1500|
|April 16, 2002|70.3078 37.1800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
