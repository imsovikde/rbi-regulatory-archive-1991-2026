## Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

## October 22, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on October 22, 2002 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of the following companies, up to 100 per cent of their paid up capital through primary/secondary markets in India, as these companies have passed resolution to this effect at their Board of Directors' meeting and special resolution in their General Body Meeting.

1. Pentamedia Graphics Ltd.
2. Pentasoft Technologies Ltd.
