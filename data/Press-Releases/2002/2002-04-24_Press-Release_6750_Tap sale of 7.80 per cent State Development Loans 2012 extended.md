## Tap sale of 7.80 per cent State Development Loans 2012 extended

## April 24, 2002

The sale `on tap' of 7.80 per cent State Development Loans, 2012 was closed in respect of Andhra Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Maharashtra, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura and West Bengal on receipt of the respective target amounts.

The sale in respect of remaining eight States, viz., Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, Jammu & Kashmir, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh will continue on April 26, 2002 (April 25, 2002 being Holiday). The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after April 26, 2002 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai ( for all States). Applications along with the payment will be received at those offices during business hours while sale is open. Applicants, submitting the bids on April 26, 2002 would also have to pay interest for three days at 7.80 per cent per annum for the securities they apply for.

It may be recalled that the `on tap' sale of the 7.80 per cent State Development Loans, 2012 which opened on April 23, 2002 for 26 States was closed on the same day in respect of 10 States, viz., Arunachal Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Manipur, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and Uttaranchal.
