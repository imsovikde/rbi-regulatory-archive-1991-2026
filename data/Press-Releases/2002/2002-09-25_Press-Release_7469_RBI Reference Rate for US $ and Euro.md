## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 25, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.4100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.5100 on September 25, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 24, 2002) were Rs.48.4100 and Rs.47.3200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
