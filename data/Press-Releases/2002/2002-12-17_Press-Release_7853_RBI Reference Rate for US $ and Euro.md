## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 17, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.1300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.4900 on December 17, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 16, 2002) were Rs.48.1600 and Rs.49.2200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
