# Andhra Pradesh State Government to raise funds from market on December 10, 2002

## Andhra Pradesh State Government to raise funds from market on December 10,

## December 5, 2002

Andhra Pradesh State Government have announced the sale of 10-year stock to the public by auction for an aggregate amount of Rs.294.65 crore. The auction will be conducted by Mumbai (Fort) office of the Reserve Bank of India on Tuesday, December 10, 2002. Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on December 10, 2002 before 14.30 hrs. Results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Wednesday, December 11, 2002.

## P. V. Sadanandan
