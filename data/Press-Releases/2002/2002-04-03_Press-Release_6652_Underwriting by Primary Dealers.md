## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## April 3, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Uniform Price Based Auction of 7-Year and 10-Year Government Stock to be held on 04th April 2002, was Re 0.0050 (half paise) for both the issues.
