## RBI Reference Rate for US $

## April 10, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.90 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.43.10 on April 10, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (April 9, 2002) were Rs. 48.94 and Rs. 42.73 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are :

|Date|Currency|
|---|---|
||1 GBP 100 YEN|
|April 9, 2002|70.0625 37.1200|
|April 10, 2002|70.2889 37.4900|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
