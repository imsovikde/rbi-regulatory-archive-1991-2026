## Sale of State Government Loans on tap

## January 23, 2002

It may be recalled that 26 State Governments have offered to sell on tap 8.30 per cent State Development Loan, 2012 having a maturity of ten years on January 28, 2002 as per Bank's Press Release dated January 22, 2002. RBI expects to raise about Rs.3700 crore in this `tap issue' tranche.
