## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 30, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.5100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.7400 on August 30, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 29, 2002) were Rs.48.5000 and Rs.47.6100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 29, 2002|74.5130|41.07|
|August 30, 2002|75.1565|41.1000|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
