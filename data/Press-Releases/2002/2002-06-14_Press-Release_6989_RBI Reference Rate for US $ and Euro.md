## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 14, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.49.03 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.46.33 on June 14, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 13, 2002) were Rs.48.9900 and Rs.46.2400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 13, 2002|71.9884|38.95|
|June 14, 2002|72.2555|39.3000|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
