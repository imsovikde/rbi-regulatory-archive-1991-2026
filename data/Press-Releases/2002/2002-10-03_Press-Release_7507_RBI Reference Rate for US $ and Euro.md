## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 3, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.7500 on October 3, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 1, 2002) were Rs.48.3600 and Rs.47.7600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
