## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## August 26, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auctions of 15 Year Government Stock and 30 Year Government Stock to be held on August 27, 2002 at Re.0.02 (two paise) for both the issues.
