## RBI offices to remain open for public on December 31, 2002

## December 26, 2002

The offices of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will remain open on Tuesday, December 31, 2002 and undertake all public transactions.

**P. V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
