## Tap Issue of State Development Loans, 2012 closed on over-subscription

## December 23, 2002

The sale `on tap' of 6.80 per cent ten year State Development Loans 2012 in respect of 22 State Governments received overwhelming response with all the States being oversubscribed. The tap sale of 6.80 per cent of SDL 2012 was, therefore, closed at 11.30 a.m. The issue opened for subscription today for a target amount of Rs.3340 crore. As the loans of all the participating States have been oversubscribed, partial allotments are being made and excess subscriptions will be refunded to the applicants.

**P. V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
