## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 12, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.4700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.2600 on September 12, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 11, 2002) were Rs.48.4500 and Rs.47.2700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

September 11, 2002 75.3567 40.46

September 12, 2002 75.2351 40.2700
