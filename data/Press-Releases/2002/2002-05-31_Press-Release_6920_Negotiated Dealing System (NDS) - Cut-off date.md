## Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) - Cut-off date

## May 31, 2002

Currently all major participants are live NDS members submitting electronic SGL transfer form for settlement of their transactions in government securities. All SGL account holders were given time only up to May 31, 2002 to become NDS members. In the light of the representations received by the Reserve Bank of India from some market participants indicating difficulties in adhering to the time-schedule such as delays in obtaining required hardware and system software, to put the NDS system in place by May 31, 2002, it has been decided to continue to allow each of such participants to submit physical SGL transfer form till the date specifically agreed in respect of each of them as the last date for such submission.
