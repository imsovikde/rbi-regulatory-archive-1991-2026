## RBI cancels licence of the Sevalal Urban Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd.

## December 13, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has cancelled vide Order dated December 4, 2002 the licence granted to the Sevalal Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd., Mandrup, Dist. Solapur (Maharashtra) to carry on banking business. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of `banking' as defined in the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co- operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
