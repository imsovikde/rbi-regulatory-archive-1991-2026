## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 20, 2002 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Mascon Global Limited up to 100 per cent of its paid up capital through primary/secondary markets in India, as the company has passed a resolution to this effect at its Board of Directors' meeting and a special resolution in its General Body Meeting.
