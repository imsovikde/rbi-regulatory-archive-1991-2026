## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## December 18, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 18, 2002 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd., up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and stock exchanges, as the company has passed a resolution to this effect at its Board and General Body Meeting.
