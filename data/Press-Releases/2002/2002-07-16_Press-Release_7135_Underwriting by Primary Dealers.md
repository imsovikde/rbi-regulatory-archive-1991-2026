## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## July 16, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 10-year Government Stock and Price Based Auction of 8.07 per cent Government Stock, 2017 to be held on July 17, 2002 at Re.0.02 (two paise) and Re.0.15 (fifteen paise) respectively.
