## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## February 13, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of 8.07% Government Stock, 2017 to be held on February 14, 2002 was 0.9 paise. **Ajit Prasad Manager**
