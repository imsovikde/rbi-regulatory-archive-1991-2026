## RBI gives `In Principle' approval for one more Primary Dealer

## May 2, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has given `in principle' approval to Citicorp Finance (India) Ltd. to set up a 100% subsidiary company for accreditation as Primary Dealer in the Government securities market.

The new company will become operational as a primary dealer after terms and conditions are settled between it and the Reserve Bank of India.
