## RBI rejects licence application of The Aryan Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd.

## February 11, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has rejected the licence application of The Aryan Co- operative Urban Bank Ltd., Hyderabad, (Andhra Pradesh) to carry on banking business in India. The bank is precluded from transacting the business of `banking' as defined under Section 22 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 (As applicable to Co-operative Societies) and it will not be in order for it, among other things, to accept deposits from public and allow withdrawals by cheque, draft, order or otherwise.
