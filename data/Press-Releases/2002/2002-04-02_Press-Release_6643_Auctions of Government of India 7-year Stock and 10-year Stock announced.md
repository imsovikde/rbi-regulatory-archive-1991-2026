## Auctions of Government of India 7-year Stock and 10-year Stock announced

## April 2, 2002

Government of India have announced sale by auction of a new 7-year Government Stock for Rs.3,000 crore (nominal) and a new 10-year Government Stock for Rs.4,000 crore (nominal).

Both the auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on Thursday, April 4, 2002 using "Uniform Price Auction" method. Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai should be submitted at that office on April 4, 2002 during banking hours. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank of India will determine the maximum rate of yield at which offers will be accepted. The results of the auctions will be announced on April 4, 2002 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 5, 2002. The new stocks will qualify for the Ready Forward facility.

Under "Uniform Price Auction" method, competitive bids offered with rates up to and including the maximum rate of yield, as determined by the Reserve Bank of India, will be accepted at the maximum rate of yield so determined. Bids quoting higher than the maximum rate of yield determined by the Reserve Bank of India will be rejected.

For both the auctions, Government Stock up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale will be allotted to eligible individuals and Institutions under the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities.

Bids for underwriting the issues of Government Securities under auctions can be submitted by the 'Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market' up to 2.30 p.m on April 3, 2002 to the CGM-In-Charge, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.
