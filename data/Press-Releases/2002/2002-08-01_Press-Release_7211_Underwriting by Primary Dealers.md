# Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## August 1, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auctions of 9.39 per cent Government Stock, 2011 and 10.18 per cent Government Stock, 2026 to be held on August 2, 2002 at Re. 0.02 (two pasie) and Re.0.03 (three paise) respectively.

## P V Sadanandan Manager
