## Auction of 10-year Government of India Stock with Call and Put Options

## July 12, 2002

The Government of India will for the first time, sell by auction a new 10-year Government Stock on July 17, 2002 (Wednesday). The Stock, with **call and put options** will be for a notified amount of Rs.3,000 crore. It will be sold through a **yield based** auction using **uniform price** auction method. The issuance of the Government Stock with call and put options is being made for the **first time** on an **experimental** basis. Fresh issuance of Government securities with such features will depend on the response and the experience gained over time.

Under the **call option** the Government will have the discretion to prematurely redeem the Government Stock at par, after giving a public notice of two months, on any coupon payment date **on or after completion of five years from the date of issuance of** **the Government Stock**. Similarly, under the **put option** the holders of the Government Stock will have the option to offer the Government Stock for premature redemption at par, after giving a notice of two months, on any coupon payment date **on or after** **completion of five years from the date of issuance of the Government Stock**. In both the events, interest will cease to accrue on the redeemed Government Stock from the coupon payment date of premature redemption.

The Government Stock up to 5 per cent of the notified amount of the sale, i.e., Rs.150 crore will be allotted to the eligible individuals and institutions under the Scheme for **Non-Competitive Bidding Facility** in the auction of Government Securities.

The **auction** will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India at its Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai. Applicants who wish to bid for the Stock will have to clearly indicate in the prescribed form (obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai) the nominal value of the Stock applied for, and the yield expected by them (up to two decimal places). The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS, while all the non-competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form. **All bids must be submitted by**

## 2.30 P.M.

The results of the **auctions** will be announced on **July 17, 2002** (Wednesday) and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on **July 18, 2002** (Thursday). The new stock will qualify for the ready forward (repo) facility.

**Bids for underwriting** of the Stock under auction can be submitted by the Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market up to 2.30 p.m. on Tuesday, **July** **16, 2002** to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.
