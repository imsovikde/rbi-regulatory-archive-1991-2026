## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 15, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.4300 on November 15, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 14, 2002) were Rs.48.2800 and Rs.48.5800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
