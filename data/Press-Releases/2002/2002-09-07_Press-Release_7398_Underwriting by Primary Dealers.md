## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## September 7, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of "7.46% Government Stock, 2017" and "8.35% Government Stock, 2022" to be auctioned on September 9, 2002 at Re.0.05 (five paise) and Re.0.08 (eight paise) respectively.
