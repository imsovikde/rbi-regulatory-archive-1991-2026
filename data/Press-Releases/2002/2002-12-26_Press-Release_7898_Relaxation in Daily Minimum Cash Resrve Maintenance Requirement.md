## Relaxation in Daily Minimum Cash Resrve Maintenance Requirement

## December 26, 2002

At present, banks are required to maintain a minimum of 80 per cent of the required cash reserve ratio (CRR) amount on a daily basis during a fortnight, which is applicable on all the days of the reporting fortnight.

On a review based on feedback received from banks, it has been decided to reduce the present requirement of minimum of 80 per cent to 70 per cent with effect from the fortnight beginning December 28, 2002.

This relaxation will provide flexibility to banks in choosing an optimum strategy of holding reserves depending upon their intra-period cash flows.
