## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 3, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.4700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.8200 on September 3, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 2, 2002) were Rs.48.4800 and Rs.47.5600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
