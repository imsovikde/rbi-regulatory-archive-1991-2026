## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 27, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.9100 on November 27, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 26, 2002) were Rs.48.2100 and Rs.47.8200 respectively.
