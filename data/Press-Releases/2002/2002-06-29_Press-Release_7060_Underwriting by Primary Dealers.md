## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## June 29, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auctions of 7.55 per cent Government Stock, 2010 and FRB Government Stock, 2017 to be held on July 1, 2002 at Re.0.02 (two paise) and Re.0.07 (seven paise) respectively.
