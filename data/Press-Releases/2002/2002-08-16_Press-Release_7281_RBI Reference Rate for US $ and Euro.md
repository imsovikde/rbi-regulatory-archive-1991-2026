## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 16, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.5800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.6300 on August 16, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 14, 2002) were Rs.48.6000 and Rs.47.9300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 14, 2002|74.7517|41.4800|
|August 16, 2002|74.5266|41.2900|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate. **P V Sadanandan Manager**
