## RBI announces rate of interest on FRBs 2017

## December 27, 2002

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2017 (FRB, 2017) applicable for the half- year January 2, 2003 - July 1, 2003 shall be 5.92 per cent per annum. It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2017 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on July 1, 2002) over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest shall be average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half-yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to December 24, 2002 works out to 5.58 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on July 1, 2002 was (+) 0.34 percent. The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
