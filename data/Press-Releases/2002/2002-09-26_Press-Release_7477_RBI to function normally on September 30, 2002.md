## RBI to function normally on September 30, 2002

## September 26, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has stated that its offices in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai will conduct normal business on Monday, September 30, 2002. Commercial banks and co- operative banks will, however, remain closed for public on that day on account of half-yearly closing of their accounts. There will, therefore, be no clearing on that day at the Brihanmumbai Bankers' Clearing House. The settlement of MICR cheques presented on September 28 will be done on Tuesday, October 1, 2002 (September 29, 2002 being Sunday).

**P.V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
