## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 12, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.6300 on December 12, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 11, 2002) were Rs.48.1900 and Rs.48.6000 respectively.
