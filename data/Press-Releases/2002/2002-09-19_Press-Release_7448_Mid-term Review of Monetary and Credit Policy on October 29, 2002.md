## Mid-term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy on October 29, 2002

## September 19, 2002

Dr. Bimal Jalan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will hold a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks for the Mid-term Review of Monetary and Credit Policy for 2002-2003 on Tuesday, October 29, 2002. The meeting will be held at 11:00 am in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
