## Investment by FIIs/NRIs /PIOs-Monitoring of overall ceiling

## April 4, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on April 4, 2002 has notified that no further purchases of shares of ICICI Ltd., should be made through stock exchanges in India on behalf of foreign institutional investors (FIIs)/ non-resident Indians (NRIs) and persons of Indian origin (PIOs) as the holdings by FIIs / NRIs and PIOs have reached the overall ceiling of 49 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
