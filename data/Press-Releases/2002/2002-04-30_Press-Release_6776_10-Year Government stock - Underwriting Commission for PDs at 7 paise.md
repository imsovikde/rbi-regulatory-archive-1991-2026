# 10-Year Government stock : Underwriting Commission for PDs at 7 paise

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## 10-Year Government stock :

## Underwriting Commission for PDs at 7 paise

## April 30, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced that the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Yield Based Auction of 10-Year Government Stock to be held on May 2, 2002 was Re.0.07 (seven paise).

## P.V. Sadanandan

## Manager
