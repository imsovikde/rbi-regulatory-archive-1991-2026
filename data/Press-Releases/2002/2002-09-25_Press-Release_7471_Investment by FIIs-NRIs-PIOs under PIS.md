## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

## September 25, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India on September 25, 2002 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in the primary/secondary markets without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 47 per cent of its paid up capital.

**P.V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
