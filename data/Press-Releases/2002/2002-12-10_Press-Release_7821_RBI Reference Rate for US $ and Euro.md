## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 10, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.1900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.6600 on December 10, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 9, 2002) were Rs.48.2200 and Rs.48.7500 respectively.
