## 364 Day Treasury Bills-Auction Announced

## September 28, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.1000 crore. The allocation to non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F.2(12)- W&M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India as amended by Government of India notification No.2(1)-W&M/2001 dated July 12, 2002.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Tuesday, October 1, 2002 (Wednesday, October 2, 2002 being a holiday) before 1430 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, October 4, 2002.
