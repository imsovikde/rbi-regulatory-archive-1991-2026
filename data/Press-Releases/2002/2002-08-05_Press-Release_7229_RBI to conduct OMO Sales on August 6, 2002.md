## RBI to conduct OMO Sales on August 6, 2002

## August 5, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to offer from its investment portfolio the following security for sale through uniform price auction on August 6, 2002.

-11.75% GOI 2004 for upto Rs.3000 crore

The application form together with the terms and conditions of the auction may be obtained from the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of th India, 16 floor, Central Office Building, Mumbai or from Public Debt Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office.

The bids should be tendered on August 6, 2002 upto 12.30 pm and should be deposited in the tender box specially kept for the purpose at Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai.

On the basis of the tenders received, the Reserve Bank will determine the minimum price upto which tenders for sale of Government securities will be accepted at the auction. The auction will be conducted on uniform price basis. The results of the auction will be displayed at the Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai. The settlement will be on August 7, 2002. The Reserve Bank has the right to accept or reject any or all the bids either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.
