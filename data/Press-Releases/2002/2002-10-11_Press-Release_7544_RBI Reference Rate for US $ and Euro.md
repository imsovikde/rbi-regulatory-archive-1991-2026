## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 11, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.7000 on October 11, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 10, 2002) were Rs.48.3400 and Rs.47.8100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
