## RBI discontinues Satellite Dealers System

## December 10, 2002

As announced in the Monetary and Credit Policy for the year 2002-03, the Reserve Bank of India has discontinued the Satellite Dealers Scheme. No entity is now authorised to act as a satellite dealer in Government securities market.

## Ajit Prasad Manager
