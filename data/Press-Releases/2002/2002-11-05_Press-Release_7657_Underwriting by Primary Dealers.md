## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## November 5, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the price based auctions of "10.03% Government Stock, 2019" and "10.18% Government Stock, 2026" to be held on November 6, 2002 at Re.0.01 (one paisa) and Re.0.009 (0.90 paisa) respectively. **Ajit Prasad Manager**
