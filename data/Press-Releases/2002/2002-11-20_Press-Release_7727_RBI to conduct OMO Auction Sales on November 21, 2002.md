## RBI to conduct OMO Auction Sales on November 21, 2002

## 20November 20, 2002

## 9.40% GOI 2012 to be sold on auction basis for upto Rs.6000 crore

The Reserve Bank of India has decided to offer from its investment portfolio the following security for sale through uniform price auction on November 21, 2002.

9.40% GOI 2012 to be sold on auction basis for upto Rs.6000 crore The auction will be held at Mumbai. The bids may be tendered in electronic format using the NDS or may be deposited in the tender box specially kept for the purpose at the Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai on November 21, 2002 up to 12.30 pm. The application form together with the terms and conditions of the auction may be obtained from the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, 16th Floor, Central Office Building, Mumbai or from Public Debt Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office. On the basis of the tenders received, the RBI will determine the minimum price upto which tenders for sale of Government securities will be accepted at the auction. The auction will be conducted on uniform price basis. The results of the auction will be displayed at the Reserve Bank of India, Fort, Mumbai. The settlement will be on November 22, 2002. The Reserve Bank has the right to accept or reject any or all the bids either wholly or partially if deemed fit without assigning any reason.
