## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 10, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.8100 on October 10, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 9, 2002) were Rs.48.3400 and Rs.47.3500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
