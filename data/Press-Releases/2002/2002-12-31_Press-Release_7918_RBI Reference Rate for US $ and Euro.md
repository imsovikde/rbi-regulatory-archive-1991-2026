## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 31, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.50.3400 on December 31, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 30, 2002) were Rs.47.9600 and Rs.50.0600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
