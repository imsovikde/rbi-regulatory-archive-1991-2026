## Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of "8.07 percent Government Stock,

## 2017" through price based auction

## February 12, 2002

The Government of India have announced sale (re-issue) by auction of “8.07 percent Government Stock, 2017” for an aggregate amount of Rs 5,000 crore (nominal).

The auction will be <u>price based</u> and will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India at Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai on Thursday, February 14, 2002. Bids in prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, RBI, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai should be submitted at that Office, on February 14, 2002 during the banking hours. The auction will be conducted on "Multiple- Price" basis. On the basis of the bids received, RBI will determine the minimum price up to which tenders for purchase of Government Stock will be accepted at the auction. Bids quoted at prices lower than the minimum price determined by Reserve Bank of India will be rejected. The results of the auction will be announced on February 14, 2002 and payment by successful bidders will be during the banking hours on February 15, 2002.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Stock under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers in the Government Securities Market’ up to 2.30 p.m. on Wednesday, February 13, 2002 to the Chief General Manager-in-Charge, Internal Debt Management Cell, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001.

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
