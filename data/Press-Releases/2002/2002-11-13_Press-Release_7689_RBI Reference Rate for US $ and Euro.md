## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 13, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.8300 on November 13, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 12, 2002) were Rs.48.2700 and Rs.48.7300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
