# RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 17, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.3300 on October 17, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 16, 2002) were Rs.48.3400 and Rs.47.5000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are: **Date Currency** **1 GBP 100 YEN** October 16, 2002 75.1711 38.89 October 17, 2002 75.0456 38.9200 **Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.

**P.V. Sadanandan** **Manager**
