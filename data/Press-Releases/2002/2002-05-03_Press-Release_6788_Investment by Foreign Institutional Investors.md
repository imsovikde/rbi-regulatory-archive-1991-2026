## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors

## May 3, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on May 3, 2002 that the aggregate net purchases of equity shares of Zee Telefilms Ltd., by foreign institutional investors (FIIs) in the primary/secondary markets has reached the limit of 28 per cent of its paid up equity capital. No further purchases of equity shares of Zee Telefilms Ltd., will, therefore, be allowed on behalf of the FIIs without obtaining prior approval of the Reserve Bank.
