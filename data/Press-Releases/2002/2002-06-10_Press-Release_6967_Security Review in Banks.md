## Security Review in Banks

## June 10, 2002

Shri Vepa Kamesam, Deputy governor had a meeting with the banks today to review the prevailing security situation and emphasise on the banks to take appropriate precautions. The meeting was attended by the Chief Executive Officers alongwith their Chief Officers responsible for security. On behalf of the Reserve Bank, Deputy Governor assured the banks that supplies of cash will be maintained as required and exhorted them to be ready with their contingency plans.
