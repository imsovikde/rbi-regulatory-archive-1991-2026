## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 13, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.4500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.5800 on September 13, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 12, 2002) were Rs.48.4700 and Rs.47.2600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
