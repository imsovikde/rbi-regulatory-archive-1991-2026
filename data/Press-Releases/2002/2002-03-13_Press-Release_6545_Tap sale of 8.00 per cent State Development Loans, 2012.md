## Tap sale of 8.00 per cent State Development Loans, 2012

## March 13, 2002

The 'on tap' sale of 8.00 per cent State Loans, 2012 was closed in respect of Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and Uttaranchal on March 13, 2002, on receipt of the respective target amounts.

The sale in respect of the loans offered by States of Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Nagaland, Orissa, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal will continue on March 14, 2002. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after the business hours on March 14, 2002 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received.

Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai (for all remaining States). Applications alongwith the payment will be received at those offices during business hours while the sale is open. Applicants, submitting the bids on March 14, 2002 would also have to pay interest for one day at 8.00 per cent per annum for the securities they apply for.
