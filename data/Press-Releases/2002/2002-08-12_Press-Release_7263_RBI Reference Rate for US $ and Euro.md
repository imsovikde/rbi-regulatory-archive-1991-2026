## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 12, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.6200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.3400 on August 12, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 9, 2002) were Rs.48.6700 and Rs.47.1800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 9, 2002|74.6257|40.28|
|August 12, 2002|74.2257|40.5200|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
