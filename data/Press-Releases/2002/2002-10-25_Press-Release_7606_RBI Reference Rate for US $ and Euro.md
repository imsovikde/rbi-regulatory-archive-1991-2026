## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 25, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.3100 on October 25, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 24, 2002) were Rs.48.4300 and Rs.47.2000 respectively.
