## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 3, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.1000 on December 3, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 2, 2002) were Rs.48.3200 and Rs.47.9700 respectively.
