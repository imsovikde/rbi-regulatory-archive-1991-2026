## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

## October 7, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting by Primary Dealers for the Price Based Auction of "7.27% Government Stock, 2013" and "7.95% Government Stock, 2032" to be held on October 8, 2002 at Re.0.01 (one paisa) and Re.0.03 (three paise) respectively.
