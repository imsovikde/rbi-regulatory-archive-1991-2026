## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 1, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.7600 on October 1, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 27, 2002) were Rs.48.3800 and Rs.47.2500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are: **Date Currency** **1 GBP 100 YEN** September 27, 2002 75.3277 39.56 October 1, 2002 76.1017 39.6100

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
