## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 20, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.3300 on November 20, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 18, 2002) were Rs.48.2000 and Rs.48.7000 respectively.
