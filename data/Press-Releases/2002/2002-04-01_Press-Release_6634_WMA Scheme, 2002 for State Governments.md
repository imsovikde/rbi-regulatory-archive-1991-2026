# WMA Scheme, 2002 for State Governments

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## WMA Scheme, 2002 for State Governments

## April 1, 2002

The revised Ways and Means Advances Scheme, 2002 will come into effect from April 1, 2002. The total revised normal WMA limits works out to Rs.6,035 crore as against the present limits of Rs.5,283 crore representing an increase of Rs.752 crore or about 14 per cent.

The main features of the Scheme are:

## Normal WMA Limits

The normal WMA limits are revised taking into account the three years’ average of revenue receipts and capital expenditure for fiscal years 1998-99, 1999-2000 and 2000-01 and to this base a ratio of 2.4 per cent is applied for the non-special category States and 2.9 per cent for the special category States, with a minimum normal WMA limit of Rs.50 crore for any state. Accordingly, the total revised normal WMA limits works out to Rs.6,035 crore as against the present limits of Rs.5,283 crore representing an increase of Rs.752 crore or about 14 per cent.

## Special WMA and Minimum Balance

The present Scheme of special WMA and Minimum Balance will continue. The special WMA continues to be linked to the investments made by State Governments in the Government of India Securities, i.e., Dated Securities and Treasury Bills.

## Overdraft Regulation Scheme

## The Overdraft Regulation Scheme is:

a) The RBI will allow a State to run an overdraft for 12 consecutive working days as at present. In case the overdraft appears in the State’s account and remains beyond 12 consecutive working days, the RBI and its agencies will stop payments on behalf of the concerned State Governments.
b) The overdraft will not exceed 100 per cent of normal WMA limit. On the first occasion that this is exceeded in a financial year, the RBI will advise the State that the overdraft amount should not exceed 100 per cent of normal WMA limit on any subsequent occasion.
c) If during the financial year, the amount of overdraft exceeds 100 per cent of WMA limit on a second or any subsequent occasion, the State will be given five working days’ notice, as is being done at present, to bring down the overdraft amount within the level of 100 per cent of normal WMA limit. If this is not adhered to, payments will be stopped.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

## Effective date

The revised limits would come into force from April 1, 2002.

*Review* The WMA Scheme 2002 is subject to review in its entirety next year.

*Background* The Ways and Means Advances (WMA) Scheme, 2001, of the State Governments has been in effect from February 1, 2001. The details of the WMA Scheme, 2001 were worked out on the basis of the recommendations of the Informal Group of the State Finance Secretaries. It was also decided that every year the Reserve Bank of India will revise the normal WMA limits taking into account the moving average of revenue receipts and capital expenditure for the latest three years and review this arrangement in

2003.
