## RBI Instructions to Cooperative Banks for G-Sec Transactions

## May 4, 2002

In response to press queries, following a report in a section of the press, the Reserve Bank of India encloses its circular dated April 20, 2002 issued to cooperative banks reiterating its existing guidelines for transactions in Government securities. The full text of the circular is also available on the RBI website www.rbi.org.in

## Alpana Killawala General Manager
