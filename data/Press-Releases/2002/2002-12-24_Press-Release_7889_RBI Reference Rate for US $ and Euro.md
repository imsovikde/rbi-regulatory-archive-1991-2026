## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 24, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.0100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.2900 on December 24, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 23, 2002) were Rs.47.9900 and Rs.49.3000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
