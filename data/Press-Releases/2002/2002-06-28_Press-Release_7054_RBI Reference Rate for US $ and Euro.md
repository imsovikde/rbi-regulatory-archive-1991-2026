## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## June 28, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.8700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.2900 on June 28, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 27, 2002) were Rs.48.8500 and Rs.48.0700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|June 27, 2002|74.5158|40.72|
|June 28, 2002|74.6123|40.8900|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
