## Investment by Foreign Institutional Investors under PIS

## June 11, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has notified today that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of United Breweries Limited up to 49 per cent of its paid up capital through primary and secondary markets in India. The company has passed resolution at its Board of Directors' and a Special Resolution in their General Body Meeting for purchase of equity shares and convertible debentures by FIIs upto 49 per cent of its paid-up capital through primary/secondary markets in India.
