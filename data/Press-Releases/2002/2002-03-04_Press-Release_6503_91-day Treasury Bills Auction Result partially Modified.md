## 91-day Treasury Bills Auction Result partially Modified

## March 4, 2002

In partial modification of the press release dated February 27, 2002 advising the results of the auction of 91-day Treasury Bills held on February 27, 2002, it is advised that the single non-competitive bid was accepted for Rs.125.885 crore instead of Rs.200 crore as advised earlier.
