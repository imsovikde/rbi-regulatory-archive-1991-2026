## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 25, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.9800 on November 25, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 22, 2002) were Rs.48.2000 and Rs.48.3300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
