## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## December 19, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.1100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.49.3400 on December 19, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 18, 2002) were Rs.48.1500 and Rs.49.4800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
