## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## October 22, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.3600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.0600 on October 22, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 21, 2002) were Rs.48.3600 and Rs.47.0400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
