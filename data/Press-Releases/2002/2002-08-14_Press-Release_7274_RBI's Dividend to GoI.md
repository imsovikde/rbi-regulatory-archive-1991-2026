## RBI's Dividend to GoI

## August 14, 2002

In response to a press query, the RBI spokesperson confirmed that the Reserve Bank Board has, at its meeting held today, decided to transfer a dividend of Rs.10,320 crore to the Government of India out of its surplus profit for the year ended June 30,

2002.
