## Monitoring FII Investments in Indian Companies

## August 9, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India has advised that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Himachal Futuristic Communications Ltd. up to 49 per cent of its paid-up capital through primary/secondary markets in India under the Portfolio Investment Scheme.. The company's board of directors and general body have passed a resolution agreeing for the purchase. **Ajit Prasad Manager**
