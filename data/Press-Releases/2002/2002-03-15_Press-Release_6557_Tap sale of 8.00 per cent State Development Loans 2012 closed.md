## Tap sale of 8.00 per cent State Development Loans 2012 closed

## March 15, 2002

The `on tap' sale of 8.00 per cent State Loans, 2012 was closed today in respect of Bihar and Uttar Pradesh.

It may be recalled that the `on tap' of 8.00 per cent State Development Loans, 2012 which opened on March 13, 2002 was closed on the same day in respect of 15 States, viz., Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Punjab, Tamil Nadu and Uttaranchal.

The issue was closed on the second day, i.e., on March 14, 2002 in respect of nine States, viz., Assam, Chhattisgarh, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Nagaland, Orissa, Rajasthan and West Bengal.

Accordingly, the tap issue for all the States has been closed as at the close of business hours on March 15, 2002.
