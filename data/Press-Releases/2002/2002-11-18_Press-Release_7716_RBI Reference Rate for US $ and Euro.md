## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 18, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.7000 on November 18, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 15, 2002) were Rs.48.2300 and Rs.48.4300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
