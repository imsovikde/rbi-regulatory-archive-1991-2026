## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 28, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.7800 on November 28, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 27, 2002) were Rs.48.2100 and Rs.47.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
