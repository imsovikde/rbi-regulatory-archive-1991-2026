## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## August 19, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.8600 on August 19, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 16, 2002) were Rs.48.5800 and Rs.47.6300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 16, 2002|74.5266|41.29|
|August 19, 2002|74.8625|41.1400|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
