# Reliance on Call/Notice Money Market : Prudential Norm

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## <u>Reliance on Call/Notice Money Market : Prudential Norm</u>

As announced in the Annual Monetary and Credit Policy Statement for 2002-2003, and after further consultations with bankers, prudential limits on the exposure of commercial banks in call/notice money market have been stipulated in two stages. The corresponding circular to all scheduled commercial banks has been issued and is available on the RBI website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>).

The limits prescribed are prudential in nature and are sufficiently high for the participants in the market and it is expected that all liquidity needs of banks would be met. Further, it is proposed to review the money market developments in a meeting with the banks in the second half of November 2002.

Alpana Killawala General Manager <u>Press Release No.2001-2002/1432</u>
