## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## September 2, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.4800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.47.5600 on September 2, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 30, 2002) were Rs.48.5100 and Rs.47.7400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|Date|Currency||
|---|---|---|
||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|August 30, 2002|75.1565|41.10|
|September 2, 2002|74.9889|40.8800|

**Note :** The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai. The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
