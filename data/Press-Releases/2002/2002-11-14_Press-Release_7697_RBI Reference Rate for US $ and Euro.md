## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

## November 14, 2002

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.48.2800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.48.5800 on November 14, 2002. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 13, 2002) were Rs.48.2800 and Rs.48.8300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
