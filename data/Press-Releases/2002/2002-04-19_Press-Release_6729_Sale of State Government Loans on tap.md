## 19 Apr 2002

# Sale of State Government Loans on tap

In partial modification to the earlier Press Release dated April 18, 2002 in connection with the sale of State Government Loans on `tap', it may be noted that the State Government of Sikkim will not be participating in the `tap' issue to be opened on April 23, 2002. Other terms and conditions of the `tap' issue remain unchanged. **Ajit Prasad Manager**
