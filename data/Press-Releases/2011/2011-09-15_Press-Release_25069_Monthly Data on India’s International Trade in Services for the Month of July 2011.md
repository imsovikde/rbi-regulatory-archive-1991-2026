## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of July 2011

The Reserve Bank is releasing provisional aggregate monthly data on India's international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for June 2011 was released on August 12, 2011.

The exports and imports of services during the month of July 2011 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|April 2011|11,469|6,889|
|May 2011|11,835|7,088|
|June 2011|11,040|7,205|
|July 2011 These provisional data will undergo revision when the Balance of Payments|10,400|5,894|

Note: Data are provisional.

(BoP) data are compiled on a quarterly basis which will be released with a lag of a quarter. The quarterly release will also provide disaggregated data on trade in services.
