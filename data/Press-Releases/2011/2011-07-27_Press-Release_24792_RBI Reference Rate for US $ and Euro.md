## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9485 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.63.6663 on July 27, 2011. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 26, 2011) were Rs.44.2873 and Rs.64.1666 respectively.
