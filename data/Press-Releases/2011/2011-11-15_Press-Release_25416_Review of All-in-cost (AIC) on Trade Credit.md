## November 15, 2011

## Review of All-in-cost (AIC) on Trade Credit

On a review of developments in the global financial markets and the fact that domestic importers are experiencing difficulties in raising Trade Credit within the existing all-in-cost (AIC) ceiling, it has been decided to revise the AIC for Trade Credits as under:

## All-in-cost over 6 month LIBOR*

## Maturity Period

## Existing Revised

Upto one year 200 bps 350 bps

More than one year upto three years

* for the respective currency of credit or applicable benchmark The all-in-cost ceilings include arranger fee, upfront fee, management fee, handling/ processing charges, out of pocket and legal expenses, if any. The above amendment in Trade Credit will come into force immediately and the enhancement in all-in-cost ceiling is applicable upto March 31, 2012, subject to review thereafter.
