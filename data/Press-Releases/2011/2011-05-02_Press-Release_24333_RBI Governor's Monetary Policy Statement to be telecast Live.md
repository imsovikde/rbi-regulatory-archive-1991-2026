## RBI Governor's Monetary Policy Statement to be telecast Live

The Reserve Bank of India will, on May 3, 2011, telecast live Governor, Dr. D. Subbarao's statement to bankers on Monetary Policy for the year 2011-12. The live telecast of the Governor's statement will commence from 11.00 a.m. The statement would also be live webcast on the Reserve Bank's website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). The full Policy document will be released on RBI's website at around 11.20 a.m. Live telecast of the Governor’s statement is one more of Reserve Bank’s initiatives in transparency in communication. In the recent past, the Reserve Bank has taken several initiatives in the procedure of announcing the Monetary Policy to bring greater effectiveness in its dissemination. Some of these include -

- Webcasting the Governor’s press conference and placing its audio recording and transcript on the Reserve Bank’s website.
- Facilitating media interaction with the Reserve Bank from across the globe through teleconference.
- Interaction with researchers/analysts on Monetary Policy through teleconference and placing its audio recording and transcript on the Reserve Bank's website.
