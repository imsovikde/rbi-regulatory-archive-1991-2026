## First Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 on July 26, 2011

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the First Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 on Tuesday, July 26, 2011. This will be done in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at

11.00 a.m. on July 26, 2011 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
