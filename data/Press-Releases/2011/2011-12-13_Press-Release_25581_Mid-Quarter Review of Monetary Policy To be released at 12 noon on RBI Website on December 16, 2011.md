## Mid-Quarter Review of Monetary Policy

## To be released at 12 noon on RBI Website on December 16, 2011

The Reserve Bank of India will release the next Mid-Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 at 12 noon on December 16, 2011. The Review will be by way of a press release and will be placed on the Reserve Bank's website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>).
