## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of September 2011

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India's international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of August 2011 were released on October 14, 2011.

The exports and imports of services during the month of September 2011 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

## (US$ Million)

## Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)

## July 2011 10,400 5,894

## August 2011 11,897 6,860

## September 2011 11,228 6,799

**Note**: Data are provisional.

As mentioned in the previous press release, monthly services data are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis. Accordingly, revised data on services receipts and payments on quarterly basis (April-June 2011) are now available in the press release on 'Developments in India's Balance of Payments during April-June 2011' placed on RBI website on September 30, 2011.
