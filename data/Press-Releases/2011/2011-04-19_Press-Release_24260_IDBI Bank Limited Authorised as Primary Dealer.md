## April 19, 2011

## IDBI Bank Limited Authorised as Primary Dealer

IDBI Bank Limited has been authorised to undertake Primary Dealer business in Government securities market with effect from Wednesday, April 20, 2011 and, concomitantly, the authorisation given to IDBI Gilts Limited as Primary Dealer stands withdrawn.
