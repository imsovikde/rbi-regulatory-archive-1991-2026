## Monthly Data on India’s International Trade in Services for the Month of October 2011

The Reserve Bank releases provisional aggregate monthly data on India's international trade in services with a lag of 45 days. Accordingly, the data for the month of September 2011 were released on November 15, 2011.

The exports and imports of services during the month of October 2011 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

## (US$ Million)

|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|---|---|---|
|July 2011|10,400|5,894|
|August 2011|11,897|6,860|
|September 2011|11,228|6,799|
|October 2011 Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the|11,455|6,820|

Note: Data are provisional.

Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis. Accordingly, revised data on services receipts and payments on quarterly basis (April-June 2011) are now available in the press release on 'Developments in India's Balance of Payments during April-June 2011' placed on RBI website on September 30, 2011.
