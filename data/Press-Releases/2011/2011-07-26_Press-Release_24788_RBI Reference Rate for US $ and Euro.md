## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2873 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.64.1666 on July 26, 2011. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 25, 2011) were Rs.44.4160 and Rs.63.8654 respectively.
