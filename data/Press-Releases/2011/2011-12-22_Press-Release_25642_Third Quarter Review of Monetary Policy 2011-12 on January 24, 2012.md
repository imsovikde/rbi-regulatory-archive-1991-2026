## Third Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 on January 24, 2012

Dr. D. Subbarao, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will announce the Third Quarter Review of Monetary Policy 2011-12 in a meeting with the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks at 11.00 a.m. on Tuesday, January 24, 2012 at the Central Office, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai.
