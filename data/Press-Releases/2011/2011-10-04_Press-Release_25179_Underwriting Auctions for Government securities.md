## Underwriting Auctions for Government securities

The Government of India have announced the sale (reissue) of "8.07 per cent Government Stock 2017” for a notified amount of ` 3,000 crore (nominal), "8.08 per cent Government Stock 2022" for a notified amount of ` 6,000 crore (nominal), "8.28 per cent Government Stock 2027” for a notified amount of ` 3,000 crore (nominal) and "8.30 per cent Government Stock 2040” for a notified amount of ` 3,000 crore (nominal) through auctions to be held on October 7, 2011. The underwriting auctions for these securities will be conducted on October 5, 2011 (Wednesday).

As per revised scheme of underwriting, dated November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) for the underwriting auctions, per Primary Dealer, are as under: (` in crore)

## Security

The underwriting auctions will be conducted using multiple price based auction method. Primary Dealers (PDs) may submit their bids for ACU auctions electronically through Negotiated Dealing System (NDS) between 10.30 a.m. and

12.30 p.m. on October 5, 2011 (Wednesday). The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs at the RBI, Fort, Mumbai on the date of issue of securities.
