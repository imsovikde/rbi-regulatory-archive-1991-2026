## Auction Calendar – LAF

The Reserve Bank of India announces the calendar of auction for Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) for the quarter July- September 2011. There is no change in the existing terms and conditions under which the Liquidity Adjustment Facility auctions are conducted.
