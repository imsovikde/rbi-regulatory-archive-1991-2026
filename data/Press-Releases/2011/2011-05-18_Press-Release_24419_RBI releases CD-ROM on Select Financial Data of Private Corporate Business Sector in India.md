## RBI releases CD-ROM on Select Financial Data of Private Corporate Business Sector in India

The Reserve Bank of India today released *'*<u>Compendium on Private</u> <u>Corporate Business Sector in India-Select Financial Statistics</u>*'* in a DVD-ROM.

The compendium, the fifth in the series, is an attempt to present, at one place, data on company finances from the various periodical studies up to the financial year 2008-09. The DVD-ROM consists of two parts. Part one comprises five chapters that briefly describe the scope of the studies, sources of data, methodology, concepts and definitions adopted for the studies. Part two contains statistical statements. The basic source of data is balance sheet, profit and loss account and other material contained in the annual accounts of the companies.

The compendium is available on RBI website (<u>[http://www.rbi.org.in](http://www.rbi.org.in)</u>). The compendium in DVD-ROM is also available from Sales Section, Division of Reports and Information Dissemination, Department of Economic and Policy Research, Reserve Bank of India, Amar Building, P.M. Road, Mumbai-400001. Order for the purchase can be placed on payment through DD/Cheque/Pay Order drawn in favour of the Reserve Bank of India, payable at Mumbai. The DVD-ROM is priced at: ` 350 (Postage free) in India and US $ 70 (Postage free) for abroad.

The Reserve Bank of India (Department of Statistics and Information Management) has been publishing studies on the financial performance of the corporate sector since 1951. The data contained in these studies have been consolidated and brought out as special publications at periodic intervals. So far, four such publications with the last one in September 2001 (covering the period 1974-75 to 1999-2000) have been brought out.
