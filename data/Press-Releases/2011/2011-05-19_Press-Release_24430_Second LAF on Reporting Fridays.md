## Second LAF on Reporting Fridays

In the light of modified operating procedure of monetary policy introduced from May 3, 2011 and introduction of the marginal standing facility (MSF), it has been decided to discontinue the second liquidity adjustment facility (SLAF) on reporting Fridays effective May 20, 2011.
