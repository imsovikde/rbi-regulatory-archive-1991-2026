## Data on ECB/FCCB for April 2011

The Reserve Bank of India has today released the data on <u>External</u> <u>Commercial Borrowings (ECB)</u> and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB), both through Automatic Route and Approval Route, for the month of April 2011.
