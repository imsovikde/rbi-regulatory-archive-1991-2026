## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.4160 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.63.8654 on July 25, 2011. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 22, 2011) were Rs.44.3763 and Rs.63.8745 respectively.
