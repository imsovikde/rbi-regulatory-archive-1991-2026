# RBI sets up Expert Group on RRBs (July 28, 199)

**The Reserve Bank of India has constituted an Expert Group to examine major issues** **relating to restructuring of Regional Rural '** **Banks (RRBs). Dr. N.K. Thingalaya, Chairman and Managing Director, Syndicate Bank is the** **Chairman of the Expert Group. The other members are: Dr. M.C. Bhandari, Chief** **General Manager, NABARD, Shri Y.S.P.** **Thorat, General Manager, Reserve Bank of** **India, Shri Sudhir Srivastava, Deputy** **Secretary, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, Shri J.S. Tomar, General Manager,** **Oriental Bank of Commerce, Shri Rattan** **Kapur, General Manager, Bank of Baroda,** **Shri K.M. Udupa, Deputy General Manager,** **Syndicate Bank (erstwhile Chairman,** **Malaprabha Gramin Bank), Shri R.S. Yadav,** **Chairman, Gorakhpur Gramin Bank and Shri**

**M.S. Nayak, Assistant General Manager,** **Canara Bank. It may be recalled that 49 RRBs**
## were identified for restructuring in 1994-95.

**The Expert Group held its first meeting today. Setting out the background of the** **restructuring exercise undertaken by the RBI for select RRBs, Shri R.V. Gupta, Deputy** **Governor, Reserve Bank, noted that the involvement of all the three agencies** **associated with RRBs, viz., sponsor banks and State as well as Central governments was** **of utmost importance for the success of the restructuring experiment. He, therefore,** **requested the Expert Group to make specific recommendations regarding the role of these** **three agencies with regard to RRBs. Shri** **Gupta laid special emphasis on training and motivational aspects for the staff of these** **RRBs.** **The Expert Group is expected to meet periodically to review the performance of** **RRBs and to advise the Reserve Bank on specific issues relating to their functioning.**
