*May 1995* **RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**NRis allowed to invest in more areas** **(May 26, 1995)** **With a view to opening up more areas for investment by non-resident Indians (NRls),** **overseas corporate bodies (OCBs) and foreign institutional investors (Flis), the Reserve** **Bank of India (RBI) has decided to allow them to invest, on a repatriation basis, in all** **activities except agriculture and plantation activities, subject to certain conditions.**

**Accordingly, companies, existing or new, public limited or private limited, engaged or** **proposing to engage in activities such as, finance, hire purcase, leasing, trading, other** **services, etc., have been allowed to issue shares/convertible debentures to NRis and** **OCBs on a repatriation basis provided the aggregate allocation to non-residents** **qualifying for the facility does not exceed 24 per cent of the new issue.
