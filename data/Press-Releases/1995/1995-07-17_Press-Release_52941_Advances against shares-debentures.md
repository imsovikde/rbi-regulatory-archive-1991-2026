# Advances against shares/debentures

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN** *July 1995*

**Advances against shares/debentures** **(July 17. 1995)** **As per the present RBI guidelines, banks are allowed to provide reasonable** **overdraft facilities to share and stock brokers against the shares and debentures** **held by them as stock in trade. The present RBI guidelines require that if the** **shares and debentures lodged as security are held beyond three months. then they** **should be transferred in the bank's name.** **The Reserve Bank has now decided to extend this period from three months to** **nine months. This relaxation applies only to share brokers registered with the** **Securities and Exchange Board of India and who are members of recognised stock** **exchanges. As before, banks are required to obtain duly executed blank transfer forms for** **the shares pledged by the share and stock brokers. While share and stock brokers are** **free to substitute the shares pledged by them, as and when necessary, in case of default in** **the account, ·banks have been asked to exercise the option to get the shares** **transferred in their names.** **further, the monetary limit of Rs. 3 lakh prescribed for advances against shares and** **debentures granted to individuals, trusts and**
