# RBI offices networked

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN April 1995

**RBI offices networked** **(April 20, 1995)** **Ail major Reserve Bank offices are now linked through the RBINet, an electr6nic** **communications network. This will facilitate expeditious transmission of inter-office** **communications and other normal correspon dence in the Reserve Bank.**

**The RBINet is a communication software which uses leased lines and public telephone** **lines as carrier. Messaging support for** **RBINet is provided by UNIX servers located at Delhi, Calcutta, Bombay and Madras. The** **network allows free format messaging and file transfers, which could be data files, text** **files and binary files in both encrypted and non-encrypted form, on the Banknet network** **using the existing computer system. It enables exchange of error-free and secured messages** **among users on'store and collect' basis.** **Some public sector banks are also linked up on the RBINet. Through its broadcast** **feature, the RBINet will enable the Reserve** **Bank to transmit circulars and other information simultaneously to all banks** **which are on the network. The network is, thus, positioned as a crucial data** **communication carrier for the various technological improvements, planned in** **the payment system, information dissemination and regular reporting of** **statistics.** **The Reserve Bank is also planning to upgrade the RBINet to a satellite based** **communications neiwork.**
