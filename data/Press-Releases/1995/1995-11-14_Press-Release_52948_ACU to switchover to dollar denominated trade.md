# ACU to switchover to dollar denominated trade

# (November 14, 1995)

**The Board of Directors of the Asian** **Clearing Union (ACU) have, in September** **1995, decided to switchover to a dollar denominated trade. Under the new system,** **commercial banks in member-countries of the** **ACU will be permitted to maintain ACU doIJar accounts with their correspondent** **banks in other ACU countries. The settlements for exports and imports will be in** **dollar terms.** **The Board of Directors of the ACU had, in May 1995, engaged Shri R. Janakiraman,** **former Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank of India as a consultant to go into the** **procedures and practices followed under the existing ACU mechanism. Under the existing** **ACU mechanism, payment for trade transactions are settled in the local currency.** **This system created several practical problems for the trade and the exporters were** **not extended facilities like pre-shipment credit in foreign currency, post-shipment** **credit in foreign currency, etc. The decision to switch over to a dollar denominated trade** **was taken by the Board of Directors at their meeting held in Colombo in September on the** **basis of the recommendations made by Shri** **Janakiraman** **The methodology for introducing the system is being worked out and the new** **system is expected to come into effect from** **January 1, 1996.**
