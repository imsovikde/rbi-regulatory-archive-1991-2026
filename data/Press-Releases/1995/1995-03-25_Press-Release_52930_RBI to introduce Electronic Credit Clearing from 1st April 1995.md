# RBI to introduce Electronic Credit Clearing from 1st April 1995

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

***April 1995*** **RBSBR.VB BANK OF INDIA BULLBT.IN**

# RBI PRESS RELEASES

**RBI to introduce Electronic Credit** **Clearing from 1st April 1995** **(March 2S, 1995)** **Towards reducing delays in handling payments and eliminating risk of frauds, loss** **of instruments in transit etc., the Reserve** **Bank of India (RBI) has decided to introduce** **"Electronic Clearing Service (ECS)" for credit transactions with effect from I st April**

**1995. The ECS will be initially introduced at** **Bombay and Madras and later extended to** **Delhi and Calcutta. The Reserve Bank has advised banks to explain to their corporate** **customers the procedures of electronic payments system and initiate the preparatory** **work for switching over to the ECS.** **Under the ECS, payments of recurring nature such as interest on company deposits,** **dividend, pensions, salaries, etc., will be directly credited to the beneficiaries' bank** **accounts. A prudent limit of Rs. 25,000 is prescribed for individual payment** **transactions. As per procedure, the corporate customers would furnish to their respective** **banks information such as, bank account number, amount of credit for each beneficiary** **etc., on magnetic tape/floppy. Banks would send these data ·mes to the RBI clearing** **houses for further processing and advising the banks to c-redit the beneficiaries' accounts, on** **the specified dates. Adequate control and security measures to protect the data files are** **provided in the procedures.** **The pilot exercises on electronic credit clearing have aJready been undertaken by the** **Reserve Bank at Madras and Bombay. The procedures have been modified on the basis** **of feedback from banks and clearing houses.** **It may be recalled that the Saraf Committee on technology issues in payment systems had** **recommended introduction of electronic clearing services.**
