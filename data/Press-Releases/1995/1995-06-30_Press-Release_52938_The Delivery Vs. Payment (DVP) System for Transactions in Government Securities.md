# The Delivery Vs. Payment (DVP) System for Transactions in Government Securities

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN June /995

## The Delivery Vs. Payment (DVP) System For Transactions in Government Securities (June 30, 1995)

The Delivery Versus Payment (DVP) System for transactions in dated Government securities maintained in the Public Debt Office, Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Bombay will be introduced with effect from July 17. I 995. The system wiII be progressively extended to other offices of the Reserve Bank. The DVP system aims at making the transactions transparent and eliminating counter party risk.

As the Central Bank of the country, RBI is concerned about the efficiency of the payment and settlement system in respect of

transactions in Government securities by virtue of its statutory role as the manager of the public debt of the country. The computerisation of SGL accounts operations was done in 1992 for the Public Debt Office at Bombay and progressively it was extended to other Public Debt Offices.

Under the existing Subsidiary General Ledger (SGL) system, RBI has been carrying out settlement in the securities account only. The underlying Funds settlement is done by the buyers and sellers without any disclosure in the SGL transfer forms. Under the proposed Delivery Versus Payment System, transfers in SGL accounts will be effected simultaneously with the funds transfer in their current accounts with RBI. The SGL transfer form has been suitably amended to provide for reporting of information regarding the price and authorisation for the transfer of funds from the buyer" s fund account to the seller· s account with the Reserve Bank. The revised transfer form will have to be lodged with the RBI not later than the working day following the date of signing.

The system will cover transactions in SGL accounts of all those who have current accounts at RBI. It will be subsequently extended to cover SGL transactions in treasury bills.

The DVP system would facilitate activising the Government securities market. The Reserve Bank_ is also examining issues in extending the DVP system to those participants in the Government securities market who do not have SGL accounts or current accounts with the RBI; the Saraf Committee on technology upgradation in the Payment System has made certain recommendations i this regard.
