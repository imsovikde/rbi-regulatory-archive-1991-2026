# Money Market Mutual Funds (MMMFs)

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## RESERVE BANK Of INDIA Bt.J',LLETIN October-November 1995

**Money Market Mutual Funds (MMMFs)Scheme (which is at present restricted only to (November 23, 1995)** **banks and financial institutions) to make it more attractive and also to provide greater** **It has been decided to permit private sector liquidity and depth to the money market:** **Mutual Funds to set up Money Market Mutual** **Funds (MMMFs). The following relaxations (a) The prescription of ceiling by the RBI** **have been made in the present MMMF on the size of the MMMFs has been** **withdrawn though the instruments in which** **MMMFs can invest would remain unchanged (viz., Treasury Bills and dated** **government securities having an unexpired maturity upto one year, call and notice** **money, commercial paper (CP), commercial bills accepted/co-accepted by** **banks and certificates of deposit).** **Subscriptions to MMMFs would, however, continue to be open only to individual** **investors.**

**(b) The prescription of limits on investments in individual instruments by** **MMMFs-minimum or maximum-is withdrawn and the MMMFs are free to** **determine the extent of their investments in each instrument. On prudential grounds,** **however, the guideline that the exposure to** **CP issued by an individual company should not be more than 3 per cent of the resources** **mobilised by the MMMFs would continue.** **While all MMMFs will require prior authorisation by the Reserve Bank of India,**
**the private sector MMMFs would need the clearance of SEBI as also the approval of the** **RBI. The detailed operational procedures for private sector MMMFs are being worked out** **by the Reserve Bank in consultation with the** **Securities and Exchange Board of India** **(SEBI) and will be announced separately.** •- **The Scheme of Money Market Mutual** **Funds (MMMFs) was introduced by the RBI in April 1992 with a view to providing** **additional short-term avenues to individual investors and ·to bring money market** **instruments within their reach.**
