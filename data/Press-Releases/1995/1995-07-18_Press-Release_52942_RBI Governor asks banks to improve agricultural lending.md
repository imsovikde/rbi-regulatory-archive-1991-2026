# RBI Governor asks banks to improve agricultural lending

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

## July 1995

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA BULLETIN

**RBI Goveraor asks banks to improve agricultural lending** **(July 18, 1995)** **Bankers have expressed their confidence in improved agricultural lending and setting up** **of specialised SSI branches during 1995-96.** **These assurances were given to Dr. C.** **Rangarajan, Governor, Reserve Bank of India at a meeting of the chief executives of public** **sector banks convened by him in Bombay.** **The meeting reviewed the flow of bank credit to agriculture, progress regarding the** **restructuring of Regional Rurual Banks, setting up of specialised SSI branches and** **other related issues. Shri P. Kotaiah,** **Chairman, NABARD and other officials of** **RBI and NABARD, also attended the meeting.** **In his opening remarks, the Governor noted that the performance of public sector banks** **in lending to agriculture-in absolute terms had shown a substantial increase during 1994-**

**95. The disbursements during the year had increased by over Rs. 2,500 crore from** **Rs. 5,404 crore in 1993-94 to Rs. 7,945.15 crore in 1994-95. He observed that the** **percentage of agricultural credit to net bank credit had, however, showed a declining trend** **from 15.03 per cent as on March 31, 1994 to**
**13.91 per cent as at the end of March 1995.** **The Governor stated that this could perhaps ee partly explained by the fact that there was** **an unprecedented increase in non-food credit during the second half of 1994-95.** **Nevertheless, the Governor stressed the urgent need for inreasing the flow of bank** **credit to the agricultural sector and exhorted the banks to identify the reasons for** **nonachievement of the targets set by some banks for credit to agricultural sector.**
**In their comments, banks pointed out that they were striving hard to improve the quality** **and quantum of agricultural credit. They stated that a factor constraining their** **achieving the credit disbursement projections was the low credit absorptive capacity of the** **core agricultural sector in certain areas.** **Bankers. however, expressed confidence that their performance in agricultural lending** **would be better during the current year than in 1994-95, largely due to the extra effort** **being put in by them.** **The Governor reiterated that banks must continue to provide focus to the agricultural** **sector and asked banks to prepare sectorwise and areawise detailed plans to bring about a** **positive increase in the flow of agricultural credit during 1995-96. He advised banks to** **prepare detailed plans along with sub-sector targets, for their internal use, based on their** **projections for 1995-96 and ensure effective follow-up and monitoring of these plans.**

**Regarding the performance of RRBs which were taken up for restructuring during I 994-** **95, the Governor was happy to note that the policy measures initiated by the Reserve Bank** **since December 1993 were bringing about an improvement in the financial indicators of 49** **RRBs. He, however, stressed the need for concerted attention on the part of sponsor** **banks to the management aspects of the restructuring process.**

**The banks felt that initiatives taken with regard to the co-operative sector regarding** **freeing of interest rates could be a desirable move as regards RRBs also. Banks were** **confident that together with focussed attention on the managerial aspects, the recent** **policy measures would considerably enhance the prospects of viability of the RRBs.** **Concluding the discussions on revamping of** **RRBs. the Governor urged the chief executives of banks to take direct and** **personal interest in the appointment of people**
