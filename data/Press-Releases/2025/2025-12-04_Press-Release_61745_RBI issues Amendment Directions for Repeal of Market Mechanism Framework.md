## December 04, 2025

## RBI issues Amendment Directions for Repeal of Market Mechanism

## Framework

The Reserve Bank of India had, on October 1, 2025, issued the <u>draft ‘Guidelines</u> <u>on Enhancing Credit Supply for Large Borrowers through Market Mechanism</u> <u>(Repeal Circular), 2025</u>, seeking feedback from the stakeholders on proposal to repeal the extant instructions issued in 2016.

2. Feedback received on the above draft Repeal Circular has been examined and it has been decided to repeal the extant instructions which were subsequently consolidated in (i) Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Concentration Risk Management) Directions, 2025; and (ii) Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Concentration Risk Management) Directions, 2025. A statement on the feedback received for the draft Directions is provided in the <u>Annex</u>**.**
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India has today issued the following Amendment Directions to repeal the ‘Guidelines on Enhancing Credit Supply for Large Borrowers through Market Mechanism’ as consolidated in Directions mentioned above:
1) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Concentration Risk Management)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2025</u>
2) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Concentration Risk Management)</u> <u>Amendment Directions, 2025</u>
