## December 02, 2025

## Alteration in the name of “Uttar Pradesh Cooperative Bank Limited, Lucknow”

## to “Uttar Pradesh State Cooperative Bank Limited, Lucknow” in the Second

## Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934

The name of “Uttar Pradesh Cooperative Bank Limited, Lucknow” has been changed to “Uttar Pradesh State Cooperative Bank Limited, Lucknow” in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 by Notification CO.DOR.RAUG.No.S4790/07.12.000/2025-2026 dated September 17, 2025, which is published in the Gazette of India (Part III-Section 4) dated October 24, 2025.
