## September 23, 2025

## Voluntary Surrender of CoR by NBFCs (including HFCs) for cancellation –

## Application form and Indicative checklist

The Reserve Bank had issued a <u>press release dated December 01, 2022</u>, on Voluntary Surrender of Certificate of Registration (CoR) by NBFCs (including HFCs) for cancellation enclosing application form and indicative checklist.

The Reserve Bank has since launched the PRAVAAH portal on <u>May 28, 2024</u>, to make it convenient for any individual or entity to apply online for various regulatory approvals and clearances in a seamless manner. As the application for voluntary surrender of CoR for cancellation is available in the said portal, the applicant NBFC (including HFCs) may upload the <u>application form along with the documents</u> <u>complete in all respects as indicated in the checklist</u>.

It may be noted that mere submission of application with documents by a company cannot be treated as cancellation of CoR. The NBFC/HFC needs to continue adhering to the guidelines/instructions issued by the RBI/NHB/other competent authority, etc. and also continue submitting requisite regulatory/supervisory returns, as applicable, until the CoR is cancelled and the decision is communicated by the Reserve Bank to the entity concerned.
