## October 13, 2025

## Launching of 112

**th** **Round of Quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) – Q3:2025-26**

The Reserve Bank has launched the <u>112</u> <u>th</u> <u>round</u> of the quarterly Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) of the Indian manufacturing sector for the reference period October- December 2025. The survey assesses business sentiments for the current quarter (Q3:2025-26) and expectations for the ensuing quarter (Q4:2025-26), based on qualitative responses on a set of indicators pertaining to demand conditions, financial conditions, employment conditions and the price situation. The outlook on key parameters for the two subsequent quarters (Q1:2026-27 and Q2:2026-27) are also covered.

2. <u>The survey findings are released in summary form on the website of the Bank</u> <u>regularly, where identity of the respondents is not revealed</u>.
3. M/s Genesis Management & Market Research Pvt. Ltd. has been authorised to conduct the survey for October-December 2025 on behalf of the Reserve Bank. While the agency will approach selected companies, other manufacturing companies are also encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey questionnaire</u> from the Bank’s website, under the sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’. The duly authenticated filled-in survey questionnaire may be e-mailed as per contact details given therein.
4. In case of any query/clarification, kindly contact us at the following address:
