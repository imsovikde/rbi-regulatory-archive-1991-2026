## April 28, 2025

||RBI announces OMO Purchase of Government of India Securities||
|---|---|---|
||On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank has decided to conduct OMO purchase auctions of Government of India securities for an aggregate amount of ₹1,25,000 crore in four tranches as follows:||
|S. No.|Auction Date|Amount (in ₹ crore)|
|1|May 6, 2025|50,000|
|2|May 9, 2025|25,000|
|3|May 15, 2025|25,000|
|4|May 19, 2025 2. Detailed instructions for each operation shall be issued separately. 3. The Reserve Bank will continue to monitor evolving liquidity and market conditions and take measures as appropriate to ensure orderly liquidity conditions.|25,000|
