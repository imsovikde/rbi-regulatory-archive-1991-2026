## March 26, 2025

## Participation of Standalone Primary Dealers in Variable Rate Repo operations

In terms of the paragraph 1(x) of the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory</u> <u>Policies dated February 06, 2020</u>, Standalone Primary Dealers (SPDs) were allowed to participate in all overnight liquidity management operations (except Marginal Standing Facility) under the current <u>Liquidity Management Framework dated</u> <u>February 06, 2020</u>. SPDs were also allowed to participate in other operations such as long-term Variable Rate Repo (VRR) operations and daily VRRs on a case-to-case basis.

2. On a review, it has now been decided to allow SPDs to participate in all Repo operations, irrespective of the tenor, conducted by the Reserve Bank.
