## September 25, 2025

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹32,000 crore on September 26, 2025

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auctions to be held on **September 26, 2025 (Friday)**.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting commitment notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore) **Minimum bidding** **Notified MUC amount per** **Security commitment per PD** **Amount PD under ACU auction**

6.68% GS 2040 16,000 381 381
6.90% GS 2065 16,000 381 381
The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **September 26, 2025 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
