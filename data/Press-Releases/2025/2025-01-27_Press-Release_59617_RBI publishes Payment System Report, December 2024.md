## January 27, 2025

# RBI publishes Payment System Report, December 2024

The Reserve Bank has today published the <u>Payment System Report, December 2024</u>. This report, in addition to analysing the trends in payment transactions carried out using various payment systems in India during the last five calendar years up to CY-2024, covers important developments in the payment ecosystem and provides an in-depth analysis into UPI. Henceforth, the Report will be published on RBI website on half yearly basis.
