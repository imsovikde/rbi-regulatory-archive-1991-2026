## Meeting Schedule of the Monetary Policy Committee for 2025-2026

As per Section 45ZI of Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, it has been decided that the Monetary Policy Committee will meet during the 2025-26 on the dates indicated below:

|Dates of meetings of Monetary Policy Committee for 2025-26|
|---|
|April 7, 8 and 9, 2025|
|June 4, 5 and 6, 2025|
|August 5, 6 and 7, 2025|
|September 29, 30 and October 1, 2025|
|December 3, 4 and 5, 2025|
|February 4, 5 and 6, 2026|
