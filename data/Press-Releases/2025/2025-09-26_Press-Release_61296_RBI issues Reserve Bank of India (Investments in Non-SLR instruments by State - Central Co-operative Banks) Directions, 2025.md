## September 26, 2025

## RBI issues Reserve Bank of India (Investments in Non-SLR instruments by

## State / Central Co-operative Banks) Directions, 2025

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has today issued <u>Reserve Bank of India</u> <u>(Investments in Non-SLR instruments by State / Central Co-operative Banks)</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u>.

2. Reserve Bank has accorded regulatory approval in April 2025 to NABARD’s proposal for setting up of a Shared Service Entity (SSE) for StCBs and CCBs wherein it is envisaged that the StCBs and CCBs can subscribe to the share capital of the SSE on a voluntary basis. In this context, the extant instructions on investment in non-SLR instruments by StCBs/ CCBs issued vide <u>circular dated July 14, 2016</u> are being suitably amended.
3. The Directions, *inter alia,* cover the following with respect to investment in the capital of SSE:
a. Inclusion of the capital of SSE as permissible non-SLR instrument
b. Exemption from prudential limits
c. Specifying an exposure limit
4. These Directions shall come into effect immediately.
