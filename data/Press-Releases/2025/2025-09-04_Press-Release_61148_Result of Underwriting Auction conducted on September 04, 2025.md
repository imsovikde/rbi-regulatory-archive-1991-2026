## Result of Underwriting Auction conducted on September 04, 2025

In the underwriting auction conducted on September 04, 2025, for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|Notified Amount (₹ crore)|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount (₹ crore)|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted (₹ crore)|Total Amount underwritten (₹ crore)|
|---|---|---|---|
|11,000|5,502|5,498|11,000|
|14,000|7,014|6,986|14,000|

||ACU||
|---|---|---|
|Nomenclature of|Commission||
|the Security|Cut-off rate (Paise per ₹100)||
|6.28% GS 2032||0.49|
|7.09% GS 2074||1.20|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on September 04, 2025.
