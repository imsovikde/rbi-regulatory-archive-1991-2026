## December 05, 2025

## RBI issues Amendment Directions for Reserve Bank of India (Undertaking of Financial Services) Directions, 2025

The Reserve Bank of India had issued <u>draft circular on ‘Forms of Business and</u> <u>Prudential Regulation for Investments’ on October 04, 2024</u>, seeking feedback from banks and other stakeholders. The objective of the draft circular was to review regulations on ringfencing of banks’ core business from other risk bearing non-core businesses as also to provide level playing field.

2. Feedback received on the draft has been examined and suitably incorporated in the final directions. A statement on the feedback received for the draft circular is provided in the <u>Annex</u>.
3. Accordingly, the Reserve Bank of India has today issued the following five Amendment Directions to amend the extant instructions on Undertaking of Financial services by various regulated entities.
(1) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Undertaking of Financial Services</u>) <u>Amendment Directions, 2025</u>.
(2) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks – Undertaking of Financial Services</u>) <u>Amendment Directions, 2025</u>.
(3) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks – Undertaking of Financial Services</u>) <u>Amendment Directions, 2025</u>.
(4) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Undertaking of</u> <u>Financial Services) Amendment Directions, 2025</u>.
(5) <u>Reserve Bank of India (Non-Operative Financial Holding Companies) Amendment</u> <u>Directions, 2025</u>.
