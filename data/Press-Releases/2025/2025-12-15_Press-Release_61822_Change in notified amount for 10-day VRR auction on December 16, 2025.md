## December 15, 2025

## Change in notified amount for 10-day VRR auction on December 16, 2025

Please refer to the <u>press release 2025-2026/1697 dated December 12, 2025</u>. On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to increase the notified amount for the 10-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction scheduled to be conducted on December 16, 2025, from ₹75,000 crore to ₹1,50,000 crore.
