## RBI to conduct 8-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction under LAF on March 13, 2025

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to conduct a Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction on March 13, 2025, Thursday, as under:

## Sl. No. Notified Amount Tenor Window Timing Date of Reversal

## (₹ crore) (day)

1 1,00,000 8 11:00 AM to 11:30 AM March 21, 2025 (Friday)
