## September 01, 2025

## Sources of Variation in India’s Foreign Exchange Reserves during April-June 2025

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released the <u>balance of payments (BoP) data</u> <u>for the first quarter (Q1), i.e., April-June of 2025-26</u> on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). On the basis of these data, the sources of variation in foreign exchange reserves during April-June 2025-26 are detailed below in Table 1.

On a balance of payments basis (*i.e.*, excluding valuation effects), foreign exchange reserves increased by US$ 4.5 billion during April-June 2025 as compared to an accretion of US$ 5.2 billion during April-June 2024. Foreign exchange reserves in nominal terms (*i.e.*, including valuation effects) increased by US$ 29.8 billion

The valuation gain, primarily reflecting depreciation of US dollar against major currencies and higher price of gold, amounted to US$ 25.3 billion during April-June 2025 as compared with a valuation gain of US$ 0.4 billion during April-June 2024.
