## November 11, 2025

## Recognition of Self-Regulatory Organisation for Payment System Operators

The Reserve Bank of India issued the <u>‘Framework for Recognition of Self-</u> <u>Regulatory Organisation (SRO) for Payment System Operators (PSOs)’ on October</u> <u>22, 2020</u>, and an <u>‘Omnibus framework for recognition of SROs for Regulated Entities</u> <u>of the Reserve Bank’ on March 21, 2024</u> inviting interested groups / associations to seek recognition as SRO in terms of the criteria laid down therein.

2. Subsequently, an application was received from ‘Self-Regulated PSO Association (SRPA)’ seeking to serve as an SRO for PSOs.
3. The application has been duly considered and it has been decided to recognise SRPA as an SRO for PSOs.
