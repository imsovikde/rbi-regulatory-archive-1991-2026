## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹22,000 crore on January 10, 2025

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **January 10, 2025 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) System between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
