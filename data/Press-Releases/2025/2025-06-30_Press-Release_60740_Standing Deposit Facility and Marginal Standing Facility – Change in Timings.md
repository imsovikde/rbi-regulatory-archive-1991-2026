## June 30, 2025

## Standing Deposit Facility and Marginal Standing Facility – Change in Timings

At present, Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) are available between 5:30 PM and 11:59 PM on all days.

2. <u>In light of the extension of market timings for call money to 7:00 PM</u>, it has been decided that the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) will be available between 7:00 PM and 11:59 PM with effect from July 01,
2025.
3. All other terms and conditions of the current SDF and MSF schemes remain unchanged.
