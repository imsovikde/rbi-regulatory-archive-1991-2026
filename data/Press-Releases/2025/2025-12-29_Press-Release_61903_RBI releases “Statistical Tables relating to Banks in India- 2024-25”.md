## December 29, 2025

## RBI releases “Statistical Tables relating to Banks in India: 2024-25”

Today, the Reserve Bank of India released its web publication entitled ‘<u>Statistical</u> <u>Tables relating to Banks in India: 2024-25</u>’ covering the activities of the Indian banking sector.

2. The publication presents entity-wise information on major items of liabilities and assets along with their maturity profile; income and expenses; select financial ratios, number of employees and details of priority sector advances for scheduled commercial banks (excluding regional rural banks). In addition, capital to risk weighted asset ratios (CRARs), non-performing assets (NPAs), exposure to sensitive sectors, contingent liabilities and unclaimed deposits are included. State wise distribution of consolidated balance sheet of rural co-operative banks are also presented.
3. The publication can be accessed on the RBI’s Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) (<u>[https://data.rbi.org.in](https://data.rbi.org.in)</u>) portal under the ‘<u>Publications</u>’ tab, where time series on all the variables have been updated up to 2024-25.
