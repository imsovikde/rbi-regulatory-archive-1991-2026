## Regulatory Sandbox: Fifth Cohort on Theme ‘Neutral’ –– Exit

In the Fifth Cohort of the Regulatory Sandbox (RS) which was ‘Theme Neutral’, five entities were shortlisted to commence testing of their products, which was communicated vide <u>Press Release dated July 26, 2024</u>.
