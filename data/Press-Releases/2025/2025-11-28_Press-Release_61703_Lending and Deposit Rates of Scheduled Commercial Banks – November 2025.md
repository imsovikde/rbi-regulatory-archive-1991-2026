November 28, 2025 **Lending and Deposit Rates of Scheduled Commercial Banks – November 2025** Data on <u>lending and deposit rates</u> of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs) (excluding regional rural banks and small finance banks) received during the month of November 2025 are set out in Tables 1 to 7. **Highlights:** **Lending Rates:**

- The weighted average lending rate (WALR) on fresh rupee loans of SCBs stood at
8.64 per cent in October 2025 (8.50 per cent in September 2025).
- The WALR on outstanding rupee loans of SCBs declined to 9.24 per cent in October 2025 from 9.26 per cent in September 2025.
- 1-Year median Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate (MCLR) of SCBs moderated to 8.50 per cent in November 2025 from 8.55 per cent in October 2025.
## Deposit Rates:

- The weighted average domestic term deposit rate (WADTDR) on fresh rupee term deposits of SCBs stood at 5.57 per cent in October 2025 (5.61 per cent in September 2025).
- The WADTDR on outstanding rupee term deposits of SCBs was 6.78 per cent in October 2025 (6.82 per cent in September 2025).
