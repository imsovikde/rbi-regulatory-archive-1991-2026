February 12, 2025 **Credit line on UPI – Extending the scope of Small Finance Banks (SFBs)** Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website an updated circular on <u>Operation of Pre-Sanctioned Credit Lines at Banks through Unified Payments</u> <u>Interface (UPI)</u>. As announced in the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies dated</u> <u>December 06, 2024</u>, the scope of credit on UPI is extended to SFBs as well.
