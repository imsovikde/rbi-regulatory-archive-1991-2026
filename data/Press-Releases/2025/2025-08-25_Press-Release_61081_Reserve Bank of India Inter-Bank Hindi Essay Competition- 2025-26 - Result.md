## August 25, 2025

## Reserve Bank of India Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition: 2025-26 - Result

With a view to encourage writing originally in Hindi on the subjects of Economics/ Finance/Banking and general interest, Reserve Bank of India had invited entries for Inter-Bank Hindi Essay competition for the year 2025-26 vide <u>press release dated 30</u> <u>May 2025</u> in which Officers and Staff members (Other than Rajbhasha Officers and Translators) of Reserve Bank of India, Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) sponsored by them have participated. The result of the above- mentioned competition is <u>attached</u> herewith.
