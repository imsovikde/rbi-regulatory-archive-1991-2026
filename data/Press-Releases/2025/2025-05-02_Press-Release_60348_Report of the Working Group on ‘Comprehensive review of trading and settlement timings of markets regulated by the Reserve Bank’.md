## May 02, 2025

**Report of the Working Group on ‘Comprehensive review of trading and settlement timings of markets regulated by the Reserve Bank’**

The Reserve Bank set up a <u>Working Group to undertake a comprehensive review</u> <u>of trading and settlement timings of markets regulated by the Reserve Bank</u>, as announced in the <u>‘Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies’ dated</u> <u>February 07, 2025</u>. The Working Group was chaired by Shri Radha Shyam Ratho, Executive Director, RBI. It was tasked with reviewing existing market timings, identifying operational challenges, examining global practices and recommending the way forward.

The Working Group has submitted its <u>report</u> and the same is being placed on the RBI website today. Comments on the Report are invited from stakeholders and members of the public by May 30, 2025 through <u>email</u>. The comments will be examined before a final view is taken on the recommendations made by the Working Group.
