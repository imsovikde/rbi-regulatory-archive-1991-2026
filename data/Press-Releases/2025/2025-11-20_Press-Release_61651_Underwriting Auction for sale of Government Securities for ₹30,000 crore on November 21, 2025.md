## November 20, 2025

## Underwriting Auction for sale of Government Securities for ₹30,000 crore on November 21, 2025

Government of India has announced the sale (re-issue) of Government Securities, as detailed below, through auction to be held on **November 21, 2025 (Friday)**.

As per the extant scheme of underwriting commitment notified on November 14, 2007, the amounts of Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) and the minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction, applicable to each Primary Dealer (PD), are as under: (₹ crore)

|Security|Notified Amount|MUC amount per PD|Minimum bidding commitment per PD under ACU auction|
|---|---|---|---|
|6.01% GS 2030|18,000|429|429|
|7.09% GS 2074|12,000|286|286|

The underwriting auction will be conducted through multiple price-based method on **November 21, 2025 (Friday)**. PDs may submit their bids for ACU auction electronically through the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber system) between **09:00 A.M.** and **09:30 A.M.** on the day of underwriting auction.

The underwriting commission will be credited to the current account of the respective PDs with RBI on the day of issue of securities.
