## June 30, 2025

## Lending and Deposit Rates of Scheduled Commercial Banks – June 2025

Data on <u>lending and deposit rates</u> of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs) (excluding regional rural banks and small finance banks) received during the month of June 2025 are set out in Tables 1 to 7.

## Highlights:

## Lending Rates:

- The weighted average lending rate (WALR) on fresh rupee loans of SCBs declined to 9.20 per cent in May 2025 from 9.26 per cent in April 2025.
- The WALR on outstanding rupee loans of SCBs dropped marginally to 9.69 per
