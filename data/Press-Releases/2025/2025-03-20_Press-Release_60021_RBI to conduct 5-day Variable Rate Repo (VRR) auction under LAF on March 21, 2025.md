## March 21, 2025

On a review of current and evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank of India has decided to conduct a 5-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction on March 21, 2025. It has been decided not to conduct the 14-day main operation for the ensuing fortnight.
