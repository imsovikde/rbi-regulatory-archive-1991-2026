## October 03, 2025

## Draft Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a branch or office) Regulations, 2025

As announced in the <u>Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies dated</u> <u>October 01, 2025,</u> the Reserve Bank had issued the Foreign Exchange Management (Establishment in India of a Branch Office or a Liaison Office or a Project Office or any other place of business) Regulations, 2016, vide <u>Notification No. FEMA</u> <u>22(R)/2016-RB. dated March 31, 2016</u>.

2. On a review, it has been decided to amend the extant regulations along the following lines:
i) The eligibility criteria for establishment of a place of business in India, are proposed to be relaxed. ii) The draft proposals offer greater operational freedom by shifting from prescriptive to a principle-based framework, which is expected to result in greater operational freedom. iii) The process for closure of non-compliant and inactive branch/office, are proposed to be simplified.
3. Comments/feedback on the <u>draft regulations</u> may be submitted through the ‘<u>Connect 2 Regulate</u>’ Section available on the RBI’s website or via <u>email</u> by **October 24, 2025,** with the subject line “Feedback on draft regulations on Branch or office”.
