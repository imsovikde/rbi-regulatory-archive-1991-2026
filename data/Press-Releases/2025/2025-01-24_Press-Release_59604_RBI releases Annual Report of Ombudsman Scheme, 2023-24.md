## January 24, 2025

## RBI releases Annual Report of Ombudsman Scheme, 2023-24

The Reserve Bank of India released the <u>Annual Report of the Ombudsman Scheme for</u> <u>the period April 1, 2023 to March 31, 2024</u>. The Annual Report covers the activities under the <u>Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS), 2021</u> as well as major developments during the year in consumer education and protection and the way forward.
