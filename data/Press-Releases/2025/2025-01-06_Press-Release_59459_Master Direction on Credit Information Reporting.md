## January 06, 2025

## Master Direction on Credit Information Reporting

The Reserve Bank of India has today issued the <u>Master Direction – Reserve Bank</u> <u>of India (Credit Information Reporting) Directions, 2025</u>. The Master Direction consolidates the existing instructions on credit information reporting and dissemination, issued to the regulated entities (REs) of the Reserve Bank.
