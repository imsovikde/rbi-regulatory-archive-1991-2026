April 22, 2025 **Shri T. Rabi Sankar re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor** The Central Government has re-appointed Shri T. Rabi Sankar as Deputy Governor, Reserve Bank of India for a period of one year with effect from May 03, 2025, or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
