## September 11, 2025

**RBI to conduct 3-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction under LAF on September 12, 2025**

On a review of the current and evolving liquidity conditions, it has been decided to conduct a Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction on Friday, September 12, 2025, as under:

## Sl. No. Notified Amount Tenor Window Timing Date of Reversal

## (₹ crore) (day)

1 1,50,000 3 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM September 15, 2025 (Monday)

2. The operational guidelines for the auction as given in the Reserve Bank’s <u>Press</u> <u>Release 2019-2020/1947 dated February 13, 2020</u> will remain the same.
