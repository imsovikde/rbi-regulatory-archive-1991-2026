Such physical bid should be submitted to Financial Markets Operation Department (email, Phone no: 022-22630982) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website ([https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewForms.aspx)) before 11:30 am. 5. The result of the auctions will be announced on the same day and successful participants should ensure availability of securities in their SGL account by 12 noon on February 14, 2025.|
