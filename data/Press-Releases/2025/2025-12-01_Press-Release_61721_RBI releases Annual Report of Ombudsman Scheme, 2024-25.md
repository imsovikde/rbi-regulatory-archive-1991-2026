## December 01, 2025

## RBI releases Annual Report of Ombudsman Scheme, 2024-25

The Reserve Bank of India released the <u>Annual Report of the Ombudsman Scheme</u> <u>for the period April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025</u>. The Annual Report covers the activities under the <u>RB-IOS, 2021</u> as well as major developments during the year in consumer education and protection and the way forward.
