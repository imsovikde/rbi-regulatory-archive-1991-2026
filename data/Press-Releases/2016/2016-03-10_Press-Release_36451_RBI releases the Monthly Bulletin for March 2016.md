## RBI releases the Monthly Bulletin for March 2016

The Reserve Bank of India today released <u>March 2016</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The bulletin includes Speeches by the Top Management and Current Statistics. This issue also includes <u>Report of the Advisory Committee on Ways and</u> <u>Means Advances to State Governments</u>.
