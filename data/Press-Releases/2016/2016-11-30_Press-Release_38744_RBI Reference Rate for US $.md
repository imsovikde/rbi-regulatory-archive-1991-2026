November 30, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.5260 on November 30, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 29, 2016) was

68.6535. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 29, 2016 November 30, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.8002 72.8431 **1 GBP** 85.2265 85.5342 **100 YEN** 61.25 60.79 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
