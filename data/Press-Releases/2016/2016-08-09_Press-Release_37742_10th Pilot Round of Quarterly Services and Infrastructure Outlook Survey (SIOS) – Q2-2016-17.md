## August 09, 2016

**th** **10 Pilot Round of Quarterly Services and** **Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS) – Q2:2016-17**

The Services and Infrastructure sectors play a pivotal role in economic development. Considering the increasing importance of these sectors, the Reserve Bank of India has initiated a regular survey to assess the business sentiments in th Services and Infrastructure Sectors. The 10 pilot round of the <u>Services and</u> <u>Infrastructure Outlook Survey</u>, which seeks assessment of the business situation for the current quarter (July-September 2016 i.e. Q2:2016-17) from the services and infrastructure companies in India, and their perceptions for the ensuing quarter (October-December 2016 i.e. Q3:2016-17). The survey schedule is very short and simple, containing qualitative questions covering a set of seven business indicators, namely, overall business situation, turnover, part-time employees, cost of finance, availability of finance, cost of inputs and profit margin.

Companies in the Services and Infrastructure Sector are encouraged to participate in the survey by downloading the <u>survey schedule</u> from the Reserve Bank’s website <u>www.rbi.org.in</u>. The duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be e-mailed or faxed as per contact details given in the survey schedule. The responses of the individual companies will be kept strictly confidential.
