## March 30, 2016

# RBI announces Results of Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition 2015-16

With a view to encouraging original writing in Hindi on banking subjects, the Reserve Bank of India conducted its annual <u>Inter-Bank Hindi Essay competition for</u> <u>2015-16</u> in which Staff Members (Except Rajbhasha Officers and Translators) of Public Sector Banks and Financial Institutions participated. The results of the Competition are:
