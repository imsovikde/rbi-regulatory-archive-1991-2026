July 14, 2016 **Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on July 14, 2016** In the underwriting auctions conducted on July 14, 2016 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

||||||(|crore)|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Nomenclature of the Security|Notified Amount|Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Additional Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Total Amount underwritten||ACU Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|
|7.35% GS 2024|3000|1512|1488|3000||0.09|
|7.61% GS 2030|8000|4011|3989|8000||0.15|
|7.50% GS 2034|2000|1008|992|2000||0.33|
|8.17% GS 2044|2000|1008|992|2000||0.37|

Auction for the sale of securities will be held on July 15, 2016.
