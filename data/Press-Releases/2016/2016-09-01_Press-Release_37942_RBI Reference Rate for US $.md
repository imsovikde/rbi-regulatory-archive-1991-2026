September 01, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9539 on September 01, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 31, 2016) was

66.9813. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 31, 2016 September 01, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6239 74.6603 **1 GBP** 87.6852 88.0511 **100 YEN** 64.89 64.78 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
