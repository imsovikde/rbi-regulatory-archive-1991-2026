## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

|Sl. No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount ` Billion) (|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|September 30, 2016 (Friday)|195|14|October 14, 2016 (Friday)|
|2|October 04, 2016 (Tuesday)|190|14|October 18, 2016 (Tuesday)|
|3|October 07, 2016 (Friday)|190|14|October 21, 2016 (Friday)|
|4|October 10, 2016 (Monday)|190|15|October 25, 2016 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
