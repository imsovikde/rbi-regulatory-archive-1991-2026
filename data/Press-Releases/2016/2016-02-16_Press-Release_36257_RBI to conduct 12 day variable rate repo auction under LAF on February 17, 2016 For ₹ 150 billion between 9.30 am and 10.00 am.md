## RBI to conduct 12 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on February 17, 2016

**For** ` **150 billion between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m.**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 12 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `150 billion on February 17, 2016, Wednesday. The auction will be conducted between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 12 day variable rate repo will take place on February 29, 2016 (Monday).
