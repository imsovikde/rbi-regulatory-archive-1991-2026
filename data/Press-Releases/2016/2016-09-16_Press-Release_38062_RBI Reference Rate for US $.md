September 16, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8804 on September 16, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 15, 2016) was

67.0248. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 15, 2016 September 16, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.3627 75.1535 **1 GBP** 88.7073 88.4694 **100 YEN** 65.58 65.52 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
