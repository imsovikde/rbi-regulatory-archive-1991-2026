May 25, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.4467 on May 25, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 24, 2016) was

67.7060. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 24, 2016 May 25, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9323 75.2098 **1 GBP** 98.0654 98.5599 **100 YEN** 61.97 61.32 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
