July 07, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.4972 on July 07, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 05, 2016) was

67.4028. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 05, 2016 July 07, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0328 74.8274 **1 GBP** 89.1537 87.6114 **100 YEN** 66.06 67.00 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
