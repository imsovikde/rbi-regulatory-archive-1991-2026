November 01, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7140 on November 01, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 28, 2016) was

66.8566. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 28, 2016 November 01, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.9071 73.1786 **1 GBP** 81.2976 81.5579 **100 YEN** 63.44 63.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
