## October 04, 2016

**RBI’s Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction for 800 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (October 04, 2016, Tuesday) between 4.00 pm to 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (Billion)|Tenor (day)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|500|1|October 05, 2016 (Wednesday)|
|2|300|13|October 17, 2016 (Monday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
