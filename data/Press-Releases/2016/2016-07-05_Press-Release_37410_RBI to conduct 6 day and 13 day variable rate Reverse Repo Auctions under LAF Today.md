## July 05, 2016

## RBI to conduct 6 day and 13 day variable rate

## Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (July 05, 2016, Tuesday) between 01.00 pm and 01.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Sl No. Notified Amount Tenor Date of Reversal

## ( Billion)

1. 250 6 July 11, 2016 (Monday)
2. 250 13 July 18, 2016 (Monday) Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers
at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
