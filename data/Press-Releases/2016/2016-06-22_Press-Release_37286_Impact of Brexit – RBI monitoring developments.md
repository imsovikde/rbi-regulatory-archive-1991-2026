## June 22, 2016

## Impact of Brexit – RBI monitoring developments

In the run up to the referendum in the United Kingdom on its continuing in the European Union (“The Brexit”), uncertainty about the poll outcome has resulted in some amount of turbulence in global financial markets, including in India. The RBI is maintaining a close vigil on developments, and will take all necessary steps, including liquidity support, to ensure orderly conditions in financial markets’.
