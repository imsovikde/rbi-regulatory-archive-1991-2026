## September 26, 2016

**RBI’s Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction for 400 billion between 4 pm and 4.30 pm Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (September 26, 2016, Monday) between 4.00 pm to 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Sl||Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.|(|. Billion)|(day)||
|1||200|1|September 27, 2016 (Tuesday)|
|2||200|4|September 30, 2016 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
