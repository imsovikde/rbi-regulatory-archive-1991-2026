## May 10, 2016

## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

## For 200 billion between 10.30 am and 11.00 am

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 200 billion today (May 10, 2016, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **10.30 am and 11.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on May 17, 2016 (Tuesday).
