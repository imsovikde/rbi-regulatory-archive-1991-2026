March 16, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3680 on March 16, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 15, 2016) was

67.2290. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 15, 2016 March 16, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6444 74.7583 **1 GBP** 95.9223 95.1708 **100 YEN** 59.31 59.40 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
