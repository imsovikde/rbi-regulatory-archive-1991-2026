November 21, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.2575 on November 21, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 18, 2016) was

68.0937. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 18, 2016 November 21, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.2134 72.3530 **1 GBP** 84.4158 84.2025 **100 YEN** 61.56 61.45 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
