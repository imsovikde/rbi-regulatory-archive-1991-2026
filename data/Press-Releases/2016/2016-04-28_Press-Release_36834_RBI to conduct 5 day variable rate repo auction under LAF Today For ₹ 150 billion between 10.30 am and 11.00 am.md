## RBI to conduct 5 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **150 billion between 10.30 am and 11.00 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 5 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `150 billion today (April 28, 2016, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between **10.30 am and 11.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 5 day variable rate repo will take place on May 3, 2016 (Tuesday).
