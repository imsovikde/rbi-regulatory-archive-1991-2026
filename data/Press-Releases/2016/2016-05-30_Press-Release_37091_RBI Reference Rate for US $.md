May 30, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3398 on May 30, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 27, 2016) was

67.0613. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 27, 2016 May 30, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0885 74.8415 **1 GBP** 98.3923 98.4373 **100 YEN** 61.12 60.49 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
