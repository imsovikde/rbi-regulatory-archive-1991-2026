March 29, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5580 on March 29, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 28, 2016) was

66.6680. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 28, 2016 March 29, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.4215 74.4717 **1 GBP** 94.3019 94.6654 **100 YEN** 58.72 58.58 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
