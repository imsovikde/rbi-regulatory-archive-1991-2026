August 19, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9296 on August 19, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 18, 2016) was

66.7939. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 18, 2016 August 19, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.5773 75.8714 **1 GBP** 87.2596 87.9723 **100 YEN** 66.84 66.82 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
