December 28, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.2250 on December 28, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 27, 2016) was

67.9967. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 27, 2016 December 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 71.0498 71.4520 **1 GBP** 83.4999 83.8622 **100 YEN** 57.97 58.06 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
