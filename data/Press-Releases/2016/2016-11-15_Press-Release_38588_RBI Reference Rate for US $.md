November 15, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7173 on November 15, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 11, 2016) was

67.0292. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 11, 2016 November 15, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.1155 72.7758 **1 GBP** 84.1753 84.4232 **100 YEN** 62.94 62.61 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
