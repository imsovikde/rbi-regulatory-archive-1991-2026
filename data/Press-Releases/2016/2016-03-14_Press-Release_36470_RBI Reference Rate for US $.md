March 14, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.0230 on March 14, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 11, 2016) was

67.0868. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 11, 2016 March 14, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.9427 74.8044 **1 GBP** 95.7530 96.3389 **100 YEN** 59.13 58.90 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
