March 04, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.2713 on March 04, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 03, 2016) was

67.3786. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 03, 2016 March 04, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.1664 73.6688 **1 GBP** 94.8960 95.2158 **100 YEN** 59.05 59.10 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
