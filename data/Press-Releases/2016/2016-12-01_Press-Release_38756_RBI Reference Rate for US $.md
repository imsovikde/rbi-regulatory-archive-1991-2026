December 01, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.3734 on December 01, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 30, 2016) was

68.5260. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 30, 2016 December 01, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.8431 72.6262 **1 GBP** 85.5342 85.6992 **100 YEN** 60.79 60.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
