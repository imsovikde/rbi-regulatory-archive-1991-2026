## RBI to conduct 3 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF Today for

` **500 billion between 10.30 am and 11.00 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auction for a notified amount of ` 500 billion today (December 23, 2016, Friday). The auction will be conducted between **10.30 am and 11.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day Variable rate Reverse Repo will take place on December 26, 2016 (Monday).
