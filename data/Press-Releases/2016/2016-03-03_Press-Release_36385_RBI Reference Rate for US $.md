March 03, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3786 on March 03, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 02, 2016) was

67.7022. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 02, 2016 March 03, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.5314 73.1664 **1 GBP** 94.6003 94.8960 **100 YEN** 59.42 59.05 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
