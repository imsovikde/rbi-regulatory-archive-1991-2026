## August 16, 2016

## RBI to conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

**for 400 billion (instead of 200 billion as announced earlier) between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

In view of the evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank of India will conduct 2 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of Rs.400 billion *(instead of Rs.200 billion as announced earlier)* today (<u>August 16, 2016, Tuesday</u>). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 2 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on August 18, 2016 (Thursday).
