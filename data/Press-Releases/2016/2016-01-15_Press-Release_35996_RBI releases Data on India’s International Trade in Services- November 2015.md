January 15, 2016 **RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: November 2015** The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of November 2015 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** (US$ Million) **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)** October 2015 13,344 7,013 November 2015 12,019 5,686

<u>Note: Data are provisional.</u>

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
