October 26, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7621 on October 26, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 25, 2016) was

66.8777. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 25, 2016 October 26, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.7897 72.8174 **1 GBP** 81.7714 81.3630 **100 YEN** 64.06 64.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
