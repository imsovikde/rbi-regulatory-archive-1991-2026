May 02, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3435 on May 02, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 29, 2016) was

66.5176. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 29, 2016 May 02, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.7303 76.0562 **1 GBP** 97.4017 97.0207 **100 YEN** 61.96 62.31 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
