## RBI to conduct 6 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **250 billion between 3.00 pm and 3.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 6 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of ` 250 billion today (August 02, 2016, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **3.00 pm and 3.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 6 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on August 8, 2016 (Monday).
