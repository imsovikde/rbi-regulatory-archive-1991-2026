December 09, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.5840 on December 09, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 08, 2016) was

67.4325. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 08, 2016 December 09, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.6113 71.7607 **1 GBP** 85.2414 85.1289 **100 YEN** 59.44 59.06 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
