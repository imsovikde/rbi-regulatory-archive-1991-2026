## Last Date for RBI’s Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition 2016-17 extended

The Reserve Bank of India has extended the last date for submission of entries for its Inter-Bank Hindi Essay Competition - 2016-17 to January 13, 2017 (Friday). Earlier, the last date for entries to the competition was November 30, 2016 (Wednesday). For more details please see <u>Press Release: 2016-2017/540</u>.
