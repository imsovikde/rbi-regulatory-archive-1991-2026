December 29, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.1241 on December 29, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 28, 2016) was

68.2250. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 28, 2016 December 29, 2016** **1 EUR** 71.4520 71.2101 **1 GBP** 83.8622 83.4180 **100 YEN** 58.06 58.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
