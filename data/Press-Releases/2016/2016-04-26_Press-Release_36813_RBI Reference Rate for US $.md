April 26, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7330 on April 26, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 25, 2016) was

66.6839. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 25, 2016 April 26, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.9527 75.1814 **1 GBP** 96.3249 96.7695 **100 YEN** 59.99 60.22 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
