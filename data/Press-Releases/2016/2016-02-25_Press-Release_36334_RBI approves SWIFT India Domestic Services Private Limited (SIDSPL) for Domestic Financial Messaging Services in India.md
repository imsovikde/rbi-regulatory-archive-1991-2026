## February 25, 2016

**RBI approves SWIFT India Domestic Services Private Limited (SIDSPL) for Domestic Financial Messaging Services in India**

The Reserve Bank of India has granted approval to SWIFT India Domestic Services Private Limited (SIDSPL) to provide messaging services for domestic financial transactions in India.

## Background

The decision to permit the use of SWIFT for domestic financial transactions was announced in the <u>Monetary Policy Statement 2012-13</u>. Currently, the messaging for inter-bank transactions in India is through Structured Financial Messaging System (SFMS). Messaging operations of SWIFT will support and strengthen the infrastructure for financial transactions in the country.
