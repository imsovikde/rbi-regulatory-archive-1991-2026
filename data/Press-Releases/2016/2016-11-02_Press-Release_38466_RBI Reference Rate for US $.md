## RBI Reference Rate for US $

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8294 on November 02, 2016.

The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 01, 2016) was

66.7140. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below:
