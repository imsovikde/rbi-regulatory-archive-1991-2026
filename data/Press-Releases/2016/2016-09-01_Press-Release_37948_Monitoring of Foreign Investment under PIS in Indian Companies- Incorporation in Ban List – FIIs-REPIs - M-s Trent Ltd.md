## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List – FIIs/REPIs : M/s Trent Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in <u>M/s Trent Ltd.</u> has crossed the limit of 24 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through Stock Exchanges in India on behalf of FIIs/RFPIs.
