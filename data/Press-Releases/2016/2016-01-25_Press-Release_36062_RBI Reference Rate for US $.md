January 25, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.6428 on January 25, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 22, 2016) was

67.7480. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 22, 2016 January 25, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.4050 73.1219 **1 GBP** 96.4461 96.7360 **100 YEN** 57.38 56.93 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
