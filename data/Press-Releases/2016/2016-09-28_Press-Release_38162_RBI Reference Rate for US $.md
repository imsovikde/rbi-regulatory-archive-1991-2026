66.4627. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 27, 2016 September 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.7373 74.4552 **1 GBP** 86.3417 86.4823 **100 YEN** 65.84 66.09 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
