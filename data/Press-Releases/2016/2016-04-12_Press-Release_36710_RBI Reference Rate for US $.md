April 12, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4950 on April 12, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 11, 2016) was

66.3925. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 11, 2016 April 12, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.7538 75.9107 **1 GBP** 93.7860 94.7487 **100 YEN** 61.53 61.38 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
