April 20, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.2406 on April 20, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 18, 2016) was

66.6663. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 18, 2016 April 20, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.2596 75.2758 **1 GBP** 94.3928 95.2076 **100 YEN** 61.73 60.82 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
