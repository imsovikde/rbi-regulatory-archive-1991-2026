## Reserve Bank releases the results of Survey of Professional

## Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 40

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators -Round 40</u> conducted during May 2016.

The results of the Survey represent views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
