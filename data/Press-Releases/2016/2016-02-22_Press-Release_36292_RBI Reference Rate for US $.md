68.4940. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **3February 18, 2016 4February 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.3297 76.1952 **1 GBP** 97.8642 97.8438 **100 YEN** 60.15 60.75 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
