## June 16, 2016

## RBI to conduct 14 day variable rate repo auction under

## LAF today for 150 billion between 12.30 p.m. and 1.00 p.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 14 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 150 billion today (June 16, 2016, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between 12.30 p.m. and 1.00 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 14 day variable rate repo will take place on June 30, 2016 (Thursday).
