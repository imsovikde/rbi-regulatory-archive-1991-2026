October 20, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7411 on October 20, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 19, 2016) was

66.7145. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 19, 2016 October 20, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.2125 73.1482 **1 GBP** 81.7853 81.9114 **100 YEN** 64.40 64.35 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
