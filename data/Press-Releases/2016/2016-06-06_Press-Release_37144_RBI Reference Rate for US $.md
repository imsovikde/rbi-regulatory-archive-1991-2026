June 06, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9614 on June 06, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 03, 2016) was

67.2415. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 03, 2016 June 06, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.9944 75.9409 **1 GBP** 96.9017 96.1700 **100 YEN** 61.83 62.60 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
