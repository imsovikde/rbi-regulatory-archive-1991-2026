November 23, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.4772 on November 23, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 22, 2016) was

68.2317. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 22, 2016 November 23, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.4348 72.7844 **1 GBP** 85.1668 84.9665 **100 YEN** 61.46 61.71 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
