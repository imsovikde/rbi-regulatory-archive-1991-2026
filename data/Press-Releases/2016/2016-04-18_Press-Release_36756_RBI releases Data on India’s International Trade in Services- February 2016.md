April 18, 2016 **RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: February 2016** The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days. The value of exports and imports of services during the month of February 2016 are given in the following Table. **Table: International Trade in Services** (US$ Million) **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

|January 2016|12,573|6,841|
|---|---|---|
|February 2016 Note : Data are provisional.|12,329|7,189|

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
