May 27, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India's Reference rate for the US Dollar is ₹ 67.0613 on May 27, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 26, 2016) was ₹ 67.2855. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the cross-currency quotes, the exchange rates of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 26, 2016 May 27, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.2185 75.0885 **1 GBP** 98.9501 98.3923 **100 YEN** 61.34 61.12 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
