## September 02, 2016

## Private Placement of Special Securities under UDAY scheme

Bihar Government proposes to issue Special Securities of a notified amount of Rs.777.26cr, under the Ujjwal Discom Assurance Yojna Scheme (UDAY). Eligible market participants interested in subscribing to these Special Securities are advised to send their bids as per the format given below please click here to send email between 10.30 am to 12.00 noon on September 6, 2016 (Tuesday).

**Investor name Spread offered over Amount to be subscribed corresponding FIMMDA yield**

Allotment of securities and settlement will take place on September 7, 2016 (Wednesday). The terms and conditions of the issuance are as under:

2. Securities will be issued in equal strips with a maturity at the end of 6, 7, 8, 9, th th th th th th 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15 year. An investor will be required to purchase entire structure and the bid amount will be uniformly distributed across all tenors.
3. Minimum size of bid will be `100 crore.
4. The uniform spread (with a cap of 75 bps over corresponding GoI FIMMDA yield) offered by the bidder will be added to the base rate to arrive at the coupon to be paid on semiannual basis.
5. Base rate will be the yield on corresponding FIMMDA Government of India G-sec yield as at close of September 2, 2016.
**6. Successful bidders will be determined after taking into account the competitive spread and the time stamp of the mail.**
7. The Reserve Bank of India will have the discretion to accept or reject any/all bids at its sole discretion. It may be recalled that the Ministry of Power, Government of India on November 20, 2015 issued an Office Memorandum (No.06/02/2015-NEF/FRP) on “UDAY (Ujjwal Discom Assurance Yojna) Scheme for Operational and Financial Turnaround of Power Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)”.
