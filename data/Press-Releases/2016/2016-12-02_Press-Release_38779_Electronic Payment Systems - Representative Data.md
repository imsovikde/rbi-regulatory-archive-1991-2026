## December 02, 2016

## Electronic Payment Systems-Representative Data

The Reserve Bank of India today released <u>provisional data for the month of</u> <u>November 2016</u> for select payment systems. The data on credit/debit cards usage and pre-paid payment instruments are representative in nature as they are sourced from some of the major participants only.

Effective <u>December 2, 2016, the data would be released on a daily basis till</u> <u>December 31, 2016</u> with a lag of one day.

It may be mentioned that the Payment System Indicators are released on a monthly basis and are a part of the RBI Bulletin <u>[https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=16609](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewBulletin.aspx?Id=16609)</u>.
