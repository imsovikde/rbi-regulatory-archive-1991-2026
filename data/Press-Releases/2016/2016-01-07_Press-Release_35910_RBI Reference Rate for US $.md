January 07, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9115 on January 07, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 06, 2016) was

66.7165. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 06, 2016 January 07, 2016** **1 EUR** 71.7002 72.3782 **1 GBP** 97.7730 97.8514 **100 YEN** 56.19 56.78 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
