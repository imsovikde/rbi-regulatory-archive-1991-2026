June 28, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.8867 on June 28, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 27, 2016) was

67.9000. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 27, 2016 June 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.8801 75.0623 **1 GBP** 90.9113 90.1739 **100 YEN** 66.69 66.69 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
