March 21, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5040 on March 21, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 18, 2016) was

66.6088. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 18, 2016 March 21, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.3012 74.9168 **1 GBP** 96.3230 95.8722 **100 YEN** 59.87 59.66 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
