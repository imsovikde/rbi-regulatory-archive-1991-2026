## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** `**250 billion between 2.00 pm and 2.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `250 billion today (March 09, 2016, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between 2.00 pm and 2.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on March 16, 2016 (Wednesday).
