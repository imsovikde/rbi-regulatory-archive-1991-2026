March 18, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6088 on March 18, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 17, 2016) was

66.8806. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 17, 2016 March 18, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0735 75.3012 **1 GBP** 95.1644 96.3230 **100 YEN** 59.59 59.87 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
