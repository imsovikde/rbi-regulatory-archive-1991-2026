## November 9, 2016

## Banks to remain open for public on Saturday, November 12 and Sunday, November 13, 2016

All scheduled and non-scheduled banks, including public, private, foreign, cooperative, regional rural and local area banks, will remain open for public on Saturday, November 12 and Sunday, November 13, 2016. <u>Banks are advised to</u> <u>keep all their branches open on November 12 and 13, 2016</u> as regular working days for transacting all business. Banks may give due publicity about availability of banking services on these days.
