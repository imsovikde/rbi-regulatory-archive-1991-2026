## February 24, 2016

## <u>RBI launches the March 2016 Round of Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS)</u>

The Reserve Bank of India has launched <u>March 2016</u> round of its Consumer Confidence Survey. The survey seeks qualitative responses on questions pertaining to economic conditions, income, spending, perceptions on prices, employment prospects from about 5,400 respondents across six metropolitan cities. The cities are *Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and New Delhi*. The results of this survey provide useful inputs for policy formulation. The Reserve Bank has been conducting Consumer Confidence Survey (CCS) on a quarterly basis since June

2010. M/s Sigma Research & Consulting Pvt. Ltd. has been engaged to conduct the field work of the Survey on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India. The agency will approach the selected respondents for information. Others who are not approached by the agency can also participate in the survey round by providing their responses using <u>linked survey schedule (Forms-Survey)</u>. The filled in <u>survey schedule</u> may be e-mailed as per details given below.
