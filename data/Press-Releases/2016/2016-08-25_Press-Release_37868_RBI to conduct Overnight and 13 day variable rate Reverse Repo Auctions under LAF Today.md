## RBI to conduct Overnight and 13 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (August 25, 2016, Thursday) between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|200|1|August 26, 2016 (Friday)|
|2|200|13|September 07, 2016 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
