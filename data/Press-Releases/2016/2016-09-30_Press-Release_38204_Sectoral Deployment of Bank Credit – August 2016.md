## Sectoral Deployment of Bank Credit – August 2016

Data on sectoral deployment of bank credit collected on a monthly basis from select 46 scheduled commercial banks, accounting for about 95 per cent of the total non- food credit deployed by all scheduled commercial banks, for the month of August 2016 are set out in <u>Statements I and II</u>. These data are also available in the Real-Time Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).

## Highlights of the sectoral deployment of bank credit are given below:

- On a year-on-year (y-o-y) basis, non-food bank credit increased by 8.2 per cent in August 2016 as compared with the increase of 8.4 per cent in August
2015.
- Credit to agriculture and allied activities increased by 13.6 per cent in August 2016 as compared with the increase of 12.1 per cent in August
2015.
- Credit to industry contracted by 0.2 per cent in August 2016 as compared with an increase of 5.0 per cent in August 2015. Major sub-sectors which witnessed contraction in credit include food processing, infrastructure, textiles and cement & cement products.
- Credit to the services sector increased by 12.1 per cent in August 2016 as compared with the increase of 5.9 per cent in August 2015.
- Personal loans increased by 18.1 per cent in August 2016, up from 17.3 per cent in August 2015.
