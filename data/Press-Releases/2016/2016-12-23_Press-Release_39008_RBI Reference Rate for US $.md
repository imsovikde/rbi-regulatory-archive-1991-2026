December 23, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.9117 on December 23, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 22, 2016) was

67.9136. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 22, 2016 December 23, 2016** **1 EUR** 70.8678 70.9474 **1 GBP** 83.9140 83.4839 **100 YEN** 57.73 57.88 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
