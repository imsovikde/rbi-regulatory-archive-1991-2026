## RBI’s Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction for 500 billion

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (November 02, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (day)|Window Timing||Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
||(billion)|||||
|1|300|5|3.00 pm to 3.30 pm||November 07, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|200|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm||November 03, 2016 (Thursday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
