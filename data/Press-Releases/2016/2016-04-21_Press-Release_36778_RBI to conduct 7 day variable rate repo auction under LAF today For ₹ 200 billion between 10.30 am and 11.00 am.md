## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF today

**For** ` **200 billion between 10.30 am and 11.00 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 200 billion today (April 21, 2016, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between **10.30 am and 11.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on April 28, 2016 (Thursday).
