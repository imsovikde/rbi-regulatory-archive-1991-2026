March 17, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8806 on March 17, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 16, 2016) was

67.3680. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 16, 2016 March 17, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.7583 75.0735 **1 GBP** 95.1708 95.1644 **100 YEN** 59.40 59.59 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
