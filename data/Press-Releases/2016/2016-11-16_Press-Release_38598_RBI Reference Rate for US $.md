November 16, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7791 on November 16, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 15, 2016) was

67.7173. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 15, 2016 November 16, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.7758 72.8422 **1 GBP** 84.4232 84.6154 **100 YEN** 62.61 62.12 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
