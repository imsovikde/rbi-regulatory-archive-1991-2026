April 18, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6663 on April 18, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 13, 2016) was

66.4293. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 13, 2016 April 18, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.4637 75.2596 **1 GBP** 94.6684 94.3928 **100 YEN** 60.98 61.73 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
