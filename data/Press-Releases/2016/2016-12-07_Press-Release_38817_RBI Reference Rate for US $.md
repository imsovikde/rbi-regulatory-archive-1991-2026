December 07, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.8707 on December 07, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 06, 2016) was

68.0322. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 06, 2016 December 07, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.1074 72.7506 **1 GBP** 86.7274 85.8632 **100 YEN** 59.68 59.41 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
