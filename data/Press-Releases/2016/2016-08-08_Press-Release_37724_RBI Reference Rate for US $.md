August 08, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7407 on August 08, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 05, 2016) was

66.8141. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 05, 2016 August 08, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.4510 74.0622 **1 GBP** 87.7470 87.2768 **100 YEN** 66.13 65.40 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
