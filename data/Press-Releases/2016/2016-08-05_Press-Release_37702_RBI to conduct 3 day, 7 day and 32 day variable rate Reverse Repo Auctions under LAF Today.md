## August 05, 2016

**RBI to conduct 3 day, 7 day and 32 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (August 5, 2016, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (₹ billion)|Tenor|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|300|3|4.00 pm and 4.30 pm|August 8, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|200|7|12.30 pm and 1.00 pm|August 12, 2016 (Friday)|

3 200 32 <u>10.30 am and 11.00 am September 6, 2016 (Tuesday)</u>

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
