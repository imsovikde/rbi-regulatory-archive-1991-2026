## June 08, 2016

## RBI shifts Supervision of Some Urban Coop Banks to Nagpur

The Reserve Bank of India has shifted supervision and monitoring of Non- Scheduled Urban Cooperative banks whose head offices are located in Dhule, Nandurbar, Jalgaon and Solapur disctricts from Mumbai to Nagpur with effect from June 07, 2016. The address for correspondence regarding these banks would now be:
