## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: January 2016

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of January 2016 are given in the following Table.

## Table: International Trade in Services

(US$ Million) **Month Receipts (Exports) Payments (Imports)**

|October 2015|13,344|7,013|
|---|---|---|
|November 2015|12,019|5,686|
|December 2015|14,038|7,187|
|January 2016 Note : Data are provisional.|12,573|6,841|

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
