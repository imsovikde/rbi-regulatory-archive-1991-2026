May 17, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7233 on May 17, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 16, 2016) was

66.8216. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 16, 2016 May 17, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.5953 75.4907 **1 GBP** 95.9625 96.5953 **100 YEN** 61.45 61.07 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
