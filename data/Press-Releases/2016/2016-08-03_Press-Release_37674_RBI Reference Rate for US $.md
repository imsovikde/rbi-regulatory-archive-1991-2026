August 03, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9368 on August 03, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 02, 2016) was

66.7612. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 02, 2016 August 03, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.5990 74.9893 **1 GBP** 88.0313 89.0126 **100 YEN** 65.32 66.26 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
