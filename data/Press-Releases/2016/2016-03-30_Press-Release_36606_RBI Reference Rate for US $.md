March 30, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4105 on March 30, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 29, 2016) was

66.5580. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 29, 2016 March 30, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.4717 75.0306 **1 GBP** 94.6654 95.6311 **100 YEN** 58.58 59.11 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
