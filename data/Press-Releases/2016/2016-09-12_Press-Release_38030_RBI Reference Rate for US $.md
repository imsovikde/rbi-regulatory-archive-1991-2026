September 12, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.9017 on September 12, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 09, 2016) was

66.5462. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 09, 2016 September 12, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0242 75.2176 **1 GBP** 88.5863 88.7719 **100 YEN** 65.13 65.26 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
