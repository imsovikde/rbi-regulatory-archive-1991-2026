## Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on June 29, 2016

In the underwriting auctions conducted on June 29, 2016 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted Government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:
