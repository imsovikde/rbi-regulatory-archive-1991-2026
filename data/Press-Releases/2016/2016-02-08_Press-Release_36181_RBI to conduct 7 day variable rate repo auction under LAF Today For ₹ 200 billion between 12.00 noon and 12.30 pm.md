## RBI to conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **200 billion between 12.00 noon and 12.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 7 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 200 billion today (February 8, 2016, Monday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 7 day variable rate repo will take place on February 15, 2016 (Monday).
