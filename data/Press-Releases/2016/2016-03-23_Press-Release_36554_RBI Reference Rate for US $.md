March 23, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8640 on March 23, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 22, 2016) was

66.5411. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 22, 2016 March 23, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.8987 74.9077 **1 GBP** 95.7526 94.9268 **100 YEN** 59.37 59.52 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
