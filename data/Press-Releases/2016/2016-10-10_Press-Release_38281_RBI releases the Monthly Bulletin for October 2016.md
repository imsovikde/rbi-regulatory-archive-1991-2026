## October 10, 2016

## RBI releases the Monthly Bulletin for October 2016

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>October 2016</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes <u>Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement</u> <u>for the Year 2016-17 Resolution of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)</u>, Reserve Bank of India, Speeches by the Top Management, an Article on <u>Performance of</u> <u>Private Corporate Business Sector during 2015-16</u> and Current Statistics.

## <u>Performance of Private Corporate Business Sector during 2015-16</u>

The article has been prepared on the basis of abridged financial results of 2,932 listed non-government non-financial (NGNF) companies. Besides giving an insight on recent trends in aggregate sales, expenditure and profit margins, the article provides a brief analysis of performance based on size and major industry groups. ‘Explanatory Notes’, containing the brief methodology followed for compilation of data and glossary of terms are available along with detailed data at <u>[https://rbi.org.in/scripts/Pr_DataRelease.aspx?SectionID=360&DateFilter=](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/Pr_DataRelease.aspx?SectionID=360&DateFilter=) Year</u>.

## The major findings are as under:

- **Sales growth**:
Sales of private corporate sector contracted in 2015-16 after significant moderation in the previous three years both at the aggregate level and for the manufacturing sector. This contraction was, however, not observed if ‘Petroleum Products’ and ‘Iron & Steel’ industries were excluded. IT sector and non-IT services sector showed improvement in terms of sales growth (Y-o-Y) compared with the previous year.

Excluding ‘Petroleum Products’ and ‘Iron & Steel’ industries, aggregate sales growth in 2015-16 was lower compared with the previous year.

- **Expenditure:**
During 2015-16, the commodity prices were on a downward spiral significantly lowering the cost of raw materials to sales ratio.

As a result, contraction in overall expenditure was witnessed both at the aggregate level and for the manufacturing sector.

<!-- PAGE 2 -->

- **Profit and Profitability:**
Operating Profit growth (Y-o-Y) improved considerably in 2015-16on account of higher contraction in overall expenditure *vis-à-vis* sales.

A noticeable turnaround was recorded in growth of operating profit and net profit for the manufacturing sector and the IT sector. Net profit margins improved across all sectors.

- **Industry-wise Performance:**
Risk profile worsened for the ‘Iron & Steel’ industry, with a significant rise in the number of vulnerable companies as well as increase in debt held by them.

‘Pharmaceuticals & Medicines’ and ‘Motor Vehicles & Other Transport Equipments’ industries performed better than the other industries in the manufacturing sector in terms of sales growth.

- **Quarterly Performance:**
An analysis of common companies over the past eight quarters showed positive sales growth (Y-o-Y) in Q4:2015-16 after four successive quarters of contraction. Operating profit growth reached its highest level in the last eight quarters.
