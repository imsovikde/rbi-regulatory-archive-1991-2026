## March 03, 2016

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List – NRIs : M/s Creative Merchants Limited

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) in <u>M/s Creative Merchants Limited</u> has crossed the limit of 10 per cent of its paid-up capital. Therefore, no further purchases of shares of this company would be allowed through Stock Exchanges in India on behalf of NRIs.
