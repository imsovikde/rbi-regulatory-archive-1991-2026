April 27, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5460 on April 27, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 26, 2016) was

66.7330. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 26, 2016 April 27, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.1814 75.1770 **1 GBP** 96.7695 96.9242 **100 YEN** 60.22 59.85 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
