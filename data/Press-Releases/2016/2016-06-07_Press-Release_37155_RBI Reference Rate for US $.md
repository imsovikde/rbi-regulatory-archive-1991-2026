June 07, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8305 on June 07, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 06, 2016) was

66.9614. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 06, 2016 June 07, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9409 75.9194 **1 GBP** 96.1700 96.9376 **100 YEN** 62.60 62.10 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
