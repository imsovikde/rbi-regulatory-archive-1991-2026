## March 28, 2016

## Private Placement of Special Securities under UDAY

The Reserve Bank of India has asked market participants who are interested in subscribing to the Special Securities under Ujjwal Discom Assurance Yojna (UDAY) Scheme through private placement route to approach the Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office latest by March 30, 2016 on Phone: 022-22705125/Fax: 022-22644158/Please <u>click here</u> to send email.

The Special Securities under UDAY are being issued by State Governments of Bihar, Haryana, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Punjab and Rajasthan. The tenure and pricing of the bonds will be in accordance with Para 7.1 (b) and (c) of the scheme, respectively.

It may be recalled that the Ministry of Power, Government of India on November 20, 2015 issued an Office Memorandum (No 06/02/2015-NEF/FRP) on “UDAY (Ujjwal Discom Assurance Yojna) Scheme for Operational and Financial Turnaround of Power Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)”.
