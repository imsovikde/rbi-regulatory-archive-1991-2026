April 28, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4045 on April 28, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 27, 2016) was

66.5460. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 27, 2016 April 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.1770 75.4222 **1 GBP** 96.9242 96.8310 **100 YEN** 59.85 61.32 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
