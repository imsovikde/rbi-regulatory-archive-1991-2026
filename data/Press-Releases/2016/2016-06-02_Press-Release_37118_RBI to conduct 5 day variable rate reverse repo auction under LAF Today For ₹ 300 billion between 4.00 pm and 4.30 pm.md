## June 02, 2016

## RBI to conduct 5 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

## For 300 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 5 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of 300 billion today (June 2, 2016, Thursday). The auction will be conducted between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 5 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on June 7, 2016 (Tuesday).
