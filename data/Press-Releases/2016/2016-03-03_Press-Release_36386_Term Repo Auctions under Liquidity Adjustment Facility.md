## March 03, 2016

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>,

Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a

reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the

## schedule given hereunder:

|Sl.No.|Date of Auction|Notified Amount ` Billion) (|Tenor (days)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|March 04, 2016 (Friday)|270|14|March 18, 2016 (Friday)|
|2|March 08, 2016 (Tuesday)|155|14|March 22, 2016 (Tuesday)|
|3|March 11, 2016 (Friday)|155|12|March 23, 2016 (Wednesday)|
|4|March 15, 2016 (Tuesday)|150|14|March 29, 2016 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons there for.
