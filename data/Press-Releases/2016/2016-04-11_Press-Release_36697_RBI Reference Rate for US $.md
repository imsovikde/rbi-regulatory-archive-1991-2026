April 11, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3925 on April 11, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 07, 2016) was

66.4698. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 07, 2016 April 11, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.8952 75.7538 **1 GBP** 94.0415 93.7860 **100 YEN** 61.04 61.53 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
