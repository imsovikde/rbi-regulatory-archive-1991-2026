August 09, 2016 **RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys** The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey – Q2: 2016-17 (Round 74)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey</u> <u>(OBICUS) – January-March 2016 (Round 33)</u> iii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey – June 2016</u> iv. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households – June 2016 (Round 44)</u>
v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators – July</u> <u>2016 (Round 41)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents
to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
