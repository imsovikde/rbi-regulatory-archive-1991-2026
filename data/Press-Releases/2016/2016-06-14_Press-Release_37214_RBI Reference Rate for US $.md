June 14, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.1520 on June 14, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 13, 2016) was

67.0737. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 13, 2016 June 14, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.5116 75.7542 **1 GBP** 95.1172 95.1007 **100 YEN** 63.32 63.46 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
