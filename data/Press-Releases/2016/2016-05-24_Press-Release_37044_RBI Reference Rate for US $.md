May 24, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7060 on May 24, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 23, 2016) was

67.3492. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 23, 2016 May 24, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.6197 75.9323 **1 GBP** 97.7641 98.0654 **100 YEN** 61.38 61.97 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
