## RBI to conduct Overnight and 7 day variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (November 16, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|150|1|4.15 pm to 4.45 pm|November 17, 2016 (Thursday)|
|2|350|7|4.15 pm to 4.45 pm|November 23, 2016 (Wednesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
