September 30, 2016 **Fourth Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement, 2016-17 at 2.30 pm on October 4, 2016**

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) will meet on October 3 and 4, 2016 for the fourth bi-monthly monetary policy review for 2016-17. The resolution of the MPC will be placed on the website at 2.30 pm on October 4, 2016.
