## RBI to conduct 28 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **250 billion between 12.30 pm and 1.00 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 28 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 250 billion today (January 08, 2016, Friday). The auction will be conducted between **12.30 pm and 1.00 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 28 day variable rate repo will take place on February 5, 2016 (Friday).
