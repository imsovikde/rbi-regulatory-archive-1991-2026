## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of 5 NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has cancelled the certificate of registration of the following non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Office Address|CoR No.|Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|M/s Jahangirabad Finance Private Limited|301, Dhaka Chamber, 39, Company Naiwala, Karol Bagh, New Delhi – 110005|B-14.03066|April 25, 2005|May 16, 2016|
|2.|Finance Private Limited|M/s A H P Motor C-205/A, Vallabh Nagar, Ring Road – 1, Raipur – 492001|B-06.00366|December 15, 2000|June 06, 2016|
|3.|M/s Onam Trading & Investment Private Limited|15, Varsha, Napean Sea Road, Mumbai – 400006|B-13.01695|January 01, 2004|June 06, 2016|
|4.|Investment Pvt Ltd|M/s Arogya Dhan 90, Naval Bhavan, Tagore Varsha Finance & Nagar, Hiran Magri, Sector 4, Udaipur, Rajasthan – 313002|B-10.00117|September 05, 2011|June 14, 2016|
|5.|M/s Protchem Finance Limited|AB 5, Second Avenue, SDV Arcade, Anna Nagar, Chennai – 600040|07.00162|March 11, 1998|July 01, 2016|

As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as laid down under clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
