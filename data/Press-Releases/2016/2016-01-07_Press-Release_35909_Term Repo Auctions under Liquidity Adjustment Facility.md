## January 07, 2016

## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22,</u> <u>2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

**Notified**

|Sl.||Amount|Tenor||
|---|---|---|---|---|
|No.||(Billion)|(days)||
|1.|January 08, 2016 (Friday)|255|14|January 22, 2016 (Friday)|
|2.|January 12, 2016 (Tuesday)|155|13|January 25, 2016 (Monday)|
|3.|January 15, 2016 (Friday)|155|14|January 29, 2016 (Friday)|
|4.|January 19, 2016 (Tuesday)|150|14|February 02, 2016 (Tuesday)|

**Date of Auction Date of Reversal**

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 8, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons there for.
