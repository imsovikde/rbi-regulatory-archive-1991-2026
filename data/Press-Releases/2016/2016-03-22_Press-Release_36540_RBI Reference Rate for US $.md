March 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5411 on March 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 21, 2016) was

66.5040. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 21, 2016 March 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.9168 74.8987 **1 GBP** 95.8722 95.7526 **100 YEN** 59.66 59.37 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
