December 06, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.0322 on December 06, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 05, 2016) was

68.1703. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 05, 2016 December 06, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.0424 73.1074 **1 GBP** 86.5354 86.7274 **100 YEN** 60.11 59.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
