October 06, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6322 on October 06, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 05, 2016) was

66.5699. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 05, 2016 October 06, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6515 74.5814 **1 GBP** 84.4772 84.6695 **100 YEN** 64.70 64.34 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
