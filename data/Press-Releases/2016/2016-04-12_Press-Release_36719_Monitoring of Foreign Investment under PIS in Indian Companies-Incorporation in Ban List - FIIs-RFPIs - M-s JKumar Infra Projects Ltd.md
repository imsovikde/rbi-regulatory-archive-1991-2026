## April 12, 2016

## Monitoring of Foreign Investment under PIS in Indian Companies-

## Incorporation in Ban List-FIIs/RFPIs :

## M/s JKumar Infra Projects Ltd.

The Reserve Bank of India has today notified that the foreign shareholding through Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Registered Foreign Portfolios Investors (RFPIs) in <u>M/s JKumar Infra Projects Ltd</u>. has crossed the permissible limit. Therefore, no further purchase of shares of this company would be allowed through stock exchanges in India on behalf of FII/RFPIs.
