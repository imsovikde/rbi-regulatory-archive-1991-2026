## December 06, 2016

## Fixed rate LAF Repo window on December 07, 2016

In continuation of the <u>Press Release dated November 30, 2015</u>, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has decided to keep the fixed rate LAF Repo window open from

9.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon on December 07, 2016. On other days, the window will continue to be operated between 9.00 a.m. and 3.00 p.m.
