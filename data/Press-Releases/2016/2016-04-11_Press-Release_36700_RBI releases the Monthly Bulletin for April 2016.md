## April 11, 2016

## RBI releases the Monthly Bulletin for April 2016

The Reserve Bank of India today released the <u>April 2016</u> issue of its monthly Bulletin. The Bulletin includes the <u>First Bi-monthly Monetary Policy Statement for the</u> <u>Year 2016-17 by Dr. Raghuram G. Rajan</u>, Governor, and <u>Monetary Policy Report-</u> <u>April 2016</u>. This issue also includes Speeches by the Top Management, Press Releases of Working Papers and Current Statistics.
