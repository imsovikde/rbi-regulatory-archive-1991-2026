December 08, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.4325 on December 08, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 07, 2016) was

67.8707. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 07, 2016 December 08, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.7506 72.6113 **1 GBP** 85.8632 85.2414 **100 YEN** 59.41 59.44 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
