## July 1, 2016

## RBI launches Survey on International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS): 2015-16 round

The Reserve Bank of India has launched the 2015-16 round of its <u>Survey on</u> <u>International Trade in Banking Services (ITBS)</u>.

The survey, conducted annually since 2006-07, provides information on financial services rendered by branches / subsidiaries of Indian banks operating abroad and branches/subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India, based on explicit / implicit fee / commission charged to customers.

The Survey Schedule for the 2015-16 round is required to be filled by the branches / subsidiaries / joint venture / associate of Indian banks operating abroad and branches / subsidiaries of foreign banks operating in India. The soft form of this <u>survey schedule</u> (both in Hindi and English – one of which can be used) with in-built validation checks are available on the RBI website under the head ‘Forms’ (available under the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘<u>Survey</u>’, which can be duly filled-in, validated and <u>emailed</u> by July 25, 2016.
