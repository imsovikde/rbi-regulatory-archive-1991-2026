May 13, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7609 on May 13, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 12, 2016) was

66.5977. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 12, 2016 May 13, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.0612 75.7202 **1 GBP** 96.1538 96.1557 **100 YEN** 61.16 61.47 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
