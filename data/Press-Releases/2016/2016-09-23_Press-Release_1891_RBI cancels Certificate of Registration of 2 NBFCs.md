## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of 2 NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has cancelled the certificate of registration of the following non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Office Address|CoR No.|Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|M/s S.P. Global Finance and Investment Private Arcade, Opp. Prakash Finance and Investment Nashik Road, Nashik – Private Limited)|Shop No. 15, City B-13.01665 Limited (Formerly Latur Traders, Jai Road, 422001||May 21, 2003|August 05, 2016|
|2.|M/s Skylark Deposit and Advances Limited|H.No. 1017/1, Field Ganj, Corner House, B- 14, Street No. 13, Ludhiana - 141008|06.00182|July 30, 2007|August 12, 2016|

As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as laid down under clause (a) of Section 45-IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
