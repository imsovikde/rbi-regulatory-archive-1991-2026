May 04, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5388 on May 04, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 03, 2016) was

66.2698. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 03, 2016 May 04, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.6145 76.4597 **1 GBP** 97.5889 96.8273 **100 YEN** 62.64 62.21 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
