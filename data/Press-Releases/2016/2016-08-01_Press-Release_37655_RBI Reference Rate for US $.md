August 1, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7412 on August 1, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 29, 2016) was

67.0340. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 29, 2016 August 1, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.2737 74.5165 **1 GBP** 88.2972 88.4388 **100 YEN** 64.69 65.08 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
