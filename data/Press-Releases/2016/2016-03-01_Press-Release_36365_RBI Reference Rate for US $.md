March 01, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.1580 on March 01, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (February 29, 2016) was

68.6160. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **3February 29, 2016 March 01, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0796 74.2172 **1 GBP** 95.1978 95.0395 **100 YEN** 60.78 60.50 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
