## RBI releases the results of Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators-Round 38

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators -Round 38</u> conducted during January 2016.

The results of the Survey represent the views of the respondent forecasters and in no way reflect the views or forecasts of the Reserve Bank of India.
