## Results of Underwriting Auctions Conducted on May 5, 2016

In the underwriting auctions conducted on May 5, 2016 for Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) of the undernoted government securities, the Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rates for underwriting commission payable to Primary Dealers as given below:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Nomenclature of the|Notified|Minimum|Additional|Total|ACU|
|Security|Amount|Underwriting Commitment (MUC) Amount|Competitive Underwriting Amount Accepted|Amount under- written|Commission Cut-off rate (paise per 100)|

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|New Issue GS 2030|8,000|4,011|3,989|8,000|0.17|
|7.73% GS 2034|2,000|1,008|992|2,000|0.39|
|8.13% GS 2045|2,000|1,008|992|2,000|0.54|
