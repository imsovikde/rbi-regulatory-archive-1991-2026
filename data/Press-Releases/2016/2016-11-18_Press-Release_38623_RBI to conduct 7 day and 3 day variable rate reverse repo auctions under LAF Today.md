## November 18, 2016

**RBI to conduct 7 day and 3 day variable rate reverse repo auctions under LAF Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (November 18, 2016, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
