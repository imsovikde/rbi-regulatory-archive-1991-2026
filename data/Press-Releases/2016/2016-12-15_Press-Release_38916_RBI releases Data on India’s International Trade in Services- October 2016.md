## RBI releases Data on India’s International Trade in Services: October 2016

The Reserve Bank releases monthly data on India’s international trade in services with a lag of around 45 days.

The value of exports and imports of services during the month of October 2016 are given in the following Table.

||Table: International Trade in Services||
|---|---|---|
|||(US$ Million)|
|Month|Receipts (Exports)|Payments (Imports)|
|October 2016|13,113|7,680|
|Note : Data are provisional.|||

Monthly data on services are provisional and would undergo revision when the

Balance of Payments (BoP) data are released on a quarterly basis.
