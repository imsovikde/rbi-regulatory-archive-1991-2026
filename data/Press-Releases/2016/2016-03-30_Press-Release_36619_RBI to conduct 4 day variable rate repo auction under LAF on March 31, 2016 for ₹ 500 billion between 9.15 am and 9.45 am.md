## March 30, 2016

## RBI to conduct 4 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on

## March 31, 2016 for 500 billion between 9.15 a.m. and 9.45 a.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 4 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 500 billion on March 31, 2016, (Thursday). The auction will be conducted between 9.15 a.m. and 9.45 a.m**.** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 4 day variable rate repo will take place on April 4, 2016 (Monday).
