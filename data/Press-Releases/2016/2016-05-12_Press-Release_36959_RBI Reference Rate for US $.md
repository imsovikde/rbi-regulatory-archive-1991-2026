May 12, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5977 on May 12, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 11, 2016) was

66.6958. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 11, 2016 May 12, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9665 76.0612 **1 GBP** 96.4355 96.1538 **100 YEN** 61.43 61.16 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
