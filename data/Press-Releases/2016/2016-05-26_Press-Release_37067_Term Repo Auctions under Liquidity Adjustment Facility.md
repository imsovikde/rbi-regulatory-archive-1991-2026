## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

**Notified** **Sl. Tenor** **Date of Auction Amount Date of Reversal** **No. (days)** <u>(</u> **Billion)**

|1|May 27, 2016 (Friday)|190|14|June 10, 2016 (Friday)|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|2|May 31, 2016 (Tuesday)|185|14|June 14, 2016 (Tuesday)|
|3|June 03, 2016 (Friday)|185|14|June 17, 2016 (Friday)|
|4|June 07, 2016 (Tuesday)|185|14|June 21, 2016 (Tuesday)|

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons therefor.
