May 26, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.2855 on May 26, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 25, 2016) was

67.4467. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 25, 2016 May 26, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.2098 75.2185 **1 GBP** 98.5599 98.9501 **100 YEN** 61.32 61.34 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
