December 05, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.1703 on December 05, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 02, 2016) was

68.3689. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 02, 2016 December 05, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.0385 72.0424 **1 GBP** 86.2816 86.5354 **100 YEN** 59.96 60.11 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
