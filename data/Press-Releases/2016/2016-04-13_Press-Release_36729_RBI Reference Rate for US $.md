April 13, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4293 on April 13, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 12, 2016) was

66.4950. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 12, 2016 April 13, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9107 75.4637 **1 GBP** 94.7487 94.6684 **100 YEN** 61.38 60.98 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
