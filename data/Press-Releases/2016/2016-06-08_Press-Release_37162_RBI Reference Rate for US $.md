June 08, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7370 on June 08, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 07, 2016) was

66.8305. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 07, 2016 June 08, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9194 75.8199 **1 GBP** 96.9376 97.0823 **100 YEN** 62.10 62.26 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
