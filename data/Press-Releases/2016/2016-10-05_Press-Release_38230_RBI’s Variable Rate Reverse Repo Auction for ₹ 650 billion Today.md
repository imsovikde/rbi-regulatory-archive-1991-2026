## RBI’s Variable Rate Reverse Repo Auction for 650 billion Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (October 05, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on February 13, 2014.

|Sl. No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor (day)|Window Timing|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|250|5|2.00 pm to 2.30 pm|October 10, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|400|1|4.00 pm to 4.30 pm|October 06, 2016 (Thursday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
