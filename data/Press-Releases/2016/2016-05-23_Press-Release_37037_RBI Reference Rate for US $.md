May 23, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.3492 on May 23, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 20, 2016) was

67.4076. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 20, 2016 May 23, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.5437 75.6197 **1 GBP** 98.4555 97.7641 **100 YEN** 61.17 61.38 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
