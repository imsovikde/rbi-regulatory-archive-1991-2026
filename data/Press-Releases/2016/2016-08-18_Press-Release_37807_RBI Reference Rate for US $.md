August 18, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7939 on August 18, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 16, 2016) was

66.8983. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 16, 2016 August 18, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0198 75.5773 **1 GBP** 86.3389 87.2596 **100 YEN** 66.68 66.84 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
