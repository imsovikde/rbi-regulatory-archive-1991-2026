August 1, 2016 **RBI to conduct 4 day and 7 day variable rate** **Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF Today** The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (August 1, 2016, Monday) between 3.00 pm and 3.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Sl No. Notified Amount ( billion)** <u>Tenor</u> **Date of Reversal** 1 250 4 August 5, 2016 (Friday) 2 100 7 August 8, 2016 (Monday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
