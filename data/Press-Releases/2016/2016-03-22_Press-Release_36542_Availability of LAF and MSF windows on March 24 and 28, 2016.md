## March 22, 2016

## Availability of LAF and MSF windows on March 24 and 28, 2016

In order to facilitate better liquidity management by the market participants, Reserve Bank of India vide <u>press release dated February 17, 2016</u> had decided to conduct Reverse Repo and MSF operations on all Mumbai holidays when the RTGS is open. Accordingly, the Reverse Repo and MSF windows will be available to market participants on March 24, 2016 between 5.30 p.m. and 7.30 p.m. Further, the regular LAF/MSF windows will be available to the market participants as usual on March 28, 2016.
