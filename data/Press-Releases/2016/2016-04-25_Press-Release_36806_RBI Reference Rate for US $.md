April 25, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6839 on April 25, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 22, 2016) was

66.4925. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 22, 2016 April 25, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.1033 74.9527 **1 GBP** 95.4433 96.3249 **100 YEN** 60.15 59.99 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
