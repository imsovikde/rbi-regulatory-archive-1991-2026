April 05, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.3248 on April 05, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 04, 2016) was

66.2430. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 04, 2016 April 05, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.3713 75.5240 **1 GBP** 94.1578 94.4200 **100 YEN** 59.44 59.98 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
