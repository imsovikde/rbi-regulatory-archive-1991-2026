## March 04, 2016

## Government of India nominates Shri Natarajan Chandrasekaran,

## Shri Bharat Narotam Doshi and Shri Sudhir Mankad, on RBI Central Board

In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (c) of the sub-section (1) of Section 8 of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, the Central Government has nominated Shri Natarajan Chandrasekaran, Shri Bharat Narotam Doshi and Shri Sudhir Mankad as directors on the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India for a period of four years with effect from March 4, 2016 or until further orders, whichever is earlier.
