May 10, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7224 on May 10, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 09, 2016) was

66.4379. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 09, 2016 May 10, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.8056 75.9568 **1 GBP** 95.7702 96.1069 **100 YEN** 61.73 61.35 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
