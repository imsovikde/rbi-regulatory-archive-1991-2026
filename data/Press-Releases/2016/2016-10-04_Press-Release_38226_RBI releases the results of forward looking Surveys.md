## RBI releases the results of forward looking Surveys

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following Surveys:

i. <u>Industrial Outlook Survey – Q3: 2016-17 (Round 75)</u> ii. <u>Quarterly Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey</u> <u>(OBICUS) – April-June 2016 (Round 34)</u> iii. <u>Consumer Confidence Survey – September 2016</u> iv. <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households – September 2016 (Round</u>
<u>45)</u>
v. <u>Survey of Professional Forecasters on Macroeconomic Indicators –</u> <u>September 2016 (Round 42)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents
to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
