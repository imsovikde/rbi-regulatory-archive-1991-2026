## December 28, 2016

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF Today

## for 250 billion between 10.30 am and 11.00 am

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo auction for a notified amount of 250 billion today (December 28, 2016, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **10.30 am and 11.00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight Variable rate Reverse Repo will take place on December 29, 2016 (Thursday).
