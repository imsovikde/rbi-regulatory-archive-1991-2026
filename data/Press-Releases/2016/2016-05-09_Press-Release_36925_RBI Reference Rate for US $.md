May 09, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4379 on May 09, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 06, 2016) was

66.5830. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 06, 2016 May 09, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.9313 75.8056 **1 GBP** 96.3456 95.7702 **100 YEN** 62.13 61.73 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
