## October 18, 2016

## RBI launches Its Quarterly OBICUS for

## July – September 2016

th The Reserve Bank of India has launched <u>35 round</u> of its Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS). The survey is for the reference period July-September 2016 (Q2:2016-17).

The Reserve Bank has been conducting Order Books, Inventories and Capacity Utilisation Survey (OBICUS) of the manufacturing sector on a quarterly basis. The survey seeks quantitative information on order books, inventories and capacity utilisation, such as, pending order books, backlog order books, total inventories, finished goods inventories, work in progress inventories, installed capacity, quantity produced, capacity utilisation, value of production, etc., from companies involved in the manufacturing activities. The information on installed capacity, quantity produced, value of production, etc., is used for calculating the capacity utilisation at industry as well as at an all India level. The survey provides a significant input to the Reserve Bank in monetary policy formulation.

A <u>detailed annual article</u> based on the results for the quarters April-June 2015 to January-March 2016 was published in the <u>September 2016</u> issue of the monthly th RBI Bulletin. The survey results for the <u>34 round (Q1:2016-17)</u> were released on RBI website on October 04, 2016.

The data collection work is undertaken in-house. During this quarter, the major manufacturing companies will be approached by the Reserve Bank. Other manufacturing companies that are not approached earlier may also participate in this survey round by downloading the <u>survey schedule</u> from the Reserve Bank’s website <u>[https://www.rbi.org.in](https://www.rbi.org.in)</u> (More Links>>Forms>>Survey) The duly authenticated filled-in survey schedule may be <u>e-mailed</u> or faxed as per contact details provided in the survey schedule.
