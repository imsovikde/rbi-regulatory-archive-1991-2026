January 14, 2016 **Result of repurchase auction of 1.44% Inflation Index GS 2023** The repurchase auction of <u>1.44% Inflation Index GS 2023</u> was conducted today. After examining the bids, it has been decided in consultation with Government of India not to accept any offer.
