March 02, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7022 on March 02, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 01, 2016) was

68.1580. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 01, 2016 March 02, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.2172 73.5314 **1 GBP** 95.0395 94.6003 **100 YEN** 60.50 59.42 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
