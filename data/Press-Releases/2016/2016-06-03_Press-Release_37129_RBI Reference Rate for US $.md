June 03, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.2415 on June 03, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 02, 2016) was

67.2533. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 02, 2016 June 03, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.3035 74.9944 **1 GBP** 97.0465 96.9017 **100 YEN** 61.58 61.83 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
