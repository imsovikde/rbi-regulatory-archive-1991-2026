## August 26, 2016

## RBI’s College of Agricultural Banking announces Results of Case Writing Competition, 2016

The Reserve Bank of India’s College of Agricultural Banking, Pune announced the results of the <u>Case Writing Competition, 2016</u>.

**Bank**

|Position|Name of the Participant|Designation||
|---|---|---|---|
|First|Ms. Mauli Sanjiv Bodiwala|Manager|The Kalupur Commercial Co-operative Bank Ltd.|
|Second|Mr. Vineet Kumar Jain|Senior Manager|Bank of Baroda|
|Third|Mr. Supriya Saha|Assistant Manager|Bangiya Gramin Vikash Bank|

The College had in February 2016, organised a Case Writing Competition on the topic: “Lending to an MSME Borrower in an Innovative Way”. The competition was meant for staff members of scheduled commercial banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), State Cooperative Banks, Urban Cooperative Banks and Non-Banking Finance Companies. A panel consisting of eminent persons from the fields of banking and academics evaluated the entries on three criteria: (a) potential of the case/story to sensitise other bankers (50% weightage); (ii) ingenuity of solutions (25% weightage); and (iii) presentation, language and clarity (25% weightage).

The winners will be given a cash prize of ₹ 20,000 (first prize), ₹ 15,000 (second prize) and ₹ 10,000 (third prize) apart from certificates.
