## RBI to conduct 3 day Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **1250 billion between 12:00 pm and 12:30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day Variable Rate Repo auction for a notified amount of ` 1250 billion today (November 29, 2016, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between **12:00 pm and 12:30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day Variable Rate Repo will take place on December 02, 2016 (Friday).
