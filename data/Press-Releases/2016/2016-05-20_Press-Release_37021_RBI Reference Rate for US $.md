May 20, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.4076 on May 20, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 19, 2016) was

67.2307. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 19, 2016 May 20, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.4530 75.5437 **1 GBP** 98.0493 98.4555 **100 YEN** 61.01 61.17 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
