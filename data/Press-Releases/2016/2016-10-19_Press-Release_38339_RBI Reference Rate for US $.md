October 19, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7145 on October 19, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 18, 2016) was

66.7268. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 18, 2016 October 19, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.5463 73.2125 **1 GBP** 81.7136 81.7853 **100 YEN** 64.14 64.40 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
