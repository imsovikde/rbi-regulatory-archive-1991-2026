## March 15, 2016

**RBI to conduct 7 day and 19 day variable rate Repo Auctions under LAF on March 16, 2016**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate repo auctions tomorrow (March 16, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount|Tenor (Days)|Window Timings|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(Billion)||||
|1|400|19|9.15 am – 9.45 am|April 4, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|300|7|10.15 am – 10.45 am|March 23, 2016 (Wednesday)|

Successful bids will get the allotment at their respective rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
