## Claim for unpaid/unredeemed securities of Central and State Government

The Reserve Bank of India hereby announces in public interest that the members of the public, holding the Central/State Government Securities in physical forms which have been matured and not yet claimed, such as Stock Certificates, Promissory Notes, Bond Ledger Accounts etc. may submit the same for obtaining repayment/redemption to the respective Public Debt Office of the Reserve Bank of India along with the application, Original Certificates duly discharged, cancelled cheque and National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) mandate duly certified from the banker and copy of one of the photo identity proof such as PAN Card/Passport/Aadhaar card/Driving License/Voter ID etc. In case of deceased holders, the successor/legal heirs may approach the Reserve Bank of India for necessary documentation.
