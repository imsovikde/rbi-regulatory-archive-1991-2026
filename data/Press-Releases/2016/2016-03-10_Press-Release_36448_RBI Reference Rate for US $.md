March 10, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.0510 on March 10, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 09, 2016) was

67.4632. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 09, 2016 March 10, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.0611 73.6220 **1 GBP** 95.6965 95.2258 **100 YEN** 59.92 58.99 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
