December 21, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.8724 on December 21, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 20, 2016) was

67.8954. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 20, 2016 December 21, 2016** **1 EUR** 70.4686 70.6077 **1 GBP** 84.1020 83.9649 **100 YEN** 57.66 57.70 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
