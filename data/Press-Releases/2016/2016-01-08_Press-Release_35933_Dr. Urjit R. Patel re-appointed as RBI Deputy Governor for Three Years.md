## January 08, 2016

## Dr. Urjit R. Patel re-appointed as RBI Deputy Governor for Three Years

The Government of India today re-appointed Dr. Urjit R. Patel as the Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank of India, for a further period of three years.

Dr. Urjit R. Patel was earlier <u>appointed as Deputy Governor</u> for a period of three years from January 11, 2013.
