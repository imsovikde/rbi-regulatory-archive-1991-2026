September 26, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7095 on September 26, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 23, 2016) was

66.6519. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Date** **Currency** **September 23, 2016 September 26, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6701 74.8814 **1 GBP** 86.9207 86.4689 **100 YEN** 66.08 66.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
