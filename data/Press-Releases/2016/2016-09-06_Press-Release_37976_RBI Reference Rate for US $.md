September 06, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5469 on September 06, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 02, 2016) was

66.8405. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 02, 2016 September 06, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.8012 74.1665 **1 GBP** 88.7040 88.6471 **100 YEN** 64.52 64.20 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
