November 24, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.6560 on November 24, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 23, 2016) was

68.4772. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 23, 2016 November 24, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.7844 72.3840 **1 GBP** 84.9665 85.3600 **100 YEN** 61.71 60.90 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
