July 12, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.1471 on July 12, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 11, 2016) was

67.1426. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 11, 2016 July 12, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.1053 74.4460 **1 GBP** 86.9161 87.8620 **100 YEN** 65.89 64.94 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
