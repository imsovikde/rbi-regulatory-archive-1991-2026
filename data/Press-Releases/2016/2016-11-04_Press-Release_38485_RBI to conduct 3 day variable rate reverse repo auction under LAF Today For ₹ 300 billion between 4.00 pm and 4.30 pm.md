## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate reverse repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **300 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate reverse repo auction for a notified amount of ` 300 billion today (November 4, 2016, Friday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate reverse repo will take place on November 7, 2016 (Monday).
