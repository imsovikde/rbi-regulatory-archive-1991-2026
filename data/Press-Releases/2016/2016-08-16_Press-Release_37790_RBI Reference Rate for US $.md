August 16, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8983 on August 16, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 12, 2016) was

66.8252. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 12, 2016 August 16, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.4633 75.0198 **1 GBP** 86.6255 86.3389 **100 YEN** 65.44 66.68 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
