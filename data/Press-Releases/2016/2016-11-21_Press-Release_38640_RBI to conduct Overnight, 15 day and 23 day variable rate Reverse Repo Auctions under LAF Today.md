## November 21, 2016

## RBI to conduct Overnight, 15 day and 23 day variable rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (November 21, 2016, Monday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
