## November 25, 2016

**RBI to conduct 3 day and 5 day variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF Today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (November 25, 2016, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

## Notified

## Tenor

## Sl. No. Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal

**(day)** **( billion)**

November 28, 1 750 3 11.30 am to 12.00 noon 2016 (Monday)

November 30, 2 750 5 11.30 am to 12.00 noon 2016 (Wednesday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
