## August 19, 2016

**RBI to conduct 3 day and 18 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF today**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (August 19, 2016, Friday) between 3.00 p.m. and 3.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.||Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
||(|Billion)|||
|1||250|3|August 22, 2016 (Monday)|
|2||150|18|September 06, 2016 (Tuesday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
