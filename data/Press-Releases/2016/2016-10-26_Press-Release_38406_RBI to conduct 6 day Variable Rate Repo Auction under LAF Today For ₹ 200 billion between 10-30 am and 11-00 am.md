## October 26, 2016

## RBI to conduct 6 day Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **200 billion between 10:30 am and 11:00 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 6 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 200 billion today (October 26, 2016, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **10:30 am and 11:00 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 6 day variable rate repo will take place on November 01, 2016 (Tuesday).
