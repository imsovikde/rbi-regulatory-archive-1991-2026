September 09, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5462 on September 09, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 08, 2016) was

66.4483. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 08, 2016 September 09, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.7676 75.0242 **1 GBP** 88.6088 88.5863 **100 YEN** 65.33 65.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
