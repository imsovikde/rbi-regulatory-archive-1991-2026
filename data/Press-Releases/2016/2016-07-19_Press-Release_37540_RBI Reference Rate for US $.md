July 19, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.1462 on July 19, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 18, 2016) was

67.1015. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 18, 2016 July 19, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.1673 74.3443 **1 GBP** 88.6881 88.7203 **100 YEN** 63.51 63.35 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
