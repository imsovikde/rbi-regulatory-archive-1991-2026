## September 26, 2016

**th** **RBI launches the 11 round of Survey on** **Foreign Collaboration in Indian Industry**

The Reserve Bank of India has been conducting the Survey on Foreign th Collaboration in Indian Industry since 1965. The <u>11 round of survey with 2014-15</u> <u>and 2015-16</u> as the reference period has now been launched.

The survey collects information on the operations of the Indian companies having foreign technical collaboration.

The <u>survey schedule</u> of this survey is required to be filled by the Indian companies having technical collaboration with foreign companies. The soft form of this survey schedule with in-built validation checks is available on the RBI website under the head ‘Forms’ (available under the ‘More Links’ at the bottom of the home page) and sub-head ‘Survey’. The duly filled-in form, after validation can be <u>emailed</u> by October 31, 2016.
