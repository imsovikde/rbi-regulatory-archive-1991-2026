## RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of 5 NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has cancelled the certificate of registration of the following non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45-IA (6) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

|Sr. No.|Name of the Company|Office Address|CoR No.|Issued On|Cancellation Order Date|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|1.|M/s Simco Consultants Private Limited,|Lakhami Tower,Vyapaar Vihar,Bilaspur (CG)-495001|B-05.03761|September 30, 2003|June 22, 2016|
|2.|M/s Lunkad Securities Ltd|13-Dr.R.S.Bhandari Marg,Indore (M.P)-452002|B-03.00154|May 11, 2002|July 21, 2016|
|3.|and Investment Private Limited|M/s Rajvir Marketing 13-Race Course Road, B-03.00156 Indore (M.P)-452002||May 11, 2002|July 21, 2016|
|4.|M/s Crystal Investments Limited|29,Thilak Street,T.Nagar,Chennai- 600017|07.00026|February 26, 1998|August 16, 2016|
|5.|M/s Shri Jaya Investments Agency Floor, Limited (formerly known as Arcot Finance & Agency Limited)|No.12,Industrial Estate, First Ekkaduthangal,Chennai- 600032|07.00017|September 08, 2010|August 04, 2016|

As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of a non-banking financial institution as laid down under clause (a) of Section 45-I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
