October 24, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8625 on October 24, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 21, 2016) was

66.8943. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 21, 2016 October 24, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.9750 72.7999 **1 GBP** 81.9589 81.6725 **100 YEN** 64.40 64.43 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
