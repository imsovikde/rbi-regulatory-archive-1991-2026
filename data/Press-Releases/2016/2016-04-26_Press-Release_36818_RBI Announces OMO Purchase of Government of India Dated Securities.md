## April 26, 2016

## RBI Announces OMO Purchase of Government of India Dated Securities

Based on the current assessment of prevailing and evolving liquidity conditions, the Reserve Bank has decided to conduct purchase of Government securities under Open Market Operations for an aggregate amount of 150 billion on April 28, 2016 (Thursday) through multi-security auction using the multiple price method:

## <u>Sr. No</u> Security Date of Maturity

|1|8.27% GS 2020|June 09, 2020|
|---|---|---|
|2|8.08% GS 2022|August 02, 2022|
|3|8.40% GS 2024|July 28, 2024|
|4|8.28% GS 2027|September 21, 2027|
|5|9.20% GS 2030|September 30, 2030|
|6|8.24% GS 2033|November 10, 2033|

There is an overall aggregate ceiling of 150 billion for all the securities in the basket put together. There is no security-wise notified amount.

## RBI reserves the right to:

- Decide on the quantum of purchase of individual securities.
- Accept less than the aggregate amount of 150 billion.
- Purchase marginally higher than the aggregate amount due to rounding off effects and other relevant factors.
- Accept or reject any or all of the offers either wholly or partially without assigning any reason. The eligible participants should submit their offers in electronic format on the Reserve Bank of India Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system between 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon on April 28, 2016 (Thursday). Only in the event of system failure, physical offers would be accepted. Such physical offer should be submitted to Financial Markets Operations Department (Please <u>click here</u> to send email; Phone no: 022-22630982) in the prescribed form obtainable from RBI website (www.rbi.org.in) before 12.00 noon. The result of the auction will be announced on the same day and payment to successful participants will be made during banking hours on April 29, 2016 (Friday). Successful participants may note to make available the requisite amount of securities in their SGL account by 12 noon on April 29, 2016 (Friday).
