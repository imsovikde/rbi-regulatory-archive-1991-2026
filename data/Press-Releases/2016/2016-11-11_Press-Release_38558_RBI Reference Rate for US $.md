November 11, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.0292 on November 11, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 10, 2016) was

66.4273. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 10, 2016 November 11, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.7113 73.1155 **1 GBP** 82.5093 84.1753 **100 YEN** 63.02 62.94 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
