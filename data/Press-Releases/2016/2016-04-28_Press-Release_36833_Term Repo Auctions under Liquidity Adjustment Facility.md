## Term Repo Auctions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility

As announced in the revised Liquidity Management Framework on <u>August 22, 2014</u>, Reserve Bank will conduct regular variable rate 14 day term repos four times during a reporting fortnight. Accordingly, four term repo auctions will be conducted as per the schedule given hereunder:

**Notified** **Sl. Tenor** **Date of Auction Amount Date of Reversal** **No. (days)** <u>(</u> **Billion)**

|1|April 29, 2016 (Friday)|190|14|May 13, 2016 (Friday)|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|2|May 03, 2016 (Tuesday)|185|14|May 17, 2016 (Tuesday)|
|3|May 06, 2016 (Friday)|185|14|May 20, 2016 (Friday)|
|4|May 10, 2016 (Tuesday)|185|14|May 24, 2016 (Tuesday)|

Further, the total notified amount of ` 745 billion has been equitably re-allocated within the four Term Repo auctions in the fortnight.

The auctions will be conducted as per the revised guidelines issued on <u>February 13,</u>

<u>2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bids. As indicated in the ‘Term Repo under Liquidity Adjustment Facility-Operational Guidelines’ dated <u>October 08, 2013</u>, the RBI reserves the right to inject less than the notified amount without assigning any reasons there for.
