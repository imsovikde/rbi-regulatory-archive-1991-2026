December 15, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.7994 on December 15, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 14, 2016) was

67.5604. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 14, 2016 December 15, 2016** **1 EUR** 71.9180 71.1555 **1 GBP** 85.5450 85.1154 **100 YEN** 58.66 57.72 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
