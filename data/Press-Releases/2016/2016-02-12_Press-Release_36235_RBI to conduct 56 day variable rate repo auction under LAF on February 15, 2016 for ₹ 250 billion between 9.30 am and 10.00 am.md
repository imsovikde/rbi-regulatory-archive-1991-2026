## February 12, 2016

## RBI to conduct 56 day variable rate repo auction under LAF

## on February 15, 2016 for 250 billion between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 56 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of 250 billion on February 15, 2016, (Monday). The auction will be conducted between 9.30 a.m. and 10.00 a.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 56 day variable rate repo will take place on April 11, 2016 (Monday).
