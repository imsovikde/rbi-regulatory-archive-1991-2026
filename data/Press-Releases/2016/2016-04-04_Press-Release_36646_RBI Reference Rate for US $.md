April 04, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.2430 on April 04, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 31, 2016) was

66.3329. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 31, 2016 April 04, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.0955 75.3713 **1 GBP** 95.0882 94.1578 **100 YEN** 59.06 59.44 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
