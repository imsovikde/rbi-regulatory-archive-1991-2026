May 16, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8216 on May 16, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 13, 2016) was

66.7609. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 13, 2016 May 16, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.7202 75.5953 **1 GBP** 96.1557 95.9625 **100 YEN** 61.47 61.45 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
