January 12, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8905 on January 12, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (January 11, 2016) was

66.7885. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **January 11, 2016 January 12, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.9264 72.7434 **1 GBP** 97.0570 97.1518 **100 YEN** 56.93 56.91 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
