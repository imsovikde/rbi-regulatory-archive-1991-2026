December 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.9136 on December 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (December 21, 2016) was

67.8724. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **December 21, 2016 December 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 70.6077 70.8678 **1 GBP** 83.9649 83.9140 **100 YEN** 57.70 57.73 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
