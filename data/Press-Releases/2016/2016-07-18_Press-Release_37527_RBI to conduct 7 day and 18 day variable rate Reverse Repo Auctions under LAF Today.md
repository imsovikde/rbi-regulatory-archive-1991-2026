## RBI to conduct 7 day and 18 day variable rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (July 18, 2016, Monday) between **11.00 am and 11.30 am** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (billion)|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|150|7|July 25, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|100|18|August 5, 2016 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get the allotment at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
