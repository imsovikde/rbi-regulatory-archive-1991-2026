September 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8451 on September 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 21, 2016) was

67.0631. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 21, 2016 September 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6949 75.0203 **1 GBP** 87.0077 87.3398 **100 YEN** 65.49 66.65 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
