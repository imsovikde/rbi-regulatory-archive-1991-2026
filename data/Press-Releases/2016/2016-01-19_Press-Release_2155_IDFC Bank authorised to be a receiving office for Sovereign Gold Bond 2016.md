## January 19, 2016

## IDFC Bank authorised to be a receiving office for Sovereign Gold Bond 2016

The Government of India, in consultation with Reserve Bank of India, has decided to notify “IDFC Bank Limited” as a receiving office for the second tranche of Sovereign Gold Bond issuance (SGB Scheme 2016). The <u>notification dated January</u> <u>14, 2016</u> has been amended to this effect. The other terms and conditions of the Notification shall remain unchanged.
