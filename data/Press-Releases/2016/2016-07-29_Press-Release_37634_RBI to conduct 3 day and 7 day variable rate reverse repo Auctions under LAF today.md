## July 29, 2016

## RBI to conduct 3 day and 7 day variable rate reverse repo

## Auctions under LAF today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (July 29, 2016, Friday) between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified Amount**

|Sl No.|(billion)|Tenor|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|200|3|August 01, 2016 (Monday)|

2 200 7 August 05, 2016 (Friday)

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
