June 15, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.1596 on June 15, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 14, 2016) was

67.1520. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 14, 2016 June 15, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.7542 75.2859 **1 GBP** 95.1007 95.1114 **100 YEN** 63.46 63.19 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
