## RBI to conduct Overnight and 2 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions today (December 14, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Notified** **Sl. Tenor** **Amount Window Timing Date of Reversal** **No. (day)** **( billion)**
