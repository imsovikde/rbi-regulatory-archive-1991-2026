April 07, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4698 on April 07, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 06, 2016) was

66.5807. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 06, 2016 April 07, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.6290 75.8952 **1 GBP** 94.0918 94.0415 **100 YEN** 60.31 61.04 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
