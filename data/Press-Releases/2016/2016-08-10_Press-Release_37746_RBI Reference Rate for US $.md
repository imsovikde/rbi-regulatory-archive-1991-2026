August 10, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7442 on August 10, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 09, 2016) was

66.9633. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 09, 2016 August 10, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.1752 74.3797 **1 GBP** 86.9920 87.0411 **100 YEN** 65.36 65.84 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
