June 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.5570 on June 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 21, 2016) was

67.4767. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 21, 2016 June 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.4511 76.0354 **1 GBP** 99.2582 99.1534 **100 YEN** 64.59 64.61 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
