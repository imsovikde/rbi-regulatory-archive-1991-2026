July 05, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.4028 on July 05, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 04, 2016) was

67.1848. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 04, 2016 July 05, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.8304 75.0328 **1 GBP** 89.3020 89.1537 **100 YEN** 65.47 66.06 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
