## November 14, 2016

**DCCBs can allow existing customers to withdraw upto** ` **24,000 from their** **accounts : RBI**

|The Reserve Bank of India has today clarified that District Central Cooperative Banks||
|---|---|
|(DCCBs) can allow their existing customers to withdraw money from their accounts||
|upto ` 24,000 per week|upto November 24, 2016. However, no exchange facility|
|against the specified bank notes (` 500 and ` 1000) or deposit of such notes|should|
|be entertained by them.|The Reserve Bank has accordingly advised all banks to|
|permit withdrawal of cash by DCCBs from their accounts based on need.|The cash|
|withdrawal limit of ` 24,000 per week is not applicable to withdrawal of cash by a||
|DCCB from its account with any other bank.||
