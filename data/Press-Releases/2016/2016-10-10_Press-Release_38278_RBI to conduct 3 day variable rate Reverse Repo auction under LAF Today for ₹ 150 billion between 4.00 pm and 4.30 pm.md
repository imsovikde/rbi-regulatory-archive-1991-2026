## October 10, 2016

## RBI to conduct 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF Today

## for 150 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo auction for a notified amount of 150 billion today (October 10, 2016, Monday). The auction will be conducted between **4.00 pm and 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo will take place on October 13, 2016 (Thursday).
