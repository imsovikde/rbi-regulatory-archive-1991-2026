August 05, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.8141 on August 05, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (August 04, 2016) was

66.9447. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **August 04, 2016 August 05, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.5965 74.4510 **1 GBP** 89.0699 87.7470 **100 YEN** 65.95 66.13 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
