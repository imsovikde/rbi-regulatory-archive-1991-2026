July 13, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.2046 on July 13, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 12, 2016) was

67.1471. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 12, 2016 July 13, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.4460 74.3484 **1 GBP** 87.8620 89.2880 **100 YEN** 64.94 64.53 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
