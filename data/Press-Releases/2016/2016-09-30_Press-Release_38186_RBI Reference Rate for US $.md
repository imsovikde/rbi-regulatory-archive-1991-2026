September 30, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6596 on September 30, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (September 29, 2016) was

66.5521. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **September 29, 2016 September 30, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.6848 74.7521 **1 GBP** 86.6109 86.4242 **100 YEN** 65.43 66.05 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
