April 29, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5176 on April 29, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 28, 2016) was

66.4045. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 28, 2016 April 29, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.4222 75.7303 **1 GBP** 96.8310 97.4017 **100 YEN** 61.32 61.96 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
