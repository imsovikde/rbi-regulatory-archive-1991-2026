July 28, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.0550 on July 28, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (July 27, 2016) was

67.2362. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **July 27, 2016 July 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 73.8859 74.3640 **1 GBP** 88.3013 88.4254 **100 YEN** 63.57 64.07 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
