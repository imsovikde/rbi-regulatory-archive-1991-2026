## November 28, 2016

## RBI to conduct Overnight Variable Rate Repo Auction under LAF Today

**For** ₹ **2000 billion between 09.30 am and 10.30 am**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct an Overnight variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ₹ 2000 billion today (November 28, 2016, Monday). The auction will be conducted between 09.30 am and 10.30 am as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight variable rate repo will take place on November 29, 2016 (Tuesday).
