## RBI to conduct Overnight and 3 day variable rate Reverse Repo

## Auctions under LAF today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (July 04, 2016, Monday) between 4.00 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl||Notified Amount|Tenor|Date of Reversal|

|2||400|3|July 07, 2016 (Thursday)|
