May 03, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.2698 on May 03, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 02, 2016) was

66.3435. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 02, 2016 May 03, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.0562 76.6145 **1 GBP** 97.0207 97.5690 **100 YEN** 62.31 62.65 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
