October 04, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.5559 on October 04, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (October 03, 2016) was

66.5330. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **October 03, 2016 October 04, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.7166 74.4428 **1 GBP** 85.8675 85.3180 **100 YEN** 65.70 65.08 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
