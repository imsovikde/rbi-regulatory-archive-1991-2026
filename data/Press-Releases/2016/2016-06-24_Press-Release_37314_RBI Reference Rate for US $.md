June 24, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.0144 on June 24, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 23, 2016) was

67.3702. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 23, 2016 June 24, 2016** **1 EUR** 76.3776 75.1015 **1 GBP** 99.4721 92.9553 **100 YEN** 64.52 66.45 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
