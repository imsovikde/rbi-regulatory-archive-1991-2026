## June 30, 2016

## Sectoral Deployment of Bank Credit – May 2016

Data on sectoral deployment of bank credit collected on a monthly basis from select 46 scheduled commercial banks, accounting for about 95 per cent of the total non-food credit deployed by all scheduled commercial banks, for the month of May 2016 are set out in <u>Statements I and II</u>. These data are also available in the Real-Time Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (<u>[http://dbie.rbi.org.in](http://dbie.rbi.org.in)</u>).

## Highlights of the sectoral deployment of bank credit are given below:

- On a year-on-year (y-o-y) basis, non-food bank credit increased by 8.4 per cent in May 2016 as compared with the increase of 9.0 per cent in May 2015.
- Credit to agriculture and allied activities increased by 15.6 per cent in May 2016 as compared with the increase of 11.0 per cent in May 2015.
- Credit to industry increased by 0.9 per cent in May 2016 as compared with the increase of 5.2 per cent in May 2015. Credit declined to several sub- sectors, some of which include food processing, infrastructure and vehicles, vehicle parts & transport equipment.
- Credit to the services sector increased by 9.3 per cent in May 2016 as compared with the increase of 9.1 per cent in May 2015.
- Personal loans increased by 19.1 per cent in May 2016, up from the increase of 16.6 per cent in May 2015.
