May 19, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.2307 on May 19, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (May 18, 2016) was

66.9131. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **May 18, 2016 May 19, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.4847 75.4530 **1 GBP** 96.6091 98.0493 **100 YEN** 61.25 61.01 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
