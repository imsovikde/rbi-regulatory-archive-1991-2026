November 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 68.2317 on November 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (November 21, 2016) was

68.2575. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **November 21, 2016 November 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 72.3530 72.4348 **1 GBP** 84.2025 85.1668 **100 YEN** 61.45 61.46 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
