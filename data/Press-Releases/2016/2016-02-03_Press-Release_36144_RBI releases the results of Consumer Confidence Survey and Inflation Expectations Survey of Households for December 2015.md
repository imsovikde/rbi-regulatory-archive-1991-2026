February 03, 2016 **RBI releases the results of Consumer Confidence Survey and Inflation Expectations Survey of Households for December 2015**

The Reserve Bank of India today released on its website the results of the following two Surveys:

(i) <u>Consumer Confidence Survey: December 2015</u> (ii) <u>Inflation Expectations Survey of Households: December 2015 (Round 42)</u> The Survey results are based on the feedback received from the respondents
to the Surveys and do not necessarily reflect the views of the Reserve Bank of India.
