## RBI to conduct 3 and 7 day variable rate Reverse Repo Auctions under LAF Today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate reverse repo auctions today (September 30, 2016, Friday) between **4.00 pm to 4.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (Billion)|Tenor (day)|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|600|3|October 3, 2016 (Monday)|
|2|300|7|October 7, 2016 (Friday)|

Successful offers will get accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected.
