**RBI to conduct Overnight and 2 day Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions under LAF on December 14, 2016**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct the following Variable rate Reverse Repo auctions on December 14, 2016 (Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.
