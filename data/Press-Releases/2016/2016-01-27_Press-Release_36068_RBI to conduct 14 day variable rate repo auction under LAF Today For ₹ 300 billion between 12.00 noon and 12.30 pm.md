## RBI to conduct 14 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**For** ` **300 billion between 12.00 noon and 12.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 14 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 300 billion today (January 27, 2016, Wednesday). The auction will be conducted between **12.00 noon and 12.30 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 14 day variable rate repo will take place on February 10, 2016 (Wednesday).
