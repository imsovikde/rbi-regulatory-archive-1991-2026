## February 17, 2016

## RBI to conduct 4 day variable rate repo auction under LAF on

## February 18, 2016 for 150 billion between 9.15 a.m. and 9.45 a.m.

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 4 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of ` 150 billion on February 18, 2016, Thursday. The auction will be conducted between 9.15 a.m. and 9.45 a.m. as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 4 day variable rate repo will take place on February 22, 2016 (Monday).
