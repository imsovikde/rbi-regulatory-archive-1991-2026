## RBI to conduct Overnight and 5 day variable rate Repo Auctions under LAF today

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate repo auctions today (March 30, 2016, Wednesday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>.

|Sl No.|Notified Amount (` Billion)|Tenor|Window Timings|Date of Reversal|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|1|300|1|10.15 am – 10.45 am|March 31, 2016 (Thursday)|
|2|100|5|12.00 pm – 12.30 pm|April 04, 2016 (Monday)|

Successful bids will get the allotment at their respective rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected.
