June 27, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 67.9000 on June 27, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 24, 2016) was

68.0144. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 24, 2016 June 27, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.1015 74.8801 **1 GBP** 92.9553 90.9113 **100 YEN** 66.45 66.69 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
