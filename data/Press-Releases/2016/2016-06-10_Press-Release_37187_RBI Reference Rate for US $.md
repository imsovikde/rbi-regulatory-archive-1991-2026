June 10, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.7948 on June 10, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (June 09, 2016) was

66.6250. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **June 09, 2016 June 10, 2016** **1 EUR** 75.8925 75.4714 **1 GBP** 96.7195 96.5719 **100 YEN** 62.41 62.40 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
