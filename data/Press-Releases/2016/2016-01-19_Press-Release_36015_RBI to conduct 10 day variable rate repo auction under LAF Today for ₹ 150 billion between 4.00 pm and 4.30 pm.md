## RBI to conduct 10 day variable rate repo auction under LAF Today

**for** ` **150 billion between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct 10 day variable rate repo auction for a notified amount of `150 billion today (January 19, 2016, Tuesday). The auction will be conducted between 4.00 pm and 4.30 pm as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful bidders will get the allotment at their respective bid rates. Bids at or below the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the 10 day variable rate repo will take place on January 29, 2016 (Friday).
