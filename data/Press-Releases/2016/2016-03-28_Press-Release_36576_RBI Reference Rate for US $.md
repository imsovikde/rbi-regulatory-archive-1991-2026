March 28, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.6680 on March 28, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (March 23, 2016) was

66.8640. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **March 23, 2016 March 28, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.9077 74.4215 **1 GBP** 94.9268 94.3019 **100 YEN** 59.52 58.72 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
