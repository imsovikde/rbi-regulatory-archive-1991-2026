## January 01, 2016

**RBI to conduct 28 day and 56 day variable rate Repo Auctions under LAF on January 01, 2016**

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct following variable rate repo auctions today (January 01, 2016, Friday) as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. **Sl. Notified Amount Tenor Window Timings Date of Reversal** **No. ( Billion)**

1. 150 28 <u>4.00 p.m. – 4.30 p.m.</u> January 29, 2016 (Friday)
2. 100 56 <u>4.00 p.m. – 4.30 p.m.</u> February 26, 2016 (Friday) Successful bids will get the allotment at their respective rates. Bids at or below
the repo rate will be rejected.
