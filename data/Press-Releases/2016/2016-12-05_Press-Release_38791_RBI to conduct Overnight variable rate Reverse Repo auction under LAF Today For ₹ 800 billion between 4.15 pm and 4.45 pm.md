## December 05, 2016

## RBI to conduct Overnight variable rate Reverse Repo auction under LAF Today

## For 800 billion between 4.15 pm and 4.45 pm

The Reserve Bank of India will conduct Overnight variable rate Reverse Repo auction for a notified amount of 800 billion today (December 5, 2016, Monday). The auction will be conducted between **4.15 pm and 4.45 pm** as per the revised guidelines on Term Repo Auctions issued on <u>February 13, 2014</u>. Successful offers will be accepted at their respective offered rates. Offers at or above the repo rate will be rejected. The reversal of the Overnight variable rate Reverse Repo will take place on December 6, 2016 (Tuesday).
