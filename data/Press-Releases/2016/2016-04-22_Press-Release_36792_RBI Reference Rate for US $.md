April 22, 2016 **RBI Reference Rate for US $** The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US Dollar is 66.4925 on April 22, 2016. The corresponding rate for the previous day (April 21, 2016) was

66.2930. Based on the reference rate for the US Dollar and the middle rates of the
cross-currency quotes, the exchange rate of EUR, GBP and JPY against the Rupee are given below: **Currency Date** **April 21, 2016 April 22, 2016** **1 EUR** 74.8978 75.1033 **1 GBP** 95.1437 95.4433 **100 YEN** 60.48 60.15 <u>Note : The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on the reference rate.</u>
