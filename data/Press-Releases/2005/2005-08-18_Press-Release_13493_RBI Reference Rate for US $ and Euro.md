## August 18, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4700 on August 18, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 17, 2005) were Rs.43.5400 and Rs.53.5800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
