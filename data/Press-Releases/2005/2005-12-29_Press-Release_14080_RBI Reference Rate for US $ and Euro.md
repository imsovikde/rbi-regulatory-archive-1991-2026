## December 29, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.5000 on December 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 28, 2005) were Rs.45.2600 and Rs.53.6900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
