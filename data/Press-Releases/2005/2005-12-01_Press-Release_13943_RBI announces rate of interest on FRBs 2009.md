# RBI announces rate of interest on FRBs 2009

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> December 1, 2005

RBI announces rate of interest on FRBs 2009

The rate of interest on the Government of India Floating Rate Bonds, 2009 (FRB, 2009) applicable for the half year (December 6, 2005 to June 5,

2006) shall be 5.81 per cent per annum. It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2009 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on December 5, 2001) over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest shall be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the said last six auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills works out to be 5.82 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on December 5, 2001 was (-) 0.01 (minus 0.01). The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release : 2005-2006/661
