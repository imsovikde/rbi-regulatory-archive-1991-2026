## July 25, 2005

Vaibhav Gems Ltd – Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India on July 25, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Vaibhav Gems Ltd., under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Vaibhav Gems Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up equity capital.
