## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9000 on July 12, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 11, 2005) were Rs.43.5600 and Rs.52.3500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
