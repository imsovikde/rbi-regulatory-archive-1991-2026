## Financial Sector Technology Vision Document on RBI Site

Governor had, in the Annual Policy Statement 2005-06, announced that the Reserve Bank would be bringing out a ‘Financial Sector Technology Vision Document’ aimed at providing banks with inputs on medium term policy perspectives. A draft ‘Vision Document’ prepared on the basis of comments / observations received from banks in the country was put on the Bank’s web site for wider comments, in May, 2005. Based on the feedback, the ‘Financial Sector Technology Vision Document’ has been finalised and has been put in the web site of the Bank.

In a nutshell, the ‘Financial Sector Technology Vision’ document, elucidates the Technology Vision for the financial sector, with coverage including the following:

- IT for regulation and supervision
- IT and IDRBT
- IT for the financial sector
- IT for Government related functions
## Vision Statement:

## ‘ IT for Efficiency and excellence’

## Corporate Objective:

**“Enabling banks to leverage on IT for better customer service, improved housekeeping and overall systemic efficiency”**

The Financial Sector Technology Vision document outlines the previews and prospects as envisioned by the Reserve Bank for the medium term period covering the period 2005-2008.
