## July 27, 2005

Holidays declared on July 27 and 28, 2005 under Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 by the Government of Maharashtra

The Government of Maharashtra has declared public holidays on July 27 and 28, 2005, under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 in the districts of Mumbai, Thane and Raigad, due to heavy rains and consequent dislocation of transport services. Consequently, Reserve Bank offices in Mumbai and Belapur will also remain closed.
