## November 29, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.2900 on November 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 28, 2005) were Rs.45.9400 and Rs.53.7400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|November 28, 2005|78.6723|38.35|
