## December 22, 2005

Monitoring of Investments by FIIs/NRIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 22, 2005 that the aggregate foreign share holdings in the undernoted banks have gone down below the limit shown against their names. The Reserve Bank has further stated that it would be in order to purchase the shares of these banks through stock exchanges on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) and Non- Resident Indians (NRIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) after obtaining its prior approval.

1. Bank of Baroda : 20 per cent
2. ICICI Bank : 74 per cent
