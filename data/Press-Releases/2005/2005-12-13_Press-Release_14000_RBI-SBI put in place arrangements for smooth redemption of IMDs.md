## December 13, 2005.

RBI-SBI put in place arrangements for smooth redemption of IMDs

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has put in place arrangements for redemption of India Millennium Deposits (IMDs), in close co-ordination with the State Bank of India (SBI). Appropriate preparations and arrangements have been made to ensure that the redemption is done smoothly, in time and without causing any impact on the money, government securities and foreign exchange markets.

The IMDs for US$ 5.5 billion raised in the year 2000 from non-resident Indians by SBI in US dollars, Pound Sterling and Euro, with the approval of the Government of India (GoI) and RBI, are due for repayment on December 29, 2005. It is estimated that the redemption would entail foreign exchange outgo of about US$ 7.3 billion (principal plus interest), which will be met entirely by RBI by way of direct sale out of its foreign exchange reserves to SBI at the prevailing market rate. The rupee consideration for this sale, which is likely to be around Rs.33,000 crore, will be paid by SBI.

The highlights of the arrangements are :

- The entire foreign exchange outgo of about US$ 7.3 billion will be met by RBI by way of sale out of its foreign exchange reserves by way of sale to SBI.
- SBI has taken adequate steps to mobilize rupee resources to purchase foreign exchange from RBI.
- The exchange loss, if any, on account of depreciation of rupee would be shared between SBI and GoI and accordingly, both the parties have contributed their respective share in the exchange loss that has accrued during certain years after the launch of IMDs. The contributions received are held with RBI in the Maintenance of Value (MoV) account.
- Going by the present liquidity conditions in the market, the system should be in a position to take care of the redemption requirements. The eligible market participants can take recourse to the Liquidity Adjustment Facility(LAF) & Second Liquidity Adjustment Facility (SLAF) of RBI for short-term residual mismatches in the rupee funds position.
