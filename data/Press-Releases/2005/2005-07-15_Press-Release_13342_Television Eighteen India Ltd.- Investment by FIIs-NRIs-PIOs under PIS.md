Television Eighteen India Ltd.:

Investment byFlls/NRls/PiOs underPIS

n       s  o   s

Foreign Institutional Investors (Flls)/Non-Resident Indians (NRls)/Persons of

Indian Origin (PlOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PiS) in the paid up

capital of the Television Eighteen India Ltd., has gone below the limit of 24 per

cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that purchase of equity

behalf of such Flls/NRls/PIOs, who have obtained its permission to invest under

PIS is in order.
