## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1600 on July 13, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 12, 2005) were Rs.43.5300 and Rs.52.9000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
