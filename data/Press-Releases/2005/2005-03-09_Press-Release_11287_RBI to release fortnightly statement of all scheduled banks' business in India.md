# RBI to release fortnightly statement of all scheduled banks' business in India

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 09, 2005 RBI to release fortnightly statement of all scheduled banks' business in India The Reserve Bank of India today released the statement all scheduled banks' business in India. The statement which was so far issued every fortnightly by the Reserve Bank's Department of Banking Operations and Development in terms of Section 43 of the Reserve Bank of India Act relates to the scheduled banks' position in India as on the close of business of February 18, 2005. The statement covers, among other things, liabilities to the banking system including demand and time deposits from banks, bank credit and investments. The statement further covers the business data from scheduled commercial banks including Regional Rural Banks and all scheduled banks, which include urban cooperative banks. The statement additionally includes food credit outstanding of scheduled commercial and state cooperative banks. The statement also comprises comparative position for Friday of the preceding fortnight and corresponding Friday of the previous year. It may be noted that the Reserve Bank has, so far, been releasing data relating to scheduled commercial banks' business in India on a fortnightly basis in its Weekly Statistical Supplement to the Reserve Bank of India Bulletin. The supplement is available on the RBI website at www.wss.rbi.org.in The additional press release on scheduled banks' data gives business data of all scheduled banks apart from the data on scheduled commercial banks' business given in the Weekly Statistical Supplement. The press release will be issued every fortnight and will be placed on the RBI website under Statistics section – Fortnightly. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2004-2005 / 940
