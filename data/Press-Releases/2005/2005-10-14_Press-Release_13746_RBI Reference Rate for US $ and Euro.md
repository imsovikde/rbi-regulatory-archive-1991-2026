## October 14, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.8900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9500 on October 14, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 13, 2005) were Rs.44.8900 and Rs.53.8300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
