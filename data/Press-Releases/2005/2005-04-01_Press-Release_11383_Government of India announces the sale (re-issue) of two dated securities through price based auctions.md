# Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of two dated securities through price based auctions

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 1, 2005 Government of India announces the sale (re-issue) of two dated securities through price based auctions. The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of “6.85 percent Government Stock 2012” for a notified amount of Rs.5, 000 crore through a price based auction using multiple price auction method. The Government of India have also announced the sale (re-issue) of “7.95 percent Government Stock 2032” for a notified amount of Rs.3, 000 crore through a price based auction using multiple price auction method. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on Tuesday, April 5, 2005. Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of both the stocks will be allotted to eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non- Competitive Bidding Facility in the Auction of Government Securities. Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, April 5, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS, while all the non-competitive bids should be submitted in the prescribed physical form. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the minimum price at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on April 5, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 6, 2005 (Wednesday). Both the stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility. Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 p.m. on April 4, 2005 (Monday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1032
