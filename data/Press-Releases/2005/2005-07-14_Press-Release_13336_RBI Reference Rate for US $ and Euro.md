## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.5400 on July 14, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 13, 2005) were Rs.43.5200 and Rs.53.1600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
