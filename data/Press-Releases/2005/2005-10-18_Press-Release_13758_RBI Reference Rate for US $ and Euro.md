## October 18, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1000 on October 18, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 17, 2005) were Rs.44.8200 and Rs.54.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|October 17, 2005|79.1700|39.22|
|October 18, 2005|78.9605|39.10|
