## September 22, 2005

Six State Governments announce the auction of State Development Loans 2015

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.839.420 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

State

|Sr.||Notified Amount||
|---|---|---|---|
|No.||(Rs. in Crore)||
|1|Assam||208.839|
|2|Jharkhand||117.812|
|3|Kerala||145.818|
|4|Maharashtra||290.000|
|5|Manipur||36.951|
|6|Tripura Total||40.000 839.420|

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on September 27, 2005 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that office on Tuesday, September 27, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 p.m. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on September 27, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on September 28, 2005 (Wednesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
