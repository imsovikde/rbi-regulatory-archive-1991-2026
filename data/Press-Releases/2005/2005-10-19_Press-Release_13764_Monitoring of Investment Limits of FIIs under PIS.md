Monitoring of Investment Limits of FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on October 19, 2005, has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Indraprastha Gas Limited should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Indrapgrastha Gas Limited has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
