## November 16, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6600 on November 16, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 14, 2005) were Rs.45.6700 and Rs.53.7100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
