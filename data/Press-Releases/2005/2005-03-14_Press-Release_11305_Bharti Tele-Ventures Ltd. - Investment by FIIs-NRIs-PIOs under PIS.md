# Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd. : Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 14, 2005 Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd. : Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on March 14, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd.  should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians(NRIs)/ Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd. has reached the trigger limit of 48 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/957
