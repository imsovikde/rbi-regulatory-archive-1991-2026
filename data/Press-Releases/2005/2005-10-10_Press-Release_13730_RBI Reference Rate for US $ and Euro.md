## October 10, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.6200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1500 on October 10, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 7, 2005) were Rs.44.3200 and Rs.53.9500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
