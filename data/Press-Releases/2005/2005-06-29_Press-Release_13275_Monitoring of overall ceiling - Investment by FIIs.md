## Monitoring of overall ceiling-Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on June 29, 2005 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Ltd. through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 74 per cent of its paid up capital, as Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Ltd. have passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
