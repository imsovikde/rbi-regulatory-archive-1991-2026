# RBI announces rate of interest on FRB 2009

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 31, 2005 RBI announces rate of interest on FRB 2009 The rate of interest on the Government of India Floating Rate Bonds, 2009 (FRB, 2009) applicable for the half year (June 6, 2005 to December 5, 2005) shall be 5.59 percent per annum. It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2009 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on December 5, 2001) over and above the variable base rate. The variable base rate for payment of interest shall be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last six auctions of Government of India 364 day Treasury Bills held up to the commencement of the respective half yearly coupon period. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the said last six auctions of Government of India 364 day Treasury Bills works out to be 5.60 per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on December 5, 2001 was (-) 0.01 (minus 0.01). The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.

P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1257
