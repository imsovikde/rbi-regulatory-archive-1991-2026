# Oriental Bank of Commerce: Investment by FIIs/NRIs/ PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in June 6, 2005 Oriental Bank of Commerce: Investment by FIIs/NRIs/ PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on June 6, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Oriental Bank of Commerce should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Oriental Bank of Commerce has reached the overall limit of 20 per cent of its paid up capital. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/1280
