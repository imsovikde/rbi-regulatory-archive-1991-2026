## December 20, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9900 on December 20, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 19, 2005) were Rs.45.2400 and Rs.54.3700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
