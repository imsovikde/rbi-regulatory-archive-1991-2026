# Tap sale of 7.02 per cent state Development Loans 2015 closed for nine states

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in January 11, 2005 Tap sale of 7.02 per cent state Development Loans 2015 closed for nine states The 'on tap' sale of 7.02 per cent ten years State Development Loans, 2015 has closed in respect of nine States, i.e. Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Tamilnadu on receipt of the respective target amounts.  Amounts of allotment in respect of these states are being advised to the investors concerned. The sale on tap in respect of loans for remaining eleven States, viz., Assam, Bihar, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Punjab, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal will be kept open on January 12, 2005 to collect the balance target amount. Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai Office. Applications will be received at these offices during banking hours while the tap sale is open.  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the tap sale during the banking hours on January 12, 2005 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on January 13, 2005 in respect of subscriptions received/allotted both on January 11 and January 12, 2005. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/736
