## October 20, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.0900 on October 20, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 19, 2005) were Rs.45.3200 and Rs.53.8600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
