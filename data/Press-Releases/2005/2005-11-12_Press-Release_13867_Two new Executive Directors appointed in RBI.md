# Two new Executive Directors appointed in RBI

## RESERVE BANK OF INDIA

**Date:** 12 Nov 2005 **Two new Executive Directors appointed in RBI**

Shri Anand Sinha and Shri. V. S. Das have been appointed Executive Directors of the Reserve Bank of India w.e.f. November 11, 2005. Shri Anand Sinha has taken charge of Department of Banking Operations and Development, Department of Banking Supervision and Foreign Exchange Department. Shri. V. S. Das has taken charge of Department of Non-Banking Supervision, Rural Planning & Credit Department, Urban Banks Department, Secretary's Department and Press Relations Division. Shri V. S. Das continues to be the Chief Public Information Officer of the Reserve Bank of India.
