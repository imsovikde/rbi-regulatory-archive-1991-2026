## August 26, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.7300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.8200 on August 26, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 25, 2005) were Rs.43.7600 and Rs.53.8600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
