## August 11, 2005

## RBI Board meets today in Mumbai

The Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank of India met today in Mumbai. The following members were present : Shri Y.H. Malegam, Prof. Mihir Rakshit, Shri K. Madhava Rao, Shri Ratan N. Tata, Dr. Amrita Patel, Shri K.P. Singh, Prof. V.S. Vyas, Shri D.S. Brar, Shri H.P. Ranina, Shri N.R. Narayana Murthy, Shri Suresh Krishna, Dr. Ashok Ganguly and Government Nominee-Shri Ashok K. Jha, Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance. Dr. Y.V. Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India chaired the meeting, Deputy Governors of the Reserve Bank Dr. Rakesh Mohan, Smt. K.J. Udeshi, Shri V. Leeladhar and Smt. Shyamala Gopinath were also present. The meeting reviewed key economic, monetary and financial developments.

The Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank meets seven times a year. Apart from holding four meetings in the four metropolises – with one meeting in New Delhi held after the Union Budget and addressed by the Finance Minister – the rest of the meetings are held in other state capitals by rotation. The main function of the Central Board of Directors of the Reserve Bank is to provide overall direction to its affairs.
