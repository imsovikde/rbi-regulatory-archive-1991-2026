## July 18, 2005

## RBI mourns demise of Dr. I. G. Patel

The employees of the Reserve Bank of India passed the following resolution at the condolence meeting:

We have learnt with profound sorrow the sad demise of Dr. I.G. Patel, former Governor of the Reserve Bank of India. Dr. Patel was Governor of the Bank from December 1, 1977 to September 15, 1982.

Dr. Patel was at the helm of affairs in the Reserve Bank at a very difficult time and grappled with a variety of complex issues with a sense of vision, purpose and firmness combined with fairness. His tenure witnessed the demonetisation of high denomination notes, nationalisation of six private sector banks, introduction of targets for priority sector lending and departmental reorganization in the Bank. He played active role in availing of the Extended Fund Facility, the largest in the history of IMF, in 1981 due to the balance of payments difficulties. As Governor of the Bank, he upheld the highest traditions of Central Banking and made invaluable contributions to the evolution of monetary and banking policy. Even after retirement he was closely associated as Chairman of the Committee for writing of History of the RBI and as a Member of the Board of Management of the Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research and as Chairman of the Committee for Licensing of New Private Sector Banks.

Dr. Patel, known to his close friends and colleagues as 'IG' had a distinguished academic career. Before coming to the Bank, he held many important positions in the Central Government like the Chief Economic Adviser, Adviser in the Planning Commission and Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs in the Ministry.

It was appropriate that Dr. Patel's distinguished career should culminate in his appointment as the First Asian Director of the London School of Economics in 1984. In recognition of his outstanding contribution in the fields of economics, finance, scholarship and education, in Britain and the Commonwealth, Dr. Patel was awarded an honorary Knighthood of the British Empire (KBE).

We express our deep sympathy to his wife, Smt. Alaknanda Patel and their daughter, Ms. Rehana and other members of the family in their bereavement.
