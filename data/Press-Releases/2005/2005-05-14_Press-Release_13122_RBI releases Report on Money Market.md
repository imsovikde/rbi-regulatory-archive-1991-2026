# RBI releases Report on Money Market

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 14, 2005 RBI releases Report on Money Market The Reserve Bank of India placed on its website the report of an internal technical group on money market. The Reserve Bank had set up the group in February 2005 to review developments and current status of money market in India and cross- country experience in money market and to suggest measures for further development of the money market in India. The Governor had, in his Annual Policy Statement on April 28, 2005, stated that the report of the technical group would be placed on the RBI website for wider dissemination. The Group has maintained that the policy thrust should be to encourage collateralised market, develop the rupee yield curve, ensure transparency and better price discovery, provide avenues for better risk management and strengthen monetary operations. With this in view, it ahs made detailed recommendations relating to call/notice money market, repo/CBLO, term money, certificate of deposits, commercial paper, FRAs/IRS, MIBOR-Linked Short-term Papers, timing of LAF. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1193
