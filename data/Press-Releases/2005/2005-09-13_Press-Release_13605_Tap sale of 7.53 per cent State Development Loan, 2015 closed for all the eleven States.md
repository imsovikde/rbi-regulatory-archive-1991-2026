## September 13, 2005

Tap sale of 7.53 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 closed for all the eleven States

The 'on-tap' sale of 7.53 per cent ten-year State Development Loan, 2015 has closed in respect of all the eleven states, viz, Arunachal Pradesh, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal on receipt of the respective target amounts. Amounts of allotment in respect of these states are being advised to the investors concerned. It may be recalled that the sale opened on September 13, 2005 on behalf of 11 states for an aggregate amount of around Rs. 2900 crore. Against this, applications for an aggregate amount of Rs. 8,850 crore were received of which, subscriptions for an aggregate amount of Rs. 2931 crore are being retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the allottees on September 15, 2005, in respect of subscriptions received on September 13, 2005.
