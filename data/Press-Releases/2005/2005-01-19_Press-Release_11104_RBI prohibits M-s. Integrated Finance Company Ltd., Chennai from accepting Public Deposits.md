# RBI prohibits M/s. Integrated Finance Company Ltd., Chennai from accepting Public Deposits

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in January 19, 2005 RBI prohibits M/s. Integrated Finance Company Ltd., Chennai from accepting Public Deposits The Reserve Bank of India has prohibited M/s. Integrated Finance Company Limited, `Vairams', 112, Sir Thyagaraya Road, T. Nagar, Chennai-600 017, from accepting any deposits, from any person, in any form, whether by way of fresh deposits or renewal, of the existing deposits or otherwise, until further orders. The Reserve Bank has also prohibited M/s. Integrated Finance Co. Ltd., in public interest, not to sell, transfer, create charge or mortgage or deal in any manner with its properties and assets, without its prior written permission. The Reserve Bank has issued these orders in exercise of powers conferred on it under Section 45MB (1) and (2) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/762
