## November 9, 2005

RBI announces rate of interest on FRBs 2012

The rate of interest on the Floating Rate Bonds, 2012 (FRB, 2012) applicable for the year (November 10, 2005 to November 9, 2006) shall be

5.96 percent per annum. It may be recalled that the rate of interest on the FRB, 2012 was set at a mark-up (as decided in the auction held on November 7, 2003) over and above the variable base rate to be announced annually. The variable base rate for payment of interest shall be the average rate (rounded off up to two decimal places) of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the last three auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills held up to November 10. The variable base rate based on the average rate of the implicit yields at cut-off prices of the said last three auctions of Government of India 364-day Treasury Bills worked out to be 5.87per cent. The mark-up decided in the auction held on November 7, 2003 was (+) 0.09 (plus 0.09). The coupon rate has been fixed accordingly.
Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/565
