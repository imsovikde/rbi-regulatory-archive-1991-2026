## September 27, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8000 on September 27, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 26, 2005) were Rs.43.9500 and Rs.52.8700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
