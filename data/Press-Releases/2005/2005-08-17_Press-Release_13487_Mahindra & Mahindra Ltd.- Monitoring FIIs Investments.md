Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd.: Monitoring FIIs Investments

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on August 17, 2005 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd., through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 49 per cent of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/213
