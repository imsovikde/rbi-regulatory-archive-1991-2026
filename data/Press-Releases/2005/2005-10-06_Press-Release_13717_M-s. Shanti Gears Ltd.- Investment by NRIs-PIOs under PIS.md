## October 6, 2005

M/s. Shanti Gears Ltd.: Investment by NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on October 6, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of M/s. Shanti Gears Ltd., should be made on behalf of Non-Resident Indians(NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that M/s. Shanti Gears Ltd., has reached the limit of 8 per cent of its paid up capital.
