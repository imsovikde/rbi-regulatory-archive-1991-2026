# RBI opens Centralised Pension Cell

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in July 1, 2005 RBI opens Centralised Pension Cell The Reserve Bank of India has centralised the Pension Payments of its staff who retired from Mumbai by setting up a Centralised Pension Cell. The cell was inauguarated today by Deputy Governor, Smt. Shyamala Gopinath. Speaking at the function, she mentioned that while decentralisation  is beneficial for better customer service, in the era of computerisation centralisation of processing is most ideal from the point of view of cost and convenience. The Cell will be headed by Mr. Philip Sequeira, Deputy General Manager and he can be contacted at his Bandra Office on Tel.No.2657 1549 for any of the matters relating to pension of RBites upto CGM level who retired from Mumbai. The pension work of retired Executives will continue to be handled by DEBC, Central Office. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2005-2006/5
