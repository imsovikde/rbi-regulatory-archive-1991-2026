## October 13, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.8900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.8300 on October 13, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 11, 2005) were Rs.44.9400 and Rs.53.9900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|October 11, 2005|78.5956|39.33|
