# 182 Day Treasury Bills Auction: Rs.500 crore under regular auction

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> November 25, 2005

182 Day Treasury Bills Auction: Rs.500 crore under regular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 182 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.500 crore. The auction will be conducted using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation to non- competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Reserve Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F.2(12)-W&M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, November 30, 2005 before 1230 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, December 2, 2005.

Taking into account all relevant factors, Reserve Bank has decided that the auction of 182-day Treasury Bills for Rs.1000 crore scheduled for November 30, 2005 under quarterly indicative schedule for MSS will not be conducted. This implies release Rs.1000 crore from MSS account since the 182-day Treasury Bills issued earlier under MSS on August 31, 2005 will redeem on December 2, 2005.
