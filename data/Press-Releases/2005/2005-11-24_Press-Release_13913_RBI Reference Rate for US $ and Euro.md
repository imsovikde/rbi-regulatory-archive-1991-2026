## November 24, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9900 on November 24, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 23, 2005) were Rs.45.7400 and Rs.54.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 23, 2005|78.8969|38.57|
|November 24, 2005|78.6956|38.46|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
