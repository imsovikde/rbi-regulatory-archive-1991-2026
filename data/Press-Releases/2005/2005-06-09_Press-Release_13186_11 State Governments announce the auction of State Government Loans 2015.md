## June 9, 2005

## 11 State Governments announce the auction of

## State Government Loans 2015

The following State Governments have announced the sale of 10-year State Development Loans for an aggregate amount of Rs.2481.15 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method.

The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on June 14, 2005 (Tuesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, June 14, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on June 14, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on June 15, 2005 (Wednesday) at Mumbai and at respective Regional Offices of Reserve Bank of India. The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.
