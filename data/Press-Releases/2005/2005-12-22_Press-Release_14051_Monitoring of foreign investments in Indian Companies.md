Monitoring of foreign investments in Indian Companies

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 22, 2005 that Non-Residents Indians (NRIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of the following companies through stock exchanges in India upto 24 per cent of their paid up capital, as these companies have passed resolutions to this effect at their General Body Meetings.

1. K. Sera Sera Productions Ltd.
2. Shanti Gears Ltd.
