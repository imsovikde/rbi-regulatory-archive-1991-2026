# Punjab National Bank: Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

SS S PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 31, 2005 Punjab National Bank: Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India has notified on March 31, 2005 that the holding of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) under the portfolio investment scheme (PIS) in the paid up capital of the Punjab National Bank has gone below the limit of 20 per cent of its paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has stated that further purchase of equity shares of Punjab National Bank on behalf of FIIs/NRIs/PIOs through stock exchanges in India would be made only with its prior approval. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/1028
