## November 3, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.6200 on November 3, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 2, 2005) were Rs.45.1800 and Rs.54.3600 respectively.
