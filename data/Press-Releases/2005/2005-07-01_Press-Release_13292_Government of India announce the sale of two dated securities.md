July 1, 2005

Government of India announce the sale of two dated securities

The Government of India have announced the sale (re-issue) of "7.27 percent

price based auction using multiple price method. The Government of India have also

method. The auctions will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBl) at

Mumbai onTuesday,July5,2005.

Up to 5% of the notified amount of the sale of the stocks will be allotted to

eligible individuals and institutions as per the Scheme for Non-Competitive Bidding

Facility in the Auction of Government Securities for both the auctions.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve

Bank of India, Mumbai Office, Public Debt Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 and RBl

website www.rbi.org.in should be submitted to that Office on Tuesday, July 5, 2005.

The NDS members should submit competitive as well as non-competitive bids in

electronic format using Primary Market Operation (PMO) module of NDS. All bids

should be submitted by 12.30 p.m..

The result of the auctions will be announced on July 5, 2005 (Tuesday) and

payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on July 6, 2005

(Wednesday).

The stocks will qualify for the ready forward facility.

Bids for underwriting of the Government Securities under auction can be

submitted by the “Primary Dealers' up to 12.30 p.m. on July 4, 2005(Monday) using

PMO module of NDS and physical bids to the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of

India, Public Debt Office,Fort, Mumbai - 400 001.
