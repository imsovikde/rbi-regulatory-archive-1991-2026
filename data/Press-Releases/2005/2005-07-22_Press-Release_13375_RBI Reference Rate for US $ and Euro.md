## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.3900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8500 on July 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 21, 2005) were Rs.43.5100 and Rs.52.8400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
