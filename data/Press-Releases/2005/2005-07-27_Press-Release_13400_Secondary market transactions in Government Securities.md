## July 27, 2005

Secondary market transactions in Government Securities

In view of severe disruptions in financial markets due to incessant rainfall in Mumbai, there will be no secondary market transactions in Government Securities today, i.e., July 27, 2005. All secondary market transactions scheduled for settlement today will accordingly settle tomorrow i.e. July 28, 2005.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/124
