## June 28, 2005

## <u>RBI places Draft Guidelines on Credit Cards in Public Domain</u>

The Reserve Bank of India today placed the draft guidelines on credit cards on its website (<u>www.rbi.org.in</u>). The guidelines, when finally issued, will be applicable to all commercial banks/Non Banking Finance Companies (NBFCs) in regard to their credit card operations and will come into effect as soon as implemented. Views/ suggestions may please be sent within a period of one month to <u>pvijayabhaskar@rbi.org.in</u> or can be faxed to 022-22150663.

It may be recalled that the Reserve Bank of India had constituted a Working Group to evolve a Regulatory Mechanism for Cards to ensure orderly growth of this segment of consumer credit and protect the interest of the banks/ NBFCs and their customers. The report of the Group was placed in public domain on April 23, 2005. The draft guidelines issued today have been framed taking into account the feedback received from the media, members of the public and others on the report of the Working Group.
