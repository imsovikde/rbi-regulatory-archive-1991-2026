## October 27, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.4800 on October 27, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 26, 2005) were Rs.44.9400 and Rs.54.5200 respectively.
