## December 5, 2005

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auctions of the "8.07 per cent Government Stock, 2017" and "7.40 per cent Government Stock, 2035" to be held on December 6, 2005 at Re.0.03 (three paise) and Re.0.039 (three point nine zero paise) per Rupees 100 respectively.
