# Tap sale of 7.17 per cent State Development Loans 2017 closed for six states

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 22, 2005 Tap sale of 7.17 per cent State Development Loans 2017 closed for six states The 'on tap' sale of 7.17 per cent twelve years State Development Loans, 2017 has closed in respect of six States, i.e. Andhra Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Orissa and Punjab on receipt of the respective target amounts.  Amounts of allotment in respect of these states are being advised to the investors concerned. The sale on tap in respect of loans for remaining ten States, viz., Assam, Bihar, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal will be kept open on February 23, 2005 to collect the balance target amount.  Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office of the respective offices of the Reserve Bank of India and Mumbai Office. Applications will be received at these offices during banking hours while the tap sale is open.  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the tap sale during the banking hours on February 23, 2005 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on February 24, 2005 in respect of subscriptions received / allotted both on February 22 and February 23, 2005. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/890
