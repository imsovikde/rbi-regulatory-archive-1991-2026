# Tap sale of 7.17 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 closed

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 23, 2005 Tap sale of 7.17 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 closed The 'on-tap' sale of 7.17 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 in respect of the remaining ten State Governments viz., Assam, Bihar, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened on February 22, 2005 on behalf of 16 States for an aggregate amount of around Rs. 6,300 crore. Against this, applications for an aggregate amount of Rs. 3,760 crore were received of which, subscriptions for an aggregate amount of Rs. 3,031 crore are being retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the allottees on February 24, 2005, in respect of subscriptions received both on February 22 and February 23, 2005. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/895
