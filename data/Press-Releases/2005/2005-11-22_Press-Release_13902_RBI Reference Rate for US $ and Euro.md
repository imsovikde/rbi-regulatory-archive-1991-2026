## November 22, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.7800 on November 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 21, 2005) were Rs.45.7800 and Rs.53.9200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 21, 2005|78.6203|38.36|
|November 22, 2005|78.7153|38.49|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
