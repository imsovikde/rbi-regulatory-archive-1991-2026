## October 31, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.4300 on October 31, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 28, 2005) were Rs.45.0900 and Rs.54.8200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|October 28, 2005|80.4631|39.11|
|October 31, 2005|80.0928|39.01|
