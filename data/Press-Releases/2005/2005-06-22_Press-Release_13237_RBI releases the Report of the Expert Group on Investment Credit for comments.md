## June 22, 2005

## RBI releases the Report of the Expert Group on Investment Credit for comments

The Reserve Bank of India today released the Report of the Expert Group on Investment Credit. The Expert Group was constituted by the Reserve Bank of India under the Chairmanship of Shri Y.S.P.Thorat, Managing Director of National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) to, inter-alia, analyse the trends in investment credit in the agriculture sector over the last decade in terms of flow of credit for different purposes and land holding groups and to suggest a road map for increasing the investment credit in a time bound manner.

The report of the Group is available on the Reserve Bank’s website (www.rbi.org.in). Views may please be sent to cgmincrpcd@rbi.org.in or csmurthy@rbi.org.in or can be faxed to 022-22658276/022-22658273/022- 22621011 by July 15, 2005.
