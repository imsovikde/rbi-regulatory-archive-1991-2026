## August 30, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.0200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.7900 on August 30, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 29, 2005) were Rs.43.7800 and Rs.53.9900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
