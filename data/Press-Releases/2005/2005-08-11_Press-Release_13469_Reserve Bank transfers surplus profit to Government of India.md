## August 11, 2005

Reserve Bank transfers surplus profit to Government of India

The Reserve Bank’s Central Board, in its meeting held today, approved the transfer of surplus profit to the Government of India amounting to Rs.5, 400 crore for the year ended June 30, 2005.

Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2005-2006/194
