## Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on September 20, 2005 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Vaibhav Gems Ltd. through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 60 per cent limits of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and General Body Meetings.
