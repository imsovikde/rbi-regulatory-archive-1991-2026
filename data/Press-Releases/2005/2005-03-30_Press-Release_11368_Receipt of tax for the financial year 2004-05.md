# Receipt of tax for the financial year 2004-05

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 30, 2005 Receipt of tax for the financial year 2004-05 The Reserve Bank of India offices at Mumbai and Navi Mumbai have made arrangements for the convenience of taxpayers to receive cash deposits representing taxes at the counters upto 3.30 p.m. on March 31, 2005. Cheques/instruments representing payment of taxes to Government would be received upto 6.30 p.m. on March 31, 2005. Instructions have also been issued to the agency banks, viz., State Bank of India and its associates, public sector banks and designated private sector banks to receive taxes beyond normal banking hours on March 31, 2005. The Reserve Bank has advised taxpayers to take advantage of these facilities. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1018
