## September 14, 2005

## 894 Bank Branches to accept IT

The Reserve Bank of India has advised income-tax assessees in the cities of Mumbai and Navi Mumbai to take advantage of paying their income tax dues at any of the designated bank branches. The income-tax department has authorised 894 computerised branches of public sector and private sector banks in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai to accept payment of income-tax dues. Of these, 833 are public sector bank branches, 23 branches of HDFC Bank, 9 branches of ICICI Bank, 16 branches of UTI Bank and 13 branches of IDBI Bank.

The Reserve Bank has also advised income-tax assessees to avoid last minute rush at the Reserve Bank's/designated banks' counters and to deposit the income-tax dues sufficiently in advance.
