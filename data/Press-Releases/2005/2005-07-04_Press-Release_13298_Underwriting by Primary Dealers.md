## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auctions of the "7.27 per cent Government Stock, 2013" and 10.25 per cent Government Stock, 2021 to be held on July 5, 2005 at Re.0.12 (Twelve paise) and Re.0.23 (twenty three paise) per Rupees 100 respectively.
