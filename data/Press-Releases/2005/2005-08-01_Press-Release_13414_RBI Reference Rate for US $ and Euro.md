## August 1, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.4500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8700 on August 1, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 29, 2005) were Rs.43.4900 and Rs.52.7300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|July 29, 2005|76.3402|38.82|
|August 1, 2005|76.4829|38.70|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
