## December 14, 2005

Marksans Pharma Ltd., Monitoring FII Investments

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on December 14, 2005 that the paid up capital of M/s. Marksans Pharma Ltd., formerly known as Tasc Pharma Ltd., has increased due to its amalgamation with M/s. Glenmark Laboratories Ltd. The Reserve Bank has further notified that it would be in order to purchase the shares of the company in the secondary market on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines.
