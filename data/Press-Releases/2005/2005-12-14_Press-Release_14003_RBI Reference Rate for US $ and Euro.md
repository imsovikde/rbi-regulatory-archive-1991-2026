## December 14, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.8400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.0100 on December 14, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 13, 2005) were Rs.46.0600 and Rs.55.0800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
