## July 26, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4200 on July 26, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 25, 2005) were Rs.43.5000 and Rs.52.4300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|July 25, 2005|75.5660|38.95|
|July 26, 2005|75.9185|38.94|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
