## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.0100 on June 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 21, 2005) were Rs.43.6100 and Rs.52.9300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
