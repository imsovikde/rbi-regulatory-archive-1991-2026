## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.6800 on June 30, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 29, 2005) were Rs.43.5500 and Rs.52.4400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
