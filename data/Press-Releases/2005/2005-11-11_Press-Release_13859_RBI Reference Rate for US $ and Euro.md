## November 11, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.5100 on November 11, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 10, 2005) were Rs.45.8100 and Rs.53.9000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 10, 2005|79.8033|38.90|
|November 11, 2005|79.7496|38.90|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
