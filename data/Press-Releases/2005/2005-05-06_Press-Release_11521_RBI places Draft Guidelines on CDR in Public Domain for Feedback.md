# RBI places Draft Guidelines on CDR in Public Domain for Feedback

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 6, 2005] RBI places Draft Guidelines on CDR in Public Domain for Feedback The Reserve Bank of India today placed on its website www.rbi.org.in proposed amendments to the existing guidelines on Corporate Debt Restructuring (CDR) for feedback from the public. Comments on the proposed amendments may be sent within a period of one month by e-mail to arappathurai@rbi.org.in or r-kumar@rbi.org.in The Reserve Bank has proposed some amendments to the existing guidelines on Corporate Debt Restructuring with a view to enhancing their scope and making them more efficient. The amendments are based on the recommendations made by a Special Group under the Chairmanship of Smt. S. Gopinath, Deputy Governor, RBI. The major modifications proposed in the existing CDR Scheme relate to extending the scheme to corporate entities on whom banks and institutions have an outstanding exposure of Rs.10 crore or more, support of 60 per cent of creditors by number in addition to the support of 75 per cent of creditors by value to make the decision making process more equitable, linking the restoration of asset classification prevailing on the date of reference to CDR Cell to implementation of package within three months from the date of approval of the package and restricting the regulatory concession in asset classification and provisioning requirement to the first restructuring. The changes proposed to the CDR Scheme also relate to convergence in the methodology for computation of economic sacrifice among banks and financial institutions, regulatory treatment of non-SLR instruments acquired while funding interest or in lieu of outstanding principal and valuation of such instruments, limiting RBI’s role to providing broad guidelines for the CDR System, enhancing balance sheet disclosures, pro-rata sharing of additional finance requirement, including one time settlement (OTS) as a part of the CDR Scheme  and discretion to the core group in dealing with wilful defaulters in certain cases. Alpana Killawala Chief General Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/1165
