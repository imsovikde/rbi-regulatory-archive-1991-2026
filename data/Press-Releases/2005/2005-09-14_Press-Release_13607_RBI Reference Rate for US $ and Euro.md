## September 14, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9700 on September 14, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 13, 2005) were Rs.43.8800 and Rs.53.9200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
