## September 21, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.5700 on September 21, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 20, 2005) were Rs.43.9100 and Rs.53.4800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|September 20, 2005|79.2817|39.48|
