## July 29, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.4900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.7300 on July 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 26, 2005) were Rs.43.5200 and Rs.52.4200 respectively.
