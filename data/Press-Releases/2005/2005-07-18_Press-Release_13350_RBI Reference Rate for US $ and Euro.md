## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.5600 on July 18, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 15, 2005) were Rs.43.5200 and Rs.52.7800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
