## October 26, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.5200 on October 26, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 25, 2005) were Rs.45.1200 and Rs.53.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|October 25, 2005|79.5736|39.01|
|October 26, 2005|80.2404|39.08|
