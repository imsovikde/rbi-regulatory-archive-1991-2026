## August 22, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.0600 on August 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 19, 2005) were Rs.43.5800 and Rs.53.0100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|August 19, 2005|78.0845|39.45|
|August 22, 2005|78.3596|39.56|
