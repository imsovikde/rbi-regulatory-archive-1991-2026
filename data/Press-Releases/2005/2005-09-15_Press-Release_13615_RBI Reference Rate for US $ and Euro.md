## September 15, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.7000 on September 15, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 14, 2005) were Rs.43.9200 and Rs.53.9700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
