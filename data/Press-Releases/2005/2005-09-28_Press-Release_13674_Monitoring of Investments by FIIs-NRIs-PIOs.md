## September 28, 2005

## Monitoring of Investments by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs

The Reserve Bank of India on September 28, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of undernoted company/bank should be made under portfolio investment scheme (PIS) on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors(FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that these companies have reached the overall limit of their paid up equity capital.

1. Bharti Tele-Ventures Ltd.
2. ICICI Bank Ltd.
