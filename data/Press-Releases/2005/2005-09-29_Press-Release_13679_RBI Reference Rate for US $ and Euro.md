## September 29, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.0900 on September 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 28, 2005) were Rs.44.0800 and Rs.53.0400 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
