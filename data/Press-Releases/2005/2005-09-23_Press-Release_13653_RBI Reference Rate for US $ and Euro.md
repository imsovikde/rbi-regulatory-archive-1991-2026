## September 23, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9300 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.3500 on September 23, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 22, 2005) were Rs.43.9000 and Rs.53.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
