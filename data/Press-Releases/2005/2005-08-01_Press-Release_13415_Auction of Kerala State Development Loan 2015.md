## August 1, 2005

Auction of Kerala State Development Loan 2015

The Kerala state government has announced the sale of a 10 year Kerala State Development Loan 2015 for a notified amount of Rs.250 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on Thursday, August 4, 2005.

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Public Debt Office, Mumbai Office, Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that office on Thursday, August 4, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, the Reserve Bank will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on August 4, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be accepted during banking hours on Friday, August 5, 2005 at Mumbai and Thiruvananthapuram. The stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.
