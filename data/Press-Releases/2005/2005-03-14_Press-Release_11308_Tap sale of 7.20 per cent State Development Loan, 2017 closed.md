# Tap sale of 7.20 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 closed

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 14, 2005 Tap sale of 7.20 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 closed The 'on-tap' sale of 7.20 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 in respect of both the states, viz., Maharashtra and Mizoram has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened today, i.e; March 14, 2005 on behalf of these two states for an aggregate amount of around Rs. 400 crore. Against this, applications for an aggregate amount of Rs. 433.196 crore were received and the full amount is being retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the allottees on March 16, 2005, in respect of subscriptions received today, i.e; March 14, 2005. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/959
