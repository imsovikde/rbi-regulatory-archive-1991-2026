## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.3500 on July 11, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (July 8, 2005) were Rs.43.6300 and Rs.52.0200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
