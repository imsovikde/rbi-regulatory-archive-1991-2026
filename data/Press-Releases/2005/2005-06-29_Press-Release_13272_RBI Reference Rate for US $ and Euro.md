## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5500 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.4400 on June 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 28, 2005) were Rs.43.5600 and Rs.52.8700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
