## December 26, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4300 on December 26, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 23, 2005) were Rs.45.1600 and Rs.53.6200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 23, 2005|78.5220|38.73|
|December 26, 2005|78.1681|38.73|
