## July 27, 2005

Cancellation of Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) and Secondary Markets transactions in Government Securities

The Government of Maharashtra has declared public holidays on July 27 and 28, 2005, under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

Consequently, there will no transactions and settlements in Government Securities and Money Markets on these two days. Settlement of all outstanding transactions will accordingly get postponed to the next working day i.e. July 29, 2005.

Transactions under Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) conducted on July 26, 2005 will accordingly be reversed on July 29, 2005.

This press release is being issued further to RBI's press releases No.122 and 124 issued on July 27, 2005 in these regards.
