## October 28, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.8200 on October 28, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 27, 2005) were Rs.45.0600 and Rs.54.4800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
