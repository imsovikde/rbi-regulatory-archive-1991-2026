## July 27, 2005

## Cancellation of Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF)

The Reserve Bank of India announces that no reverse repo/repo auction will be held under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility today i.e., July 27,

2005.The reversal of yesterday's LAF transactions will now take place on July 28, 2005 instead of today. This measure is being taken due to incessant rainfall in Mumbai disrupting normal financial activities.
G.Raghuraj
