## December 12, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.2000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.6800 on December 12, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 9, 2005) were Rs.46.2200 and Rs.54.5600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 9, 2005|80.9104|38.33|
|December 12, 2005|81.3166|38.23|
