## October 11, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9900 on October 11, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 10, 2005) were Rs.44.6200 and Rs.54.1500 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|October 10, 2005|78.6115|39.25|
|October 11, 2005|78.5956|39.33|
