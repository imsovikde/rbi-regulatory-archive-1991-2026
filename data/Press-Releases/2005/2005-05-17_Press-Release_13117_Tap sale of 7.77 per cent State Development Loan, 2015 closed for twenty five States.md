# Tap sale of 7.77 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 closed for twenty five States

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 17, 2005 Tap sale of 7.77 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 closed for twenty five States The 'on-tap' sale of 7.77 per cent ten-year State Development Loan, 2015 has closed in respect of twenty five States, viz, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Orissa, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Tamilnadu, Tripura, Uttaranchal and West Bengal on receipt of the respective target amounts.  Amounts of allotment in respect of these States are being advised to the investors concerned. The on-tap sale in respect of loans for Uttar Pradesh will be kept open on May 18, 2005 to collect the balance target amount.  Applications in the prescribed form can be obtained from the Public Debt Office, Reserve Bank of India, Kanpur and Mumbai Office. Applications will be received at these offices during banking hours while the tap sale is open.  The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the tap sale during the banking hours on May 18, 2005 having regard to the borrowing requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the investors on May 19, 2005 in respect of subscriptions received / allotted both on May 17 and May 18, 2005. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1206
