## August 23, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.6700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.3800 on August 23, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 22, 2005) were Rs.43.5900 and Rs.53.0600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|August 22, 2005|78.3596|39.56|
