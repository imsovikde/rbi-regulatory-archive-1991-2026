## December 5, 2005

Special SLBC Meeting to discuss Flood Relief Measures in Tamil Nadu

In the context of the recent unprecedented rains in the State of Tamil Nadu leading to widespread floods and consequent destruction of life and property, a Special meeting of the State Level Bankers Committee (SLBC) was convened on December 1, 2005. The meeting, which was attended by officials of the State Government, Reserve Bank of India, banks and financial institutions, noted that the floods had wrought havoc in 22 of the 29 districts and several villages had been marooned. It was decided to invoke, with immediate effect, the Reserve Bank of India’s standing instructions to banks for providing relief in the event of natural calamities.

The relief measures involve conversion of existing loans and grant of fresh loans for new crops, including working capital. It was decided that the special District Consultative Committees would be convened in the affected districts to look into specific packages for measures that would be required for each of the affected districts.

As the recent rains over the last week in Chennai have led to dislocation in normal life, another special meeting of the SLBC was held today to ensure that the banks are geared to provide necessary financial relief to the affected persons. The meeting noted that all bank branches, ATMs and currency chests functioned normally. While banks assured that they would provide consumption loans to the affected persons as also assistance for repairs and reconstruction of dwelling units, reconstruction of small enterprises, the SLBC decided to constitute a Special Task Force comprising senior officials of the State Government, Reserve Bank of India, banks, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development, National Housing Bank, Small Industries and Development Bank of India and Life Insurance Corporation as well as United India Insurance. The Task Force will assess the extent of damage and specifically recommend special relief measures which may be needed.
