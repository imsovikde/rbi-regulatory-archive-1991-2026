Pantaloon Retail (India) Limited: Investment by FIIs

The Reserve Bank of India on December 30, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Pantaloon Retail (India) Limited, under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Pantaloon Retail (India) Limited has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
