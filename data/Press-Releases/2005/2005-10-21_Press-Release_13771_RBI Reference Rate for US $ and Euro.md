## October 21, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.0900 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3100 on October 21, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 20, 2005) were Rs.45.2000 and Rs.54.0900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|October 20, 2005|79.5656|39.08|
|October 21, 2005|80.0821|39.09|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
