## December 21, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.8600 on December 21, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 20, 2005) were Rs.45.0300 and Rs.53.9900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 20, 2005|79.3068|38.64|
|December 21, 2005|79.7106|38.73|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
