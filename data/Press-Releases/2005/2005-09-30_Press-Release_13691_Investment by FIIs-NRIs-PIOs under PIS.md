## Investment by FIIs/NRIs/PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on September 30, 2005, has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Vijaya Bank should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)/Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) through stock exchanges in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Vijaya Bank has reached the limit of 18 per cent of its paid up capital.
