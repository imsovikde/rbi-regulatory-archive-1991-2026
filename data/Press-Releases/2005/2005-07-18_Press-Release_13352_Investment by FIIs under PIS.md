## Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on July 18, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of undernoted companies should be made under portfolio investment scheme (PIS) on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that these companies have reached the limit of 22 per cent of their paid up capital.

1. Grasim Industries Ltd.
2. Tata Tea Ltd.
