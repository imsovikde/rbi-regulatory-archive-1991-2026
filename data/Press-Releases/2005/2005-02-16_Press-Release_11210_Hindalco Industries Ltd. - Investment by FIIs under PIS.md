# Hindalco Industries Ltd. : Investment by FIIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 16, 2005 Hindalco Industries Ltd. : Investment by FIIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on February 16, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Hindalco Industries Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Hindalco Industries Ltd., has reached the trigger limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/866
