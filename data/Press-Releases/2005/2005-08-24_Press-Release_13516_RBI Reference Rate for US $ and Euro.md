## August 24, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.7200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.2300 on August 24, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 23, 2005) were Rs.43.6700 and Rs.53.3800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|August 23, 2005|78.5929|39.72|
