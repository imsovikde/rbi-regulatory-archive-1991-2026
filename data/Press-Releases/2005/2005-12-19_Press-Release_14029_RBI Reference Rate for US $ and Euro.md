## December 19, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3700 on December 19, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 16, 2005) were Rs.45.3800 and Rs.54.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
