## November 17, 2005

Foreign shareholdings in Private Sector Banks under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has notified on November 17, 2005 that it would be in order for Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs)/Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to purchase the shares of Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd. through stock exchanges in India under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS). The Reserve Bank has stated that consequent on the amalgamation of Bank of Punjab with Centurion Bank with effect from October 1, 2005, the paid up capital of the erstwhile Centurion Bank now known as Centurion Bank of Punjab Ltd., has increased and therefore aggregate foreign share holdings have gone below the ban limit.
