## October 11, 2005

Mid-term Review of Annual Policy for 2005-06 on October 25

As announced in the Annual Policy Statement for 2005-06, Dr. Y.V.Reddy, Governor, Reserve Bank of India will present the Mid-term Review of Annual Policy Statement for 2005-06 on Tuesday, October 25, 2005. He will meet the chief executives of major scheduled commercial banks on that date at 11.00 a.m. in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai.

Ajit Prasad Manager
