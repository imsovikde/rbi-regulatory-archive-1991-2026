# Government of Kerala announces the auction of Kerala State Government Loan 2015

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 15, 2005 Government of Kerala announces the auction of Kerala State Government Loan 2015 The State Government of Kerala have announced the sale of a 10 year Kerala State Development Loan for a notified amount of Rs.300 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on April 20, 2005 (Wednesday). Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai-400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Wednesday, April 20, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The results of the auction will be announced on April 20, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be during banking hours on April 21, 2005 (Thursday) at Mumbai and Thiruvananthapuram. The stock will qualify for the ready forward facility. Bids for underwriting of the Kerala Government Securities under auction may be submitted by the ‘Primary Dealers’ up to 12.30 p.m. on April 19, 2005 (Tuesday) to the Chief General Manager, Internal Debt Management Department, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Fort, Mumbai 400 001. P. V. Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1080
