## November 2, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3600 on November 2, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 31, 2005) were Rs.45.1100 and Rs.54.4300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|October 31, 2005|80.0928|39.01|
|November 02, 2005|79.8150|38.69|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
