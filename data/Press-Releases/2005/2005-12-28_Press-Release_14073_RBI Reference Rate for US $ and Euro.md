## December 28, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6900 on December 28, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 27, 2005) were Rs.45.2000 and Rs.53.5000 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
