## June 21, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.6100 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.9300 on June 21, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 20, 2005) were Rs.43.5400 and Rs.53.2200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|June 20, 2005|79.5062|40.05|
|June 21, 2005|79.4901|39.88|
