## October 25, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9100 on October 25, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 24, 2005) were Rs.45.1400 and Rs.53.9400 respectively.
