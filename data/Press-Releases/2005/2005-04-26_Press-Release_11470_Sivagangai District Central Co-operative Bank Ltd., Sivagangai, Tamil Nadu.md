# Sivagangai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., Sivagangai, Tamil Nadu

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 26, 2005 Sivagangai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., Sivagangai, Tamil Nadu There have been enquiries regarding the present status of the Sivagangai District Central Co-operative Bank Ltd., which has been placed under directions recently. National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) has undertaken an inspection of the bank to find out its financial position as on March 31, 2005. It has been ascertained from NABARD that the audit work of the bank as on March 31, 2005 is still under progress and the relevant data pertaining to the financial position as on that date is yet to be made available to the inspection team. NABARD expects the inspection to be completed and the Report made available by the first week of May 2005. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1116
