# Tap Sale of State Government Loans

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u>

September 6, 2005 Tap Sale of State Government Loans Eleven State Governments viz; Arunachal Pradesh, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Madhya Pradesh, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal have offered to sell 7.53 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 having maturity of ten years aggregating around Rs. 2900 crore on September 13, 2005 by way of tap sale under the normal market borrowing programme.

The sale will be held at Mumbai for all the 11 States as also at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after close of banking hours on or after September 13, 2005 having regard to the subscriptions received. The payment by the successful applicants will be made on September 15, 2005.

Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Offices at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the aforesaid Offices during banking hours while the sale is open.

Ajit Prasad Manager
