## December 13, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.46.0600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.55.0800 on December 13, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 12, 2005) were Rs.46.2000 and Rs.54.6800 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|December 12, 2005|81.3166|38.23|
|December 13, 2005|81.6782|38.41|
