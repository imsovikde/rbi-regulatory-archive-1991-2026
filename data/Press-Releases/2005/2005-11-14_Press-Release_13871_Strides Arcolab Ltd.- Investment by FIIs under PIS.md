## November 14, 2005

Strides Arcolab Ltd.: Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on November 14, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Strides Arcolab Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through primary/secondary markets in India without the prior permission of the Reserve Bank. The Reserve Bank has stated that Strides Arcolab Ltd., has reached the limit of 22 per cent of its paid up capital.
