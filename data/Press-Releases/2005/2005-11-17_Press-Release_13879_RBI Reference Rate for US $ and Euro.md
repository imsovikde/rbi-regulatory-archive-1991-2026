## November 17, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.7700 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4300 on November 17, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 16, 2005) were Rs.45.8200 and Rs.53.6600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|November 16, 2005|79.4381|38.41|
|November 17, 2005|78.6146|38.49|
