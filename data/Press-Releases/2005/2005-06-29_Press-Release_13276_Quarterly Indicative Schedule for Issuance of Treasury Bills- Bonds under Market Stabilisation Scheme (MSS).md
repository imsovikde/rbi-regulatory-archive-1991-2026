The issuance of Treasury Bills under the MSS will be undertaken by increasing the notified amounts for the regular auctions of 91-day, 182-day and 364-day Treasury Bills. Thus, the issuances under MSS covered by the above quarterly indicative schedule will be undertaken in addition to the regular issuances of Treasury Bills and dated securities.

The issuance of 91-day and 364-day Treasury Bills in this quarter will essentially be the roll over of these Treasury Bills issued earlier. However, on account of shortfall in the absorption in 364-day Treasury Bills auction conducted under MSS on August 18, 2004, the net proposed issuance of 364- day Treasury Bills will be around Rs. 249 crore (face value). In the case of 182-day Treasury Bills, the net proposed issuance will be Rs. 6,000 crore as there will not be any rollover. Taking into account the redemption of 6.18 per cent Government Stock 2005 issued under MSS for Rs. 20,000 crore on September 3, 2005, there will be net release of liquidity amounting to Rs. 13,751 crore.

The above indicative schedule is subject to variations depending on market conditions and other relevant factors. In addition to the proposed issuances indicated in the schedule, the Reserve Bank will also have the flexibility for additional issuances of Treasury Bills and dated securities based on the prevailing liquidity conditions and other relevant factors.

It may be recalled that on March 25, 2004 a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Government of India and the Reserve Bank of India was signed for launching of MSS. The scheme was launched through a system of releasing quarterly indicative schedules for issuances of Treasury Bills/dated securities under MSS. So far, under the scheme five indicative quarterly schedules have been released on March 25, 2004, June 29, 2004, September 29, 2004, December 31, 2004 and March 28, 2005. This indicative schedule for July-September 2005 quarter is in continuation to the earlier schedules.
