## September 22, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6200 on September 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 21, 2005) were Rs.43.9200 and Rs.53.5700 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|September 21, 2005|79.3481|39.36|
