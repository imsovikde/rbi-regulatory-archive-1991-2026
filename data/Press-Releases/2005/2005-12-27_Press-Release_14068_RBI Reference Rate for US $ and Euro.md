## December 27, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.2000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.5000 on December 27, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 26, 2005) were Rs.45.1200 and Rs.53.4300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
