## August 4, 2005

RBI releases Report of the Internal Group on Rural Credit and Micro Finance

The Union Finance Minister in his statement on Union Budget – 2005- 2006 had suggested that the Reserve Bank of India should examine the ways and means to enable banks would give more focus to provide credit to the rural and farm households through innovative measures and promote and empower MFIs so that they can intermediate between the lending banks and the beneficiaries. Accordingly, an internal group was set up by the Reserve Bank to examine the issue of allowing banks to adopt “agency” model by using the Civil Society Organisations (CSOs) and other external entities for providing credit support to the rural and farm sectors, study the feasibility and modalities of appointment of “Banking Correspondents” to function as intermediaries between the lending banks and the beneficiaries, suggest steps to promote Micro Finance Institutions (MFIs) and examine the extent of regulation, if need be, of MFIs. The draft report submitted by the group was placed on the website of the Reserve Bank on June 2, 2005 inviting responses from public. The responses received from public were analysed by the committee and after incorporating these suggestions, wherever necessary, the report has been modified.

A copy of the modified report is now placed on the website of the Reserve Bank. This report will form the document based on which the Reserve Bank will be holding discussions with the Government of India and other agencies concerned while firming up the policies on this subject.

The modified report of the Group is available on the Reserve Bank of India’s website (www.rbi.org.in).
