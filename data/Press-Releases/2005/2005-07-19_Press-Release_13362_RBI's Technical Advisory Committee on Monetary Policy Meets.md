## July 19, 2005

## RBI's Technical Advisory Committee on Monetary Policy Meets

The First Meeting of the Technical Advisory Committee on Monetary Policy was held today in the Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai. The meeting was chaired by Dr.Y.V.Reddy, Governor.

The Committee reviewed the macroeconomic developments in the context of the forthcoming first quarterly review of the Annual Policy Statement for the year 2005-06, scheduled for July 26, 2005.

It may be recalled that with a view to further strengthening the consultative process in monetary policy, the Reserve Bank of India had set up a Technical Advisory Committee on Monetary Policy with external experts in the areas of monetary economics, central banking, financial markets and public finance.
