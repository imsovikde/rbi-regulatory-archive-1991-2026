## November 30, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.9400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.1600 on November 30, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (November 29, 2005) were Rs.45.9200 and Rs.54.2900 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:
