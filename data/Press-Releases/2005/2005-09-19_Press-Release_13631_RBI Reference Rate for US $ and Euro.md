## September 19, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.9000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.1500 on September 19, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (September 16, 2005) were Rs.43.8700 and Rs.53.9100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
