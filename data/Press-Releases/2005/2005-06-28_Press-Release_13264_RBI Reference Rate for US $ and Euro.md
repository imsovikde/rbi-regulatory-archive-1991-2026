## RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.5600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.52.8700 on June 28, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (June 27, 2005) were Rs.43.4900 and Rs.52.8200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
