## December 16, 2005

## Monitoring FII Investments under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India has on December 16, 2005 notified that the Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) holdings in the undernoted companies have gone down below 22 per cent of their paid up capital. The Reserve Bank has further notified that it would be in order to purchase the shares of these companies in the secondary market on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) subject to the existing ceilings and guidelines.

1. Hindalco Industries Ltd.
2. Trent Ltd.
