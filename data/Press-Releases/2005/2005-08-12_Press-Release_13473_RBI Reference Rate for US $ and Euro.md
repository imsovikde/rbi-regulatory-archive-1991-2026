## August 12, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.6000 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.54.3300 on August 12, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 11, 2005) were Rs.43.5800 and Rs.54.0300 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

|---|---|---|
|August 11, 2005|78.3329|39.49|
