## October 24, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.1400 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9400 on October 24, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 21, 2005) were Rs.45.0900 and Rs.54.3100 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|October 21, 2005|80.0821|39.09|
|October 24, 2005|79.7330|39.05|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.

The SDR-Rupee rate will be based on this rate.
