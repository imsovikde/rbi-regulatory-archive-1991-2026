# Calendar for Regular Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills

**RESERVE BANK OF INDIA**

|||Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 Calendar for Regular Auction of Government of India Treasury Bills The Reserve Bank of India announces the following calendar of Treasury Bills issuance for the period April 1, 2005 to March 31, 2006.||e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 29, 2005|
|Type of of T-Bills|Periodicity|Notified Amount (Rs. Crore)|Day of Auction|Day of Payment|
|91-Day|Weekly|500|Every Wednesday|Following Friday|
|182-Day|Fortnightly|500|Wednesday preceding the non-Reporting Friday|Following Friday|
|364-Day|Fortnightly|1000|Wednesday preceding the Reporting Friday|Reporting Friday|

The issuance of Treasury Bills covered by this Calendar will be in addition to the issuances of Treasury Bills as given in the indicative Schedule for Issuance of Treasury Bills/Bonds under Market Stabilisation Scheme (MSS) released on March 28, 2005 by the Reserve Bank.

The Reserve Bank of India retains the right to vary the amount and timing, if emerging circumstances warrant, without assigning any reason. The calendar is thus subject to change, if circumstances so warrant, including for reasons such as intervening holidays. Such changes, if any, will be communicated through regular press releases.

The auction of treasury bills will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No. F 2(12)- W&M/97, dated March 31, 1998 issued by Government of India, as amended from time to time.
