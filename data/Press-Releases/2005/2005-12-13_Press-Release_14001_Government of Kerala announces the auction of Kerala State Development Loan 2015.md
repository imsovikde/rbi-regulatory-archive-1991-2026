## December 13, 2005

Government of Kerala announces the auction of Kerala State Development Loan 2015

The State Government of Kerala have announced the sale of a 10-year Kerala State Development Loan for a notified amount of Rs.361.14 crore through a yield based auction using multiple price auction method. The auction will be conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at Mumbai on December 15, 2005 (Thursday).

Bids in the prescribed form obtainable from the Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai Office (Public Debt Office), Fort, Mumbai - 400 001 should be submitted to that Office on Thursday, December 15, 2005. The competitive bids by the NDS members should be submitted only in electronic format using NDS. All bids should be submitted by 12.30 P.M. On the basis of the bids received, RBI will determine the maximum yield at which offers will be accepted.

The result of the auction will be announced on December 15, 2005 and payment by successful bidders will be accepted during banking hours on December 16, 2005 (Friday) at Reserve Bank of India, Mumbai and Thiruvanathapuram. The stock will qualify for the ready forward facility.
