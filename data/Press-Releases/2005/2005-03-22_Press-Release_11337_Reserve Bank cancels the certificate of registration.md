# Reserve Bank cancels the certificate of registration

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 22, 2005 Reserve Bank cancels the certificate of registration The Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred on it under Section 45 IA of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, has cancelled the certificate of registration issued to the following companies for carrying on the business of a Non-Banking Financial Institution: Name of the Company Cancelled on 1. Twin Cities Investments & Finances Limited, SBI Staff Colony, New Bakaram, Hyderabad-500 080 March 2, 2005 2. Parasnath Fin-Sec Pvt. Ltd., 78, Jai Jawan Colony, Scheme No.3, Tonk Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan March 4, 2005 3. Sri Ramalingeshwara Leasing & Autofin Pvt. Ltd., Door No.5-7-72, Opposite Congress Office, Prakasham Bazar, Nalgonda-508 001 March 2, 2005 As such, the above companies cannot transact the business of a non- banking financial institution as defined in Clause (a) of Section 45 I of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
