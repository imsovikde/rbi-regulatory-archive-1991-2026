# Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 1, 2005 Monitoring FIIs investment in Indian Companies The Reserve Bank of India has notified on February 1, 2005 that Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) can now purchase under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) equity shares and convertible debentures of Gujarat Ambuja Cements Ltd. through primary markets and stock exchanges in India upto 40 per cent limits of its paid up capital, as the company has passed resolutions to this effect at its Board of Directors' and  General Body Meetings. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/801
