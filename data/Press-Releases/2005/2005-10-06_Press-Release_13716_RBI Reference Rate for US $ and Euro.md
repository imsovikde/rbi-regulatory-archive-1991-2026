## October 6, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.44.2600 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.4100 on October 6, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (October 5, 2005) were Rs.44.3100 and Rs.52.9200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
