Roll numbers of candidates for Phase I examination for recruitment of Officers in Grade `B' (DR)

Phase I examination for the recruitment of Officers in Grade `B' (DR) is scheduled to be held on October 16, 2005. Admission letters to the admitted candidates for the examination have been sent by post. The role numbers and certain other particulars of the admitted candidates are available on the Reserve Bank of India's website i.e., www.rbi.org.in
