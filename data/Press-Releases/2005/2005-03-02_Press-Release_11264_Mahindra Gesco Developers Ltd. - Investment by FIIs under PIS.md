# Mahindra Gesco Developers Ltd. : Investment by FIIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 2, 2005 Mahindra Gesco Developers Ltd. : Investment by FIIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India on March 2, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Mahindra Gesco Developers Ltd., should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Mahindra Gesco Developers Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/918
