## August 5, 2005

Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd.: Investment by FIIs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on August 5, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd., under Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) should be made on behalf of Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) through stock exchanges in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 24 per cent of its paid up capital.
