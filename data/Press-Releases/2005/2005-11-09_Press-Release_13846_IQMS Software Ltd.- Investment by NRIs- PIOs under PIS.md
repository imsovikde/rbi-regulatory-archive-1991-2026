## November 9, 2005

IQMS Software Ltd.: Investment by NRIs/ PIOs under PIS

The Reserve Bank of India on November 9, 2005 has notified that no further purchases of equity shares of IQMS Software Ltd., should be made on behalf of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) in the secondary markets in India. The Reserve Bank has stated that IQMS Software Ltd., has reached the overall limit of 10 per cent of its paid up capital.
