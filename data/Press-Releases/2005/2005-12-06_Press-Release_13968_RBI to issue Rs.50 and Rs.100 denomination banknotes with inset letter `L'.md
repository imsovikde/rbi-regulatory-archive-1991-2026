## December 6, 2005

RBI to issue Rs.50 and Rs.100 denomination banknotes with inset letter `L'

The Reserve Bank of India will shortly issue Rs.50 and Rs.100 denomination banknotes with inset letter `L' in both numbering panels in Mahatma Gandhi Series – 2005 bearing the signature of Dr. Y.V. Reddy, Governor. Except for the change in the inset letter, the design of these notes to be issued now is similar in all respects to the banknotes in Mahatma Gandhi Series with additional/new security features issued in August 2005. All banknotes in the denomination of Rs.50 and Rs.100 issued by the Bank in the past will continue to be legal tender.
