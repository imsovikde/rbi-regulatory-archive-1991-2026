# Tap sale of 7.77 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 closed

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in May 18, 2005 Tap sale of 7.77 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 closed The 'on-tap' sale of 7.77 per cent State Development Loan, 2015 in respect of Uttar Pradesh has been closed today. It may be recalled that the sale opened on May 17, 2005 on behalf of 26 States for an aggregate amount of around Rs.7,300 crore with a green shoe option of 10% of the target amount. Against this, applications for an aggregate amount of Rs.23,051 crore were received of which, subscriptions for an aggregate amount of Rs.7,554 crore are being retained. As notified earlier, payment will be required to be made by the allottees on May 19, 2005, in respect of subscriptions received both on May 17 and May 18, 2005. Ajit Prasad Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/1212
