## August 29, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.43.7800 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.9900 on August 29, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (August 26, 2005) were Rs.43.7300 and Rs.53.8200 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the rupee are:
