# Tap sale of 7.17% State Government Loan, 2017 for Rs.6300 crore on February 22, 2005

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in February 15, 2005 Tap sale of 7.17% State Government Loan, 2017 for Rs.6300 crore on February 22, 2005 Sixteen State Governments, namely, Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Kerala, Maharahstra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Orissa, Punjab, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal have offered to sell 7.17 per cent State Development Loan, 2017. Having maturity of twelve years, the loans aggregating about Rs. 6300 crore will be sold on February 22,  2005 by way of tap sale. The sale will be under the normal/debt swap market-borrowing programme of the states. The sale will be held at Mumbai for all the 16 states as also at the respective Regional Offices of the Reserve Bank of India. The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during or after close of banking hours on or after February 22, 2005. Successful applicants will have to make the payment on February 24, 2005. Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Offices of the Reserve Bank of India will be received at its Mumbai and other Regional Offices during banking hours while the sale is open. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release: 2004-2005/861
