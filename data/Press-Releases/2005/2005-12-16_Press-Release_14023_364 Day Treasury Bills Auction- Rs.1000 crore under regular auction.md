# 364 Day Treasury Bills Auction: Rs.1000 crore under regular auction

` PRESS RELEASE

RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 <u>www.rbi.org.in</u> \hindi Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358, 2270 3279 <u>e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in</u> December 16, 2005

364 Day Treasury Bills Auction: Rs.1000 crore under regular auction

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction of 364 day Government of India treasury bills for a notified amount of Rs.1000 crore. The auction will be conducted using "Multiple Price Auction" method. The allocation to non-competitive bidders will be outside the notified amount, at the discretion of the Reserve Bank. The sale will be subject to the terms and conditions specified in the General Notification No.F.2(12)-W&M/97 dated 31 March 1998 issued by Government of India and as amended from time to time.

Tenders should be submitted in the prescribed form on Wednesday, December 21, 2005 before 1230 hours. The results will be announced the same evening. Payment by successful bidders will be on Friday, December 23, 2005.

Taking into account all relevant factors, Reserve Bank has decided that the auction of 364-day Treasury Bills for Rs.1000 crore scheduled for December 21, 2005 under quarterly indicative schedule for MSS will not be conducted.

P.V.Sadanandan Manager
