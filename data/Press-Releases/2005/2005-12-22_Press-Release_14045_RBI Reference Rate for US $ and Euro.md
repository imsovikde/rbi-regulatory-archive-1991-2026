## December 22, 2005

RBI Reference Rate for US $ and Euro

The Reserve Bank of India’s Reference Rate for the US dollar is Rs.45.3200 and the Reference Rate for Euro is Rs.53.6400 on December 22, 2005. The corresponding rates for the previous day (December 21, 2005) were Rs.45.3700 and Rs.53.8600 respectively. Based on the Reference Rate for US dollar and middle rates of the cross currency quotes at 12 noon, the exchange rates of GBP and Japanese Yen against the Rupee are:

Currency Date

||1 GBP|100 YEN|
|---|---|---|
|December 21, 2005|79.7106|38.73|
|December 22, 2005|79.0426|38.57|

Note: The reference rate is based on 12 noon rates of a few select banks in Mumbai.
