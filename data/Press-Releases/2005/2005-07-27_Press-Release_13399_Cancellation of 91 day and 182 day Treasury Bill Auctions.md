July 27, 2005 Cancellation of 91 day and 182 day Treasury Bill Auctions

The Reserve Bank of India announces the cancellation of auction of 91-day Government of India treasury bills and 182-day Government of India treasury bills scheduled for today, i.e., July 27, 2005, for Rs.2000 crore and Rs. 1500 crore, respectively, due to incessant rainfall in Mumbai disrupting normal financial activities.

It may be recalled that the announcement of the aforesaid auctions was announced on July 23, 2005.

G.Raghuraj
