## September 6, 2005

## Underwriting by Primary Dealers

The Reserve Bank of India has set the cut-off rate for underwriting bids by Primary Dealers for the auction of "5.69 per cent Government Stock, 2018 and 30 year Government Stock, 2035" to be held on September 8, 2005 at Re.0.3 (three paise) and Re.0.5 (five paise) respectively per Rupees 100.
