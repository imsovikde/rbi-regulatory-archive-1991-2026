# On Tap Sale of State Development Loans Aggregating around Rs. 400 crore, on March 14, 2005

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in March 7, 2005 On Tap Sale of State Development Loans Aggregating around Rs. 400 crore, on March 14, 2005 The Governments of Maharashtra and Mizoram have offered to sell 7.20 per cent State Development Loan, 2017 having a maturity of twelve years, aggregating around Rs. 400 crore, on March 14, 2005 by way of tap sale for repayment of Rural Infrastructure Development Fund (RIDF) loans to the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD). The sale will be held at Reserve Bank of India, Guwahati and at Mumbai (for both the States). The Reserve Bank of India reserves the right to close the sale during the banking hours on or after March 14, 2005 having regard to the requirements of the State Governments and the subscriptions received. The date of payment will be March 16, 2005. Applications in the prescribed form obtainable from the Public Debt Offices at the Reserve Bank of India will be received at the Reserve Bank offices during banking hours while the sale is open. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release :2004-2005/936
