# M/s. Max India Ltd. : Monitoring of investments by NRIs/POIs under PIS

<!-- PAGE 1 -->

` PRESS RELEASE PRESS RELATIONS DIVISION, Central Office, Post Box 406, Mumbai 400001 Phone: 2266 0502 Fax: 2266 0358,  2270 3279 RESERVE BANK OF INDIA www.rbi.org.in www.rbi.org.in\hindi e-mail: helpprd@rbi.org.in April 6, 2005 M/s. Max India Ltd. : Monitoring of investments by NRIs/POIs under PIS The Reserve Bank of India has notified on April 6, 2005 that Non- Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (POIs) can now purchase equity shares and convertible debentures of M/s. Max India Ltd. upto 24 per cent of its paid up capital through stock exchanges in India, as the company has passed a resolution to this effect at its General Body Meeting. P.V.Sadanandan Manager Press Release : 2004-2005/1049
