### <a id="faq-1"></a>1. What are the active Reserve Bank of India Master Directions for Commercial Banks in 2026?
- **Topic Domain:** Commercial Banks Controlling Framework
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 35A of Banking Regulation Act 1949
- **Controlling Document:** [2026-01-14_Master-Direction_13271_Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Internal Ombudsman) Directions, 2026.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2026/2026-01-14_Master-Direction_13271_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Commercial%20Banks%20-%20Internal%20Ombudsman%29%20Directions%2C%202026.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Commercial Banks operate under primary Master Directions issued under the Banking Regulation Act 1949 and Reserve Bank of India Act 1934. Core frameworks include the Master Direction on Financial Statements (Presentation and Disclosures), Master Direction on Interest Rate on Deposits, Master Direction on Priority Sector Lending, and Master Direction on Commercial Banks Internal Ombudsman Directions 2026.

### <a id="faq-2"></a>2. Which Reserve Bank of India Master Direction governs Internal Ombudsman timelines and compensation?
- **Topic Domain:** Customer Service and Internal Ombudsman
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 35A of Banking Regulation Act 1949
- **Controlling Document:** [2026-01-14_Master-Direction_13271_Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Internal Ombudsman) Directions, 2026.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2026/2026-01-14_Master-Direction_13271_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Commercial%20Banks%20-%20Internal%20Ombudsman%29%20Directions%2C%202026.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
The Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Internal Ombudsman) Directions 2026 require regulated banks to appoint an Internal Ombudsman. Complaints rejected partly or wholly by the bank must be reviewed by the Internal Ombudsman before communicating final decisions to complainants.

### <a id="faq-3"></a>3. Which Reserve Bank of India directions govern Credit Information Companies under CICRA 2005?
- **Topic Domain:** Credit Information Companies (CICs)
- **Statutory Authority:** Sections 11 and 37 of Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act 2005
- **Controlling Document:** [2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12926_Reserve Bank of India (Credit Information Companies) Directions, 2025 (Updated as on July 01, 2026).md](../../data/Master-Directions/2025/2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12926_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Credit%20Information%20Companies%29%20Directions%2C%202025%20%28Updated%20as%20on%20July%2001%2C%202026%29.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Credit Information Companies are regulated under the Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act 2005 (CICRA 2005). Reserve Bank of India circulars mandate free annual full credit reports to individuals, compensation of 100 rupees per calendar day for delay in updating credit records beyond 30 days, and deployment of senior management compliance monitoring dashboards.

### <a id="faq-4"></a>4. What directions govern Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) for Regulated Entities?
- **Topic Domain:** KYC, CDD, and AML Directives
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 35A Banking Regulation Act 1949 read with Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002
- **Controlling Document:** [2025-11-28_Master-Direction_13141_Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025 (Updated as on October 1, 2026).md](../../data/Master-Directions/2025/2025-11-28_Master-Direction_13141_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Commercial%20Banks%20%E2%80%93%20Know%20Your%20Customer%29%20Directions%2C%202025%20%28Updated%20as%20on%20October%201%2C%202026%29.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
The Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction applies to all regulated entities including banks, NBFCs, and payment system providers. It specifies Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP), Periodic Updation schedules based on risk classification, and Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) uploads within statutory time limits.

### <a id="faq-5"></a>5. What is the penalty framework and statutory authority under Banking Regulation Act Section 35A?
- **Topic Domain:** Enforcement and Supervisory Directives
- **Statutory Authority:** Sections 35A, 46, and 47A of Banking Regulation Act 1949
- **Controlling Document:** [INDEX.md](../../data/Notifications/2026/INDEX.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Section 35A empowers the Reserve Bank of India to issue directions to banking companies in public interest, to prevent banking affairs from being conducted detrimentally to depositors, or to secure proper management. Monetary penalties for non-compliance are enforced under Section 47A read with Section 46 of the Banking Regulation Act 1949.

### <a id="faq-6"></a>6. Which Master Direction governs Outsourcing of Financial Services by NBFCs?
- **Topic Domain:** NBFC Risk Management and Governance
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 45JA and 45L of Reserve Bank of India Act 1934
- **Controlling Document:** [2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12943_Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025 (Updated as on December 29, 2025).md](../../data/Master-Directions/2025/2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12943_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Non-Banking%20Financial%20Companies%20%E2%80%93%20Know%20Your%20Customer%29%20Directions%2C%202025%20%28Updated%20as%20on%20December%2029%2C%202025%29.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
The Reserve Bank of India Master Direction on Outsourcing of Financial Services by NBFCs restricts outsourcing of core management functions including internal audit, credit appraisal, and KYC compliance. The NBFC board remains ultimately responsible for outsourced actions, data confidentiality, and business continuity.

### <a id="faq-7"></a>7. What directions govern Digital Payment Security Controls and Framework for Offline Payments?
- **Topic Domain:** Payment and Settlement Systems
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 10(2) and Section 18 of Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007
- **Controlling Document:** [INDEX.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2021/INDEX.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Digital Payment Security Controls directions mandate multi-factor authentication, end-to-end encryption, automated anomaly detection, and customer alert systems. The framework for offline payments permits proximity card and wallet transactions without real-time internet connectivity subject to per-transaction and cumulative wallet limits.

### <a id="faq-8"></a>8. Which Master Direction applies to Non-Banking Financial Company Systemically Important Non-Deposit taking Company (NBFC-ND-SI)?
- **Topic Domain:** NBFC Prudential Regulations
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 45JA of Reserve Bank of India Act 1934
- **Controlling Document:** [2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12943_Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies - Know Your Customer) Directions, 2025 (Updated as on December 29, 2025).md](../../data/Master-Directions/2025/2025-11-28_Master-Direction_12943_Reserve%20Bank%20of%20India%20%28Non-Banking%20Financial%20Companies%20%E2%80%93%20Know%20Your%20Customer%29%20Directions%2C%202025%20%28Updated%20as%20on%20December%2029%2C%202025%29.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
NBFC-ND-SI entities with asset size of 500 crore rupees or more are governed by the Scale Based Regulation (SBR) framework and Master Direction on NBFC-SI. It establishes minimum capital adequacy (CRAR of 15 percent), Tier 1 capital minimum of 10 percent, credit concentration limits, and liquidity coverage ratio (LCR) standards.

### <a id="faq-9"></a>9. Which Master Direction controls Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs) prudential exposure limits?
- **Topic Domain:** Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs)
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 21 and Section 35A of Banking Regulation Act 1949 (As Applicable to Co-operative Societies)
- **Controlling Document:** [INDEX.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2016/INDEX.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Urban Co-operative Banks are categorized into a 4-tier regulatory structure (Tier 1 through Tier 4) based on deposit base. Prudential exposure limits cap single borrower exposure at 15 percent and group borrower exposure at 25 percent of Tier 1 capital, with 50 percent priority sector lending mandates.

### <a id="faq-10"></a>10. Which directions regulate Small Finance Banks (SFBs) and Payments Banks?
- **Topic Domain:** Differentiated Banking Licences
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 22 and 35A of Banking Regulation Act 1949
- **Controlling Document:** [INDEX.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2016/INDEX.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Operating Guidelines for Small Finance Banks require a minimum 75 percent priority sector lending allocation and 50 percent of loan portfolio in tickets up to 25 lakh rupees. Payments Banks are prohibited from lending, must maintain a minimum CRAR of 15 percent, and must invest at least 75 percent of demand deposits in statutory government securities.

### <a id="faq-11"></a>11. Which Master Direction governs Fraud Risk Management for Commercial Banks and NBFCs?
- **Topic Domain:** Fraud Risk Monitoring and Governance
- **Statutory Authority:** Section 35A Banking Regulation Act 1949 and Section 45L Reserve Bank of India Act 1934
- **Controlling Document:** [INDEX.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2024/INDEX.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
The Reserve Bank of India Master Directions on Fraud Risk Management mandate a dedicated Special Committee of the Board, early warning signal (EWS) tracking, reporting of accounts classified as fraud to the Central Fraud Registry (CFR), and principles of natural justice through a reasoned show cause notice before fraud declaration.

### <a id="faq-12"></a>12. What is the statutory framework under Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) 1999 for Overseas Direct Investment (ODI)?
- **Topic Domain:** Foreign Exchange Management
- **Statutory Authority:** Sections 10(4) and 11(1) of Foreign Exchange Management Act 1999 (FEMA)
- **Controlling Document:** [2016-01-04_Master-Direction_10199_Master Direction - Direct Investment by Residents in Joint Venture (JV) - Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) Abroad.md](../../data/Master-Directions/2016/2016-01-04_Master-Direction_10199_Master%20Direction%20%E2%80%93%20Direct%20Investment%20by%20Residents%20in%20Joint%20Venture%20%28JV%29%20-%20Wholly%20Owned%20Subsidiary%20%28WOS%29%20Abroad.md)

**Statutory Answer:**
Overseas investment by Indian entities is governed by the Foreign Exchange Management (Overseas Investment) Rules 2022 and Master Direction on Foreign Investment in India. Eligible Indian entities may invest in Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) or Overseas Portfolio Investment (OPI) up to 400 percent of net worth under the automatic route via Authorised Dealer Category-I banks.
